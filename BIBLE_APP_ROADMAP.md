The aim, plainly: a Logos-class study tool for the awakening reader, at $0 entry, with everything that matters faithful to what Yahuah (God) actually said.

The funding plan it slots into is the Mission Statement's: ten thousand partners contributing one hundred dollars a month, twenty-five percent funding the assembly itself, seventy-five percent going back out to the hungry, the homeless, the widow's broken roof, free home repair, the destitute member who needs an interest-free loan up to ten thousand dollars on Deuteronomy 23:19 terms. The app is one of the channels that gathers the ten thousand. It is not the work; it serves the work.

## III. Locked Decisions

Settled with Yoshi. These do not get re-litigated unless Yoshi reopens them.

**Canon scope.** The Protestant 66 (Genesis through Revelation) is the canon and the free tier. Everything beyond — the Apocrypha, Enoch, Jubilees, Jasher, Maccabees, 4 Ezra, 2 Baruch, the Books of Adam and Eve, the Testament of the Twelve Patriarchs, and the rest of the Cepher catalog plus the broader pseudepigrapha we choose to include — sits behind the $4.99 extras tier.

**Platform.** Web-first Progressive Web App. The PWA installs to home screen on iPhone and Android, works offline once installed, supports push notifications, and feels native to a reader who does not dig into the difference. Donations and subscriptions route through Stripe on the web — donations to the 501(c)(3) take 0% platform fee, subscriptions take ~2.9% + 30¢ (versus 15-30% inside Apple's or Google's stores). Native wrappers via Capacitor for the App Store and Play Store come in Phase 2, and they exist for cold discovery only — using Apple's reader-app allowance, the in-store version directs subscribers back to the web for the actual purchase. Desktop comes only if power-study users ask for it.

**AI policy.** Zero AI in the canon text itself. Translation, sacred-name restoration, parenthetical insertion, *son of man → son of Adam* substitution, and verse boundaries are all done by a deterministic Python pipeline that codifies the voice skill's rules. Output is byte-stable, reproducible, and verifiable against the existing published Restored Names editions for the books that overlap. Zero AI in Yoshi's study notes — those are written by Yoshi and projected from the Teaching Corpus into the app verbatim. AI is permitted only as a librarian over already-Yoshi-approved material: semantic search across the canon and the commentary, cross-reference suggestions, Q&A grounded in the Teaching Corpus folders. The model retrieves; it never authors.

**Funding routing.** The 501(c)(3) is the legal home. Donations route through the assembly's existing nonprofit standing and are tax-deductible. Subscription and tier purchases also route through the nonprofit — the Bylaws and Benevolence Policy already in place cover the appropriations. **Critically: the app does NOT publish auditability claims anywhere user-facing.** Churches under 501(c)(3) are exempt from Form 990 disclosure for sound reasons; voluntary auditability claims create attack surface critics weaponize. The line is between *kingdom-pattern statements* (allowed and welcome) and *auditability claims* (out): a sentence that says *we operate on the older church model — twenty-five cents to the assembly, seventy-five cents back out to the destitute, the widow, the broken roof* is a value statement of how the assembly is structured, not a public-disclosure commitment, and it stays. A sentence that says *the books are open, the accounting is transparent, plainly tracked, every dollar accounted for, come audit us* is the auditability claim that critics can demand receipts on, and that has to leave any user-facing surface — the app, the website, the back-cover copy, the App Store listing, all of it. The Bylaws and Benevolence Policy govern how funds are used internally and are board-overseen; they are not externally published. Refined and clarified 2026-05-10 after Claude's prior overreach (treating the 25/75 ratio statement itself as the problem, when only the books-are-open / plainly-tracked auditability language is). The 25/75 split as a kingdom pattern is in. The auditability frame on top of it is out.

## VI. Standing Rules

Apply throughout the build. Violations get caught at Phase 8 if not earlier.

**The voice gate is non-negotiable.** Every line of text the app shows the reader passes the voice-skill standard. The 12 Red Lines and the 12-point editorial checklist are the test. The 1234 of Truth is the master filter. If a sentence pits faith against Torah, the sentence is wrong. If a passage uses the Jew-and-Gentile binary as the framework, the passage is wrong. If a "come home" section prescribes practice, the section is wrong. Every place the canon or the commentary touches Galatians, Romans, or the Judaizer question, the through-line traces Pharisees → Galatian agitators → modern Christianity → modern Hebrew Roots false inclusion → Islam — same flesh-credential gospel, four costumes, one antichrist.

**The voice skill is the standard — external Restored Names editions are DATA, not authority.** Cepher, ISR's Scriptures, Halleluyah Scriptures, Pidgeon's work, and the Greek and Hebrew lexicons themselves are sources we read; the voice skill is the lens we read them through. Verification flow: *check this against the voice skill, not check this against Cepher.* When an external source agrees with the voice skill, that is confirmation. When an external source disagrees, the voice skill wins — and we should be FINDING the external source's errors using the framework, not absorbing them. This applies especially to passages where the standard religious systems (Catholic, Protestant, Hebrew Roots, Messianic) and the standard Restored Names publishers have all inherited the same error from a common source. The voice skill stands above every external source, every translation, every lexicon. The framework is the gold standard.

**Publish-then-edit, not perfect-before-launch.** Yoshi's published-book track record (seventeen books in three months) demonstrates the model: ship work that has the foundation right, find errors in subsequent passes, fix and republish. The work that gets published gathers partners; the work that waits for perfect-before-launch never gets there. With the voice skill as the foundation, even significant errors read to the partner as minor contradictions or "maybe I misunderstood" moments — not as red flags about the work's integrity. Phase 8 (voice-skill verification) is therefore an ongoing discipline rather than a perfectionist hard-gate before launch. Ship version 1 when the foundation is right and the basics work. Find errors. Fix. Ship version 1.1. Trust the foundation to carry the partner through the editing iterations.

**Apply judgment, land the work, Yoshi reviews at project completion — do not gate on inline approvals.** Locked 2026-05-10 session 4 after Yoshi caught the bottleneck: *"i approve things at completion of project so ask questions here and apply them, i dont have time to go read over everything you write the moment you write it, that takes away from efficiency."* The drafting-then-await-approval pattern (DRAFT files in `_scratch/` queued for Yoshi's read-and-bless before landing) was forcing Yoshi to read his own thinking twice — once when he wrote the original instruction, again when Claude expanded it — and was the wrong architecture. The correct pattern: Claude applies the standing voice gate plus the path-decision authority granted in Section IX, lands the work in place, logs what landed and why in Section X, and Yoshi reviews at session close or project completion. Open questions still get logged in Section IX and surface in the next-session handoff prompt; they don't block work that the locked path-authority covers. The supercomputer is supposed to be doing 100x the work, not asking the user to re-read what he just wrote.

**The voice skill is a non-contradiction filter, not a content roadmap.** Locked 2026-05-10 session 4 after Yoshi caught Claude treating the skill as a template to weave into every output: *"voice skill is not a roadmap, its to make sure nothing contradicts it, its not insert this and every aspect of it into every single thing you write."* The skill is the lens. A piece of writing passes the gate when nothing in it contradicts the framework — not when every framework piece is present. Mission Statement language, app commentary, UI strings, study-note prose, and any other deliverable should be evaluated for *whether the writing contradicts anything in the skill*, not for *whether the writing exhibits everything in the skill*. The Red Lines and the 12-point editorial checklist are pass/fail standards on contradiction, not coverage requirements. When drafting, write the cleanest piece that says what the piece needs to say; thread framework references only where the piece's argument requires them. Threading the entire framework into every paragraph produces dense, exhausting copy and misses the point of having a focused deliverable.

**The roadmap is sequential, not a fork.** Locked 2026-05-10 session 5 after Yoshi caught Claude opening session 5 with *"which would you like me to advance — Phase 3 iteration, Phase 4 opening, or off-roadmap?"* — and again, fairly, said *"how is it that a roadmap leaves you wondering wich direction to turn? i thought you built a roadmap that takes you from point a to z with all the inbetween lined out."* He's right. The roadmap is the path from A to Z. Phases close in order. When the current phase is open, the work to do is the next iteration of the current phase — not a fork between this phase and the next. Yoshi-rendered to preserve the two Greek words distinctly in English with a footnote.

- **Psalm 81:3 (the dark / concealed moon)** — Hebrew *keseh* shares its root with *kasah* meaning *to cover, to conceal*. The literal force is *the concealed moon* — the dark moon at astronomical conjunction, not the first visible sliver that later rabbinic tradition uses to start the month. Translators flattened it to "new moon" or "full moon" without preserving the concealment. Yoshi-rendered as something like *the moon's concealment* or *the dark moon* with a footnote explaining the Hebrew root and why the choice matters for the feast calendar.
