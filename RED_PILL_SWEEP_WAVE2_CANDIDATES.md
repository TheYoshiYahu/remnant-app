**What this file is.** The checker-UNGATED candidate list for wave 2 of the Witness sweep.
Every entry below was derived natively through the TRANSFER TEST (no external "deity of
Christ" lists consulted): the NT gives Yahusha (Jesus) a title, an act, or an argument that
the Tanakh says belongs to Yahuah (God) ALONE — or that Yahuah (God) said HE HIMSELF would
do. Every entry still needs (1) card authoring with both sides quoted in full from the
restored canon text, and (2) checker gating against Red Line #12 (son of Adam restoration +
kaph-comparative preserved at Daniel 7:13 / Revelation 1:13 / Revelation 14:14), checklist
10 (Nature of God — Formed/Formless, no Trinity assumption, no modalism), and the come-and-
see authoring posture — BEFORE any loader run. Nothing here duplicates the 39 marked verses
of `data-schema/migrations/session204_red_pill_seed_v1.sql`.

**Entry format.** Each candidate gives: the ref to mark · the one-line transfer (NT claim ←
Tanakh anchor) · why it passes the test · which side(s) carry the mark. "Also mark" lines
name parallel/companion verses that ride the same card. One mark for all classes per
Yoshi's S204 call; the class lives on the card.

**Mark-side convention (matches seed v1).** `both` = the Tanakh anchor is itself a witness
verse and carries the mark alongside the NT claim. `NT` / `Tanakh` = single-side mark; the
unmarked side still appears quoted in full on the card.

- Transfer: *if ye believe not that I am, ye shall die in your sins* ← Exodus 3:14; Isaiah 43:10
- Passes: he makes belief that he is the I AM the condition of life — a condition only the I AM of the bush may set.

- Transfer: *when ye have lifted up the Son of Adam, then shall ye know that I am* ← Isaiah 43:10 (*that ye may know and believe me, and understand that I am he*)
- Passes: Isaiah's know-and-believe-that-I-am-he formula belongs to Yahuah (God)'s witness-trial speeches; he claims its fulfillment at the lifting up.
- Mark: NT (John 8:28)

### John 13:19 + Isaiah 43:10 — That ye may believe that I AM
- Transfer: *that, when it is come to pass, ye may believe that I am* ← Isaiah 43:10 (near-verbatim) + Isaiah 41:23; 46:9-10 (foretelling proves who Elohim (God) is)
- Passes: he claims BOTH halves of Isaiah's exclusive test — declaring the end before it happens, so that they believe *I am he.* Foreknowledge-as-proof is Yahuah (God)'s own courtroom signature.

## CONSIDERED AND EXCLUDED (the test holding the line)

Recorded so the checker and future sweeps do not re-derive these. Each fails the transfer
test as stated — merely "high Christology" in the inherited sense, or anchored to something
the Tanakh does NOT reserve to Yahuah (God) alone:

- **Revelation 3:7 (key of David) ← Isaiah 22:22** — Isaiah's key is given to Eliakim, a man.
  The anchor is not Yahuah-exclusive; the verse is messianic stewardship, not transfer.
- **John 15:1 (the true vine) ← Psalm 80:8** — the vine in the Tanakh is Yashar'el (Israel),
  not Yahuah (God). The claim is covenant-embodiment, not a Yahuah-alone title.
- **2 Thessalonians 2:8 ← Isaiah 11:4** — Isaiah 11:4's breath-of-his-lips already belongs
  to the Branch figure; the anchor is messianic, not Yahuah-exclusive.
- **John 5:46 (Moses wrote of me) ← Deuteronomy 18:15-18** — the prophet-like-Moses is a
  prophecy OF the Messiah, not a Yahuah-alone prerogative.
- **Matthew 28:19 (the name, singular)** — the inherited reading leans Trinity-formula;
  nothing in it survives the transfer test on its own. Leave unmarked.
- **Luke 1:43 (the mother of my Lord)** — vocative honor, no Tanakh-exclusive anchor.
- **John 13:13 (Master and Lord) ← Malachi 1:6** — Malachi's master-honor argument extends
  to human masters by analogy; not exclusive enough to gate cleanly.
- **Genesis 1:26 (let us make)** — the framework can read it, but as a standalone mark it
  invites the inherited plural-persons debate rather than the transfer test. The creation
  transfer is carried cleanly by John 1:3 / Colossians 1:16 ← Isaiah 44:24. Yoshi's call if
  it should ride anyway.
