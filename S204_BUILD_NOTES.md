## What shipped
**Schema** — `data-schema/migrations/session204_red_pill_witness.sql`:
`red_pill_verses` (verse_id UNIQUE, claim_class direct/title/act/
structural, class_label, card_title, card_md, anchor_refs TEXT[],
tier_required default free). Curated-overlay pattern (same shape as
xref threads / tool_annotations). Idempotent.

**Seed** — `data-schema/migrations/session204_red_pill_seed_v1.sql`,
GENERATED by `restoration-pipeline/_session204_build_red_pill_seed.py`
(do not hand-edit the SQL; edit the generator and re-run). 30 cards /
39 marked verses. Every quote is the exact restored verse text pulled
live from canon.json. ON CONFLICT (verse_id) DO UPDATE — re-running
refreshes cards. Type specimens all in: John 8:58 ↔ Exodus 3:14, Good
Shepherd ↔ Ezekiel 34 + Psalm 23:1, Luke 21 ↔ Zechariah 14 + Acts
1:11-12, Romans 7:1-4 first-husband-died, John 14:15 ↔ Exodus 20:6,
Mark 14:62 ↔ Daniel 7:13 (the kaph taught on the card, never
flattened).

quote-don't-cite, sacred names. Verdict: 27 PASS / 3 FLAG; all three
flags were single-phrase fixes (missing John 1:18 quote in the 8:58
card, bare "Yahuah Ra'ah" parenthetical, one use of "person"), fixed
in the generator and re-emitted. Set ships clean.
