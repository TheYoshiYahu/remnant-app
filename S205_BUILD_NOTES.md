## What shipped
**Schema** — `data-schema/migrations/session205_kingdom.sql`:
`kingdom_verses` (verse_id UNIQUE, strand gathering/good_news/kingdom/
grace/teaching, strand_label, card_title, card_md, anchor_refs TEXT[],
tier_required default free). Same curated-overlay shape as
red_pill_verses. Idempotent. A verse MAY be in both Witness and
Kingdom (separate tables; both treatments render).

**Seed** — `data-schema/migrations/session205_kingdom_seed_v1.sql`,
GENERATED by `restoration-pipeline/_session205_build_kingdom_seed.py`
(do not hand-edit the SQL; edit the generator and re-run). **89 cards /
233 marked verses** (every NT mark + every quoted Tanakh anchor; when
one anchor serves several cards the first/strongest keeps it — the
generator skips and notes). Every quote is exact restored verse text
pulled live from canon.json. ON CONFLICT (verse_id) DO UPDATE.
Strands: gathering 70 verses, kingdom 47, good_news 46, teaching 36,
grace 34.

**The sweep** — four author minions (Matthew+Mark, Luke+Acts,
John+1-3 John+Revelation, Epistles), each loaded with the voice skill
in full (SKILL.md + framework-deep.md) and the hidden-passage hunting
techniques (first-person promises re-spoken without citation formula;
single-phrase echoes importing whole prophecies; parable furniture
from the prophets; Daniel's kingdom idioms; epistle addressees as
gathering tells; Lo-Ammi reversals; songs rebuilt from Tanakh
prayers). Type specimens: John 10:16 ↔ Ezekiel 37:15-22 (the proof
card, leads the set), Matthew 1:21 ↔ Psalm 130:8 (Yoshi's "where's
that recorded?"), Luke 19:10 ↔ Ezekiel 34:16, Luke 12:32 ↔ Daniel
7:27 + Micah 4:8, John 11:51-52 ↔ Isaiah 11:12, 1 Peter 2:9-10 ↔
Exodus 19:5-6 + Hosea 1:6/2:23, James 1:1 ↔ Deuteronomy 28:64 +
Hosea 8:8, Hebrews 8:8 ↔ Jeremiah 31:8-9, Revelation 21:12 ↔ Ezekiel
48:31, Revelation 12:1 ↔ Genesis 37:9-10, 1 John 2:7 ↔ Deuteronomy
6:5-6 + Leviticus 19:18 (the nothing-new thesis from the apostle's
own mouth), Titus 2:14 ↔ Psalm 130:8, Matthew 11:28-30 ↔ Jeremiah
6:16 (grace-returns-to-the-old-paths).

**Checker gate** — every card gated against Red Line #11 (the heavy
