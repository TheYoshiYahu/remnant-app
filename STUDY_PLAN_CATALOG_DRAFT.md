# Study-Plan Catalog — DRAFT for redline

The product list. The whole library, read **in order of event**, broken into
**ages** (big time-periods) and, inside each age, **themed plans** — small,
coherent groupings of chapters someone can read in a sitting or two. Each
themed plan is a sellable unit; a whole age can also be sold as a bundle.

Extras (⟡) are woven in at their event-point and can be toggled off for a
canon-only version of any plan. Once you've redlined this list, I author the
exact chapter-by-chapter order inside each plan (event order) and wire the
reader + store to it.

> Each plan below will carry: a **title**, a one-line **blurb**, its **ordered
> chapter set** (canon + extras), and a **tier/price** hook. Titles and
> groupings are first-draft — rename, split, merge, reorder freely.

---

## Age I — Creation & the Watchers

- **In the Beginning** — the six days, the garden, the breath of life. *Genesis 1–2 · ⟡ Jubilees 2 · ⟡ 2 Enoch (creation)*
- **The Fall & the First Family** — the serpent, the exile from Eden, Cain and Abel. *Genesis 3–4 · ⟡ 1–2 Adam & Eve · ⟡ Jasher 1*
- **The Watchers** — the sons of Elohim, the giants, the corruption of the earth. *Genesis 6:1–4 · ⟡ 1 Enoch 1–36 · ⟡ Jubilees 5*
- **The Flood** — the ark, the deluge, the covenant of the bow. *Genesis 6–9 · ⟡ Jasher · ⟡ Jubilees 6*
- **Babel & the Nations** — the tower, the scattering, the table of nations. *Genesis 10–11 · ⟡ Jubilees 8–10 · ⟡ Jasher*

## Age II — The Patriarchs

- **Job: The Oldest Story** — suffering, the whirlwind, the answer of Elohim. *Job (whole)*
- **Abraham's Journey** — the call, the covenant, the binding of Isaac. *Genesis 12–24 · ⟡ Jubilees 11–23 · ⟡ Apocalypse of Abraham · ⟡ Jasher*
- **Isaac & Jacob** — the wells, the ladder, the wrestling, the twelve. *Genesis 25–36 · ⟡ Jubilees 24–32*
- **Joseph in Egypt** — the dreams, the pit, the rise, the reunion. *Genesis 37–50 · ⟡ Testaments of the XII · ⟡ Jasher*

## Age III — Egypt & the Exodus

- **Out of Egypt** — the bush, the plagues, the Passover, the sea. *Exodus 1–18 · ⟡ Jubilees 46–48*
- **Sinai & the Covenant** — the mountain, the ten words, the tabernacle. *Exodus 19–40 · ⟡ Jubilees 49–50*
- **The Priesthood & the Law** — the offerings, the feasts, the holiness code. *Leviticus*
- **The Wilderness Years** — the wandering, the rebellion, the second law. *Numbers · Deuteronomy*

## Age IV — Conquest & the Judges

- **Into the Land** — Jericho, the conquest, the inheritance. *Joshua*
- **The Judges & Ruth** — the cycle of apostasy, the deliverers, the kinsman-redeemer. *Judges · Ruth*

## Age V — The United Kingdom

- **The Rise of David** — Samuel, Saul, the shepherd-king, the wilderness years. *1 Samuel · (Davidic psalms of flight)*
- **King David** — Jerusalem, the ark, the covenant, the fall and the grief. *2 Samuel · 1 Chronicles · (Davidic psalms)*
- **Solomon's Glory** — the temple, the wisdom, the song. *1 Kings 1–11 · 2 Chronicles 1–9 · Proverbs · Ecclesiastes · Song of Solomon*

## Age VI — The Divided Kingdom & the Prophets

- **The Kingdom Splits** — Rehoboam, Jeroboam, two thrones. *1 Kings 12–16 · 2 Chronicles 10–16*
- **Elijah & Elisha** — fire on Carmel, the still small voice, the mantle. *1 Kings 17 – 2 Kings 13*
- **The Northern Prophets** — to Nineveh, to Israel, before the fall. *Jonah · Amos · Hosea*
- **Isaiah & the Holy One** — the throne, the remnant, the suffering servant. *Isaiah · Micah · ⟡ Ascension of Isaiah*
- **The Fall of Judah** — Josiah's reform, the last kings, Jeremiah's tears. *2 Kings 18–25 · 2 Chronicles 29–36 · Nahum · Zephaniah · Habakkuk · Jeremiah · Lamentations*

## Age VII — The Exile

- **By the Rivers of Babylon** — the visions of Ezekiel, the courts of Daniel, the lament for Zion. *Ezekiel · Daniel · ⟡ 2 Baruch · ⟡ 3 Baruch*

## Age VIII — The Return & Second Temple

- **The Return** — the decree, the rebuilt temple, the wall, the last prophets. *Ezra · Haggai · Zechariah · Esther · Nehemiah · Malachi · ⟡ 1 Esdras*
- **Between the Testaments** — the revolt, the wisdom, the four hundred silent years. *⟡ 1–4 Maccabees · ⟡ Sirach · ⟡ Wisdom · ⟡ Tobit · ⟡ Judith · ⟡ the rest of the Apocrypha*

## Age IX — The Messiah

- **The Word Made Flesh** — the announcements, the birth, the boyhood. *Luke 1–2 · Matthew 1–2 · John 1*
- **The Kingdom Proclaimed** — baptism, temptation, the first disciples, early Galilee. *Matthew 3–4 · Mark 1–3 · Luke 3–6 · John 1–4*
- **Miracles & Parables** — the sermon, the signs, the kingdom in stories. *Matthew 5–18 (woven) · Mark 4–9 · Luke 7–17 · John 5–11*
- **The Road to Jerusalem** — the final journey, the entry, the temple. *Matthew 19–23 · Mark 10–12 · Luke 18–21 · John 12*
- **The Passion Week** — the supper, the garden, the cross. *Matthew 24–27 · Mark 13–15 · Luke 22–23 · John 13–19*
- **He Is Risen** — the empty tomb, the appearances, the ascension. *Matthew 28 · Mark 16 · Luke 24 · John 20–21*

## Age X — The Early Assembly & the Letters

- **The Spirit & the Assembly** — Pentecost, the early church, Stephen, the turning of Saul. *Acts 1–12 · James*
- **Paul to the Nations** — the journeys and the letters written along the way. *Acts 13–28 (woven with) Galatians · 1–2 Thessalonians · 1–2 Corinthians · Romans · the prison epistles · ⟡ Acts 29 (Sonnini)*
- **The Last Letters** — the pastoral charge, the call to endure. *1–2 Timothy · Titus · Hebrews · 1–2 Peter · Jude · 1–3 John*

## Age XI — The Revelation

- **The Unveiling** — the throne, the seals, the new heavens and new earth. *Revelation*

  grouping; it spans the 1 Kings / 2 Kings seam.
- **Tier/price** per plan is a hook in the data model, not set here — tell me
  how you want plans priced (free canon-only, paid with extras, per-age
  bundle, all-access) and I'll wire it.
