**Title:** The lost sheep first — the woman of Canaan, the divorced house, and the children's bread (Deuteronomy 24; Hosea 2; Mark 7)

It ain't new. A woman cries after him on the coast of Tyre and Sidon, and the disciples want her sent away. His answer is to them, and it tells them who she is:

*But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 15:24)

That was his whole errand. He had given the twelve the same boundary before he ever met her:

*But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 10:6)

And she is not a stranger to the house. Hear what she calls him:

*And, behold, a woman of Canaan came out of the same coasts, and cried unto him, saying, Have mercy on me, O Adonai (Lord), thou Son of David; my daughter is grievously vexed with a devil.* (Matthew 15:22)

*Son of David* is the covenant word to David's house in the mouth of a woman on a Canaanite coast. She knew whose house the king came from, and she worshipped him. The sheep hear the voice because they are sheep, and Hosea had already named the place where such sheep would be found:

*Then said Elohim (God), Call his name Lo-ammi: for ye are not my people, and I will not be your Elohim (God).* (Hosea 1:9)

*Yet the number of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) shall be as the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured nor numbered; and it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God).* (Hosea 1:10)

So why does he call her a dog, and why is the bread withheld? Because the house she belongs to was put away:

*And I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Yashar'el (Israel) committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce; yet her treacherous sister Yahudah (Judah) feared not, but went and played the harlot also.* (Jeremiah 3:8)

And the Torah he gave does not let a husband take back the wife he has put away:

*Her former husband, which sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife, after that she is defiled; for that is abomination before Yahuah (LORD): and thou shalt not cause the land to sin, which Yahuah Elohayka (the LORD thy God) giveth thee for an inheritance.* (Deuteronomy 24:4)

A woman is bound to her husband as long as he lives, and only a death frees her to be married again:

*For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband.* (Romans 7:2)

*Wherefore, my brethren, ye also are become dead to the law by the body of Messiah (Christ); that ye should be married to another, even to him who is raised from the dead, that we should bring forth fruit unto Elohim (God).* (Romans 7:4)

He has not died yet. The divorced house cannot be seated at his table while he lives as that husband, so the bread is the children's, the undivorced house of Yahudah (Judah), and the scattered are what we became out there, dogs under the table. Mark keeps the word that carries the timing:

*But Yahusha (Jesus) said unto her, Let the children first be filled: for it is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it unto the dogs.* (Mark 7:27)

First. Not never. She hears the order and does not argue it. She asks for what falls from the table now, and he calls that faith great. The redeeming of the house that was put away comes on the other side of his death, and Hosea says how it ends:

*And I will sow her unto me in the earth; and I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy; and I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people; and they shall say, Thou art my Elohim (God).* (Hosea 2:23)

The order of the sending shows the same two halves. Before the death, *go not into the way of the Gentiles*; after it:

*Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit):* (Matthew 28:19)

Not a new people added to the house, but the same house sought in every place it was scattered to, once the death that frees it had been died. Matthew 15 does not say the death is the reason; Deuteronomy, Romans and Mark's *first* say it, line upon line. The woman of Canaan is a woman from that land and a sheep of the house, revealed by her hearing, given the crumbs before her time and the table after it.

## card 124214

Isaiah 56:8 — *Adonai Yahuah (The Lord GOD) which gathereth the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel) saith, Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him.* *But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 15:24) Isaiah's order is the order of this coast. The outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel) are gathered, and *others* are gathered to him *beside those that are gathered unto him*: the house already home, and the scattered house still to be brought. When he says he is sent only to the lost sheep, that second gathering is what he is naming, and the woman is of it.

Matthew 28:19 — *Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit):* *But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 15:24) Before his death he says he is sent only to *the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)*, and the twelve were told *go not into the way of the Gentiles* (Matthew 10:5).
