## card 2705

Luke 17:14 — *And when he saw them, he said unto them, Go shew yourselves unto the priests. And it came to pass, that, as they went, they were cleansed.* *And Yahusha (Jesus) says unto him, See you tell no man; but go your way, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.* (Matthew 8:4) In Matthew the man is healed and then told to *shew thyself to the priest*. Here ten men hear nearly the same word, *Go shew yourselves unto the priests*, and are cleansed *as they went*. Yahusha (Jesus) heals them, and then he sends them down the road Moses laid out.
