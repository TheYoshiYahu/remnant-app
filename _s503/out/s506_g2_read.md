- NOTE: John 20:1 — *The first day of the week cometh Mary Magdalene early, when it was yet dark, unto the sepulchre, and seeth the stone taken away from the sepulchre.* *And when the sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome, had bought sweet spices, that they might come and anoint him.* (Mark 16:1) John follows Mary Magdalene alone, *early, when it was yet dark*. Mark has her with the others and the spices. John has her seeing *the stone taken away* and running with the news. Whoever else is with her, she is the one who is there first.
- MEMBER: John 20:1 — *Mary Magdalene early, when it was yet dark, unto the sepulchre.* John shows Mary Magdalene at the sepulchre in the dark, the first to see the stone moved.

**card 5370** — mark 16:8 → matthew 28:8
- NOTE: Matthew 28:8 — *And they departed quickly from the sepulchre with fear and great joy; and did run to bring his disciples word.* *And they went out quickly, and fled from the sepulchre; for they trembled and were amazed: neither said they any thing to any man; for they were afraid.* (Mark 16:8) Mark tells how they trembled. Matthew shows the same fear and says it came with *great joy*, so they *did run to bring his disciples word*. Fear is in both accounts, and in Matthew it does not keep them from the errand they were sent on.
- MEMBER: Matthew 28:8 — *departed quickly from the sepulchre with fear and great joy.* Matthew keeps the women's fear but joins it to joy and a run to carry the word to the disciples.
- (was: thread:women-as-the-first-witnesses-of-the-resurrection-and-the-faithful-witness-substance | *And they went out quickly, and fled from the sepulchre; for they trembled and were amazed.* The fear is the faithful-witness fear of the resurrection-substance breaking into the world.)

**card 5372** — mark 16:9 → matthew 28:9
- NOTE: Matthew 28:9 — *And as they went to tell his disciples, behold, Yahusha (Jesus) met them, saying, All hail. And they came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him.* *Now when Yahusha (Jesus) was risen early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven devils.* (Mark 16:9) Mark says he appeared first to Mary Magdalene. Matthew shows the meeting. As they went, Yahusha (Jesus) *met them*, and they *held him by the feet, and worshipped him*. The first to be seen by him are the women, and they take hold of him and worship.
- MEMBER: Matthew 28:9 — *they came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him.* Matthew shows the women meeting the risen Yahusha (Jesus), taking hold of his feet and worshipping.

**card 5371** — mark 16:9 → john 20:11
- NOTE: John 20:11 — *But Mary stood without at the sepulchre weeping: and as she wept, she stooped down, and looked into the sepulchre,* *Now when Yahusha (Jesus) was risen early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven devils.* (Mark 16:9) Mark says he appeared first to Mary Magdalene. John shows how it went. She *stood without at the sepulchre weeping* and stooped to look in, and it is to her that Yahusha (Jesus) then speaks and sends. The first to be seen is the woman who would not leave the grave.
- MEMBER: John 20:11 — *as she wept, she stooped down, and looked into the sepulchre.* John shows Mary weeping at the empty tomb and looking in, the woman Mark says he appeared to first.
