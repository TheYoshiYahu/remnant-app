# S507 — the four Luke-batch thread summaries to read before anything deploys (229, 692, 680, 558). Model: Claude Fable 5.1

## `effort-over-accomplishment`

**TITLE:** Effort over accomplishment — the just man falls seven times and rises, and the one who gives the new heart says both halves (Proverbs 24; Ezekiel 36; Ephesians 2; Isaiah 46)

It ain't new. The one who says *I do not this for your sakes* is the same one who says *depart from me, ye that work iniquity*, and the one who promises a new heart is the same one who says that heart will loathe itself for what it did. Both are in the same book, often in the same chapter, and they were never two voices. Proverbs puts the whole of it in one line:

*For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.* (Proverbs 24:16)

The just man is not the man who never fell. Nobody is that man:

*For there is not a just man upon earth, that doeth good, and sinneth not.* (Ecclesiastes 7:20)

*If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;* (1 Kings 8:46)

*For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of Elohim (God);* (Romans 3:23)

So the line between the just and the wicked is not drawn at the fall. It is drawn at what the man does after it. The just rises. The wicked stops his ears:

*He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination.* (Proverbs 28:9)

*But they refused to hearken, and pulled away the shoulder, and stopped their ears, that they should not hear.* (Zechariah 7:11)

Hear what is done for the house, and for whose sake:

*Therefore say unto the house of Yashar'el (Israel), Thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); I do not this for your sakes, O house of Yashar'el (Israel), but for mine holy name’s sake, which ye have profaned among the heathen, whither ye went.* (Ezekiel 36:22)

*A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.* (Ezekiel 36:26)

*And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* (Ezekiel 36:27)

And read what the new heart does first, in the very same chapter:

*Then shall ye remember your own evil ways, and your doings that were not good, and shall lothe yourselves in your own sight for your iniquities and for your abominations.* (Ezekiel 36:31)

That is the sign of the new heart: not a head held high because it is saved, and not a head held high because it is righteous, but a head that goes lower the more mercy it sees. Job, Isaiah and Daniel all have it:

*I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear: but now mine eye seeth thee.* (Job 42:5)

*Wherefore I abhor myself, and repent in dust and ashes.* (Job 42:6)

*Then said I, Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts).* (Isaiah 6:5)

*We have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even by departing from thy precepts and from thy judgments:* (Daniel 9:5)

The one who gives the heart for his own name's sake is the one who also says a name can be blotted out, and that a righteous man who turns away is not carried by what he did before:

*And Yahuah (LORD) said unto Moses, Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.* (Exodus 32:33)

*But when the righteous turneth away from his righteousness, and committeth iniquity, and doeth according to all the abominations that the wicked man doeth, shall he live? All his righteousness that he hath done shall not be mentioned: in his trespass that he hath trespassed, and in his sin that he hath sinned, in them shall he die.* (Ezekiel 18:24)

*And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.* (Matthew 7:23)

*He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life, but I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels.* (Revelation 3:5)

Paul holds both halves in three verses, and the pulpit usually stops reading after the second:

*For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of Elohim (God):* (Ephesians 2:8)

*Not of works, lest any man should boast.* (Ephesians 2:9)

*For we are his workmanship, created in HaMashiach Yahusha (Christ Jesus) unto good works, which Elohim (God) hath before ordained that we should walk in them.* (Ephesians 2:10)

The gift is for his name's sake, and the walk is the works he ordained. The one who keeps the first half and drops the second has put a period where there was a colon. The one who keeps the second half and drops the first builds a righteousness of the flesh and holds his head up for it. Scripture holds both, because it is one voice. Yahusha (Jesus) sets the measure for a brother at seven times in a day, the proverb's own number:

*And if he trespass against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him.* (Luke 17:4)

And the whole of it is sealed by the end he declared before any of it began:

*Remember the former things of old: for I am Elohim (God), and there is none else; I am Elohim (God), and there is none like me,* (Isaiah 46:9)

*Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure:* (Isaiah 46:10)

The end is the gathering: the twelve tribes home, the new heart given, the statutes walked in. The man who falls and rises is on that road. The man who stops his ears has left it, whatever he accomplished. Effort over accomplishment is not a loophole. It is the shape of the whole book.


## `son-of-adam-as-judge-and-ladder`

**TITLE:** The Son of Adam — the seed's own name: the ladder at Bethel, the judgment given to him, the ransom, and the going up (Genesis 28; Daniel 7; Psalm 8; Mark 10; John 1)

It ain't new. The name Yahusha (Jesus) uses for himself more than any other, the Son of Adam, has a lineage in it. Adam's book of generations runs straight down to Luke's:

*This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that Elohim (God) created man, in the likeness of Elohim (God) made he him;* (Genesis 5:1)

*Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of Elohim (God).* (Luke 3:38)

John opens the name with Jacob's ladder:

*And he saith unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of Elohim (God) ascending and descending upon the Son of Adam.* (John 1:51)

*And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of Elohim (God) ascending and descending on it.* (Genesis 28:12)

*And, behold, Yahuah (LORD) stood above it, and said, I am Yahuah Elohim (the LORD God) of Abraham thy father, and the Elohim (God) of Isaac: the land whereon thou liest, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed;* (Genesis 28:13)

What Jacob saw set up on the earth with its top in heaven, the angels going up and down on it, Yahusha (Jesus) says they will see upon himself. He is the ladder. The one who stood above it and spoke the promise to Jacob's seed is the one now standing in front of Nathanael in flesh, and the flesh he stands in is that seed. The name carries the judgment too, and the reason given is the name itself:

*And hath given him authority to execute judgment also, because he is the Son of Adam.* (John 5:27)

Daniel had seen one like him brought before the Ancient of days and given the kingdom. Daniel says *like*, and it stays *like* on his page; what is given there is the kingdom that does not pass:

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.* (Daniel 7:13)

*And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:14)

The psalm asks what the son of Adam is that he should be visited at all, and answers with dominion:

*What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of Adam, that thou visitest him?* (Psalm 8:4)

*Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:* (Psalm 8:6)

Mark has the name at the forgiving of sins, at the sabbath, at the suffering, and at the ransom:

*But that ye may know that the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins, (he saith to the sick of the palsy,)* (Mark 2:10)

*Therefore the Son of Adam is Adonai (Lord) also of the sabbath.* (Mark 2:28)

*And he began to teach them, that the Son of Adam must suffer many things, and be rejected of the elders, and of the chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise again.* (Mark 8:31)

*For even the Son of Adam came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.* (Mark 10:45)

Being master of the sabbath is not the sabbath put away; it is the one who made the day saying whose it is. And the ransom is the servant Isaiah saw bearing the iniquities of the many:

*He shall see of the travail of his soul, and shall be satisfied: by his knowledge shall my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities.* (Isaiah 53:11)

Hebrews says why the name is a seed-name and not a title any man could wear:

*For verily he took not on him the nature of angels; but he took on him the seed of Abraham.* (Hebrews 2:16)

Ezekiel saw a man's likeness on the throne above the firmament long before the Son of Adam said where he had been before:

*And above the firmament that was over their heads was the likeness of a throne, as the appearance of a sapphire stone: and upon the likeness of the throne was the likeness as the appearance of a man above upon it.* (Ezekiel 1:26)

*And no man hath ascended up to heaven, but he that came down from heaven, even the Son of Adam which is in heaven.* (John 3:13)

*What and if ye shall see the Son of Adam ascend up where he was before?* (John 6:62)

*Therefore, when he was gone out, Yahusha (Jesus) said, Now is the Son of Adam glorified, and Elohim (God) is glorified in him.* (John 13:31)

The one who came down took Adam's line in the flesh, and the one who goes up is the same, and the name goes with him both ways. The ladder, the judgment, the ransom and the glory all hang on one word the translators flattened to *man*: Adam.


## `my-sheep-hear-my-voice-hearing-reveals-not-creates`

**TITLE:** My sheep hear my voice — hearing shows what a sheep already was (Ezekiel 34; John 10; John 8; Jeremiah 31; Revelation 13)

It ain't new. The shepherd who says his sheep hear his voice is the shepherd Ezekiel heard say he would go and find them himself:

*For thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out.* (Ezekiel 34:11)

*As a shepherd seeketh out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered; so will I seek out my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.* (Ezekiel 34:12)

Then the sentence the pulpit has to turn around to keep its gospel:

*But ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep, as I said unto you.* (John 10:26)

*My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me:* (John 10:27)

*And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.* (John 10:28)

*My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.* (John 10:29)

Read the order. He does not say they are not his sheep because they do not believe. He says they do not believe because they are not his sheep. The pulpit preaches that believing makes a sheep. The shepherd says a sheep hears, and the hearing shows what it was. He says the same thing in the same gospel more than once:

*He that is of Elohim (God) heareth Elohim's (God’s) words: ye therefore hear them not, because ye are not of Elohim (God).* (John 8:47)

*And he said, Therefore said I unto you, that no man can come unto me, except it were given unto him of my Father.* (John 6:65)

*He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.* (Matthew 13:11)

The Father gave them, and the giving is old:

*Yahuah (LORD) hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.* (Jeremiah 31:3)

*For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.* (Romans 8:29)

*And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.* (Revelation 13:8)

The names were written before the world was laid. The hearing comes in time and shows which names are there. So when Yahusha (Jesus) says to a woman in a house, or to a blind man on the road, that her faith or his faith has saved them, the faith is a sheep hearing:

*And he said to the woman, Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace.* (Luke 7:50)

*And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto him, Receive thy sight: thy faith hath saved thee.* (Luke 18:42)

And the other sheep are not another kind:

*And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd.* (John 10:16)

Those are the scattered of the house, out in the places Ezekiel named, and they will hear because they are his. This is why the good news is told everywhere and chased nowhere. The word goes out wherever the Father sends it, the sheep in the crowd hear, and the ones who will not hear are left where they stand:

*And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.* (Matthew 10:14)

*Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest they trample them under their feet, and turn again and rend you.* (Matthew 7:6)

The shepherd goes after the one that is lost because it is his:

*What man of you, having an hundred sheep, if he lose one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine in the wilderness, and go after that which is lost, until he find it?* (Luke 15:4)

He does not go after what was never his. Hearing does not make a sheep. It finds one.


## `the-seventy-sent-out-as-yasharel-numbered-against-the-seventy-nations`

**TITLE:** The seventy sent out two and two — the number of the nations, the number of Jacob's house, and the elders under Moses (Genesis 10; Deuteronomy 32; Numbers 11; Luke 10)

It ain't new. Seventy is a counted number before Luke uses it. The nations after the flood, the house of Jacob going down into Egypt, and the elders Moses gathered to the tabernacle all come to seventy, and Luke alone tells of seventy sent out:

*After these things Yahusha (Lord) appointed other seventy also, and sent them two and two before his face into every city and place, whither he himself would come.* (Luke 10:1)

Count the nations first:

*These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, in their nations: and by these were the nations divided in the earth after the flood.* (Genesis 10:32)

The families listed in that chapter come to seventy. Then count the house that went down into Egypt:

*And the sons of Joseph, which were born him in Egypt, were two souls: all the souls of the house of Jacob, which came into Egypt, were threescore and ten.* (Genesis 46:27)

*And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were seventy souls: for Joseph was in Egypt already.* (Exodus 1:5)

Moses sings that the two numbers were set against each other on purpose, when the nations were divided after Babel:

*When the El Elyon (most High) divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Deuteronomy 32:8)

*For the LORD’S portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance.* (Deuteronomy 32:9)

The oldest Hebrew copies of that verse read *sons of Elohim (God)* where the later copies read *children of Yashar'el (Israel)*; either way the bounds of the nations were set by a number, the number is Jacob's, and Jacob himself is the portion kept back. The nations went under other princes at the dividing, and scripture does not hide it:

*I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the El Elyon (most High).* (Psalm 82:6)

*But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes.* (Psalm 82:7)

*Arise, O Elohim (God), judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.* (Psalm 82:8)

In the wilderness the number of the house repeats in its elders:

*And Yahuah (LORD) said unto Moses, Gather unto me seventy men of the elders of Yashar'el (Israel), whom thou knowest to be the elders of the people, and officers over them; and bring them unto the tabernacle of the congregation, that they may stand there with thee.* (Numbers 11:16)

So when Yahusha (Jesus) sends seventy before his face, it is the house numbered as it was numbered going into Egypt and under Moses, sent out into the cities the nations were given at the dividing, with the charge the twelve had:

*And heal the sick that are therein, and say unto them, The kingdom of Elohim (God) is come nigh unto you.* (Luke 10:9)

*Even the very dust of your city, which cleaveth on us, we do wipe off against you: notwithstanding be ye sure of this, that the kingdom of Elohim (God) is come nigh unto you.* (Luke 10:11)

When they come back he tells them what he saw while they went, and what they were given to do to the princes of those cities:

*And the seventy returned again with joy, saying, Adonai (Lord), even the devils are subject unto us through thy name.* (Luke 10:17)

*And he said unto them, I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven.* (Luke 10:18)

*Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.* (Luke 10:19)

The serpent and the seed of the woman have been at enmity since the garden, and the treading was promised there:

*And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.* (Genesis 3:15)

Then he says why the thing is hidden from some and seen by others, and it is not their cleverness:

*In that hour Yahusha (Jesus) rejoiced in spirit, and said, I thank thee, O Father, Yahuah (Lord) of heaven and earth, that thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them unto babes: even so, Father; for so it seemed good in thy sight.* (Luke 10:21)

*All things are delivered to me of my Father: and no man knoweth who the Son is, but the Father; and who the Father is, but the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him.* (Luke 10:22)

The Father hides and the Son reveals. The seventy go out as the house numbered against the nations, and in those cities the ones it was shown to hear them, and the ones it was hidden from get the dust of their own street.
