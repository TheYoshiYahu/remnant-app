## card 161403 — Joshua 12:2 → Psalm 136:19  · thread `joshua-12-sihon-and-og-the-two-great-kings-overthrown`

## card 161409 — Joshua 12:6 → Psalm 136:21  · thread `joshua-12-the-land-given-for-an-heritage-as-yahuah-promised-the-fathers`

## card 177475 — 2 Esdras 7:30 → Jeremiah 4:23  · thread `2-esdras-7-silence-and-the-raising`

Jeremiah 4:23 — *I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light.* *And the world shall be turned into the old silence seven days, like as in the former judgments: so that no man shall remain.* (2 Esdras 7:30) Esdras is told the world goes back to *the old silence* for seven days, *so that no man shall remain*. John 20:25 — *The other disciples therefore said unto him, We have seen Yahusha (Lord).
