This is not another reprint of the 1840 Moses Samuel translation. This is a restored-names edition with full Nazarite commentary, built for the awakening remnant.

Why does Jasher matter? The Spirit named it. Joshua 10:13 cites the book of Jasher as the record of the sun standing still over Gibeon. 2 Samuel 1:18 cites it again for the lament of David over Jonathan. No other book outside the canon is named by name in the canonical scripture — not the book of the wars of YAHUAH, not the chronicles of the kings, not any ancient work. Only Jasher. The very volume now in your hand is cited by name in the inspired text.

If YAHUAH has opened your eyes to His Torah, if you have begun to see that your fathers inherited lies (Jeremiah 16:19), if you are watching the removal of the wicked and the restoration of the righteous in real time — this commentary was written for you. Not to make you proud. Not to make you feel special. But to anchor your feet in the Word, to show you that you are not alone, and to prepare you for what is coming.
