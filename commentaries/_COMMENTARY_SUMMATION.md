# Commentary Summation — The Standard for the Chapter-by-Chapter Pass

**Authored by Yoshi, transcribed session 17, 2026-05-11.**

This is the standing reference the chapter-by-chapter commentary pass measures against. Every commentary file in this folder reads against this summation before it lands. The voice skill is the lens; this document names the chapter-level lens.

---

## I. The Three-Fold Lens — Every Chapter, Every Time

Every chapter of canon, apocrypha, pseudepigrapha, the apostolic fathers, Josephus, Sonnini, and every other witness in the library is read against its relation to:

1. **The seed of promise** — the chosen line, the remnant Yah preserves.
2. **The scattering to the nations** — the dispersion among the gentiles.
3. **The last-days gathering** — the restoration, the wheat harvest, the prophesied return.

Chapters that don't speak directly to one of the three still sit somewhere on the arc. Name where. If a chapter is purely narrative, the commentary names how the narrative sets up the seed line, foreshadows the scattering, or pre-figures the gathering. If a chapter is law, the commentary names how the statute preserves the seed, conditions the scattering, or shapes the regathered nation. If a chapter is prophecy, the commentary names which phase it sits in — and whether it has been fulfilled, partly fulfilled, or is still ahead.

## II. Pulling From the Corpus

Each chapter's commentary draws from:

- **The voice skill's relevant section** for that chapter's framework hook.

Watch closely. Start from the framework. Never modify from the inherited reading.

## III. Grace, and the Opportunity That Can Be Lost

The gospel — the good news of the kingdom — is the **restoration and gathering**: the wheat harvest, Yah's prophesied grace to come. Commentary points to every point of grace as it surfaces in the text.

And though the gathering is by grace, the chaff are still sifted. All the remnant receive the opportunity by grace, **but the opportunity can be lost**. The New Testament warns of this because Torah and the prophets already taught it. Commentary surfaces both halves of that pattern wherever the chapter touches it — the grace freely offered, and the warning that the offer can be forfeited.

## IV. The Eschatological Filter — What Has Not Happened Yet

The framework reads end-times prophecy as **not yet substantially fulfilled.** Concrete tests, drawn from the texts themselves:

- **Ezekiel 20 has not happened.** A nation has not been born in a day.
- **70 AD is not the Day.** The tares have not been burned. The wheat has not been harvested.
- **The Olivet judgment is future.** Yahusha has not stood on the Mount of Olives to war with all the nations He brought against her to lay her desolate for her abominations.
- **The cosmic signs have not landed.** The sun and the moon have not darkened.

Commentary on any chapter that touches these passages or their typology names the framework reading explicitly: this is ahead, not behind. Preterist collapses, Christian Zionist 1948-as-fulfillment readings, and any commentary that reads the Olivet Discourse as substantially exhausted by the second-Temple destruction get filtered out at the commentary level, not just at the rendering level.

## V. The Remnant — Ezekiel 9, Zechariah 12, Zechariah 13

The remnant who survive the Day are named across the prophets in a consistent pattern:

- **Ezekiel 9** — the mark of Yah falls on those who sigh and cry out against the abominations in Jerusalem.
- **Zechariah 13:8–9** — He will leave one third alive in the land.
- **Zechariah 12:10** — that surviving third is the one that looks on Him whom they pierced.

Commentary on these chapters and on every passage that ties to them surfaces the pattern. The mark of Yah is conferred on the posture of crying out against abominations; the third part is preserved through the fire; the surviving remnant recognizes the pierced one.

## VI. Two Falsifications by Internal Contradiction

The framework dismisses two positions by their own internal contradiction, **not** by external argument:

- **The Black Hebrew Israelite movement is wrong by default.** It teaches that only black are the seed of promise. But 1/3 of Israel isn't black, so the Zech 13 surviving remnant alone collapses the claim. Same structural failure as the Quran: the Quran affirms the Bible is true, and then contradicts it — if the Bible is true, the Quran is false; if the Quran is true, the Quran's own affirmation of the Bible falsifies the Quran. Both collapse on their own premises before any framework critique lands.

- **The corollary stands two ways.** First — they *are* Judah, from the tribe of Judah, and **Jew-hate is never justifiable**. It is ignorance, a theme of the devil. The framework is pro-Judah without qualification. Second — and inseparably — **the abominations of the counterfeit gathering still draw the prophetic outcry.** The Ezekiel 9 mark falls on those who cry out against the abominations *in* Jerusalem, not against Jerusalem herself.

## VII. The Posture — System, Not People; Sit Out, Speak Up

The pattern Yah Himself walks across the prophets:

> He brings desolation on her for her abominations.
> Then He stands on the Mount of Olives to war with all the nations He brought against her.

Both moves are His. The framework's posture follows His:

- **Do not support the counterfeit.** No alliance with the system that draws the desolation.
- **Do not raise arms against her.** Yah is the one who brings the nations against her; we are not those nations.
- **Sit it out and speak up.** Cry out against the abominations, as Ezekiel 9 names — the abominations of the system, never the people.
- **Speak truth in love** (Eph 4:15).

Commentary that touches political-eschatological territory — Romans 11, the Olivet Discourse, Daniel, the Revelation, the prophets' oracles against Jerusalem and against the nations — holds this posture explicitly.

This Bible is being built to be **more lucrative and more informative than Logos or anything else has ever produced.** The absolute study Bible of the Remnant of Promise. The go-to. Every chapter of the commentary pass is written to that standard. Treat it as such.

## IX. Related Roadmap Earmark — Strong's With Nikkudot Stripped, Grouped Column View

Surfaced session 17 alongside this summation. A must-have feature in the Strong's-integration territory of Phase 5:

> A toggle that takes out all the nikkudot and shows every word with the same consonantal form in a column, with definitions beside them — so the reader can see the truth of the words and the Masoretic error.

The Masoretic vowel-pointing tradition disambiguated identical consonantal roots into distinct lexical entries by editorial choice. The framework reading is that a portion of those choices obscured connections the unpointed text preserved. The grouped-column view, with nikkudot off, makes the connection visible to any reader without requiring them to read Hebrew. Earmarked in Section V Phase 5 and tracked in Section IX of the roadmap.
