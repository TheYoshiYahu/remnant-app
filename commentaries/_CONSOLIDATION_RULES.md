## The four STANDING VOICE RULES (Yoshi's corrections on the Matthew 1 template)
Apply each WHEREVER its topic arises in your chapter:

1. **Paternal seed / marriage-covenant absorption.** The seed of promise runs
   through the MEN. A wife and children are called by what the man is. A foreign
   woman marrying a son of Yashar'el (Israel) bears Yashar'el children (absorbed
   into his seed-identity via the two-become-one of Genesis 2:24); a daughter of
   Yashar'el marrying a foreign man is absorbed into HIS identity and lost to the
   seed. The mechanism is non-symmetrical. NO foreign MAN ever converts into the
   seed of promise — a man cannot change his father's blood; foreign men who
   attach to the covenant always were and always will be strangers and
   sojourners (gerim). Never present foreign-inclusion-by-faith-confession.

2. **The Formed came IN the flesh — not formed AT it.** Yahusha (Jesus) is the
   Formed, the eternally expressed Word drawn from the Formless, the God of every
   Tanakh theophany. The incarnation is the already-existing Formed TAKING ON
   flesh and remaining who he has always been — NOT the moment the Son came into
   being. Never the "began at Bethlehem" reading. (Father = the Formless infinite,
   never seen; Son = the Formed, seen in every theophany, now in flesh.)

3. **Genealogies (where they appear).** Matthew's genealogy is the husband's
   legal-royal line through Yoseph (Joseph), Miryam's (Mary's) husband; Luke's is
   Miryam's (Mary's) own line through her father Heli, with Yoseph (Joseph)
   entered as son-in-law. The apparent discrepancy resolves because they trace
   two different people's descent — the husband's legal line and the mother's
   blood line — a coordinated double witness.

4. **The Trinity — what the inherited view MISSES, in good faith.** Never argue
   the Trinity down or spar with it (a Christian reader will bounce). Approach it
   as what the inherited Christian reading, held in good faith, quietly misses:
   the simpler, older thing the canon shows — one Yahuah (God), Formless in
   source, Formed in expression, the same Formed of every theophany now in flesh.
   Lead with the scripture and let it expose the gap; the divinity the formula
   wanted to protect is already here, older than the formula.
