## Scope finding — Jubilees was authored under the current convention; Jasher was not

The diagnostic split is sharp and matters for future wheels:

**Jubilees commentary was authored fully under the voice convention.** The
parsed commentary shows 251 `Yahuah (title case)` markers, 0 bare `Israel`
outside parens, 0 bare `Judah` outside parens, 0 son-of-man USE cases, 0
Head-of-Days territory. The Sacred Names convention is applied at every
mention. The book is essentially clean against the voice skill as it stands
in S58.

**Jasher commentary was authored under an earlier convention.** The verses
use `YAHUAH` (all-caps typography) 826 times and `Yahuah` (title case) 0
times. The commentary uses `YAHUAH` 455 times and `Yahuah` 45 times. Bare
`Israel` appears 241 times in prose (outside parens); only 10 of 91 chapters
use `Yashar'el (Israel)` at all, and 6 of those 10 also use bare `Israel`
inconsistently within the same chapter. Bare `Judah` appears 107 times.
Bare `God` appears 21 times in prose. The full Sacred Names application to
Jasher commentary is roughly Phase-D-scale work and was **deferred from
S58 by Yoshi's choice** to keep S58 scoped to the S57(b) Enoch parallel
(Son-of-Adam USE/MENTION + Head-of-Days + kaph-comparative) rather than the
full convention re-apply. See *Deferred to a separate wheel* below.

## What S58 fixed — Jasher Q39 (matches S57(b) Enoch scope)

### Son-of-Adam restorations — USE cases (Red Line #12)

**ch3 — 1 Enoch 71 quote, restored to match S57(b) Enoch corpus.**
Before: *When 1 Enoch 71 records the Head of Days saying to Enoch "You are
the **son of man** who was born unto righteousness," the phrase is a
son-of-Adam designation*
After: *When 1 Enoch 71 records the Head of Days saying to Enoch "You are
the **son of Adam** who was born unto righteousness," the phrase is a
son-of-Adam designation*
Rationale: S57(b) restored the Enoch ch71 commentary to use *son of Adam*
in the direct address from the angel. The Jasher ch3 quote of that same 1
Enoch 71 line now matches the published Enoch reading. The follow-up gloss
*"the phrase is a son-of-Adam designation"* now affirms rather than
translates — appropriate, the affirmation is the point.

**ch30 — John 1:51 USE, both the prose identification and the verse-text
quote restored.**
Before: *John 1:51 … identifies the **Son of Man** as the ladder itself:
Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the
angels of Elohim ascending and descending upon the **Son of man**.*
After: *John 1:51 … identifies the **Son of Adam** as the ladder itself:
Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the
angels of Elohim ascending and descending upon the **Son of Adam**.*
Rationale: The gospels' *Son of Man* is the Messianic title — Red Line #12
absolute. Both the commentary's identification and the John 1:51 quotation
restore.

**ch68 — title-list USE.**
Before: *Yahusha, who is also Jesus, also Immanuel, also **Son of Man**,
also Son of David, also Word, also Lamb, also Rider of the White Horse.*
After: *Yahusha, who is also Jesus, also Immanuel, also **Son of Adam**,
also Son of David, also Word, also Lamb, also Rider of the White Horse.*
Rationale: Title-list naming the Messianic appositives for Yahusha. The
"also Jesus" in the appositive position is preserved as MENTION-style
name-listing (the line is enumerating what Yahusha is also called) — same
posture as the Sacred Names convention's body-text rule, applied to a
name-recital context.

**ch43 v46 — verse text vocative, beast addressing Jacob.**
Before: *And now therefore, O **son of man**, I am in your hands*
After: *And now therefore, O **son of Adam**, I am in your hands*
Rationale: The beast (a lion, per v44–47) speaks to Jacob as *son of man*
— vocative addressing a human, prophetic-address register per Red Line #12.
*Son of Adam* lowercase, generic. Modest scope expansion beyond the
commentary-only USE/MENTION carve-out: one singular verse hit, clean Red
Line #12 application, surfaced for awareness.

### Head-of-Days reconciliation (Red Line #12 + S52 paren convention)

**ch3 — first-mention paren + subsequent prose restoration.**
First-mention: *The one Enoch sees on the throne is the **Ancient of Days***
→ *The one Enoch sees on the throne is the **Head of Days (Ancient of
Days)*** (first-mention paren per S52).

**ch9 — first-mention paren.**
*The Christology of the prophets is the Christology of the prophets — the

walking in the flesh*

statement (not a Daniel cross-ref). First-mention paren convention applied.

## MENTION carve-outs preserved — non-trivial inventory

These are the cases where the un-restored phrasing was preserved
intentionally because the text is *mentioning* a phrase rather than *using*
it. The carve-outs match the S57(b) Enoch precedent (Charles redirect /
scare-quoted title references in ch46/37/62/71). Inline carve-outs:

**Jasher ch3 — three MENTION cases preserved:**

1. *…the mirror-image error R.H. Charles committed when he assumed the
   phrase **son of man** was a title reserved for Messiah (Christ) alone
   and rewrote passages that did not fit his assumption.* — MENTION: Charles's
   phrase being discussed. The framing *"the phrase son of man"* signals
   MENTION. PRESERVE.

2. *Charles's error — making **son of man** a messianic title — produced
   his rewrite of the Ethiopic.* — MENTION: Charles's framing being
   discussed. PRESERVE.

3. *The reverse error — accepting the **son-of-man** identification as
   messianic and thereby enthroning Enoch — would produce a book that turns
   the greatest witness into the one witnessed.* — MENTION: hyphenated
   compound naming the identification being rejected. PRESERVE (also: the
   hyphenated form *son-of-man* is doing technical-term work, like *son-of-Adam
   designation* a few lines above).

**Jubilees ch2 + ch3 — serpent-question MENTION preserved:**

4. ch2: *…the sign Yahuah gave a peculiar people above all peoples that
   they should keep it together with the angels. **Did God really say?**
   The serpent has been asking this question of the seventh day from every
   Sunday pulpit for seventeen centuries.* — MENTION: the serpent's
   Genesis 3 question being quoted as a recurring motif. PRESERVE.

5. ch3: *…the voice of the serpent is the voice of the whole series. **Did
   God really say?** It is not asked once in the garden and then retired.
   It is the same voice at Constantine's Sunday Law, the same voice in the
   Council of Laodicea…* — same MENTION as #4. PRESERVE.

**Jubilees ch10 — verse-quote MENTION preserved:**

6. ch10: *"…for if some of them are not left to me, I shall not be able to
   execute the power of my will on **the sons of men**." And Yahuah grants
   the bargain…* — MENTION: Jubilees verse text quoted verbatim (Mastema
   bargaining). PRESERVE (and: per *Deferred to a separate wheel* below,
   plural *sons of men* restoration is out of S58 scope).

**Jubilees ch31 — antichrist-binary MENTION preserved:**

7. ch31: *…firstborn son is gathered back. Three categories. Two houses.
   One Messiah. The reader who has carried **the Jew and Gentile binary**
   all their reading life can put it down here.* — MENTION: the binary is
   the lie being dismantled (Red Line #1 — the binary is the framework
   error this commentary names and refuses). The phrase *the Jew and
   Gentile binary* is invoking the antichrist binary as a concept to be put
   down, not affirming it as a framework. PRESERVE.

## Deferred to a separate wheel — Jasher Sacred Names application

The S58 scope was *S57(b) Enoch parallel.* The following Jasher-specific
patterns are **not** the Enoch S57(b) parallel and are deferred to a future
wheel:

- **86 plural *sons of men* in Jasher verse text** (and the 7 *sons of men*
  verse-quote citations they generate in Jasher commentary across ch2, ch5,
  ch6). The plural is the seed-of-Adam lineage broadly. Red Line #12's
  *every instance, every time* logic applies to the plural too (*bnei adam*
  → *sons of Adam*), but the published Jasher edition uses *sons of men*
  consistently. The S57(b) Enoch wheel restored singular USE cases (74
  restorations); the plural is a wheel of its own with different scope.

- **241 bare *Israel*, 107 bare *Judah*, 21 bare *God* in Jasher
  commentary** (outside parens). The Sacred Names convention requires
  `Yashar'el (Israel)`, `Yahudah (Judah)`, `Elohim (God)` at every mention.
  Only 10 of 91 chapters currently apply the convention; 58 chapters use
  bare English exclusively. This is roughly Phase-D-scale work for the
  commentary and is its own wheel.

- **826 `YAHUAH` all-caps in Jasher verse text + 455 `YAHUAH` in Jasher
  commentary** (typography). The voice skill calls for `Yahuah` (title
  case). Mechanical normalization, no theology. Same wheel scope as the
  Sacred Names application; deferred together.

The durability flag from S57 still applies: any commentary-side fixes
written into `parsed/*.json` are blown away on the next Phase D re-parse
unless `parse_published_editions.py` is extended with post-parse transforms,
or the published `.txt` source is fixed at the surface. S58's fixes are no
exception — they ride on the parsed JSON the same way S57(b)'s did.
