# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 1

Matthew opens the New Testament canon with seventeen verses the Reformation's preaching tradition has dismissed as *the begats.* The genealogy is doing far more than establishing first-century Yahudi (Jewish) parentage — it is a covenant-architecture argument carrying the shape of the gathering hope the prophets named. (1) The opening phrase *the book of the generation* (Greek *biblos genéseōs*) echoes the Septuagint of Genesis 5:1's *toledot,* placing Yahusha's (Jesus's) line in conversation with Adamic-line continuity, not first-century pedigree records. (2) The triple-fourteen structure (Avraham (Abraham)-to-David, David-to-Babylonian-captivity, Babylonian-captivity-to-Messiah) is a triple-Davidic gematria signature — *David* in Hebrew is *dalet* + *vav* + *dalet* = 14. The construction is the argument. (3) The four women in the line — Tamar, Rachab, Ruth, *her that had been the wife of Uriah* — are markers of seed-preservation by Yahuah's (God's) sovereign hand through narratives that should have broken the line, not the inherited Christian reading of *Gentile inclusion in the line of Christ.* (4) The Babylonian-captivity hinge, named four times in seventeen verses, identifies the unfinished prophetic business the Messiah arrives to address — the gathering of the scattered seed of the twelve tribes, both houses (northern Yashar'el (Israel) deported by Assyria in 722 BCE; southern Yahudah (Judah) deported by Babylon in 605–586 BCE). The post-exilic remnant under Zerubbavel and Ezra was not the prophetic ingathering Hosea 1:11 and Isaiah 11:12 named. (5) The name *Yahusha* (Hebrew *Yah saves* — *Yahu,* the short divine name, plus *sha* from *yasha,* to save) is preserved over the latinized *Jesus* because v.21's reason-clause — *he shall save his people from their sins* — IS the etymology of the Hebrew name made visible; the inherited *Jesus* renders the verse a tautology in English. *His people* in v.21 is not the post-resurrection church projected backwards but the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel) the prophets named, locked explicitly by Matthew 10:5-6 and 15:24; salvation-from-sin in this register is the gathering-and-cleansing of Ezekiel 36:24-27. (6) The closing *Immanuel — Elohim (God) with us* (v.23, quoting Isaiah 7:14) identifies the child as the Formed of every Tanakh theophany — the Yahuah (Lord) who walked with Adam, appeared to Avraham, wrestled with Ya'aqov (Jacob), spoke from the burning bush — now in flesh. Chapter 1 is the front door of the gospel of the gathering.
