# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 10

Matthew 10 is the commissioning of the twelve, falling exactly where Matthew 9 left off — the King had just seen the multitudes as *sheep having no shepherd* and named the harvest plenteous and the labourers few. (1) The number is the architecture. Twelve apostles for twelve tribes — not eleven, not thirteen. The eschatological endpoint locks it: Matthew 19:28's *twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel),* and Revelation 21:12-14's new Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) with twelve gates bearing *the names of the twelve tribes* and twelve foundations bearing *the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.* The Christian-replacement reading that takes the church as undifferentiated post-tribal Israel collapses on the architecture: twelve-tribe-restoration from commissioning to consummation. (2) The mission-scope at vv.5-6 is the most load-bearing scope-statement in the gospel. *Go NOT into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not: BUT go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* Two explicit exclusions bracket the positive scope. *The house of Yashar'el (Israel)* is the Tanakh-prophetic-technical-term for the SCATTERED SEED — the ten northern tribes divorced through the Assyrian captivity (Jeremiah 50:6; Hosea 1:6-11; Ezekiel 34:11-13); the same scope is locked at Matthew 15:24. This is NOT a temporary first-phase tactical instruction superseded by the Great Commission; Matthew 28:19's *teach all nations* extends the SAME scope to the FULL TERRITORY of the dispersion. It is NOT the modern state of Israel; it is NOT a generic spiritual category open to any believer — the Hebrew Roots false-inclusion gospel is Christian replacement theology in Torah clothing. (3) *He that endureth to the end shall be saved* (v.22) is the chapter's prooftext for the grace-that-can-be-lost framework — repeated at Matthew 24:13, locked at Hebrews 10:36-39 (*if any man draw back, my soul shall have no pleasure in him*) and 2 Timothy 2:12 (*if we deny him, he also will deny us*). The institutional-Christian *once saved, always saved* doctrine collapses on the perseverance-architecture the King himself lays down. *Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Yashar'el (Israel), till the Son of Adam be come* (v.23) opens the gathering-mission's present-age tense as a STANDING MISSION across centuries until the Daniel 7:13-14 coming-in-glory. (4) Supporting beats. *Freely ye have received, freely give* (v.8) names the kingdom-economy inverse of Pharisaic-credential transactional religiosity (the same Pharisaic-pattern returns in institutional Christianity's monetization of ministry). *Shake-the-dust-off* (vv.14-15) — Yashar'el (Israel) cities that reject the King's message are placed where Gentile-territory had been (Acts 13:51 records the apostolic application). *Gehenna* (v.28) is the Hebrew *Gei-Hinnom,* the Valley of Hinnom named at Jeremiah 7:31-32; *destroy both soul and body* is the second-death of Revelation 20:14-15, NOT the inherited Christian eternal-conscious-torment overlay. The sword-not-peace (vv.34-36) is the seed-war (Genesis 3:15) surfacing at family-level per Micah 7:6 — the King does not command the strife; the hearing of his voice produces it where the household is divided over whether to hear. The twelve are sent; the labourers go; the gathering of the scattered seed is formally underway.
