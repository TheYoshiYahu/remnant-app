# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 11

Matthew 11 carries two framework-major anchors in its second half: the Father-Son revelation-economy prayer at vv.25-27 and the *come unto me ... my yoke is easy* invitation at vv.28-30. (1) Yochanan's (John's) prison-question (vv.2-3) is NOT a faith-failure but a legitimate-clarification under the inaugurated-but-not-yet tension. The Tanakh-prophets generally carried the prophetic-compression — first-coming and second-coming visible as one horizon. Yahusha's (Jesus's) answer at vv.4-5 cites the Messianic-credential signature (Isaiah 35:5-6; 29:18-19; 26:19; 61:1) — and DELIBERATELY OMITS the *day of vengeance of our Elohim (God)* clause from Isaiah 61:2. The same deliberate-stopping happened at the Nazareth synagogue (Luke 4:17-21). The signal is the gap itself: first-coming credential fulfilled; day-of-vengeance reserved for the second-coming. (2) *Behold, I send my messenger before thy face* (v.10) draws Malachi 3:1 forward at full implicit-Christology — Malachi names the messenger as preparing the way for *Yahuah (the LORD), whom ye seek, [who] shall suddenly come to his temple.* The King is identifying himself as the *Yahuah (LORD)* whose way Yochanan prepares. Yochanan as the Eliyahu (Elijah) of Malachi 4:5-6 (vv.13-14) is the inaugural beat — *if ye will receive it* — with the dreadful-day eschatological fulfillment still ahead. (3) The Father-Son revelation-economy prayer (vv.25-27) is the chapter's direct statement of the framework's nature-of-God reading. The Father is *Lord of heaven and earth* (Genesis 14:19; Deuteronomy 10:14). *Hid from the wise and prudent, revealed unto babes* — those who are sheep before the foundation of the world hear when the Shepherd calls (John 10:27); hearing reveals the lineage, does not create it. *All things are delivered unto me of my Father* names the Son's derived authority. The unique-mutual-knowledge architecture — *no man knoweth the Son but the Father; neither knoweth any man the Father, save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal him* — is the Matthean anchor of the Formless-Formed reading the canon carries (John 1:18; 14:6-9). It rules out the Trinitarian-three-co-equal-persons construction (which flattens the asymmetry) AND the modalist-collapse (which dissolves the distinction). (4) The *come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden* invitation (vv.28-30) is NOT the antinomian proof-text the Christian-commentary tradition has weaponized. *Yoke* in second-temple rabbinic vocabulary was technical — *the yoke of the Torah,* the body-of-teaching one accepted from a rabbi. The contrast is NOT yoke-vs-no-yoke but Pharisaic-oral-tradition-yoke vs the King's-rest-yoke. The labour-and-heavy-laden are the *am ha'aretz* bearing the rabbinic *takkanot,* *gezerot,* and *seyag latorah* fence-architecture Matthew 23:4 names explicitly (*they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne*). The King's yoke IS the Father's-Torah-internalized — the heart-Torah of Jeremiah 31:33 and Ezekiel 36:26-27, with the indwelling-Ruach supplying the substance the credential-without-substance pattern lacked. Acts 15:10 locks it (Kefa (Peter) rejecting *a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear*); 1 John 5:3 locks it (*his commandments are not grievous*); Jeremiah 6:16 is the direct Tanakh-anchor (*ask for the old paths... and ye shall find rest for your souls*). The Spirit who lives in the new-heart citizens is the Spirit who spoke at Sinai. Spirit-led life IS Torah life.
