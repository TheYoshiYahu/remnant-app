# Commentary on Matthew 13

**Framework apologetic for the Yoshi rendering. LANDED 2026-05-19 (session 84).**

The thirteenth chapter of the Matthew sweep. **The parables-of-the-kingdom chapter — the seed-war-pattern at maximum-parable-density.** Seven parables structured in two settings (four from the boat to the multitudes — sower, wheat-and-tares, mustard seed, leaven; three in the house to the disciples — hidden treasure, pearl-of-great-price, dragnet) frame the kingdom's-arrival-and-progress-through-the-age. Four framework-major moments land: the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6:9-10 hardening-citation at vv.13-15 (the cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11); the wheat-and-tares parable at vv.24-30 with the FRAMEWORK-MAJOR interpretation at vv.36-43 (the direct Adam-and-the-Tares architecture from the framework's framework-deep apparatus, the post-harvest-sifting per Red Line #11, the *children of the kingdom* and *children of the wicked one* seed-distinction across the field-of-the-world); the dragnet at vv.47-50 (the recapitulated post-harvest-sifting); the rejection at Natsareth (Nazareth) at vv.53-58 (the *prophet-not-without-honor-save-in-his-own-country* identification of the Pharisaic-pattern at the hometown-level). The chapter dismantles the inherited Christian collapse of the mustard-seed-and-leaven into kingdom-triumphalism-throughout-the-age and restores the framework reading: the field is the world, the tares are sown in among the wheat across the age, and the burning-of-the-tares precedes the gathered-wheat's-shining-as-the-sun in the kingdom of their Father. Reads against `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` as the chapter-level lens, against the voice skill as the spine. Cross-reference apparatus at §12.

## §1. The setting and the parable of the sower

*The same day went Yahusha (Jesus) out of the house, and sat by the sea side. And great multitudes were gathered together unto him, so that he went into a ship, and sat; and the whole multitude stood on the shore. And he spake many things unto them in parables, saying, Behold, a sower went forth to sow* (vv.1-3).

**The framework reads the setting in continuity with Matt 12.** The chapter opens *the same day* — the chapter-of-conflicts-and-credentialing density-peak of Matt 12 closes; the parables-of-the-kingdom chapter opens. The framework reads this as deliberate-architectural-juxtaposition. The chapter that landed the Shabbat-controversies, the Beelzebul-attribution, the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach warning, the sign-of-Yonah refusal, the empty-house parable, and the kinship-redefinition closes with the rejection-disposition of the Pharisaic-system locked. The chapter that opens *the same day* explains in parable-form WHY the King now teaches in parable-form rather than in plain-discourse — the seed-war-pattern between hearing-and-not-hearing has reached the point where the King's-teaching is now veiled-to-the-system and revealed-only-to-the-hearing-disciples.

The framework reads the setting itself in framework-substance. The King teaches from a boat-pulpit on the Sea of Galilee; the multitudes stand on the shore. The Galilee-territory is the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 9:1-2 light-in-darkness territory of the scattered northern house (per Matt 4:13-16). The lake-pulpit reaches the scattered-seed-territories. The multitudes who gather include the *am-haaretz* (the people-of-the-land that the Pharisaic-system categorized as unlearned-and-cursed per John 7:49 *this people who knoweth not the law are cursed*) — the marginalized whom the Pharisaic-credentialing-system had excluded.

**The parable of the sower** (vv.3-9). *Behold, a sower went forth to sow; And when he sowed, some seeds fell by the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them up: Some fell upon stony places, where they had not much earth: and forthwith they sprung up, because they had no deepness of earth: And when the sun was up, they were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered away. And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprung up, and choked them: But other fell into good ground, and brought forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold. Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.*

The framework reads the parable in framework-register and reserves the full-unpacking for the interpretation Yahusha (Jesus) himself gives at vv.18-23 (§3). The framework notes the structural-substance now:

The sower is unnamed-but-implied: the One who has been sowing the kingdom-message from Matt 3 forward (Yochanan (John), Yahusha (Jesus), the twelve disciples sent at Matt 10). The seed is the word-of-the-kingdom (v.19 will name it). The four soils are four kinds-of-hearers; the parable structures the diverse-response-pattern that the gospel encounters across the field-of-the-world.

The framework reads the *who hath ears to hear, let him hear* closing (v.9) as the direct address to the cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11. The hearing is the revealing — those who hear hear because they are sheep; those who do not hear do not because they are not (John 10:26-27). The parable-form itself ENACTS the diagnostic the parable is teaching: those-with-ears-hear; those-without-ears-do-not. The King is sowing in parable-form precisely because the seed-war-pattern between hearing-and-not-hearing must now be visible-in-the-form-of-the-teaching itself.

## §2. Why parables — the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6:9-10 hardening-citation

*And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables? He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given. For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath. Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand. And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias (Isaiah), which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive: For this people's heart is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes they have closed; lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and should understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them. But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear. For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them* (vv.10-17).

**The framework reads vv.10-17 as the chapter's first framework-major moment.** The disciples ask the question (*why speakest thou unto them in parables?*) that the parable-form invites; the King's answer locks the cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnostic per Red Line #11 with direct Tanakh-citation.

**The mysteries-given disposition** (v.11). *Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.* The framework reads this in framework-register. The Greek *dedotai* (passive-perfect-tense, *it has been given*) names a giving-that-precedes-the-hearing. The disciples are not hearing because they figured-out-the-parables and the multitudes are not failing-to-hear because they could not work-it-out. The giving precedes the receiving; the receiving makes the hearing possible. This is the cause-and-effect-reversal of Red Line #11: *if you hear, you are sons of Avraham (Abraham); you are sons of Adam; you are the seed of promise.* You did not choose to hear and become these things. You heard because you are these things. Hearing reveals what was always true. The mysteries are given to those who have-ears-because-they-are-sheep.

**The principle of accumulation** (v.12). *For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.* The framework reads this in continuity with Mark 4:24-25 and Luke 8:18 — the having-and-not-having operates at the level of hearing-and-the-disposition-toward-the-Word. Those-who-receive-the-Word-with-disposition-to-hear receive-more; those-who-receive-the-Word-with-disposition-to-not-hear lose-even-what-they-had-superficially.

The framework reads this against the Pharisaic-system's-trajectory. The system had-the-Tanakh, had-the-temple, had-the-priestly-architecture, had-the-rabbinic-tradition; the system has refused-to-hear the King's-credentialing-substance; the system will lose-even-what-it-had at the AD 70 destruction-of-the-temple. The principle operates at the system-level AND at the individual-level.

**The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6:9-10 citation** (vv.13-15). The framework reads the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6:9-10 citation as the LOAD-BEARING Tanakh-anchor for the cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnostic. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6 is the prophet's-commissioning-vision; Yahuah (the LORD) sends Yeshayahu (Isaiah) to a people whose hearing-and-seeing has already-closed. The prophet's-mission is not to make-them-hear (impossible — they have already closed); the prophet's-mission is to proclaim-the-word and let-the-word-itself-reveal-the-hearers-from-the-non-hearers.

The framework reads the *lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and should understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them* line (v.15b) carefully in framework-register. The Christian-commentary-tradition has sometimes-read this as God-actively-preventing-the-people-from-hearing-so-they-cannot-be-saved (a hard-determinist reading). The framework rules this reading out. The framework reads the line in its Tanakh-substance: the people's-heart has already-waxed-gross; their-ears have already-dulled; their-eyes they-have-closed. The condition is past-tense and ACTOR-initiated — *their* heart, *their* ears, *their* eyes, closed-by-them. The *lest at any time they should see ... and I should heal them* is then the OUTCOME-CLAUSE of the hardening-already-done: in the state-of-the-already-hardened, the proclamation-does-not-result-in-conversion-and-healing because the disposition-to-hear is gone. The Father does not actively block the hardening; the people have actively chosen it; the proclamation continues to reveal-the-hardening rather than penetrate it.

The framework reads this in continuity with the Pharaoh-hardening pattern in Shemot (Exodus). Pharaoh hardened-his-own-heart at Exodus 8:15, 8:32, 9:34; the Father confirmed-the-hardening at Exodus 9:12, 10:1, 10:20, 11:10. The pattern is consistent: the actor-initiates the disposition; the Father confirms-and-uses the disposition for the unfolding of his purposes. The disposition is the actor's; the consequence-of-the-disposition is the Father's-judicial-handing-over.

**The disciples' blessing** (vv.16-17). *But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear. For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.* The framework reads this as the King's-blessing of the disciples in the substance the cause-and-effect-of-hearing has just established. They see and hear because they have-been-given-to-see-and-hear; the giving precedes their-seeing-and-hearing; the blessing names what they have received as gift.

The framework reads the *many prophets and righteous men* line in continuity with 1 Kefa (Peter) 1:10-12 — the prophets inquired-into-the-salvation-that-would-come; they saw-it-from-afar (Hebrews 11:13); they did not see-it-as-the-disciples-see-it-in-the-flesh. The disciples in Matt 13:16-17 are positioned at the unique-moment of the King's-presence-in-the-flesh; their seeing-and-hearing is the realized-substance the prophets foretold.

**The framework's contrast with Reformed-monergistic-determinism.** The framework holds the cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11 (election-precedes-hearing; the hearing-reveals-the-elect) WITHOUT collapsing into the Reformed-monergistic-determinism that denies the actor's-responsibility-for-the-disposition. The framework reads scripture's-witness as: the actor's-disposition is the actor's; the Father's-knowing-from-the-foundation-of-the-world is the Father's; the two operate in scripture's-own-architecture without the Reformed-system's-collapse into hard-determinism OR the Arminian-system's-collapse into self-determinative-libertarian-freedom. The Father knows; the actor disposes; the hearing reveals.

## §3. The interpretation of the sower — the four soils

*Hear ye therefore the parable of the sower. When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and understandeth it not, then cometh the wicked one, and catcheth away that which was sown in his heart. This is he which received seed by the way side. But he that received the seed into stony places, the same is he that heareth the word, and anon with joy receiveth it; Yet hath he not root in himself, but dureth for a while: for when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, by and by he is offended. He also that received seed among the thorns is he that heareth the word; and the care of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, choke the word, and he becometh unfruitful. But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and understandeth it; which also beareth fruit, and bringeth forth, some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty* (vv.18-23).

**The framework reads the interpretation in continuity with vv.10-17's hearing-diagnostic.** The four soils represent four kinds-of-hearers; the fourfold-pattern enacts the cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnostic at the response-level.

**Soil 1 — the way side** (v.19). The Word arrives but is not-understood; the wicked-one catches it away. The framework reads the wicked-one's-snatching as the same actor identified at Matt 13:39 (the wheat-and-tares interpretation): the devil. The not-understanding precedes the snatching; the snatching exploits the not-understanding. The framework reads the *fowls came and devoured them up* of v.4 in the same register that Bereshit (Genesis) 15:11 carries — the unclean-birds-of-prey descend-on-the-covenant-sacrifice; Avraham (Abraham) drives them away. The Tanakh-substance of fowls-as-adversaries-to-the-covenant operates here.

**Soil 2 — stony places** (vv.20-21). The Word arrives with joy but does-not-take-root; the disposition does not survive tribulation-or-persecution. The framework reads this as the partial-deliverance-without-internal-replacement pattern Matt 12:43-45 just described in the empty-house parable. The Word reaches the surface; the new-heart-of-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 does not internalize; the first-test-from-outside reveals the shallow-rooting. The framework reads this in continuity with `grace-and-the-opportunity-that-can-be-lost` — the Word given is grace; the failure-to-internalize is the path-to-losing-what-was-given.

The framework reads the *tribulation or persecution* substance in framework-register. The first-century-disciples faced both — persecution from the Pharisaic-system that conspired-against-the-King (Matt 12:14) and would extend to the apostolic-band (Acts 4-12 onward); tribulation as the broader Tanakh-substance of the day-of-Ya'aqov's (Jacob's)-trouble (Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 30:7). The kingdom-citizens-disposition under tribulation-and-persecution is one of the key-diagnostics of root-substance.

**Soil 3 — among thorns** (v.22). The Word arrives and grows but is choked-by the care-of-this-world and the deceitfulness-of-riches. The framework reads this in continuity with Matt 6:19-34's treasures-in-heaven and seek-first-the-kingdom teaching. The Mammon-or-Elohim binary Matt 6:24 named operates here. The thorns are NOT the Word's-failure; the thorns are the active-disposition of competing-allegiance that strangles the Word's-fruit-bearing.

The framework reads *the deceitfulness of riches* (*apatē tou ploutou*) carefully. *Apatē* is deception. Riches are not-themselves-condemned (Tanakh-witness of Avraham (Abraham), Yitschaq (Isaac), Ya'aqov (Jacob), David, Iyov (Job) — covenant-blessed-with-wealth). The deceit-of-riches is the substance-that-promises-life-but-cannot-deliver — the promise that wealth makes-the-heart-secure or the heart-fulfilled. The Mammon-system in framework-substance operates by this deception.

**Soil 4 — good ground** (v.23). The Word arrives, is heard, is understood, bears fruit-thirtyfold-sixtyfold-hundredfold. The framework reads the *understandeth it* substance carefully. The understanding is NOT mere-intellectual-comprehension; the understanding is heart-receiving with the disposition-to-walk-in-it. The framework reads this in continuity with the *na'aseh v'nishma* (do-and-hear) Tanakh-substance at Shemot (Exodus) 24:7 — the hearing-and-doing IS the understanding in Tanakh-substance. The Word is internalized into the heart-Torah-walking-life; the new-heart of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 internalizes; the fruit follows.

The framework reads the variable-fruit-yield (thirty / sixty / hundredfold) as the recognition that the kingdom-citizens' fruit-yield varies by Spirit-empowering-and-circumstances-of-life, without the variable-yield disqualifying any of the three from being good-ground. The Tanakh-precedent: Bereshit (Genesis) 26:12 — Yitschaq (Isaac) sowed and reaped *an hundredfold* in the land of Gerar. The hundredfold-yield is the covenant-blessing-substance the Tanakh anchors.

## §4. The parable of the wheat and tares

*Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a man which sowed good seed in his field: But while men slept, his enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat, and went his way. But when the blade was sprung up, and brought forth fruit, then appeared the tares also. So the servants of the householder came and said unto him, Sir, didst not thou sow good seed in thy field? from whence then hath it tares? He said unto them, An enemy hath done this. The servants said unto him, Wilt thou then that we go and gather them up? But he said, Nay; lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the wheat with them. Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn* (vv.24-30).

**The framework reads the parable in framework-register, reserving the full-unpacking for the interpretation Yahusha (Jesus) himself gives at vv.36-43 (§7).** The framework notes the structural-substance now.

The parable's-architecture: the man-which-sowed-good-seed-in-his-field; the enemy-who-sowed-tares-while-men-slept; the wheat-and-tares-grown-together-until-the-harvest; the harvest-time gathering-the-tares-first-for-burning-then-the-wheat-into-the-barn. The framework reads each element as the King-himself-will-interpret at vv.36-43. The parable is structurally-positioned to be the chapter's-load-bearing-Adam-and-the-Tares anchor.

**The framework reads *let both grow together until the harvest* (v.30) carefully.** This is the parable's-mid-act instruction that has produced enormous Christian-commentary-controversy. The framework reads it in framework-register:

- The *let-both-grow-together* is NOT a license for the kingdom-citizens to refuse-to-discern in the present age. The kingdom-citizens are taught to discern false-prophets by fruit (Matt 7:15-20), to test the spirits (1 Yochanan (John) 4:1), to mark-and-avoid those-who-cause-divisions-against-the-teaching (Romans 16:17). The discernment-disposition is mandated.
- The *let-both-grow-together* IS a refusal-of-the-final-judicial-uprooting that the kingdom-citizens are NOT authorized to perform in the present age. The kingdom-citizens are not the reapers (v.39 will identify the reapers as the angels). The kingdom-citizens are not authorized to determine-final-judgment-on-individuals; the Father has reserved the harvest-judgment for himself and the reapers-he-appoints.
- The framework reads the *lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the wheat with them* substance against the kingdom-citizens-history of attempts to root-out-the-counterfeit-by-force. The Tanakh-Yahudim (Jewish)-history of forced-conversions and the institutional-Christian-history of inquisitions and forced-baptisms and crusades AGAINST-the-supposed-tares all violate the *let-both-grow-together* instruction. The Father will sort at the harvest; the kingdom-citizens are not the sorting-agency in the present-age.
- The framework reads this in continuity with `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VII's *sit out, speak up* posture. The kingdom-citizens speak-truth and cry-out-against-abominations; the kingdom-citizens do not raise-arms-against-the-system to root-out-the-counterfeit. The Father brings the desolation; the kingdom-citizens do not bring the desolation.

The framework reads this WITHOUT collapsing into the Christian-pacifist-absolutization. The Tanakh's-judicial-architecture (the cities-of-refuge, the courts-of-elders, the death-penalty-for-capital-offenses, the warfare-against-the-Canaanites-in-the-conquest) operates in its-own-Tanakh-frame; the *let-both-grow-together* is the kingdom-citizens'-disposition toward the eschatological-final-judgment which is the Father's-prerogative, not toward the present-age-judicial-functions the Torah establishes.

## §5. The mustard seed and the leaven

*Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took, and sowed in his field: Which indeed is the least of all seeds: but when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and becometh a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches thereof. Another parable spake he unto them; The kingdom of heaven is like unto leaven, which a woman took, and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened* (vv.31-33).

**The framework reads these two short parables carefully in framework-register, against the inherited-Christian collapse into kingdom-triumphalism-throughout-the-age.**

**The mustard seed** (vv.31-32). The inherited Christian-commentary-tradition has read the mustard-seed-parable as the kingdom's-growth-from-small-beginnings-to-global-dominance-throughout-the-age. The framework reads with framework-caution.

- The substance of the parable: the smallness of the mustard-seed → the greatest-among-herbs → a tree → birds-of-the-air-lodge-in-the-branches.
- The framework reads the trajectory of *becoming a tree so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches* against TWO Tanakh-anchors that have-mixed-substance in this register.
- Anchor 1: Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 17:22-24, 31:6, Daniel 4:12 — trees-with-birds-of-the-air-lodging-in-the-branches as imagery of kingdoms-providing-shelter to nations-and-peoples. The kingdoms-of-the-world (Babylon at Daniel 4, the cedar-of-Lebanon at Ezekiel 31) and the future-Messianic-kingdom (Ezekiel 17:22-24) both carry the bird-lodging imagery. The image is not categorically-positive-or-negative; it depends on the kingdom.
- Anchor 2: Matt 13:4 and 13:19 — the BIRDS in the parable of the sower itself are identified as the wicked-one. The birds-of-the-air in the present chapter's-own-vocabulary carry this register.
- The framework holds the parable's-tension in framework-substance. The mustard-seed-kingdom grows. The growth itself is real. The bird-lodging-in-the-branches in the present-age can carry both substances — legitimate-shelter-for-the-nations-being-drawn-toward-the-kingdom AND the unwelcome-development of corruption-finding-shelter-in-the-kingdom-movement.
- The framework rules out the inherited-Christian-collapse into pure-triumphalism. The kingdom's-growth in the present-age IS not a uncomplicated-progress-toward-global-dominance; the wheat-and-tares parable just-given establishes that BOTH wheat AND tares grow-together-until-the-harvest. The mustard-seed-parable read in framework continues the same architecture: the kingdom grows; the growth itself produces structures that birds-of-different-substance can lodge-in; the post-harvest-sifting will sort it.
- The framework reads the parable as the kingdom-citizens' realistic-expectation in the present-age. The kingdom IS growing. The growth IS visible. The growth ALSO produces structures-that-counterfeit-can-shelter-in. Both substances operate-in-the-present-age; the harvest sorts.

**The leaven** (v.33). The inherited Christian-commentary-tradition has often-read the leaven-parable as paralleling the mustard-seed: the kingdom's-positive-permeation of the world.

The framework reads with significant-framework-caution. Scripture's-other-uses of LEAVEN are consistently-negative:

- Shemot (Exodus) 12:15 — leaven-removed-from-the-house at the Pesach (Passover) preparation; the seven-days-of-unleavened-bread is the covenant-substance of separation-from-corruption.
- Vayikra (Leviticus) 2:11 — no leaven in any meal-offering offered to Yahuah (the LORD).
- Matt 16:6, 11-12 — Yahusha (Jesus) warns the disciples to beware *the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees,* and the gospel-itself identifies the leaven as their *doctrine.*
- Luke 12:1 — the leaven of the Pharisees identified as their hypocrisy.
- 1 Korinthians (Corinthians) 5:6-8 — *a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump* — Paul deploys leaven as the metaphor for sin-in-the-assembly to be purged.
- Galatians 5:9 — Paul again — *a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump* — in the context of the Judaizer-influence corrupting the Galatian-assembly.

The framework reads the leaven-parable in continuity with the consistent-Tanakh-and-apostolic-usage. The framework's reading: the leaven IS the corruption-substance hidden-in-the-meal that spreads-until-the-whole-lump is leavened. The kingdom-of-heaven-in-the-present-age is LIKE this in the sense that the unwelcome-development of corruption-within-the-kingdom-movement is the consistent-pattern across the age. The Pharisaic-leaven, the Judaizer-leaven, the antinomian-leaven, the institutional-Christian-Sunday-Law-leaven, the Hebrew-Roots-false-inclusion-leaven — all operate by the same hidden-and-spreading mechanism the parable names.

The framework rules out the inherited-Christian-collapse into kingdom-permeation-triumphalism. The leaven-parable read with scripture's-consistent-leaven-usage is a warning-parable about the unwelcome-development across the age, NOT a triumphalist-progress narrative.

The framework reads the *three measures of meal* in the same register. The three-measures (*sata tria* — about a bushel) is the substance Sarah prepared for the visit of Yahuah (the LORD) and the two-malakim (angels) at Bereshit (Genesis) 18:6 — the covenant-meal-substance. The leaven-hidden-in-the-covenant-meal-substance is the framework's-most-careful reading of the parable.

The framework holds the tension. The kingdom IS growing across the age (mustard-seed). The kingdom-movement is ALSO subject to leavening (leaven-parable). Both parables continue the wheat-and-tares architecture. The harvest will sort.

## §6. The Psalm 78:2 citation — the parables-method's Tanakh-anchor

*All these things spake Yahusha (Jesus) unto the multitude in parables; and without a parable spake he not unto them: That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world* (vv.34-35).

The framework reads vv.34-35 as the Tanakh-citation-anchor for the parable-method itself. Matthew quotes Psalm 78:2 (the King's-own-method connected to the psalmist's-method). The framework reads Psalm 78 in its-Tanakh-substance — Asaph's-recounting of the Tanakh-history-of-Yashar'el (Israel) from the Egypt-deliverance through the wilderness-rebellion through the Davidic-establishment, structured as the *parable* (*mashal*) of Yashar'el (Israel)'s-history that surfaces the meaning-of-the-Father's-dealings.

The framework reads the *things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world* (*kekrymmena apo katabolēs kosmou*) substance in framework-register. The mysteries-of-the-kingdom that the parables-reveal-to-those-with-ears-to-hear are NOT new-doctrines-invented-at-the-incarnation. The mysteries are the substance-kept-secret-from-the-foundation-of-the-world that the prophets-foretold-and-the-King-now-reveals.
