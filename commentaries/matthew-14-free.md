# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 14

Matthew 14 is the chapter where the rejection-trajectory the gospel has been tracing in the religious-system mirrors itself in the political-system, and the King's response — withdrawal followed by deepened self-disclosure — operates at a stature Tanakh-precedent locates in Yahuah (the LORD) himself. Three movements anchor the chapter. (1) The execution of Yochanan (John) the Witness (vv.1-12). Herod-Antipas the tetrarch — son of Herod-the-Great, ruler of the Galilee-and-Perea territory — hears the fame of Yahusha (Jesus) and immediately supplies the explanation his guilty conscience reaches for: *this is Yochanan (John) the Baptist; he is risen from the dead.* The first man who confesses a resurrection-of-the-prophet in the gospel is the man who had the prophet killed. The flashback unfolds the execution: Yochanan (John) had spoken the Torah-substance into Antipas's face on the unlawful marriage to Herodias (Philip's wife, in violation of Leviticus 18:16 and 20:21); Antipas feared the multitude that counted Yochanan (John) a prophet; the daughter of Herodias danced at the birthday-feast; the king made the rash-public-oath; the daughter (instructed by her mother) asked for the head on a charger; Herod was *sorry,* but for the oath's sake and them which sat with him at meat he commanded it given. *Sorry* names the Pharisaic-style of public-credentialing-trap, not repentance — fear of looking weak before the dinner-guests outweighed fear of Yahuah (the LORD). The Izevel-Eliyahu (Jezebel-Elijah) pattern of women using court-influence to destroy the prophet's-voice repeats in costume. The Witness-mission Yochanan (John) had named at John 3:30 (*He must increase, but I must decrease*) is now structurally enacted: the Witness's voice goes silent; the King's voice continues. (2) The feeding of the five thousand (vv.14-21). The King withdraws by ship into a desert-place; the multitudes follow on foot; he is moved with compassion, heals their sick, and feeds them with five loaves and two fishes. Five thousand men, plus women and children; twelve baskets remaining — the twelve resonating with the twelve tribes whose gathering the prophets named. The substance enacted is the shepherd-Yahuah (the LORD) of Ezekiel 34:11-14 (*I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out*) and Psalm 23 (*Yahuah (the LORD) is my shepherd ... Thou preparest a table before me*) and Isaiah 40:11 (*He shall feed his flock like a shepherd*) — the substance Yahuah (the LORD) said in the first person he himself would do is being done by the King in person. The Elisha-precedent at 2 Kings 4:42-44 (twenty loaves feeding a hundred with leftover) is exceeded; the deeper Mosheh (Moses)-mediated wilderness-manna of Exodus 16:4, 16:15 is recapitulated, with the bread-from-heaven feeding the lost sheep in the wilderness without mediation. The greater-than-Elisha and greater-than-Eliyahu (Elijah) the gospel had named (Matthew 11:14) is in the action. (3) The walking on the water and the disciples' recognition-declaration (vv.22-33). The King prays alone on the mountain (the Mosheh (Moses)-on-Sinai and Eliyahu (Elijah)-on-Horev (Horeb) pattern). In the fourth watch he walks on the sea — what Yahuah (the LORD) does in the Tanakh: *which alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the waves of the sea* (Job 9:8); *Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters* (Psalm 77:19); *Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters* (Habakkuk 3:15). *It is I; be not afraid* (v.27) is *egō eimi* — the Greek of the Tanakh's Exodus 3:13-14 rendering of *Ehyeh asher Ehyeh (I AM THAT I AM),* the burning-bush self-naming. The fourth-watch voice across the dark water is the same Voice; the John-gospel *I am* sayings chain (John 6:35, 8:12, 10:7, 10:11, 11:25, 14:6, 15:1, 8:58 — *before Avraham (Abraham) was, I am*) unfolds the same disclosure. Kefa (Peter) walks while his eye is on the King; sinks when it shifts to the wind. The King catches him and names the diagnostic — *O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?* — but does NOT rebuke him for leaving the boat; the rebuke is on the shifted-gaze. The disciples in the boat worship — the first explicit corporate-recognition the gospel records: *Of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God).* The healing at Gennesaret (vv.34-36) closes the chapter — the diseased touch only the hem of his garment and are made perfectly whole; the contagion-of-restoration the priestly-system at Haggai 2:12 had said does NOT normally move through the garment-hem flows in the King's direction. Matthew 15 will open with the credentialing-system arriving from Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) to challenge the disciples' hand-washing — the seed-war the parables-chapter named continuing at the religious-register now after the political-register has displayed it.
