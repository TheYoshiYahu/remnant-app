# Short-form commentary on Matthew 14

The chapter walks three movements — the execution of Yochanan (John) the Witness at Herod-Antipas's hand (vv.1-12); the feeding of the five thousand in the Galilee-desert-place (vv.13-21) with the shepherd-Yahuah (the LORD)-substance of Ezekiel 34 and Psalm 23 and Isaiah 40 enacted; the walking-on-the-water and Kefa's (Peter's) sinking-and-rescuing (vv.22-33) with the *it is I* / *egō eimi* speaking the Tanakh's Ehyeh-self-naming of Exodus 3:14 across the dark water — and closes with the healings at Gennesaret (vv.34-36) where the diseased touch the hem of the King's garment and are made perfectly whole. One framework move the chapter's closing-scene carries — and that the free.md walks only at the level of the Chaggai (Haggai) 2:12 contagion-direction reversal — locks the chapter at its deepest Tanakh-Messianic-architecture register: the hem-of-the-garment healing at vv.34-36 is the King wearing the Torah-prescribed *tsitsit* (fringe) on the *kanaph* (corner) of his garment, with the healing flowing through the kanaph enacting the Tanakh's-own-Messianic-prophecy of Malachi 4:2 — *the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings (kanaph)*.

**The healing-through-the-hem at vv.34-36 is read with the King-walks-in-the-Torah-prescribed-tsitsit substance — the chapter's closing-scene as the Tanakh-Messianic-prophecy of Malachi 4:2's *Sun of righteousness ... healing in his wings* enacted in the visible-substance, with the kingdom-power flowing through the Torah-prescribed-corner of the King's garment.** The scene's narrative:

*And when they were gone over, they came into the land of Gennesaret. And when the men of that place had knowledge of him, they sent out into all that country round about, and brought unto him all that were diseased; And besought him that they might only touch the hem of his garment: and as many as touched were made perfectly whole.* (Matthew 14:34-36)

The Greek *kraspedon* (hem, fringe, border) renders the Hebrew *tsitsit* — the Torah-prescribed fringe on the *kanaph* (corner) of the covenant-garment. The Torah's command is direct:

*Speak unto the children of Yashar'el (Israel), and bid them that they make them fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and that they put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue: And it shall be unto you for a fringe, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the commandments of Yahuah (the LORD), and do them; and that ye seek not after your own heart and your own eyes, after which ye used to go a whoring.* (Numbers 15:38-39)

The King wore the *tsitsit*. The covenant-substance-on-the-corners-of-his-garment was Torah-mandated; he kept the commandment-of-the-fringe as every Torah-observant Yahudi (Jew) of his day did. The diseased of Gennesaret beseech that they might only touch the fringe — the same fringe the woman with the issue of blood reached for at Matthew 9:20-22 — and *as many as touched were made perfectly whole.* The kingdom-power flowing through the King's Torah-prescribed-corner is the load-bearing substance the chapter's closing-scene displays.

The Tanakh's last prophet had named the architecture in the closing-verses of his book:

*But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings; and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.* (Malachi 4:2)

The Hebrew *kanaph* (wings) of *the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings* is the same Hebrew word that names the corner-of-the-garment-where-the-tsitsit-attaches at Bamidbar (Numbers) 15:38 — the same word, the same load-bearing covenant-substance. Malachi's-Messianic-substance: the Sun-of-righteousness would-arise with healing-in-his-kanaph — *healing-in-his-corners*, *healing-in-his-hems*. The chapter's closing-scene at Gennesaret IS the prophet's-Messianic-architecture enacted in the visible-substance. The kingdom-power flowing through the Torah-prescribed-corner of the King's-garment IS the Malachi-4-substance landed in the King's-credentialing across the Galilee-territory's-scattered-northern-house per Matt 4:13-16.

The framework reads this against the inherited-Christian portrayal of the King as Torah-detached or Torah-abolishing. The text says the opposite. The King who is the *Sun of righteousness* of Malachi's closing-verse walks in the Torah-prescribed garment-substance the prophet's-book of Bamidbar (Numbers) commanded; the healing flows through the Torah-prescribed-substance. The Torah-substance and the Messianic-substance are not in competition — they are one architecture, with the Tanakh-prescribed-fringe of the covenant-garment carrying the Tanakh-promised-healing-of-the-Sun-of-righteousness in the chapter's-closing-act. The inherited-Christian-collapse that pits the Torah-substance against the Messianic-substance is dismantled by the chapter's-own-closing-scene.
