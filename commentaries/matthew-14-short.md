# Short-form commentary on Matthew 14

Matthew 14 is the chapter where the rejection-trajectory the gospel has been tracing in the religious-system mirrors itself in the political-system, and the King's response — withdrawal followed by deepened self-disclosure — operates at a stature Tanakh-precedent locates in Yahuah (the LORD) himself. The chapter has three movements. The execution of Yochanan (John) the Witness at Herod's hand (vv.1-12) — the political-system's-rejection-disposition naked in the silencing of the prophet. The feeding of the five thousand (vv.14-21) — Yahusha (Jesus) realizing the prophet's-shepherd-feeding-the-scattered-sheep substance in Galilee-territory, against the immediate backdrop of the silenced voice of the previous-prophet. The walking on the water and the disciples' recognition-declaration (vv.22-33) — the King treading the waves as Yahuah (the LORD) himself does in the Tanakh-witness, with the disciples' first corporate confession landing as *Of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God).* The chapter that opens with the king-of-the-world silencing the prophet closes with the disciples in the boat recognizing the King who is the Son of the Father whose voice the prophet had borne.

*At that time Herod the tetrarch heard of the fame of Yahusha (Jesus), And said unto his servants, This is Yochanan (John) the Baptist; he is risen from the dead; and therefore mighty works do shew forth themselves in him.* (Matthew 14:1-2)

The Herod here is Herod-Antipas, the tetrarch — one of the four sons of Herod-the-Great among whom the Herodian kingdom had been divided. Antipas ruled the Galilee-and-Perea territory where Yahusha's (Jesus's) ministry was concentrated. The fame of the King has reached the palace; Antipas hears the report and immediately reaches for the explanation his guilty conscience supplies — *this is Yochanan (John) the Baptist; he is risen from the dead.* Hold what this identification reveals. Antipas had Yochanan (John) executed. He should know Yochanan (John) is dead. The superstitious-pagan-resurrection-attribution is a tell. The guilty conscience is supplying a counterfeit-resurrection because the real-Tanakh-resurrection the prophet had preached has not yet landed in the system's understanding, but the conscience knows that prophet-killed-righteously-by-the-Father does not stay killed. The first man who confesses a resurrection-of-the-prophet in the gospel is the man who had the prophet killed.

The narrative then flashes back to how the execution happened.

*For Herod had laid hold on Yochanan (John), and bound him, and put him in prison for Herodias' sake, his brother Philip's wife. For Yochanan (John) said unto him, It is not lawful for thee to have her.* (Matthew 14:3-4)

Herodias was the wife of Antipas's brother Philip. Antipas had divorced his first wife (the daughter of the Nabatean king Aretas) to marry Herodias, who had divorced Philip to marry Antipas. The arrangement violates the Torah at two points. The prohibition on taking a brother's wife is direct:

*Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy brother's wife: it is thy brother's nakedness.* (Leviticus 18:16)

*And if a man shall take his brother's wife, it is an unclean thing: he hath uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall be childless.* (Leviticus 20:21)

The exception for the levirate-marriage-to-raise-up-seed for a dead-and-childless brother (Deuteronomy 25:5-6) does not apply: Philip is alive, and Herodias has a daughter by him. Yochanan (John) had spoken the Torah-substance into Antipas's face. The court's-displeasure is not over the king's right to do what kings do; it is over the prophet refusing to soften the Torah for the king's-pleasure. The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) and Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) and Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) and Eliyahu (Elijah) pattern had named what prophets do — they speak the word of Yahuah (the LORD) into the courts of kings without revising it for political-convenience. Yochanan (John) walked the same line.

*And when he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.* (Matthew 14:5)

Antipas's restraint is not righteousness; it is political-calculation. The multitudes regard Yochanan (John) as a prophet. To execute a prophet publicly is to incur the popular-anger that the previous chapter has named already at Matthew 11:11 — *Among them that are born of women there hath not risen a greater than Yochanan (John) the Baptist.* The system that holds Antipas's throne is the same Roman-Herodian-collaborative structure that crowd-pressure can disturb. He waits.

*But when Herod's birthday was kept, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod. Whereupon he promised with an oath to give her whatsoever she would ask. And she, being before instructed of her mother, said, Give me here Yochanan (John) the Baptist's head in a charger. And the king was sorry: nevertheless for the oath's sake, and them which sat with him at meat, he commanded it to be given her. And he sent, and beheaded Yochanan (John) in the prison. And his head was brought in a charger, and given to the damsel: and she brought it to her mother.* (Matthew 14:6-11)

The scene mirrors a long Tanakh-pattern of women using court-influence to destroy the prophet's-voice. Izevel (Jezebel) hunted Eliyahu (Elijah). The shape repeats. The daughter of Herodias dances at the king's birthday; the king is pleased; the king makes the rash-and-public oath; the daughter is instructed-by-her-mother what to ask. The narrative names Herod's emotional-state with one word — *sorry.* The word does not name repentance. The word names the Pharisaic-style of public-credentialing-trap, where the actor has bound himself to a course by the witnesses-at-the-table. The king-of-the-world's-fear of looking weak before the dinner-guests outweighs the king-of-the-world's-fear of Yahuah (the LORD). The head is brought on a charger. The political-system has displayed in one scene what its rejection-disposition reaches when the prophet's-voice is silenced.

*And his disciples came, and took up the body, and buried it, and went and told Yahusha (Jesus).* (Matthew 14:12)

The disciples of Yochanan (John) — distinct from the disciples of Yahusha (Jesus) but in fellowship with them — bury the body and report the news. The Yochanan-mission-substance the Witness had named at John 3:30 (*He must increase, but I must decrease*) is now structurally enacted. The Witness's-voice goes silent; the King's-voice continues.

*When Yahusha (Jesus) heard of it, he departed thence by ship into a desert place apart: and when the people had heard thereof, they followed him on foot out of the cities.* (Matthew 14:13)

The withdrawal-pattern the King has used before — Matthew 4:12 after Yochanan's (John's) initial imprisonment, Matthew 12:15 after the Pharisaic conspiracy-to-destroy — operates again. The withdrawal is not retreat; it is the meek-King-disposition the prophet had named at Isaiah 42:1-4, the *he shall not strive, nor cry; neither shall any man hear his voice in the streets* posture the gospel quoted at Matthew 12:18-21. The multitudes follow him on foot. They have followed the prophet to the wilderness for the immersion; they follow the King to the desert place now. The substance the prophet pointed to is here. The wilderness-territory the prophet named at Yeshayahu's (Isaiah's) 40:3 — *the voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of Yahuah (the LORD)* — is the territory where the King now is. The voice has been silenced; the Way is here.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went forth, and saw a great multitude, and was moved with compassion toward them, and he healed their sick. And when it was evening, his disciples came to him, saying, This is a desert place, and the time is now past; send the multitude away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves victuals. But Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, They need not depart; give ye them to eat. And they say unto him, We have here but five loaves, and two fishes. He said, Bring them hither to me. And he commanded the multitude to sit down on the grass, and took the five loaves, and the two fishes, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, and brake, and gave the loaves to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude. And they did all eat, and were filled: and they took up of the fragments that remained twelve baskets full. And they that had eaten were about five thousand men, beside women and children.* (Matthew 14:14-21)

Hold what is happening. The multitude is in a desert place; evening is coming; they need to be fed. The disciples' calculation is the calculation of finitude — *we have here but five loaves, and two fishes.* The King's response inverts the calculation. *Bring them hither to me.* What the disciples have is enough when it is brought to the King; what the King has done with five loaves and two fishes is not measurable by the loaves-and-fishes-arithmetic that produced the disciples' calculation.

But hold the Tanakh-substance the King is enacting. The prophet had named the substance centuries before:

*For thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out. As a shepherd seeketh out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered; so will I seek out my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day. And I will bring them out from the people, and gather them from the countries, and will bring them to their own land, and feed them upon the mountains of Yashar'el (Israel) by the rivers, and in all the inhabited places of the country. I will feed them in a good pasture, and upon the high mountains of Yashar'el (Israel) shall their fold be: there shall they lie in good fold, and in a fat pasture shall they feed upon the mountains of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Ezekiel 34:11-14)

The shepherd-substance Ezekiel 34 names is Yahuah (the LORD) himself seeking out his scattered sheep, feeding them on the mountains, gathering them out of the territories where they have been scattered. The prophet had located the substance in the first person of Yahuah (the LORD): *I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out.* The King in the desert-place feeding the multitudes on the grass with the five loaves and two fishes is enacting in real time what the prophet had said Yahuah (the LORD) himself would do. The shepherd-substance of Psalm 23 is the same:

*Yahuah (the LORD) is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. ... Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies.* (Psalm 23:1-2, 5)

The Galilee-multitude makes-to-lie-down on the green grass; the King prepares the table in the desert-place; the multitudes are filled. The Isaiah 40:11 substance is enacted:

*He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.* (Isaiah 40:11)

The shepherd the prophet named is the King in the desert-place. The lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel) — the Galilee-territory the King's-mission had been concentrated on — are being fed by the shepherd-Yahuah (the LORD) the prophet promised would come.

And the Elisha-precedent the King is exceeding had been named in the Tanakh's own record:

*And there came a man from Baal-shalisha, and brought the man of Elohim (God) bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley, and full ears of corn in the husk thereof. And he said, Give unto the people, that they may eat. And his servitor said, What, should I set this before an hundred men? He said again, Give the people, that they may eat: for thus saith Yahuah (the LORD), They shall eat, and shall leave thereof. So he set it before them, and they did eat, and left thereof, according to the word of Yahuah (the LORD).* (2 Kings 4:42-44)

Elisha multiplied twenty loaves to feed a hundred men with leftover. The King multiplies five loaves to feed five thousand men, plus women and children, with twelve baskets remaining. The Tanakh-precedent is exceeded by a factor that names what is happening. The Elisha-substance is fulfilled at a stature the prophet's-loaves only foreshadowed. The greater-than-Elisha is here. The greater-than-Eliyahu (Elijah) the King had already named at the Yochanan (John) identification of Matthew 11:14 is here in the action the prophets had foreshadowed.

And the deeper Tanakh-precedent the feeding lands. The wilderness-manna of Exodus 16, where Yahuah (the LORD) himself fed the multitudes of Yashar'el (Israel) in the wilderness through Mosheh (Moses), is the substance the desert-place-feeding echoes:

*Then said Yahuah (the LORD) unto Mosheh (Moses), Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you; and the people shall go out and gather a certain rate every day, that I may prove them, whether they will walk in my law, or no.* (Exodus 16:4)

*And when the children of Yashar'el (Israel) saw it, they said one to another, It is manna: for they wist not what it was. And Mosheh (Moses) said unto them, This is the bread which Yahuah (the LORD) hath given you to eat.* (Exodus 16:15)

The wilderness-feeding had been Yahuah's (the LORD's) own provision through the mediation of Mosheh (Moses). The desert-place-feeding by the lake is Yahuah's (the LORD's) provision through the King in person — the substance that operated through the mediator at Sinai operates here without mediation, in the King's own hands looking up to heaven and blessing the loaves. The pattern of bread-from-heaven is being recapitulated and exceeded, with the One who is the bread-from-heaven feeding the lost sheep in the wilderness himself. The disciples will be sent through this same architecture in chapter 16:5-12 when the King will name them away from the leaven of the Pharisees and remind them of the feeding-substance they have just participated in — *do ye not yet understand, neither remember the five loaves of the five thousand* (Matthew 16:9). The substance is not a one-time spectacle; it is the substance of who the King is. The disciples are being trained to recognize it.

Hold one detail. The twelve baskets that remain. Twelve. The twelve disciples each gather a basket of fragments. The number resonates with the twelve tribes the scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel) has been split among. The King's-feeding produces the fragments the twelve gather — twelve baskets of remainder from the desert-place table, twelve disciples to be the foundation-stones of the renewed-Yashar'el (Israel) in the King's reign, twelve tribes whose gathering the prophets had named. The numerology is not strained imagery; it is the Tanakh-substance the King is anchoring with the meal.

*And straightway Yahusha (Jesus) constrained his disciples to get into a ship, and to go before him unto the other side, while he sent the multitudes away. And when he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into a mountain apart to pray: and when the evening was come, he was there alone.* (Matthew 14:22-23)

The King sends the disciples ahead by boat. He sends the multitudes away. He goes up the mountain to pray. The pattern that began with the withdrawal at v.13 deepens into solitude with the Father. The Tanakh-pattern of Mosheh (Moses) on the mountain, of Eliyahu (Elijah) on Horev (Horeb), of David in the wilderness alone with the Father — the King carries the same pattern. The communion with the Father is the source of the disposition that walks the next miracle.

*But the ship was now in the midst of the sea, tossed with waves: for the wind was contrary. And in the fourth watch of the night Yahusha (Jesus) went unto them, walking on the sea. And when the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit; and they cried out for fear.* (Matthew 14:24-26)

Hold what is happening on the lake. The boat is in the midst of the sea, in the fourth watch — between three and six in the morning — with the wind against them. The disciples are exhausted from rowing. The King appears. He is walking on the sea.

The Tanakh names what walking-on-the-sea is. Iyov (Job) names it in the description of Yahuah (the LORD) himself:

*Which alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the waves of the sea.* (Job 9:8)

The psalmist names it as the way of Yahuah (the LORD) in the great waters:

*Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are not known. Thou leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Mosheh (Moses) and Aharon (Aaron).* (Psalm 77:19-20)

The prophet Habakkuk names it in the war-poem of the Father's-coming:

*Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters.* (Habakkuk 3:15)

The walking-on-the-sea is what Yahuah (the LORD) does in the Tanakh-witness. The King doing it on the Sea of Galilee in the fourth watch is doing what Yahuah (the LORD) does. The disciples seeing him assume it must be a spirit, because the only category for a figure-walking-on-the-water their lake-experience knows is something-other-than-flesh. The disciples are seeing the truth they cannot yet name: this is the One the prophet described treading the waves.

*But straightway Yahusha (Jesus) spake unto them, saying, Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.* (Matthew 14:27)

The Greek behind *it is I* is *egō eimi* — the same construction the Tanakh's-Greek-rendering of Exodus 3:14 uses for the divine self-naming at the burning bush. The Tanakh-source is the Mosheh (Moses)-at-the-bush passage:

*And Mosheh (Moses) said unto Elohim (God), Behold, when I come unto the children of Yashar'el (Israel), and shall say unto them, The Elohim (God) of your fathers hath sent me unto you; and they shall say to me, What is his name? what shall I say unto them? And Elohim (God) said unto Mosheh (Moses), Ehyeh asher Ehyeh (I AM THAT I AM): and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Yashar'el (Israel), Ehyeh (I AM) hath sent me unto you.* (Exodus 3:13-14)

The Greek of the Tanakh rendered the Hebrew *Ehyeh* as *egō eimi* — *I am.* The King speaks the same *egō eimi* across the water to the terrified disciples. The naming is doing what the walking is doing — naming the speaker as the One the Tanakh names with the Name above every name, the One who told Mosheh (Moses) at the bush that he was who he was, the One who has been in the third-person witness of the prophets *I, even I, will seek out my sheep* now speaking in the first person *it is I* to the sheep he is seeking. The disciples do not unpack it in the moment; they hear the voice and the fear breaks. The unpacking comes a few verses later when they fall down and worship him as the Son of Elohim (God), and unpacks further across the gospel as the *egō eimi* sayings accumulate — *I am the bread of life* (John 6:35), *I am the light of the world* (John 8:12), *I am the door of the sheep* (John 10:7), *I am the good shepherd* (John 10:11), *I am the resurrection, and the life* (John 11:25), *I am the way, the truth, and the life* (John 14:6), *I am the true vine* (John 15:1), and the load-bearing *before Avraham (Abraham) was, I am* (John 8:58). The voice across the water at the fourth watch is the same Voice. The disciples are hearing the burning-bush Voice in the dark water.

*And Kefa (Peter) answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come. And when Kefa (Peter) was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Yahusha (Jesus). But when he saw the wind boisterous, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me. And immediately Yahusha (Jesus) stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?* (Matthew 14:28-31)

Kefa (Peter) asks for participation in what the King is doing. *If it be thou, bid me come.* The King says *come.* Kefa (Peter) comes down out of the ship and walks on the water — walks, in the substance the King is walking, by the King's-word. The diagnostic of what changes operates clearly in the narrative: while his eye is on the King, he walks; when his eye shifts to the wind boisterous, he sinks. The disposition of looking-at-the-King is the disposition that walks on the water. The disposition of looking-at-the-circumstance-around-the-King is the disposition that sinks. The cry *Lord, save me* is the brief and adequate prayer of the disciple-who-is-about-to-drown. The King immediately stretches forth his hand and catches him. The *O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?* is the King's-naming of what shifted. Kefa (Peter) had the faith to come out of the boat; the wind-boisterous moved the disposition; the doubt entered; the walking ceased. The King has named the disciple's-disposition and the catching simultaneously.

The kingdom-citizens are being trained by what just happened. The King does not rebuke Kefa (Peter) for getting out of the boat. He rebukes him for shifting his gaze from the King to the wind. The walking-on-the-water is not a stunt the disciples are being shown; it is the disposition the kingdom-citizens are being trained in. The eye-on-the-King is the substance that walks above the storm; the eye-on-the-storm is the substance that sinks into the storm. The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) will name the same pattern decades later — *we walk by faith, not by sight* (2 Corinthians 5:7) — and the Hebrews-witness will lock it — *looking unto Yahusha (Jesus) the author and finisher of our faith* (Hebrews 12:2). The kingdom-citizens' walk is sustained by the disposition Kefa (Peter) has just modelled in its breaking and its restoration. The catching-of-the-disciple is what the King does when the disposition fails; the disposition is what the disciple is responsible for sustaining. Both substances are in the scene together.

*And when they were come into the ship, the wind ceased. Then they that were in the ship came and worshipped him, saying, Of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God).* (Matthew 14:32-33)

The King enters the ship; the wind ceases. The disciples respond with the first explicit corporate-recognition the gospel has recorded. *Of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God).* The substance the disciples have been receiving across the gospel — the immersion-voice naming the Son at Matthew 3:17, the casting-out of demons that recognized him as Son of Elohim (God) before any human voice did at Matthew 8:29, the parables and the healings and the feeding-of-the-five-thousand of the chapter — now lands in the disciples' own confession. They worship. The worship is the Tanakh-disposition reserved for Yahuah (the LORD) alone, and the disciples worship the King in the boat. The architecture the gospel has been training — the Son who does what the Father does, the Formed who is the One who walked-on-the-waves the Tanakh-prophets foretold, the King who is what Yahuah (the LORD) said he himself would be among his people — is locking into the disciples' confession.

*And when they were gone over, they came into the land of Gennesaret. And when the men of that place had knowledge of him, they sent out into all that country round about, and brought unto him all that were diseased; And besought him that they might only touch the hem of his garment: and as many as touched were made perfectly whole.* (Matthew 14:34-36)

The boat lands at Gennesaret on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee. The men of the place recognize the King and send word into the surrounding country. The diseased are brought; they beseech him that they might only touch the hem of his garment; and *as many as touched were made perfectly whole.* The hem-of-the-garment touch echoes the woman with the issue of blood at Matthew 9:20-22 — she touched the hem and was healed by the disposition-toward-the-King her touching expressed. The pattern that operated in one woman now operates in the multitudes at Gennesaret. The kingdom-power flows through the touch the disposition-toward-the-King reaches for. The Tanakh-precedent of the priestly-garment-hem the prophet Chaggai (Haggai) — book name Haggai — named is in view:

*If one bear holy flesh in the skirt of his garment, and with his skirt do touch bread, or pottage, or wine, or oil, or any meat, shall it be holy? And the priests answered and said, No.* (Haggai 2:12)

Haggai 2 names the principle that holiness does not transfer through the garment-hem to the unclean. The King-of-Gennesaret reverses what the prophet had named as the natural-direction of contagion. Touching his garment-hem makes the diseased whole. The contagion-of-holiness the prophet had said does not normally operate through the garment now operates because the One whose garment it is is not within the priestly-Haggai-2 architecture but above it. The Lord of the Sabbath is the Lord of the garment-hem. The kingdom-power flows in the direction the priestly-system cannot move it. The contagion-of-restoration is from him outward to those who reach for it.

The chapter has now stationed three witnesses to who the King is. The shepherd-of-Ezekiel-34 feeding the scattered-flock in the desert-place. The treading-the-waves substance of Yahuah (the LORD) at Job 9 and Psalm 77 and Habakkuk 3 enacted by the King crossing the lake in the fourth watch. The contagion-of-restoration flowing through the garment-hem to whoever reaches for it at Gennesaret. The disciples' confession in the boat — *Of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God)* — is the substance the chapter has been demonstrating. Matthew 15 will open with the credentialing-system arriving from Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) to challenge the disciples for not washing-hands-before-bread — the system that silenced the prophet at the political-level now reaching at the religious-level to silence what the King has been demonstrating in the Galilee. The seed-war the parables-chapter named continues to operate at every register the gospel reaches.
