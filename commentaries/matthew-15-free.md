# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 15

Matthew 15 is the chapter where the credentialing-system silenced at the political-level in the killing of Yochanan (John) returns at the religious-level to challenge the King directly, and where the King speaks the most direct mission-scope statement of his earthly ministry. Four movements anchor the chapter. (1) The Pharisaic-scribal delegation from Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) challenges the disciples' non-observance of the rabbinic hand-washing tradition (vv.1-9). The King counter-charges: the *tradition of the elders* has not merely added to the Torah but has voided it at specific points. The Korban-evasion is the diagnostic instance — a man could declare assets *Corban* (dedicated to Elohim (God)) and thereby block their use for his parents' support while retaining personal use, structurally voiding the fifth commandment (Exodus 20:12, 21:15-17; Mark 7:10-13 carries the explicit *Corban* word). The King cites Isaiah 29:13 — *This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth ... but their heart is far from me ... teaching for doctrines the commandments of men* — the same diagnostic the prophet had named seven centuries earlier. (2) The heart-defilement-versus-mouth-defilement teaching (vv.10-20). *Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.* Hold what this verse does and does NOT say: the verse dismantles the rabbinic hand-washing-defilement tradition the chapter opened on; it does NOT abolish Leviticus 11's clean-and-unclean food architecture (the text never mentions dietary commandments). *Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up* (v.13) names the Pharisaic-system as the planting the harvest of Matthew 13:39-42 will root up; *let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind* names what the system is. The catalogue of heart-defilement (evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies) tracks the second-table of the Decalogue extended to the heart's-substance — the inversion the rabbinic-fence had performed (un-washed-hands defile / heart-substance does not) is dismantled and the Torah's-architecture restored. (3) The withdrawal into Tsor (Tyre) and Tsidon (Sidon) and the Canaanite-woman's daughter (vv.21-28) — the framework-major mission-scope-lock. The Canaanite-woman (ethnically Canaanite, not of Yashar'el (Israel)) addresses the King as *Son of David.* The King's response is ignored-then-explicit: *I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* (v.24). The Greek *ouk apestalēn ei mē* is emphatically negative — there is no other audience. This is the most direct mission-scope statement in the gospel, confirming the twelve's commission at Matthew 10:5-6. The Christian-commentary tradition has misread the scene in two directions: (a) treating v.24 as pre-cross-mission-statement abolished by Matthew 28:19's Great Commission (the text never overrides v.24 — *all nations* extends geography because the scattered sheep are everywhere; *his sheep hear his voice* per John 10:27 is the lock); (b) treating the Canaanite-woman's faith-confession as a paradigm for nations-becoming-Yashar'el (Israel) by belief. The text gives the opposite. The King's *it is not meet to take the children's bread, and to cast it to dogs* (v.26) places the woman in the dog-category by his own naming; her response — *Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters' table* — does NOT contest the architecture. She submits to the seed-priority structure and asks only for the mercy the architecture itself permits at the crumb-level. *Great is thy faith* (v.28) names the disposition that submitted, NOT a confession that overrode the scope-lock. The Tanakh-precedent is in 1 Kings 17:1-9 — Eliyahu (Elijah) sent to a widow of Tsarpat (Zarephath) in Tsidon (Sidon) territory during famine, the prophet himself cites this at Luke 4:25-26: *Unto none of them was Eliyahu (Elijah) sent, save unto Tsarpat (Zarephath), a city of Tsidon (Sidon), unto a woman that was a widow.* Single grace-extension to a non-Yashar'el (Israel) person within the seed-priority architecture is the Tanakh's own pattern; the King is enacting what the prophet had laid down. The architecture is preserved by the exception, NOT dissolved. (4) Healings on the mountain by the Sea of Galilee (vv.29-31) — the multitudes glorify *the Elohim (God) of Yashar'el (Israel),* the covenant-name-substance recognized inside the covenant-architecture. The feeding of the four thousand (vv.32-39) is a distinct event from the feeding of the five thousand at Matthew 14, in different territory (the Decapolis area east of the lake per Mark 7:31), with seven loaves and seven baskets remaining — the Tanakh's number of covenant-completion (the seven days of creation, the seven feasts, the seven sevens of the Yovel-cycle), parallel to the twelve-baskets-of-the-five-thousand-feeding (the twelve-tribe gathering substance). The King will name the two feedings together at Matthew 16:9-10 as the pedagogical-substance for disciples who needed to see twice. Matthew 16 will open with the Pharisees and Sadducees coming together to demand a sign from heaven, followed by Kefa's (Peter's) confession at Caesarea Philippi — *Thou art the Messiah (Christ), the Son of the living Elohim (God)* — and the King's first explicit announcement of his death and resurrection.
