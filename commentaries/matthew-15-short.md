# Short-form commentary on Matthew 15

The chapter walks four movements — the Pharisaic-Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)-delegation's-challenge over the disciples' non-observance of the hand-washing-tradition and the King's-counter-charge that the Pharisaic-Corban-evasion of the fifth-commandment voids-the-Torah (vv.1-9 — anchored at the *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition* declaration and the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 29:13 *teaching for doctrines the commandments of men* citation); the heart-defilement-versus-mouth-defilement teaching that dismantles the Pharisaic-fence-architecture's-inversion without abolishing the Torah's-clean-and-unclean substance (vv.10-20); the Canaanite-woman pericope at Tsor (Tyre) and Tsidon (Sidon) territory with the King's-most-direct-mission-scope statement — *I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* — and the woman's-faith-honored within the architecture rather than overruling it (vv.21-28); the Galilee-mountain-healings (vv.29-31) and the feeding-of-the-four-thousand with seven-loaves-and-seven-baskets-remaining (vv.32-39) — and one framework move the chapter's-closing-scene carries — and that the free.md walks only at the level of the second-feeding-being-distinct-from-the-first — locks the chapter at its deepest Tanakh-Messianic-architecture register: the seven-baskets-of-the-second-feeding read as the Yovel-cycle-completion-substance, paired with the twelve-baskets-of-the-first-feeding's twelve-tribes-gathering-signaling, the two feedings together encoding the framework's eschatological-arc the prophets had named.

**The seven-baskets-of-the-feeding-of-the-four-thousand at vv.32-39 paired with the twelve-baskets-of-the-feeding-of-the-five-thousand at Matt 14:20 read as the King's-pedagogical-double-encoding of the framework's eschatological-arc — twelve-tribes-gathered out of all countries (Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 37:21) into Yovel-restoration-completion (Vayikra (Leviticus) 25:8-10; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 61:1-2's *acceptable year of Yahuah (the LORD)*).** The scene's narrative:

*Then Yahusha (Jesus) called his disciples unto him, and said, I have compassion on the multitude, because they continue with me now three days, and have nothing to eat: and I will not send them away fasting, lest they faint in the way. ... And they did eat, and were filled: and they took up of the broken meat that was left seven baskets full. And they that did eat were four thousand men, beside women and children.* (Matthew 15:32, 37-38)

The King has just fed five thousand at Matt 14:13-21 with five-loaves-and-two-fishes and twelve-baskets-remaining. He now feeds four thousand with seven-loaves-and-a-few-fishes and seven-baskets-remaining. The disciples' question at v.33 — *whence should we have so much bread in the wilderness, as to fill so great a multitude?* — comes from disciples who saw the first-feeding firsthand, and yet they ask. The King names the disposition explicitly at Matt 16:9-10: *do ye not yet understand, neither remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets ye took up? Neither the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many baskets ye took up?* The doubling is pedagogical. The two number-sets are NOT incidental. The Tanakh-substance the numerologies carry is the framework's eschatological-arc the prophets had named.

The twelve-baskets of the first feeding signal the twelve-tribes-gathering. The Tanakh-anchor:

*And say unto them, Thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); Behold, I will take the children of Yashar'el (Israel) from among the heathen, whither they be gone, and will gather them on every side, and bring them into their own land.* (Ezekiel 37:21)

*Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that they shall no more say, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, which brought up the children of Yashar'el (Israel) out of the land of Egypt; But, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, which brought up and which led the seed of the house of Yashar'el (Israel) out of the north country, and from all countries whither I had driven them; and they shall dwell in their own land.* (Jeremiah 23:7-8)

The twelve-baskets-remaining-after-the-five-thousand-have-eaten-and-been-filled signal the twelve-tribes-of-the-gathered-scattered-seed restored, with the abundance-of-the-King's-bread overflowing the twelve-tribe-vessel of the covenant-people. The framework's scattered-seed-gathering substance the prophets had named operates in the first-feeding's numerology.

The seven-baskets of the second feeding signal the Yovel-completion. The Tanakh-anchor:

*And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years unto thee, seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee forty and nine years. ... And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.* (Leviticus 25:8, 10)

*The Spirit of Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) is upon me; because Yahuah (the LORD) hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound; To proclaim the acceptable year of Yahuah (the LORD), and the day of vengeance of our Elohim (God).* (Isaiah 61:1-2)

The seven-architecture is the Tanakh's covenant-completion and Sabbath-rest substance: the seven days of creation with the Shabbat at the climax, the seven feasts of the covenant year, the seven-sevens-of-the-Yovel-cycle culminating in the fiftieth-year's-Yovel-proclamation-of-liberty-throughout-all-the-land. The Yovel is the architecture of every-man-returning-to-his-possession and to-his-family — the restoration of the scattered inheritance to the covenant-people. The King at the Natsareth (Nazareth) synagogue had read Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 61:1-2 and named-himself as the One the prophet had-named (Luke 4:18-21 — *this day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears*). The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 61 *acceptable year of Yahuah (the LORD)* is the Yovel-substance the King had named-himself-as-the-One-proclaiming. The seven-baskets-remaining-after-the-four-thousand-have-eaten-and-been-filled signal the Yovel-completion-substance — the gathering-restored, the inheritance-returned, the liberty-proclaimed-throughout-all-the-land.

The two feedings together encode the architecture's-completion-arc. The twelve-baskets-of-the-first-feeding name the gathering of the scattered-twelve-tribes the prophets had named (Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 37:21; Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:8-10; Hosea 1:10-11; Zekharyah (Zechariah) 10:6-10); the seven-baskets-of-the-second-feeding name the Yovel-completion-the-gathering-lands-in (Vayikra (Leviticus) 25:8-10; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 61:1-2). The King is enacting the visible-substance of both moves in the two feedings — first the gathering, then the completion. The pedagogical-doubling is not incidental-repetition; it is the framework's eschatological-arc displayed in the visible-substance of the bread-multiplication-and-the-baskets-remaining.

The framework reads this against the inherited-Christian portrayal of the second-feeding as a categorical-Gentile-feeding (the Decapolis-territory-as-Gentile-territory). The Decapolis included substantial Yahudi (Jewish) and scattered-tribal population alongside the Hellenistic-administrative-overlay — the Trans-Jordan tribes (Gad, Reuben, half-Manasheh (Manasseh)) had territory in the region per Bamidbar (Numbers) 32:33-42. The four-thousand-fed are the scattered-seed of the Trans-Jordan-tribes the King has-found-in-the-territory; the seven-baskets-remaining signal the Yovel-completion-substance the gathering-lands-in. The inherited-Christian-categorical-Gentile-feeding reading erases the Tanakh-Yovel-substance and substitutes the false-inclusion-gospel's-architecture; the framework restores the substance the King's-own-double-feeding-and-Matt 16:9-10-explicit-naming-of-the-two-numerologies displays.
