## §1. The Pharisaic-traditions controversy — *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition*

*Then came to Yahusha (Jesus) scribes and Pharisees, which were of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), saying, Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread. But he answered and said unto them, Why do ye also transgress the commandment of Elohim (God) by your tradition? For Elohim (God) commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death. But ye say, Whosoever shall say to his father or his mother, It is a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; And honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free. Thus have ye made the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition. Ye hypocrites, well did Esaias (Isaiah) prophesy of you, saying, This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me. But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men* (vv.1-9).

**The framework reads vv.1-9 as the chapter's first framework-major moment — the direct Pharisaic-traditions-controversy at the *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition* declaration.**

**The scribes-and-Pharisees-from-Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)** (v.1). The framework reads the from-Yerushalayim designation in framework-substance. The Pharisaic-system's-headquarters has dispatched-a-delegation to investigate-and-confront the King in the Galilee-territory. The trajectory of opposition has-deepened beyond the local-Galilee-Pharisees of Matt 9:11, Matt 12:2 to the institutional-system's central-authority now actively-traveling-to-confront. The framework reads this in continuity with the seed-war-pattern's intensification from Matt 12:14's *they ... held a council against him, how they might destroy him*.

**The hand-washing-tradition-charge** (v.2). *Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.*

The framework reads the hand-washing-tradition carefully:

- The Torah-substance does NOT command-routine-hand-washing-before-meals. The Torah commands priests to wash-hands-and-feet before-temple-service (Shemot (Exodus) 30:18-21); the Torah commands ritual-purification after specific-impurities (Vayikra (Leviticus) 15 and elsewhere); the Torah does NOT command laypersons to wash-their-hands before every meal.
- The hand-washing-tradition is *paradosis tōn presbyterōn* — the tradition-of-the-elders. The framework reads this in the rabbinic-oral-tradition-architecture (eventually-codified in the Mishnah's-tractate-Yadayim and the Talmudic-development that-followed). The hand-washing-before-meals is a tradition-developed by the Pharisaic-school as ritual-purification-extending the priests'-temple-service-substance to-all-Yashar'el (Israel) at-the-meal-table.
- The Pharisaic-tradition is NOT condemned-by-the-King-as-categorically-evil; the Pharisaic-tradition is condemned-by-the-King-when-it-elevates-itself-above-Torah-and-displaces-Torah-substance. The framework reads this distinction carefully.

**The King's counter-charge** (v.3). *Why do ye also transgress the commandment of Elohim (God) by your tradition?*

The framework reads the King's-rhetorical-move in framework-substance. The Pharisaic-charge accused-the-disciples of transgressing-the-tradition; the King's-counter accuses-the-Pharisaic-system of transgressing-the-COMMANDMENT (the Torah itself). The King is not relativizing-the-categories; the King is elevating-the-Torah-above-the-tradition and exposing-the-Pharisaic-system's-inversion.

**The Corban-tradition example** (vv.4-6). The King's-example: the Torah-commandment *honour thy father and mother* (Shemot (Exodus) 20:12, the fifth-commandment) and *he that curseth father or mother, let him die the death* (Shemot (Exodus) 21:17). The Pharisaic-tradition: *whosoever shall say to his father or his mother, It is a gift (qorban — *korban*), by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; And honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free.*

The framework reads the Corban-tradition in framework-substance:

- The Torah's-fifth-commandment substance is parental-honor at the holistic-level including-financial-support-in-old-age. The Tanakh-pattern is consistent (Bereshit (Genesis) 47:12 — Yoseph (Joseph) supporting-Ya'aqov (Jacob); 1 Shemu'el (Samuel) 22:3-4 — David arranging-for-his-parents' protection; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58:7's *not to hide thyself from thine own flesh*).

The wealth often-remained-in-the-son's-use during-his-lifetime (the vow operated-as-a-legal-fiction blocking-the-parental-claim while-not-actually-transferring-the-asset-to-the-temple). The Pharisaic-tradition's-effect was: the son retained-his-wealth, the parents were-not-supported, and the son was-counted-righteous by-the-Pharisaic-credentialing-system for-his-*korban*-vow.
- The King's-charge: the Pharisaic-tradition *has made the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect.* The Pharisaic-tradition has overridden the Torah's-clear-substance through-a-rabbinic-legal-mechanism. The framework reads this as the diagnostic-substance for the Pharisaic-system as the modern Judaizer-predecessor.

**The framework's reading of the Mark 7 parallel.** The Markan parallel (Mark 7:1-23) adds-detail beyond Matthew's-account, including the explicit-Aramaic *Corban* (*qorban*) word at Mark 7:11. The framework reads both accounts in coherence.

But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.*

The framework reads the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 29:13 citation in framework-substance:

The Pharisaic-tradition is *commandments of men*; the Torah is the *commandment of Elohim (God).* The two-categories are not flattened; the King names the Pharisaic-tradition as commandments-of-men taught-as-doctrines.

## §2. The heart-defilement teaching — to-the-multitude and to-the-disciples

*And he called the multitude, and said unto them, Hear, and understand: Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man. Then came his disciples, and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Pharisees were offended, after they heard this saying? But he answered and said, Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up. Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch. Then answered Kefa (Peter) and said unto him, Declare unto us this parable. And Yahusha (Jesus) said, Are ye also yet without understanding? Do not ye yet understand, that whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught? But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart; and they defile the man. For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies: These are the things which defile a man: but to eat with unwashen hands defileth not a man* (vv.10-20).

**The framework reads vv.10-20 carefully in framework-register. This passage has been weaponized by the inherited-Christian-pulpit to teach the abolition-of-the-Torah's-dietary-laws. The framework reads against that weaponization.**

**The King's-summary to-the-multitude** (vv.10-11). *Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.* The framework reads the substance carefully:

- **The context is the hand-washing-tradition.** The King is responding-to-the-Pharisaic-charge of disciples-eating-bread-with-unwashed-hands. The substance-being-addressed is the Pharisaic-tradition-of-ritual-purification-before-meals, NOT the Torah's-dietary-commandments.
- **What goes-into-the-mouth in this context is BREAD-EATEN-WITH-UNWASHED-HANDS.** The eating-of-clean-bread-with-unwashed-hands cannot defile-the-eater because the bread itself is clean and the hand-washing-tradition is rabbinic-not-Torah.
- **The framework reads the *defileth* (*koinoi*) language in framework-substance.** The Greek *koinoō* (to make-common, to make-unclean) operates in the rabbinic-purity-architecture. The King is addressing the rabbinic-purity-categories the Pharisaic-tradition applies, not the Torah's-categorical-clean-and-unclean.

**The framework rules out the inherited-Christian-reading that this passage abolishes the Torah's-dietary-commandments.**

- **The Torah's-dietary-substance is established at Vayikra (Leviticus) 11 and Devarim (Deuteronomy) 14.** The clean-and-unclean categories operate at the species-level. Pig-flesh is unclean by Torah-definition; shellfish are unclean; etc. The Torah's-categories pre-date and ground the Pharisaic-tradition; the King is not addressing the Torah-categories at Matt 15.
- **The King's-disciples continued keeping-the-Torah-dietary-commandments after the resurrection.** Acts 10's-Kefa (Peter)-vision must be read carefully: Kefa (Peter)'s response in Acts 10:14 (*Not so, Lord; for I have never eaten any thing that is common or unclean*) demonstrates the King's-disciples post-resurrection had-NOT-eaten-unclean-food at-any-point in their following-of-the-King. If Matt 15:11 had abolished-the-dietary-commandments, Kefa (Peter)'s-response in Acts 10:14 makes no sense. The framework reads Acts 10's-vision in framework-substance: the vision is about Gentile-inclusion-into-the-covenant (Acts 10:28's *Elohim (God) hath shewed me that I should not call any man common or unclean*) and the same applies-to-the-scattered-seed-coming-home; the vision is NOT-about-the-abolition of the dietary-categories. (The framework reads Acts 10 in continuity with `lost-sheep-mission-scope` and Red Line #7's seed-distinction-substance.)
- **Mark 7:19 contains the editorial-comment *purging all meats*** (*katharizōn panta ta brōmata*) that the inherited-Christian-translations have used as evidence-for-the-abolition-reading. The framework reads the Markan-parenthetical carefully. The Greek-grammar in the most-faithful manuscripts is debated; the participle *katharizōn* in some-readings refers to the digestive-process (the body purging-the-food through-elimination) rather than to-the-King's-pronouncement-abolishing-the-dietary-categories. The framework holds the Mark 7:19 reading in the digestive-process reading, consistent with Matt 15:17's *whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught* — the King's-own-explanation surfaces the same digestive-elimination-substance.

**The framework's reading of the substance** (vv.16-20). The King's-explanation to-the-disciples: *whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught.* The substance is that food-eaten-passes-through-the-body and is-eliminated; the food-eaten does not corrupt-the-heart-substance. The defilement-substance operates at the heart-and-mouth (intentions-and-words-and-deeds) level, not at the eaten-food's-ritual-purity-categorization level.

The framework reads *out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies* (v.19) in continuity with the Tanakh's-heart-substance-architecture:

- Bereshit (Genesis) 6:5 — *Yahuah (the LORD) saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.*
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 17:9 — *the heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?*
- The new-heart-of-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 is the framework's-answer to the heart's-substance.

The framework reads the King's-list (evil-thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false-witness, blasphemies) as the second-table-of-the-Decalogue's-violations specified at the heart-and-mouth level. The King is restoring the Torah's-substance to the heart-level — the heart-Torah of Jeremiah 31:33 / Ezekiel 36:26-27 — NOT abolishing the Torah's-categories.

**The framework reads the every-plant-not-planted-by-the-Father saying** (v.13). *Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.* The framework reads this in continuity with the wheat-and-tares parable of Matt 13:24-30 + interpretation at vv.36-43. The seed-war-pattern at Matt 13's-architecture extends to-the-system-level: the Pharisaic-system's-traditions are tares in the field of the religious-substance.

**The framework reads the blind-leaders-of-the-blind saying** (v.14). *Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch.* The framework reads this in continuity with the cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnostic per Red Line #11. The framework reads the *let them alone* (*aphete autous*) in framework-substance. The King's-instruction to-the-disciples is not the abandonment-of-the-Pharisaic-system-as-individuals-to-perdition; the instruction is the kingdom-citizens' present-age-disposition of not-engaging-the-system-on-its-own-terms. ## §3. The Canaanite-woman pericope — the test-case for the Lost-Sheep-Mission-Scope crux
