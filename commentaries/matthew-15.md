## §1. The Pharisaic-traditions controversy — *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition*

*Then came to Yahusha (Jesus) scribes and Pharisees, which were of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), saying, Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread. For Elohim (God) commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death. But ye say, Whosoever shall say to his father or his mother, It is a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; And honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free. Thus have ye made the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition. Ye hypocrites, well did Esaias (Isaiah) prophesy of you, saying, This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me. But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men* (vv.1-9).

**The framework reads vv.1-9 as the chapter's first framework-major moment — the direct Pharisaic-traditions-controversy at the *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition* declaration.**

**The scribes-and-Pharisees-from-Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)** (v.1). The framework reads the from-Yerushalayim designation in framework-substance. **The hand-washing-tradition-charge** (v.2). *Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.*

The framework reads the hand-washing-tradition carefully:

- The Torah-substance does NOT command-routine-hand-washing-before-meals. The Torah commands priests to wash-hands-and-feet before-temple-service (Shemot (Exodus) 30:18-21); the Torah commands ritual-purification after specific-impurities (Vayikra (Leviticus) 15 and elsewhere); the Torah does NOT command laypersons to wash-their-hands before every meal.
- The hand-washing-tradition is *paradosis tōn presbyterōn* — the tradition-of-the-elders. The framework reads this in the rabbinic-oral-tradition-architecture (eventually-codified in the Mishnah's-tractate-Yadayim and the Talmudic-development that-followed). The hand-washing-before-meals is a tradition-developed by the Pharisaic-school as ritual-purification-extending the priests'-temple-service-substance to-all-Yashar'el (Israel) at-the-meal-table.
- The Pharisaic-tradition is NOT condemned-by-the-King-as-categorically-evil; the Pharisaic-tradition is condemned-by-the-King-when-it-elevates-itself-above-Torah-and-displaces-Torah-substance. The framework reads this distinction carefully.

The Pharisaic-charge accused-the-disciples of transgressing-the-tradition; the King's-counter accuses-the-Pharisaic-system of transgressing-the-COMMANDMENT (the Torah itself). The King is not relativizing-the-categories; the King is elevating-the-Torah-above-the-tradition and exposing-the-Pharisaic-system's-inversion.

**The Corban-tradition example** (vv.4-6). The framework reads the Corban-tradition in framework-substance:

- The Torah's-fifth-commandment substance is parental-honor at the holistic-level including-financial-support-in-old-age. The Tanakh-pattern is consistent (Bereshit (Genesis) 47:12 — Yoseph (Joseph) supporting-Ya'aqov (Jacob); 1 Shemu'el (Samuel) 22:3-4 — David arranging-for-his-parents' protection; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58:7's *not to hide thyself from thine own flesh*).

## §2. The heart-defilement teaching — to-the-multitude and to-the-disciples

*And he called the multitude, and said unto them, Hear, and understand: Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man. Then came his disciples, and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Pharisees were offended, after they heard this saying? And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch. Then answered Kefa (Peter) and said unto him, Declare unto us this parable. And Yahusha (Jesus) said, Are ye also yet without understanding? Do not ye yet understand, that whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught? But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart; and they defile the man. For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies: These are the things which defile a man: but to eat with unwashen hands defileth not a man* (vv.10-20).

**The framework reads vv.10-20 carefully in framework-register. This passage has been weaponized by the inherited-Christian-pulpit to teach the abolition-of-the-Torah's-dietary-laws. The framework reads against that weaponization.**

**The King's-summary to-the-multitude** (vv.10-11). *Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.* The framework reads the substance carefully:

- **The context is the hand-washing-tradition.** The King is responding-to-the-Pharisaic-charge of disciples-eating-bread-with-unwashed-hands. The substance-being-addressed is the Pharisaic-tradition-of-ritual-purification-before-meals, NOT the Torah's-dietary-commandments.
- **What goes-into-the-mouth in this context is BREAD-EATEN-WITH-UNWASHED-HANDS.** The eating-of-clean-bread-with-unwashed-hands cannot defile-the-eater because the bread itself is clean and the hand-washing-tradition is rabbinic-not-Torah.
- **The framework reads the *defileth* (*koinoi*) language in framework-substance.** The Greek *koinoō* (to make-common, to make-unclean) operates in the rabbinic-purity-architecture. The King is addressing the rabbinic-purity-categories the Pharisaic-tradition applies, not the Torah's-categorical-clean-and-unclean.

**The framework rules out the inherited-Christian-reading that this passage abolishes the Torah's-dietary-commandments.**

- **The Torah's-dietary-substance is established at Vayikra (Leviticus) 11 and Devarim (Deuteronomy) 14.** The clean-and-unclean categories operate at the species-level. Pig-flesh is unclean by Torah-definition; shellfish are unclean; etc. The Torah's-categories pre-date and ground the Pharisaic-tradition; the King is not addressing the Torah-categories at Matt 15.
- **The King's-disciples continued keeping-the-Torah-dietary-commandments after the resurrection.** Acts 10's-Kefa (Peter)-vision must be read carefully: Kefa (Peter)'s response in Acts 10:14 (*Not so, Lord; for I have never eaten any thing that is common or unclean*) demonstrates the King's-disciples post-resurrection had-NOT-eaten-unclean-food at-any-point in their following-of-the-King. If Matt 15:11 had abolished-the-dietary-commandments, Kefa (Peter)'s-response in Acts 10:14 makes no sense. The framework reads Acts 10's-vision in framework-substance: the vision is about Gentile-inclusion-into-the-covenant (Acts 10:28's *Elohim (God) hath shewed me that I should not call any man common or unclean*) and the same applies-to-the-scattered-seed-coming-home; the vision is NOT-about-the-abolition of the dietary-categories. (The framework reads Acts 10 in continuity with `lost-sheep-mission-scope` and Red Line #7's seed-distinction-substance.)
- **Mark 7:19 contains the editorial-comment *purging all meats*** (*katharizōn panta ta brōmata*) that the inherited-Christian-translations have used as evidence-for-the-abolition-reading. The framework reads the Markan-parenthetical carefully. The Greek-grammar in the most-faithful manuscripts is debated; the participle *katharizōn* in some-readings refers to the digestive-process (the body purging-the-food through-elimination) rather than to-the-King's-pronouncement-abolishing-the-dietary-categories. The framework holds the Mark 7:19 reading in the digestive-process reading, consistent with Matt 15:17's *whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught* — the King's-own-explanation surfaces the same digestive-elimination-substance.

**The framework's reading of the substance** (vv.16-20). The King's-explanation to-the-disciples: *whatsoever entereth in at the mouth goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught.* The substance is that food-eaten-passes-through-the-body and is-eliminated; the food-eaten does not corrupt-the-heart-substance. The defilement-substance operates at the heart-and-mouth (intentions-and-words-and-deeds) level, not at the eaten-food's-ritual-purity-categorization level.

The framework reads *out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies* (v.19) in continuity with the Tanakh's-heart-substance-architecture:

- The new-heart-of-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 is the framework's-answer to the heart's-substance.

The framework reads the King's-list (evil-thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false-witness, blasphemies) as the second-table-of-the-Decalogue's-violations specified at the heart-and-mouth level. **The framework reads the every-plant-not-planted-by-the-Father saying** (v.13). *Every plant, which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.* The framework reads this in continuity with the wheat-and-tares parable of Matt 13:24-30 + interpretation at vv.36-43.
