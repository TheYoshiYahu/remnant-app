# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 16

Matthew 16 is the chapter where everything the gospel has been laying down arrives at the first explicit articulation — the first categorical confession of the King's identity by a disciple, the first explicit announcement of the death-and-resurrection, the first calling of the kingdom-citizens to take up the cross. Five movements anchor the chapter. (1) The Pharisees and Sadducees come together demanding a sign from heaven (vv.1-4). The two factions are theologically opposed at multiple points (the Pharisees affirm the resurrection, angels, and oral tradition; the Sadducees deny the resurrection and angels and hold only the five books of Mosheh (Moses)) — yet they confederate against the King per the Psalm 2:1-2 pattern. The King refuses the sign-on-demand and restates the sign-of-Yonah (Jonah) from Matthew 12:39-40 — the resurrection-credential is what the system will receive. (2) The leaven-of-the-Pharisees-and-Sadducees warning (vv.5-12). The disciples mis-hear at the bread-level; the King corrects them by reminding them of the two feedings; the text closes with the explicit identification: *not beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.* The Greek *didachē* is doctrine — the system's teaching is what the leaven is. Two doctrinal-corruptions named in one warning: Pharisaic addition-to-Torah (the tradition-overriding-Torah pattern Matthew 15:1-9 dismantled) and Sadducean subtraction-from-canon (denial of resurrection and angels). Mark 8:15 adds Herod as a third leaven — the Herodian accommodation-to-imperial-power. (3) The framework-major Caesarea-Philippi confession (vv.13-20). The location is deliberate: Banias/Paneas at the foot of Mount Hermon, the headwaters of the Yarden (Jordan), the Pan-grotto cut into a massive rock-cliff, the Herodian temple of Augustus, and the Tanakh-memory of the Danite resettlement at Laish per Judges 18:30-31. Pagan credentialing, imperial credentialing, and Tanakh-idolatry-memory layered at one geography. The King asks the credentialing-question. Kefa (Peter) confesses: *Thou art the Messiah (Christ), the Son of the living Elohim (God)* (v.16). The King's blessing — *flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven* (v.17) — names the cause-and-effect-of-hearing: the Father reveals, the disciple responds, hearing reveals what was already true. *Upon this rock I will build my church* (v.18) carries the framework-major weight against the inherited-Catholic-Petrine-papacy misreading. The Greek wordplay distinguishes *Petros* (masculine, a stone) from *petra* (feminine, the bedrock). The Tanakh's-Rock (*ha-tsur*) is consistently Yahuah (the LORD) — Deuteronomy 32:4, Psalm 18:2, Psalm 95:1, Isaiah 17:10 — and the apostolic-witness identifies the King as the Rock: *that Rock was the Messiah (Christ)* (1 Corinthians 10:4); the King as the living-stone and chief-corner-stone of 1 Peter 2:4-8 (Kefa (Peter)-himself disclaiming any reading that makes himself the foundation); *Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) himself being the chief corner stone* of Ephesians 2:20; the foundation-stone of Isaiah 28:16; the refused-stone-become-head-of-the-corner of Psalm 118:22. The Petros-Petra wordplay names Kefa (Peter) as *a* stone among the apostolic-foundation built upon the Rock-of-the-King. The papal-primacy architecture and the Reformation's-rock-is-the-confession alternative are both ruled out — the rock is the King himself. The *ekklēsia* (church) is the LXX-*qahal* of Yashar'el (Israel) — the called-out-ones of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed — NOT the inherited-Christian replacement-Israel construct. *The gates of hell shall not prevail* uses *pylai hadou* (gates of Hades = gates of Sheol per the LXX) — the Tanakh's grave-realm gates (Isaiah 38:10, Psalm 9:13, Job 38:17), NOT the institutional-Christian eternal-conscious-torment-hell; the assembly built on the resurrected-King partakes of the resurrection-substance the gates of death cannot hold. *The keys of the kingdom of heaven* draw from Isaiah 22:22's-Davidic-steward-substance (Revelation 3:7 re-applies the same to the King); the binding-and-loosing is apostolic-authority to declare the Torah's-substance against the Pharisaic-tradition's-corruption, extended to the assembly at Matthew 18:18 — NOT the Roman-Catholic-magisterial-authority-to-define-doctrine-against-scripture. (4) The first explicit passion-and-resurrection announcement (vv.21-23). *From that time forth* marks the pivot. The *must* (*dei*) is divine-necessity drawn from the Tanakh — Isaiah 53's suffering-servant, Zechariah 13:7's smitten-shepherd (the King will cite this at Matthew 26:31), Zechariah 12:10's pierced-one. *Be raised again the third day* connects the sign-of-Yonah three-days-and-three-nights to the actual resurrection. Hold the cross-architecture: the cross opens the door-home for the scattered-seed-who-walked-into-the-curse, but does NOT cancel the curse-system — Romans 6:14 and 6:15 are one breath; the Reformation's-truncation at v.14 produces the antichrist-gospel in covenant-clothing. Kefa's (Peter's) rebuke functions as the wilderness-temptation in disciple's-voice — the no-passion trade is the same architectural-substance as the kingdoms-without-the-cross trade the King refused at Matthew 4:8-10. *Get thee behind me, Satan* echoes Matthew 4:10's response to the same disposition. The King names the substance-of-the-moment, not Kefa (Peter) categorically — Kefa (Peter) remains the just-blessed disciple. (5) The take-up-the-cross discipleship-cost and the some-standing-here saying (vv.24-28). The cross-bearing kingdom-citizens follow the King into the substance he has just announced. *Whosoever will save his life shall lose it* — the kingdom-citizens' investment-in-the-kingdom over self-preservation (John 12:25 parallel). *Gain the whole world, and lose his own soul* connects to Matthew 4:8-10's wilderness-kingdoms-of-the-world-trade — the kingdoms are under watcher-rebellion-administration in the present age; no exchange-rate the world can offer is enough for the *psychē* (Hebrew *nephesh*). *Reward every man according to his works* is the post-harvest-sifting of Matthew 7:21-23 + Matthew 13:36-43 + Matthew 25:31-46 + Psalm 62:12 + Romans 2:6 — the works as the fruit-of-the-new-heart-that-internalized-or-did-not-internalize per the prophet's new-heart-substance at Ezekiel 36:26-27. *I never knew you, depart from me, ye that work iniquity* is spoken to the wheat-whose-fruit-failed, NOT to tares. *Some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom* (v.28) finds its immediate-narrative-fulfillment at the Matthew 17:1-8 transfiguration six days later — Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) on the high mountain see the King transfigured, with Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah), the cloud, the Father's-voice. Kefa (Peter)-himself names the transfiguration at 2 Peter 1:16-18 as the *eyewitnesses of his majesty* — the eyewitness-foretaste of *the power and coming of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ).* The preterist-reading collapsing the saying into AD 70 is ruled out — AD 70 was the destruction of the temple; AD 70 was not the day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) with the resurrection and the gathering and the visible coming on the clouds. The saying carries dual-resonance: immediate transfiguration-foretaste + broader eschatological-second-coming-substance. Matthew 17 will open with the King taking three of the disciples up the high mountain and the transfiguration the chapter just promised.
