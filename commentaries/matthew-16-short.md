# Short-form commentary on Matthew 16

The chapter walks five movements — the Pharisaic-and-Sadducean confederation's-sign-from-heaven-demand-and-the-King's-refusal-with-the-sign-of-Yonah (Jonah)-restated (vv.1-4); the leaven-of-the-Pharisees-and-Sadducees-named-explicitly-as-their-doctrine (vv.5-12); the Caesarea-Philippi-confession with Kefa's (Peter's) *thou art the Messiah, the Son of the living Elohim (God)* and the King's-response *upon this rock I will build my assembly ... I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven* (vv.13-20); the first-explicit-passion-prediction and Kefa's (Peter's) rebuke and the King's *get thee behind me, Satan* counter-rebuke (vv.21-23); the take-up-the-cross discipleship-cost teaching with the reward-according-to-works substance and the *some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom* bridge into the transfiguration (vv.24-28). The free.md walks the chapter through each movement, including the *key of the house of David* citation from Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 22:22 at the surface-level the keys-substance requires. One framework-substance the chapter's-Caesarea-Philippi-confession encodes — and that the free.md walks only at the single-verse-anchor level — the short.md walks at the fuller Tanakh-substance the King's-words bear: the entire-passage of Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 22:15-25's Shevna-and-Elyakim succession read as the prophetic-architecture for the demotion-of-the-self-credentialing-Shevna-class and the elevation-of-the-Elyakim-class-of-apostolic-stewardship that the King enacts at Matt 16:13-20.

**The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 22:15-25 Shevna-and-Elyakim succession of Davidic-stewardship — read against Matt 16:19's keys-of-the-kingdom-given-to-the-apostolic-stewardship.** The Tanakh-passage opens with the prophet sent against the self-credentialing steward:

*Thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) of hosts, Go, get thee unto this treasurer, even unto Shevna (Shebna), which is over the house, and say, What hast thou here? and whom hast thou here, that thou hast hewed thee out a sepulchre here, as he that heweth him out a sepulchre on high, and that graveth an habitation for himself in a rock?* (Isaiah 22:15-16)

Shevna (Shebna) is *al ha-bayit* — the steward over the house of Hizkiyahu (Hezekiah), the highest officer of the Davidic-administration in his day. The Yahuah-charge against him is not a charge of administrative-failure; the charge is of self-credentialing — he has used the steward's-office to hew out for himself a sepulchre on high, a habitation in the rock, the monument-of-the-Pharisee built while-in-office. He has confused the stewardship the master entrusted with a personal-credentialing-platform. The judgment is the demotion: *behold, Yahuah (the LORD) will carry thee away with a mighty captivity, and will surely cover thee. He will surely violently turn and toss thee like a ball into a large country: there shalt thou die ... and I will drive thee from thy station, and from thy state shall he pull thee down* (vv.17-19). The steward who built himself the monument is removed from the office; the office stands.

The elevation follows immediately. *And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Elyakim (Eliakim) the son of Chilkiyahu (Hilkiah): and I will clothe him with thy robe, and strengthen him with thy girdle, and I will commit thy government into his hand: and he shall be a father to the inhabitants of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and to the house of Yahudah (Judah)* (vv.20-21). The robe and the girdle and the government pass from Shevna to Elyakim by Yahuah's-own-action. Elyakim does not lobby for the office; Yahuah calls him *my servant* — the disposition of the steward-who-receives is the opposite of the disposition of the steward-who-credentialed-himself. And then the central declaration: *and the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open* (v.22). The key is the steward's-authority delegated by the king to administer the king's-house; the key-on-the-shoulder is the visible-token of the office; the open-and-shut is the operative-authority Elyakim now exercises in the master's name. *And I will fasten him as a nail in a sure place; and he shall be for a glorious throne to his father's house* (v.23) — Elyakim is the load-bearing peg the father's-house hangs upon, in the new arrangement Yahuah has named.

The Tanakh's own closing of the passage carries a warning the inherited-Catholic-Petrine-papacy reading of Matt 16 cannot absorb: *in that day, saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts), shall the nail that is fastened in the sure place be removed, and be cut down, and fall; and the burden that was upon it shall be cut off* (v.25). Even Elyakim's nail will eventually be removed. Even the elevated-Elyakim-stewardship is not permanent; the office passes; the office's-permanence belongs to the King-of-the-House alone, not to any steward however faithful. The prophet at v.25 is naming the architectural-limit of every-delegated-stewardship: the steward serves the master, the steward is not the master, the steward's-tenure is at the master's-disposal. When the Hitgalut (Revelation)-letter to the assembly at Philadelphia carries the same key-of-the-house-of-David substance forward — *these things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth* (Revelation 3:7) — the holder of the key is the King himself, the ultimate-Steward-of-the-House-who-is-also-the-Son-of-the-Master, the *nail fastened in a sure place* who is never cut down because his throne is the everlasting-throne the Tanakh had named at 2 Shemu'el (Samuel) 7:13 and Daniel 7:14.

Now read Matt 16:13-20 against the full Shevna-and-Elyakim succession. The King has just walked, in vv.1-4, into direct confrontation with the contemporary Shevna-class — the Pharisaic-and-Sadducean-second-Temple-aristocracy that holds the visible-steward-offices of the Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)-administration: the chief priests, the rulers, the scribes, the Sanhedrin-membership. They have hewed themselves sepulchres on high (the cosmetic-righteousness of Matt 23:27's *whited sepulchres ... full of dead men's bones*); they have made the steward-office into the self-credentialing-platform (the *commandments of men* of Matt 15:9 — building the Pharisaic-fence as the personal-credential-architecture; the Sadducean-truncation-of-the-canon as the aristocratic-class-protection-against-the-resurrection-substance that would undermine their settled-power). The Yahuah-charge against the Shevna-class is the same charge in different-costume: they have confused-the-stewardship-the-master-entrusted with their own credentialing-platform. The King's-departure at v.4 — *and he left them, and departed* — is the prophet-sent-against-the-steward's-act executed at the gospel-pivot. The Shevna-class is being put on notice: the office will pass.

Kefa's (Peter's) confession at v.16 is the elevation-of-Elyakim. *Flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven* (v.17): the steward Yahuah elevates does not lobby for the office; the Father calls his servant; the disposition is the opposite of the Shevna-architecture. The Petros-Petra wordplay positions Kefa-as-a-stone in the apostolic-foundation built upon the Rock-of-the-King (the substance the free.md walks against the Catholic-Petrine-papacy and Reformation-rock-is-the-confession alternatives); the upon-this-rock is the King-as-the-bedrock-of-the-house. And then v.19's central declaration: *I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.* The keys-on-the-shoulder of Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 22:22 are now the keys-of-the-kingdom-of-heaven entrusted to Kefa (and extended to the assembly at Matt 18:18). The apostolic-stewardship is the Elyakim-substance — the new office Yahuah is establishing at the gospel-pivot, with the binding-and-loosing-authority (the same open-and-shut substance of Isaiah 22:22) committed into the apostolic-hand. The Shevna-class is being demoted; the Elyakim-class is being elevated; the Davidic-house's-stewardship is being transferred at the moment-of-the-King's-direct-action.

The framework's Tanakh-substance-bears-the-architecture-and-the-warning-together. The Catholic-Petrine-papacy reading over-extends the Elyakim-figure into a permanent-papal-succession the Tanakh's-own-pattern explicitly-rules-out (Isaiah 22:25's *the nail ... shall be removed, and be cut down, and fall* applies to every steward); the apostolic-stewardship is real but it is steward-substance, not master-substance — the apostolic-keys are administered in continuity with the Torah-the-King-restored, not as a magisterial-authority-to-define-doctrine-against-scripture; the binding-and-loosing of vv.19, like Elyakim's open-and-shut of Isaiah 22:22, operates as delegated-authority-from-the-master-of-the-house, not as the steward's-own-substance. The Reformation's-rock-is-the-confession-not-Kefa alternative correctly-refuses-the-papal-collapse but misses the Tanakh's named demotion-and-elevation of stewards that the gospel-pivot enacts. Both inherited readings flatten the Tanakh-architecture; the framework restores the Shevna-and-Elyakim succession the prophet-and-the-King jointly carry. The King is establishing his assembly upon himself as the Rock; the King is committing the keys-of-the-house to the apostolic-stewardship as Yahuah committed the keys to Elyakim; the King's-own-keys remain his (Revelation 3:7); the apostolic-keys are entrusted-and-administered, with the steward-architecture of Isaiah 22's-closing-warning standing behind every delegated office — including the apostolic-office — across the present-age.
