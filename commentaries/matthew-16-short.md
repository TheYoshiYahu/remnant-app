# Short-form commentary on Matthew 16

Matthew 16 is the chapter where everything the gospel has been laying down arrives at the first explicit articulation. The categorical confession of the King's identity by a disciple — *thou art the Messiah (Christ), the Son of the living Elohim (God)* — lands in the mouth of Kefa (Peter) at Caesarea Philippi. The King's first explicit announcement that he must suffer, be killed, and rise the third day follows immediately. The first calling of the kingdom-citizens to take up the cross and follow lands next. The chapter that opens with the Pharisees-and-Sadducees demanding a sign from heaven closes with the King promising that some standing there would not taste death till they saw the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom — the bridge into the transfiguration of the following chapter. Five movements anchor the chapter. The Pharisees-and-Sadducees demand a sign from heaven and the King refuses, restating the sign-of-Yonah (Jonah) (vv.1-4). The disciples mis-hear the King's warning about the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees and the King corrects them, naming the leaven explicitly as their doctrine (vv.5-12). The Caesarea-Philippi confession with the King's blessing of Kefa (Peter), the upon-this-rock substance, the keys of the kingdom, and the gates-of-hell-shall-not-prevail saying (vv.13-20). The first explicit passion-and-resurrection announcement, Kefa's (Peter's) rebuke, and the King's *get thee behind me, Satan* counter-rebuke (vv.21-23). The take-up-the-cross discipleship-cost teaching with the save-life-lose-life paradox, the gain-the-whole-world-lose-the-soul saying, the Son-of-Adam-coming-in-glory-and-rewarding-according-to-works substance, and the some-standing-here saying that bridges into the transfiguration (vv.24-28).

*The Pharisees also with the Sadducees came, and tempting desired him that he would shew them a sign from heaven. He answered and said unto them, When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red. And in the morning, It will be foul weather to day: for the sky is red and lowring. O ye hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky; but can ye not discern the signs of the times? A wicked and adulterous generation seeketh after a sign; and there shall no sign be given unto it, but the sign of the prophet Yonah (Jonah). And he left them, and departed.* (Matthew 16:1-4)

Hold what the alliance is. The Pharisees and the Sadducees are theologically opposed at multiple points. The Pharisees affirm the resurrection, the angels, and the binding-authority of the oral tradition layered around the Torah; the Sadducees deny the resurrection, deny the angels, and hold only to the five books of Mosheh (Moses) as authoritative. Their parties' relationship across the period is contentious. And yet they come together against the King. The Tanakh pattern is named at the second Psalm:

*Why do the goyim rage, and the people imagine a vain thing? The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against Yahuah (the LORD), and against his anointed, saying, Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.* (Psalm 2:1-3)

The system's opposition to the Messianic-substance unites factions otherwise opposed. The seed-war pattern that the kingdom-citizens are watching operate at the religious-leadership level produces this confederation. The Pharisaic and Sadducean disagreements are real; the confederation against the King is also real; the deeper architecture is what the kingdom-citizens learn to see.

What they demand is *a sign from heaven.* The credential-on-demand pattern. The system asks the King to perform on its terms. The Tanakh has signs in its memory — Mosheh's rod that became a serpent and back; the plagues of Egypt; Eliyahu's (Elijah's) fire that fell on the altar at Carmel after his prayer; the regression of the sundial in Hizkiyahu's (Hezekiah's) day. The system would receive any of those as authenticating. The King has been performing credentials of a different order through the entire ministry — the healing of every disease at the beginning of the gospel, the casting out of demons at Matthew 12 with the *kingdom of Elohim (God) is come unto you* declaration, the multiplication of the loaves twice in the preceding two chapters, the walking on the water at Matthew 14, the healings at the mountain in Matthew 15 — and the system has not received these. The system asks for a different sign on its terms. The King refuses.

*Ye can discern the face of the sky; but can ye not discern the signs of the times?* The system reads weather from the sky's appearance, and reads it well. The system cannot read the Messianic-substance that has been on display for the duration of the King's-ministry. The capacity for natural discernment is intact; the disposition toward the Messianic-substance has set against it. The cause-and-effect-of-hearing the King will name at Matthew 13:13-15 operates here in another register: the seeing operates where the disposition is open; the seeing fails where the disposition has hardened. The Pharisaic-and-Sadducean confederation has hardened.

The sign that will be given is the sign-of-Yonah, restated from Matthew 12:39-40 unchanged. The resurrection. The three-days-and-three-nights in the heart of the earth, after which the King will be raised. The system that refuses the credentials already given will receive the credential of the resurrection — and the system will, even after receiving it, harden against it. The structural architecture the King has already laid down at Matthew 13 holds.

*And he left them, and departed.* The King speaks the truth and departs. The system's disposition is set; the King does not engage the credentialing-system on its own terms. The pattern Yahusha (Jesus) is teaching his disciples by demonstration: speak, and when the disposition is set, depart. Do not stay to argue at the credentialing-system's table.

The disciples are on the boat across the lake. They have forgotten bread.

*And when his disciples were come to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread. Then Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees. And they reasoned among themselves, saying, It is because we have taken no bread. Which when Yahusha (Jesus) perceived, he said unto them, O ye of little faith, why reason ye among yourselves, because ye have brought no bread? Do ye not yet understand, neither remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets ye took up? Neither the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many baskets ye took up? How is it that ye do not understand that I spake it not to you concerning bread, that ye should beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees? Then understood they how that he bade them not beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.* (Matthew 16:5-12)

The King's warning, the disciples' mis-hearing, the King's correction. The architecture of the correction is what teaches.

*The leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.* The disciples hear *leaven* and reason about whether they have or have not brought leavened bread. The King's gentle frustration: *O ye of little faith, why reason ye among yourselves, because ye have brought no bread?* They have just witnessed the King multiply five loaves to feed five thousand with twelve baskets remaining; they have just witnessed the King multiply seven loaves to feed four thousand with seven baskets remaining; the bread-question is not the King's-question. The bread-question is not a question at all when the King is present.

What the King is naming is something else. The text gives the final word: *Then understood they how that he bade them not beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.* The Greek word is *didachē* — teaching, doctrine, what the system instructs. The leaven is the system's teaching. The metaphor preserves the Tanakh-substance of leaven as the corrupting-agent that permeates the whole batch — small in quantity at introduction, total in effect at completion. The unleavened bread of the Pesach (Passover) and the seven-day feast of Matzot (Unleavened Bread) carries the same substance from the opposite direction: the covenant-life prepared and eaten in the absence of the corrupting-agent, fresh and unspoiled, the substance of the Tanakh's-original-instruction.

*Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Messiah (Christ) our passover is sacrificed for us: Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.* (1 Corinthians 5:7-8)

*A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.* (Galatians 5:9)

Two leavens are named in the King's warning, and the two are different in their costumes while being one in their architectural-substance. The Pharisaic leaven is the tradition-overriding-Torah pattern Matthew 15:1-9 dismantled — the rabbinic-fence built around the Torah that voids the Torah at specific points (the Korban-evasion of the fifth commandment Mark 7:10-13 names explicitly), the teaching-for-doctrines the commandments of men against which the prophet had spoken at Isaiah 29:13. The Sadducean leaven is the truncation-of-the-canon pattern — denying the resurrection of the dead, denying the angels, denying everything except what could be wrung from the five books of Mosheh (Moses) read by their narrow lights. Two opposite errors: addition-to-the-Torah that voids the Torah; subtraction-from-the-Torah's-and-the-prophets'-witness that empties the canon. The King's-warning names both as leaven and lumps them together. The architectural-substance is the same: the system's doctrine is what corrupts. The disciples are being trained to discern the doctrinal-substance, not merely the visible credentialing.

The Markan parallel adds a third leaven the disciples are warned against on the same crossing:

*And he charged them, saying, Take heed, beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, and of the leaven of Herod.* (Mark 8:15)

Herod-Antipas — the political-system that beheaded Yochanan (John) at Matthew 14 — is named as a third leaven with its own doctrinal-and-political substance. The three leavens — Pharisaic addition-to-Torah, Sadducean subtraction-from-canon, Herodian accommodation-to-imperial-power — operate as a triad across the religious-and-political-architectures of the King's-day. The kingdom-citizens are warned against all three. The triad is recognizable today in different costumes — the institutional-Christian-system's subtraction of the Torah-substance (the Sadducean costume in different vestments), the same system's addition of human traditions and credentialing-architectures the Father never commanded (the Pharisaic costume in different vestments), and the entire ecclesiastical-political accommodation that has run from Constantine onward (the Herodian costume in different vestments). The same three leavens in different clothes.

The King and the disciples come to the coasts of Caesarea Philippi.

*When Yahusha (Jesus) came into the coasts of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, saying, Whom do men say that I the Son of Adam am?* (Matthew 16:13)

Hold what the location is. Caesarea Philippi — Banias today, the Greek *Paneion* of antiquity — sits at the foot of Mount Hermon, the snow-capped mountain at the northern edge of the historical Yashar'el (Israel)-territory. The Yarden (Jordan) river is born at this place; one of its major springs flows out from beneath a massive rock cliff with a cave-grotto cut into it. The grotto and the cliff were the center of a Hellenistic cult to the Greek god Pan — the *Paneion* gave the place its Greek name. Herod-the-Great had built a temple to Augustus at the site; the temple complex was visible against the cliff. The territory of Dan in the Tanakh had passed through this region — the Danite resettlement at Laish, with its graven image set up in the high places per Judges 18:30-31, located within reach of the same area. Pagan credentialing, imperial credentialing, and the memory of Tanakh-idolatry layered on top of each other at one geographical-location.

The King brings the disciples to this place and asks the credentialing-question. *Whom do men say that I the Son of Adam am?* He is standing in front of the rock-face with the Pan-grotto cut into it. The Augustus-temple is somewhere in view. The competing credentialings of the world surround them. And the King's-question is the credentialing-question of who he is.

*Son of Adam* — the lineage-weight of the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise that the gospel has been carrying since Matthew 1. The King names himself by the lineage-title at the moment of the credentialing-question; he is asking not whom-do-men-say-some-cosmic-figure-is but whom-do-men-say-I-am, the Son-of-Adam, the seed-of-the-promise the prophets named.

*And they said, Some say that thou art Yochanan (John) the Immerser: some, Eliyahu (Elijah); and others, Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah), or one of the prophets.* (Matthew 16:14)

The multitudes' speculations name him in the prophet-pattern. Herod-Antipas had spoken the Yochanan-resurrected speculation at Matthew 14:2 from his guilty conscience; that speculation has spread among the multitudes. Eliyahu (Elijah) — the prophet whose return is named at the close of the Tanakh as the forerunner of the day of Yahuah (the LORD): *Behold, I will send you Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD)* (Malachi 4:5). Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) — the weeping-prophet of the destruction-of-the-first-temple, whose witness against the religious-system of his day is being recognized as paralleling the King's-witness against the religious-system of this day. *One of the prophets* — the broader recognition that something prophet-class is in motion. The multitudes can see something is happening; they cannot yet land on what.

*He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am?* (Matthew 16:15)

The King turns the question to the disciples. The multitudes' speculations are reported; the disciples' confession is asked. The Greek emphasizes the contrast — *but ye, who do you say I am?*

*And Shim'on (Simon) Kefa (Peter) answered and said, Thou art the Messiah (Christ), the Son of the living Elohim (God).* (Matthew 16:16)

The confession lands. *Thou art the Messiah (Christ), the Son of the living Elohim (God).* Two halves to it, each carrying its own weight.

*The Messiah (Christ).* The Greek is *ho Christos* with the definite article — THE Messiah (Christ), not A messiah. The Tanakh-anointed-substance of the Davidic-line. The seed of David promised the everlasting throne at 2 Samuel 7:12-16. The shoot from the stump of Yishai (Jesse) of Isaiah 11. The branch of David of Jeremiah 23:5-6. The king who reigns in righteousness of Isaiah 32:1. The pierced-one whom they will look upon at Zechariah 12:10. The Aaronic-priestly-anointing-substance fused with the Davidic-kingly-anointing-substance in the King-after-the-order-of-Melek Tsadiq (Melchizedek) of Psalm 110. The whole Tanakh's-Messianic-architecture in the substance Kefa (Peter) just named. *The* Messiah (Christ), not *a* messiah.

*The Son of the living Elohim (God).* The Tanakh's-living-Elohim (God) language carried into Messianic identification. The God who is not the dead idols of the nations — the dead Pan-shrine the King is standing in front of as Kefa (Peter) speaks, the dead Augustus-temple within view — but the living-One who creates and acts and speaks. *The Son* of this living-Elohim (God) — the Formed expression of the Formless-Source. The Father is the formless-infinite; the Son is the Formed-Voice that spoke at Sinai, the One whose creative-Word fashioned the cosmos, the One the disciples have now seen multiply bread and walk on water and heal at a word. The Son-of-the-living-Elohim (God) Kefa (Peter) is naming is the Tanakh's-Elohim (God)-of-Yashar'el (Israel)-in-the-flesh.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Shim'on (Simon) Bar-Yonah (Bar-Jonah): for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.* (Matthew 16:17)

The King's blessing. Hold what it teaches about how Kefa's (Peter's)-confession came to be.

*Flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee.* The confession is not the product of Kefa's (Peter's)-reasoning. The disciples have seen the same things together over the course of the gospel; they have not all confessed. The reasoning-from-the-evidence does not produce the categorical-recognition. The disciples on the boat at Matthew 14:33 had spoken a preliminary version of this confession — *of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God)* — after the walking-on-the-water; what Kefa (Peter) speaks at Caesarea Philippi is the same substance deepened to the explicit-Messianic-categorical-confession. The deepening is not the product of further reasoning.

*But my Father which is in heaven.* The Father is the source of the revelation. The Father has revealed the substance to Kefa (Peter); Kefa (Peter)'s-confession is the response-to-the-revelation-already-given. The architecture the King had named at Matthew 11:25-27 is operating here:

*All things are delivered unto me of my Father: and no man knoweth the Son, but the Father; neither knoweth any man the Father, save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal him.* (Matthew 11:27)

The Father reveals the Son to those to whom the Father reveals the Son. The Yochanan-gospel parallel names the architecture directly:

*No man can come to me, except the Father which hath sent me draw him.* (John 6:44)

The cause-and-effect-of-hearing operates here at its weight-bearing substance. Kefa (Peter) did not figure out the Messianic-categorical-substance. Kefa (Peter) did not choose to confess and thereby become a sheep. Kefa (Peter) was always a sheep — *if you hear, you are sons of Avraham (Abraham), sons of Adam, the seed of promise* — and the Father, in his own timing, revealed the substance to one of his own. The hearing reveals what was already true. The confession is the seal of what the Father has done in revealing.

*And I say also unto thee, That thou art Kefa (Peter), and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.* (Matthew 16:18)

This verse has carried more institutional-architecture weight than perhaps any single verse in the gospels. The Roman-Catholic-Petrine-papacy reading takes this verse as the founding-charter of the apostolic see at Rome and the unbroken-succession of Roman pontiffs from Kefa (Peter) forward as the visible head of the universal church. The Protestant-Reformation flinched from that reading and proposed an alternative — the rock is Kefa's (Peter's) confession, not Kefa (Peter) himself — that solved the papal problem while leaving the church-substance largely as the Reformation inherited it. Read carefully against the Tanakh-substance and the apostolic-witness, the verse is not what either inherited tradition has taught.

The Greek text uses two different words. *Sy ei Petros, kai epi tautē tē petra oikodomēsō mou tēn ekklēsian* — *thou art Petros, and upon this petra I will build my ekklēsia.* *Petros* (masculine) names a stone, a movable piece of rock that can be picked up and thrown. *Petra* (feminine) names a rock-mass, an immovable outcrop, the bedrock itself. The two words are not synonyms; the Greek deliberately distinguishes them. *Thou art a stone* — and *upon this bedrock* I will build my assembly. The wordplay names Kefa (Peter) and points elsewhere for the bedrock.

Where does the bedrock-language come from in the Tanakh? It comes from one source consistently. The Rock is Yahuah (the LORD).

*He is the Rock, his work is perfect: for all his ways are judgment: a Elohim (God) of truth and without iniquity, just and right is he.* (Deuteronomy 32:4)

*I will love thee, O Yahuah (the LORD), my strength. Yahuah (the LORD) is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my Elohim (God), my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.* (Psalm 18:1-2)

*For who is Elohim (God) save Yahuah (the LORD)? or who is a rock save our Elohim (God)?* (Psalm 18:31)

*O come, let us sing unto Yahuah (the LORD): let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.* (Psalm 95:1)

*Because thou hast forgotten the Elohim (God) of thy salvation, and hast not been mindful of the rock of thy strength.* (Isaiah 17:10)

The Tanakh's-Rock is Yahuah (the LORD). The Messianic-architecture — the Son as the Formed-Voice of Yahuah (the LORD) — places the Son in the same Rock-position the Tanakh names. The apostle Paul names this directly when he reads the wilderness-Rock from which the water flowed for Yashar'el (Israel):

*And did all drink the same spiritual drink: for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was the Messiah (Christ).* (1 Corinthians 10:4)

The wilderness-Rock at Refidim (Rephidim) was the Messiah (Christ). The Tanakh's-Rock is the Son. And when Kefa (Peter) himself writes his first apostolic letter, the rock-substance is the substance Kefa (Peter)-himself attributes to the King, not to Kefa-himself:

*To whom coming, as unto a living stone, disallowed indeed of men, but chosen of Elohim (God), and precious, Ye also, as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house, an holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to Elohim (God) by Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ). Wherefore also it is contained in the scripture, Behold, I lay in Tsion (Zion) a chief corner stone, elect, precious: and he that believeth on him shall not be confounded.* (1 Peter 2:4-6)

The King is the living-stone. The King is the chief-corner-stone. The disciples — including Kefa (Peter) — are *lively stones* built into the spiritual house upon him. Kefa (Peter)-himself, writing his own letter under the Spirit's-inspiration, identifies the King-as-the-foundation-stone and the assembly-as-stones-built-upon. Kefa (Peter)-himself disclaims any reading that places himself in the foundation-position. The Petrine reading the Roman-Catholic-Petrine-papacy constructs is not the reading Kefa (Peter) holds for himself.

The Tanakh-anchor Kefa (Peter) is citing is from the prophet:

*Therefore thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD), Behold, I lay in Tsion (Zion) for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste.* (Isaiah 28:16)

And the other Tanakh-anchor that the King himself will cite in the parable of the wicked-husbandmen at Matthew 21:42 is from the psalm:

*The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner. This is Yahuah's (the LORD's) doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.* (Psalm 118:22-23)

The King is the refused-stone become the head-of-the-corner. The King is the foundation-stone laid in Tsion (Zion). The King is the wilderness-Rock from which the water flowed. The King is the *ha-tsur* of the Tanakh consistently. And when the apostle Paul writes to the assembly at Ephesos (Ephesus), he names the architecture in the same direction:

*And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) himself being the chief corner stone; In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in Yahuah (the LORD).* (Ephesians 2:20-21)

The apostles and prophets are the foundation-layer; the King is the chief-corner-stone upon which the whole structure aligns and rises. Kefa (Peter) is in the foundation-layer with the other apostles; the King is in the chief-corner-stone position. The apostolic-witness across multiple letters lands at the same architecture.

The Petros-Petra wordplay names Kefa (Peter) as *a stone* — and that is the stone Kefa (Peter) is. Kefa (Peter) takes his place among the stones of the apostolic-foundation laid upon the Rock-of-the-King. The Petros is real; the Petra is not Petros. The naming-of-Kefa-as-Petros at the moment of the confession is the King recognizing Kefa-as-one-of-the-stones-being-set-in-the-foundation; the *upon this rock* points at the bedrock the foundation rests upon, which the Tanakh and the apostolic-witness identify consistently as Yahuah (the LORD) the Formed-Voice — the King himself.

The Roman-Catholic-Petrine-papacy architecture, built on the inherited reading that *this rock = Kefa = the first pope*, is what the Tanakh-substance and the apostolic-witness rule out. The papal-primacy-and-magisterial-authority and the institutional-succession-from-Kefa-to-Rome are not what the verse establishes. What the verse establishes is the building of the assembly upon the Son who is the Formed-Voice that spoke at Sinai, that flowed as living-water through the wilderness, that has now stood at Caesarea Philippi in flesh and received the confession of the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise in the mouth of one of his disciples.

What the King will build is *my church.* The Greek is *mou tēn ekklēsian* — my *ekklēsia.* The word translates the Hebrew *qahal* throughout the Greek-Tanakh — the called-out-assembly of Yashar'el (Israel). *The qahal of Yahuah (the LORD)* at Deuteronomy 23:1-8 names the covenant-assembly with the conditions of its membership; *the qahal of Yashar'el (Israel)* at Joshua 8:35 names the gathered-people hearing the Torah read at the renewal-of-the-covenant at Ebal and Gerizim; *I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the qahal will I praise thee* at Psalm 22:22 names the King-in-the-midst-of-the-assembly the letter to the Hebrews cites as Messianic at Hebrews 2:12. The *ekklēsia* the King will build is the called-out-ones of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed in continuity with the Tanakh's-qahal-substance.

The institutional-Christian *church* that has been inherited from the Reformation backward through Rome backward through Constantine is the displacement-architecture that operates as antichrist-substance. The replacement-Israel-spiritualized-into-an-institution architecture is not what the King's-*ekklēsia* is. The *ekklēsia* the King is building is the lost-sheep-of-the-house-of-Yashar'el (Israel) gathered out of the nations through the proclamation of the kingdom, sifted post-harvest, brought through the wilderness of the people per Ezekiel 20:35-38, made the kingdom of priests of Exodus 19:6 / Revelation 5:10 in the millennial reign of the King. The gathering the prophets named is what the King's-*ekklēsia* is the present-age-form of. The institutional-Christian-replacement-construct that has been built on top of Matthew 16:18 is not the *ekklēsia* the King names.

*And the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.* The Greek is *pylai hadou* — *the gates of Hades.* *Hades* in the Greek-Tanakh translates the Hebrew *Sheol* — the underworld, the realm of the dead, the grave. The institutional-Christian-doctrine of an eternal-conscious-torment-hell has been read back into the *hadēs* of this verse for centuries; the Tanakh-substance does not carry that reading. The Tanakh's-Sheol is the place of the dead — the grave-realm where the dead go and wait. *I shall go down into Sheol unto my son mourning,* Ya'aqov (Jacob) says at Genesis 37:35 over the supposed-death of Yosef (Joseph). *As the cloud is consumed and vanisheth away: so he that goeth down to Sheol shall come up no more,* Job 7:9. The Sheol of the Tanakh is the grave's-domain. The gates-of-Sheol carry the power-of-death-and-the-grave.

*The gates of the grave* the King is naming are the doors that hold the dead. The Tanakh imagery is direct:

*I said in the cutting off of my days, I shall go to the gates of the grave (sheol): I am deprived of the residue of my years.* (Isaiah 38:10)

*Have mercy upon me, O Yahuah (the LORD); consider my trouble which I suffer of them that hate me, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death.* (Psalm 9:13)

*Have the gates of death been opened unto thee? or hast thou seen the doors of the shadow of death?* (Job 38:17)

The gates-of-Sheol are the doors of the grave-realm that hold the dead. *Shall not prevail against it* — the gates of the grave will not hold the assembly. The substance the King is announcing is the resurrection-substance. The assembly built on the Rock partakes of the King's-resurrection; the gates of the grave that close on death are opened by the King who rises through them. The sign-of-Yonah pre-announced four verses earlier at v.4 is the substance of v.18's-promise: the King will die, the King will rise, and the assembly built on the resurrected-King will rise with him at the first resurrection per the prophets and per Revelation 20:4-6. The gates that hold death do not hold the King; therefore they do not hold the assembly built on him.

The militaristic-imagery the institutional-Christian-tradition has read into the verse — gates as defensive-installations of Satan's-kingdom that the church-on-offense assaults and breaches — is not what the Tanakh-substance carries. The image is not the church assaulting Satan's-gates; the image is the King's-resurrection breaking open the gates-of-the-grave that would otherwise hold the dead. The assembly's-substance is resurrection-substance; the gates of death do not prevail against the assembly because they did not prevail against the King.

*And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.* (Matthew 16:19)

The keys-substance derives from the prophet at the closing of the chapter against Shevna (Shebna) the steward and the elevation of Elyaqim (Eliakim) in his place:

*And the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open. And I will fasten him as a nail in a sure place; and he shall be for a glorious throne to his father's house.* (Isaiah 22:22-23)

The Tanakh-key-substance is the steward's-authority delegated by the king to administer the king's-house. The steward holds the keys and acts on the king's-behalf — opening and shutting at the king's-direction. The substance the prophet names of Elyaqim (Eliakim) the King applies to himself at the letter to the assembly at Philadelphia:

*These things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth.* (Revelation 3:7)

The key of David belongs to the King. And the King delegates the keys of the kingdom-of-heaven to Kefa (Peter) here, and to the assembly more broadly at Matthew 18:18. The stewards open the kingdom-substance through the proclamation; the stewards shut against the corruptions that would void the kingdom-substance.

*Whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.* The rabbinic vocabulary of *asar* (bind) and *hattir* (loose) names the rabbinic-decision-making about what the Torah's-substance forbids and permits. The Pharisaic-system had used this binding-and-loosing-authority to construct the fence-around-Torah that voided the Torah at points (the Korban-evasion Matthew 15 dismantled). The King is now giving the binding-and-loosing-authority to the apostle — and through the apostle to the assembly — to declare the Torah's-substance against the Pharisaic-tradition's-corruption.

The Roman-Catholic-magisterial-authority reading takes this binding-and-loosing as the church's-authority-to-define-doctrine-binding-on-the-conscience independent of and even against scripture. The Tanakh and the apostolic-witness read against this. The apostolic binding-and-loosing operates in continuity with the Torah's-substance, not against it. The apostles are stewards of what the King-the-master-of-the-house has given; they administer the kingdom's-substance as stewards, not innovators. The binding-and-loosing is the apostolic-authority to declare what the Torah-as-the-King-restored-it binds and loosens — to declare that the Pharisaic-Korban-evasion is bound (forbidden) because it voids the fifth commandment, to declare that the Pharisaic-hand-washing-tradition is loosened (the Torah does not require it), to declare that table-fellowship with the lost-sheep-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is loosened (Matthew 9:10-13), to declare the Pharisaic-and-Sadducean-leaven bound (warned against). The substance the apostolic-keys are stewarding is the Torah-as-the-King-restored-it.

*Then charged he his disciples that they should tell no man that he was Yahusha (Jesus) the Messiah (Christ).* (Matthew 16:20)

The Messianic-secret pattern. The categorical-confession has just landed in the inner-circle of the disciples; the public-proclamation of the Messianic-categorical-recognition would accelerate the conflict-trajectory before the King's-time. The Pharisees and Sadducees have just been confederating against the King at vv.1-4; the death-conspiracy reached the level of public-council at Matthew 12:14. The King is moving the disciples toward the substance the cross will require of them, but the public-Messianic-categorical-disclosure must wait until the appointed-time. The meek-and-not-striving substance of the prophet's-Servant-Song the King cited at Matthew 12:18-21 is operating: *he shall not strive, nor cry; neither shall any man hear his voice in the streets.* The categorical-disclosure is saved for the moment the King chooses, which will be at the triumphal entry into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) at Matthew 21 and at the Sanhedrin trial at Matthew 26.

The chapter has reached the categorical-confession; what the categorical-Messianic-substance includes is what the next movement opens.

*From that time forth began Yahusha (Jesus) to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day. Then Kefa (Peter) took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Yahuah (Lord): this shall not be unto thee. But he turned, and said unto Kefa (Peter), Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of Elohim (God), but those that be of men.* (Matthew 16:21-23)

*From that time forth.* The pivot is marked. The categorical-confession has landed; the King now opens what the categorical-Messianic-substance includes. The first explicit announcement of the death-and-the-resurrection.

*He must go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.* The Greek word is *dei* — *it is necessary,* a divine-necessity-must. The King is not saying this might happen; the King is saying this must happen. The must derives from the Tanakh-prophetic-substance the King has been carrying through the gospel. The suffering-servant of the prophet:

*He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not. Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did esteem him stricken, smitten of Elohim (God), and afflicted. But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed.* (Isaiah 53:3-5)

The smitten-shepherd of the prophet:

*Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow, saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts): smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered: and I will turn mine hand upon the little ones.* (Zechariah 13:7)

The King will cite this verse to the disciples at Matthew 26:31 on the night of the arrest. The smiting-of-the-shepherd that scatters the sheep is the King's-passion and the disciples'-scattering at Gethsemane.

The pierced-one of the prophet, named in the second-coming-substance the King will carry forward:

*And they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.* (Zechariah 12:10)

The Tanakh-prophetic-architecture is the source of the *must.* The King is not innovating a passion-doctrine; the King is naming the Tanakh-substance he has come to fulfill. The disciples should have heard the prophets' substance all along.

*And be raised again the third day.* The resurrection-credential pre-announced at v.4's-sign-of-Yonah is now explicit at the disciple-level. The three-days-and-three-nights in the heart of the earth the King declared at Matthew 12:40 is now connected to the third-day-raising the King declares to the disciples. The sign-of-Yonah is not a metaphor; the sign-of-Yonah is the actual-resurrection the King is about to undergo, and the King is now telling the disciples it will happen.

Hold the architecture of the cross. The cross opens the door-home for the scattered-seed-who-walked-into-the-curse by walking contrary to him. The cross does NOT cancel the curse-system; the believer-who-turns-back-into-rebellion does not stand outside the curse — he steps back into it. The apostle names this directly:

*What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? Elohim (God) forbid. How shall we, that are dead to sin, live any longer therein?* (Romans 6:1-2)

*For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace. What then? shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? Elohim (God) forbid.* (Romans 6:14-15)

Two verses, one breath. The Reformation's-truncation that cuts the sentence at *not under law but under grace* and treats the cut as the standalone-gospel produces the antichrist-gospel in covenant-clothing. The grace that delivers from the dominion of sin is the grace that delivers into walking-with-the-Father; the believer who treats the grace as license-to-sin is the empty-house of Matthew 12:43-45 the unclean-spirit returns to with seven worse than himself. The cross opens the door; the door opens to the walking; the walking is the new-heart's-Torah-substance the prophet named at Ezekiel 36:26-27. The cross-as-cancellation-of-the-curse-system reading is what the Reformation produced; the cross-as-door-home-with-the-curse-system-still-operative-against-the-rebellion reading is what the apostolic-witness holds.

The King is about to walk this. The disciples will witness it. The substance is the foundation of the assembly the King has just announced he will build.

*Then Kefa (Peter) took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Yahuah (Lord): this shall not be unto thee.* The same Kefa (Peter) who just confessed the Messianic-categorical-substance now rebukes the King over the passion-substance the categorical-substance includes. The Father had revealed the first half to Kefa (Peter) at v.16; the second half — the Messianic-substance-includes-the-suffering — is not yet internalized. The disciples expected a political-Messianic-deliverer who would take the throne and break the yoke of Rome; the Messianic-suffering-substance the King is now naming contradicts that expectation. The Tanakh had named both the conquering-King and the suffering-Servant; the rabbinic-tradition had often split the two into two-Messianic-figures (sometimes called Mashiach ben David and Mashiach ben Yosef in later rabbinic literature) to handle the apparent contradiction. The King is now naming that the two are one — that the conquering-King is the suffering-Servant — and the conclusion does not yet land for Kefa (Peter).

*Be it far from thee, Yahuah (Lord): this shall not be unto thee.* Kefa (Peter) loves the King and cannot accept that the King will be killed. The disposition is recognizable. The disposition is also adversarial.

*But he turned, and said unto Kefa (Peter), Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of Elohim (God), but those that be of men.*

The Greek is *hypage opisō mou, satana* — *get thee behind me, satan.* The phrase echoes the King's-response to the actual-adversary in the wilderness at Matthew 4:10 — *hypage, satana, get thee hence, satan.* Kefa's (Peter's)-rebuke is functioning, in the moment, as the wilderness-temptation in the disciple's-voice. The wilderness-trade was kingdoms-without-the-cross — *the adversary taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them; and saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me* (Matthew 4:8-9). The King had refused. Kefa's (Peter's)-rebuke at v.22 is the same trade in the disciple's-mouth — Messianic-glory-without-the-passion, the throne-without-the-cross. The King's-response is the same as in the wilderness because the disposition being offered is the same.

Hold this carefully. The King is not categorically identifying Kefa (Peter) with the adversary. Kefa (Peter) remains the just-blessed disciple of v.17 to whom the Father has revealed the Messianic-substance. Kefa (Peter) will go on to be one of the foundation-stones of the apostolic-foundation. What the King is naming is the substance-of-the-moment — the disposition Kefa (Peter) has just expressed is the disposition the adversary used in the wilderness, and the King's-response to that disposition is the same regardless of whose mouth speaks it. *Thou art an offence unto me* — the Greek *skandalon,* a stumbling-block, the very thing the prophet had named the King-himself would be to the unbelieving in Yashar'el (Israel) at Isaiah 8:14 (*he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Yashar'el (Israel)*). Kefa (Peter)'s-disposition would be the stumbling-block to the King's-mission. The King rebukes the disposition; the disciple remains.

*For thou savourest not the things that be of Elohim (God), but those that be of men.* The diagnostic. The disposition of human-perspective alone produces the rebuke Kefa (Peter) just expressed. The things-of-Elohim (God) require the revelation the Father gives. Kefa (Peter) had received the revelation about the categorical-Messianic-substance at v.17; Kefa (Peter) had not yet received the revelation about the suffering-substance the King is now disclosing. The human-perspective on what-a-Messiah-should-be filled in the gap. The disciples — and the kingdom-citizens through every era — are taught: where the Father has not yet revealed, the human-perspective fills in with what the human-perspective wants the answer to be. The disposition Kefa (Peter) is being rebuked for is universal across the kingdom-citizens' present-age formation. The remedy is the same: receive what the Father reveals; do not insist on what the flesh imagines about what the Father should reveal.

The King now turns to the disciples broadly. The teaching that follows is for all of them, and for the kingdom-citizens-through-every-era.

*Then said Yahusha (Jesus) unto his disciples, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me. For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it. For what is a man profited, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul? or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul? For the Son of Adam shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works. Verily I say unto you, There be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom.* (Matthew 16:24-28)

The discipleship-cost teaching now follows from the announcement of what the King-himself is about to undergo. The pattern the King has just announced for himself is what the kingdom-citizens are called to walk after him.

*If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.* The cross-language for the kingdom-citizens. The King has used this phrase once before, at Matthew 10:38 in the mission-discourse: *and he that taketh not his cross, and followeth after me, is not worthy of me.* What was named in the mission-discourse is now expanded in the discipleship-cost teaching after the King's-own-passion has been announced.

The cross is the Roman-execution-instrument. The disciples who hear these words know what a cross is from direct-experience — the Roman-administration crucified rebels along the roads of the empire as a public-deterrent. Josephus records mass-crucifixions across the period. To take up one's cross is to walk to one's execution. The metaphor's-content is the kingdom-citizens' willingness to walk into death for the King's-sake when the present-age requires it. The Tanakh-prophets' pattern — Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) thrown into the pit, Daniel into the lions' den, Hananyah and Misha'el and Azaryah (Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego) into the furnace, Eliyahu (Elijah) fleeing Izevel (Jezebel) — is the substance the kingdom-citizens are inheriting.

*Let him deny himself.* The Greek *aparnēsasthō heauton* — let him refuse-himself, reject-himself, say-no-to-himself. The substance is not the inherited-Christian-asceticism that constructs self-denial as a generic-spiritual-discipline of giving-up-things-for-Lent or chastising-the-body to prove devotion. The substance is the kingdom-citizens' subordination-of-self-interest to the kingdom's-substance — the willingness to set aside the natural-self's-preferences and self-preservation when the kingdom-substance requires it. The same disposition the King has just demonstrated in refusing the no-passion trade in Kefa's (Peter's)-mouth.

*Take up his cross, and follow me.* The taking-up is active, not endured. The follow-me is the substance Yochanan (John)-Immerser's-disciples-and-Andrew-and-Shim'on (Simon) had been called to at the beginning of the gospel. The cross-bearing follow is what the call has been deepening toward all along.

*For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it.* The paradox of save-life-by-losing-life. The kingdom-citizens who try to preserve the natural-life by avoiding the cross-cost lose what they are trying to preserve — the eternal-life-substance the kingdom carries. The kingdom-citizens who lose the natural-life for the King's-sake find what they were unaware they were saving — the eternal-life-substance. The Yochanan-gospel parallel locks the same architecture:

*He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that hateth his life in this world shall keep it unto life eternal.* (John 12:25)

The pattern operates across the apostolic-witness. The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) writes from Roman-prison toward his expected-execution:

*For to me to live is the Messiah (Christ), and to die is gain. ... For I am in a strait betwixt two, having a desire to depart, and to be with the Messiah (Christ); which is far better.* (Philippians 1:21, 23)

The kingdom-citizens' disposition toward present-age-death is not the natural-disposition; the King has reframed what life-and-death mean.

*For what is a man profited, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul? or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul?* The exchange-rate question. The Greek *psychē* — the soul, the life-substance, the Hebrew *nephesh* of the whole-person. The Greek *kosmos* — the world-system, the kingdoms-of-the-world in their present arrangement under the watcher-rebellion-administration the King had named at the wilderness-temptation:

*Again, the adversary taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them; And saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me. Then saith Yahusha (Jesus) unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God), and him only shalt thou serve.* (Matthew 4:8-10)

The kingdoms-of-the-world are under the adversary's-present-age-administration; the adversary could offer them to the King because they were within the adversary's-present-age-grant per Job 1-2 and the Daniel 7-architecture. To gain the whole-world is to make the trade the King refused — to take the kingdoms on the adversary's-terms. The kingdom-citizens are being warned: do not make the trade. The whole-world is not worth the soul. No exchange-rate the world can offer is high enough to be a fair-trade for the nephesh.

The Tanakh-substance is the same:

*None of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to Elohim (God) a ransom for him: (For the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever.)* (Psalm 49:7-8)

No price the world's-economy can name is enough to redeem the soul. The exchange the King is naming is impossible at the world's-side of the trade. The kingdom-citizens are not making a calculation of whether the world's-payment is high enough; the kingdom-citizens are refusing to put the soul on the auction-block at all.

*For the Son of Adam shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works.* The second-coming-substance. The first-coming the chapter has been opening — the gathering and the credentialing, with the cross at its center — is one motion; the second-coming the verse now names is the other. The Son-of-Adam-coming-in-the-glory-of-the-Father-with-his-angels is the dreadful-day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) the prophets named, the day-of-judgment that closes the present-age. *And he shall reward every man according to his works.*

The reward-according-to-works substance the King names is the post-harvest-sifting that follows the wheat-and-tares-harvest. The wheat-and-tares parable at Matthew 13:36-43 had laid down the harvest: the tares bound and burned; the wheat gathered into the King's-barn. The reward-according-to-works that follows the harvest sifts the wheat: the wheat-that-bore-fruit gathered to its inheritance; the wheat-whose-fruit-failed under the day-of-judgment per Matthew 7:21-23 (*not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven*). The Tanakh-anchor for the works-evaluation substance is direct:

*Also unto thee, O Adonai (Lord), belongeth mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work.* (Psalm 62:12)

And the apostolic-restatement:

*Who will render to every man according to his deeds: To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality, eternal life: But unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, indignation and wrath.* (Romans 2:6-8)

The works the kingdom-citizens are being warned about are the fruit-of-the-new-heart. The new-heart the prophet named at Ezekiel 36:26-27 is given to the willing — *a new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you ... and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* The walking is the fruit. The reward is according to the fruit. The kingdom-citizens are not earning the kingdom-entry by their works; the kingdom-citizens are evidencing the new-heart's-Torah-substance by the works that flow from it. *I never knew you, depart from me, ye that work iniquity* of Matthew 7:23 is spoken to the wheat-whose-fruit-failed, not to the tares. The tares are bound and burned at the harvest; the wheat-whose-fruit-failed is sifted at the post-harvest evaluation. The kingdom-citizens carry both the inheritance and the warning.

*Verily I say unto you, There be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom.* The chapter's-closing saying. The phrase has produced two centuries of Christian-commentary-controversy. Hold it carefully.

The institutional-Christian-tradition has proposed three main readings. The preterist-reading takes the saying as referring to the AD 70 destruction of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) and the temple — the *some standing here* lived to see the destruction, and the destruction was the Son-of-Adam coming-in-the-kingdom in judgment. The reading does not stand against the prophets' day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) architecture. AD 70 was the destruction of the temple; AD 70 was not the day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) the prophets named, the dreadful-day with the resurrection, the gathering of the elect from the four winds, the visible coming on the clouds with all the holy angels, the kingdom-reign-on-the-earth. The day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) has not substantially fulfilled. The preterist-reading is the institutional-Christian-attempt to handle the apparent-near-term-promise of the saying by collapsing the eschatology into a past-event. The prophetic-eschatology does not collapse.

The eschatological-second-coming-reading takes the saying as referring to the second-coming itself, with the *some standing here* still alive (in some form) at the consummation. The reading is compatible with the prophetic-eschatology but does not match the immediate-narrative-context of the gospel — Matthew 17 opens *and after six days* with the transfiguration immediately following.

The transfiguration-foretaste-reading takes the saying as referring to the transfiguration that is six days away in the narrative. Matthew 17:1-8 will record the King taking Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) up the high mountain. The King is transfigured before them — his face shines as the sun, his garments become white as the light. Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) appear with him. A cloud overshadows them. The Father's-voice speaks: *This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.* Three of the disciples — *some standing here* — see the King in the transfigured-glory that pre-figures the second-coming-glory. The transfiguration is the foretaste; the second-coming is the substance the foretaste anticipates. And Kefa (Peter)-himself confirms this reading in his own apostolic-letter decades later:

*For we have not followed cunningly devised fables, when we made known unto you the power and coming of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ), but were eyewitnesses of his majesty. For he received from Elohim (God) the Father honour and glory, when there came such a voice to him from the excellent Glory, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. And this voice which came from heaven we heard, when we were with him in the holy mount.* (2 Peter 1:16-18)

Kefa (Peter) is naming the transfiguration as the substance of *the power and coming of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ).* Kefa (Peter)-himself reads the transfiguration as the eyewitness-foretaste of the King's-coming. The *some standing here* saying reads with the transfiguration as the immediate-narrative-fulfillment and the broader eschatological-second-coming-substance as the larger-resonance the foretaste was pointing toward. The bridge into the next chapter is what the saying lays.

The chapter that opened with the Pharisees-and-Sadducees demanding a sign from heaven closes with the King promising that three of the disciples would see the foretaste of the coming the system would not see at all. The credentialing-system asked for a sign on its terms and was refused; the disciples were given a sign on the King's-terms that the system would never have recognized as authenticating. The credential of the resurrection — the sign-of-Yonah — is being prefigured to three of the disciples on the mountain six days away. Matthew 17 will open with the King taking Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) up to the high mountain. The transfiguration. The cloud. The Father's-voice. Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah). The foretaste of the coming-in-the-kingdom the King has just promised. The chapter that explicitly announced the cross will be followed by the chapter that explicitly displays the glory the cross opens onto.
