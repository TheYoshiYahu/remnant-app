## §1. The Pharisees-and-Sadducees-tempting + the sign-of-Yonah-refused-again

*The Pharisees also with the Sadducees came, and tempting desired him that he would shew them a sign from heaven. He answered and said unto them, When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red. And in the morning, It will be foul weather to day: for the sky is red and lowring. O ye hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky; but can ye not discern the signs of the times? A wicked and adulterous generation seeketh after a sign; and there shall no sign be given unto it, but the sign of the prophet Yonah (Jonah). And he left them, and departed* (vv.1-4).

**The framework reads vv.1-4 in framework-substance — the Pharisaic-and-Sadducean confederation against the King intensifying; the sign-of-Yonah refusal restated.**

**The Pharisees-and-Sadducees-together.** The framework reads the alliance carefully. The two factions are theologically-opposed at multiple-points (the Pharisees affirm-the-resurrection-and-angels-and-the-Tanakh-oral-tradition; the Sadducees deny-the-resurrection-and-angels-and-hold-only-the-Mosaic-five-books per the Tanakh-and-Josephus accounts) — yet they are united-in-their-opposition-to-the-King. The framework reads this in continuity with Tehillim (Psalm) 2:1-2's *the kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against Yahuah (the LORD), and against his anointed.* The opposition-to-the-Messianic-substance unites factions otherwise-opposed; the seed-war-pattern operates at the system-level despite the system's internal-divisions.

**The sign-from-heaven request.** The Pharisees-and-Sadducees demand-a-sign — the same credential-on-demand pattern of Matt 12:38-39. The Tanakh-pattern: Mosheh's-rod-and-the-Tanakh-signs at the Exodus (Shemot (Exodus) 4 + 7-11); Eliyahu's-fire-from-heaven at Carmel (1 Melakhim (Kings) 18:36-38); the sign-substance the system would receive as authenticating-substance.

The framework reads the King's-refusal in framework-substance:

- **The signs already given have-been-rejected.** Matt 8-9's healings, Matt 12's exorcisms-and-restored-hand, Matt 14's-feeding-and-walking-on-the-sea — the credentialing-substance has-been-given. The system asks for MORE-and-on-its-own-terms.
- **The discerning-the-sky-but-not-the-signs-of-the-times charge** (vv.2-3). The framework reads the King's-charge as the system's-disposition-of-selective-discernment — capable-of-natural-discernment, incapable-of-recognizing-the-Messianic-substance because the disposition-against-the-King has already-set per the cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnostic of Matt 13:13-15.
- **The sign-of-Yonah restated** (v.4). The framework reads the restatement of Matt 12:39-40 — the resurrection-credential will be the sign-given to the wicked-and-adulterous-generation; the framework's reading per `sign-of-yonah-resurrection-credential` thread (PROPOSED at Matt 12) carries.
- **The King's-departure** (v.4b). *And he left them, and departed.* The framework reads the departure-substance in continuity with `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VII's *sit out, speak up* posture. The King speaks-the-truth-and-departs; the system's-disposition is set; the King does not engage-the-system-on-its-own-credentialing-terms.

## §2. The leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees — *their doctrine*

*And when his disciples were come to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread. Then Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees. And they reasoned among themselves, saying, It is because we have taken no bread. Which when Yahusha (Jesus) perceived, he said unto them, O ye of little faith, why reason ye among yourselves, because ye have brought no bread? Do ye not yet understand, neither remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets ye took up? Neither the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many baskets ye took up? How is it that ye understand not that I spake it not to you concerning bread, that ye should beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees? Then understood they how that he bade them not beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees* (vv.5-12).

**The framework reads vv.5-12 in framework-substance — the King's-warning of the leaven explicitly identified as the DOCTRINE (didachē) of the Pharisees-and-Sadducees.**

**The bread-forgotten and the leaven-warning** (vv.5-7). The disciples have-forgotten-bread; the King's-warning *beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees* the disciples mis-hear at the physical-bread level. The framework reads the disciples' mis-hearing in framework-substance — the disciples are still-learning the parable-and-figurative substance; the King will gently-correct.

**The King's-correction and the explicit-identification** (vv.8-12). The King reminds-the-disciples of the two-feedings (the five-thousand with twelve-baskets-left + the four-thousand with seven-baskets-left) — the bread-substance is not the concern when the King is present. The disciples *then understood* that the warning is about the DOCTRINE (*didachē*) of the Pharisees-and-Sadducees, not about leavened-bread.

**The framework reads the explicit-identification as the load-bearing scripture-anchor for the `leaven-as-corruption-substance` thread (PROPOSED at Matt 13:33).** The framework's reading of Matt 13:33's leaven-parable is confirmed here at the gospel-explicit-level: the leaven is THE DOCTRINE OF THE SYSTEM. The Pharisaic-leaven (the tradition-overriding-Torah pattern of Matt 15:1-9) AND the Sadducean-leaven (the rejection-of-the-resurrection-and-the-angels per Matt 22:23-33 etc.) are both DOCTRINAL-CORRUPTIONS-OF-THE-COVENANT-SUBSTANCE.

The framework reads this in continuity with the broader-framework-architecture:

- The Pharisaic-leaven and the Sadducean-leaven are different-doctrinal-corruptions but the SAME-architectural-substance — corruptions-of-the-covenant-substance that operate from-within the religious-system.
- The framework reads the leaven-warning as the King's-pastoral-substance for the disciples (and the kingdom-citizens-throughout-the-age). The disciples are taught to discern the doctrinal-substance, not merely the visible-credentialing.
- The framework reads this in continuity with the framework's-modern-Judaizer-architecture per the voice skill's framework-deep. The leaven-of-the-system operates today as the institutional-Christian-doctrine that subtracts-the-Torah-substance, names-Torah-as-bondage, replaces-the-Shabbat-with-Sunday, replaces-the-feasts-with-Babylonian-festivals. The framework reads the modern-Judaizer-doctrine as the same-architectural-substance the King warned-against in different-costume.

## §3. The Caesarea-Philippi confession — the framework-major moment

*When Yahusha (Jesus) came into the coasts of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, saying, Whom do men say that I the Son of Adam am? And they said, Some say that thou art Yochanan (John) the Immerser: some, Eliyahu (Elijah); and others, Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah), or one of the prophets. He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am? And Shim'on (Simon) Kefa (Peter) answered and said, Thou art the Messiah, the Son of the living Elohim (God). And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Shim'on (Simon) Bar-Yonah (Bar-Jona): for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven. And I say also unto thee, That thou art Kefa (Peter), and upon this rock I will build my assembly; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven. Then charged he his disciples that they should tell no man that he was Yahusha (Jesus) the Messiah* (vv.13-20).

**The framework reads vv.13-20 as the chapter's FRAMEWORK-MAJOR moment — the Caesarea-Philippi confession with the rock-and-keys-and-assembly substance.**

**The Caesarea-Philippi setting.** The framework reads the setting carefully. Caesarea-Philippi (the Banias / Paneas-region at the foot of Mount Hermon, north of the Galilee, the territory of Philip-the-tetrarch) was the location of:

- A major-spring-source of the Yarden (Jordan) river (one of the headwaters);
- A Hellenistic-shrine-and-grotto dedicated to the Greek-god Pan (the *Paneion* — hence Paneas, hence Banias);
- The Tanakh-substance of Dan / Laish from Shofetim (Judges) 18 — the territory of Dan's-northern-resettlement and the early-Yashar'el (Israel)-idolatry pattern (Dan's-graven-image at Shofetim (Judges) 18:30-31);
- A massive-rock-face-cliff with the cave-grotto, and a Herodian-temple-of-Augustus built nearby.

The framework reads the setting as deliberate. The King takes-the-disciples to a location surrounded-by-pagan-credentialing (the Pan-shrine), Hellenistic-political-credentialing (the Augustus-temple), and Tanakh-idolatry-memory (the Dan-resettlement). At this location of competing-credentialings, the King asks the credential-question.

**The credential-question and the multitudes' speculations** (vv.13-14). *Whom do men say that I the Son of Adam am?* The framework reads the question carefully:

- *Son of Adam* per Red Line #12 — restored from *son of man* — the lineage-weight of the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise preserved.
- The multitudes' speculations (Yochanan (John) the Immerser, Eliyahu, Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah), or one of the prophets) demonstrate the credentialing-substance is recognized at the prophet-pattern level but the Messianic-substance is not yet recognized at the categorical-substance.
- Herod-Antipas's-Yochanan (John)-resurrected speculation (Matt 14:2) is one of these; the broader-multitudinous-speculation-pattern reflects the variety of recognitions-without-the-categorical-recognition of the King-as-the-Messiah.

**Kefa's (Peter's) confession** (v.16). *Thou art the Messiah, the Son of the living Elohim (God).* The framework reads the confession in framework-substance:

- **The Messiah.** *Christos* = the Anointed = the Tanakh-Messianic substance of the Davidic-line + the Aaronic-priestly-anointing-substance + the prophetic-mission-substance. The King is THE-Messiah (the definite-article in the Greek: *ho Christos*), not A-messiah.
- **The Son of the living Elohim (God).** The framework reads this in continuity with the framework's nature-of-God reading. The King-is-the-Son-of-the-Formless-Source per Matt 11:25-27's Father-Son-revelation-economy. The Son-of-the-living-Elohim is the Formed-Expression-of-the-Formless-Source.
- **The confession's depth.** The framework reads Kefa's (Peter's) confession as the first-explicit-categorical confession-of-the-King-as-the-Messiah-and-the-Son-of-Elohim by a disciple. The Matt 14:33 preliminary-confession (*of a truth thou art the Son of Elohim (God)*) is pre-figured here in deeper-explicit-substance. The framework reads the disciples' confession-trajectory in continuity with the cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11 — Kefa (Peter) confesses because-he-has-been-given-to-hear-and-see, not because-he-has-figured-it-out.

**The King's-blessing of Kefa (Peter)** (v.17). *Blessed art thou, Shim'on (Simon) Bar-Yonah (Bar-Jona): for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.*

The framework reads the blessing in framework-substance:

- **The Father's-revelation precedes Kefa's (Peter's)-confession.** The framework's-cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11 is explicit. The Father has-revealed-the-substance to Kefa (Peter); Kefa (Peter)'s-confession is the response-to-the-revelation-already-given.
- **The flesh-and-blood-NOT-revelation substance.** The framework reads this in continuity with the Father-Son-revelation-economy of Matt 11:25-27. The mysteries-of-the-kingdom are revealed-by-the-Father; the flesh-and-blood reasoning-substance does NOT-produce-the-revelation. The framework reads this against the inherited-Reformed-monergistic-determinism's-collapse and against the Arminian-self-determinative-libertarian-freedom's-collapse — the Father's-revelation precedes; the actor's-response follows; the architecture is preserved.

**The rock-and-the-assembly declaration** (v.18). *And I say also unto thee, That thou art Kefa (Peter), and upon this rock I will build my assembly; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.*

**The framework reads this declaration in framework-substance with significant-attention to the rock-question.**

- **The Greek wordplay.** *Sy ei Petros, kai epi tautē tē petra oikodomēsō mou tēn ekklēsian.* Kefa (Peter) = *Petros* (masculine, a stone); *this rock* = *petra* (feminine, a rock-mass or a rocky outcrop). The framework reads the wordplay carefully.
- **The framework's reading of the rock-substance.** The framework reads the rock as the King-himself, NOT-as-Kefa (Peter)-as-the-papal-foundation. The framework's reasoning:
  - The Caesarea-Philippi setting itself has a massive-rock-face-cliff (the Paneion's-grotto rock). The King-pointing-toward-this-visible-rock would have-been-the-natural-physical-gesture; the King says *upon this rock* not *upon thee.*
  - The Tanakh-substance of *the Rock* (*ha-tsur*) is consistently Yahuah (the LORD) himself. Devarim (Deuteronomy) 32:4 — *he is the Rock, his work is perfect.* Tehillim (Psalm) 18:2 — *Yahuah (the LORD) is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my Elohim (God), my strength, in whom I will trust.* Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 17:10. Tehillim (Psalm) 95:1. The Tanakh-substance of the Rock is divine-substance.
  - The framework's-Christological-architecture per the framework's nature-of-God reading. The King is the Formed-Voice-of-Sinai per the framework's reading; the Tanakh's-Rock-substance is the Formed-Voice's-substance; the King-as-the-Rock is the consistent-Tanakh-architecture.
  - 1 Korinthians (Corinthians) 10:4 — *that Rock was the Messiah* — Paul's apostolic-restatement; the Tanakh-wilderness-Rock-from-which-the-water-flowed is identified-as-the-Messiah-with-Yashar'el (Israel)-in-the-wilderness. The same architecture operates at Matt 16:18.
  - 1 Kefa (Peter) 2:4-8 — Kefa (Peter)'s-own-letter identifies the King as the *living stone* and the *chief corner stone*; Kefa (Peter)'s-own-reading positions the King-as-the-foundation-stone, not Kefa-as-the-foundation. The framework reads this as Kefa (Peter)'s-own-disclaiming of any reading-that-makes-himself-the-foundation.
  - Ephesians 2:20 — *built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Yahusha (Jesus) the Messiah (Christ) himself being the chief corner stone.* The framework reads this as the apostolic-restatement of the same architecture.
- **The framework rules out the inherited-Roman-Catholic reading that the rock = Kefa (Peter)-as-the-first-pope.** The inherited-Roman-Catholic-reading collapses the wordplay's-substance and produces the architecture of papal-primacy-and-magisterial-authority. The framework reads this as the institutional-Christian-system's-construction that the Tanakh-substance and the broader-apostolic-witness rule out.
- **The Petros-Petra wordplay's framework-reading.** The framework reads Kefa (Peter)'s-being-named-a-stone (*Petros*) in continuity with Kefa (Peter)'s-being-IN-the-rock (the Tanakh-Rock-substance of the King). Kefa (Peter) is a-stone in the assembly being-built-upon-the-Rock-of-the-King; Kefa (Peter) is not himself the Rock. The framework's-reading preserves Kefa (Peter)'s-honored-position as a-stone-and-apostle without collapsing the framework's-Christological-substance.

**The assembly substance.** *My assembly* = *mou tēn ekklēsian.* The framework reads *ekklēsia* in framework-substance:

- *Ekklēsia* = the called-out-ones. The Greek substance is the assembly-of-the-citizens called-out-of-their-private-affairs-to-the-public-gathering.
- The LXX-substance of *ekklēsia* = the Hebrew *qahal* — the assembly-of-Yashar'el (Israel), the covenant-people-gathered (Devarim (Deuteronomy) 23:1-8 the *qahal Yahuah*; Yehoshua (Joshua) 8:35 the *qahal Yashar'el*; Tehillim (Psalm) 22:22 = Hebrews 2:12's *in the midst of the assembly will I sing praise unto thee*).
- The framework reads the King's-assembly substance in continuity with the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed thesis. The *ekklēsia* is the called-out-ones of the gathering — the lost-sheep-of-the-house-of-Yashar'el (Israel) gathered-to-the-King-and-the-substance-of-the-kingdom. The framework rules out reading the *ekklēsia* as the inherited-Christian *church* — a metaphorical-Yashar'el (Israel)-substance that replaces the prophetic-twelve-tribes-gathering. The framework reads the *ekklēsia* as the covenant-assembly being-gathered through-the-King's-kingdom-proclamation, in continuity with the Tanakh's *qahal*-substance.

**The gates-of-hell substance.** *Gates of hell shall not prevail against it.*

The framework reads the gates-of-hell substance carefully:

- **The Greek *pylai hadou*** = the gates of *Hades*. The framework reads *Hades* in the Tanakh-substance of *Sheol* — the underworld-grave-realm-of-the-dead, NOT the institutional-Christian-eternal-conscious-torment-hell of inherited Christian-doctrine.
- **The Tanakh-substance of *Sheol*** at Bereshit (Genesis) 37:35, Iyov (Job) 7:9, Tehillim (Psalm) 16:10, Tehillim (Psalm) 86:13. The *Sheol*-substance is the grave-and-the-underworld-realm.
- **The gates-of-Sheol-substance** in the Tanakh imagery (Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 38:10's *the gates of the grave (sheol)*; Tehillim (Psalm) 9:13's *thou that liftest me up from the gates of death*; Iyov (Job) 38:17's *have the gates of death been opened unto thee?*). The gates-of-Sheol carry-the-power-of-death-and-the-grave.
- **The framework's reading.** The gates-of-Sheol shall-not-prevail-against the assembly the King is building. The framework reads this in continuity with the resurrection-substance — the King will conquer-death-and-the-grave through-the-resurrection (the sign-of-Yonah pre-announced at Matt 12:39-40 and Matt 16:4); the assembly built-on-the-Rock-of-the-King will partake-of-the-resurrection-substance. The gates-of-death-and-the-grave will not hold-the-assembly; the resurrection-of-the-first-resurrection per `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §V's remnant-substance + framework-deep's Adam-and-the-Tares architecture operate.
- **The framework rules out the institutional-Christian-reading** that interprets the gates-of-hell as militaristic-imagery-of-Satanic-attack-against-the-church-and-the-church's-evangelistic-conquest. The framework reads the imagery in Tanakh-substance — the gates-of-the-grave do not hold the resurrection-substance the assembly partakes-of through-the-King.

**The keys-of-the-kingdom and the binding-and-loosing substance** (v.19). *And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.*

The framework reads the keys-substance in framework-substance:

- **The Tanakh-substance of the keys.** Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 22:22 — *the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open.* The Tanakh-substance of the keys is the steward's-substance — the authority-given-to-the-steward to open-and-shut on-behalf-of-the-master. The framework reads this in continuity with Matt 18:18's-extension of the binding-and-loosing-authority to-the-assembly-as-a-whole (not Kefa (Peter)-exclusively).
- **The binding-and-loosing substance.** The Tanakh-rabbinic-substance of *asar* (bind) and *hattir* (loose) operates in the rabbinic-decision-making about what is forbidden (bound) and permitted (loosed) under the Torah's-substance. The framework reads the apostolic-binding-and-loosing-substance as the apostolic-authority to declare-the-Torah's-substance against-the-Pharisaic-tradition's-corruption. The apostles (and the kingdom-assembly through-them) carry-the-authority to-declare-what-the-Torah's-substance-binds-and-loosens.
- **The framework rules out the inherited-Roman-Catholic reading** that constructs the binding-and-loosing as the magisterium's-authority-to-define-doctrine-against-scripture. The framework reads the binding-and-loosing in continuity with the Torah's-substance, not against it.

**The disciples'-instruction-to-tell-no-man** (v.20). The framework reads the instruction in framework-substance — the King's-Messianic-secret pattern operates at this stage of the ministry. The system is conspiring; the public-proclamation of the Messianic-categorical-recognition would-accelerate-the-conflict-trajectory before the King's-time. ## §4. The first-passion-prediction and Kefa's (Peter's) rebuke

*From that time forth began Yahusha (Jesus) to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day. Then Kefa (Peter) took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee. But he turned, and said unto Kefa (Peter), Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of Elohim (God), but those that be of men* (vv.21-23).

**The framework reads vv.21-23 as the first-passion-prediction of the gospel.**

**The *must* (*dei*) substance** (v.21). *How that he MUST go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem).* The framework reads the *dei* substance carefully. The Greek *dei* names a divine-necessity — what-must-happen by the Father's-purpose. The framework reads this in continuity with the Tanakh-prophetic-substance:

- The Tanakh-substance of the suffering-servant of Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 53 — the architecture of the substitutionary-suffering the framework reads at the King's-passion.
- The Tanakh-substance of the smitten-shepherd of Zecharyahu (Zechariah) 13:7 — the King-cites this at Matt 26:31.
- The Tanakh-substance of the pierced-one of Zecharyahu (Zechariah) 12:10 — the second-coming-substance carried forward to the resurrected-King's-second-coming.

**The framework reads the King's-suffering-and-killing-and-raising substance in framework-register, holding-the-architecture of grace-and-the-opportunity-that-can-be-lost per the voice skill's framework-deep:**

- The cross opened the door-home for the scattered seed who walked-into-the-curse by walking-contrary-to-him.

- Romans 6:14 (*not under law but under grace*) and Romans 6:15 (*shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? Elohim (God) forbid*) are one breath; the Reformation's-truncation at v.14 is rejected.

The framework reads the first-passion-prediction in this architectural-substance. The King's-suffering opens-the-door-home through-the-Deuteronomy-21:23 curse-of-the-tree (per Galatians 3:13's framework-reading); the door-substance is real; the consequence-system continues in the present-age until-the-eschatological-consummation per `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §IV.
