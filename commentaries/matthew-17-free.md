# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 17

Matthew 17 is the chapter where the foretaste of Matthew 16:28 lands six days later on the high mountain. Five movements anchor the chapter. (1) The transfiguration (vv.1-8). The King takes Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) up an unnamed *high mountain apart* (the Sinai-and-Horeb theophany pattern, not a fixed geographic identification) and is transfigured before them — *his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light* (v.2). The Greek *metemorphōthē* names the visible-form-change: the Formed-essence beneath the flesh-veil becomes momentarily visible. The architecture is the gospel's deepest window into the nature of the One who came in the flesh — the Father is the Formless source (*no man hath seen Elohim (God) at any time,* John 1:18); the Son is the Formed expression of the Formless drawn-into-form (the God-of-the-Tanakh interactions, the One who *emptied himself, taking the form of a servant,* Philippians 2:7); the transfiguration pulls the servant-form-veil aside so the disciples see the form-of-Elohim (God) it had clothed. The face-shining substance differs from Mosheh's (Moses') face shining at Exodus 34:29 — Mosheh's face reflected glory and faded (2 Corinthians 3:13); the King's face emanates glory inherently. The visual-substance recurs at Daniel 7:9's Ancient-of-Days, Daniel 10:5-6's Man-Clothed-in-Linen, and Revelation 1:13-16's *one like unto the Son of Adam* (the kaph-comparative preserved and the named Standard capitalized — Yochanan (John) is the disciple at both the transfiguration AND the Patmos-vision; the substance is the same Formed-One). Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) appear *talking with him* about the exodus the King would *accomplish at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)* (Luke 9:31) — the Torah-witness and Prophets-witness testifying-to the King's coming-passion. The forefathers remain asleep awaiting the resurrection (Hebrews 11:39-40); the transfiguration is vision-substance, not the first-resurrection itself. Kefa's (Peter's) *three tabernacles* proposal carries the Sukkot (Tabernacles)-resonance (Zechariah 14:16's Messianic-age-feast; John 1:14's *the Word was made flesh, and dwelt (eskēnōsen — tabernacled) among us*) — the instinct is right, the architecture is wrong because the three are not co-equal. The bright-cloud is the *kavod* (glory)-cloud of Sinai (Exodus 24:16-18), the Tabernacle's-dedication (Exodus 40:34-35), and the Temple's-dedication (1 Kings 8:10-11) — the Formless-Father's-presence-marker. The Father's-voice combines three Tanakh-Messianic-witnesses in one utterance: *this is my beloved Son* (Psalm 2:7's Davidic-Royal-Son), *in whom I am well pleased* (Isaiah 42:1's Suffering-Servant — same combined-citation as Matthew 3:17), *hear ye him* (Deuteronomy 18:15's prophet-like-unto-Mosheh, restated apostolically at Acts 3:22-23 and Acts 7:37). The Father's *hear ye him* corrects Kefa (Peter)'s three-tabernacles directly — Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) are witnesses; the King is the substance the witnesses point toward. The disciples' fall-on-the-face is the universal Tanakh-theophany-response (Isaiah 6:5, Ezekiel 1:28, Daniel 10:9, Revelation 1:17); the King's-touch-and-*arise-and-be-not-afraid* is the Tanakh-heavenly-messenger-pattern the King occupies because he IS the One the messengers served. *Yahusha (Jesus) only* (v.8) is the final correction: the King is the tabernacle of John 1:14 — one tabernacle suffices. (2) The Eliyahu-is-come-already explicit-statement (vv.9-13). The Messianic-secret continues — *tell the vision to no man, until the Son of Adam be risen* (v.9); Kefa (Peter) will name the transfiguration only post-resurrection at 2 Peter 1:16-19. The disciples' scribal-eschatology question (*why then say the scribes that Eliyahu (Elijah) must first come?*) is answered with both-fulfillments: future-tense *Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things* (Revelation 11's two-witnesses + Acts 3:21's *times of restitution of all things*); past-tense *Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed* (v.12) — Yochanan (John) the Immerser as the Eliyahu-pattern-prophet at the first-coming per Malachi 4:5-6 and Matthew 11:14, with Herod-Antipas and Herodias as the Achav (Ahab) and Izevel (Jezebel) of the first-century recapitulation. *Likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them* ties the King's-coming-passion to the Eliyahu-pattern-prophet's-martyrdom. (3) The demoniac-boy at the foot of the mountain and the mustard-seed-faith teaching (vv.14-21). The disciples could not cast out the demon despite the Matthew 10:1 / 10:8 commission and previous-mission-successes (Mark 6:13, Luke 9:6). The King's-rebuke of *faithless and perverse generation* echoes Mosheh's (Moses') song against the wilderness-generation at Deuteronomy 32:5, 20 — the rebuke is generation-wide, not narrowly disciple-aimed. The King exorcises immediately. The disciples' *unbelief* (*oligopistia* — little-faith, the same word at Matthew 14:31 and 16:8) is the obstacle. The mustard-seed-faith of Matthew 13:31-32's-smallest-of-seeds-with-explosive-potential names a qualitative substance — faith of the right substance, even small in quantity, against faith of wrong substance regardless of quantity. The mountain-moving substance carries the Tanakh-mountains-tremble-at-the-presence-of-Yahuah (the LORD) pattern (Psalm 46:2, Isaiah 40:4, Isaiah 64:1) — kingdom-impossibilities have a different floor because the kingdom is the present-incursion of the Creator. The inherited-Christian-prosperity-gospel detachment of *nothing shall be impossible* from the kingdom-mission substance does not stand against the textual-substance — faith without love-substance is *nothing* (1 Corinthians 13:2). *Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting* (v.21) — fasting remains a kingdom-substance-discipline (the King taught it at Matthew 6:16-18; the apostolic-assembly continued it at Acts 13:2-3 and Acts 14:23); the inherited-Christian-collapse of fasting into optional-or-abolished does not stand against the text. (4) The second passion-prediction (vv.22-23). *The Son of Adam shall be betrayed (paradidotai — delivered-up, the Isaiah 53 Suffering-Servant verb) into the hands of men: and they shall kill him, and the third day he shall be raised again.* The threefold-prediction pattern (Matthew 16:21, 17:22-23, 20:17-19) produces a disciples' progression — active-rebuke first, *exceeding sorry* passive-grief second, confused-thrones-request third. The disciples' arc of learning the *must* (*dei*) of the passion runs across all three. (5) The temple-tax / Kefa-fishing-the-coin pericope (vv.24-27). The half-shekel temple-tax is Exodus 30:11-15's atonement-money (*to make an atonement for your souls*) — Tabernacle-collection at Exodus 38:25-28, temple-repair under Yoash (Joash) at 2 Kings 12:4-16, third-of-a-shekel post-exile at Nehemiah 10:32-33, re-established annual collection by the first century. The King's-question *of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers?* applies the Tanakh-monarchic-precedent: kings do not tax their own sons. *Then are the children free* names the categorical-architecture — the King is the Son of the One whose Temple this is; the Son and those-whom-the-Son-makes-brethren (Hebrews 2:11, John 20:17's *my Father, and your Father*) are categorically-free of the Father's-tax to the Father's-house. *Notwithstanding, lest we should offend them* — the Greek *skandalisōmen* — names the lest-we-cause-stumbling-block disposition the apostolic-witness will spell out at 1 Corinthians 8:13, 1 Corinthians 10:33, Romans 14:13 — kingdom-freedom exercised so it does not become a stumbling-block to those-still-coming. The miraculous coin-in-the-fish's-mouth (*statēr* = four-drachmas = exactly two half-shekels for the King and Kefa (Peter), no remainder) is the Owner-of-the-sea's-sovereign-direction — the Adamic-dominion of Psalm 8:6-8 and the Yonah (Jonah)-pattern of Jonah 1:17's-great-fish-appointed. The half-shekel-as-atonement-money pre-figures the cross: the categorically-exempt King pays the atonement-substance the categorically-bound owe, demonstrating in miniature what Hebrews 7:27's *this he did once, when he offered up himself* will complete at full-scale. The chapter is one motion across the glory-on-the-mountain and the half-shekel-from-the-fish's-mouth; the glory is what the cross opens onto, and the cross is what the categorically-exempt One walks into for the sake of the categorically-bound. Matthew 18 will open with the disciples asking *who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?* — and the King will set a little child in their midst.
