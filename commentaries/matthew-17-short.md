# Short-form commentary on Matthew 17

Matthew 17 is the chapter where the foretaste promised at the close of the previous chapter lands. The *some standing here* of Matthew 16:28 see the Son of Adam in his glory after six days. The transfiguration on the high mountain — the King's face shining as the sun, his garments white as the light, Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) appearing, the bright cloud overshadowing, the Father's-voice declaring *this is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him* — is the gospel's deepest window into the nature of the God who came in the flesh. The chapter that opened with the visible glory closes with the King paying a half-shekel for himself and Kefa (Peter) from a coin pulled from a fish's mouth. Five movements anchor the chapter. The transfiguration on the high mountain with the Father's-voice combining three Tanakh witnesses in one utterance (vv.1-8). The coming-down-from-the-mountain charge to silence-until-the-resurrection and the King's explicit identification of Yochanan (John) the Immerser as the Eliyahu (Elijah) who was to come (vv.9-13). The demoniac-boy the disciples could not heal and the King's mustard-seed-faith teaching with the prayer-and-fasting saying (vv.14-21). The second passion-prediction (vv.22-23). The temple-tax pericope with the *children are free* substance and the coin from the fish's mouth (vv.24-27).

*And after six days Yahusha (Jesus) taketh Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) his brother, and bringeth them up into an high mountain apart, and was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light. And, behold, there appeared unto them Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) talking with him. Then answered Kefa (Peter), and said unto Yahusha (Jesus), Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Mosheh (Moses), and one for Eliyahu (Elijah). While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them: and behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him. And when the disciples heard it, they fell on their face, and were sore afraid. And Yahusha (Jesus) came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid. And when they had lifted up their eyes, they saw no man, save Yahusha (Jesus) only.* (Matthew 17:1-8)

*After six days.* The narrative-link is precise. Matthew 16:28 closed with the King's promise that *there be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom.* Six days later — the seventh day, in the count that includes the day of speaking — the King takes three of those standing-there up the mountain. The foretaste-of-the-coming is what the three are about to see. The eschatological-substance the saying anticipates carries forward to the second-coming-in-glory the prophets named at the dreadful-day-of-Yahuah (the LORD); the immediate-narrative-fulfillment is the transfiguration that opens this chapter. Both are real. The transfiguration does not exhaust the saying; the transfiguration is the foretaste the saying promised.

The six-days-and-the-seventh resonance carries Tanakh-substance. The Sinai pattern is the closest:

*And the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) abode upon mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days: and the seventh day he called unto Mosheh (Moses) out of the midst of the cloud. And the sight of the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) was like devouring fire on the top of the mount in the eyes of the children of Yashar'el (Israel). And Mosheh (Moses) went into the midst of the cloud, and gat him up into the mount.* (Exodus 24:16-18)

The cloud on Sinai for six days, the voice on the seventh, Mosheh (Moses) going up into the cloud — the architecture is the same architecture the disciples are about to walk into. The Sinai-theophany pattern recurs at the mountain of the transfiguration, with the substance now made visible at a deeper level: the One who spoke from the cloud at Sinai is the One standing on this mountain in flesh, with the Father's-voice from the cloud declaring his identity.

*Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John).* The three of the inner circle. The same three are present at the raising of Yair's (Jairus's) daughter (Mark 5:37) and at Gethsemane (Matthew 26:37). The Tanakh-standard for testimony is *at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established* (Deuteronomy 19:15). Three witnesses to what the eye cannot otherwise have seen and the mouth alone cannot otherwise have established. The Catholic-Petrine-papacy reading that elevates Kefa (Peter) alone to a primacy-of-the-witness over the others does not stand against the textual-substance — the three witness together, and each of the three will bear apostolic-testimony to what was seen. Yochanan (John) will write the gospel that opens *the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth* (John 1:14). Kefa (Peter) will write *we were eyewitnesses of his majesty* (2 Peter 1:16). Ya'aqov (James) will die under Herod-Agrippa at Acts 12:2 carrying what he had seen on the mountain.

The mountain is unnamed in the text. The traditional-Christian identification with Mount Tabor is conjectural; the textual-emphasis is on the *high mountain apart* pattern that the Tanakh repeatedly names as the place of theophany — Sinai for Mosheh (Moses), Horeb for Eliyahu (Elijah) at *the still small voice* (1 Kings 19:8-13), Tsion (Zion) for the prophets — not on a particular geographic point. The disciples are taken to the high mountain apart for the same reason the prophets were: the substance about to be displayed requires the high-place-set-apart for its showing.

*And was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light.* The Greek is *metemorphōthē* — his form-was-changed in their sight. The veil-of-the-flesh is pulled back. The Formed-essence the King had laid aside in the incarnation per the apostle's word — *Who, being in the form of Elohim (God), thought it not robbery to be equal with Elohim (God): But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men* (Philippians 2:6-7) — becomes visible on the mountain for the three disciples to see. The King has always been the Formed-Voice of Yahuah (the LORD); the flesh has been the veil. On the mountain, the veil is momentarily pulled aside, and the disciples see what has been there all along.

Hold the architecture of who-the-King-is in the substance the Tanakh names. The Father is the formless infinite — the Source, the unseen, the One whom *no man hath seen at any time* (John 1:18). The Son is the Formed expression of the formless Source — the One who appeared to Avraham (Abraham) at Mamre, the One who wrestled Ya'aqov (Jacob) at Penuel, the One who spoke from the burning bush to Mosheh (Moses), the One who led Yashar'el (Israel) through the wilderness in the pillar of cloud and fire, the One who came down on Sinai and made the mountain shake. The God of the Tanakh interactions is the Son — the Formed-Voice through whom all things were made. *In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with Elohim (God), and the Word was Elohim (God). The same was in the beginning with Elohim (God). All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made* (John 1:1-3). The Son came in the flesh, took the form of a servant, walked the dust of the earth. On the high mountain, the form of the servant is briefly transparent to the form of Elohim (God) that the servant-form had clothed.

The face-shining substance is the Tanakh-glory pattern. When Mosheh (Moses) came down from Sinai, his face shone:

*And it came to pass, when Mosheh (Moses) came down from mount Sinai with the two tables of testimony in Mosheh's (Moses') hand, when he came down from the mount, that Mosheh (Moses) wist not that the skin of his face shone while he talked with him.* (Exodus 34:29)

Mosheh's (Moses') face shone by reflection. The glory had been near him, and the face caught it as a face catches sunlight. The shining faded. The apostle Paul will name the fading at 2 Corinthians 3:13. The King's face on the mountain does not shine by reflection. The King's face shines because the King is the source. The light comes from inside the King outward, not from outside the King reflected. The same kind of difference the prophet Daniel will name in his vision of the One who stands among the lampstands:

*I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire.* (Daniel 7:9)

And the vision the prophet Daniel sees of the Man-Clothed-in-Linen:

*Then I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a certain man clothed in linen, whose loins were girded with fine gold of Uphaz: His body also was like the beryl, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.* (Daniel 10:5-6)

And the vision the apostle Yochanan (John) will have on Patmos decades after he stood on this mountain:

*And in the midst of the seven candlesticks one like unto the Son of Adam, clothed with a garment down to the foot, and girt about the paps with a golden girdle. His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire; And his feet like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace; and his voice as the sound of many waters. And he had in his right hand seven stars: and out of his mouth went a sharp twoedged sword: and his countenance was as the sun shineth in his strength.* (Revelation 1:13-16)

The kaph-preserved *one like unto the Son of Adam* names the figure Yochanan (John) sees on Patmos — the same Son of Adam the disciple had seen transfigured on the mountain, now glorified beyond the foretaste, his countenance shining as the sun the way the King's-face shone on the high mountain. The disciple Yochanan (John) is in both visions. The substance is the same substance. The transfiguration is the gospel's-foretaste of what Yochanan (John) will see again in full on Patmos.

*And, behold, there appeared unto them Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) talking with him.* The Torah-witness and the Prophets-witness, standing together with the King. The two whom the Tanakh names as the foundational-mediators of the covenant-substance — Mosheh (Moses) the lawgiver, Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet whose return is foretold at the close of the Tanakh — appearing with the One whom the law and the prophets had pointed toward. The Luke gospel adds what they were speaking of:

*Who appeared in glory, and spake of his decease which he should accomplish at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem).* (Luke 9:31)

The Greek word for *decease* is *exodos* — his exodus. The Torah-witness and the Prophets-witness are speaking with the King about the exodus he is about to accomplish at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) — his death, his resurrection, the leading-out of the gathered into the inheritance the way Mosheh (Moses) led the first generation out of Egypt. The King's-passion is the exodus the disciples will witness. The two who stand with him on the mountain understand what is coming. The disciples do not yet.

Hold this carefully. The forefathers who walked the covenant faithfully in their lifetimes — Avraham (Abraham), Yitschaq (Isaac), Ya'aqov (Jacob), Mosheh (Moses), David, the prophets, the faithful unnamed of every generation back — are asleep, awaiting the resurrection. The apostle names this directly:

*And these all, having obtained a good report through faith, received not the promise: Elohim (God) having provided some better thing for us, that they without us should not be made perfect.* (Hebrews 11:39-40)

The forefathers have not yet been made perfect; they await the resurrection. Mosheh (Moses) died and was buried by Yahuah (the LORD) himself at Deuteronomy 34:5-6; his resting-place is the resting-place Yahudah (Jude) 9 references in the dispute between Michael and the adversary over Mosheh's (Moses') body. Eliyahu (Elijah) was taken up alive in the whirlwind at 2 Kings 2:11 — the Chanok (Enoch) parallel of Genesis 5:24 (*Chanok (Enoch) walked with Elohim (God): and he was not; for Elohim (God) took him*). The two have different exit-patterns; both await the resurrection. The transfiguration is not the first resurrection. The transfiguration is the vision in which Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) appear in glorified-form to the King and the three disciples for the testimony their appearance bears. The first resurrection is the eschatological-future-event the prophets named, when the dead in Messiah (Christ) rise and the gathered are made perfect together. Yahuah (the LORD) is able to show the forefathers in glorified-form in a vision without that vision being the resurrection itself.

*Then answered Kefa (Peter), and said unto Yahusha (Jesus), Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Mosheh (Moses), and one for Eliyahu (Elijah).* Kefa's (Peter's) instinct is right and his proposal is wrong, and the wrongness is in the architecture. The Mark gospel adds the diagnostic on Kefa's (Peter's) state: *for he wist not what to say; for they were sore afraid* (Mark 9:6). Kefa (Peter) is afraid, and the afraid-disciple reaches for the cultic-pattern he knows. What he reaches for is Sukkot (Tabernacles). The feast of booths is the feast of Yahuah (the LORD) dwelling with his people in tabernacles — the wilderness-substance carried forward as the annual-remembrance per Leviticus 23:33-43, and the eschatological-substance of the Messianic-age the prophet Zechariah named:

*And it shall come to pass, that every one that is left of all the nations which came against Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) shall even go up from year to year to worship the King, Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts), and to keep the feast of tabernacles.* (Zechariah 14:16)

Sukkot (Tabernacles) is the Messianic-age-feast. Kefa (Peter) recognizes the substance of what he is seeing — the glory of the King displayed, the prophets standing with him, the cloud about to come — and reaches for the appropriate cultic-form. The instinct is sound. The proposal is wrong in one respect: three tabernacles. The three figures are not co-equal. Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) are witnesses; the King is the substance the witnesses witness-to. Three equal-tabernacles would flatten the architectural-distinction. The Father will correct Kefa (Peter) immediately.

*While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them: and behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.* The bright-cloud is the *kavod* (glory) cloud of the Tanakh:

*And it came to pass, as Aharon (Aaron) spake unto the whole congregation of the children of Yashar'el (Israel), that they looked toward the wilderness, and, behold, the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) appeared in the cloud.* (Exodus 16:10)

*Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) filled the tabernacle. And Mosheh (Moses) was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) filled the tabernacle.* (Exodus 40:34-35)

*And it came to pass, when the priests were come out of the holy place, that the cloud filled the house of Yahuah (the LORD), So that the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud: for the glory of Yahuah (the LORD) had filled the house of Yahuah (the LORD).* (1 Kings 8:10-11)

The glory-cloud at Sinai, at the Tabernacle's-dedication, at the Temple's-dedication — and now at the transfiguration. The same substance. The Formless-Father's-presence marked by the bright-cloud, the way the presence of Yahuah (the LORD) has always been marked when the substance comes near. And the voice that speaks out of the cloud is the Father's-voice. The Tanakh-pattern of voice-from-the-cloud is the Father speaking. *And Yahuah (the LORD) came down in the cloud, and spake unto him, and took of the spirit that was upon him, and gave it unto the seventy elders* (Numbers 11:25). *He spake unto them in the cloudy pillar* (Psalm 99:7). The same voice from the same cloud speaks now over the King on the mountain.

The voice carries three Tanakh-texts in one utterance.

*This is my beloved Son* — the substance of the second Psalm:

*Yahuah (the LORD) hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee. Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.* (Psalm 2:7-8)

The Davidic-royal-Son enthronement-substance. The King is the Son of the second-Psalm, the begotten-Son whose inheritance is the nations. The same line was spoken at the immersion at the Yarden (Jordan) at Matthew 3:17 — the same voice, the same Tanakh-anchor, the same Son.

*In whom I am well pleased* — the substance of the first servant-song of the prophet:

*Behold my servant, whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the goyim (Gentiles). He shall not cry, nor lift up, nor cause his voice to be heard in the street. A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.* (Isaiah 42:1-3)

The Suffering-Servant substance. Matthew 12:18-21 quoted this passage as the longest Matthean Tanakh-citation, applying it to the King's meek-and-not-striving disposition through the public ministry. The Father's-voice now combines the Davidic-royal-Son enthronement substance with the Suffering-Servant election — the same King is both. The two strands of Tanakh-Messianic-prophecy that the rabbinic tradition often split into two-figures-of-deliverance (a triumphal-Messiah and a suffering-Messiah) are revealed at the transfiguration to be the same Messiah (Christ). The Father pronounces it directly. The royal-Son IS the chosen-Servant; the chosen-Servant IS the royal-Son.

*Hear ye him* — the substance of the prophet-like-unto-Mosheh promise:

*Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken... I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth; and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him. And it shall come to pass, that whosoever will not hearken unto my words which he shall speak in my name, I will require it of him.* (Deuteronomy 18:15, 18-19)

The prophet-like-unto-Mosheh whom Yahuah (the LORD) promised at the renewal-of-the-covenant. The promise of the prophet whose words must be heard the way Mosheh's (Moses') words were heard. The apostle Kefa (Peter) himself will cite this verse in his Pentecost-sermon and again at the temple in his second sermon, naming the King as the Prophet:

*For Mosheh (Moses) truly said unto the fathers, A prophet shall Yahuah your Elohim (the LORD your God) raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear in all things whatsoever he shall say unto you. And it shall come to pass, that every soul, which will not hear that prophet, shall be destroyed from among the people.* (Acts 3:22-23)

And Stephan (Stephen) will cite the same Deuteronomy passage at Acts 7:37 before the council that stones him. The Father's *hear ye him* on the mountain is the explicit-corrective to Kefa's (Peter's) three-tabernacles proposal. Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) are witnesses; the King is the prophet to whom the witnesses point. The voice of the prophet-like-unto-Mosheh is the voice the kingdom-citizens are commanded to hear. The Torah-witness and the Prophets-witness do not stand co-equal with the King; the Torah-witness and the Prophets-witness testify-to the King and direct the disciples to hear him. The architecture the apostle to the Hebrews will state directly is being established on the mountain:

*Elohim (God), who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds.* (Hebrews 1:1-2)

The witnesses by whom Yahuah (the LORD) spoke in time past stand on the mountain with the Son by whom Yahuah (the LORD) now speaks. The disciples are commanded to hear the Son.

*And when the disciples heard it, they fell on their face, and were sore afraid.* The Tanakh-theophany-response is the only response the disciples can have. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) before the vision of the throne in Isaiah 6 — *woe is me! for I am undone* (Isaiah 6:5). Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at the river Chebar at the vision of the glory — *I fell upon my face* (Ezekiel 1:28). Daniel before the angel at the river Hiddekel — *I fell into a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground* (Daniel 10:9). Yochanan (John) on Patmos at the vision of the *one like unto the Son of Adam* — *I fell at his feet as dead* (Revelation 1:17). The pattern is universal: the kingdom-citizen at the appearance of the holy substance falls on his face, undone. The disciples' response is the appropriate-Tanakh-pattern, not a failure of nerve.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid.* The King's-touch is the Tanakh-pattern of the heavenly-messenger's-touch-after-the-prostration. *Then there came again and touched me one like the appearance of a man, and he strengthened me* (Daniel 10:18). *And he laid his right hand upon me, saying unto me, Fear not* (Revelation 1:17). The King occupies the position of the heavenly-messenger in the Tanakh-vision-pattern because the King IS the One whom the heavenly-messengers served. The pattern that prophets had walked across the Tanakh — the vision, the prostration, the touch, the *fear not* — is the King's pattern in the flesh now reaching the disciples.

*And when they had lifted up their eyes, they saw no man, save Yahusha (Jesus) only.* The closing scene is the final correction to the three-tabernacles proposal. The witnesses have testified; the witnesses depart. The Son remains. *Yahusha (Jesus) only.* The substance the witnesses witness-to is enough. The King is the tabernacle the Father has set among his people — the *eskēnōsen* of John 1:14, *the Word was made flesh, and dwelt (tabernacled) among us.* No second tabernacle for Mosheh (Moses) is needed; no third tabernacle for Eliyahu (Elijah) is needed. The King is the tabernacle. The disciples come down from the mountain with the substance compressed into one image they will not be able to articulate until after the resurrection.

*And as they came down from the mountain, Yahusha (Jesus) charged them, saying, Tell the vision to no man, until the Son of Adam be risen again from the dead. And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the scribes that Eliyahu (Elijah) must first come? And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things. But I say unto you, That Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed. Likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them. Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of Yochanan (John) the Immerser.* (Matthew 17:9-13)

*Tell the vision to no man, until the Son of Adam be risen again from the dead.* The Messianic-secret pattern continues from Matthew 16:20. The transfiguration cannot be told yet because the resurrection has not yet happened, and the transfiguration's-substance lands properly only when the resurrection-credential has authenticated the King. The vision is reserved for post-resurrection-disclosure. Decades later, when Kefa (Peter) writes his second letter knowing his death is near, he will tell the vision then, and it will land as the foundation of the prophetic-witness:

*For we have not followed cunningly devised fables, when we made known unto you the power and coming of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ), but were eyewitnesses of his majesty. For he received from Elohim (God) the Father honour and glory, when there came such a voice to him from the excellent glory, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. And this voice which came from heaven we heard, when we were with him in the holy mount. We have also a more sure word of prophecy.* (2 Peter 1:16-19)

The order matters. The resurrection happens. The vision becomes proclaimable. The proclamation establishes the *more sure word of prophecy.* The Father's-voice from the mountain authenticates the whole.

The disciples' question — *why then say the scribes that Eliyahu (Elijah) must first come?* — comes from the scribal-eschatology of the last prophet at the close of the Tanakh:

*Behold, I will send you Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD): And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.* (Malachi 4:5-6)

The scribes had read this verse and concluded that an Eliyahu (Elijah)-figure would precede the Messiah's-coming. The disciples have just seen Eliyahu (Elijah) on the mountain — the actual Eliyahu (Elijah) in glorified-form — and they wonder how the scribal-expectation works if the King is already here and the actual Eliyahu (Elijah) is the one who just appeared and departed.

The King's answer carries both fulfillments. *Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things.* The future-tense is the second-fulfillment substance — the Eliyahu (Elijah) of Revelation 11, one of the two-witnesses who prophesy in the days of the great-and-dreadful-day-of-Yahuah (the LORD), shut up the heavens that it rain not in the days of their prophecy (Revelation 11:6 — the Eliyahu-1-Kings-17-and-18 substance), and are martyred in the streets of the great-city. The restoration-of-all-things substance is the prophesied-restoration the prophets named: the gathering of the scattered seed, the re-engrafting of the natural branches at the first-resurrection, the kingdom-of-priests to the nations left alive after the day. Acts 3:21 names this *the times of restitution of all things, which Elohim (God) hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began.*

*But I say unto you, That Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed.* The past-tense is the first-fulfillment substance. Yochanan (John) the Immerser was the Eliyahu-pattern-prophet at the first-coming. The Matthew 11:14 statement — *and if ye will receive it, this is Eliyahu (Elijah), which was for to come* — is now restated explicitly: *Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already.* The Eliyahu-pattern that ran in the Tanakh — the prophet who stood against the kings and the apostate-priesthood, who confronted Achav (Ahab) and Izevel (Jezebel) at Mount Carmel and in the wine-vineyard of Navot (Naboth) — recurred in Yochanan (John) the Immerser standing against Herod-Antipas and Herodias. The Herod-and-Herodias of Matthew 14 are the Achav (Ahab) and Izevel (Jezebel) of the first-century, and the Eliyahu (Elijah)-figure who confronted them was killed for it the way the Tanakh-Eliyahu (Elijah) was hunted for it. *They have done unto him whatsoever they listed.* Yochanan's (John's) beheading at Matthew 14:1-12 is the Eliyahu (Elijah)-pattern reaching its first-coming-fulfillment.

*Likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them.* The King now ties his own coming-passion to the Eliyahu-pattern-prophet's-martyrdom. The pattern that ran from the Tanakh-prophets through Yochanan (John) the Immerser will run through the King himself. The system that kills the prophets will kill the One the prophets pointed toward. The chapter just opened with the glory; the chapter immediately re-grounds the disciples in the passion. The two are one motion. The glory the disciples just saw is the glory the cross opens onto.

*Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of Yochanan (John) the Immerser.* The hearing-and-understanding pattern Matthew 13:13-15 had named operates here. The disciples hear and understand; the scribes hear and do not understand. The cause-and-effect-of-hearing the King had laid down at the parables-chapter is operating at the disciple-level now.

The three come down from the mountain with the King to find the rest of the disciples surrounded by a multitude. The other nine had not been on the mountain. While the three were seeing the foretaste-of-the-coming, the nine were failing to heal a demon-possessed boy.

*And when they were come to the multitude, there came to him a certain man, kneeling down to him, and saying, Lord, have mercy on my son: for he is lunatick, and sore vexed: for ofttimes he falleth into the fire, and oft into the water. And I brought him to thy disciples, and they could not cure him. Then Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said, O faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you? bring him hither to me. And Yahusha (Jesus) rebuked the devil; and he departed out of him: and the child was cured from that very hour. Then came the disciples to Yahusha (Jesus) apart, and said, Why could not we cast him out? And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you. Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.* (Matthew 17:14-21)

The father's-petition is the Tanakh-petitioner pattern. *Lord, have mercy on my son.* The same opening the Canaanite-woman of Matthew 15 used (*have mercy on me, O Lord, thou son of David*), the same opening the two-blind-men of Matthew 9 used (*thou son of David, have mercy on us*). The kingdom-petitioners speak the petition that recognizes the King's-mercy as the source. The Greek for *lunatick* is *selēniazomai* — moon-struck, the affliction associated in the Tanakh-and-Second-Temple period with the moon's-cycles. The symptoms the father names — *ofttimes he falleth into the fire, and oft into the water* — are the symptoms the Mark and Luke parallels expand at Mark 9:18 and Luke 9:39: grand-mal-style seizures with the boy thrown to the ground, foaming, gnashing the teeth, the demon attempting to destroy the child by fire-and-water. The affliction is not the natural-affliction of disease alone; the affliction is demonic-affliction operating through the natural-faculties. The kingdom-substance the King has been demonstrating since Matthew 4 distinguishes the two and addresses each at its level.

*And I brought him to thy disciples, and they could not cure him.* The disciples had been commissioned for this. *And when he had called unto him his twelve disciples, he gave them power against unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal all manner of sickness and all manner of disease* (Matthew 10:1). The commission of Matthew 10:8 was direct: *Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.* The Mark-and-Luke parallels at Mark 6:13 and Luke 9:6 record the disciples' previous-successful-exorcisms — they have done this before. The failure-at-this-case is not categorical; the failure is at this particular case for a particular reason the King will name.

*O faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you?* The King's-rebuke is wider than the disciples. The Greek is *genea apistos kai diestrammenē* — *faithless and twisted generation.* The phrase echoes Mosheh's (Moses') song against the wilderness-generation:

*They have corrupted themselves, their spot is not the spot of his children: they are a perverse and crooked generation.* (Deuteronomy 32:5)

And later in the same chapter:

*And he said, I will hide my face from them, I will see what their end shall be: for they are a very froward generation, children in whom is no faith.* (Deuteronomy 32:20)

The wilderness-generation's-faithlessness was the substance the prophets named through the long arc of Yashar'el (Israel)'s history; the faithlessness reaches the King's-day in the religious-system of the leadership and the broader-people's-disposition. The King's-rebuke is not narrowly aimed at the disciples alone — it is aimed at the *generation* in which the disciples are forming. The disciples are inheriting the faithlessness; the disciples must come out of it. The exodus-substance the King is leading at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) is the exodus from the unbelieving-generation as well as from death itself.

*Bring him hither to me. And Yahusha (Jesus) rebuked the devil; and he departed out of him: and the child was cured from that very hour.* The King exorcises without difficulty. The substance the disciples could not move flows through the King without obstruction. The kingdom-substance is the King's-substance; the substance flows where the King wills it; the disciples' failure was not the King's-power's failure.

*Why could not we cast him out? ... Because of your unbelief.* The Greek word the King uses is *oligopistia* — little-faith. The same word he used to Kefa (Peter) on the water at Matthew 14:31 (*O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?*); the same word he used about the disciples at the loaves-and-baskets at Matthew 16:8 (*O ye of little faith, why reason ye among yourselves, because ye have brought no bread?*). The little-faith pattern is the disciples' chronic-disposition — they have-seen, but the seeing-and-the-faith-substance do not always-track-together. The cause-and-effect-of-hearing diagnosis the King has laid down works here too. The disciples have-seen the kingdom-substance over months of ministry; the seeing has not yet produced the level of faith-substance the harder cases require.

*If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you.* The mustard-seed substance was the parable of Matthew 13:31-32 — the smallest-of-seeds with the explosive-potential to grow into the tree the birds-of-the-air lodge in. The substance is qualitative, not quantitative. Faith of the right-substance, even at small-quantity, moves what cannot be otherwise moved. Faith of the wrong-substance, regardless of quantity, moves nothing. The disciples' little-faith here is small in quantity AND off in substance — the disciples have been measuring the case against their previous-successes rather than against the King's-authority that has been the source of every success they had. The faith-substance that operates in the kingdom-mission is faith in the King and in the Father who sent him, not faith in the disciples'-own-spiritual-credentialing.

*Say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove.* The mountain-moving hyperbole is the Tanakh-pattern of the visible-impossible-becoming-possible at the speaking of the kingdom-substance. *Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea* (Psalm 46:2). *Every valley shall be exalted, and every mountain and hill shall be made low* (Isaiah 40:4). *Oh that thou wouldest rend the heavens, that thou wouldest come down, that the mountains might flow down at thy presence* (Isaiah 64:1). The mountains-tremble-at-the-presence-of-Yahuah (the LORD) substance becomes the kingdom-citizens'-prayer-substance: the same Yahuah (the LORD) who moves the mountains at his presence is the One whose Spirit moves through the disciples' prayer-and-action when the substance is right. *Nothing shall be impossible unto you* — the kingdom-impossibilities have a different floor than the world's-impossibilities, because the kingdom is the present-incursion of the One who created all things and who holds them by the Word of his power.

Hold what this is not. The inherited-Christian-prosperity-gospel reading takes the *nothing shall be impossible* substance and detaches it from the kingdom-mission substance — turns it into a name-it-and-claim-it formula by which the believer is supposed to be able to command material-blessings into existence by sufficient-faith. That is not what the King is saying. The disciples' failure that occasions this teaching was a failure in the kingdom-mission — the casting-out-of-the-demon for the freeing-of-the-affliction-victim. The faith-substance flows in the kingdom-mission, not in private-prosperity-acquisition. The apostle holds the architecture firmly: *and though I have all faith, so that I could remove mountains, and have not love, I am nothing* (1 Corinthians 13:2). Faith without love-substance is faith that has missed the kingdom's-orientation. The mustard-seed-faith that moves mountains operates in love-substance toward the kingdom's-mission; it does not operate as a private-power-acquisition for the believer's-comfort.

*Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.* The qualifier on the end of the teaching adds the practical-substance. Some classes of demonic-affliction require the deeper-disposition of prayer-and-fasting for the kingdom-substance to flow. The King is teaching that fasting remains a kingdom-substance-discipline. The inherited-Christian-collapse of fasting into something either-optional-or-abolished does not stand against the textual-substance. The King fasted forty days at the beginning of his ministry. The King taught the kingdom-citizens how to fast at Matthew 6:16-18 — *but thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; that thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret.* The disciples-of-Yochanan (John) and the Pharisees-disciples both fasted at Matthew 9:14, and the King's-answer was not that fasting was abolished but that the disciples would fast after the Bridegroom was taken from them (Matthew 9:15). The apostolic-assembly fasted at Acts 13:2-3 before sending Sha'ul (Paul) and Bar-Naba (Barnabas) on the mission, and at Acts 14:23 before ordaining elders. Fasting continues in the kingdom-citizens' practice; the King teaches its weight here.

*And while they abode in Galilee, Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, The Son of Adam shall be betrayed into the hands of men: and they shall kill him, and the third day he shall be raised again. And they were exceeding sorry.* (Matthew 17:22-23)

The second-passion-prediction. The pattern of three predictions runs through the gospel — Matthew 16:21, Matthew 17:22-23, Matthew 20:17-19. The threefold-naming is deliberate, the way the prophets named coming-events more than once so the witnesses could not later claim they had not been warned. The first-prediction at Matthew 16:21 produced Kefa's (Peter's) active-rebuke and the King's *get thee behind me, Satan.* The second-prediction here produces *exceeding sorry* — passive-grief now, not active-rebuke. The third-prediction at Matthew 20:17-19 will produce the request of the sons of Zavdai (Zebedee) for thrones in the kingdom (vv.20-23) — the disciples will not yet have grasped the passion-substance even when the King is about to walk into it. The disciples' progression through the predictions is a long-arc of learning. The King is teaching them in advance so that when the substance comes, they will have heard it before and will be able to recover.

*The Son of Adam shall be betrayed into the hands of men.* The Greek verb is *paradidotai* — *delivered-up,* the passive of *paradidōmi.* The same word the Greek of the prophet uses through Isaiah 53 — *and Yahuah (the LORD) hath laid on him the iniquity of us all* / *he was oppressed, and he was afflicted, yet he opened not his mouth: he is brought as a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is dumb, so he openeth not his mouth* / *he was numbered with the transgressors; and he bare the sin of many.* The Suffering-Servant of Isaiah 53 is *delivered-up* through the entire passage, and the King now names himself as the One who will be *delivered-up* in the same way. The Tanakh-prophetic-architecture is again the source of the *must.*

*And the third day he shall be raised again.* The resurrection-credential renamed. The sign-of-Yonah (Jonah) the King had announced at Matthew 12:40 (*for as Yonah (Jonah) was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of Adam be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth*) is the substance the second-prediction holds in front of the disciples. The exodus the King is about to accomplish at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) — the exodus Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) were speaking with him about on the mountain — has the third-day-resurrection as its decisive-credential. The disciples exceeding-sorry response is the natural-response of those who love him; the resurrection-substance the King is also naming is the substance they will only be able to receive when they have walked through what the prediction announces.

*And when they were come to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), they that received tribute money came to Kefa (Peter), and said, Doth not your master pay tribute? He saith, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Yahusha (Jesus) prevented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Shim'on (Simon)? of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers? Kefa (Peter) saith unto him, Of strangers. Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, Then are the children free. Notwithstanding, lest we should offend them, go thou to the sea, and cast an hook, and take up the fish that first cometh up; and when thou hast opened his mouth, thou shalt find a piece of money: that take, and give unto them for me and thee.* (Matthew 17:24-27)

The chapter that opened with the glory closes with a half-shekel coin from a fish's mouth. The architecture is deliberate. The same King who stood on the high mountain with his face shining as the sun also pays the temple-tax for himself and Kefa (Peter) with a coin pulled out of the Galilee.

The tribute-money is the half-shekel temple-tax of the Torah:

*Yahuah (the LORD) spake unto Mosheh (Moses), saying, When thou takest the sum of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) after their number, then shall they give every man a ransom for his soul unto Yahuah (the LORD), when thou numberest them; that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them. This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of Yahuah (the LORD). Every one that passeth among them that are numbered, from twenty years old and above, shall give an offering unto Yahuah (the LORD). The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, when they give an offering unto Yahuah (the LORD), to make an atonement for your souls.* (Exodus 30:11-15)

The half-shekel is atonement-money. The Torah names it explicitly — *to make an atonement for your souls.* Every male over twenty in Yashar'el (Israel) gives the half-shekel; the rich do not give more, the poor do not give less; the substance is the same atonement-substance for every covenant-member. In Mosheh's (Moses') day, the half-shekel collection funded the Tabernacle's-construction (Exodus 38:25-28). In Yoash's (Joash's) day, it funded the temple-repair (2 Kings 12:4-16). In Nechemyah's (Nehemiah's) post-exile reconstruction, the rate was adjusted to a third-of-a-shekel for the temple-service support (Nehemiah 10:32-33). By the first-century, the half-shekel had been re-established as the annual temple-tax, collected from every adult Yahudi (Jew) over twenty for the support of the temple-sacrifices, the temple-maintenance, and the priestly-service. The tax was levied as the people's-atonement-substance — the corporate-contribution to the atonement that the daily-sacrifices accomplished on behalf of the gathered.

After AD 70, Vespasian would redirect the half-shekel collection to the *fiscus Iudaicus* — the Roman Jewish-tax that funded the Capitoline Jupiter temple in Rome. The atonement-money would be redirected to the pagan-system that destroyed Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) and the temple — an antichrist-substance Roman-substitution. But that lies in the future. At the time of Matthew 17, the tax is still going to the temple-service in Yerushalayim (Jerusalem).

The collectors approach Kefa (Peter), not the King directly. Kefa (Peter) answers *yes* on the King's-behalf — perhaps because Kefa (Peter) has been used to the King paying the tax in previous years, perhaps because Kefa (Peter) does not yet see the architecture the King is about to unfold. The King anticipates Kefa (Peter) at the house and prevents him from raising the matter: *what thinkest thou, Shim'on (Simon)? of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers?*

The Tanakh-precedent is clear. Kings do not tax their own sons. The custom-and-tribute flows from foreign-peoples and subject-populations into the king's-house; the king's-sons receive from the house, not pay into it. David's-sons in his court did not pay tribute to David. Shlomo's (Solomon's)-sons did not pay tribute to Shlomo (Solomon). The architecture of monarchic-tax-collection is universal across the ancient Near East — the children-of-the-king are free.

*Kefa (Peter) saith unto him, Of strangers. Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, Then are the children free.* The conclusion lands. The King is the Son of the One whose temple this is. The half-shekel is the temple-tax. The Father's-Son does not pay the Father's-tax to the Father's-house — the Father's-Son is free of the obligation by the architecture of who-the-Son-is. The King has just stood on the mountain hearing the Father's-voice declare him the beloved-Son; the same Son is the One the temple-tax is being demanded from. The categorical-architecture is exposed. The King is the Owner-Son of the temple, not a subject-of-the-temple-tax.

The same architecture extends to the King's-disciples — those whom the King has named brothers and to whom the Father is also Father by the Son's-adoption. *I ascend unto my Father, and your Father; and to my Elohim (God), and your Elohim (God)* (John 20:17 — the apostolic-substance that will be made explicit after the resurrection). *For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren* (Hebrews 2:11). The children of the Father are categorically-free of the temple-tax. The King is naming the architecture that the disciples will inherit and that the apostolic-witness will unfold post-resurrection.

But the King does not claim the exemption. *Notwithstanding, lest we should offend them.* The Greek for *offend* is *skandalisōmen* — that we cause stumbling-block. The same root the King had used at Matthew 16:23 when he called Kefa's (Peter's)-rebuke a *skandalon* unto him. The kingdom-citizen does not throw the kingdom-exemption in the face of the still-unawakened-system in a way that creates a stumbling-block to those who could otherwise come home. The disposition is the same disposition the apostle Paul would later spell out across multiple letters:

*Wherefore, if meat make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth, lest I make my brother to offend.* (1 Corinthians 8:13)

*Even as I please all men in all things, not seeking mine own profit, but the profit of many, that they may be saved.* (1 Corinthians 10:33)

*Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the shabbatot (sabbaths)... Let us not therefore judge one another any more: but judge this rather, that no man put a stumblingblock or an occasion to fall in his brother's way.* (Colossians 2:16; Romans 14:13)

The kingdom-citizen exercises the kingdom-freedom in a way that does not become a stumbling-block to those-still-coming. The King models the substance at the half-shekel: categorically-exempt, voluntarily-paying, lest-we-offend. The kingdom-citizens will inherit the disposition.

*Go thou to the sea, and cast an hook, and take up the fish that first cometh up; and when thou hast opened his mouth, thou shalt find a piece of money: that take, and give unto them for me and thee.* The miraculous-substance is the Owner-of-the-sea's-sovereign-direction. The same King who walked on the water in the previous chapters now directs a fish to bring up the precise-coin needed for the precise-tax for the precise-two-men. The Greek for *piece of money* is *statēr* — a four-drachma coin, exactly two half-shekels, the tax for the King and for Kefa (Peter) with no remainder. The substance is the Adamic-dominion-over-the-fish of the second psalm of David:

*Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet: All sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field; The fowl of the air, and the fish of the sea, and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas.* (Psalm 8:6-8)

The Yonah (Jonah)-pattern is also in view — *now Yahuah (the LORD) had prepared a great fish to swallow up Yonah (Jonah)* (Jonah 1:17). The Owner-of-the-sea appoints fish to his purposes. At the great-scale, a great-fish swallows a fleeing-prophet and delivers him to the city he was sent to. At the small-domestic-scale, a fish brings up a coin in its mouth for the tax-payment of the King and his-disciple. The same sovereign-Owner-of-the-sea.

Hold what the half-shekel atonement-substance pre-figures. *To make an atonement for your souls.* The King who is categorically-exempt from the atonement-tax pays the atonement-tax for himself-as-categorically-free and for Kefa (Peter)-as-categorically-free. The substance models, at the small-scale, what the King is about to walk at the cross-scale. The categorically-exempt One pays the atonement-substance that the categorically-bound owe. The half-shekel-from-the-fish's-mouth is the gospel's-foreshadowing in miniature of the cross-payment in full. The categorically-free King will pay what categorically-bound men cannot pay. The disciples will not see the architecture clearly until after the resurrection; but the King is laying it down here for them to find later when they read the gospel as the apostolic-assembly compiles it. *He needeth not daily, as those high priests, to offer up sacrifice, first for his own sins, and then for the people's: for this he did once, when he offered up himself* (Hebrews 7:27). The categorically-exempt One pays the atonement once-for-all. The half-shekel-from-the-fish's-mouth is the architectural-rehearsal.

The chapter that opened with the King in glorified-form on the mountain closes with the King paying a half-shekel as if he were any other Yahudi (Jewish) man in Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum). The two scenes are one motion. The glory is what the cross opens onto, and the cross is what the categorically-exempt One walks into for the sake of the categorically-bound. The disciples have seen the glory; the disciples have seen the foretaste-of-the-coming; the disciples will see the cross and the resurrection that authenticates the glory; the disciples will eventually understand that the glory was not other than the cross-substance but the cross-substance's-completion. The next chapter will open with the disciples asking *who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?* — and the King will set a little child in their midst and begin teaching them what the kingdom's-architecture-of-greatness actually is.
