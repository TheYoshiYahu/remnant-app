# Commentary on Matthew 17

**Framework apologetic for the Yoshi rendering. LANDED 2026-05-19 (session 85).**

The seventeenth chapter of the Matthew sweep. **The chapter of the transfiguration on the high mountain — the FRAMEWORK-MAJOR moment of the Formless-Formed nature-of-God reading at maximum-visual-substance — and the chapter that completes the Yochanan (John)-as-Eliyahu pattern at the *Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already* explicit-statement.** The chapter opens with the transfiguration after six days (vv.1-8) — the King takes Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) up into a high mountain apart; the King is transfigured before them, his face shining as the sun, his raiment white as the light; Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) appear talking with him; Kefa (Peter) proposes three tabernacles; a bright cloud overshadows them; the Father's-voice declares *this is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him;* the disciples fall on their face sore afraid; the King touches them with the *arise, and be not afraid* substance. Pivots through the coming-down-from-the-mountain charge to tell-no-man-the-vision-till-the-Son-of-Adam-be-risen and the explicit Eliyahu-is-come-already discussion (vv.9-13) — *Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things. But I say unto you, That Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed.* Lands at the demoniac-boy at the foot of the mountain and the disciples' inability + the King's grain-of-mustard-seed-faith teaching (vv.14-21). Closes with the second-passion-prediction (vv.22-23) — *the Son of Adam shall be betrayed into the hands of men: and they shall kill him, and the third day he shall be raised again* — and the temple-tax-coin from the fish's-mouth (vv.24-27) where the King teaches the children-are-free architecture before paying-the-tax-lest-we-offend. Three framework-major moments anchor the chapter: the **transfiguration as FRAMEWORK-MAJOR for the nature-of-God reading** — the Formed-Son visible in glorified-form on the mountain while the Father's-Voice speaks from the bright cloud, the Formless-Formed grid made-visible at maximum-substance; the **Father's-Voice combined-citation** at v.5 — Psalm 2:7's *thou art my Son* + Isaiah 42:1's *in whom my soul delighteth* + Deuteronomy 18:15's *hear ye him* — naming the King as both the Davidic-royal-Son AND the Servant AND the Prophet-like-unto-Mosheh in one breath; the **Eliyahu-is-come-already explicit-statement** completing the framework's Yochanan-as-Eliyahu-pattern thread at the King's-own-mouth level. ## §1. The transfiguration on the high mountain — FRAMEWORK-MAJOR for the nature-of-God

*And after six days Yahusha (Jesus) taketh Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) his brother, and bringeth them up into an high mountain apart, and was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light. And, behold, there appeared unto them Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) talking with him. Then answered Kefa (Peter), and said unto Yahusha (Jesus), Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Mosheh (Moses), and one for Eliyahu (Elijah). While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them: and behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him. And when the disciples heard it, they fell on their face, and were sore afraid. And Yahusha (Jesus) came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid. And when they had lifted up their eyes, they saw no man, save Yahusha (Jesus) only* (vv.1-8).

**The framework reads vv.1-8 as the chapter's FRAMEWORK-MAJOR moment — the transfiguration on the high mountain as the Formless-Formed nature-of-God reading made visible at maximum-substance.**

**The *after six days* temporal-link** (v.1a). The Matt 16:28 saying *there be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the Son of Adam coming in his kingdom* finds its immediate-narrative-fulfillment in the transfiguration after six days. The framework reads the temporal-link carefully — the disciples' standing-here is satisfied at the foretaste-level by the transfiguration-vision; the full-coming-in-his-kingdom remains future per the eschatological filter of `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §IV. The framework does NOT collapse Matt 16:28 into the transfiguration as the *exhaustive* fulfillment — the *coming in his kingdom* substance carries forward to the second-coming-in-glory at Matt 24:30 / Matt 25:31 / Daniel 7:13-14. The transfiguration is the foretaste; the consummation is ahead. Kefa's-own-naming at 2 Peter 1:16-18 names the transfiguration as the *eyewitnesses of his majesty* and as the *more sure word of prophecy* — the foretaste authenticates the prophetic-witness of the consummation.

The *six days* numerical-resonance reads in framework-substance: the Bereshit (Genesis) 1 six-days-of-creation pattern + the Shemot (Exodus) 24:16 six-days-before-Yahuah-called-Mosheh-out-of-the-cloud-on-the-seventh pattern. The transfiguration is positioned as a Mosheh-on-Sinai-pattern theophany — the King ascends the mountain after six days and the Voice speaks from the cloud, recapitulating the Sinai-theophany at the Formless-Formed grid's-maximum-visibility.

**The three disciples and the high mountain** (v.1b). The framework reads the three-disciples-chosen as the inner-circle witness-pattern — Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John). The same three are present at the raising-of-Yair's-daughter (Mark 5:37) and at Gethsemane (Matt 26:37). Two-or-three-witnesses Tanakh-standard (Deuteronomy 19:15) preserved at the highest-mystery moments of the gospel-narrative. The framework rules out any Roman-Catholic primacy-of-Kefa-alone reading — the witness is three, not one; the substance is the testimony of multiple-witnesses to the same vision.

The *high mountain apart* setting reads in framework-substance as the second-Sinai motif extended. The Sermon-on-the-Mount of Matt 5-7 established the King's-second-Sinai teaching from the throne-place; the transfiguration extends the second-Sinai motif to the visible-glory level. The framework reads the unnamed high mountain in continuity with the Tanakh's-mountain-theophany pattern (Sinai at Shemot (Exodus) 19-24; Horeb at 1 Melakhim (Kings) 19:8-13 where Eliyahu (Elijah) met Yahuah (the LORD); the Mount-of-Transfiguration completes the pattern by bringing both Mosheh's-mountain-theophany witness and Eliyahu's-mountain-theophany witness into one vision with the King). The traditional-Christian-identification of Mount Tabor is uncertain; the framework reads the geographic-unnamed-substance as the textual-emphasis on the mountain-theophany-pattern, not on the specific geographic location.

**The transfiguration itself** (v.2). *And was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light.* The Greek *metemorphōthē* — the visible-form-changed. The framework reads the transfiguration in framework-substance: **the King's-Formed-essence beneath-the-flesh becomes-visible.** The everyday-walking-disciples have-walked-with-the-King for substantial-time; they have-seen him preach, heal, exorcise, walk-on-water, feed-multitudes. They have NOT seen him in his glorified-form. At the transfiguration, the Formed-essence the King has always-been (Psalm 8:5's-Formed-One; John 1:1-3's-Word; Philippians 2:6-7's-form-of-Elohim laid-aside at the incarnation) is made-visible for the foretaste-of-the-consummation.

The framework reads this in framework-substance per the voice skill's framework-deep nature-of-God section:

- **The Father is the Formless infinite**, the source from which the Formed proceeds.
- **The Son (Yahusha (Jesus)) is the Formed** — the expression-of-the-Formless drawn-into-form. He is the God-of-the-Tanakh-narratives — the one who-appeared-to-Avraham (Abraham), wrestled-with-Ya'aqov (Jacob), spoke-from-the-fire-at-Sinai, led-Yashar'el (Israel)-through-the-wilderness.
- **In the flesh, the Formed-essence is veiled** — Yahusha (Jesus) walks the dust-of-the-earth in the form-of-a-servant per Philippians 2:7 (*ekenōsen heauton, morphēn doulou labōn* — *emptied himself, taking the form of a servant*).
- **At the transfiguration, the veil-is-momentarily-pulled-back** — the Formed-essence the King has-always-been shines-through-the-flesh. The disciples see what has been there all along.

The *face did shine as the sun* substance reads in continuity with the Tanakh's-Yahuah-glory-pattern:

- **Shemot (Exodus) 34:29-35** — Mosheh's-face shines-after-encountering-Yahuah on Sinai; the face-shines because the glory has been-reflected-onto-Mosheh's-face. The King's-face shines NOT-by-reflection but by inherent-glory; the King IS-the-glory-source, not merely the glory-reflector. Mosheh's face reflected glory and faded (2 Cor 3:13's-veiled-fading-glory); the King's face emanates glory inherently.
- **Daniel 7:9-10** — the Ancient-of-Days's *garment ... white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool.* The Daniel-7 Ancient-of-Days vision and the transfiguration share visual-substance.
- **Daniel 10:5-6** — Daniel's-vision of the man-clothed-in-linen with the face-as-the-appearance-of-lightning shares visual-substance.
- **Revelation 1:13-16** — the *one like the Son of Adam* (preserving the kaph-comparative per Red Line #12) whose *countenance was as the sun shineth in his strength* — the same substance as the transfigured-King made-explicit at the post-resurrection vision-to-Yochanan (John) on Patmos.

The framework reads all four passages as visions-of-the-same-Formed-One — the God-of-the-Tanakh who-came-in-the-flesh, glorified-visible at the moments-when-the-veil-is-pulled-back. The Revelation 1:13-16 vision and the transfiguration-vision are the same Formed-One seen by the same disciple (Yochanan (John) is present at both visions).

**The appearance of Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah)** (v.3). *And, behold, there appeared unto them Mosheh (Moses) and Eliyahu (Elijah) talking with him.* The framework reads the appearance carefully and per Red Line #11.

The framework reads Mosheh and Eliyahu in framework-substance:

- **Mosheh (Moses) represents the Torah-witness** — the Torah-giver, the mediator of the first-Sinai covenant, the prophet-pattern-Yahusha-fulfills per Deuteronomy 18:15.
- **Eliyahu (Elijah) represents the Prophets-witness** — the Tishbite prophet, the restorer-of-the-faith-in-Yashar'el (Israel) during Achav (Ahab) / Yezevel (Jezebel)'s-apostasy, the pattern-prophet whose-second-fulfillment Yochanan (John) the Immerser carries per Malachi 4:5-6 and Matt 11:14.
- **The two-witnesses-pattern** — the Torah-and-Prophets together (the *nomos-and-prophētai* phrase the King uses at Matt 5:17, Matt 7:12, Matt 22:40) testifying-to-the-King at the central-theophany. The framework reads this as the Tanakh-itself testifying-to-the-King's-Messianic-identity at the maximum-visual-substance moment.

**Per Red Line #11: the forefathers-who-walked-the-covenant-faithfully are asleep, awaiting the resurrection.** The framework reads the appearance of Mosheh and Eliyahu in framework-substance per the Red Line #11 architecture without flattening:

- **Mosheh (Moses) died and was buried by Yahuah (the LORD) himself** (Deuteronomy 34:5-6). His resting-place is the Mosheh-body Yahudah (Jude) 9 references at the Michael-and-the-devil-disputation. Mosheh's-natural-state is asleep-awaiting-the-resurrection. The framework reads the transfiguration-appearance as a vision-substance — Mosheh-appears in glorified-form at the vision-moment without being-resurrected-yet. The natural-branches-grafting-in is at the first-resurrection per Red Line #11; the transfiguration is the vision-foretaste, not the resurrection-event itself.
- **Eliyahu (Elijah) was taken up alive in the whirlwind** at 2 Melakhim (Kings) 2:11. His exit-from-this-life is a distinct-pattern from Mosheh's-death. The framework reads Eliyahu's-substance preserved-without-tasting-death as the Chanok (Enoch)-and-Eliyahu-parallel (Bereshit (Genesis) 5:24's *Chanok (Enoch) walked with Elohim (God): and he was not; for Elohim (God) took him*). The transfiguration-appearance of Eliyahu in glorified-form is also vision-substance — the King-in-Formed-glory communing with the Tanakh-witnesses for the disciples'-benefit.
- **The framework does NOT collapse the transfiguration into a resurrection-pre-fulfillment for Mosheh and Eliyahu specifically.** The Red Line #11 framework holds — Mosheh-asleep-awaiting-the-first-resurrection per Hebrews 11:39-40's *they without us should not be made perfect.* The transfiguration is vision-of-glorified-form for the disciples' instruction; the first-resurrection is the eschatological-future-event.

**They are *talking with him* about something specific.** Luke 9:31 names the substance: *who appeared in glory, and spake of his decease which he should accomplish at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem).* The Mosheh-and-Eliyahu vision-conversation centers on the King's-coming-passion. The Torah-and-Prophets witnesses authenticate the King's-going-up-to-Yerushalayim trajectory the King has just begun naming-explicitly at Matt 16:21's first-passion-prediction.

**Kefa's (Peter's) *three tabernacles* proposal** (v.4). *Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Mosheh (Moses), and one for Eliyahu (Elijah).* The framework reads Kefa's-proposal in framework-substance — the Sukkot (Tabernacles) resonance is unmistakable. Kefa (Peter) recognizes-the-theophany-substance and reaches-for-the-Sukkot-architecture as the appropriate-celebration-pattern.

The framework reads the Sukkot-resonance carefully:

- **Sukkot (Tabernacles) is Yahuah-tabernacles-with-his-people feast** of Vayikra (Leviticus) 23:33-43; Yahuah (the LORD) instructs the gathered to dwell-in-booths for seven days as a remembrance of the wilderness-sojourn and an anticipation of the Messianic-age-Yahuah-dwelling-with-his-people.
- **Sukkot's eschatological-fulfillment** is named at Zechariah 14:16-19 — *every one that is left of all the nations which came against Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) shall even go up from year to year to worship the King, Yahuah of hosts (LORD of hosts), and to keep the feast of tabernacles.* The framework reads this verse in continuity with Red Line #7's nations-receive-priestly-instruction-from-the-gathered-remnant-in-the-millennial-reign architecture. Sukkot is the Messianic-age-feast par excellence.
- **The Yochanan (John) 1:14 *the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us* substance — *eskēnōsen en hēmin* — *tabernacled-among-us.*** The framework reads the *eskēnōsen* substance in framework-substance — the incarnation IS-the-Sukkot-substance at the first-fulfillment-level. The Formed-One has tabernacled-with-his-people in the flesh.

Kefa's-proposal is wrong in execution but right in instinct. The framework reads Mark 9:6 explicitly: *for he wist not what to say; for they were sore afraid.* Kefa's-proposal is reactive — the disciples are sore-afraid; Kefa-reaches-for-the-cultic-pattern that the theophany-substance suggests. But the framework reads the proposal's wrongness in framework-substance: **the three figures are not co-equal.** Mosheh-and-Eliyahu are witnesses; the King is the substance. Three-equal-tabernacles flatten the architectural-distinction. The Father's-voice from the cloud will correct Kefa's-proposal directly at v.5.

**The bright cloud and the Father's-voice** (v.5). *While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them: and behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.*

The framework reads v.5 as the chapter's central-utterance.

**The bright cloud** reads in framework-substance as the *kavod*-glory-cloud of the Tanakh:

- **Shemot (Exodus) 13:21-22; 14:19-20; 40:34-38** — the pillar-of-cloud-and-fire leading Yashar'el (Israel); the *kavod* (glory) filling the Tabernacle.
- **Shemot (Exodus) 19:9, 16, 18; 24:15-18** — the cloud on Sinai through which Yahuah (the LORD) spoke and called Mosheh into.
- **1 Melakhim (Kings) 8:10-11** — the cloud filling the Temple at the dedication; the priests cannot stand to minister because of the cloud's-glory.
- **Isaiah 4:5** — eschatological-cloud-and-fire-of-glory upon Mount Tsion (Zion) in the day of restoration.
- **Daniel 7:13** — *one like the Son of Adam coming with the clouds of heaven* (preserving the kaph-comparative per Red Line #12).

The framework reads the bright-cloud-at-the-transfiguration as the same-substance-cloud as Sinai and the Temple-dedication. The Formless-presence is present at the visible-substance of the cloud. The cloud-substance has-always-been the Tanakh's-presence-marker; at the transfiguration, the cloud is present at the Formed-Son's-glorified-display, marking the Formless-Father's-presence simultaneously.

**The Father's-voice** *out of the cloud* is the Formless-Father's-direct-speech. The framework reads this carefully. Yahuah (the LORD) speaks-out-of-the-fire-at-Sinai (Devarim (Deuteronomy) 4:12, 33); Yahuah (the LORD) speaks-out-of-the-cloud (Vayikra (Leviticus) 16:2; Tehillim (Psalm) 99:7). The Father's-voice-from-the-cloud at the transfiguration is the Formless-presence speaking the Formless-Father's-acknowledgment of the Formed-Son.

The framework reads the voice in continuity with the Yarden (Jordan)-baptism voice of Matt 3:17 — *this is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.* The same Voice; the same Witness. The Yarden-voice launched the public-ministry; the transfiguration-voice reinforces it at the moment-of-the-disciples'-deepest-confusion about the passion-trajectory.

**The combined-citation in the Father's-voice.** The framework reads the v.5 utterance as a three-Tanakh-text combined-citation:

1. ***This is my beloved Son*** — **Tehillim (Psalm) 2:7** — *Yahuah (the LORD) hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee.* The Davidic-royal-Son enthronement-psalm. The King is the Davidic-Messiah-King of Tehillim (Psalm) 2 — explicitly-claimed by the Father at the moment of the visible-glory-display.
2. ***In whom I am well pleased*** — **Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 42:1** — *Behold my servant, whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the goyim (nations).* The Suffering-Servant Tanakh-substance. The framework reads the combination in framework-substance — the same King is BOTH the Davidic-Royal-Messiah AND the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 42 Servant. The two-strands of Tanakh-Messianic-prophecy converge in the One.
3. ***Hear ye him*** — **Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:15** — *Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken.* The prophet-like-unto-Mosheh Tanakh-promise. The framework reads the *hear ye him* substance as the Father's-explicit-direction — the disciples are commanded to hear the Son specifically; not Mosheh-and-Eliyahu-and-the-Son co-equally, but the Son who is the prophet-like-unto-Mosheh-promised at Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18.

The framework reads the combined-citation as the Father's-correction of Kefa's-proposal. **Three-equal-tabernacles is wrong; one Son must be heard.** Mosheh and Eliyahu are witnesses-to-the-King; the King is the substance the witnesses witness-to. The Devarim-18:15 *hear ye him* substance positions the Torah-and-the-Prophets witness-status under the Son's-authoritative-voice. The Acts 3:22-23 apostolic-restatement of Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:15 confirms the framework's reading.

**The disciples' fall-on-their-face fear** (v.6). *And when the disciples heard it, they fell on their face, and were sore afraid.* The framework reads the disciples' response in continuity with the Tanakh-theophany-response-pattern:

- **Daniel 8:17** — Daniel falls on his face at Gavri'el's (Gabriel's) approach.
- **Daniel 10:9** — Daniel falls into a deep sleep on his face at the vision.
- **Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 1:28** — Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) falls upon his face at the vision of the glory of Yahuah (the LORD).
- **Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 6:5** — Yeshayahu's (Isaiah's) *woe is me!* response at the vision of Yahuah-Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts).
- **Revelation 1:17** — Yochanan (John) falls at the feet of the *one like the Son of Adam* as dead.

The disciples'-response is the appropriate-Tanakh-pattern at theophany-substance. The framework reads the disciples'-fear NOT as failure but as Tanakh-faithful-recognition of the holy-substance.

**The King's-touch and the *arise, and be not afraid* substance** (v.7). *And Yahusha (Jesus) came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid.* The framework reads the King's-touch in continuity with the Tanakh-pattern of the heavenly-messenger's-touch-after-the-prostration (Daniel 8:18, 10:10, 10:18; Revelation 1:17's *fear not*). The framework reads the King's-occupation-of-the-heavenly-messenger-role as confirming the Formed-Son's-Tanakh-identity — the King DOES what the heavenly-messengers DO in the Tanakh-theophany-pattern, because he IS the One they served.

**Yahusha (Jesus) only** (v.8). *And when they had lifted up their eyes, they saw no man, save Yahusha (Jesus) only.* The framework reads the closing-scene as the consummation of the Father's-voice. Mosheh-and-Eliyahu have departed; only the Son remains. The witnesses have-borne-their-testimony; the substance-they-witness-to remains. The framework reads this in framework-substance as the practical-corrective to Kefa's-three-tabernacles-proposal at the visual-level — only one Son requires the dwelling, because only one Son IS-the-tabernacle-substance per the Yochanan (John) 1:14 *eskēnōsen* substance.

The framework reads the chapter's-opening-eight-verses as the gospel's-deepest-window into the Formless-Formed grid. The Father (Formless) speaks-from-the-cloud; the Son (Formed) stands in glorified-form on the mountain; the Tanakh-witnesses (Torah-and-Prophets) bear-their-testimony; the disciples-respond in Tanakh-theophany-fear-pattern; the Son-only remains. The framework's-nature-of-God reading is made-visible at maximum-substance.

## §2. The coming-down-from-the-mountain charge and the Eliyahu-is-come-already explicit-statement

*And as they came down from the mountain, Yahusha (Jesus) charged them, saying, Tell the vision to no man, until the Son of Adam be risen again from the dead. And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the scribes that Eliyahu (Elijah) must first come? And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things. But I say unto you, That Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed. Likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them. Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of Yochanan (John) the Immerser* (vv.9-13).

**The framework reads vv.9-13 as the completion of the Yochanan (John)-as-Eliyahu-pattern thread at the King's-own-mouth level.**

**The tell-no-man-the-vision-till-the-resurrection charge** (v.9). The framework reads the charge in continuity with the Messianic-secret-pattern of Matt 16:20 (tell-no-man that he is Yahusha (Jesus) the Messiah). The transfiguration-vision is reserved for post-resurrection-disclosure because the resurrection is the credential-event the vision authenticates per the sign-of-Yonah resurrection-credential of Matt 12:39-40 and Matt 16:4. The framework reads the disclosure-timing as the gospel-narrative-substance — the disciples'-pre-resurrection-experience of the vision becomes the post-resurrection-apostolic-witness (Kefa's-own-naming at 2 Peter 1:16-18 confirms the framework's reading).

**The scribes'-eschatology and the disciples'-question** (v.10). *Why then say the scribes that Eliyahu (Elijah) must first come?* The framework reads the disciples'-question in framework-substance. The scribes'-eschatology of Malachi 4:5-6 — *Behold, I will send you Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD)* — predicts an Eliyahu-figure preceding the Messianic-day. The disciples have just witnessed the King in glorified-form; if the King is the Messiah, the disciples wonder, where is the Eliyahu-figure of Malachi 4? The disciples'-question is a legitimate-eschatological-question framed in scribal-vocabulary.

**The King's-two-part answer** (vv.11-12). The framework reads the King's-answer at framework-substance — *Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things. But I say unto you, That Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not* — as the eschatological-double-fulfillment framework's first-and-second-coming architecture made-explicit.

The framework reads the two-part answer in framework-substance:

- **Eliyahu (Elijah) truly shall first come, and restore all things** (v.11). The framework reads the future-tense-substance in continuity with Malachi 4:5-6's-eschatological-coming. The framework's-eschatological-filter (`_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §IV) holds — the second-coming Eliyahu is the Revelation 11 two-witnesses substance, the second-fulfillment of the Eliyahu-pattern at the dreadful-day-of-Yahuah preceding the King's-return. The restore-all-things substance is the restoration of the gathered-remnant's-covenant-life per Acts 3:21's *until the times of restitution of all things, which Elohim (God) hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began.* The framework reads this restoration as the prophesied gathering and restoration the prophets-name throughout Tanakh.
- **Eliyahu (Elijah) is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed** (v.12). The framework reads the past-tense-substance as the first-fulfillment-level — Yochanan (John) the Immerser as the Eliyahu-pattern prophet. The Matt 11:14 *this is Eliyahu (Elijah), which was for to come* is now restated-explicitly. The *they knew him not* substance reads in framework-substance as the Pharisaic-Sadducean-system's-rejection of the Eliyahu-pattern witness; the *have done unto him whatsoever they listed* substance reads in framework-substance as the Herod-Antipas / Herodias execution of Yochanan (John) at Matt 14:1-12 — the Achav (Ahab) / Yezevel (Jezebel) Tanakh-architecture recapitulating at the Herod-Antipas / Herodias level per `eliyahu-pattern-prophet-martyrdom` thread (PROPOSED at Matt 14).

**The framework reads the *likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them* substance** at the end of v.12 as the King's-explicit-tying of his own coming-passion to the Eliyahu-pattern-prophet's-martyrdom. ## §5. The temple-tax-coin from the fish's-mouth

*And when they were come to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), they that received tribute money came to Kefa (Peter), and said, Doth not your master pay tribute? He saith, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Yahusha (Jesus) prevented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Shim'on (Simon)? of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers? **The framework reads vv.24-27 as the King's-teaching of the children-are-free architecture demonstrated via the miraculous-tax-payment from the fish's-mouth.**

**The tribute-money substance** (v.24). The framework reads the tribute-money in framework-substance. The *didrachma* (Greek for two-drachma) is the half-shekel temple-tax of Shemot (Exodus) 30:11-16 — the atonement-money required of every-male-Yashar'eli (Israelite)-over-twenty-years for the support of the tabernacle/temple service. The framework reads the half-shekel substance in continuity with the Tanakh's-atonement-money-pattern — the tax was levied for the Temple's-maintenance and was understood as the people's-atonement-substance.

By the first-century, the half-shekel temple-tax was an annual-collection administered by the Temple authorities. After 70 AD, Vespasian redirected the half-shekel collection to the *fiscus Iudaicus* (the Roman Jewish-tax) supporting the Capitoline Jupiter temple in Rome — a Roman-substitution that would have read to faithful Yahudim (Jewish) as antichrist-substance under Tanakh-prophetic-substance. At the time of Matt 17, the tax was still going to the Temple-service.

**Kefa's (Peter's) response and the King's-question** (vv.25-26). The framework reads Kefa's-quick-affirmative-response in framework-substance — Kefa speaks-before-asking-the-King; the King anticipates Kefa's-return and preempts him with the kings-of-the-earth-take-custom-of-strangers question. The framework reads the King's-question as the Tanakh-precedent-application — Davidic-kings did not tax-their-own-sons; tribute-was-collected from foreign-peoples or from subject-populations.

**The *children are free* architecture** (v.26). The framework reads the King's-conclusion *then are the children free* carefully. The framework reads this in framework-substance — the King is the Son of the Father whose Temple this is. The half-shekel-tax goes to the Father's-Temple service; the Father's-Son and his-disciples (who-are-children-by-the-Son's-adoption-substance) are categorically-exempt under the Tanakh-precedent. The framework reads this NOT as the abolition of the Temple-tax-for-others but as the demonstration of the King's-categorical-identity — the King is the Owner-Son of the Temple, not a subject-paying-into-it.

The framework reads this in continuity with the *Lord-of-the-Shabbat* substance of Matt 12:8 — the King's-self-disclosure-of-categorical-identity over the institutions of the covenant. The King is the Lord-of-the-Shabbat; the King is the Son of the Temple-Father; the children-of-the-King are free. The framework reads this in continuity with the Yochanan (John) 8:35-36 *the Son abideth ever. If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed* substance.

**The miraculous-tax-payment from the fish's-mouth** (v.27). *Notwithstanding, lest we should offend them, go thou to the sea, and cast an hook, and take up the fish that first cometh up; and when thou hast opened his mouth, thou shalt find a piece of money: that take, and give unto them for me and thee.*

The framework reads this in framework-substance. The King could-have-claimed-the-exemption but does-not — the *lest we should offend them* substance positions the King's-payment as the disciples'-witness-substance, not as the King's-obligation-substance. The framework reads this in continuity with the King's-Sermon-substance of Matt 5:14-16 (the kingdom-citizens-as-the-light-of-the-world) and Romans 14:13-21 / 1 Corinthians 8 / 1 Corinthians 10:23-33 (the apostolic-substance of voluntary-restraint-for-the-sake-of-others).

The *piece of money* (Greek *statēr* — a four-drachma coin, exactly equal to two half-shekels — the tax for two men) reads at the precise-substance level — the coin is sufficient for the King and for Kefa, no more, no less. The framework reads the miraculous-substance carefully — the King exercises the Formed-cloud-rider's-sovereign-creation-authority (Job 38, Tehillim (Psalm) 8:8 *whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas* substance; Yonah (Jonah)'s-great-fish-pattern) at the small-domestic-substance-level. The King has-walked-on-the-sea at Matt 14; he has-fed-the-multitudes from-five-loaves and from-seven-loaves at Matt 14 and Matt 15; he now demonstrates-the-sovereign-creation-authority at the coin-in-the-fish's-mouth level.

The framework reads the substance in continuity with Yonah (Jonah)'s-great-fish appointed-by-Yahuah (the LORD) at Yonah (Jonah) 1:17 — the sovereign-Owner-of-the-sea appoints fish-and-coins to his purposes. The framework reads the *sign-of-Yonah* trajectory carrying into the small-domestic-substance — the King's-sovereign-authority extends from the resurrection-credential level to the tax-payment level.

The framework reads the coin-paid-for-me-and-thee substance carefully. The King pays-for-himself (categorically-free but voluntarily-paying for the witness-substance) and for Kefa (who-as-a-son-of-the-Father-by-the-Son's-adoption is also categorically-free but voluntarily-paying). The framework reads this in framework-substance as the pre-figuration of the King's-payment-of-the-substitutionary-substance at the cross — the categorically-exempt King pays the substance the categorically-bound owe. The half-shekel-as-atonement-money substance carries the pre-figuration weight — the King pays the atonement-substance for himself-as-categorically-free and for the disciple-as-categorically-free, demonstrating the substance the cross will fulfill at the full-atonement-level.

The framework reads the chapter as a unified-arc: the transfiguration-glory-on-the-mountain (vv.1-8) — the Formed-Son in glorified-form with the Father's-voice; the Eliyahu-pattern-completed (vv.9-13); the kingdom-power-flowing-through-faith (vv.14-21); the second-passion-prediction (vv.22-23); the children-are-free-but-the-King-pays (vv.24-27). The glory-on-the-mountain is bracketed by the passion-trajectory at both ends; the transfiguration is the foretaste-of-the-glory the passion-will-reach-by-the-cross-and-resurrection-and-coming-in-glory.
