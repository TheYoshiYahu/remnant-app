# Short-form commentary on Matthew 18

Matthew 18 is the King's fourth major discourse — the kingdom-citizens' internal architecture for the assembly the King is building. The chapter opens with the disciples' question of who-is-the-greatest and the King's answer in the form of a little-child set in the midst. The chapter closes with the unforgiving-servant parable and the chapter-closing word that the Father's forgiveness of his disciples is conditioned on their forgiveness of their brothers from the heart. Between those two ends the King teaches the millstone-warning against causing the little-ones to stumble, the lost-sheep parable of the shepherd who leaves the ninety-and-nine for the one, the four-step assembly-discipline procedure with the binding-and-loosing-authority now extended from Kefa (Peter) alone at Matthew 16:19 to the assembly as a whole, and the seventy-times-seven forgiveness answer that inverts the seventy-and-sevenfold vengeance of Lamekh (Lamech) in Genesis 4:24. Five movements anchor the chapter. The greatness-as-child teaching with the receive-one-such-little-child substance (vv.1-5). The millstone-woe against causing offence to the little-ones with the cut-off-hand-foot and pluck-out-eye sin-amputation against the gehenna-of-fire (vv.6-9). The despise-not-the-little-ones warning with their-angels-behold-the-Father and the lost-sheep parable with the Father's-will-substance (vv.10-14). The four-step assembly-discipline procedure with the binding-and-loosing-extended-to-the-assembly and the two-or-three-gathered-in-my-name substance (vv.15-20). Kefa's (Peter's) question and the seventy-times-seven answer with the unforgiving-servant parable and the chapter-closing word on forgiveness from the heart (vv.21-35).

*At the same time came the disciples unto Yahusha (Jesus), saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven? And Yahusha (Jesus) called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them, and said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven. Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven. And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me.* (Matthew 18:1-5)

*Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?* The disciples come to the King with the question that has been moving among them. Mark's gospel preserves what Matthew omits: *and he came to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum): and being in the house he asked them, What was it that ye disputed among yourselves by the way? But they held their peace: for by the way they had disputed among themselves, who should be the greatest* (Mark 9:33-34). The disciples have been disputing the question on the road. They have just come down from the high mountain of the transfiguration where three of them saw the foretaste-of-the-coming and the other nine failed to cast out the demon from the boy. The inner-circle-three who were on the mountain have a thing to defend; the nine who stayed below have a thing to recover from. Both reach for hierarchical position. The disciples have imbibed substantial kingdom-substance over months of ministry; the disciples have not yet shed the natural-kingdoms' hierarchy-substance. The King will address it directly at Matthew 20:25-28 — *ye know that the princes of the goyim (Gentiles) exercise dominion over them, and they that are great exercise authority upon them. But it shall not be so among you: but whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister; and whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant.* Here at Matthew 18, the King addresses it through a child set in the middle of them.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them.* The visible teaching-device. The King could have answered the disciples' question with words alone; instead, he draws a particular child into the conversation and stands the child in the center of the gathered men. The acted-parable belongs to the Tanakh's-prophetic vocabulary. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) walked naked and barefoot for three years as a sign-and-wonder against Egypt and Ethiopia (Isaiah 20:2-4). Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) was sent down to the potter's house to watch the wheel break and the vessel be reformed (Jeremiah 18:1-6). Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) was made to lie on his side for the iniquity of the house of Yashar'el (Israel) (Ezekiel 4:4-6); he was made to eat his bread by weight and drink his water by measure (Ezekiel 4:9-17); he was made to bring forth his stuff at evening as one going into captivity (Ezekiel 12:1-7); he was made to keep silent when his wife died as a sign to the people (Ezekiel 24:15-24). Hoshea (Hosea) was commanded to take a wife of whoredoms and name his children Lo-Ammi and Lo-Ruhamah as the sign of the divorce-judgment against the northern house (Hosea 1:2-9). The acted-parable runs through the prophets. The King takes the child and sets the child in the middle of the disciples as the visible-substance of his answer.

*Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.* The Greek for *converted* is *straphēte* — *turned around.* The Tanakh-substance is *shuv* — the turning back, the repentance-substance the prophets named at the heart of the covenant-renewal. *And shalt return unto Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God), and shalt obey his voice* (Deuteronomy 30:2). *Turn thou me, and I shall be turned; for thou art Yahuah my Elohim (the LORD my God). Surely after that I was turned, I repented* (Jeremiah 31:18-19). *O Yashar'el (Israel), return unto Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God); for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity* (Hosea 14:1). *Therefore also now, saith Yahuah (the LORD), turn ye even to me with all your heart* (Joel 2:12). The turning-around is the kingdom-entry-condition. And the turning lands the disciple in the disposition of a little child.

Hold what the child-substance is and what it is not. The institutional-Christian-spiritualization of childhood has taken the verse as a sentimental gesture toward childlike-innocence as an aesthetic — a soft, warm, vaguely-Romantic image of children as morally pure beings who teach their elders by their unsullied presence. That is not what the King is naming. The Hebrew-Roots-false-inclusion-reading takes the verse as universal — anyone can become as a little child by simple willingness, and the kingdom is therefore open to anyone who adopts the disposition — and again the substance is collapsed. The King is naming four specific dispositions the child carries that the adult-disciple is being called back to.

The child's humility. The child does not claim hierarchical position. The child has not yet learned to compete with siblings for primacy at the family table; the child has not yet learned to lobby the parent for preferment over peers. The child receives what the parent gives. The disciples who have been disputing on the road over who is the greatest have not yet shed this hierarchical-substance. *Before honour is humility* (Proverbs 15:33). *By humility and the fear of Yahuah (the LORD) are riches, and honour, and life* (Proverbs 22:4). *For thus saith the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy; I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones* (Isaiah 57:15). *To this man will I look, even to him that is poor and of a contrite spirit, and trembleth at my word* (Isaiah 66:2). The Tanakh-*anawim* (the humble-poor) are the substance the Father dwells with. The kingdom-citizen's greatness is measured by the depth of the humility-substance, not by the height of the position-substance.

The child's receptiveness. The child trusts the parent's instruction. The child does not adjudicate the parent's teaching against the child's own prior-frameworks; the child does not weigh the parent's words against the consensus of a peer-group of children and decide whether the parent is credible. The child hears and receives. The disciples must come into this disposition before the substance the King is teaching can land in them. The Pharisaic and Sadducean leaven the King had warned of at Matthew 16:6 was the leaven of the adult-system that adjudicates the King's teaching against its own prior-architecture and rejects what does not fit. The child-disciple does not do that. *Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger* (Psalm 8:2) — the King will quote this verse against the chief priests and scribes at Matthew 21:16 when they complain about the children crying *Hosanna to the son of David* in the temple. The child's receptive-substance is the substance the Father has ordained to silence the systems that have closed against the kingdom-substance.

The child's dependence. The child has no illusion of self-sufficiency. The child cannot feed himself, clothe himself, defend himself, or even direct himself; the child knows it. The disciple who has imbibed the substance of the kingdom must come to the same recognition — that the disciple is in the kingdom because the Father called him and the Son set him in the assembly, not because the disciple's own resources or competencies earned him the place. *My soul is even as a weaned child. Let Yashar'el (Israel) hope in Yahuah (the LORD) from henceforth and for ever* (Psalm 131:2-3). The weaned-child has stopped grasping for what the breast cannot continue to provide; the weaned-child rests in the mother's lap and trusts her for what the next provision will be. The disciple is to be the weaned-child resting in the Father's lap, trusting the Father for the kingdom-provision the Son has secured.

The child's honesty. The child has not yet learned to mask internal-state with external performance. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness pattern of Matthew 6:1-18 — the alms-trumpeted, the prayer-publicly-displayed, the fasting-with-the-disfigured-face-so-as-to-appear-unto-men — has not yet set in. The child says what is so. The disciple is called back to that substance. The kingdom-citizen's *yea* is *yea* and *nay* is *nay* per Matthew 5:37; the child still operates that way naturally. The adult-system that the disciples have absorbed teaches the opposite — say what gets results, posture for the position, manage the appearance. The kingdom-substance reverses the system.

*Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.* The inversion lands. The disciples ask *who is the greatest?* and the King answers in terms of humility-substance, not in terms of position-substance. The kingdom's-greatness is measured by the humility-substance. The teaching will be restated at Matthew 23:11-12 against the scribes and the Pharisees — *but he that is greatest among you shall be your servant. And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.* The kingdom-inversion will run through the rest of the gospel and into the apostolic-witness: *Elohim (God) resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble* (1 Peter 5:5, citing Proverbs 3:34). *Humble yourselves in the sight of Yahuah (the LORD), and he shall lift you up* (James 4:10).

*And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me.* The receive-the-little-child substance extends what the King has just taught. The disciple is not only to become-as-a-child; the disciple is to receive-the-children-the-Father-sends. The substance is the same substance the King will name at the sheep-and-goats judgment of Matthew 25:31-46 — *inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.* The kingdom-citizen's disposition is tested at the level of how the citizen treats those who cannot reciprocate, who have no power-substance to confer, who are easily overlooked. *In my name* — *epi tō onomati mou,* upon-my-name — is the disposition-substance of receiving on the King's-authority, not on the disciple's-own-preference. The disciple receives the child because the King-stands-behind-the-child, not because the child has earned the receiving.

*But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea. Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh! Wherefore if thy hand or thy foot offend thee, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into everlasting fire. And if thine eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into hell fire.* (Matthew 18:6-9)

The King now turns from the disciple's disposition toward the child to the disciple's-and-the-system's responsibility for not-causing-the-child-to-stumble. The substance is one of the heaviest warnings the King issues in the gospel.

*Whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me.* The Greek for *offend* is *skandalisē* — to cause-to-stumble. The Tanakh-*mikshol* substance — the stumbling-block — runs through the prophets as the deliberate-or-careless act of placing-a-thing-in-the-way-of-a-walker that causes the walker to fall. *Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy Elohim (God): I am Yahuah (the LORD)* (Leviticus 19:14). *Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way, take up the stumblingblock out of the way of my people* (Isaiah 57:14). The kingdom-citizen's-responsibility is the opposite of the placing-of-the-stumbling-block; the kingdom-citizen-who-becomes-the-substance-that-causes-the-stumble has reversed the kingdom-disposition into its opposite.

The *little ones which believe in me* are not exclusively children-in-age. The substance extends to all believers-in-the-vulnerable-position — the new-disciple who has just begun to hear, the awakening-believer who is still coming out of the system they inherited, the believer-without-the-credentials-of-the-religious-establishment who can be easily steered by the credentialed-class. The Tanakh's-vulnerable-protected pattern runs through the Torah and the prophets. *Thou shalt neither vex a stranger, nor oppress him: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt. Ye shall not afflict any widow, or fatherless child. If thou afflict them in any wise, and they cry at all unto me, I will surely hear their cry; And my wrath shall wax hot, and I will kill you with the sword* (Exodus 22:21-24). *Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of the stranger, nor of the fatherless; nor take a widow's raiment to pledge* (Deuteronomy 24:17). *Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow* (Isaiah 1:17). *A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is Elohim (God) in his holy habitation* (Psalm 68:5). The vulnerable have a particular standing with the Father; the wrath against those who exploit them is named directly in the Torah. The King is announcing that the same architecture now operates around the little-ones-who-believe in him.

*It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.* The deliberate-extreme-hyperbole. The Greek *mylos onikos* names the upper-stone of the grinding-mill — the heavy stone-wheel turned by donkey-power, weighing in the range of several hundred pounds, by no human-strength removable once tied. The picture is grotesque on purpose. The kingdom-citizen-who-causes-the-little-one-who-believes-to-stumble is in a position where the millstone-and-the-sea would have been the mercy. The Mark gospel preserves the same teaching at Mark 9:42; the Luke gospel at Luke 17:1-2 carries the equivalent phrase — *it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend one of these little ones.* The substance is consistent across the gospels. The King is naming the seriousness with which the Father holds the offense against the vulnerable-believer.

The offense-substance is not narrowly the individual disciple's-personal sin against an individual believer. The substance is also the structural-doctrinal substance the systems perpetuate against the believers in their care. Institutional Christianity's antinomian doctrine is the offense-substance at the doctrinal level — the inherited pulpit teaches the little-ones-who-believe that the Torah is bondage, that the law is the curse, that the believer is *not under law but under grace* in a register where grace cancels-the-standard rather than restores-the-walker. The little-ones-who-believe stumble in their kingdom-walk because they have been taught to reject the very substance the Spirit of Yahuah (the LORD) enables them to keep — the Spirit *will cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them* (Ezekiel 36:27). The Hebrew-Roots false-inclusion gospel is the offense-substance at the lineage-collapse level — the doctrine teaches the little-ones that any believer who Torah-keeps is thereby Israel, regardless of bloodline, regardless of the prophetic-restoration architecture, and the little-ones stumble at the seed-distinction the prophets maintain. The Pharisaic traditions-of-the-elders were the offense-substance at the law-overriding level the King had dismantled at Matthew 15:1-9 — the Korban-evasion that voided the fifth commandment under the cloak of religious-zeal. The Galatian agitators' circumcision-party at the time of the apostolic-letters was the offense-substance at the inversion-of-the-covenant-order — telling the scattered seed they had to become Yahudim (Jews) by ritual-flesh before they could come into the covenant, when the covenant was already theirs by the Father's promise to Avraham (Abraham) and was being received through the King the prophets had named. The millstone-warning lands on the systems and on the leadership-who-perpetuate-them. The little-ones-themselves are victims, not enemies. The architecture of the warning is system-aimed; the patience-toward-the-deceived holds throughout.

*Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh.* The King names the inevitability and maintains the responsibility together. The present-age operates under the watcher-rebellion administration — *we know that the whole world lieth in wickedness* (1 John 5:19); the seed-war continues; the offenses come because the architecture-of-the-fallen-age guarantees they will. The inevitability does not excuse the man-by-whom-the-offense-comes. The architecture is the same the prophet had named at Isaiah 24:5-6 — *the earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant. Therefore hath the curse devoured the earth, and they that dwell therein are desolate.* The earth is defiled by its inhabitants; the curse devours; the responsibility for the defiling stays with those who do the defiling.

*Wherefore if thy hand or thy foot offend thee, cut them off, and cast them from thee.* The King now turns the warning inward to the disciple's own substance. The same stumbling-block substance that operates between disciples can operate inside the single disciple. The hand-and-foot-and-eye that occasion the disciple's-own stumble must be cut off, cast away, plucked out. The teaching parallels Matthew 5:29-30 in the Sermon on the Mount; the Mark gospel preserves the same teaching at Mark 9:43-48 with the additional citation of the prophet's *their worm dieth not, and the fire is not quenched.* The substance is hyperbolic. The King is not commanding literal-self-mutilation; the substance is the kingdom-citizen's-radical-disposition against the disciple's-own-stumbling-substance, to be amputated rather than managed.

Hold what the inherited-Christian-once-saved-always-saved doctrine cannot account for in this teaching. The cross does not cancel the disciple's-responsibility for the disciple's-walk. The cross opens the door home; the door remains real; the walker walks through the door and into the way the door opens onto. The walker who tolerates the hand-or-foot-or-eye that causes the walker's own stumbling-substance is walking against the door the cross opens. The amputation is the disciple's-substance-response to the cross-substance-given. The substance is in continuity with what the apostle writes the Corinthians — *I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway* (1 Corinthians 9:27). The apostle who preached the grace-substance-of-the-cross also disciplined his own body for fear of becoming a castaway. The two substances are not in tension; the two substances are one motion of one obedience.

*Cast into everlasting fire ... cast into hell fire.* The Greek in v.8 is *eis to pyr to aiōnion* — *into the age-during fire* — and the Greek in v.9 is *eis tēn geennan tou pyros* — *into the gehenna of fire.* The institutional-Christian-doctrine of an eternal-conscious-torment-hell has been read back into these phrases for centuries, and that read-back is what the Tanakh-substance does not carry. The same read-back the King's-*gates of hell* statement at Matthew 16:18 suffered — *hadēs* there as *grave-realm,* not as the medieval-Christian-hell-of-eternal-conscious-torment — operates here on *gehenna.* The substance is recoverable from the Tanakh.

The word *gehenna* is the Greek transliteration of the Aramaic-Hebrew *gei-Hinnom* — the Valley of the sons of Hinnom (*gei ben-Hinnom*), a specific geographical valley running along the southern and western edges of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem). The valley appears repeatedly in the Tanakh, and what it carries is judgment-substance attached to a particular-historical-place.

*And he defiled Topheth, which is in the valley of the children of Hinnom, that no man might make his son or his daughter to pass through the fire to Molech.* (2 Kings 23:10)

The valley had been the place where the apostate-kings of Yahudah (Judah) had passed their sons and daughters through the fire to Molech and Ba'al — the abomination-of-child-sacrifice that the prophets named as the substance the curse-of-Deuteronomy-28 came down upon. Yoshiyah (Josiah) the king cleansed the valley as part of the reformation; the valley became, from his reign forward, the dump where the refuse of the city was burned. The fires burned continuously; the worms consumed what the fires did not. The prophet had named the valley as the place where the judgment-substance would land at the day-of-the-LORD:

*Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that it shall no more be called Topheth, nor the valley of the son of Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter: for they shall bury in Topheth, till there be no place. And the carcases of this people shall be meat for the fowls of the heaven, and for the beasts of the earth; and none shall fray them away.* (Jeremiah 7:32-33)

And the closing verse of the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) names the substance at the consummation-of-the-age:

*And they shall go forth, and look upon the carcases of the men that have transgressed against me: for their worm shall not die, neither shall their fire be quenched; and they shall be an abhorring unto all flesh.* (Isaiah 66:24)

The gehenna-substance the King names is the judgment-substance of the Tanakh's-valley-of-Hinnom carried forward to the eschatological-day-of-the-LORD. The substance is real; the judgment is real; the warning the King issues is severe. What the substance is not is the medieval-Christian-eternal-conscious-torment-hell read back into the term — the doctrine that pictures the wicked maintained in conscious existence under torture without end as the Father's perpetual-active-judgment on them. The Tanakh's-gehenna is a place of destruction, not a place of maintenance. The wicked perish in the gehenna; the worm consumes what the fire does not; the substance is the carcases of the men that have transgressed, not the perpetually-tormented-souls-maintained-for-the-purpose. The institutional-Christian-doctrine has built on a Greek-philosophical-soul-immortality assumption the Tanakh does not carry. The kingdom-citizen takes the warning seriously because the destruction is real; the kingdom-citizen does not import the eternal-conscious-torment substance the Tanakh does not name and that does not stand against the textual-substance.

The two-fold warning of v.8 and v.9 — *everlasting fire* and *gehenna of fire* — both carry the same Tanakh-substance. *Aiōnion* (everlasting, age-during) names the duration the gehenna-fire burns in the eschatological-day; the wicked who are cast into it perish under that duration. The substance the disciple is warned about is real destruction. The disciple cuts off the hand, the foot, the eye that causes the stumble because the kingdom-entry-criterion is real and the kingdom-entry-failure is destruction. *It is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed.* Life is the kingdom's-substance, the resurrection-substance, the inheritance-substance the prophets named. The maimed-disciple-entering-the-kingdom is incomparably better off than the whole-disciple-cast-into-the-gehenna. The teaching lands as a deliberate-extreme to drive the disciple's-disposition against the disciple's-own-stumbling-substance.

*Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones; for I say unto you, That in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father which is in heaven. For the Son of Adam is come to save that which was lost. How think ye? if a man have an hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doth he not leave the ninety and nine, and goeth into the mountains, and seeketh that which is gone astray? And if so be that he find it, verily I say unto you, he rejoiceth more of that sheep, than of the ninety and nine which went not astray. Even so it is not the will of your Father which is in heaven, that one of these little ones should perish.* (Matthew 18:10-14)

The chapter pivots now from the warning against causing the stumble to the protection extended over the little-ones and the pursuit the shepherd will undertake for any who go astray. The substance is one motion. The same little-ones the King has just warned the disciples not to offend are the same little-ones whose angels stand in the Father's presence and who are sought when they stray.

*Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones.* The Greek for *despise* is *kataphronēsēte* — to think-down-on, to hold-in-contempt. The disciples' natural-disposition from v.1 — the hierarchical-question — was a disposition that despises what is lower-on-the-hierarchy. The King names the kingdom-disposition as the opposite: do not despise. Do not consider the little-one as beneath your notice. Do not write off the new-believer who-cannot-yet-articulate-what-they-know; do not write off the awakening-disciple-who-is-still-coming-out; do not write off the believer-without-the-credentials. The disposition the King is forming is the kingdom-citizen-who-pays-attention to the very members the system overlooks.

*For I say unto you, That in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father which is in heaven.* The Tanakh-and-Second-Temple guardian-messenger pattern. The Torah names the messenger-of-Yahuah (the LORD) walking before the people through the wilderness — *behold, I send a messenger before thee, to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared* (Exodus 23:20). The Psalms name the messenger-charge over the kingdom-walker — *for he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways* (Psalm 91:11). The prophet Daniel names the prince-of-Persia and Michael-the-prince and the various angelic-substances operating around the nations — *but the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me* (Daniel 10:13). The book of Acts records the apostolic-assembly's recognition of the guardian-messenger substance — when Kefa (Peter) was released from prison and came to the door, the gathered assembly said *it is his angel* (Acts 12:15). The letter to the Hebrews summarizes the architecture: *are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?* (Hebrews 1:14). The little-ones have messengers; the messengers stand-before-the-Father with continuous-access; the Father's-attention is upon-the-little-ones through the messengers' standing-before-him. The disciple who despises the little-one is despising a substance the Father attends to directly through his messengers. The architecture of the substance is what the King is laying down.

*For the Son of Adam is come to save that which was lost.* The verse the King speaks is the kingdom's-mission-statement in compressed form. The Lukan parallel at Luke 19:10 carries it explicitly — *for the Son of Adam is come to seek and to save that which was lost.* The Greek for *that which was lost* is *to apolōlos* — the perished, the destroyed, the cut-off. The substance reaches back to the King's-own mission-scope statement at the sending-out of the twelve: *go not into the way of the goyim (Gentiles), and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not: But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Matthew 10:5-6). And forward to the Canaanite-woman pericope: *I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Matthew 15:24). The lost-sheep-of-the-house-of-Yashar'el (Israel) are the scattered-seed of the prophet's-restoration the King has come to gather. The verse at Matthew 18:11 is the same mission-scope restated. The Son of Adam — the seed of Adam, the seed of promise, the One in whom the lineage of Avraham (Abraham), Yitschaq (Isaac), and Ya'aqov (Jacob) reaches its fullness — has come to save the lost-substance of his own.

*How think ye? if a man have an hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doth he not leave the ninety and nine, and goeth into the mountains, and seeketh that which is gone astray?* The parable proper. The shepherd's-substance is the substance of the man who-cannot-leave-the-one-to-the-mountain-alone. The ninety-and-nine are safe; the one is at risk; the shepherd goes after the one. The substance lands at two levels simultaneously.

The first level is the assembly's-internal-little-ones in the chapter-context. The chapter's framework-major moment lands here. The King is laying down the assembly's-internal-procedure for restoration when a brother has trespassed, and the binding-and-loosing-authority first given to Kefa (Peter) at Matthew 16:19 is now extended explicitly to the assembly as a whole. The architecture of apostolic-authority moves from the individual Kefa (Peter)-as-first-confessor to the corporate-assembly-as-gathered-body.

The four-step assembly-discipline procedure. Step one: *go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone.* The private-confrontation. The Tanakh-substance is the direct command of Leviticus 19:17 — *thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.* The Torah commands the direct-rebuke as the alternative to harboring-the-grievance-in-the-heart or to gossiping-the-matter-through-the-community. The kingdom-citizen's-procedure is to confront the brother directly first, privately. *If he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.* The desired-outcome is restoration. The procedure exists for the gaining-back of the brother, not for the cataloging of the brother's offenses or for the bypassing-to-public-shame. The substance is restoration-aimed at every step.

Step two: *take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.* The Tanakh-evidentiary standard from Deuteronomy 19:15 — *one witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.* The witness-standard the Torah named for capital-and-civil-cases the King applies to the assembly's-internal-pastoral discipline. The witnesses' role is two-fold — to establish the substance of the trespass (so the matter is not the private-claim of one party against another but is a substance-confirmed by witnesses who can themselves attest), and to provide supplementary-witness to the brother who would-not-hear the private-confrontation. The witnesses also serve as the substance-restraint on the offended-party — the offended-party who would have to bring witnesses to substantiate the charge cannot inflate the charge or fabricate it without their participation. The Torah-substance protects both the brother-accused and the brother-accusing from the corruptions either could fall into. The apostle Paul will apply the same standard to leadership-discipline in the assembly — *against an elder receive not an accusation, but before two or three witnesses* (1 Timothy 5:19).

Step three: *if he shall neglect to hear them, tell it unto the assembly.* The Greek for *assembly* is *ekklēsia.* The second-explicit-use of the word in the gospel after Matthew 16:18, and the use here confirms the substance of the assembly the King is building. The *ekklēsia* is the called-out-ones of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed in continuity with the Tanakh's-*qahal* substance — the assembly of Yashar'el (Israel) gathered at Sinai (Exodus 19:5-6), the assembly of Yashar'el (Israel) hearing the Torah at the renewal-of-the-covenant (Deuteronomy 31:30, Joshua 8:35), the assembly-of-Yahuah (the LORD) the Psalms praise in (Psalm 22:22, cited Messianically at Hebrews 2:12). The *ekklēsia* the King is building is the present-age-form of the prophetic-gathering. The third step is the substance brought before the gathered-assembly so the assembly can speak its-corporate-witness to the brother. The substance is the brother's restoration; the assembly is now the assembly's-substance brought-to-bear.

Step four: *if he neglect to hear the assembly, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.* The Greek for *heathen man* is *ethnikos* — the man-of-the-nations, the one outside the covenant-membership the assembly-substance represents. *Publican* is *telōnēs* — the tax-collector, the substance the King's-own mission-scope had repeatedly engaged with at the table (Matthew 9:9-13's calling of Mattityahu (Matthew), Matthew 11:19's *friend of publicans and sinners*). Hold what this fourth step is and is not. The substance is not condemnation-to-permanent-exclusion. The substance is the disposition-shift from internal-assembly-member to mission-target. The unrepentant-brother is positioned as one to whom the kingdom-message must be re-proclaimed from-outside-the-assembly's-internal-fellowship — the very disposition the King had walked toward the publicans-and-sinners throughout his ministry. The assembly's-internal-fellowship is the substance temporarily-withdrawn pending-restoration; the kingdom-witness-toward-the-brother continues. The institutional-Christian-collapse of this step into permanent-shunning-and-condemnation is what the Tanakh-substance and the King's-own-mission-disposition rule out. The brother who-has-refused the assembly-witness has placed himself outside the assembly's-internal-fellowship; the assembly's-mission-toward-the-brother is what continues. Restoration remains the goal at every step including this one.

*Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.* The substance the King had first given to Kefa (Peter) alone at Matthew 16:19 is now extended explicitly to the assembly. The binding-and-loosing in rabbinic vocabulary was the Pharisaic-and-rabbinic decision-making about what the Torah's-substance forbids and permits — *asar* (bind) and *hattir* (loose). The Pharisaic-system had used this authority to construct the fence-around-Torah that voided the Torah at points; the King's-extension of the authority to the apostolic-assembly is the explicit-transfer of the steward-substance from the corrupt-stewards to the apostolic-stewards. The keys of the kingdom-of-heaven that derive from the Isaiah 22:22 substance — *and the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open* — now operate through the apostolic-assembly as the stewards of the kingdom-substance.

The locus-classicus of the apostolic-binding-and-loosing in action is the Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) Council of Acts 15. The question before the apostolic-assembly was the question of the scattered seed living as Gentiles-among-the-nations who were now returning to the King — what of the circumcision-party's demand that these returning-seed be circumcised after the manner of Mosheh (Moses) before they could be saved. The apostolic-council met, the apostles and elders spoke, and the apostolic-decision was rendered in the apostolic-letter: *for it seemed good to the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit), and to us, to lay upon you no greater burden than these necessary things; That ye abstain from meats offered to idols, and from blood, and from things strangled, and from fornication: from which if ye keep yourselves, ye shall do well* (Acts 15:28-29). The apostolic-assembly bound the four substances the returning-seed were to abstain from; the apostolic-assembly loosed the rest of the Pharisaic-tradition-around-circumcision-as-saving-rite the circumcision-party had been adding. The apostolic-assembly will exercise the same authority again across the apostolic-letters — Sha'ul (Paul) speaks the binding-and-loosing through 1 Corinthians 5 against the man-with-his-father's-wife and again at 2 Corinthians 2:5-11 when the man's-repentance has come; Sha'ul (Paul) speaks it across Galatians against the agitators; Yochanan (John) speaks it through the seven-letters of Revelation 2-3 against the corruptions in the seven assemblies. The substance the keys are stewarding through the assembly is the Torah-as-the-King-restored-it.

Hold what the binding-and-loosing-extended is and is not. The Roman-Catholic-magisterial-authority reading takes the binding-and-loosing as the church's-authority to define-doctrine-binding-on-the-conscience independent-of and even-against scripture — the assembly elevated to a position above the Torah-substance it stewards. The Tanakh-substance and the apostolic-witness do not stand against this reading; the substance rules it out. The apostolic binding-and-loosing operates in continuity-with the Torah-substance, not against it. The apostles are stewards of what the King-the-master-of-the-house has given; they administer the kingdom's-substance as stewards, not innovators. The papal-primacy-concentration-of-binding-and-loosing in Kefa (Peter)-as-the-first-pope is what the verse at Matthew 18:18 makes impossible — the very same authority was extended-to-the-assembly-as-a-whole, not concentrated in Kefa (Peter) alone and his successors. The assembly's-binding-and-loosing-authority is the apostolic-substance for naming the doctrinal-counterfeit and naming the doctrinal-truth at every generation of the kingdom-citizens.

*Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven.* The substance follows directly from the binding-and-loosing. The two-or-three-witnesses-standard of the assembly-discipline procedure carries over to the kingdom-citizens'-corporate-prayer substance. The two-or-three-agreeing on-earth is the same substance as the two-or-three-witnesses, now operating in the prayer-mode rather than the discipline-mode. Hold what this is not. The institutional-Christian-prayer-meeting reading that turns the verse into a generic-formula-for-prayer-power — gather two-or-three, agree on the request, the request is granted regardless of the request's-substance — is the misreading the verse cannot bear. *For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.* The King's-presence-substance with the kingdom-citizens'-corporate-substance. The Tanakh-precedent is at Malachi 3:16 — *then they that feared Yahuah (the LORD) spake often one to another: and Yahuah (the LORD) hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared Yahuah (the LORD), and that thought upon his name.* The kingdom-citizens' speaking-to-one-another in the fear of Yahuah (the LORD) was already a substance the Father attended-to in the Tanakh; the King now names the substance of his own personal-presence with the gathered-two-or-three. *In my name* — *eis to emon onoma,* into-my-name — is the substance the gathering-is-around, not merely the formula-recited-at-the-beginning. The gathering-in-the-King's-name is the gathering-that-makes-the-King-the-substance-of-the-gathering. Where that substance is, the King is in the midst.

Hold this against the institutional-Christian-individualistic-reading of the verse that treats v.20 as a generic-promise of the King's-presence with the lone-believer who-prays-alone. The substance is the corporate-substance gathered-in-the-King's-name. The Mishnah (Avot 3:2, 3:6) had named the Shekinah-substance among even two students-of-Torah gathered together; the King's-substance carries the Tanakh-trajectory forward into the assembly-substance with the King's-personal-presence now substituted for the abstract-Shekinah-presence the rabbis had taught. The King-is-the-presence the gathering-in-his-name is the gathering-into. The two-or-three-gathered is the kingdom-assembly in its smallest faithful-form; the King's-presence sanctifies the smallest faithful-form as fully as the largest.

*Then came Kefa (Peter) to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times? Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.* (Matthew 18:21-22)

Kefa (Peter) has been listening through the assembly-discipline-procedure. The procedure has assumed a brother-who-sins, an offended-party, a confrontation, a possible-restoration. Kefa's (Peter's) question takes the procedure-substance and presses it on the personal-substance: *how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him?* And Kefa (Peter), already accustomed to the kingdom-substance exceeding the system-substance, proposes a number he thinks is generous — *till seven times?* The rabbinic-tradition of the day taught that forgiveness was due three times; on the fourth offense, the offended party was free to refuse forgiveness. Kefa (Peter) more-than-doubles the rabbinic-standard. He thinks he is being generous.

*I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.* The Greek is *hebdomēkontakis hepta* — *seventy times seven,* or possibly *seventy-seven* (the construction is grammatically ambiguous and both readings have textual-support). The substance does not turn on the arithmetic. The Tanakh-substance the King is citing is the substance the architecture lands on.

The Tanakh-substance is Lamekh's (Lamech's) song at Genesis 4:23-24. Five generations from Adam in the line of Cain, Lamekh (Lamech) takes two wives, has children, and brags to his wives in the form of a song:

*And Lamekh (Lamech) said unto his wives, Adah and Tsillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamekh (Lamech), hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamekh (Lamech) seventy and sevenfold.* (Genesis 4:23-24)

Lamekh's (Lamech's) boast is the boast of escalated-vengeance. Cain had killed Hevel (Abel) and Yahuah (the LORD) had set the mark on Cain that promised sevenfold-vengeance on anyone who killed Cain (Genesis 4:15). Lamekh (Lamech) hears about that protection-clause and announces his own version — *if Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamekh (Lamech) seventy and sevenfold.* The Cainite-line that began with Cain's-murder-of-his-brother has now escalated-the-vengeance-substance through five generations to the point where Lamekh (Lamech) is killing-young-men over wounds-and-hurts and announcing the unlimited-retaliation-substance as his glory. The Cainite-line is the seed-war-line operating in its full-substance — the line that produces Yuval (Jubal) and Tuval-Cain (Tubal-cain) and the inventions-of-the-arts-and-of-metallurgy that the Tanakh names as the substance of the Cainite-civilization (Genesis 4:21-22), and the line that produces Lamekh's (Lamech's) escalating-vengeance-song-substance the same generation.

The King's-substance inverts Lamekh's (Lamech's)-substance exactly. *If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamekh (Lamech) seventy and sevenfold* becomes *until seventy times seven* — the same numerical-form, the opposite-substance. Lamekh's (Lamech's) seventy-and-sevenfold-vengeance becomes the King's-seventy-times-seven-forgiveness. The seed-war-line's escalation-substance is inverted into the kingdom-line's forgiveness-substance. The substance the King is naming is the practical-infinity of kingdom-forgiveness — the disciple does not count. The arithmetic is not the point; the disposition is the point. The kingdom-citizen who counts the offenses-forgiven against him as a budget-that-can-run-out has reverted to the Lamekh-substance under a cosmetic-overlay of forgiveness-language. The kingdom-citizen who does not count operates in the disposition the King is forming.

The substance is in continuity with the Tanakh's-Father-forgiveness pattern. *Yahuah (the LORD) is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy. He will not always chide: neither will he keep his anger for ever. He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities. For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward them that fear him. As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us* (Psalm 103:8-12). *Who is an El (God) like unto thee, that pardoneth iniquity, and passeth by the transgression of the remnant of his heritage? he retaineth not his anger for ever, because he delighteth in mercy. He will turn again, he will have compassion upon us; he will subdue our iniquities; and thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea* (Micah 7:18-19). *I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions for mine own sake, and will not remember thy sins* (Isaiah 43:25). The Father's-forgiveness-substance is the substance the kingdom-citizen's-forgiveness reflects. The disciple who has-received from the Father the seventy-times-seven extends the seventy-times-seven to the brother. The King is about to make the architecture explicit through the parable.

*Therefore is the kingdom of heaven likened unto a certain king, which would take account of his servants. And when he had begun to reckon, one was brought unto him, which owed him ten thousand talents. But forasmuch as he had not to pay, his lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife, and children, and all that he had, and payment to be made. The servant therefore fell down, and worshipped him, saying, Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay thee all. Then the lord of that servant was moved with compassion, and loosed him, and forgave him the debt. But the same servant went out, and found one of his fellowservants, which owed him an hundred pence: and he laid hands on him, and took him by the throat, saying, Pay me that thou owest. So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.* (Matthew 18:23-35)

The unforgiving-servant parable lays the architecture under Kefa's (Peter's)-question and the seventy-times-seven answer.

*Ten thousand talents.* The magnitude is staggering. The Greek *talanton* names a unit of weight and value; a single talent in the first-century is approximately 6,000 denarii; a denarius is roughly a day's-wage for a laborer; ten thousand talents is approximately sixty million denarii — roughly two hundred thousand years of a laborer's wages. The deliberate-hyperbole is on purpose. The substance the parable is naming is impossibility-of-repayment, not literal-accounting. The disciple's-substance of sin-debt before the Father is what the ten-thousand-talents represents — a debt the disciple cannot pay, not by labor, not by penance, not by the fullness of his life. The architecture-of-impossibility is the architecture-of-grace; the substance the cross will pay is the substance that no human-labor can.

*Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.* The servant's-petition. Hold the substance carefully. The servant does not ask for the debt-cancellation; the servant asks for time-to-pay. The petition assumes the servant's-ability to discharge the debt given enough patience. The petition is the substance of the man who-does-not-yet-understand the magnitude of his own debt. The lord could have granted the petition strictly — granted the time, watched the servant fail to pay, returned the substance to its original-status. The lord does not.

*Then the lord of that servant was moved with compassion, and loosed him, and forgave him the debt.* The lord-grants more than the petition. The Greek for *moved with compassion* is *splanchnistheis* — *was-moved-in-the-bowels,* the deep-visceral-substance the Tanakh-Father exercises toward his people. *And Elohim (God) heard their groaning, and Elohim (God) remembered his covenant with Avraham (Abraham), with Yitschaq (Isaac), and with Ya'aqov (Jacob). And Elohim (God) looked upon the children of Yashar'el (Israel), and Elohim (God) had respect unto them* (Exodus 2:24-25). The compassion-substance moves the lord beyond the bounds of the servant's-petition into the bounds of his own-merciful-substance. The lord forgives the debt that the servant had only asked time-to-pay. The grace-substance exceeds the petitioner's-substance-of-request. This is the kingdom's-grace.

The Tanakh-anchor under the lord's-act is the Jubilee-substance of the Torah. The Torah commands the release-of-debts every seventh year and the great-release in the Jubilee-year — every fiftieth year — when the debts are canceled, the slaves are freed, and the inheritances return to the family-lines from which they had been alienated:

*And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family. A jubile shall that fiftieth year be unto you: ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of thy vine undressed. For it is the jubile; it shall be holy unto you ... In the year of this jubile ye shall return every man unto his possession. And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbour, or buyest ought of thy neighbour's hand, ye shall not oppress one another.* (Leviticus 25:10-14)

The Jubilee-substance is the substance of debt-release at the appointed-time. The substance the lord performs in the parable is the Jubilee-substance compressed into the single-transaction — the unpayable-debt forgiven in the moment of the petitioner's-appeal-for-mercy. The substance the King is teaching is the kingdom-Jubilee — the Father's-act of releasing the unpayable-debt of the disciples through the cross-substance the King is about to walk into. The architecture of the substance is Levitical-Jubilee; the application is the kingdom-grace-substance the cross opens. The disciples will not see the architecture clearly until after the resurrection; the King is laying it down here as the parable they will return to.

*But the same servant went out, and found one of his fellowservants, which owed him an hundred pence.* The Greek *dēnaria hekaton* — a hundred denarii, roughly a hundred days' wages. The debt is real; the debt is significant; the debt is not trivial. But the debt is approximately six-hundred-thousand-times-less than the debt the lord had just forgiven the first servant. The architecture of the parable depends on the ratio. The first-servant has been forgiven what he could not pay; the first-servant immediately demands what his fellow-servant could-potentially-pay.

*And he laid hands on him, and took him by the throat, saying, Pay me that thou owest.* The substance of the first-servant's-disposition toward the fellow-servant is the seed-war-substance operating among fellow-servants. The taking-by-the-throat — the choking-grip — is the violence-substance of the Cainite-line operating now between two who-have-both-been-set-in-the-king's-house. The first-servant has been positioned by the lord's-grace; the first-servant operates against the fellow-servant in the disposition of the unforgiving-creditor.

*And his fellowservant fell down at his feet, and besought him, saying, Have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.* The fellow-servant's-petition mirrors the first-servant's-petition word for word. *Have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.* The same words, the same disposition — the same plea for time-to-pay. The architecture the King is constructing presses the parallel hard. The first-servant is hearing in the fellow-servant's-mouth the very petition he himself had just spoken to the lord — and refusing the petition the lord had honored. *And he would not: but went and cast him into prison, till he should pay the debt.* The kingdom-substance the first-servant had received does not flow through him to the fellow-servant. The substance stops at his own-substance.

*So when his fellowservants saw what was done, they were very sorry, and came and told unto their lord all that was done.* The other servants report the matter. The substance of what-the-first-servant-did is the substance the assembly attends-to. The kingdom-citizens' awareness of their fellow-citizens' walk is the substance of v.15-17 operating in the parable-form. *Then his lord, after that he had called him, said unto him, O thou wicked servant, I forgave thee all that debt, because thou desiredst me: shouldest not thou also have had compassion on thy fellowservant, even as I had pity on thee?* The lord's-charge is direct. The first-servant is *wicked* — Greek *ponēre,* the substance-of-wickedness, the same word the King had used for the wicked-one who sows the tares (Matthew 13:38-39). The substance the first-servant has shown is the seed-war-substance the lord's-grace had momentarily-suspended; the lord now names the substance for what it is.

*And his lord was wroth, and delivered him to the tormentors, till he should pay all that was due unto him.* The lord-revokes. The substance once-forgiven is restored-to-debt-status; the first-servant is now-required to pay what he had been previously-forgiven. The substance the lord had remitted comes back upon the first-servant because the first-servant has shown that the remission did not actually-transform him. The substance-the-grace-was-given-to-form did not form. The architecture lands directly on the disciple's-walk.

Hold what this is and what it is not against the institutional-Christian-once-saved-always-saved doctrine. The doctrine teaches that the substance-the-cross-paid cannot be revoked under any subsequent-disciple-substance — once the cross-substance has been received, the disciple-substance is permanently-secured against revocation regardless of how the disciple walks. The substance does not stand against the text. The unforgiving-servant parable is the most direct articulation in the gospels of the architecture that the substance the grace-has-given can be lost. The lord forgave; the lord-was-wroth; the lord delivered the same-servant who-had-been-forgiven to the tormentors. The forgiveness-given is reversed when the disposition-the-forgiveness-was-given-to-form does not form. The cross opened the door home; the door is real; the walker walks through the door and into the way that the door opens onto. The disciple who does not walk the substance is the disciple whose grace-substance has not formed him. The parable preserves the architecture in its starkest gospel-form.

The substance is in continuity with the King's-own teaching elsewhere. *For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses* (Matthew 6:14-15 — the explicit-conditional clause attached to the Lord's-Prayer). The conditional-substance is direct. *He shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment* (James 2:13 — the apostolic-restatement). *Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Messiah (Christ) forgave you, so also do ye* (Colossians 3:13). *Be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as Elohim (God) for Messiah's (Christ's) sake hath forgiven you* (Ephesians 4:32). The apostolic-witness across multiple letters lands at the same architecture — the disciple's-forgiveness-of-the-brother is the substance the disciple-has-received from the Father reaching its application in the disciple's-life. Where the application does not occur, the substance-received was not the substance-formed.

*So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.* The chapter's-closing word. The Father's-action toward the disciples is now the substance the parable has illustrated. The conditional is direct — *if ye from your hearts forgive not.* The Greek for *from your hearts* is *apo tōn kardiōn hymōn* — *from your hearts.* The substance must be substance-of-the-heart, not substance-of-the-mouth-or-of-the-public-display. The heart-Torah of the new-covenant the prophets named is the substance:

*But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Yashar'el (Israel); After those days, saith Yahuah (the LORD), I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their Elohim (God), and they shall be my people.* (Jeremiah 31:33)

*A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* (Ezekiel 36:26-27)

The Torah-substance is to be in the disciples' inward parts, written on the hearts, with the new-heart-of-flesh given by the Father and the Spirit causing the walking. The substance the chapter closes on is the heart-substance. The forgiveness-from-the-heart is the new-heart-substance operating; the forgiveness-of-the-mouth-without-the-heart is the substance the parable rules out as a non-substance the Father-will-respond-to with the revocation-of-the-forgiveness-given. The architecture the chapter closes on is the heart-architecture of the new-covenant the King is bringing.

The chapter as a unified arc. The kingdom-citizens'-greatness-as-child-substance (vv.1-5) → the millstone-warning against offending the little-ones with the cut-off-hand-foot-pluck-out-eye sin-amputation under the gehenna-of-the-Tanakh-valley-of-Hinnom-judgment (vv.6-9) → the despise-not-the-little-ones warning with the their-angels-behold-the-Father substance and the lost-sheep parable applied at both the assembly-internal-level and the canon-wide-lost-sheep-of-Yashar'el (Israel) mission-scope under the Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34 shepherd-substance (vv.10-14) → the four-step assembly-discipline procedure under the Torah-witness-and-evidentiary-standards with the binding-and-loosing-authority extended from Kefa (Peter) alone to the assembly as a whole and the two-or-three-gathered-in-the-King's-name substance under the Malachi 3:16 Tanakh-precedent (vv.15-20) → the seventy-times-seven inversion of Lamekh's (Lamech's) seventy-and-sevenfold vengeance with the unforgiving-servant parable as the gospel's-most-direct articulation of the architecture-of-the-grace-that-can-be-lost under the Levitical-Jubilee debt-release substance, closing on the heart-Torah substance of the new-covenant (vv.21-35). The internal architecture of the King's-assembly is laid out from the disposition through the protection through the seeking through the discipline through the forgiveness. The disciples have been given the kingdom-citizens'-blueprint for the assembly they will be set into after the resurrection. The next chapter will open with the King setting out from Galil (Galilee) toward the coasts of Yahudah (Judea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan) — the long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) the gospel has been pointing toward since Matthew 16:21 — and the Pharisees will meet him with the divorce-question, and the kingdom-citizens'-disposition toward the marriage-substance and the children-and-the-rich-young-ruler will land into the substance the assembly will be carrying.
