## §1. Greatness-as-child — the disciples'-question and the King's-answer

*At the same time came the disciples unto Yahusha (Jesus), saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven? And Yahusha (Jesus) called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them, and said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven. Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven. And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me* (vv.1-5).

**The framework reads vv.1-5 in framework-substance — the disciples'-question reveals the disciples' kingdom-misunderstanding; the King's-answer locks the kingdom-citizens'-greatness-substance as child-substance.**

**The disciples'-question** (v.1). *Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?* The framework reads the question carefully. The disciples are coming-off the transfiguration-vision (Matt 17:1-8); the inner-circle-three (Kefa (Peter), Ya'aqov (James), Yochanan (John)) have-witnessed the glorified-King and the Torah-and-Prophets witnesses; the natural-temptation-substance among-the-twelve is for hierarchy-substance to assert-itself. The Markan-parallel at Mark 9:33-34 specifies the disciples-had-been-disputing-among-themselves *who should be the greatest* — the question reaches the King after the disciples-have-been-jockeying-for-position internally.

The framework reads the disciples'-disposition in continuity with the broader-Tanakh-and-Apocrypha pattern of the seed-war operating through pride-and-hierarchy at the disciples'-level. The disciples have-imbibed-substantially of the kingdom-substance; they have not-yet shed-the-natural-kingdoms'-hierarchy-substance per `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration` thread. The King will address-this directly at Matt 20:25-28 (*ye know that the princes of the goyim (Gentiles) exercise dominion over them ... it shall not be so among you*); here at Matt 18, the King-addresses-it through the child-substance.

**The King's-action — calling and setting** (v.2). *And Yahusha (Jesus) called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them.* The framework reads the action as the visible-teaching-device — the King sets a little child in the midst before-speaking. The child becomes the visible-substance the King teaches-about. The framework reads this in continuity with the Tanakh's-prophetic-acted-parable pattern (Yeshayahu (Isaiah) walking naked-and-barefoot at Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 20:2-4; Yirmeyahu's (Jeremiah's) potter's-house at Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 18; Yechezk'el's (Ezekiel's)-acted-parables at Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 4-5, 12, 24; Hoshea's (Hosea's)-marriage at Hoshea (Hosea) 1).

**The conversion-and-childlikeness substance** (v.3). *Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.* The framework reads the *converted* (Greek *straphēte* — turned-around) substance per the Tanakh-*teshuvah* (turning) pattern (Devarim (Deuteronomy) 30:1-10; Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:18-19's *turn thou me, and I shall be turned*; Hoshea (Hosea) 6:1-3; Yo'el (Joel) 2:12-13). The kingdom-entry requires turning, and the turning requires becoming-as-little-children.

**The framework reads the little-children substance carefully.** The substance is NOT (a) Christian-spiritualization-of-childhood (the inherited-Reformation-reading that elevates child-substance to a sentimental-virtue while losing-the-Tanakh-grounding) NOR (b) the false-inclusion-gospel-pattern that universalizes child-substance into a generic-everyone-can-be-saved substance. The framework reads the child-substance in framework-substance:

- **The child's-humility** — the child does-not-claim hierarchical-position; the child receives what the parent gives.
- **The child's-receptiveness** — the child trusts the parent's-instruction; the child does not adjudicate the parent's-teaching against the child's-prior-frameworks.
- **The child's-dependence** — the child has-no-illusion of self-sufficiency before the parent's-provision.
- **The child's-honesty** — the child has-not-yet-learned to mask-internal-state with external-performance (the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness pattern of Matt 6:1-18 has not-yet-set-in).

The framework reads the child-substance in continuity with Tehillim (Psalm) 131:1-2's *my soul is even as a weaned child;* Tehillim (Psalm) 8:2's *out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength;* Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 11:6's *little child shall lead them* eschatological-substance.

**The greatness-substance** (v.4). *Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.* The framework reads the inversion — the disciples ask *who is the greatest?* and the King answers in terms of humility-substance, not in terms of position-substance. The kingdom-citizens'-greatness is measured by the humility-substance, not by the position-substance. The framework reads this in continuity with Mishlei (Proverbs) 11:2 (*before honour is humility*); Mishlei (Proverbs) 15:33; Mishlei (Proverbs) 22:4; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 57:15's *I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit;* Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 66:2's *to this man will I look, even to him that is poor and of a contrite spirit, and trembleth at my word.*

**The receive-one-such-little-child substance** (v.5). *And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me.* The framework reads the substance as the kingdom-citizens'-disposition-towards-those-who-cannot-reciprocate. The framework reads this in continuity with Matt 25:31-46's *inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me* sheep-and-goats substance — the kingdom-citizens'-disposition is tested at the receive-the-little-ones level.

The framework reads the *in my name* substance carefully. *In my name* (*epi tō onomati mou* — *upon my name*) is the kingdom-citizens'-substance-of-receiving — receiving on-the-King's-authority and on-the-King's-substance, not merely receiving-children-as-children-on-the-disciples'-own-preferences. The framework reads this in continuity with the Acts 4:12's-name-substance and the Yochanan (John) 14:13-14's-in-my-name-prayer-substance.

## §2. The millstone-warning + the eye-and-hand-and-foot sin-amputation teaching

*But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea. Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh! Wherefore if thy hand or thy foot offend thee, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into everlasting fire. And if thine eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into hell fire* (vv.6-9).

**The framework reads vv.6-9 in framework-substance — the King's-substantial-warning against offending the little ones + the disciple's-sin-amputation responsibility at the kingdom-citizens'-disposition level.**

**The millstone-warning** (v.6). *But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.* The framework reads the warning carefully.

The *offend* (Greek *skandalisē* — cause to stumble) substance reads in continuity with the Tanakh-*mikshol* (stumbling-block) pattern. The King's-warning is against the substance of causing the *little ones who believe* to stumble in their walk-of-faith. The King's-substance is *it were better* to suffer that execution than to be the substance who causes the stumble.

The framework reads the *little ones which believe in me* substance carefully. The little-ones are NOT exclusively-children-in-age — the substance extends to all believers-in-the-vulnerable-position. The framework reads this in continuity with the broader-canon's-vulnerable-protected-by-Yah pattern (Shemot (Exodus) 22:21-24's-widow-and-orphan; Devarim (Deuteronomy) 24:17-22's-stranger-fatherless-widow; Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 1:17's *seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow;* Tehillim (Psalm) 68:5's *a father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows*).

- **The Galatian-agitators'-circumcision-party** was the offence-substance at the inversion-of-covenant-order per Galatians' verse-by-verse framework in framework-deep.

The framework reads the millstone-warning's-application as the framework's-most-substantial-warning to any leader-or-system-that-causes-the-little-ones-to-stumble. The framework reads this in framework-substance as the King's-loading of the modern-Judaizer-architecture-warning at the eschatological-judgment-level.

**The *woe unto the world because of offences* substance** (v.7). The framework reads v.7 as the King's-acknowledgment of the inevitability-of-offences in the present-age (kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration per voice skill's framework — the seed-war ensures the offences-come) AND the maintained-responsibility of the man-by-whom-the-offence-cometh. **The eye-and-hand-and-foot sin-amputation teaching** (vv.8-9). *Wherefore if thy hand or thy foot offend thee, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into everlasting fire. The framework reads vv.8-9 as the King's-disciple-internal-application of the offence-substance from vv.6-7. The framework reads the substance carefully:

The *everlasting fire* and *hell fire* (Greek *to pyr to aiōnion* and *tēn geennan tou pyros*) substance reads at the Tanakh-substance level. ## §3. The lost-sheep-shepherd parable — and the Father's-will none-of-these-little-ones-perish

How think ye? if a man have an hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doth he not leave the ninety and nine, and goeth into the mountains, and seeketh that which is gone astray? ## §4. The assembly-discipline pattern + binding-and-loosing extended + two-or-three-gathered
