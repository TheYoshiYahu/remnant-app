# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 19

Matthew 19 opens the King's long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem). The fourth major discourse closed at Matthew 18; the King now departs from Galil (Galilee) and crosses into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan), and the chapter lays out the kingdom-citizens'-substance toward four substances the inherited systems have most thoroughly confused — marriage, children, possessions, and reward. Five movements anchor the chapter. (1) The geographic-shift to Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan) (vv.1-2). The Matthean discourse-closing-formula *when Yahusha (Jesus) had finished these sayings* marks the fourth-of-the-King's-five-discourses complete (Matthew 7:28, 11:1, 13:53, 19:1, 26:1 — the New-Mosheh (Moses) five-discourse architecture). The Trans-Yarden (Jordan) Peraean territory was the customary pilgrimage-route for Galilean-Yahudi (Jewish) believers heading to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) and is itself Tanakh-substance — the Yahoshua (Joshua)-entry-from-beyond-the-Yarden (Jordan) substance under which the original-entry into the inheritance ran (Joshua 3:14-17), the substance the King-as-the-true-Yahoshua (the name *Yahusha* is the same name as *Yahoshua* — Yahuah-saves) recapitulates in the long-final-pilgrimage. The Peraean-multitudes-following and healing-substance continue the Isaiah-35 Messianic-credential — *then the eyes of the blind shall be opened* — at the eastern-bank territories with substantial scattered-tribes' representation. (2) The Pharisees'-divorce-question + the King's-appeal-behind-Mosheh (Moses)-to-the-creation-ordinance + the eunuchs-for-the-kingdom (vv.3-12). The Pharisaic *is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?* is the Hillel-vs-Shammai debate-trap on Deuteronomy 24:1's *some uncleanness (ervat davar)* — Hillel's *any cause* (even burnt food) vs Shammai's *substantial sexual-impurity* — with the second-prong of forcing the King against Mosheh (Moses) if the King condemns the divorce-substance Deuteronomy 24:1 permits. The King refuses the Hillel-Shammai frame entirely and appeals behind Mosheh (Moses) to Bereshit (Genesis) 1-2's creation-ordinance — *male and female created he them* (Genesis 1:27) and *they shall be one flesh* (Genesis 2:24) — naming the Father's-will-from-the-beginning as the substance-of-reference. *Mosheh (Moses), because of the hardness of your hearts, suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so* (v.8) lays the framework-major substance for reading the Torah architecturally — the creation-ordinance substance is the Father's-will-from-the-beginning; Deuteronomy 24:1's permission (Greek *epetrepsen* — *permitted, suffered,* not commanded) is concession-to-hardness-of-heart; both substances are Torah-preserved, both stand together, the kingdom-citizen walks toward the Father's-will-from-the-beginning while-acknowledging-the-concession the Torah-preserved for the hardness-of-heart-condition. The architectural-pattern runs elsewhere — Deuteronomy 17:14-20's king-concession parallels at 1 Samuel 8:7's *they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them.* The Tanakh-prophetic-substance against putting-away is Malachi 2:14-16's *Yahuah Elohim (the LORD God) of Yashar'el (Israel)... hateth putting away.* The *except-it-be-for-fornication* exception-clause preserves the substance of substantial sexual-impurity (Deuteronomy 22:13-21, Leviticus 20:10-21) as the genuine substance-breaking-substance from-the-inside, in continuity with Yahuah's-own-act of putting-away-the-northern-house at Jeremiah 3:8's *for all the causes whereby backsliding Yashar'el (Israel) committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce* — divine-precedent for the substantial-spiritual-adultery exception. The Roman-Catholic-no-divorce-no-remarriage-absolute reading collapses the exception-clause out of the substance through the annulment-substance-procedure; the substance does not stand against the King's-own-mouth. The disciples' *it is not good to marry* reaction reveals how thoroughly the Hillelite-any-cause-substance had set into the assumptions of the day. The eunuchs-for-the-kingdom saying (vv.10-12) names three categories — born-so from the womb, made-so by men, and made-themselves-so for the kingdom-of-heaven's-sake — under the Isaiah 56:3-5 Tanakh-substance where *the eunuchs that keep my sabbaths, and choose the things that please me, and take hold of my covenant* receive *a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters* in the Father's-house (the Isaiah 56 strangers-and-eunuchs are the scattered-seed-coming-home, not random pagan converts being-added). The voluntary-celibacy-for-the-kingdom is gift-substance, not mandate — the King-himself walked it, the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) walked it (1 Corinthians 7:7, 32-33), and the substance is offered to those-to-whom-it-is-given, not imposed. The Roman-Catholic mandatory-clerical-celibacy imposes-the-gift-as-mandate; the Reformation over-correction-with-marriage-mandate treats-the-celibate-kingdom-citizen as-incomplete. Both are ruled out at the King's *all men cannot receive this saying, save they to whom it is given.* (3) The little-children-brought-to-the-King and the disciples'-rebuke-rebuked (vv.13-15). The disciples'-rebuke (Greek *epetimēsan* — same substance Kefa (Peter) had operated in when he rebuked the King at Matthew 16:22) reveals they have not yet internalized the Matthew 18:1-5 substance the King had just taught one chapter earlier. The King's-rebuke-of-the-rebuke and the *suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven* (v.14) restates the matt-18:1-5 substance at the practical-substance level — the kingdom belongs to those-who-bear-the-four-fold-child-substance (humility, receptiveness, dependence, honesty), not to the original-innocence-by-default reading the institutional-Christian-tradition has built. The King's-laying-on-of-hands is the Tanakh-substance of paternal-blessing (Genesis 48's Ya'aqov (Jacob)-blessing-the-sons-of-Yoseph (Joseph); Numbers 6:24-26's priestly-blessing) enacted by the King-himself. (4) The rich-young-ruler + camel-and-the-eye-of-a-needle + with-Elohim (God)-all-things-are-possible (vv.16-26). The young-man's *what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?* substantial-question meets the King's *why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, Elohim (God)* probe — not the King denying his divinity (which the substance does not bear) but the King pressing the young-man to consider whether he means what his own words have implied, with the Formless-Father / Formed-Son architectural-substance under the question. The King's-direct-answer *if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments* (v.17) is one of the most-load-bearing substances in the entire gospel against the antinomian-Paul-reading the institutional-Christian-tradition has built — the King's-own-mouth at the kingdom-entry-criterion-level. The substance is in continuity with Matthew 5:17-19's *think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets; I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil,* Matthew 7:21-23's *I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity (anomia)* — Greek *anomia* is *lawlessness, without-Torah*; the substance the King dismisses at that judgment-day is confession-without-Torah-walking. The Tanakh-substance runs through Deuteronomy 30:15-19's *choose life... keep his commandments,* Leviticus 18:5's *which if a man do, he shall live in them,* and Proverbs 4:22's *they are life unto those that find them.* The apostolic-restatement at 1 John 2:3-4 — *hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar* — is the 1234 of Truth at the apostle Yochanan (John)'s-mouth, with 1 John 5:3, Revelation 14:12's *here are they that keep the commandments of Elohim (God),* and Revelation 22:14's *blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life.* The institutional-Christian *not under law but under grace* phrase, torn from the apostle Sha'ul (Paul)'s own writings and deployed as proof-text against Matthew 19:17, does not stand — the apostle himself was Torah-observant, took Nazarite-vows, paid for four men's offerings at Acts 21:23-26; *not under law* names *not-under-Torah-as-condemning-system,* not *not-under-Torah-as-the-way-of-life-the-Father-gave* the Spirit *will cause you to walk in* per Ezekiel 36:27. The King's-selection of five-of-the-Ten-Commandments (commandments 5-9 — the second-table-toward-the-neighbor) plus Leviticus 19:18's *love thy neighbour as thyself* tests the young-man at the neighbor-substance the heart-Torah-test will land on. The young-man's *all these things have I kept from my youth up* names letter-keeping without the heart-Torah-substance of Jeremiah 31:33 and Ezekiel 36:26-27 the King had unpacked at Matthew 5:21-48. *If thou wilt be perfect, go and sell that thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come and follow me* (v.21) — the Greek *teleios* is the Tanakh-*tamim* (Genesis 17:1, Deuteronomy 18:13, Matthew 5:48); the test is diagnostic, not categorical (different kingdom-citizens face different obstacles — the substance is heart-Torah-diagnostic, not universal-poverty-mandate, not Marxist-redistribution). The diagnostic reveals the young-man's love-of-neighbor stopped at the substance of his own possessions — the love-that-does-not-extend-to-the-poor-who-could-be-fed by what the young-man-keeps-back, against the Tanakh's-Torah-substance of giving-to-the-poor (Deuteronomy 15:7-8, Proverbs 19:17, Psalm 41:1, Isaiah 58:6-7). The young-man's *went away sorrowful: for he had great possessions* (v.22) is heart-substance the King-beholds-with-love (Mark 10:21) — open-substance for the return. The camel-through-the-eye-of-a-needle hyperbole (vv.23-24) is straight Tanakh-hyperbole-pattern, not the Needle's-Eye-gate explanation (no such gate is historically documented at any period); the substance is impossibility-deliberately-stated under the Tanakh-prophetic-substance against trusting-in-riches at Proverbs 30:7-9, Jeremiah 9:23-24, Deuteronomy 8:11-14, Psalm 49:6-7, Psalm 62:10, with the apostolic-restatement at James 5:1-2. The disciples' *who then can be saved?* (v.25) reveals the inherited-substance that had associated material-prosperity with the Father's-blessing (Genesis 13:2, 26:12, Deuteronomy 28); the King's *with men this is impossible; but with Elohim (God) all things are possible* (v.26) lands the Father's-substance making-the-impossible-possible at the Tanakh-anchor of Genesis 18:14's *is any thing too hard for Yahuah (the LORD)?*, Jeremiah 32:17, 27, Job 42:2, and Luke 1:37's *with Elohim (God) nothing shall be impossible* — bounded by the new-heart-substance of Ezekiel 36:26-27 the Father's-substance enables, not universalist-determinism overriding the cause-and-effect-of-walking the kingdom-citizen-substance. (5) Framework-major — Kefa (Peter)'s-question + the twelve-thrones-judging-the-twelve-tribes statement + the hundredfold-and-everlasting-life + the many-first-shall-be-last bridge to Matthew 20 (vv.27-30). Kefa (Peter)'s *we have forsaken all, and followed thee; what shall we have therefore?* (v.27) is apostolic-substance asking-the-King the substance of the discipleship-following's-substance — not prosperity-substance. The King's-answer *in the regeneration when the Son of Adam shall sit in the throne of his glory, ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel)* (v.28) is the King's-mouth most-direct-statement of the consummated-kingdom-architecture the framework's-thesis carries. The Greek *palingenesia* (*regeneration, re-genesis*) is the new-heavens-and-new-earth substance of Isaiah 65:17 and Isaiah 66:22, the *apokatastasis* (*restoration*) of Acts 3:21's *times of restitution of all things, which Elohim (God) hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began,* the cosmic-renewal-substance of Romans 8:19-21. The *Son of Adam shall sit in the throne of his glory* substance is the Daniel 7:13-14 *one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven... there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom* substance (kaph-comparative incarnation-honoring — the figure is Yahusha (Jesus), the harmonized-identification clear when scripture harmonizes with itself), restated by the King-himself at the Matthew 25:31 sheep-and-goats substance. The *twelve thrones... judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel)* is the framework-major-anchor for the twelve-tribes-restoration-architecture — the twelve-apostles named-from-the-twelve-tribes-substance at Matthew 10:2-4 are seated in the consummated-kingdom as-judges-of-the-twelve-tribes-of-Yashar'el (Israel). The Tanakh-architecture runs through Ezekiel 37:21-22's two-sticks-becoming-one (the northern-house and southern-house gathered-into-one under-the-one-king), Isaiah 11:12's *he shall set up an ensign for the goyim (nations), and shall assemble the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel), and gather together the dispersed of Yahudah (Judah) from the four corners of the earth,* Jeremiah 31:31's new-covenant-with-both-houses, the Ezekiel 47-48 twelve-tribes-land-allotment in the consummated-kingdom, and Revelation 21:12-14's twelve-gates-named-for-the-twelve-tribes-of-Yashar'el (Israel)-with-twelve-foundations-named-for-the-twelve-apostles-of-the-Lamb. The replacement-theology-substance that collapses the twelve-tribes into the church-as-the-spiritual-Israel is what the King's-own-mouth-at-Matthew-19:28 rules out — the twelve-tribes-of-Yashar'el (Israel) at the regeneration are the paternal-blood remnant the prophet-substance has-promised, not a spiritualized-self-selection. The Hebrew-Roots-false-inclusion-gospel that-collapses-the-twelve-tribes into anyone-who-believes-and-Torah-keeps-is-therefore-Yashar'el (Israel) is also ruled out — the wild-olive-branches at Romans 11 are the scattered-seed-living-as-goyim (Gentiles)-among-the-nations coming home, not non-Yashar'el (Israel)-substances being-grafted-in; both branches on the olive-tree are Yashar'el (Israel). The Greek *krinontes* (*judging*) is governance-substance the Tanakh-Shofetim (Judges) walked-in (Judges 2:16) — the apostolic-twelve carrying forward what the Shofetim (Judges) carried in their generation, now at the consummated-substance under the King-on-the-throne-of-glory. The *every one that hath forsaken houses... shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life* (v.29) extends from the apostolic-twelve to all-kingdom-citizens; the Markan-parallel at Mark 10:29-30 makes the substance explicit — *an hundredfold now in this time, houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands, with persecutions; and in the world to come eternal life* — the kingdom-family-substance multiplied-now in the corporate-substance (Matthew 12:46-50, Acts 2:44-45, Acts 4:32-37), with-persecutions (the cost), and everlasting-life in the coming age. The *many that are first shall be last; and the last shall be first* (v.30) bridges directly to Matthew 20:1-16's laborers-in-the-vineyard parable — the kingdom-economy's-inversion of the natural-economy, the kingdom-ranking by the Father's-substance not by natural-substance (when-arrived, how-much-time-served, how-much-substance-given). The chapter teaches the kingdom-citizens'-substance from the marriage-substance through the children-substance through the possessions-substance through the discipleship-cost-substance into the consummated-kingdom-substance the framework-major twelve-thrones-twelve-tribes statement lands at, and the next chapter opens with the laborers-in-the-vineyard parable unpacking the *first-shall-be-last* substance directly.
