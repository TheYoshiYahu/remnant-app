# Short-form commentary on Matthew 19

Matthew 19 opens the King's long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem). The fourth major discourse closed at the end of Matthew 18; the King now departs from Galil (Galilee) and crosses into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan), the very territory through which Yahoshua (Joshua) led the people into the inheritance at the original entry under Mosheh's (Moses') successor. The chapter that follows lays out the kingdom-citizens'-substance toward four substances the inherited systems have most thoroughly confused: marriage, children, possessions, and reward. Five movements anchor the chapter. The geographic-shift from Galil (Galilee) to Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan) and the multitudes-following with the healing-substance continued (vv.1-2). The Pharisees'-divorce-question with the King's-appeal-behind-Mosheh (Moses)-to-the-creation-ordinance and the *from-the-beginning-it-was-not-so* substance, the *suffered-because-of-the-hardness-of-your-hearts* clarification, the *except-it-be-for-fornication* exception-clause, and the eunuchs-for-the-kingdom-of-heaven saying (vv.3-12). The little-children-brought-to-the-King with the disciples'-rebuke rebuked and the *of-such-is-the-kingdom-of-heaven* restatement of Matthew 18:1-5 at the practical-substance level (vv.13-15). The rich-young-ruler with the *why-callest-thou-me-good* probe and the *if-thou-wilt-enter-into-life-keep-the-commandments* answer at the kingdom-entry-criterion-level, the King's-selection of five-of-the-Ten-plus-the-Leviticus-19:18-summary, the *all-these-things-have-I-kept-from-my-youth-up* claim, the heart-Torah-test at the substance-of-mammon (*if-thou-wilt-be-perfect, go-and-sell*), the young-man's-departure-sorrowful, the camel-and-the-eye-of-a-needle hyperbole, the disciples'-*who-then-can-be-saved*, and the *with-Elohim (God)-all-things-are-possible* substance (vv.16-26). Kefa (Peter)'s-question *we-have-forsaken-all* and the framework-major answer of the twelve-thrones-judging-the-twelve-tribes-of-Yashar'el (Israel) at the regeneration, the *hundredfold-and-everlasting-life* substance, and the *many-that-are-first-shall-be-last* word that closes the chapter and opens directly into the vineyard-laborers parable of Matthew 20 (vv.27-30).

*And it came to pass, that when Yahusha (Jesus) had finished these sayings, he departed from Galil (Galilee), and came into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan); and great multitudes followed him; and he healed them there.* (Matthew 19:1-2)

*When Yahusha (Jesus) had finished these sayings.* The Matthean discourse-closing-formula. The fourth of the King's-five major discourses has closed at the end of Matthew 18; the formula that closed the first discourse at Matthew 7:28 (*and it came to pass, when Yahusha (Jesus) had ended these sayings*), the second at Matthew 11:1, the third at Matthew 13:53, and the fifth at Matthew 26:1 marks the deliberate structuring of the gospel as five-discourse architecture under the New-Mosheh (Moses) pattern. Mosheh (Moses) gave the people five books at Sinai; the King delivers five major discourses across the Matthean gospel as the substance the kingdom-citizens carry into the assembly. The fourth-discourse-close sets the King in motion toward the final-discourse that will be delivered in the precincts of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) itself across Matthew 23-25.

*He departed from Galil (Galilee), and came into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan).* The geographic-shift is the long-foretold motion toward Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) the King has been pointing toward since the first passion-prediction at Matthew 16:21 — *from that time forth began Yahusha (Jesus) to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.* The Galil (Galilee)-mission is now closing; the trajectory toward the cross is now opening. *Beyond the Yarden (Jordan)* names the Trans-Yarden (Jordan) Peraean territory, the eastern-bank region administered by Herod-Antipas alongside Galil (Galilee). The customary pilgrimage-route for Galilean-Yahudi (Jewish) believers heading to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) for the feasts ran the eastern-bank to avoid the Samaritan-territory that lay along the western-bank's-direct route. The King is taking the customary pilgrimage-route, and the route is itself a Tanakh-substance.

The Yahoshua (Joshua)-entry-from-beyond-the-Yarden (Jordan) substance frames the geographic-motion. The original-entry into the inheritance ran from-beyond-the-Yarden (Jordan) across-the-river into-the-land — Mosheh (Moses) saw the land from Mount Nebo on the eastern-bank and died there; Yahoshua (Joshua) led the people across the Yarden (Jordan) on dry ground (Joshua 3:14-17), set up the twelve stones at Gilgal (Joshua 4:20), and began the inheritance-substance. The King now-traverses-the-same-territory in the long-final-pilgrimage. The substance the prophet had pointed toward at Hosea 2:14-15 — *behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her. And I will give her her vineyards from thence, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope: and she shall sing there, as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt* — is the substance the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed will pass through. The King-as-the-true-Yahoshua (Joshua) (the name *Yahusha* is the same name as *Yahoshua* — Yahuah-saves) is gathering, and the geographic-substance bears witness to the architecture.

*And great multitudes followed him; and he healed them there.* The Peraean-multitudes gather; the healing-substance continues. The Isaiah-35 Messianic-credential-substance — *then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert* (Isaiah 35:5-6) — continues at the substance of the King's-action wherever he goes. The Peraean territory was, like the Galil (Galilee)-of-the-goyim (Gentiles) of Matthew 4:15-16, a mixed-population region with substantial scattered-tribes' representation among its inhabitants. The kingdom-mission's-scope continues to land at the territories where the lost-sheep-of-the-house-of-Yashar'el (Israel) are gathering home.

*The Pharisees also came unto him, tempting him, and saying unto him, Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause? And he answered and said unto them, Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning made them male and female, And said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they twain shall be one flesh? Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore Elohim (God) hath joined together, let not man put asunder. They say unto him, Why did Mosheh (Moses) then command to give a writing of divorcement, and to put her away? He saith unto them, Mosheh (Moses), because of the hardness of your hearts, suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so. And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery.* (Matthew 19:3-9)

The Pharisees meet the King in the Peraean territory with a question that is, on its surface, a question about Mosaic case-law and, beneath the surface, a trap with two prongs. The trap will fail because the King answers neither side of the trap but moves the substance behind both sides of the trap to the place the substance actually lives.

*Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?* The Greek *kata pasan aitian* — *according to every cause* — names the rabbinic-school debate of the day. The Pharisaic-tradition was divided on the substance of Deuteronomy 24:1's *some uncleanness* (Hebrew *ervat davar* — *nakedness of thing*). The school of Shammai read the substance strictly — *some uncleanness* meant substantial-sexual-impurity, and divorce was permitted only on that ground. The school of Hillel read the substance permissively — *some uncleanness* was *anything* the husband found displeasing, and the famous example the school gave was *even if she burnt his food.* Hillel's-reading produced an effective regime of any-cause divorce; Shammai's-reading restricted divorce to the substance of substantial sexual-impurity. The Pharisees-tempting hope the King will land on one side of the Hillel-Shammai debate and thereby alienate the followers of the other side. They also hope to land the King against Mosheh (Moses) himself if the King condemns the divorce-substance Deuteronomy 24:1 permits. The trap has two prongs.

*Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning made them male and female.* The King refuses the Hillel-Shammai-frame of the debate and appeals behind Mosheh (Moses) to Bereshit (Genesis) 1-2. The substance the King is citing is the creation-ordinance:

*So Elohim (God) created man in his own image, in the image of Elohim (God) created he him; male and female created he them.* (Genesis 1:27)

*Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.* (Genesis 2:24)

The two Tanakh-substances the King names are pre-Sinai, pre-Mosheh (Moses), pre-the-hardness-of-heart-permission of Deuteronomy 24:1 — they are the Father's-will-from-the-beginning before the covenant-architecture had to engage with the substance of human hardness. *Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore Elohim (God) hath joined together, let not man put asunder.* The King's-application lays the substance directly — the one-flesh-substance the Father-instituted at the beginning is a substance the Father-himself-joined; the Father's-joining is not a substance human authority can-undo at preference. The substance is creation-ordinance, not case-law.

The King's-method here is framework-major for reading the Torah architecturally. The Tanakh-substance is layered, and the layers are not all the same kind. The creation-ordinance substance of Bereshit (Genesis) 1-2 is the Father's-will-from-the-beginning; the Sinai-Torah substance of Shemot (Exodus)-through-Devarim (Deuteronomy) carries the covenant-instruction; and within the Sinai-Torah are particular substances the Torah-itself names as concessions to the substance of hardness-of-heart that the community carries. The kingdom-citizen reading the Torah carefully distinguishes these layers — the Father's-will-from-the-beginning, the covenant-instruction Sinai delivers, and the concessions-the-covenant-instruction makes to the hardness-of-heart-substance. The King's-method is the kingdom-citizens'-method.

*Why did Mosheh (Moses) then command to give a writing of divorcement, and to put her away?* The Pharisees push back. They detect the King's-architectural-move and try to force the conflict with Mosheh (Moses). They re-frame the Deuteronomy 24:1 substance from *permission* to *command* — Mosheh (Moses) *commanded* the bill of divorcement, they say, so the King's-appeal to the creation-ordinance must come against Mosheh's (Moses')-command. The Pharisaic-tradition's pattern of Torah-overreading surfaces directly. The substance of Deuteronomy 24:1 was never a command-to-divorce. The verse is grammatically a protasis-apodosis structure — *when a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement* — regulating what must happen *if* a man divorces, not commanding that any man divorce. The Pharisees push the Tanakh-substance harder than the Tanakh-substance says.

*Mosheh (Moses), because of the hardness of your hearts, suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so.* The King's-clarification lands at the substance directly. The Greek *epetrepsen* — *permitted, suffered* — names what Mosheh (Moses) actually did. The bill of divorcement was a permission, not a command. The permission was given *because of the hardness of your hearts.* The substance of hardness-of-heart in the covenant-community was the substance Mosheh's (Moses')-legislation engaged with — the community at Sinai and across the wilderness had a substance of stiff-necked hardness the Torah named directly (Exodus 32:9, Exodus 33:3, Exodus 33:5, Deuteronomy 9:6, Deuteronomy 9:13, Deuteronomy 10:16, Deuteronomy 31:27). The legislation preserved the substance of regulated-divorce because the alternative under hardness-of-heart conditions would have been worse — wives cast out without papers, with no legal protection, with no possibility of remarriage, vulnerable to the substance the Torah-elsewhere protects. The bill-of-divorcement was the Torah-substance protecting the woman who was being put away in a system where the husband's-hardness-of-heart was operative. The Torah was not endorsing what was happening; the Torah was regulating what was happening and limiting the damage.

Hold this carefully against the inherited-Christian-collapse of the Tanakh. The institutional-Christian-reading has too often treated the King's-words here as an abrogation of Deuteronomy 24:1 — as if the King were saying *Mosheh (Moses) gave you a corrupt law and I am now replacing it.* The substance does not stand. The Torah-permission of Deuteronomy 24:1 is not abolished by the King's-statement; the King's-statement names the substance of the permission for what it always was — a concession-to-hardness-of-heart, not the Father's-will-from-the-beginning. The Torah-substance is preserved; the Father's-will-from-the-beginning is restored to view; the kingdom-citizen now reads both substances together and walks toward the substance that lies in the Father's-will-from-the-beginning while-acknowledging-the-substance the Torah-preserved for the hardness-of-heart-condition.

The architectural-pattern of permission-to-hardness-of-heart runs elsewhere in the Tanakh as well. Deuteronomy 17:14-20's-king-substance is the parallel — *when thou art come unto the land which Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) giveth thee... and shalt say, I will set a king over me, like as all the goyim (nations) that are about me; Thou shalt in any wise set him king over thee.* The Torah preserved the substance of the human-king the people would eventually demand; the substance was concession-to-hardness-of-heart, not the Father's-will-from-the-beginning (the Father's-rule was the substance-from-the-beginning). The substance ran exactly as Mosheh (Moses) had foreseen — at 1 Samuel 8 the people came to Shmuel (Samuel) demanding *a king to judge us like all the nations,* and Yahuah (the LORD) said to Shmuel (Samuel): *they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not reign over them* (1 Samuel 8:7). The Torah-substance preserved the regulated-form of what the community would do under hardness-of-heart; the prophet-named-the-substance for what it was. The same architectural-pattern operates at the divorce-substance the King is now addressing.

The Tanakh-prophetic substance against unfaithful-putting-away. The prophet Malachi names the substance directly:

*Yahuah (the LORD) hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt treacherously: yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant. And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the Ruach (spirit). And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth. For Yahuah Elohim (the LORD God) of Yashar'el (Israel) saith that he hateth putting away.* (Malachi 2:14-16)

Malachi names what the Father's-substance toward putting-away always was: *he hateth putting away.* The Tanakh-prophetic-substance is the substance the King's-words restore to view. The Father has-never-preferred-divorce; the Father has-permitted-the-regulated-form for the substance of hardness-of-heart in the covenant-community; the Father's-will-from-the-beginning is the one-flesh-substance the kingdom-citizen walks-toward.

*And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery.* The King's-substantive-ruling. The *except-it-be-for-fornication* exception-clause preserves the substance of substantial-sexual-impurity as a substance that genuinely-breaks-the-one-flesh-bond. The Greek *porneia* is broader than narrowly-adultery — the substance includes the range of substantial-sexual-impurity the Tanakh names at Deuteronomy 22:13-21 (the substantial-sexual-impurity discovered at the marriage) and at Leviticus 20:10-21 (the range of sexual prohibitions the Tanakh names as substance-breaking-substances). The exception-clause is not the Hillelite-any-cause-divorce-substance the Pharisees were trying to lock in; the exception-clause is the recognition that some substances genuinely break the one-flesh-bond from-the-inside, and the kingdom-citizen who has had the substance broken from-the-inside is not bound to maintain a fiction-of-bond against a substance that has-already-broken-it.

Hold what this is against the inherited-Christian-Catholic-reading of no-divorce-no-remarriage as absolute. The Roman-Catholic-tradition has read the *what-Elohim (God)-hath-joined-together-let-not-man-put-asunder* substance as an absolute prohibition against any-divorce under any circumstances, with the exception-clause read out of the substance through the annulment-as-the-substance-the-marriage-was-never-real-substance procedure. The substance does not stand. The King's-own-mouth preserved the exception-clause directly. The kingdom-citizen who has had the one-flesh-bond broken from-the-inside by the substance of substantial-sexual-impurity is not bound. The substance is in continuity with the Father's-own-substance — at Jeremiah 3:8 the Father himself names what he had done with the northern-house: *and I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Yashar'el (Israel) committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce.* The Father, in the prophet's-voice, names his own-act of putting-away-the-northern-house on the substance of substantial-spiritual-adultery. The exception-clause the King-preserves at Matthew 19:9 stands on the same architectural-substance the Father-himself walked at the substance of the divine-marriage-broken from-the-inside.

The Markan-parallel at Mark 10:2-12 carries the same substance with one notable difference — the Markan-account does not preserve the *porneia*-exception-clause. The Lukan-parallel at Luke 16:18 also-does-not-preserve-the-exception. The Markan-and-Lukan substance is the King's-teaching at its-most-substantial against the Hillelite-any-cause-substance; the Matthean substance preserves the exception-clause that the Mosaic-Torah-substance already-recognized at Deuteronomy 22:13-21 and Leviticus 20:10-21. The two substances are one substance — the King's-substantive-correction against the Hillelite-any-cause-substance with the substantial-sexual-impurity-substance preserved as the genuine substance-breaking-substance the kingdom-citizen recognizes. The apostle Paul will apply the same substance at 1 Corinthians 7:10-16 with one additional case the apostle is addressing — the kingdom-citizen married to an unbelieving-spouse who departs has been left, and is not under bondage in such cases; the substance extends the architectural-substance of substantial-breaking to-the-substance the kingdom-citizen-did-not-cause.

*His disciples say unto him, If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry. But he said unto them, All men cannot receive this saying, save they to whom it is given. For there are some eunuchs, which were so born from their mother's womb: and there are some eunuchs, which were made eunuchs of men: and there be eunuchs, which have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake. He that is able to receive it, let him receive it.* (Matthew 19:10-12)

The disciples respond to the King's-substance with a reaction that itself reveals how thoroughly the Hillelite-any-cause-substance had set into the assumptions of the day. *If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry* — if the marriage-bond is the substance the King has just named, with no any-cause-escape, then marriage itself becomes a substance the disciples are not-sure they want. The reaction is honest. The kingdom-substance of marriage demands more than the inherited-system-substance had been requiring; the disciples are reckoning with the demand.

*All men cannot receive this saying, save they to whom it is given.* The King names a substance the kingdom-citizens are about to discover — not everyone is given the substance of marriage. Some are given the substance of celibacy in some form, and the King is about to name the three categories the substance comes in.

*For there are some eunuchs, which were so born from their mother's womb: and there are some eunuchs, which were made eunuchs of men: and there be eunuchs, which have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake.* The three categories. Those born-so from the womb; those-made-so by men; and those-who-have-made-themselves-so for the kingdom-of-heaven's-sake. The first two are conditions-imposed. The third is voluntary — the kingdom-citizen who chooses celibacy as the substance of undistracted kingdom-service.

The Tanakh-anchor for the eunuch-substance is at Isaiah 56. The prophet had named the substance:

*Neither let the son of the stranger, that hath joined himself to Yahuah (the LORD), speak, saying, Yahuah (the LORD) hath utterly separated me from his people: neither let the eunuch say, Behold, I am a dry tree. For thus saith Yahuah (the LORD) unto the eunuchs that keep my sabbaths, and choose the things that please me, and take hold of my covenant; Even unto them will I give in mine house and within my walls a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters: I will give them an everlasting name, that shall not be cut off.* (Isaiah 56:3-5)

The Isaiah 56-substance is the substance of the scattered-seed-coming-home. The strangers and the eunuchs of Isaiah 56 are not random pagan converts who-have-decided-to-join. The strangers are the sons-of-the-stranger — the scattered-seed of Yashar'el (Israel) living in the territories of their exile as strangers from their own covenant-identity, now being-gathered-home; the *Yahuah (the LORD)-hath-utterly-separated-me-from-his-people* fear they speak is the Lo-Ammi fear of Hosea 1 — *I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people* (Hosea 2:23). The eunuchs are the substance of those-who-cannot-produce-physical-descendants — by birth, by men, or as becomes clear at Matthew 19, by voluntary-substance for the kingdom-of-heaven's-sake. The prophet promises the eunuchs *a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters* in the Father's-house. The substance is not the negation-of-the-physical-seed-line; the substance is the Father's-place-and-name for those who, in the architecture of the gathering, do-not-add-to-the-physical-seed-line at the level of their own descendants. The substance is honored, not negated.

The King's-substance at Matthew 19:12 extends the Tanakh-substance forward. The voluntary-eunuch-for-the-kingdom-of-heaven's-sake takes the substance into the kingdom-mission. The King himself walked this substance — Yahusha (Jesus) lived celibately as the substance of undistracted kingdom-mission. The apostle Paul walked the same substance and wrote to the Corinthians about it: *for I would that all men were even as I myself. But every man hath his proper gift of Elohim (God), one after this manner, and another after that... He that is unmarried careth for the things that belong to the Lord, how he may please the Lord: But he that is married careth for the things that are of the world, how he may please his wife* (1 Corinthians 7:7, 32-33). The substance is gift-substance — Paul is clear that not every kingdom-citizen receives it. *He that is able to receive it, let him receive it,* the King says. The substance is offered as a substance some-receive, not as a substance imposed on all.

Hold this against two inherited-substance collapses. The Roman-Catholic-tradition of mandatory-clerical-celibacy — imposing the substance on the priesthood as a categorical-requirement regardless of whether the substance is given — is exactly the Pharisaic-tradition-substance the King had warned against at Matthew 15:1-9 in a different register. The substance ruled out by the King at Matthew 19:11 (*save they to whom it is given*) is what the Catholic-tradition imposes universally. The substance is gift, not mandate, and the systems that mandate the gift are operating against the substance the King-himself named. On the other side, the Reformation's-over-correction against the Roman-Catholic-mandate has too often produced a marriage-mandate-instead — a default in which the unmarried-adult is treated as a substance-incomplete and the celibate-kingdom-citizen is treated as a substance-defective. The substance is also ruled out at the King's-mouth. Voluntary-celibacy-for-the-kingdom-of-heaven's-sake is a substance the King-honors; the kingdom-citizen who receives it walks in a substance the King-himself walked.

*Then were there brought unto him little children, that he should put his hands on them, and pray: and the disciples rebuked them. But Yahusha (Jesus) said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven. And he laid his hands on them, and departed thence.* (Matthew 19:13-15)

The little-children-brought-to-the-King pericope follows directly. The Markan-parallel at Mark 10:13-16 and the Lukan-parallel at Luke 18:15-17 preserve the same substance with additional language; the Markan-account adds *and he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them,* and the Markan-and-Lukan accounts both add the King's-categorical-statement *verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of Elohim (God) as a little child, he shall not enter therein.* The substance is the practical-substance restatement of what the King had taught at Matthew 18:1-5.

*Then were there brought unto him little children.* The substance is parents and family-members bringing-children to the King-for-blessing. The Tanakh-substance under the act runs through Bereshit (Genesis) 48 where Ya'aqov (Jacob) lays his hands on Ephrayim (Ephraim) and Menashsheh (Manasseh) and blesses them — *the messenger which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads* (Genesis 48:16) — and through the priestly-blessing of Numbers 6:24-26 — *Yahuah (the LORD) bless thee, and keep thee: Yahuah (the LORD) make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee.* The laying-on-of-hands-and-blessing of the children was Tanakh-substance. The parents are bringing the children to the King-as-the-Tanakh-Father-of-blessing in flesh.

*And the disciples rebuked them.* The disciples have not yet internalized the substance the King had taught in the previous chapter. The matt-18:1-5 substance had been the *converted-and-become-as-little-children* substance; the matt-18:6-9 substance had been the *whoso-shall-offend-one-of-these-little-ones* warning with the millstone-and-the-sea woe-substance; the matt-18:10-14 substance had been the *take-heed-that-ye-despise-not-one-of-these-little-ones* warning with the lost-sheep-substance of the shepherd-going-after-the-one. The disciples at matt-19:13 are immediately operating against what the King had just taught. The Greek *epetimēsan* (*rebuked*) is the same substance the disciples had operated in when Kefa (Peter) rebuked the King at Matthew 16:22 over the first passion-prediction. The disciples'-substance is exactly the substance the King had been forming-against, and the King now-rebukes-the-rebuke.

*Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.* The King's-correction is direct. The Greek *aphete* (*suffer, allow*) names the substance of the disciples'-needing-to-stop-blocking-the-children's-approach. The disciples have placed themselves as a substance between-the-children-and-the-King — exactly the substance the millstone-warning of Matthew 18:6-9 had named as offense-substance. The King re-opens the way. *For of such is the kingdom of heaven.* The substance the kingdom-belongs-to is the substance the children-carry — not because they-have-not-yet-sinned (the substance of original-innocence the institutional-Christian-tradition has read into the verse is the substance the Tanakh-and-the-King-do-not-name); but because the four-fold child-substance of humility, receptiveness, dependence, and honesty the King had unpacked at Matthew 18:1-5 is the disposition-substance the kingdom-citizens walk-in. The kingdom belongs to those-who-bear-the-substance.

*And he laid his hands on them, and departed thence.* The King performs the substance the parents had brought their children for. The laying-on-of-hands is the Tanakh-substance of paternal-blessing-substance enacted by the King-himself. The substance is honored; the children-receive; the King-departs.

*And, behold, one came and said unto him, Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life? And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, Elohim (God): but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments. He saith unto him, Which?
