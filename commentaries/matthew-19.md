## §1. The geographic-shift from Galilee into Yahudah (Judea) beyond Yarden (Jordan)

*And it came to pass, that when Yahusha (Jesus) had finished these sayings, he departed from Galilee, and came into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond Yarden (Jordan); and great multitudes followed him; and he healed them there* (vv.1-2).

**The framework reads vv.1-2 as the geographic-pivot-substance — the King leaves Galilee for the final-approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) and the passion-trajectory.**

**The *finished these sayings* substance** (v.1a). **The Yahudah (Judaea)-beyond-Yarden geographic-substance** (v.1b). The crowds-from-beyond-Yarden gather; the healing-substance continues; the kingdom-substance is proclaimed in the territories that — like the Galilee-of-the-goyim of Matt 4:15-16 — were-mixed-population territories with substantial-scattered-tribes' representation.
