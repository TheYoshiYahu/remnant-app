# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 2

Matthew 2 is the gathering-of-the-scattered walking out its first scenes. The Christmas-card reading flattens almost every beat; the framework reads what the chapter actually carries. (1) The *wise men from the east* (Greek *magoi*) are not generic Gentile worshippers but the institutional inheritors of the Persian-Babylonian Magi caste — the same caste Daniel was made master over (Daniel 2:48). Their Hebrew Messianic knowledge is plausible because the prophetic tradition Daniel taught was alive in the eastern dispersion where the scattered seed of Yashar'el (Israel) had been resettled — Halah, Habor, the river of Gozan, the cities of the Medes, Babylon, Persia, Parthia. The star they followed is the Numbers 24:17 Balaam prophecy of *a Star out of Ya'aqov (Jacob).* Their arrival is the first beat of the gathering, not the Jew-and-Gentile-inclusion thesis the inherited reading imports. (2) Herod the Great is the Edomite-on-the-Davidic-throne — Idumean by paternal blood (his father Antipater Idumean, his mother Cypros Nabataean), installed by Rome in 37 BCE over the Hasmonean line. The Beth-lehem slaughter is the latest beat of the Esau-against-Ya'aqov hatred-pattern that runs from Genesis 27:41 through Malachi 1:3. (3) The flight to Egypt and Matthew's quotation of Hosea 11:1 (*Out of Egypt have I called my son*) is identification, not loose proof-texting — Yahusha (Jesus) IS Yashar'el (Israel), recapitulating the people's history in his own person; the exodus becomes his infancy-exodus, the wilderness will become his forty-day temptation, the mountain at Sinai will become the Sermon on the Mount. The Pharaoh-Mosheh (Moses) infant-preservation pattern is in view. (4) The Beth-lehem slaughter quoting Jeremiah 31:15 (*Rachel weeping for her children*) names the whole chapter, not one sad verse — Jeremiah 31 is the gathering-and-new-covenant chapter (vv.8-10, 16-17, 31-33). Matthew 2:18 and Matthew 26:28's *blood of the new testament* are bracketing the same chapter of Jeremiah twenty-six chapters apart. (5) The closing settlement in *Natsareth* (Nazareth) — Branch-town — is the prophetic-name fulfillment of the *netser*-Branch prophecies (Isaiah 11:1, Jeremiah 23:5, Zechariah 3:8) made geographic, in the despised northern territory where the scattered tribes of Naphtali, Zevulun, and Yissakhar (Issachar) had lived before the Assyrian deportation. The King is in the right place.
