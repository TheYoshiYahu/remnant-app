# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 20

Matthew 20 continues the King's long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem). The geographic-arc laid down at Matthew 19:1 has crossed Galil (Galilee) into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan), and the chapter now closes the eastern-bank Peraean stretch and arcs through Yericho (Jericho) on the final leg toward the city. Four movements anchor the chapter, with two of them carrying framework-major weight. (1) The laborers-in-the-vineyard parable (vv.1-16). The parable unpacks the *first-shall-be-last-and-last-shall-be-first* substance the previous chapter closed on at Matthew 19:30. The householder is the Father, the vineyard is Yashar'el (Israel)'s covenant-body the prophets named (Isaiah 5:1-7's *the vineyard of Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts) is the house of Yashar'el (Israel)*; Jeremiah 12:10), and the hiring at the first-hour and third-hour and sixth-hour and ninth-hour and eleventh-hour with the same penny paid to each at sunset displays the kingdom-economy's-substance — the Father's-grace distributed by the householder's-will, not the natural-economy's-substance of merit-by-time-served. The first-hour-laborers'-grumbling (Greek *egongyzon*) is the Tanakh-wilderness-substance the Septuagint uses across Exodus 16:2's *the whole congregation of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) murmured against Mosheh (Moses) and Aharon (Aaron) in the wilderness* and Numbers 14:27's *how long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me?* The householder's *friend* (Greek *hetaire*) is the polite-address-of-correction the King uses elsewhere only at Matthew 22:12 (the man without the wedding-garment) and Matthew 26:50 (the betrayer Yahudah (Judas)); the *is thine eye evil, because I am good?* closes with the Hebrew *good-eye / evil-eye* idiom-substance the King has already named at Matthew 6:22-23 and the Tanakh-wisdom-substance running through Deuteronomy 15:9's *thine eye be evil against thy poor brother* and Proverbs 23:6 / 28:22 — the disposition-of-generosity-vs-stinginess, not optical-physiology. The closing *many be called, but few chosen* (v.16) lands the parable at the cause-and-effect-of-hearing the King has unfolded through every kingdom-parable since Matthew 13 — the *many* called because the citizens-of-the-kingdom are scattered-among-the-nations and the proclaimers do-not-know-them-by-sight (proclamation goes broadly because the sheep are scattered), and the *few chosen* (Greek *eklektoi*) because the names are written in the Book of Life from the foundation of the world (Revelation 13:8, Revelation 17:8), and the sheep-hear-because-they-are-sheep (John 10:26-27 — *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep... my sheep hear my voice*). The substance is the BOTH-AND-not-EITHER-OR pattern the canon carries — neither the Reformed-monergistic-determinism that collapses the substance to the Father-unilaterally-chooses-without-disposition-walked, nor the Arminian-self-determinative-libertarian-freedom that collapses the substance to the human-chooses-without-the-Father's-foreknowledge; the hearing reveals what was already true before the foundation, and the substance-walked is the substance-walked-by-the-citizen-who-is. The parable also carries an additional historic-redemptive substance at the band-edge — the early-hired prefigure the substance-of-Yahudah (Judah) the southern-house that-retained-the-tribal-identity through-the-exile-and-return, and the eleventh-hour-laborers prefigure the substance-of-the-scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel) the ten-northern-tribes now being-gathered-home through-the-King's-proclamation; the same-penny-of-inheritance distributed by-the-Father's-will rules out the murmuring-against-the-late-coming-of-the-scattered-seed-substance whether-from-the-southern-house, from-Christian-replacement-theology, or-from-Hebrew-Roots-Torah-keeping-substance. (2) The third passion-prediction (vv.17-19). The most-specific of the three predictions — the first laid at Matthew 16:21 (*suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day*), the second at Matthew 17:22-23 (*the Son of Adam shall be betrayed into the hands of men*), the third here adding *unto the chief priests and unto the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him to the goyim (Gentiles) to mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him: and the third day he shall rise again.* The substance lays the condemnation-substance at the Pharisaic-Sadducean-system (the chief priests and scribes the gospel has confronted since Matthew 3:7) and the execution-substance at the Roman-administration (the Tanakh-pattern of the goyim-administration-as-instrument-of-judgment at Isaiah 10:5-7's *Ashshur (Assyrian), the rod of mine anger* and Jeremiah 25:8-9's *Nevukhadnetstsar (Nebuchadrezzar)... my servant*); the *mock-scourge-crucify* triple is laid against Psalm 22:7-8 (the mocking), Isaiah 50:6 (the scourging), and Isaiah 53:5 (the crucifying-substance the Suffering-Servant bore); the *third-day raising* seals the prediction at the sign-of-Yonah resurrection-credential (Matthew 12:39-40) and the Hosea 6:1-2 *after two days will he revive us; in the third day he will raise us up* substance. The *must* (Greek *dei*) of divine-necessity that opened the first prediction continues — the cross is the substance the Son walks-into knowingly under the Father's-determination from before the foundation of the world, not a defeat imposed by circumstances. (3) Framework-major — the mother-of-Zavdai (Zebedee)'s petition + the cup-of-suffering substance + the ten-other-apostles' indignation + the kingdom-citizens'-greatness-as-servant + the *give-his-life-a-ransom-for-many* substance (vv.20-28). The petition for the thrones-on-the-King's-right-and-left lands *immediately* after the third passion-prediction the disciples have just heard — the juxtaposition itself is the substance: the King names the cross, and the disciples ask for the highest-honor positions in the kingdom-they-have-not-yet-understood. The Markan-parallel at Mark 10:35-37 names Ya'aqov (James) and Yochanan (John) themselves as the petitioners; Matthew preserves the mother-walking-the-petition-with-her-sons; both substances are true — the family-of-Zavdai (Zebedee) walks the petition together, and the King's-answer addresses-the-sons directly. The King's *ye know not what ye ask. Are ye able to drink of the cup that I shall drink of?* invokes the Tanakh-cup-of-wrath-substance the prophets have carried throughout the prophetic-corpus — Psalm 75:8's *in the hand of Yahuah (the LORD) there is a cup, and the wine is red... the wicked of the earth shall wring them out, and drink them,* Isaiah 51:17, 21-22's *the cup of his fury... the cup of trembling,* Jeremiah 25:15-16's *the wine cup of this fury at my hand,* Ezekiel 23:32-34's *thou shalt drink of thy sister's cup,* Habakkuk 2:16's *the cup of Yahuah's (the LORD's) right hand shall be turned unto thee.* The cup the King is about to drink is the substance-of-Yahuah's-judgment-on-the-substance-of-sin substantially-poured-on-the-Son-of-Adam-on-the-cross — the same cup he will pray to have pass at the garden of Gethsemane (*if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt,* Matthew 26:39). The substance opens the door-home through the new-heart-substance Ezekiel 36:26-27 had named and the new-covenant-substance Jeremiah 31:31-34 had named — the cross does not cancel the curse-system the Way warns against; the substance-of-walking-off-the-Way still bears the substance-of-the-consequence, but the door-home and the Spirit causing-the-disciple-to-walk in the Father's-statutes is the substance the cup-the-King-drinks opens onto. The baptism-substance (*to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with*) reads in continuity with the cup as the Tanakh-substance-of-being-overwhelmed-with-suffering at Psalm 42:7's *all thy waves and thy billows are gone over me* and Psalm 69:1-2's *the waters are come in unto my soul.* The sons' *we are able* is confidence-without-comprehension; the King confirms they will-drink-the-cup at the apostolic-mission-cost-level (not at the substitutionary-substance-level) — Ya'aqov (James) the son of Zavdai (Zebedee) is killed by Herodes-Agrippa-I at Acts 12:2 as the first apostolic-martyr; Yochanan (John) walks the substance-of-tribulation across the long-apostolic-mission to Patmos (Revelation 1:9). The King's *not mine to give, but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared of my Father* preserves the Father's-prerogative at the assignment-of-the-positions per the Father-Son-architecture the canon names — the Father is the Formless-infinite the source-of-all-substance, the Son is the Formed the substance-drawn-out-of-the-Formless the Tanakh-encounters present in pre-incarnate-substance from the prophets onward, and the Son's-eternal-submission-to-the-Father is the substance the King walks in the flesh and before the flesh (John 5:19-20, 6:38, 14:10); the substance rules out both the Trinitarian-co-equal-persons-without-the-eternal-submission construction and the modalist-collapse-of-Father-and-Son-into-one-undifferentiated-substance. The ten-other-apostles' indignation (v.24) is not about the wrongness of the petition but the priority of it — all twelve walk the same natural-kingdoms'-hierarchy-substance, and the two-sons simply asked first. The King's *ye know that the princes of the goyim (Gentiles) exercise dominion (Greek katakyrieuousin) over them, and they that are great exercise authority (katexousiazousin) upon them. But it shall not be so among you* lays the kingdom-categorically against the natural-kingdoms'-administration the Tanakh-prophetic-witness names as the watcher-rebellion-administration (Daniel 10:13, 20's *the prince of Paras (Persia)... the prince of Yavan (Grecia)* the prophet Daniel encountered behind the human-administrations; Ephesians 6:12's *we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities... spiritual wickedness in high places*; 1 John 5:19's *the whole world lieth in wickedness*). The kingdom-citizens'-substance is the categorical-inversion — *whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister (diakonos — table-server); And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant (doulos — bond-servant)* — in continuity with the *blessed-are-the-poor-in-spirit* of Matthew 5:3, the *whosoever-shall-humble-himself-as-this-little-child* of Matthew 18:4, the *of-such-is-the-kingdom-of-heaven* of Matthew 19:14, the foot-washing the King models at John 13:15-17, the Christological-substance of the Philippian-letter (*let this mind be in you, which was also in Messiah Yahusha (Christ Jesus): Who, being in the form of Elohim (God), thought it not robbery to be equal with Elohim (God): But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant,* Philippians 2:5-8), and the apostle Kefa (Peter)'s exhortation to the elders (*neither as being lords over Elohim (God)'s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock,* 1 Peter 5:1-3). The chapter's framework-major moment then lands at v.28 — *even as the Son of Adam came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.* The King names himself explicitly as the Tanakh-Suffering-Servant of Isaiah 53 and the *kofer*-ransom-substance of the Torah. The Greek *lytron anti pollōn* (*ransom in-place-of many*) reads at the Torah's-*kofer*-substance — Exodus 21:30's *the ransom of his life,* Exodus 30:12-13, 16's *every man a ransom for his soul unto Yahuah (the LORD)... to make an atonement for your souls* (the half-shekel-substance the King had referenced at Matthew 17:24-27's temple-tax), Numbers 35:31-33's *ye shall take no satisfaction (kofer) for the life of a murderer... the land cannot be cleansed of the blood that is shed therein, but by the blood of him that shed it* (the substance no natural-*kofer*-could-cover, requiring the substantial-blood the Son-of-Adam alone could give). Psalm 49:6-9 named the substance the prophet-king David had-walked-in: *none of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to Elohim (God) a ransom for him: (for the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever)* — no natural man can give; only the Father's-Son can. Isaiah 53:10-12 is the central-Tanakh-anchor the King draws from: *when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin... by his knowledge shall my righteous servant justify many; for he shall bear their iniquities... he bare the sin of many, and made intercession for the transgressors* — the *many* (Hebrew *rabbim*) the King names at Matthew 20:28 is the *many* of the Servant-passage at Isaiah 53:11-12; and Isaiah 53:5-6's *he was wounded for our transgressions... Yahuah (the LORD) hath laid on him the iniquity of us all* is the substance the King explicitly claims as-his-own-coming-substance. Daniel 9:24-27's seventy-weeks-substance — *to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness... Messiah shall be cut off, but not for himself* — is the atonement-substance the ransom accomplishes. The *for many* (*anti pollōn*) reads at the three-category architecture the canon names — neither the universalist-collapse (which would erase substance-of-disposition) nor the limited-atonement-restrictivism (which would erase the broad-substance the Father has known), but the substantial-multitude the Father's-Book-of-Life from-the-foundation-of-the-world has named — the citizens of the house of Yahudah (Judah) that-retained-the-tribal-identity, the citizens of the scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel) the ten-northern-tribes now being-gathered-home through-the-King's-proclamation, and the substance the Father's-foreknown-citizens walk through-the-gathering-arc. The apostolic-witness restates the substance throughout — the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) at Romans 3:24-25's *being justified freely by his grace through the redemption (apolytrōsin) that is in Messiah Yahusha (Christ Jesus): Whom Elohim (God) hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood,* at 1 Timothy 2:5-6's *one mediator between Elohim (God) and men... Who gave himself a ransom (antilytron) for all*; the writer of Hebrews at Hebrews 9:11-15's *by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us*; the apostle Kefa (Peter) at 1 Peter 1:18-19's *redeemed... with the precious blood of Messiah (Christ), as of a lamb without blemish and without spot*; the apostle Yochanan (John) at Revelation 5:9's *thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to Elohim (God) by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation.* The unified apostolic-witness preserves the King's-own-mouth Tanakh-Suffering-Servant self-identification at the chapter-substance-pivot. (4) The two-blind-men at Yericho (Jericho) and the *Son of David* Messianic-confession (vv.29-34). The chapter closes at Yericho (Jericho), the city of the original-entry-into-the-Land under Yahoshua (Joshua) son of Nun (Joshua 6:20's wall-falling-flat substance) and the city carrying the substance of the curse-on-the-rebuilders (Joshua 6:26, fulfilled in 1 Kings 16:34's Chi'el (Hiel) the Bethelite). The King-passes-through Yericho on the final-approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) — the substance the original-entry-into-the-Land had walked through, the King now walks through into the new-entry the cross-and-resurrection-substance will open. The two-blind-men's cry *thou Son of David, have mercy on us* invokes the Tanakh-Davidic-Messianic-substance the prophets carried throughout the canon — the Davidic-covenant at 2 Samuel 7:12-16 (*I will set up thy seed after thee... I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever*), the rod-of-Yishai (Jesse) substance at Isaiah 11:1-2, 10, the righteous-Branch at Jeremiah 23:5-6 (*his name whereby he shall be called, Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS)*), the David-the-servant-king of the restored-twelve-tribes at Ezekiel 34:23-24 and Ezekiel 37:24-25. The marginalized-substance recognizes-the-Davidic-Messianic-substance the surrounding multitude cannot see — physically-blind-but-seeing-the-King — the seed-war-hearing-pattern the King's *my sheep hear my voice* of John 10:26-27 names, operating at the marginalized-level the gospel has been preserving through the Matt 9:27-31 Galilean-two-blind-men, the Matt 15:22 Canaanite-woman, and now the Yericho (Jericho)-two-blind-men, with the substance anticipating the Matt 21:9 triumphal-entry *Hoshanna (Hosanna) to the Son of David,* the Matt 21:15 children-in-the-temple substance, and the Matt 22:41-46 King's-own-question about the Son-of-David. The multitude's-rebuke (the system-substance not wanting the recognition disturbed — the same pre-rejection-substance walking-toward what the disciples did at the children-substance the King rebuked at Matthew 19:13-15) meets the blind-men's-greater-cry, the substantial-persistence-in-the-cry the Tanakh-Psalms-of-distress preserve (Psalm 130:1-2's *out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Yahuah (LORD)*; Psalm 6:1-2). The King-stops, calls, asks (*what will ye that I shall do unto you?* — honoring the marginalized with attention-and-articulation), and-touches-the-eyes; the substance is the Isaiah-35 Messianic-credential the gospel has been carrying since Matthew 11:4-5 — *then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped* (Isaiah 35:5), *to open the blind eyes, to bring out the prisoners from the prison* (Isaiah 42:6-7), *Yahuah (the LORD) openeth the eyes of the blind* (Psalm 146:8) — and the closing *they followed him* is the discipleship-substance the substantial-hearing-and-seeing produces, the cause-and-effect-of-hearing the chapter opened on at the parable-substance now substantiated at the chapter-closing-substance. The chapter teaches the kingdom-citizens'-substance from the laborers'-substance through the disciples'-substance to the marginalized's-substance, with the King's-substantial-substitutionary-substance laid-down at the chapter-pivot at the framework-major *ransom-for-many* statement of v.28, and the next chapter opens with the triumphal-entry into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) — the colt-and-the-foal, the multitudes spreading-garments-and-branches and-crying *Hoshanna (Hosanna) to the Son of David,* the cleansing-of-the-temple at the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)'s house-of-prayer-substance from Jeremiah 7, the cursing-of-the-fig-tree, and-the-question-of-the-King's-authority opening the controversy-section of the Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)-week.
