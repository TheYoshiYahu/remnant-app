# Short-form commentary on Matthew 20

Matthew 20 continues the King's long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) opened at Matthew 19:1. The geographic-trajectory laid down at the close of the previous chapter — *he departed from Galil (Galilee), and came into the coasts of Yahudah (Judaea) beyond the Yarden (Jordan)* — now closes the eastern-bank Peraean stretch and arcs through Yericho (Jericho) on the final leg toward the city. Four movements anchor the chapter, and two of them carry framework-major weight. The laborers-in-the-vineyard parable at vv.1-16 unpacks the *first-shall-be-last-and-last-shall-be-first* substance the previous chapter closed on at Matthew 19:30 — the parable lays bare the kingdom-economy's-substance as the Father's-grace-distributed-by-the-householder's-will, not the natural-economy's-substance of merit-by-time-served, and the closing word *many be called, but few chosen* lands the parable at the cause-and-effect-of-hearing the King-himself has been unfolding through every parable of the kingdom since Matthew 13. The third passion-prediction at vv.17-19 is the most-specific of the three (after Matthew 16:21 and Matthew 17:22-23) — the King names the chief priests and the scribes as the condemnation-agents, the goyim (Gentiles) as the executing administration, the mocking-and-scourging-and-crucifying as the specific suffering, and the third-day-raising as the closing-credential. The mother-of-Zavdai (Zebedee)'s petition for thrones-on-the-King's-right-and-left at vv.20-23 — laid in the disciples'-hearing immediately after the third passion-prediction — reveals the natural-kingdoms'-hierarchy-substance still operating beneath the disciples'-substance even at this late hour, and the King's-cup-of-suffering answer reads at the Tanakh-cup-of-wrath substance the prophets have carried; the ten-other-apostles'-indignation at v.24 confirms that all of them walk the same natural-substance, and the King's *but it shall not be so among you* at vv.25-27 lays the kingdom-citizens'-greatness-as-servant substance against the natural-kingdoms'-domination-substance under the watcher-rebellion-administration the Tanakh and the apostolic-witness name from beyond the curtain of the present age. At v.28 the chapter lands its framework-major moment — *even as the Son of Adam came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many* — the King's-own-mouth explicit identification of himself as the Tanakh-Suffering-Servant of Isaiah 53 and the *kofer*-ransom-substance of the Torah, with the *for many* substance read against the *rabbim* of the Servant-passage and the Three-Categories architecture of the framework. The chapter closes at vv.29-34 with the two-blind-men of Yericho (Jericho) crying *thou Son of David, have mercy on us* — the marginalized-substance recognizing the Davidic-Messianic-substance the surrounding system cannot see, the multitude-rebuke met by the louder-cry, the King's-compassion opening the blind-eyes per the Isaiah-35-Messianic-credential the gospel has carried since Matthew 11:4-5, and the *they followed him* substance closing the chapter at the disciple-substance the seeing-eyes produce.

*For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard. And when he had agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, he sent them into his vineyard. And he went out about the third hour, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace, And said unto them; Go ye also into the vineyard, and whatsoever is right I will give you. And they went their way. Again he went out about the sixth and ninth hour, and did likewise. And about the eleventh hour he went out, and found others standing idle, and saith unto them, Why stand ye here all the day idle? They say unto him, Because no man hath hired us. He saith unto them, Go ye also into the vineyard; and whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive.* (Matthew 20:1-7)

*For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder.* The parable is a kingdom-of-heaven parable — one of the great teaching-vehicles by which the King unfolds the kingdom-substance to those-with-ears-to-hear and conceals it from those-without. The householder is the Father; the vineyard is Yashar'el (Israel)'s covenant-body the prophets have named; the laborers are those-the-Father-calls into the vineyard's-substance across the day's hours. The Tanakh-vineyard-substance lands at the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah)'s great vineyard-song at the opening of his book — *now will I sing to my wellbeloved a song of my beloved touching his vineyard. My wellbeloved hath a vineyard in a very fruitful hill: and he fenced it, and gathered out the stones thereof, and planted it with the choicest vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and also made a winepress therein: and he looked that it should bring forth grapes, and it brought forth wild grapes* (Isaiah 5:1-2). The prophet closes the song by naming the substance the vineyard represented: *for the vineyard of Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts) is the house of Yashar'el (Israel), and the men of Yahudah (Judah) his pleasant plant: and he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold a cry* (Isaiah 5:7). The vineyard's-substance is Yashar'el (Israel)'s-covenant-body the Father has planted and-fenced and-tended-for-fruit. The prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) carries the same substance — *many pastors have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my portion under foot, they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness* (Jeremiah 12:10). The parable's-householder is the Father standing over the Tanakh-vineyard-substance the prophets have named, hiring laborers into the substance the Father is still cultivating in the age the parable is set in.

*Early in the morning.* The first-hour-hiring lands at the day's-opening — the agreed-wage between the householder and the first-hired is named explicitly: *a penny a day.* The Greek for *penny* is *dēnarion* — the Roman silver denarius the day-laborer in the first-century Levantine-economy received as the standard wage for a full day's-work. The wage is fair-by-the-natural-economy; the laborer-agrees-with-the-householder for the penny; the laborer goes-into-the-vineyard with the agreement-substance in hand. *And he went out about the third hour, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace.* The third-hour is nine-o'clock by the modern reckoning (counting from sunrise at the sixth-hour-of-the-prior-night through the day's twelve daylight-hours). The substance laid in the next four hiring-rounds adds-laborers at the third-hour, the sixth-hour (noon), the ninth-hour (three-in-the-afternoon), and the eleventh-hour (five-o'clock, one hour before sunset). At the third-hour and onward the wording-of-the-agreement shifts — the householder does-not-agree to a fixed-wage with the later-hired; the householder says *whatsoever is right I will give you.* The substance is laid: the early-hired have an agreed-wage; the later-hired have the householder's-promise-of-rightness without a fixed-wage. The substance the parable will turn on at the payment-hour is laid in this shift between the first agreement and the later promises.

*So when even was come, the lord of the vineyard saith unto his steward, Call the labourers, and give them their hire, beginning from the last unto the first. And when they came that were hired about the eleventh hour, they received every man a penny. But when the first came, they supposed that they should have received more; and they likewise received every man a penny.* (Matthew 20:8-10)

*Beginning from the last unto the first.* The payment-order is the reversal-substance the parable's-closing-line will name explicitly. The eleventh-hour-laborers receive their penny first; the first-hour-laborers receive their penny last; and the substance the first-hour-laborers see in the meantime is the eleventh-hour-laborers receiving-the-penny that-equals-what-was-agreed-for-the-first-hour-laborers. *They supposed that they should have received more.* The natural-economy's-substance of merit-by-time-served operates in the first-hired's-substance: more-hours-worked must mean more-pay-received. The natural-economy's-substance assumes-that-the-eleventh-hour-laborers receiving-the-penny establishes-the-rate-up-from-the-penny for the first-hour-laborers. The substance does not hold; the householder pays-each-the-penny-agreed. The first-hired-receive the penny-agreed; the substance the parable will turn on is laid: the first-hired have not been cheated. The first-hired received what they agreed to. The substance the first-hired complain-about is not a wage-injustice; the substance the first-hired complain-about is the householder's-grace toward the eleventh-hour-laborers exceeding the natural-economy's-expectation. The kingdom-economy's-substance has entered the natural-economy's-frame and broken-it-open at the substance of the householder's-will-toward-the-late-hired.

*And when they had received it, they murmured against the goodman of the house, Saying, These last have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day.* (Matthew 20:11-12)

The first-hired's-grumbling is the substance the parable lays bare. *They murmured against the goodman of the house.* The Greek for *murmured* is *egongyzon* — the same root the Septuagint uses across the wilderness-narratives where Yashar'el (Israel) murmurs against Yahuah (the LORD) in the desert. *And the whole congregation of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) murmured against Mosheh (Moses) and Aharon (Aaron) in the wilderness* (Exodus 16:2); *and the people thirsted there for water; and the people murmured against Mosheh (Moses)* (Exodus 17:3); *all the children of Yashar'el (Israel) murmured against Mosheh (Moses) and against Aharon (Aaron): and the whole congregation said unto them, Would Elohim (God) that we had died in the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt)!* (Numbers 14:2); *how long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me? I have heard the murmurings of the children of Yashar'el (Israel), which they murmur against me* (Numbers 14:27). The wilderness-pattern of Yashar'el (Israel)'s-murmuring-against-Yahuah-substance is the Tanakh-pattern the first-hired-laborers' substance recapitulates. The substance is the same in both registers: the substance refuses-the-substance-the-Father-gives by asserting-the-natural-substance-against-it. The wilderness-substance refused-the-manna by-comparing-it-to-the-fleshpots-of-Mitsrayim (Egypt); the laborers'-substance refuses-the-penny-the-householder-distributes by-comparing-it-to-the-natural-economy's-expectation. The substance the householder will name in his answer is the substance that-the-wilderness-pattern carried-forward.

*But he answered one of them, and said, Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst not thou agree with me for a penny? Take that thine is, and go thy way: I will give unto this last, even as unto thee. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own? Is thine eye evil, because I am good?* (Matthew 20:13-15)

*Friend.* The Greek is *hetaire* — a polite-address-of-acquaintance, not the warm *philos* that names the King's-friends at John 15:14-15. The address-form lands in three places in the Matthean gospel and-each-place carries-correction-following: here at the laborers'-parable to the murmuring-first-hired; at Matthew 22:12 to the man-without-the-wedding-garment thrown-out-of-the-feast; at Matthew 26:50 to Yahudah (Judas) at the moment-of-the-betrayal-kiss. The address is the polite-form-of-substantial-correction the King uses when the substance-being-addressed has-walked-against-the-substance-the-King-stands-in. The householder addresses the first-hired with the same form — *hetaire* — and the correction follows.

*I do thee no wrong.* The justice-substance of the agreement holds. The first-hired-agreed to the penny; the first-hired-received the penny. The substance is not a-wrong-against-the-laborer. *Didst not thou agree with me for a penny?* The agreement-substance the laborer-walked-into is the substance the householder-honors. *Take that thine is, and go thy way.* The penny-agreed is the laborer's-substance; the laborer-may-take-it-and-go. The substance is closed at the level of the agreement.

*I will give unto this last, even as unto thee. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own?* The householder's-prerogative over his own substance is the substance the parable lays. The eleventh-hour-laborers receive-the-same-penny because-the-householder-wills it; the householder's-substance is the householder's-substance to distribute; the substance is lawful-by-the-householder's-will. The substance is the Father's-prerogative over the Father's-grace. The Tanakh-substance the parable echoes lands at Job 41:11 — *who hath prevented me, that I should repay him? whatsoever is under the whole heaven is mine.* The Father's-grace is the Father's-grace; the Father-distributes-the-substance at the substance the Father-wills.

*Is thine eye evil, because I am good?* The closing-question is a Tanakh-Hebrew-idiom — the *good-eye* and the *evil-eye* substance the King has already named at the heart of the Sermon-on-the-Mount. *The light of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light. But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great is that darkness!* (Matthew 6:22-23). The Hebrew-idiom-substance is not optical-physiology; the *good-eye* (*ayin tovah*) is the disposition-of-generosity-toward-the-other, and the *evil-eye* (*ayin ra'ah*) is the disposition-of-stinginess-and-envy-toward-the-other. The Tanakh-substance carries the idiom-pattern through the wisdom-literature and the Torah's-social-statutes: *beware that there be not a thought in thy wicked heart, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, is at hand; and thine eye be evil against thy poor brother, and thou givest him nought; and he cry unto Yahuah (the LORD) against thee, and it be sin unto thee* (Deuteronomy 15:9); *eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats* (Proverbs 23:6); *he that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him* (Proverbs 28:22). The *evil-eye* is the substance-of-stinginess-toward-the-Father's-generosity-to-the-other; the *good-eye* is the substance-of-rejoicing-in-the-Father's-generosity-to-the-other. The first-hired-laborer's *evil-eye* is the substance of begrudging-the-householder-for-the-householder's-substantial-grace toward the eleventh-hour-laborer. The substance the parable names is the disposition-of-the-laborer-toward-the-Father's-grace-when-the-grace-falls-on-someone-else. The kingdom-citizens'-substance is not the *evil-eye*-substance; the kingdom-citizens'-substance is the *good-eye*-substance that-rejoices-when-the-substance-falls-on-the-fellow-laborer.

*So the last shall be first, and the first last: for many be called, but few chosen.* (Matthew 20:16)

*So the last shall be first, and the first last.* The closing-restatement of Matthew 19:30 — the bridge-substance the parable was sent to unpack. The kingdom-economy's-substance does not rank by the natural-substance of when-arrived, how-much-time-walked, or how-much-substance-given. The kingdom-economy's-substance ranks by the Father's-substance-of-grace-distributed-by-his-will. The first-by-the-natural-economy can-be-last-in-the-kingdom-substance; the last-by-the-natural-economy can-be-first-in-the-kingdom-substance. The substance is the inversion-pattern that-runs through the whole-gospel — through the *blessed-are-the-poor-in-spirit* of the Sermon-on-the-Mount, through the *whosoever-shall-humble-himself-as-this-little-child* of Matthew 18:4, through the *every-one-that-hath-forsaken-houses-shall-receive-an-hundredfold* of Matthew 19:29, through the *whosoever-will-be-great-among-you-let-him-be-your-minister* the King is about to deliver in this very chapter at vv.26-27.

*For many be called, but few chosen.* The closing-clause lands the parable at the substance the King has been unfolding through every parable of the kingdom — the cause-and-effect-of-hearing the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) had named at the call-vision of his prophetic-commissioning. *And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not. Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed* (Isaiah 6:9-10). The substance the prophet was sent into is the substance the parables-of-the-kingdom carry-forward in the King's-own-teaching — *therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand. And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Yeshayahu (Esaias), which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive* (Matthew 13:13-14). The substance the prophets carried-forward and the King unfolds-in-the-parables is the substance that-the-hearing-reveals-what-was-already-true at the substance-level. The apostle Yochanan (John) will carry the same substance forward in the King's-own-mouth-statement at the good-shepherd-discourse — *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep, as I said unto you. My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me* (John 10:26-27). The substance the Shepherd-himself names: hearing-reveals; the sheep-hear-because-they-are-sheep; the not-of-his-sheep-do-not-hear because-they-are-not. The substance is not the hearing-makes-the-sheep; the substance is the sheep-hear-because-they-are-the-sheep. The substance is the cause-and-effect-of-hearing the framework has read through every parable since Matthew 13.

The *many* substance reads with the gospel-proclamation broadly. The proclamation is to the *many* because the citizens-of-the-kingdom are scattered-among-the-nations and the proclaimers-do-not-know-the-citizens-by-sight. The proclamation goes-broadly because the sheep-are-scattered. The *few chosen* substance reads with the substance-the-Father-has-known from-the-foundation-of-the-world. The names-written-in-the-Book-of-Life from-the-foundation-of-the-world lands at the apostle Yochanan (John)'s-revelation-vision: *and all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world* (Revelation 13:8); *the beast that thou sawest was, and is not; and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition: and they that dwell on the earth shall wonder, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, when they behold the beast that was, and is not, and yet is* (Revelation 17:8). The substance the apostle Yochanan (John) names is the substance the canon carries: the Father-knows-his-own from-the-foundation-of-the-world; the names-are-not-added after-the-foundation but-can-be-blotted-out by the substance-walked. *Yahuah (the LORD) knoweth them that are his* (2 Timothy 2:19); *the foundation of Elohim (God) standeth sure, having this seal* (same verse). The cause-and-effect-of-hearing is the seal of the Father's-foreknowledge upon the substance-the-citizen-walks.

The substance is BOTH-AND, not EITHER-OR. The inherited-Reformed-monergistic-determinism that-collapses-the-substance into the-Father-unilaterally-chooses-without-the-substance-of-disposition-walked is read-out by the cause-and-effect-architecture the King's-own-mouth names — the substance the citizen-walks is the substance-the-Father-knew-from-the-foundation, and the substance-walked is the substance-walked-by-the-citizen-who-is. The inherited-Arminian-self-determinative-libertarian-freedom that-collapses-the-substance into the-human-chooses-without-the-Father's-foreknowledge is also read-out — the hearing-reveals-what-was-already-true; the substance-of-disposition is the substance-the-Father-knew-from-the-foundation; the hearing-does-not-create the substance-of-disposition. The *many called, but few chosen* substance is the seal of both substances at once — the broad-call going-out because the sheep-are-scattered, and the *chosen* (Greek *eklektoi*) substance walking-as-the-citizens-the-Father-has-known-from-before-the-foundation. The Matthean gospel will restate the substance at Matthew 22:14 — *for many are called, but few are chosen* — closing the wedding-feast-parable on the same substance the laborers'-parable closes on here.

The laborers'-parable carries an additional substance at the historic-redemptive-arc level. The first-hour-laborers and the eleventh-hour-laborers stand-in-the-vineyard at the same payment-hour despite-the-different-hours-of-their-entry. The substance reads with the Tanakh-architecture of Yashar'el (Israel)'s-vineyard-substance under the prophets and the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed at the consummation. The first-hour-laborers are the substance-of-Yahudah (Judah) the southern-house that-retained-the-substance-of-the-Torah and-the-temple-and-the-priesthood-and-the-tribal-identity through-the-exile-and-return; the eleventh-hour-laborers are the substance-of-the-scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel) the northern-ten-tribes-divorced-through-the-Assyrian-captivity now-being-gathered-home through-the-King's-proclamation-of-the-kingdom. The substance the Father-distributes-at-the-payment-hour is the same-substance for both — the penny-of-inheritance the Father's-grace-distributes by-the-Father's-will. The substance the early-hired-could-murmur-against is the substance-of-the-late-hired-receiving-the-same-inheritance the Father had-promised to-the-scattered-seed throughout-the-prophets. The substance the parable rules-out at the historic-redemptive-arc level is the substance-of-the-Yahudah (Judah)-substance murmuring-against-the-Father for-the-substance-the-Father-distributes-to-the-scattered-seed-coming-home; the substance the parable rules-out at the post-apostolic level is the substance-of-the-Christian-replacement-theology murmuring-against-the-substance-the-Father-distributes-to-the-paternal-blood-remnant-of-the-twelve-tribes; the substance the parable rules-out at the Hebrew-Roots-level is the substance-of-the-Torah-keeping-Hebrew-Roots-substance murmuring-against-the-substance-the-Father-distributes-to-the-scattered-seed-still-in-the-Father's-knowing-but-still-on-the-road-home. The kingdom-citizens'-substance is the *good-eye*-substance that-rejoices-when-the-eleventh-hour-laborer-receives-the-same-penny — when the late-comer-arrives, when the scattered-seed-comes-home, when the Father's-substance-distributes-by-the-Father's-will.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) going up to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) took the twelve disciples apart in the way, and said unto them, Behold, we go up to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem); and the Son of Adam shall be betrayed unto the chief priests and unto the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death, And shall deliver him to the goyim (Gentiles) to mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him: and the third day he shall rise again.* (Matthew 20:17-19)

The third passion-prediction. The Matthean gospel has carried three explicit passion-predictions across the chapters since Caesarea-Philippi at Matthew 16:13-20. The first lands at Matthew 16:21 — *from that time forth began Yahusha (Jesus) to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.* The first prediction lays the substance generally — the *must* (Greek *dei*) of divine-necessity, the suffering at the hands of the elders-and-chief-priests-and-scribes, the killing, the third-day-raising. The second lands at Matthew 17:22-23 immediately after the transfiguration — *while they abode in Galil (Galilee), Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, The Son of Adam shall be betrayed into the hands of men: and they shall kill him, and the third day he shall be raised again. And they were exceeding sorry.* The second prediction adds the substance of the betrayal (the Greek *paradidotai* — *delivered up*) and-tightens the substance toward-the-betrayer-substance Yahudah (Judas) will walk. The third prediction here at Matthew 20:17-19 is the most-specific of the three — adding the chief-priests-and-scribes-specific-condemnation, the delivery-to-the-goyim (Gentiles) for the execution-substance, the substance-of-mocking-and-scourging-and-crucifying, and the third-day-raising sealed at the close.

*The Son of Adam shall be betrayed unto the chief priests and unto the scribes.* The title-substance carries the Adamic-seed-of-promise about-to-suffer the substance the Tanakh-Suffering-Servant has prophesied throughout the prophets. The substance lands at Isaiah 53:3 — *he is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not.* The substance the prophet named is the substance the chief-priests-and-the-scribes will-walk in-the-condemnation-substance. The chief-priests-and-the-scribes are the substance-of-the-Pharisaic-Sadducean-system the gospel has been confronting since the wilderness-baptism — *but when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?* (Matthew 3:7) — the same system-substance that-will-deliver-the-King-to-the-cross.

*They shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him to the goyim (Gentiles) to mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him.* The condemnation-substance lands at the chief-priests-and-the-scribes; the execution-substance lands at the goyim (Gentiles) — the Roman-administration the Pharisaic-Sadducean-system cannot-carry-the-death-sentence-without. The Tanakh-substance the deliverance-to-the-goyim recapitulates is the prophet-pattern of the goyim-administration-as-instrument-of-Yahuah's-judgment — *O Ashshur (Assyrian), the rod of mine anger, and the staff in their hand is mine indignation. I will send him against an hypocritical nation, and against the people of my wrath will I give him a charge, to take the spoil, and to take the prey, and to tread them down like the mire of the streets. Howbeit he meaneth not so, neither doth his heart think so; but it is in his heart to destroy and cut off nations not a few* (Isaiah 10:5-7); *therefore thus saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts); Because ye have not heard my words, Behold, I will send and take all the families of the north, saith Yahuah (the LORD), and Nevukhadnetstsar (Nebuchadrezzar) the king of Babel (Babylon), my servant, and will bring them against this land, and against the inhabitants thereof, and against all these nations round about, and will utterly destroy them* (Jeremiah 25:8-9). The goyim-administration acts-as-instrument while-meaning-it-not-so. The Roman-administration will-mock-and-scourge-and-crucify the King without-knowing the substance of-what-they-walk-in.

*To mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him.* The three-fold-specification lands the substance per the Tanakh-prophetic-witness on the substance-of-the-Servant's-suffering. *All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying, He trusted on Yahuah (the LORD) that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him* (Psalm 22:7-8).
