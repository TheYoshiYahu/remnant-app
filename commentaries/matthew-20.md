## §1. The laborers-in-the-vineyard parable — the kingdom-economy's-not-the-natural-economy

*For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard. And when he had agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, he sent them into his vineyard. And he went out about the third hour, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace, And said unto them; Go ye also into the vineyard, and whatsoever is right I will give you. And they went their way. Again he went out about the sixth and ninth hour, and did likewise. And about the eleventh hour he went out, and found others standing idle, and saith unto them, Why stand ye here all the day idle? They say unto him, Because no man hath hired us. He saith unto them, Go ye also into the vineyard; and whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive. So when even was come, the lord of the vineyard saith unto his steward, Call the labourers, and give them their hire, beginning from the last unto the first. And when they came that were hired about the eleventh hour, they received every man a penny. But when the first came, they supposed that they should have received more; and they likewise received every man a penny. And when they had received it, they murmured against the goodman of the house, Saying, These last have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day. But he answered one of them, and said, Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst not thou agree with me for a penny? Take that thine is, and go thy way: I will give unto the last, even as unto thee. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own? Is thine eye evil, because I am good? So the last shall be first, and the first last: for many be called, but few chosen* (vv.1-16).

**The framework reads vv.1-16 as the FRAMEWORK-MAJOR parable of the kingdom-economy's-not-the-natural-economy + the *many be called, but few chosen* substance.**

**The narrative-substance** (vv.1-10). The framework reads the parable's-narrative carefully. The householder hires-laborers at-five-different-hours throughout-the-day (early-morning + third-hour + sixth-hour + ninth-hour + eleventh-hour) and-pays-each-the-penny-agreed regardless-of-hours-worked. The framework reads the substance:

- **The penny (Greek *dēnarion*) is the standard-day-wage** for a Tanakh-Levantine-region laborer; the agreed-wage is fair-by-the-natural-economy.
- **The substance is NOT that the early-hired-were-cheated.** They received the penny-agreed.
- **The substance IS that the late-hired-received the same-penny-as-the-early-hired.** The householder's-substance toward-the-late-hired exceeds the natural-economy's-substance.

**The first-hired's-grumbling** (vv.11-12). *They murmured against the goodman of the house, Saying, These last have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day.* The framework reads the substance carefully:

- **The substance is the natural-economy's-substance asserting-itself against the kingdom-economy's-substance.** The first-hired apply the natural-economy's-substance (more-hours = more-pay) against the householder's-substance (same-penny-for-each-laborer).
- **The substance is the Tanakh-Yashar'el-wilderness-substance** — the substance-of-grumbling-against-Yahuah-substance the Tanakh-pattern names (Shemot (Exodus) 16:2 / 17:3 + Bemidbar (Numbers) 14 / 16's-wilderness-grumbling pattern).

**The householder's-answer** (vv.13-15). *Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst not thou agree with me for a penny? Take that thine is, and go thy way: I will give unto the last, even as unto thee. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own? Is thine eye evil, because I am good?* The framework reads the householder's-answer carefully:

- **The *friend* (Greek *hetaire*) substance** is the polite-address that-precedes-correction — same-substance as the King-to-Yahudah (Judas) at Matt 26:50 and to the man-without-the-wedding-garment at Matt 22:12. The substance is the polite-address-with-substantial-correction-following.
- **The *I do thee no wrong* substance** is the justice-substance — the laborer's-substance has-not-been-cheated; the laborer's-substance received-what-was-agreed.
- **The *is thine eye evil, because I am good?* substance** is the Hebrew-idiom-substance per the framework's-Matt-6:22-23 reading (the *good-eye*-as-generous + *evil-eye*-as-stingy idiom from `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` Matt 6 entry). The substance is the laborer's-stinginess-of-disposition toward-the-householder's-substance-of-generosity. The framework reads the substance in continuity with Devarim (Deuteronomy) 15:9 / Mishlei (Proverbs) 23:6 / Mishlei (Proverbs) 28:22 *evil-eye* Tanakh-substance pattern.

**The *so the last shall be first, and the first last* substance** (v.16a). The framework reads the substance as the continuation of the Matt 19:30 substance + the parable's-application. The substance is the kingdom-citizens'-substance-of-non-ranking-by-natural-substance.

**The *many be called, but few chosen* substance** (v.16b). The framework reads this carefully in framework-substance per Red Line #11.

The framework reads the substance:

- **The *many be called* substance** reads with the gospel-proclamation broadly — the kingdom-substance is proclaimed-widely; the lost-sheep-of-the-house-of-Yashar'el (Israel) mission-scope per Red Line #7 reaches-the-scattered-tribes-in-their-territories; the proclamation goes broadly because we do-not-know-the-citizens-by-sight per Three-Categories from voice skill.
- **The *few chosen* substance** reads with the cause-and-effect-of-hearing per Red Line #11 — the names-written-in-the-Book-of-Life-from-the-foundation-of-the-world hear-and-respond per John 10:27 + Revelation 13:8 + 17:8; the not-of-his-sheep do-not-hear per John 10:26; the *chosen* (Greek *eklektoi*) substance reads with the elect-substance per the framework's reading of election-as-Father-known-from-the-foundation-of-the-world.
- **Framework rules out the inherited-Reformed-monergistic-determinism reading** that-collapses-the-substance into Father-unilaterally-chooses-without-substance-of-disposition. The framework reads in framework-substance — the hearing reveals; the disposition-substance is the substance-of-disposition; the Father-knows from the foundation-of-the-world; the substance-walked is the substance-walked.
- **Framework rules out the inherited-Arminian-self-determinative-libertarian-freedom reading** that-collapses-the-substance into the human-chooses-without-the-Father's-foreknowledge. The framework reads in framework-substance — the substance is BOTH-AND-NOT-EITHER-OR: the substance of disposition is real, the Father's-knowing-from-the-foundation is real, the substance-walked reveals what was-true-substance-before-the-foundation.

The framework reads the parable in framework-substance — the kingdom-economy's-substance is the Father's-substance-of-grace operating-at-the-substance-the-householder-wills; the laborer's-substance-of-grumbling against-the-substance is the *evil-eye*-Tanakh-substance the kingdom-citizens'-substance must-NOT-walk. The *many be called, but few chosen* substance closes-the-parable at the framework's-cause-and-effect-of-hearing-substance per Red Line #11.

## §4. The ten-disciples'-indignation + the kingdom-citizens'-greatness-as-servant + the FRAMEWORK-MAJOR ransom-for-many
