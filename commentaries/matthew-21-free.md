# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 21

Matthew 21 closes the long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) the previous chapter walked through Yericho (Jericho) and opens the final week before the cross. Six movements, one framework-major. (1) The triumphal-entry at vv.1-11 brings the King into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) on the foal-of-an-ass per Zechariah 9:9 (*rejoice greatly, O daughter of Tsion (Zion); shout, O daughter of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem): behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass*). The Matthean account preserves both-animals — the ass and the colt — at the Hebrew-parallelism of the prophet's-line; the Matthean signature-citation formula (*that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet*) lands the entry within the Tanakh-prophesied-substance the gospel has used since Matthew 1:22 onward; the omission of the *just, and having salvation* clause from Zechariah 9:9 holds back the substance the first-coming-walks-into but the second-coming-walks-out-fully, the same pattern the Lukan account followed at Luke 4:18-19's Isaiah 61 citation. The ass-substance is the royal-Tanakh-mount-substance (Judges 5:10, 2 Samuel 16:2, 1 Kings 1:33), not the donkey-as-humble of modern reading; the meekness-substance the prophet named is the *praus* / *ani* substance the King had laid for the kingdom-citizens at Matthew 5:5 and claimed for himself at Matthew 11:29 — the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) coming-to-his-city does not come in the natural-kingdoms'-substance-of-domination. The multitudes' cry — *Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD)* — quotes Psalm 118:25-26's pilgrim-Hallel-substance, with *Son of David* the Davidic-Messianic-confession the marginalized-substance had carried at Matthew 9:27, Matthew 15:22, Matthew 20:30-31 now-public at the multitude-level. Psalm 118 runs through the whole chapter — the multitudes here, the children at v.15, and the King-himself at vv.42-43 of the husbandmen-parable all quoting the same Hallel-Psalm. The city's-question *Who is this?* and the multitude's-answer *the prophet of Natsareth (Nazareth) of Galil (Galilee)* preserves the Galilean-light substance of Isaiah 9:1-2 the gospel had laid at Matthew 4:13-16 — true but incomplete, since the marginalized-substance had already recognized more than the city now sees.

(2) The Temple-cleansing at vv.12-17 brings the King against the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership's monetization of the Court-of-the-goyim (Gentiles). The King cites Isaiah 56:7 — *my house shall be called the house of prayer* — whose context names the sons-of-the-stranger that-join-themselves to-Yahuah (the LORD) and the outcasts of-Yashar'el (Israel) the Father is-gathering to-his-holy-mountain. The Markan parallel at Mark 11:17 preserves the *for all people* (*pasin tois ethnesin*) clause the Matthean abbreviates; the substance the prophet had named is the gathering-of-the-scattered (the same substance the prophet Hoshea (Hosea) had named at *Lo-Ammi* becoming *sons of the living Elohim (God)* at Hosea 1:10, and the King-himself had-named at *other sheep I have, which are not of this fold* at John 10:16), not a categorical-inclusion of the unrelated-nations into the covenant-body. The King cites Jeremiah 7:11 — *but ye have made it a den of thieves* — whose Jeremiah-7 context is one of the Tanakh's most-substantial Temple-judgment-passages: the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) standing-in-the-gate of the First-Temple naming the leadership-substance trusting-in-the-Temple while-walking-against the covenant. The First-Temple was destroyed within-a-generation; the King citing the same-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)-substance at the Second-Temple-cleansing prefigures the destruction the Olivet-discourse at Matthew 24:1-2 will explicitly name. The healing of the blind-and-the-lame in the Temple (v.14) reverses the rabbinic-substance reading of 2 Samuel 5:8 as a categorical-exclusion of the blind-and-the-lame from the Temple-courts; the Isaiah 35:5-6 Messianic-credential the King had laid in the wilderness-credentialing at Matthew 11:4-5 is now walked-out at the very-court the system had categorically-excluded the credentialed-substance from. The children-in-the-Temple crying *Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David* and the King's citation of Psalm 8:2 — *out of the mouth of babes and sucklings* — invokes the whole Psalm-8 Adamic-substance (*what is mortal man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of Adam, that thou visitest him?*, Psalm 8:4) at the children's-substance the system-cannot-silence. The King lodges at Beit-Anyah (Bethany) — *the house of poverty* — not in the city the system controls.

(3) The cursing-of-the-fig-tree at vv.18-22 brings the substantial-prophetic-action against the leaves-without-fruit substance of the leadership-substance — the Tanakh-fig-tree-substance the prophets had carried as the covenant-fruitfulness substance. The substance lands at Hosea 9:10 (*I found Yashar'el (Israel) like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers as the firstripe in the fig tree at her first time: but they went to Ba'al-pe'or (Baal-peor)*), Jeremiah 8:13 (*there shall be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the fig tree, and the leaf shall fade*), Joel 1:7 (*he hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig tree*), and Micah 7:1 (*there is no cluster to eat: my soul desired the firstripe fruit*). The substance the King-walks-against is the leadership-substance, not the people-of-Yashar'el (Israel) categorically — the same distinction the matt-13 tares, the matt-15 blind-leaders-of-the-blind, and the matt-23 seven-woes hold throughout the gospel. The faith-and-mountain-moving teaching that follows extends Matthew 17:20's mustard-seed-faith substance — substantial-faith as qualitative-disposition, not quantitative-measurement. The mountain-substance lands at Psalm 46:2, Isaiah 40:4, Isaiah 54:10. The closing *whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive* is not the prosperity-substance the modern-pulpit has distorted-the-substance-into; the King's later Upper-Room qualifier at John 14:13-14 (*whatsoever ye shall ask in my name*) and the apostle Yochanan (John)'s epistle-qualifier at 1 John 5:14-15 (*if we ask any thing according to his will*) lock the substance to the Father's-will operating-through-the-disciple.

(4) The authority-challenge at vv.23-27 brings the formal-rabbinic credentialing-substance (*by what authority doest thou these things?*) against the King's-substance whose-authority is conferred directly-by-the-Father at the Yarden (Jordan)-baptism (Matthew 3:17) and at the transfiguration-mount (Matthew 17:5). The King redirects through the baptism-of-Yochanan (John) substance the system had already rejected. Yochanan (John) is the Eliyahu (Elijah)-pattern-prophet of Malachi 4:5-6 (*behold, I will send you Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD)*) the King had explicitly named at Matthew 11:13-14 and Matthew 17:11-13. The system's-reasoning-with-itself reveals political-calculus rather than truth-substance; the *we cannot tell* is substantially-false (the system knows; the system refuses-to-name); the King's-withholding-of-the-substance is not evasion but the consequence — the substance the system asks-for is the substance the system has-already-disqualified-itself-from-receiving by-rejecting Yochanan (John).

(5) The parable-of-the-two-sons at vv.28-32 lays the doing-of-the-Father's-will against the saying-yes-without-doing. The first-son-substance says *I will not* and-afterward-repents-and-goes; the second-son-substance says *I go, sir* (the *Lord, Lord* substance) and-walks-not. The parable is the substantial-parabolic-substance for Matthew 7:21-23 — *not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven*; the *I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity* (Greek *anomia*, *Torah-lessness*) names the substance directly. The publicans-and-harlots-substance the Pharisaic-system had-categorically-excluded walk the first-son's-substance — repenting-and-doing — and-enter-the-kingdom-of-Elohim (God) before the chief-priests-and-elders walking the second-son's-substance. The apostolic-witness restates the substance throughout: *but be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only* (James 1:22); *faith without works is dead* (James 2:17); *for not the hearers of the law are just before Elohim (God), but the doers of the law shall be justified* (Romans 2:13); *and hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar* (1 John 2:3-4).

(6) The framework-major moment of the chapter lands at the parable-of-the-wicked-husbandmen at vv.33-46. The vineyard-substance is Isaiah 5:1-7's vineyard-of-Yahuah explicit — *for the vineyard of Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts) is the house of Yashar'el (Israel), and the men of Yahudah (Judah) his pleasant plant* — with every element-substance the prophet's-song laid (planting, hedging, winepress, tower, looking-for-fruit) preserved. The husbandmen-substance is the leadership-substance, NOT the people-of-Yashar'el (Israel) categorically. The servants-substance is the prophets-substance the Tanakh names (*I have even sent unto you all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them: Yet they hearkened not unto me*, Jeremiah 7:25); the killing-and-stoning is the Tanakh-pattern of prophet-killing — Zecharyah-ben-Yehoyada (Zechariah son of Jehoiada) stoned in-the-court-of-the-Temple at 2 Chronicles 24:20-22 (the same Zecharyah the King will-name at the seven-woes at Matthew 23:35), Uriyahu (Urijah) at Jeremiah 26:20-23, Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) cast-into-the-pit at Jeremiah 38:6, the substance Nechemyah (Nehemiah) had named at Nehemiah 9:26. The *last of all he sent unto them his son* substance is the substance the apostle Paul names at Hebrews 1:1-2 (*Elohim (God), who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son*); the husbandmen recognize the heir-substance and-still-walk the substance-of-killing the substance to seize the inheritance; the *cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him* pre-figures the apostle Paul's *Yahusha (Jesus) also... suffered without the gate* at Hebrews 13:12.

The King cites Psalm 118:22-23 — *the stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is Yahuah's (the LORD's) doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes* — the same Hallel-Psalm the multitudes and the children had cried-from. The stone-substance runs through the prophets — Ya'aqov (Jacob)'s pillar at Genesis 28:18-22, the Even-ezer (Eben-ezer) at 1 Samuel 7:12, the *stone of stumbling and rock of offence to both the houses of Yashar'el (Israel)* at Isaiah 8:14, the *tried stone, a precious corner stone* at Isaiah 28:16, the stone-cut-without-hands at Daniel 2:34-35, 44-45, the stone-with-seven-eyes at Zechariah 3:9, the headstone with shoutings at Zechariah 4:7 — and is restated by the apostle Kefa (Peter) at Acts 4:11 (*this is the stone which was set at nought of you builders, which is become the head of the corner*) and 1 Peter 2:4-8, and by the apostle Paul at Romans 9:33.

Then the chapter-pivot at v.43 — *the kingdom of Elohim (God) shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof.* Three substance-distinctions are load-bearing. (a) The *taken from you* addresses the chief-priests-and-elders the King-stands-in-front-of — the leadership-substance — NOT categorical-Yashar'el (Israel). (b) The *nation* is the Greek *ethnos* — singular dative *ethnei,* not the plural *ethnesi* the categorical-three-categories-nations substance would have carried. The substance is one nation, bringing-forth fruit — the bringing-forth-fruit-remnant of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed of both houses. (c) The architecture lies across the canon: the King-himself laid the substance at Matthew 19:28 — *ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel)*; the apostle Paul before-Agrippa at Acts 26:6-7 — *the promise made of Elohim (God) unto our fathers: Unto which promise our twelve tribes, instantly serving Elohim (God) day and night, hope to come*; the apostle Ya'aqov (James) addressing his epistle *to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad* at James 1:1; the apostle Yochanan (John)'s-Revelation-vision naming the twelve-tribes-sealed at Revelation 7:4-8 and the twelve-tribes-on-the-twelve-gates of the new Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) at Revelation 21:12-14; the apostle Paul at Ephesians 2:19-20 — *built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) himself being the chief corner stone.* The apostle Kefa (Peter) lays the most-direct restatement of the King's-*nation* in 1 Peter 2:9-10 — *but ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation (*ethnos hagion,* singular), a peculiar people... Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of Elohim (God)* — addressed to the *strangers scattered* of 1 Peter 1:1, restating the prophet Hoshea (Hosea)'s *Lo-Ammi* / *Ammi* substance at Hosea 1:10 (*yet the number of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) shall be as the sand of the sea*). The substance is the gathered-scattered-seed of the twelve-tribes, walking the new-heart-substance of Ezekiel 36:26-27 and the new-covenant-substance of Jeremiah 31:31-34 — not the categorical-displacement of Yashar'el (Israel) by a substance-not-Yashar'el.

Three inherited-readings of v.43 fall to the substance the canon-itself walks. The Christian-replacement-theology that-the-categorical-church-replaces-Yashar'el (Israel) collapses the *ethnos* singular against the architectural-substance the apostles lay; the Hebrew-Roots-false-inclusion that-the-Torah-keeping-categorical-anyone replaces-the-current-leadership collapses the substantial-seed-of-promise the prophets had named-tribe-by-tribe; the Reformation-sola-fide-without-fruit that-the-bringing-forth-fruit is-incidental collapses the load-bearing-clause itself — the nation-substance is *named-by* the bringing-forth-fruit, and the fruit-substance is the Spirit-empowered-heart-Torah-walking the prophets and the apostles name throughout the canon. The closing *whosoever shall fall on this stone shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder* (v.44) reads the substance through Isaiah 8:14-15's stumbling-substance and Daniel 2:34-35's stone-grinding-substance at the substantial-Day-of-Yahuah (the LORD) substance the prophets Yo'el (Joel) and Mal'akhi (Malachi) had named at the close-of-the-Tanakh (Joel 2:11, Malachi 3:2, Malachi 4:1).

The chapter closes at the chief-priests-and-Pharisees perceiving the King-spoke-of-them and seeking-to-lay-hands-on-him, held-back only by the multitude that-took-him-for-a-prophet. The substance the system now-walks is the substance Matthew 26:3-5 will-walk-out at the Sanhedrin-substance gathering-to-the-palace-of-Kayafa (Caiaphas) to-plot the King's-death. The chapter arcs from the triumphal-entry through the Temple-cleansing through the fig-tree through the authority-challenge through the parable-of-the-two-sons through the framework-major husbandmen-parable, and-closes-into the substance the next chapter will-walk-out as the King's-substance walks-the-substance-of-the-cross knowingly-and-willingly toward the substance the Father had-determined from-before-the-foundation-of-the-world.
