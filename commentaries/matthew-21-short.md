# Short-form commentary on Matthew 21

Matthew 21 closes the long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) the previous chapter walked through Yericho (Jericho) and opens the final week before the cross. The chapter's substance carries six movements, and one of them is the framework-major moment for the kingdom-stewardship-substance the whole gospel has been pointing toward since the opening chapters. The triumphal-entry at vv.1-11 brings the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) on the foal-of-an-ass the prophet Zecharyah (Zechariah) had foretold at Zechariah 9:9, with the multitudes crying the Davidic-Messianic-confession-substance — *Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David* — out of Psalm 118:25-26 at the recognition-substance the marginalized-substance carried at the matt-20 Yericho (Jericho)-two-blind-men passage now-broadened-to-the-multitude. The cleansing-of-the-Temple at vv.12-17 reads the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah)'s house-of-prayer substance at Isaiah 56:7 against the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)'s den-of-thieves substance at Jeremiah 7:11, with the King's-substance overturning the moneychangers' tables in the Court-of-the-goyim (Gentiles) and healing-the-blind-and-the-lame whom the rabbinic-substance had categorically-excluded from the Temple-substance per 2 Samuel 5:8, and the children-in-the-Temple crying *Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David* out of Psalm 8:2's *out of the mouth of babes and sucklings* substance. The cursing-of-the-fig-tree at vv.18-22 walks the Tanakh-fig-tree-substance the prophets carried as the covenant-fruitfulness-substance — Hosea 9:10, Jeremiah 8:13, Joel 1:7, Micah 7:1 — as the substantial-judgment on the leadership-substance whose appearance-of-leaves carries no fruit-substance, and the faith-and-mountain-moving teaching that follows extends the mustard-seed-faith-substance the King had laid at Matthew 17:20. The chief-priests-and-elders' authority-challenge at vv.23-27 brings the formal-rabbinic credentialing-substance against the King's-substance, and the King's-counter-question about the baptism-of-Yochanan (John) catches the system-substance in its own substance-of-refusing-to-name-the-prophet-substance — the system that cannot name Yochanan as prophet cannot receive the King-the-prophet-pointed-to. The parable-of-the-two-sons at vv.28-32 lays the doing-the-Father's-will substance against the saying-yes-without-doing substance, with the publicans-and-harlots-go-into-the-kingdom-before-you substance landing the cause-and-effect-of-hearing at the marginalized-substance-against-the-system-substance level. At vv.33-46 the chapter lands its framework-major moment — the parable-of-the-wicked-husbandmen with the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah)'s vineyard-substance of Isaiah 5:1-7 walked-out at the leadership-level, the stone-which-the-builders-rejected substance from Psalm 118:22-23 quoted by the King-himself, and the chapter-pivot at v.43 — *the kingdom of Elohim (God) shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof* — the stewardship-of-the-vineyard transferred-from-the-current-leadership-stewards-of-Yahudah (Judah) to-the-bringing-forth-fruit-remnant of-the-gathering-of-the-scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel) per the apostolic-foundation-of-the-twelve-tribes-restoration architecture the prophets and the apostolic witness lay across the whole canon. The chapter closes at vv.45-46 with the chief-priests-and-Pharisees recognizing the King-spoke-of-them and being held-back from laying-hands-on-him only by the multitude's-substance.

*And when they drew nigh unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and were come to Beit-Pagi (Bethphage), unto the mount of Olives, then sent Yahusha (Jesus) two disciples, Saying unto them, Go into the village over against you, and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me. And if any man say ought unto you, ye shall say, The Lord hath need of them; and straightway he will send them.* (Matthew 21:1-3)

The geographic-substance of the entry lands at Beit-Pagi (Bethphage) — the *house of unripe figs,* a small Levitical-and-priestly village on the eastern-slope of the Mount of Olives just outside the city. The naming of the village is itself a substance the chapter will turn on: the fig-substance the village-name carries will be the substance the King-walks against the unfruitful-leadership-substance at vv.18-22 just hours later, and the location-substance sits on the same Mount of Olives the prophet Zecharyah (Zechariah) had named as the substance-of-the-Father's-feet at the consummation — *and his feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives, which is before Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) on the east, and the mount of Olives shall cleave in the midst thereof toward the east and toward the west, and there shall be a very great valley; and half of the mountain shall remove toward the north, and half of it toward the south* (Zechariah 14:4). The Mount of Olives is the substance the second-coming will-walk-out; the King's-substance pre-figures the substance now at the first-coming-substance, ascending-the-same-mount toward the city-and-the-cross. The Olivet-discourse the King will deliver at Matthew 24 is laid-on-this-same-mountain.

The substance of the ass-and-the-colt is the substance the prophet Zecharyah (Zechariah) had foretold. *Go into the village over against you, and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her.* The Matthean account preserves both-animals — the ass-and-the-colt — where the Markan account at Mark 11:7, the Lukan account at Luke 19:35, and the Yochanan (John) account at John 12:14 mention only the colt. The Matthean preservation of both is the substance of the Hebrew-parallelism of Zechariah 9:9 read at the Hebrew-parallelism level: *rejoice greatly, O daughter of Tsion (Zion); shout, O daughter of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem): behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass* (Zechariah 9:9). The Hebrew-parallelism names the same-mount in two-clauses — *an ass* in the first clause, *a colt the foal of an ass* in the second — and the Matthean account preserves the two-clause-substance the prophet had laid. The ass-substance is not the donkey-as-humble-of-modern-Christian-reading; the ass-substance is the royal-Tanakh-mount-substance the Tanakh-pattern carries — *speak, ye that ride on white asses, ye that sit in judgment, and walk by the way* (Judges 5:10), and the Davidic-substance reading of the mule-and-the-ass for the king's-household — *and Ziba (Tsiva) said unto the king, The asses be for the king's household to ride on, and the bread and summer fruit for the young men to eat; and the wine, that such as be faint in the wilderness may drink* (2 Samuel 16:2). The mule-the-Davidic-court used at Shlomoh (Solomon)'s anointing — *and king David said, Call me Tsadoq (Zadok) the priest, and Natan (Nathan) the prophet, and Benayahu (Benaiah) the son of Yehoyada (Jehoiada). And they came before the king. The king also said unto them, Take with you the servants of your lord, and cause Shlomoh (Solomon) my son to ride upon mine own mule, and bring him down to Gihon* (1 Kings 1:32-33) — names the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-into at the entry-substance. The colt-no-man-had-yet-sat-upon the Markan-and-Lukan accounts add — *go your way into the village over against you; in the which at your entering ye shall find a colt tied, whereon yet never man sat: loose him, and bring him hither* (Luke 19:30) — reads with the dedicated-mount-substance the Torah-pattern carries for sacred-substance, like the red-heifer-never-yoked of Numbers 19:2 and the heifer-not-wrought-with of Deuteronomy 21:3. The dedicated-mount-substance carries the King.

The *Lord hath need of them* substance lays the King's-prerogative over the substance of the village's-substance. The King-the-villagers-do-not-yet-see is the substance-the-villagers-yield-to at the King's-request without challenge. The substance is laid at the substance of the Father's-providence prepared-the-mount-substance the King-walks-into.

*All this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, Tell ye the daughter of Tsion (Zion), Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass. And the disciples went, and did as Yahusha (Jesus) had commanded them, And brought the ass, and the colt, and put on them their clothes, and they set him thereon.* (Matthew 21:4-7)

The Matthean signature-quotation formula — *that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet* — lands the entry-substance within the Tanakh-prophesied-substance the Matthean account has used since the opening chapters at Matthew 1:22-23, Matthew 2:15, Matthew 2:17-18, Matthew 2:23, Matthew 4:14-16, Matthew 8:17, Matthew 12:17-21, Matthew 13:35. Each prior use of the formula attaches a Tanakh-citation to a specific event-substance the King-walks; the present use attaches the entry to Zechariah 9:9 at the King-comes-to-his-city substance the prophet had foretold. The substance the prophet had named carries forward — the daughter of Tsion (Zion), the King-coming, the meekness-substance, the foal-of-an-ass — and the Matthean account preserves the four substance-elements while-abbreviating the prophet's-substance at one point: the Matthean citation omits the *just, and having salvation* clause Zechariah 9:9 carried. The omission is not careless; the omitted clause carries the King's-substance-of-vindication-and-deliverance the chapter-now is walking-into but not-yet-walking-out. The first-coming-substance carries the meekness-and-the-foal; the *just, and having salvation* substance is the substance the prophet had carried for the King's-larger-arc the cross-and-resurrection-substance will-unfold and-the-second-coming-substance will-walk-out-fully. The Matthean account at Luke 4:18-19 follows the same Tanakh-citation pattern — the King-cites Isaiah 61:1-2 in the wilderness-synagogue at Natsareth (Nazareth) and stops short at *the acceptable year of Yahuah (the LORD),* omitting the *day of vengeance of our Elohim (God)* clause the prophet's-verse continued into. The same-pattern operates here: the substance the prophet had named is named in full at the prophet's-page; the substance the Matthean account names at the King's-first-coming preserves what-the-first-coming-walks-into and-holds-back the substance-the-second-coming-will-walk-out.

*Meek, and sitting upon an ass.* The meekness-substance is the Greek *praus,* the Tanakh-Hebrew *ani* — the substance-of-substantial-non-domination the King-himself had laid at the head of the kingdom-citizens'-substance in the Sermon-on-the-Mount: *blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth* (Matthew 5:5). The same meekness-substance the King-claims for himself at the great rest-invitation: *come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light* (Matthew 11:28-30). The meekness-substance is the substance the King-walks against the natural-kingdoms'-domination-substance the King had just-named at Matthew 20:25 — *the princes of the goyim (Gentiles) exercise dominion over them.* The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) coming-to-his-city does not come in the substance of the natural-kingdoms'-show-of-force; the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) comes in the substance the prophet had named — meek, lowly, on the foal-of-an-ass — and the substance lays the King's-kingdom-substance against the substance the watcher-rebellion-administration the apostle Paul names at Ephesians 6:12 operates the natural-kingdoms-under: *for we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.*

*And a very great multitude spread their garments in the way; others cut down branches from the trees, and strawed them in the way. And the multitudes that went before, and that followed, cried, saying, Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD); Hoshia-na (Hosanna) in the highest. And when he was come into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), all the city was moved, saying, Who is this? And the multitude said, This is Yahusha (Jesus) the prophet of Natsareth (Nazareth) of Galil (Galilee).* (Matthew 21:8-11)

The garments-and-branches-spread-in-the-way is the substance the people walk for the king-coming-to-the-city. The Tanakh-pattern of garments-spread-for-the-king lands at Yehu (Jehu)'s anointing — *then they hasted, and took every man his garment, and put it under him on the top of the stairs, and blew with trumpets, saying, Yehu (Jehu) is king* (2 Kings 9:13). The garments-spread substance is the substance-of-royal-acclamation the people walked for the king. The branches-substance ties into the Sukkot (Tabernacles) pattern of branches-waved-before-Yahuah (the LORD) per the Torah-statute at Leviticus 23:40 — *and ye shall take you on the first day the boughs of goodly trees, branches of palm trees, and the boughs of thick trees, and willows of the brook; and ye shall rejoice before Yahuah Eloheichem (the LORD your God) seven days.* The substance the multitudes walk is the substance-of-Tanakh-royal-acclamation overlaid-with-the-substance-of-the-Sukkot (Tabernacles)-and-Hallel-substance the Tehillim-118 the multitudes will-cry-from is at the heart of.

*Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David.* The cry is the substance-of-*hoshi'a-na* the Tanakh-Hallel-substance of Psalm 118 carries — the *save now* substance of the Psalm: *save now, I beseech thee, O Yahuah (LORD): O Yahuah (LORD), I beseech thee, send now prosperity. Blessed be he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD): we have blessed you out of the house of Yahuah (the LORD)* (Psalm 118:25-26). The Psalm-118-substance is not a generic-praise-shout; it is the Tanakh-cry-of-pilgrimage-deliverance the pilgrim-substance cried at the approach-to-the-Temple. The multitudes' substance carries the *Son of David* address-substance the marginalized-substance has been crying since the matt-9 two-blind-men passage at Matthew 9:27-31, the Canaanite-woman at Matthew 15:22, the matt-20 Yericho (Jericho)-two-blind-men at Matthew 20:30-31, and now-publicly the multitude-substance walks the Davidic-Messianic-confession-substance the system has refused-to-name. The substance the marginalized-substance had-recognized at the substance the system-could-not-see is now-being-cried-by-the-multitude at the entry-substance.

*Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD).* The Psalm-118:26 substance the multitudes cry is the same-Psalm the children-in-the-Temple will cry from at v.16 (*out of the mouth of babes and sucklings*) and the same-Psalm the King-himself will cite at vv.42-43 of the wicked-husbandmen-parable (*the stone which the builders rejected*). Psalm 118 is the substantial-Tanakh-anchor running through the whole chapter — the same Hallel-Psalm the pilgrim-multitudes carried into the city at every Pesach (Passover), now-being-walked-out at the substance-of-the-substance-the-King-walks-into. *Hoshia-na (Hosanna) in the highest* lands the cry at the heights-of-heaven the angelic-substance Psalms 148 and Psalm 150 had-named at the close of the Hallel-substance.

*All the city was moved.* The Greek for *moved* is *eseisthē* — the same root that names the earthquake-substance at the crucifixion (*and, behold, the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom; and the earth did quake, and the rocks rent,* Matthew 27:51) and the resurrection-substance (*and, behold, there was a great earthquake: for the angel of Yahuah (the LORD) descended from heaven,* Matthew 28:2). The city's-substance is shaken at the King's-substance arriving, and the city's-substance will-be-shaken again at the substance-of-the-cross and again at the substance-of-the-resurrection. The same Greek root carries the substance through.

*Who is this?* The city's-question reveals the substance the city has-not-recognized. The multitude's-answer — *This is Yahusha (Jesus) the prophet of Natsareth (Nazareth) of Galil (Galilee)* — preserves the substance the prophet-from-Galil (Galilee) substance carries from the Galilean-light-substance the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) had foretold at Isaiah 9:1-2: *nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation, when at the first he lightly afflicted the land of Zevulun (Zebulun) and the land of Naphtali (Naphtali), and afterward did more grievously afflict her by the way of the sea, beyond the Yarden (Jordan), in Galil (Galilee) of the goyim (nations). The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined.* The Matthean account had already laid the same prophet-substance at Matthew 4:13-16. The multitude-substance answers truly-but-incompletely — the King-is-the-prophet, but the King is-also the Son-of-David the multitudes have-just-been-crying-to, and the King is-also the Son-of-Adam who-is-about-to-walk-the-cross. The city has-not-yet-recognized the substance the marginalized-substance had-recognized and the multitudes are-now-crying. The cause-and-effect-of-hearing the King's-own-mouth-statement at John 10:26-27 will-name (*my sheep hear my voice... ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep*) operates at the substance the city-as-a-whole has-not-walked-into.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went into the temple of Elohim (God), and cast out all them that sold and bought in the temple, and overthrew the tables of the moneychangers, and the seats of them that sold doves, And said unto them, It is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer; but ye have made it a den of thieves.* (Matthew 21:12-13)

The Temple-cleansing follows the entry-substance directly. The King-having-entered-the-city walks-into the Temple-substance and-performs the substantial-prophetic-action the Tanakh-prophet-pattern had-walked at-prior-moments-of-Temple-corruption. The action is laid at the Court-of-the-goyim (Gentiles) — the outermost court of the Temple-precinct, the one space the goyim (Gentiles) and the ritually-impure could-enter for the prayer-substance the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) had-named the Temple-was-for. The moneychangers were exchanging the secular-Roman-and-Greek currencies for the Tyrian-shekel-substance the priestly-authority required for the Temple-tax the King-himself had-honored at Matthew 17:24-27, and the dove-sellers were providing the poor-person's-offering the Torah-statute named for the woman-after-childbirth (*and if she be not able to bring a lamb, then she shall bring two turtles, or two young pigeons; the one for the burnt offering, and the other for a sin offering: and the priest shall make an atonement for her, and she shall be clean,* Leviticus 12:8) and for the leper's-cleansing (Leviticus 14:22). The substance the King-walks-against is not the existence-of-the-currency-exchange or the existence-of-the-dove-selling per se; the substance the King-walks-against is the substance of the currency-exchange and the dove-selling operating-within the Court-of-the-goyim (Gentiles) and-displacing the substance of prayer the Court-of-the-goyim (Gentiles) was-meant-for. The Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership-substance had-monetized the Temple-substance and-displaced the substance of prayer with the substance of the market.

*My house shall be called the house of prayer.* The King-cites the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 56:7. The Tanakh-context at Isaiah 56 is the substance-of-the-sons-of-the-stranger the substance-of-the-Father gathers-to-his-mountain: *also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves to Yahuah (the LORD), to serve him, and to love the name of Yahuah (the LORD), to be his servants, every one that keepeth the Shabbat (Sabbath) from polluting it, and taketh hold of my covenant; Even them will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer: their burnt offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon mine altar; for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people. Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) which gathereth the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel) saith, Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him* (Isaiah 56:6-8). The substance the prophet had named is the substance-of-the-Father-gathering — *the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel)* and the sons-of-the-stranger-substance the prophet had named as the substance of the same gathering. The Markan parallel at Mark 11:17 preserves the *for all people* (*pasin tois ethnesin*) substance the Matthean abbreviates; the *all people* substance is the substance-of-the-gathered-citizens-of-the-kingdom from-every-territory of the scattering — the substance the King-himself will name at John 10:16 (*and other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd*), the substance the prophet Hosea had named at Hosea 1:9-10 of *Lo-Ammi (not my people)* becoming *the sons of the living Elohim (God),* and the substance the Tanakh-prophets had laid throughout the prophetic-corpus at Isaiah 11:11-12, Jeremiah 31, Ezekiel 37, and Zechariah 10 of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed-of-the-twelve-tribes. The Isaiah-56-stranger-substance is the substance of the scattered-seed-coming-home, not the substance of a categorical-inclusion of the unrelated-nations into the covenant-body the prophets had named.

*But ye have made it a den of thieves.* The King-cites the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 7:11. The Jeremiah-7 context is the substance of one of the Tanakh's most-substantial Temple-judgment-passages — the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) standing-in-the-gate of the First-Temple and naming the substance the leadership-substance walked: *thus saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts), the Elohim (God) of Yashar'el (Israel), Amend your ways and your doings, and I will cause you to dwell in this place. Trust ye not in lying words, saying, The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), are these. For if ye throughly amend your ways and your doings; if ye throughly execute judgment between a man and his neighbour; If ye oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, and shed not innocent blood in this place, neither walk after other elohim (gods) to your hurt: Then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers, for ever and ever. Behold, ye trust in lying words, that cannot profit. Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Ba'al (Baal), and walk after other elohim (gods) whom ye know not; And come and stand before me in this house, which is called by my name, and say, We are delivered to do all these abominations? Is this house, which is called by my name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? Behold, even I have seen it, saith Yahuah (the LORD)* (Jeremiah 7:3-11). The Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)-7 substance is laid at the substance of the leadership-substance trusting-in-the-Temple-substance while-walking-against the substance of the covenant the Temple was-for. The First-Temple-substance was destroyed by the Bavli (Babylonian) hand within-a-generation of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)'s prophecy; the King-citing the same-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah)-substance at the Second-Temple-cleansing names the same-substance against the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership and-prefigures the destruction-of-the-Second-Temple the King will-name explicitly at the Olivet-discourse at Matthew 24:1-2. The *den of thieves* substance is not merely the substance of the monetization; it is the substance of the leadership-substance using-the-Temple-as-cover-for-the-substance-of-its-walking-against the Father's-substance the Temple was-meant-to-house.

*And the blind and the lame came to him in the temple; and he healed them.* (Matthew 21:14)

The healing-substance in the Temple is itself a substantial-prophetic-action against the rabbinic-substance the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership had-walked. The rabbinic-substance had-read 2 Samuel 5:8 as a categorical-exclusion of the blind-and-the-lame from the Temple-substance: *and David said on that day, Whosoever getteth up to the gutter, and smiteth the Yevusi (Jebusites), and the lame and the blind, that are hated of David's soul, he shall be chief and captain. Wherefore they said, The blind and the lame shall not come into the house.* The rabbinic-substance had-categorically-extended the proverbial-saying into an actual-exclusion of the blind-and-the-lame from the Temple-courts. The King-substance reverses-the-substance categorically — the blind-and-the-lame the system had-excluded from the Temple come to-the-King in-the-Temple, and the King-heals-them in-the-very-court the system had-excluded-them-from. The substance the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) had-named as the substance-of-the-Messianic-credential is walked-out at the substance-of-the-very-court the system had categorically-excluded the substance-of-the-credential-substance-the-prophet-named: *then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert* (Isaiah 35:5-6). The healing-substance at the Temple-substance lays the same substance the King had laid in the wilderness-credentialing-substance at Matthew 11:4-5 (*go and shew Yochanan (John) again those things which ye do hear and see: the blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them*) into the Temple-substance directly. The substance of the Messianic-credential walks-the-substance the system had categorically-excluded into-the-Temple-substance.

*And when the chief priests and scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children crying in the temple, and saying, Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David; they were sore displeased, And said unto him, Hearest thou what these say? And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Yea; have ye never read, Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise? And he left them, and went out of the city into Beit-Anyah (Bethany); and he lodged there.* (Matthew 21:15-17)

The chief-priests-and-scribes' *sore displeased* is the Greek *ēganaktēsan* — the substance of substantial indignation, the same root the King used at Matthew 20:24 to name the ten-apostles' substance at the mother-of-Zavdai (Zebedee)'s petition. The system-substance is indignant-at-the-substance the children are-walking — the same Davidic-Messianic-confession the multitudes have-just-walked at the entry, now coming-out-of-the-children's-mouths in-the-very-court of the Temple. *Hearest thou what these say?* is the system-substance asking-the-King to silence-the-children. The substance the system-substance asks-for is the substance the King-will-not-give; the King-receives-the-children's-substance as truthful-substance.

*Have ye never read, Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise?* The King-cites Psalm 8:2 — and the Psalm-8 substance carries an additional weight the King's-citation invokes at the same time. The Psalm opens at the Adamic-substance-and-the-Son-of-Adam-substance: *O Yahuah (LORD) our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth! who hast set thy glory above the heavens.
