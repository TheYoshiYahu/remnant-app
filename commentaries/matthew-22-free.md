The Reformation-substance had truncated the gospel at *welcomed in by faith,* *saved by grace through faith,* *not under law but under grace* — phrases the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) categorically rejected as standalone-substance at Romans 6:1-2 (*shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? (2) The tribute-to-Caesar question at vv.15-22 brings the unlikely-alliance of Pharisees-and-Herodians — the Pharisaic-substance walking-against-the-Roman-administration united-with-the-Herodian-substance supporting-the-Herodian-Roman-collaboration — both against the King at a forced-binary trap. *Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?* If yes, the Zealot-substance of Yehudah (Judas) the Galilean the historian Yosef ben Mattityahu (Josephus) had named in *Antiquities*-18 walks against the King as collaborator; if no, the Roman-administration walks against the King as seditious. The Roman-denarius the speakers produce bears Tiberius Caesar's image with the inscription *TI CAESAR DIVI AVG F AVGVSTVS* (*Tiberius Caesar, son of the divine Augustus, Augustus*) on the obverse and Livia as supreme-high-priest (*Pontifex Maximus*) on the reverse — the substance of Caesar's-divinity-claim the Torah's-second-commandment at Exodus 20:4-5 had categorically walked against. The coin in the speakers' purse in the Temple-court itself is its own admission. *Whose is this image?* The Greek *eikōn* is the substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) will use of the Son-substance at Colossians 1:15 (*the image of the invisible Elohim [God]*) and of the citizen at Romans 8:29, the same Septuagint-Greek the Genesis 1:26-27 creation-substance carries (*let us make man in our image*). *Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto Elohim (God) the things that are Elohim's (God's).* The substance is not the modern-Constantinian-collapse into a substantial-separation-of-church-and-state with two-co-equal-spheres; the natural-administration is what the Father permits-for-an-age, the substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) at Ephesians 6:12 names as operating under the principalities-and-powers-of-the-darkness — not a co-equal-substance to the Father's. The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) restates at Romans 13:1-7 (*let every soul be subject unto the higher powers ... tribute to whom tribute is due*), the apostle Kefa (Peter) at 1 Peter 2:13-17 (*submit yourselves to every ordinance of man for the Lord's sake*). And the categorical-precedence the apostles Kefa (Peter) and the eleven walked out at Acts 5:29 (*we ought to obey Elohim [God] rather than men*): the Tanakh-pattern is Shadrakh (Shadrach), Meishakh (Meshach), and Aved-Nego (Abed-nego) refusing the image of Nevukhadnetstar (Nebuchadnezzar) at Daniel 3 (*we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image*); Daniel-substance praying against the decree of Daryavesh (Darius) at Daniel 6; the prophet Eliyahu (Elijah) standing against the Ba'al (Baal)-substance of Ach'av (Ahab) and Yezevel (Jezebel) at 1 Kings 18; and the apostle Yochanan (John)'s apocalyptic-vision at Revelation 13:1-18 — the beast and the mark substance the citizen-of-the-kingdom cannot receive even into suffering (*here is the patience and the faith of the saints,* Revelation 13:10). The Pharisees-and-Herodians marvelled and departed — the trap collapsed on itself.

(3) The Sadducees-on-resurrection question at vv.23-33 brings the priestly-aristocracy-substance — the *Tsadoqim,* descended from Tsadoq (Zadok) the priest of 2 Samuel 8:17 and 1 Kings 1:8, 32-39, the same line the prophet Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) had named at the restored-Temple-vision at Ezekiel 44:15-16 — that walks the Torah-only substance with the prophets-and-the-writings walked-against and the resurrection-and-the-angels-and-the-spirit categorically denied, as the apostolic-historian Luke names at Acts 23:8 (*for the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection, neither angel, nor spirit*). (5) At vv.41-46 the chapter lands its framework-major moment.
