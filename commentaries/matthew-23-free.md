# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 23

Matthew 23 is the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel)'s final-public-discourse against the scribes-and-Pharisees — the longest direct-condemnation passage in the Matthean-gospel — walked-out in three movements that close the public-engagement the prior chapter ended on the system-substance's silence. (1) The introduction at vv.1-12 addresses the multitudes-and-the-disciples about the leadership-substance seated *in Mosheh's (Moses's) seat,* distinguishing the Torah-substance the scribes-and-Pharisees read-from-the-seat from the substance the leadership walks-themselves — *they say, and do not.* The substance is carefully-distinguished: the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is not endorsing the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance the King had-already-walked-against at Matthew 15:1-9; the substance the leadership reads-from-Mosheh's-seat is the Torah-substance the Father gave-at-Sinai through the hand of Mosheh (Moses), and that substance is to-be-walked though the leadership's own works are not-the-model. The heavy-burdens-substance (*they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne*) walks against the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel)'s *my yoke is easy, and my burden is light* at Matthew 11:28-30 — the apostles' decision at Acts 15:10 (*a yoke ... which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear*) names the same Pharisaic-oral-tradition the King walks-against, NOT the Torah-substance the apostles after-the-Council still-walked themselves (the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) at Acts 21:20-26 walks-into-the-Temple to-pay-for-four-men's-offerings). The for-to-be-seen-of-men display — *make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments* — names not the Tefillin-substance the Torah commanded at Deuteronomy 6:8 and 11:18 and Exodus 13:9, 16, nor the Tsitsit-substance the Torah commanded at Numbers 15:38-40 and Deuteronomy 22:12 (the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) himself walked-the-Tsitsit — the *hem of his garment* the woman with the issue of blood touched at Matthew 9:20 and the multitudes touched at Matthew 14:36), but the *making-broad* and *enlarging* of them for display. The status-substance — *the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues, And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi* — is the natural-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-already-walked-against at Matthew 18:1-5 and Matthew 20:25-28. *Be not ye called Rabbi ... call no man your father upon the earth ... neither be ye called masters* walks not the categorical-prohibition of teaching-relationship (the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) names himself *a teacher of the Gentiles* at 1 Timothy 2:7; the disciples address the King as *Rabbi* throughout the Yochanan (John)-account without correction) nor the categorical-prohibition of the natural-father (the King honors-his-mother at the cross at John 19:26-27; the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) names himself a spiritual-father at 1 Corinthians 4:15), but the substance-of-religious-status — the same substance the modern-Roman-Catholic-priestly-paternity walks and the modern-Christian-pastoral-substance walks at the substance-of-the-pastor-as-religious-father over the people-substance the pastor-shepherds. The kingdom-inversion closes the introduction: *he that is greatest among you shall be your servant. And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.* The substance the prophet-Mishlei (Proverbs) walks at Proverbs 16:18 and Proverbs 29:23, the apostle-Ya'aqov (James) at James 4:6, 10, the apostle-Kefa (Peter) at 1 Peter 5:5-6, and the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) himself at the foot-washing of John 13:13-14.

(2) The seven woes at vv.13-36 (with the textual-variant additional woe at v.14 in some manuscripts) walk one continuous direct-condemnation across seven discrete moves. The Greek *ouai* is the Tanakh-prophetic-word the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) walked at Isaiah 5:8-23, the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 22:13 and 23:1, and the prophet-Habakkuk at Habakkuk 2:6-19 against the leadership-of-the-First-Temple; the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks the same Tanakh-prophetic-substance against the leadership-of-the-Second-Temple. The *hypocrites* (Greek *hypokritai*) names the gap between the substance the leadership proclaims and the substance the leadership walks. The first woe at v.13 (*ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men*) — the leadership-substance holds-the-key the leadership-substance does-not-use, the Lukan-parallel at Luke 11:52 (*ye have taken away the key of knowledge*) walks the same substance. The textual-variant at v.14 (*ye devour widows' houses, and for a pretence make long prayer*) — the Tanakh-substance has walked the widow-substance from Exodus 22:22 through Deuteronomy 24:17 and 27:19 through the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 1:17, 23 and 10:2 through the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 7:6 and 22:3 through the prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at Ezekiel 22:7 through the prophet-Zekharyah (Zechariah) at Zechariah 7:10 through the prophet-Mal'akhi (Malachi) at Malachi 3:5. The second woe at v.15 (*ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of Gehinnom (Gehenna) than yourselves*) is the framework-major-direct-diagnostic of the false-inclusion-gospel-substance — the substance the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) walked-out-against in his letter-to-the-Galatians at Galatians 1:6-9 (*another gospel*), Galatians 5:1-12 (*if ye be circumcised, Messiah (Christ) shall profit you nothing*), and Galatians 6:12-13 (*they constrain you to be circumcised; only lest they should suffer persecution*), the substance the apostles' Council at Acts 15:1, 5, 10 named-and-rejected. The same diagnostic operates in five costumes: the Pharisaic-Sadducean-rabbinic-system through ADDITION (adding oral-tradition to the Torah-substance the Father gave); the Galatian-agitators through INVERSION (inverting the apostolic-gospel into a flesh-credential-conversion-system); modern-institutional-Christianity through SUBTRACTION (subtracting the Torah-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out-as-light, treating the covenant-people-of-Yashar'el (Israel) as-replaced-by-the-church-substance); modern-Hebrew-Roots-false-inclusion through COLLAPSE (*anyone who Torah-keeps is Yashar'el (Israel),* walking the Pharisaic-rabbinic-proselyte-pattern in Torah-substance-language); and Islam through TRANSFER (transferring the covenant to a different bloodline through the Shahada-confession). Five costumes. One substance. Same flesh-credential-gospel. Same voiding of the Torah-substance. Same denial of the Father's-prophetic-gathering-of-the-twelve-tribes-of-Yashar'el (Israel). Each fails the contrary-to-Torah-test the apostle-Yochanan (John) names at 1 John 2:3-4 (*he that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar*). The third woe at vv.16-22 (*ye blind guides ... swear by the temple or the gold of the temple*) walks the Pharisaic-oath-distinction-substance against the Torah's-direct-substance at Leviticus 19:12, Numbers 30:2, and Deuteronomy 23:21-23, the same substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-already-walked at Matthew 5:33-37 (*swear not at all*) and the apostle-Ya'aqov (James) walks at James 5:12. The fourth woe at vv.23-24 (*ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith*) walks the Tanakh-prophetic-summary-substance the prophet-Mikhah (Micah) had-named at Micah 6:8, the prophet-Hosheia (Hosea) at Hosea 6:6, the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 1:11-17, the prophet-Amos at Amos 5:21-24, and the prophet-Zekharyah (Zechariah) at Zechariah 7:8-10. The *these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone* substance preserves the both-and architecture the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks against the post-Reformation collapse of the Torah into the weightier-matters-alone — the heart-Torah-substance the prophet-Mosheh (Moses) had-named at Deuteronomy 30:6, the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 31:31-34, and the prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at Ezekiel 36:26-27 walks the WHOLE-Torah-substance, not the weightier-matters-alone. *Strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel* names both unclean-animals per Leviticus 11:4-7 and 11:20-23. The fifth woe at vv.25-26 (*ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess*) walks the heart-defilement-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-already-walked at Matthew 15:10-20, the prophet-Shemu'el (Samuel) at 1 Samuel 16:7 (*Yahuah (the LORD) looketh on the heart*), and the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) at Romans 2:28-29 (*circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter*). The sixth woe at vv.27-28 (*ye are like unto whited sepulchres ... within full of dead men's bones*) walks the Torah's-corpse-defilement-substance at Numbers 19:11-22 against the Pharisaic-rabbinic-practice of whitewashing-tombs-before-the-feast — the leadership-substance is itself the corpse-defilement the whitewash was warning against. The prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at Ezekiel 13:10-12 (*daubed it with untempered morter*) walks the same covering-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) names. The seventh woe at vv.29-36 is the framework-major substance the chapter has-been-walking-toward — the leadership builds-the-tombs-of-the-prophets the same leadership's-fathers killed, and the leadership's own-words name the leadership's-paternity (*the days of our fathers*). The *fill ye up then the measure of your fathers* substance walks the Tanakh-substance the Father had-walked at Genesis 15:16 (*the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full*) and Deuteronomy 32:34-35, the same substance the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) will walk at 1 Thessalonians 2:14-16 — the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) is-himself a Yahudi (Jew), and the substance names the leadership-substance, not the Yahudim (Jews)-as-a-people. The *ye serpents, ye generation of vipers* (Greek *opheis, gennēmata echidnōn*) walks the seed-of-the-serpent-substance from Genesis 3:15 — the substance of two-seeds walked-out across the Tanakh through Genesis 4 (Hevel (Abel) and Qayin (Cain)), Genesis 6:1-4 (the watcher-rebellion the prophet-Henok (Enoch) names at 1 Enoch 6-7), and into the wheat-and-tares architecture the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-already-walked at Matthew 13:24-30 and 13:36-43. The same substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks at John 8:44 (*ye are of your father the devil*), the prophet-Yochanan (John)-the-Witness walks at Matthew 3:7, and the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-walked at Matthew 12:34. The substance is the seed-war-substance walked-into-the-leadership-substance — NOT the Yahudim (Jews)-as-a-people categorically (the King is himself a Yahudi (Jew); the apostles are Yahudim (Jews); the multitudes at v.1 are Yahudim (Jews); the lament at vv.37-39 walks toward gathering Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)'s children). The seed-war walks within every people the same way it walks within Yashar'el (Israel). The *from the blood of righteous Hevel (Abel) unto the blood of Zecharyah (Zechariah) son of Berekhyahu (Barachias), whom ye slew between the temple and the altar* names the WHOLE-Tanakh-prophet-killing-pattern — the first-righteous-blood at Genesis 4:8-10 (the substance the writer-of-Hebrews carries at Hebrews 11:4 and 12:24) and the last-prophet-killing of the Hebrew-canon-order at 2 Chronicles 24:20-22 (Zecharyah (Zechariah) ben Yehoyada (son of Jehoiada) stoned in the Temple court). The patronymic *son of Berekhyahu (Berechiah)* the writing-prophet-Zecharyah (Zechariah) walks at Zechariah 1:1 — the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks both substances at-once: the prophet-killing-pattern walks-across-the-WHOLE-Tanakh. *Behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: and some of them ye shall kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute them from city to city* walks-forward into the apostolic-substance — the apostle-Stephen at Acts 7:51-53 and the stoning at Acts 7:59, the apostle-Ya'aqov (James)-the-brother-of-the-King under the leadership-substance, and the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul)'s scourgings-in-the-synagogues at 2 Corinthians 11:24-25. *Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation* walks-toward the 70 CE Roman destruction of the Second-Temple the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) makes-explicit at Matthew 24:1-2 — substantial-judgment-of-the-leadership-substance, NOT the substantial-completion of the Day-of-Yahuah-substance the prophet-Yo'el (Joel) at Joel 2-3, the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 13, the prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at the Gog-and-Magog-substance of Ezekiel 38-39, and the prophet-Zekharyah (Zechariah) at Zechariah 14 walked. The preterist-collapse that-reads the Olivet-discourse-substance as completed-at-70 CE fails-the-substance.

(3) The chapter lands its second framework-major moment at the lament of vv.37-39: *O Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), thou that killest the prophets, and stonest them which are sent unto thee, how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not! Behold, your house is left unto you desolate. For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD).* The doubled-address *Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)* walks the Tanakh-tender-pattern of *Avraham (Abraham), Avraham (Abraham)* at Genesis 22:11, *Ya'aqov (Jacob), Ya'aqov (Jacob)* at Genesis 46:2, *Mosheh (Moses), Mosheh (Moses)* at Exodus 3:4, *Shemu'el (Samuel), Shemu'el (Samuel)* at 1 Samuel 3:10, *Marta (Martha), Marta (Martha)* at Luke 10:41, *Shimon (Simon), Shimon (Simon)* at Luke 22:31, and *Sha'ul (Paul), Sha'ul (Paul)* at Acts 9:4. The Father walks the judgment-substance at Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) in the substance of the tender-doubled-name — the city the Father loves, the city the Father set-the-Name-upon at Deuteronomy 12:5, the city the Shekinah-substance dwelt-in at 1 Kings 8:10-11 and departed-from at Ezekiel 10:18-19 and 11:22-23. *How often* names the Father's-repeated-extension across the Tanakh — the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 7:25-26 and 25:4 and 35:15 (*all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them*), the prophet-Nechemyah (Nehemiah) at Nehemiah 9:26 (*they slew thy prophets which testified against them*), the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 65:2 (*I have spread out my hands all the day unto a rebellious people*). The mother-bird-gathering-substance — *even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings* — walks the Tanakh-substance the prophet-Mosheh (Moses) walked at Deuteronomy 32:11-12 (*as an eagle stirreth up her nest, fluttereth over her young, spreadeth abroad her wings*), the Tehillim (Psalms) across Psalm 17:8, 36:7, 57:1, 61:4, 63:7, and 91:4 (*hide me under the shadow of thy wings* and parallels), Ruth 2:12 (*under whose wings thou art come to trust*), and the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 31:5 (*as birds flying, so will Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts) defend Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)*). The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) speaks-the-Father's-voice at the lament — the substance the Tanakh-substance had-been-walking-from. The *ye would not* names the leadership-substance's categorical-refusal walked-against the Father's-repeated-extension. The substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks at John 10:26-27 (*ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep ... my sheep hear my voice*) walks the same cause-and-effect-of-hearing the framework's-Adam-and-the-tares-architecture identifies — the leadership-substance is the seed-of-the-serpent-substance walked-into-the-leadership-substance, the wheat-and-tares the King walked at Matthew 13. The substantial-judgment-of-the-leadership-substance is the substance that-clears-the-way for the gathering-substance — the substance the prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at Ezekiel 34:1-31 walks (the false-shepherds are judged AND the Father-himself comes-to-shepherd-the-sheep). The *house left desolate* walks the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 12:7 and 22:5 and the departure-of-the-Shekinah substance at Ezekiel 10:18-19 and 11:22-23 — the Second-Temple the prophet-Chaggai (Haggai) had-named-as-comparatively-empty at Haggai 2:3 is now-the-leadership's-house, not the Father's-house. *Your house* — not the Father's-house any longer. The King walks-out-of-the-Temple at the close of this chapter; the next chapter opens with the disciples showing the King the buildings of the Temple at Matthew 24:1-2 — the substantial-judgment walks-toward the Olivet-discourse-substance. The *Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD)* citation from Psalm 118:26 is the substance the multitudes-and-the-children shouted at the triumphal-entry of Matthew 21:9 and 21:15 — the substance the leadership-substance refused-to-walk at the first-coming the leadership-substance will-have-to-walk at the substantial-second-coming-substance. The substance walks-toward the surviving-third-substance the prophet-Zekharyah (Zechariah) at Zechariah 13:8-9 walks (*they shall call on my name, and I will hear them: I will say, It is my people; and they shall say, Yahuah (the LORD) is my Elohim (my God)*) and the *they shall look upon me whom they have pierced* substance of Zechariah 12:10. The substance walks-with the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) at Romans 11:25-27 — the substantial-gathering-of-all-Yashar'el (Israel) at the end; the fulness-of-the-goyim-substance is the scattered-seed-of-Yashar'el (Israel)-among-the-nations being-gathered-home, NOT the categorical-nations being-grafted-in as new branches per the framework's olive-tree-architecture. The lament is not the Father's-categorical-rejection-of-Yerushalayim (Jerusalem); the lament is the Father's-tender-heart walking-the-gathering-substance EVEN-at-the-moment-of-the-substantial-judgment of the leadership-substance the chapter has-walked-out. The two substances are not in tension — the substantial-judgment of the false-shepherds is the substance that-clears-the-way for the True-Shepherd's-gathering. The chapter walks-toward the Olivet-discourse-substance of Matthew 24-25 the next chapter opens-with. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel)'s public-engagement with the leadership-of-the-Second-Temple ends here. The substance from-this-point-forward walks-toward the Mount-of-Olives and the cross.
