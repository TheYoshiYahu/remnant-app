## The introduction — *the scribes and the Pharisees sit in Mosheh's (Moses's) seat*

*Then spake Yahusha (Jesus) to the multitude, and to his disciples, Saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Mosheh's (Moses's) seat: All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.* (Matthew 23:1-3)

The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) opens by addressing the multitude-and-the-disciples — not by addressing the scribes-and-Pharisees themselves first, though that address will come at v.13 and continue through the seven-woes. The opening address-frame matters: the people-substance is the audience the King-walks-toward, not the leadership-substance the King-walks-against. The seven-woes that follow are spoken-aloud-in-the-hearing of the multitudes-and-the-disciples and of the scribes-and-Pharisees together; the leadership-substance is being-named in the hearing of the people-substance the leadership-victimizes. The pattern is the Tanakh-prophet-pattern — the prophet stands in the gate of the Temple and the prophet names the leadership-corruption-substance in the hearing of the people the leadership-shepherds. The substance the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) walked at Jeremiah 7:1-15 — *stand in the gate of Yahuah's (the LORD's) house, and proclaim there this word* — is the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks here. The Tanakh-prophetic-substance is the substance the King-walks-into; the chapter is not a-new-thing the King-walks but the substance of the Father's-prophetic-substance the prophets had-already-carried.

*Mosheh's (Moses's) seat* — the Greek *Mōuseōs kathedras*, the Hebrew *moshav Mosheh* — is the rabbinic-teaching-substance: the seat from which the Torah-substance was read-aloud and expounded in the synagogue-substance. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) acknowledges the legitimate-substance the scribes-and-Pharisees walk-at-the-seat — the public-reading-and-exposition of the Torah-substance is a legitimate-substance the leadership-substance was-entrusted-with — and immediately distinguishes that legitimate-substance from the substance the scribes-and-Pharisees walk in their own works. *All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.* The substance the leadership-substance bids-from-the-seat is the Torah-substance — the same Torah-substance the Father gave-at-Sinai through the hand of Mosheh (Moses); the substance the leadership-substance walks-themselves is the substance of public-display and private-non-walking. The Torah-substance is to-be-walked; the scribes-and-Pharisees' walking-of-it is not-the-model.

The substance is carefully-distinguished. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is not offering a blanket-endorsement of the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance — at Matthew 15:1-9 the King-walked the substance of *making the commandment of Elohim (God) of none effect by your tradition,* citing the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah)'s *this people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me. But in vain they do worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.* The Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance the King-walks-against is the substance-of-additions-to-the-Torah, the substance the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) names at Galatians 1:14 as *the traditions of my fathers* the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) himself walked-in before his Damascus-substance, the substance the apostles will-name at Acts 15:10 as *a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear.* The Torah-substance the leadership-substance reads-from-the-seat is not the same substance as the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance the leadership-substance has-built-around-the-Torah-substance; the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel)'s instruction here is to walk the Torah-substance the leadership-reads-from-the-seat, not the works of the leadership themselves. The doing-of-the-Father's-will-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out at Matthew 7:21-23 is the substance the chapter is-pointing-toward: *not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.*

*For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men's shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers.* (Matthew 23:4)

The heavy-burdens-substance walks against the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-already-walked at Matthew 11:28-30: *come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.* The contrast is direct — the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance is heavy; the Father's-Torah-substance is light. The apostle-Yochanan (John) will-walk the same substance at 1 John 5:3: *for this is the love of Elohim (God), that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.* The substance the apostle-Yochanan (John) names is the Father's-Torah-substance, not the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance the leadership-substance binds on others. The apostles' decision at the Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)-Council preserves the same distinction — *now therefore why tempt ye Elohim (God), to put a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?* (Acts 15:10). The apostle-Kefa (Peter)'s *yoke our fathers were not able to bear* is not the Torah-substance — the apostles after-the-Council still-walked-the-Torah-substance themselves (the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) at Acts 21:20-26 walks-into-the-Temple to-pay-for-four-men's-offerings to demonstrate he walks-the-Torah-substance) — the *yoke* the apostle-Kefa (Peter) names is the Pharisaic-oral-tradition-substance that-the-Galatian-agitators-substance was-attempting-to-impose-on-the-scattered-seed coming-home-through the apostolic-mission. The Torah-substance the Father gave is light; the Pharisaic-additions-to-the-Torah are heavy. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) names the substance directly.

*But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments, And love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues, And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.* (Matthew 23:5-7)

The *make broad their phylacteries* — the Greek *platynousin ta phylaktēria,* the Hebrew *yagdilu ha-tefillin* — names the Tefillin-substance the Torah-substance had-walked at Deuteronomy 6:8 (*thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be as frontlets between thine eyes*) and Deuteronomy 11:18 and Exodus 13:9, 16. The Tefillin-substance is the Torah's-own-substance — the binding of the Torah-words on the hand and between the eyes — and the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is not naming the Tefillin-substance as a problem; the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) names the *making-broad* of the Tefillin as the problem. The substance is not the Tefillin themselves but the making-them-larger-for-the-display-substance. Same with the *enlarge the borders of their garments* — the Greek *megalynousin ta kraspeda,* the Hebrew *yagdilu ha-tsitsit* — the Tsitsit-substance the Torah-substance had-walked at Numbers 15:38-40 (*speak unto the children of Yashar'el (Israel), and bid them that they make them fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and that they put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue: And it shall be unto you for a fringe, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the commandments of Yahuah (the LORD), and do them*) and Deuteronomy 22:12. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) himself walked-the-Tsitsit-substance — the *hem of his garment* the woman-with-the-issue-of-blood touched at Matthew 9:20 (*and, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment*) and the multitudes touched at Matthew 14:36 was the Tsitsit-substance the Torah-substance had-walked. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) does not walk against the Tefillin-substance or the Tsitsit-substance; the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks against the *making-broad* and the *enlarging* — the leadership-substance's turning of the Torah-substance into a substance-for-display.

The *uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues, And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi* is the substance-of-status the natural-substance walks-toward — the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) has-already-walked-against at Matthew 18:1-5 (*except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven*) and Matthew 20:25-28 (*whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister; And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant*) and at the *whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased* substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) had-walked at Matthew 18:4. The substance of religious-status walked-toward by the leadership-substance is the inversion of the kingdom-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks. The substance the prophet-Mishlei (Proverbs) had-named — *pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall* (Proverbs 16:18) — is the Tanakh-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-from.

*But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your Master, even Messiah (Christ); and all ye are brethren. And call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven. Neither be ye called masters: for one is your Master, even Messiah (Christ). But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant. And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.* (Matthew 23:8-12)

The substance walks carefully. The King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) does not categorically-prohibit the use of the Hebrew word *rabbi* as a teaching-relationship-marker — the apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) names himself *a teacher of the Gentiles* (1 Timothy 2:7) and the apostle-Yochanan (John)'s gospel records the disciples calling-the-King *Rabbi* at John 1:38 and John 1:49 and John 3:2 and John 4:31 and John 6:25 and John 9:2 and John 11:8 without correction. The substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-against at vv.8-12 is the substance-of-religious-status — the leadership-substance's love-of-being-called-Rabbi as the substance-of-elevated-status, not the substance of teaching-relationship. The same with *call no man your father upon the earth.* The Torah's-fifth-commandment substance at Exodus 20:12 (*honour thy father and thy mother*) carries the natural-father-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) does not overturn — the King-himself honors-his-mother Miryam (Mary) at the cross at John 19:26-27, charging the apostle-Yochanan (John) to take-her-into-his-own-home. The apostle-Sha'ul (Paul) names himself a spiritual-father at 1 Corinthians 4:15 (*for though ye have ten thousand instructors in Messiah (Christ), yet have ye not many fathers: for in Messiah (Christ) Yahusha (Jesus) I have begotten you through the gospel*) without contradicting the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel)'s instruction here. The substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-against is the substance-of-religious-paternity — the substance the leadership-substance walks at the substance-of-the-substantial-religious-status, the substance the modern-Roman-Catholic-priestly-paternity walks at the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out-against, the same substance the modern-Christian-pastoral-substance walks at the substance-of-the-pastor-as-religious-father over the people-substance the pastor-shepherds.

The kingdom-inversion-substance is the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) closes-with — *he that is greatest among you shall be your servant. And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.* The substance the prophet-Iyov (Job) had-named at Job 22:29 — *he shall save the humble person* — is the Tanakh-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-from. The same substance the prophet-Mishlei (Proverbs) walks at Proverbs 29:23 — *a man's pride shall bring him low: but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit* — and the apostle-Ya'aqov (James) walks at James 4:6, 10 — *Yahuah (the LORD) resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble... Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up* — and the apostle-Kefa (Peter) walks at 1 Peter 5:5-6 — *Yahuah (the LORD) resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble. Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of Elohim (God), that he may exalt you in due time* — and the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) himself walked at the foot-washing-substance of John 13:13-14 (*ye call me Master and Lord: and ye say well; for so I am. If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet*). The kingdom-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks is the inversion of the leadership-status-substance the scribes-and-Pharisees walk; the substance the chapter is-built-on is this inversion. The introduction sets up the seven-woes that follow — the leadership-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is about-to-name-out-loud walks the substance the introduction has-just-walked-against.

## The seven woes against the scribes and the Pharisees — one continuous direct-condemnation walked-across-seven-discrete-moves

### The first woe — *ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men*

*But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.* (Matthew 23:13)

The Greek word for *woe* is *ouai* — a Tanakh-prophetic-word the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah) had-walked across the six-woes of Isaiah 5:8-23 against the leadership-of-the-First-Temple, the same word the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) walks at Jeremiah 22:13 and Jeremiah 23:1 against the leadership-of-the-First-Temple, the same word the prophet-Habakkuk walks at Habakkuk 2:6-19 against the leadership-of-the-house-of-Yahudah (Judah). The substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-into is the substance the Tanakh-prophets had-walked — the same substance, the same word, against the leadership-of-the-Second-Temple. The seven-woes that follow are not-an-innovation; they are the Tanakh-prophetic-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out at the leadership-of-the-current-generation.

The *hypocrites* the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) names — the Greek *hypokritai* — is the substance-of-the-stage-actor, the one-who-plays-a-part the heart-substance does-not-walk. The substance is the substance the introduction has-just-walked-against: *they say, and do not.* The leadership-substance reads-the-Torah-from-the-seat but-does-not-walk-the-Torah-itself; the leadership-substance binds-heavy-burdens but-does-not-lift-them; the leadership-substance makes-broad-the-Tefillin but-does-not-bind-the-Torah-on-the-heart. The hypocrisy is the gap between the substance the leadership-substance proclaims and the substance the leadership-substance walks.

The substance of the first-woe is the leadership-substance's *shutting-up-the-kingdom-of-heaven-against-men.* The Greek *kleiete tēn basileian tōn ouranōn emprosthen tōn anthrōpōn* is precise — the leadership-substance closes-the-kingdom-doors-in-the-face-of-the-people-substance. The kingdom-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) has-been-walking-out from the opening of the gospel at Matthew 4:17 (*from that time Yahusha (Jesus) began to preach, and to say, Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand*) is the substance the leadership-substance was-entrusted-with the stewardship-of — the same stewardship the prior chapter's wicked-husbandmen-parable at Matthew 21:33-46 walked-out — and the leadership-substance refuses-to-go-in and refuses-to-let-others-go-in. The Lukan-parallel at Luke 11:52 walks the same substance — *woe unto you, lawyers! for ye have taken away the key of knowledge: ye entered not in yourselves, and them that were entering in ye hindered.* The substance is the same: the leadership-substance holds-the-key the leadership-substance does-not-use, and refuses-to-pass-it-to-others. The Pharisaic-rabbinic-substance has-built layer-upon-layer of oral-tradition between the people-substance and the Torah-substance, layer-upon-layer of credential-requirement between the people-substance and the kingdom-substance the Father is-bringing-in through-the-King. The kingdom-doors close. The system-not-people posture holds at the substance of the first-woe — the substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out-against is not the people the leadership-shepherds but the leadership-substance that-keeps-the-people-from-the-kingdom-substance.

### (The textual-variant additional woe at v.14 in some manuscripts) — *ye devour widows' houses, and for a pretence make long prayer*

*Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows' houses, and for a pretence make long prayer: therefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.* (Matthew 23:14)

The verse stands in some manuscripts and not in others — the Markan-parallel at Mark 12:40 and the Lukan-parallel at Luke 20:47 both-carry the substance; the substance is preserved-in-the-Synoptic-substance regardless of which Matthean-manuscript-tradition is read. The substance is the substance-of-the-leadership-substance devouring-the-economic-substance of the most-vulnerable-substance — the widow-substance — while-making-a-pretense of substantial-piety in the form of long-prayer. The Tanakh-substance has-walked-the-widow-substance from the opening-Torah-substance at Exodus 22:22 (*ye shall not afflict any widow, or fatherless child*) through Deuteronomy 24:17 and Deuteronomy 27:19 (*cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow*) through the prophet-Yeshayahu (Isaiah)'s indictment of the leadership-of-the-First-Temple at Isaiah 1:17 (*seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow*) and Isaiah 1:23 (*they judge not the fatherless, neither doth the cause of the widow come unto them*) and Isaiah 10:2 through the prophet-Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 7:6 and Jeremiah 22:3 through the prophet-Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) at Ezekiel 22:7 through the prophet-Zekharyah (Zechariah) at Zechariah 7:10 through the prophet-Mal'akhi (Malachi) at Malachi 3:5 (*I will be a swift witness... against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless*). The Tanakh-substance walks the widow-substance at the substance of the Father's-particular-concern; the leadership-substance the King-of-Yashar'el (Israel) walks-out-against here has-walked-the-substance categorically-against the Tanakh-substance the leadership-substance reads-from-Mosheh's (Moses's)-seat. The long-prayer-for-pretence is the substance-of-religious-display the introduction has-just-walked-against — the for-to-be-seen-of-men substance now walked-out at the most-damaging-substance, where the religious-display is the cover for the economic-devouring of the vulnerable.

### The second woe — *ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte... ye make him twofold more the child of Gehinnom (Gehenna) than yourselves* — the framework-major-direct-diagnostic of the false-inclusion-gospel

*Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of gehenna than yourselves.* (Matthew 23:15)

The substance of the second-woe is the framework-major-direct-diagnostic the chapter walks-out.
