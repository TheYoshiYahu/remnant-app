# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 3

Matthew 3 opens the public ministry at the Yarden (Jordan): the Eliyahu-pattern prophet calling repentance, the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership identified as the contemporary serpent-seed-within-the-covenant, the lineage-claim-without-fruit rejected, and the Messiah publicly invested with the Father's voice and the Ruach (Spirit) descending dove-form. (1) Yochanan (John) the Immerser is the Eliyahu (Elijah) Malachi 4:5-6 promised — *before the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD),* locked explicitly by Yahusha (Jesus) at Matthew 11:14. The wilderness is where the covenant standard is recoverable: Yashar'el (Israel) was constituted there; Ezekiel 20's future ingathering brings the scattered seed *into the wilderness of the peoples* for the rod-sifting. (2) The Isaiah 40:3 *voice in the wilderness* quotation sits in the chapter that opens Isaiah's second-half gathering-and-return prophecies — first the partial Babylonian-Persian return, then the deeper full return Yahusha's (Jesus's) mission addresses. The repentance Yochanan calls for is the Tanakh-*teshuvah,* the prophetic turning back to Yahuah (the LORD) and his Torah — not the Reformation's believe-the-gospel-of-justification-by-faith-alone repentance. (3) *Generation of vipers* (Greek *gennēmata echidnōn,* offspring of vipers) is a seed-war identification reading against the Genesis 3:15 enmity-between-the-seeds — picked up by Yahusha (Jesus) at Matthew 23:33 and John 8:44 (*ye are of your father the devil*). The Pharisaic-Sadducean apparatus is the first-century beat of the Judaizer-pattern: flesh-credential gospel voiding the heart-Torah it claims to keep. The system is identified; the people are offered the way home. (4) Yochanan's *Elohim (God) is able of these stones to raise up children unto Avraham (Abraham)* is NOT the inclusion-of-Gentiles-by-faith proof-text — he is rejecting the lineage-claim *without fruit* (Paul makes the same point at Romans 9:6-7). The stones read against Isaiah 51:1-2's *rock whence ye are hewn* (Avraham himself); the Lo-Ammi children of Hosea 1:10 gathered and named *sons of the living Elohim (God)* are the stones-into-children-of-Avraham in view. The *every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down* warning is the post-harvest sifting of Ezekiel 20:37-38 at the front of the gospel. (5) Yahusha's (Jesus's) immersion is the symbolic re-crossing of the Yarden — the first Yahoshua (Joshua) led the entry into the land at the close of the wilderness wandering; the second Yahusha (Jesus), bearing the same name *Yah saves,* opens his ingathering ministry in the same water. The voice from heaven combines Psalm 2:7 (Davidic King) and Isaiah 42:1 (Servant) in one sentence; the dove carries Genesis 1:2's *Ruach hovering* and Genesis 8:11's Noach (Noah)-dove-with-olive-leaf. The Formless Father (heard, not seen), the Formed Son (in flesh in the water), and the Ruach mark the moment — one Yahuah (God), Formless in source, Formed in expression — not the fourth-century Trinity read backwards into the scene.
