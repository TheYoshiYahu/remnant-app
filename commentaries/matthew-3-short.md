# Short-form commentary on Matthew 3

The chapter overview walks the main beats — Yochanan (John) the Immerser as the Eliyahu (Elijah)-pattern prophet at the Yarden (Jordan), the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership identified through the seed-war lens, the stones-to-children rejection of the lineage-claim-without-fruit, and the immersion of Yahusha (Jesus) with the Father's voice and the Ruach (Spirit) descending. Two textual moves tighten the Eliyahu-pattern reading at the level the overview does not surface — the garment-detail at the front of the chapter and the river itself at the close.

**The Eliyahu (Elijah) garment-detail.** The Tanakh names Eliyahu (Elijah) by his costume — the wild-prophet appearance, leather girdle and all.

*And they answered him, He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Eliyahu (Elijah) the Tishbite.* (2 Kings 1:8)

Matthew names Yochanan (John) in the same costume in the same order.

*And the same Yochanan (John) had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey.* (Matthew 3:4)

The leather girdle is the textual lock. The Eliyahu-pattern identification rides on the wilderness location and the before-the-day-of-Yahuah role; Matthew's costume detail ties the identification to the actual Tanakh verse that named Eliyahu by garment. Tanakh prophet and gospel prophet meet on the same description.

**The Yarden (Jordan) as the river of the prophetic mantle.** The river where Yahusha (Jesus) is immersed carries a Tanakh-prophetic-action history beyond the Yahoshua (Joshua) entry into the land. The same river carried the Eliyahu (Elijah) / Elisha mantle-transfer at the close of Eliyahu's earthly ministry.

*And Eliyahu (Elijah) took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, so that they two went over on dry ground. ... He took up also the mantle of Eliyahu (Elijah) that fell from him, and went back, and stood by the bank of Yarden (Jordan); And he took the mantle of Eliyahu (Elijah) that fell from him, and smote the waters, and said, Where is Yahuah (the LORD) Elohim (God) of Eliyahu (Elijah)? and when he also had smitten the waters, they parted hither and thither: and Elisha went over.* (2 Kings 2:8, 13-14)

The Eliyahu mantle passed at the Yarden. The Eliyahu-pattern prophet — Yochanan — stands at the same Yarden when the one mightier than he comes to be immersed there. The river of the prophetic mantle is the river of the Messianic investiture. The Yarden was chosen the way the prophets used it; the gospel reads the river the way Tanakh wrote it.
