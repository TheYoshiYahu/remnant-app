# Commentary on Matthew 3

---

## §1. Yochanan (John) the Immerser as the Eliyahu-pattern prophet at the wilderness threshold

Yochanan (John) is the Eliyahu-pattern prophet. The Tanakh closes — in the Christian arrangement — with Malachi's prophecy of an Eliyahu sent before the day of Yahuah.

The Tanakh's closing prophecy and the gospel's opening prophet meet at the Yarden (Jordan). Yahusha (Jesus) will explicitly identify Yochanan (John) as the promised Eliyahu twice in this gospel.

*And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the scribes that Elias must first come? And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Elias truly shall first come, and restore all things. But I say unto you, That Elias is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed. Likewise shall also the Son of Adam suffer of them. Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of Yochanan (John) the Immerser.* (Matthew 17:10-13)

The framework reading attends to what the Eliyahu-pattern carries. Eliyahu was the prophet of the divided-kingdom era who confronted the Ba'al-system at Mount Carmel, called Yashar'el (Israel) to choose, and prepared the people for Yahuah's (God's) action against the syncretism that was destroying the northern house.

*And Eliyahu (Elijah) came unto all the people, and said, How long halt ye between two opinions? if Yahuah (the LORD) be Elohim (God), follow him: but if Ba'al, then follow him. And the people answered him not a word.* (1 Kings 18:21)

Yochanan stands at the analogous threshold. The people are not in Egypt-style external oppression but in internal religious corruption; the temple-system has become a Pharisaic-Sadducean apparatus that has crowded out the prophetic-covenant standard; the day of Yahuah's action against the system is approaching. The Eliyahu-Yochanan parallel is structural, not merely temperamental. Both are wilderness-camel-hair prophets calling the covenant people to repentance before the day of Yahuah. The garment-detail is itself an Eliyahu-echo.

*And they answered him, He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Eliyahu (Elijah) the Tishbite.* (2 Kings 1:8)

*And the same Yochanan (John) had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey.* (Matthew 3:4)

In Yeshayahu's context, the wilderness-voice is the prophetic preparation for the return from Babylonian exile — and chapters 40 through 66 are densely concerned with the return of the scattered seed and the comforting of Yerushalayim. The framework reading hears Matthew identifying Yochanan as the prophet preparing the way for the deeper return — the full return from the long scattering that the Babylonian-Persian partial return did not address. The wilderness location is the deliberate echo of the Tanakh-pattern: Yashar'el was constituted as a covenant people in the wilderness, the prophets denounced the syncretism by calling people back to the wilderness-covenant-standard, and the future ingathering of Ezekiel 20 is to be conducted by Yahuah bringing the scattered seed *into the wilderness of the peoples* for the rod-sifting.

*As I live, saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD), surely with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out, will I rule over you: And I will bring you out from the people, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out. And I will bring you into the wilderness of the people, and there will I plead with you face to face.* (Ezekiel 20:33-35)

The wilderness is the recurring theological location of covenant-renewal. Yochanan preaches there because that is where the covenant standard is recoverable.

The repentance Yochanan calls for is the Tanakh-*teshuvah* — the turning back to Yahuah and to his Torah. It is not a Reformation-style *believe-the-gospel-of-justification-by-faith-alone* repentance; it is the prophetic *turn from your evil ways and live* of Ezekiel.

*Therefore I will judge you, O house of Yashar'el (Israel), every one according to his ways, saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD). Repent, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions; so iniquity shall not be your ruin. Cast away from you all your transgressions, whereby ye have transgressed; and make you a new heart and a new spirit: for why will ye die, O house of Yashar'el (Israel)?* (Ezekiel 18:30-31)

Yochanan's immersion is the public sign of the turning. He is not introducing a new sacrament; he is performing the prophetic-sign-action that names what the people must do internally.

## §2. The kingdom of heaven is at hand — what the announcement means

*Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.* (Matthew 3:2)

The phrase *kingdom of heaven* (*basileía tōn ouranōn* in the Greek) is Matthew's preferred construction; the parallel Synoptic Gospels (Mark, Luke) use *kingdom of Elohim (God).* The two phrases name the same reality — the kingdom is the kingdom of heaven (its seat) and the kingdom of Elohim (its sovereign). Matthew, writing to an audience steeped in Yahudi (Jewish) reverence for the divine name, uses the heaven-as-circumlocution form throughout his gospel.

The framework reading hears *kingdom of heaven at hand* as the proclamation that the prophetic-gathering kingdom of Daniel is at the threshold.

*And in the days of these kings shall the Elohim (God) of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed: and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever.* (Daniel 2:44)

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:13-14)

The Daniel-kingdom is the destination; the Messiah is the king; the gathering is the gathering of the citizens; the day of Yahuah is the moment of the transfer. Yochanan is announcing that the threshold is here.

The framework holds that *kingdom at hand* names the inauguration, not the consummation. The kingdom is opened, the King is revealed, the gathering begins — but Ezekiel 20 has not yet happened, the cosmic signs of the day of Yahuah have not landed, the Olivet judgment is future, the dreadful day is ahead. The first-coming opens the kingdom; the second-coming consummates it. *At hand* means proximate, not completed. The Reformation tradition has often collapsed the at-hand into the now-fully-realized — in different ways across different streams, by preterism, by amillennialism, by realized-eschatology forms of replacement theology — and the framework reads it as a real inauguration awaiting a real consummation. The kaph-comparative at Daniel 7:13's *one like the Son of Adam* is itself the framework's standing reading-discipline note: the figure is Yahusha (Jesus), the Formed who took on flesh, and the kaph honors the incarnation while the named Standard remains the cloud-rider. The kingdom he brings is the same kingdom Daniel saw — inaugurated in his first coming, consummated in his second.

## §3. *Generation of vipers* — the Pharisees and Sadducees identified through the seed-war lens

The Pharisees and Sadducees come down to the Yarden — apparently to be immersed, or possibly to observe. Yochanan does not welcome them. He identifies them as *generation of vipers* — *gennēmata echidnōn* in the Greek, literally *offspring of vipers.* The identification is not generic insult. It is a seed-war identification.

The framework reading places this against the seed-war prophecy of Genesis.

The serpent-seed pattern surfaces across the Tanakh in different forms: Cain killing his brother Hevel (Abel) and the line continuing through the substitute Sheth; the Nephilim of Genesis 6 (the offspring of the fallen watchers and the daughters of men, fused into the seed-line in ways that need careful reading — the seed-war runs within every people, with sons of Adam whose names were written in the Book of Life from the foundation of the world running alongside the tares who were woven in by the fallen watchers); the giants of the land Yashar'el (Israel) was commanded to displace; the persistent enemies of the covenant people across the Tanakh.

Yochanan calling the Pharisees and Sadducees *offspring of vipers* is identifying them as the contemporary face of the serpent-seed within the covenant community. Yahusha will use the same identification later.

*Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.* (John 8:44)

The identification is consistent across the gospel; it is not Yochanan's personal vehemence. It is the framework reading of who the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system actually was — the contemporary face of the original Judaizer pattern. The flesh-credential gospel that demanded circumcision and Torah-performance as the means of covenant standing while voiding the heart-Torah the prophets named runs through the Pharisees in Yochanan's time, through the Galatian agitators in Paul's time, through institutional Christianity in our time, through modern Hebrew Roots' false-inclusion gospel, and through Islam — four costumes on one antichrist architecture. The *vipers* identification at Matthew 3:7 is the gospel-front-end version of that diagnostic. The system they represented was the Judaizer-pattern in its first-century beat — claiming covenant standing by lineage-and-performance while voiding the heart-Torah the prophets named.

The system, not the people inside it. Many of the Pharisees as individuals were sincere men trying to keep Torah within the system handed to them; some, like Nicodemus and Yoseph (Joseph) of Arimathea, will turn and follow Yahusha. The system is the *vipers* identification; individual Pharisees who turn are received. The framework's posture toward people — patience, not condescension — applies to Pharisees as it does to every people. The Eliyahu-pattern prophet calls the system out by name — *generation of vipers* — and offers the door of repentance to anyone inside who will turn.

## §4. *We have Avraham (Abraham) to our father* — the lineage-claim, the fruit-demand, and what the framework does NOT say here

This is one of the most carefully-walked passages in the chapter for the framework. The Christian inclusion-gospel reads it as the proof-text for *anyone can become a child of Avraham by faith — even Gentiles, even stones — because lineage does not matter, only confession matters.* That reading collapses the scattered seed of Yashar'el (Israel) into a spiritual category open to self-selection by faith — which the prophets do not authorize; the seed is a paternal blood line named, traced, and promised gathering. It splices unrelated peoples into Yashar'el by faith-confession — which the olive-tree architecture of Romans 11 forbids; both branches on the tree are Yashar'el, with no non-olive branch grafted in. And it imports a false-inclusion grafting reading into a passage that does not carry it. The framework reads the passage at the depth it actually carries, and the framework does NOT read it as the Christian inclusion-gospel reads it.

What Yochanan is actually saying:

**First**, *think not to say within yourselves, We have Avraham to our father.* The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership is invoking patrilineal descent as the credential of covenant standing — *we are Avraham's seed, so we are right with Elohim.* Yochanan is rejecting that invocation. The lineage-claim is not a substitute for the fruit-demand. The seed of Avraham is required to bear the fruit of the seed of Avraham — the heart-Torah, the prophetic-covenant standard, the love of Yahuah with all the heart that Deuteronomy 6 commands.

*And thou shalt love Yahuah Elohayka (the LORD thy God) with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might. And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart.* (Deuteronomy 6:5-6)

The Pharisees and Sadducees are claiming the lineage while voiding the heart-Torah by their additions (Pharisees) and their rationalist subtractions (Sadducees). The lineage is real; the fruit is missing; the lineage-claim alone does not stand. This is the same point Paul will make later.

*Not as though the word of Elohim (God) hath taken none effect. For they are not all Yashar'el (Israel), which are of Yashar'el (Israel): Neither, because they are the seed of Avraham (Abraham), are they all children: but, In Yitschaq (Isaac) shall thy seed be called. That is, They which are the children of the flesh, these are not the children of Elohim (God): but the children of the promise are counted for the seed.* (Romans 9:6-8)

The seed of Avraham by paternal blood is not automatically the seed of Avraham by fruit-bearing covenant life. The lineage-claim must be confirmed by the fruit of the lineage. Paul is making the same point Yochanan made first.

**Second**, *Elohim is able of these stones to raise up children unto Avraham.* The Christian inclusion read takes this as *Elohim can make Gentile-stones into Avrahamic-children by faith.* The framework reads it differently. The verse says Elohim is ABLE — not that he WILL, and certainly not that he ALREADY HAS by the mechanism of faith-confession alone. The verse names Yahuah's sovereignty over the seed-line: he is not bound to the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership's invocation of their own lineage. If they will not bear fruit, he is sovereign enough to raise up children to Avraham from stones rather than from them. This is a warning to the system invoking lineage, not a doctrine of universal-Gentile-inclusion.

The framework reading goes further: the *stones* image plausibly echoes Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 51.

In Yeshayahu 51, Avraham himself is named as the *rock* from which the seed of Yashar'el was hewn. Yochanan saying that Elohim can raise up children unto Avraham from stones reads naturally as the Yeshayahu-51 image: Elohim can recover the seed of Avraham from the rock-hewn lineage in ways the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system cannot control. The scattered seed in the dispersion — Hosea's Lo-Ammi children who are to be told *ye are the sons of the living Elohim* — are the stones-into-children-of-Avraham Yochanan is pointing at.

The verse is the gathering-of-the-scattered prophecy in compressed form, not the inclusion-of-the-nations doctrine.

**Third**, *every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.* The framework reading holds this together with the post-harvest sifting architecture the prophets carry — the wheat is gathered out of the nations, and then the gathered wheat is sifted again, with the rebels purged out before entry.

*And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant: And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me: I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Yashar'el (Israel): and ye shall know that I am Yahuah (the LORD).* (Ezekiel 20:37-38)

The unfruitful wheat is *blotted out* from the book of life — and the warning to be blotted out is given to the wheat, not to the tares.

*He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life, but I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels.* (Revelation 3:5)

*And Yahuah (the LORD) said unto Mosheh (Moses), Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.* (Exodus 32:33)

Yochanan is naming the same sifting at the front door of the gospel. The tree-and-fruit metaphor will become Yahusha's consistent teaching surface across the gospel.

*Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit. A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit. Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.* (Matthew 7:17-20)

The fruit-test is the sifting-mechanism that operates throughout the kingdom-citizenship reality.

The framework's careful reading of Matthew 3:9-10:

- The lineage of Avraham is real and matters — Yochanan does not deny patrilineal descent from Avraham.
- The lineage-claim alone is not credential — the fruit of the lineage is required.
- Yahuah is sovereign over the seed-line — he can raise up children to Avraham from stones if the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership will not bear fruit (the framework reads this against Yeshayahu 51's rock-hewn-lineage image and Hosea's Lo-Ammi-to-sons-of-the-living-Elohim arc, not against the Christian inclusion-of-Gentile-stones-by-faith image).
- The unfruitful tree is hewn down — the post-harvest sifting is at hand for any wheat that does not bear fruit.

What Yochanan is NOT saying:

- Faith alone makes one a child of Avraham regardless of lineage.
- The covenant standard is being transferred from the seed of Avraham to a multi-ethnic faith-community.
- Lineage does not matter at all.
- Gentiles by belief have replaced unfaithful Yahudim (Jews) in the covenant household.

The framework holds all four of those Christian readings as false. The seed of Avraham is being warned that lineage-claim-without-fruit is insufficient; the sovereignty of Yahuah over the seed-line is being asserted against the Pharisaic-Sadducean attempt to monopolize the lineage; the gathering is at hand and the sifting is at hand together.

## §5. The immersion of Yahusha (Jesus) — the Formed in flesh going into the water, the Father's voice from heaven, the Ruach (Spirit) descending

*Then cometh Yahusha (Jesus) from Galilee to Yarden (Jordan) unto Yochanan (John), to be immersed of him. But Yochanan (John) forbad him, saying, I have need to be immersed of thee, and comest thou to me? And Yahusha (Jesus) answering said unto him, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness. Then he suffered him.* (Matthew 3:13-15)

Yahusha comes from Galilee to the Yarden to be immersed. Yochanan recognizes him and protests — *I have need to be immersed of thee.* Yochanan sees who Yahusha is. The exchange establishes that Yahusha's immersion is not for repentance from his own sin (he has none); it is *to fulfil all righteousness* — to walk through every aspect of the covenant-life Yashar'el was given, including the prophetic-sign-action Yochanan is performing.

The framework reading deepens the *fulfill all righteousness* phrase. Yahusha is recapitulating Yashar'el — he carries the people's identity in his own person, walking the path the people walked. The immersion in the Yarden is the symbolic re-crossing of the river that Yashar'el crossed under Yahoshua (Joshua) to enter the land of promise.

*And it came to pass, when the people removed from their tents, to pass over Yarden (Jordan), and the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people; And as they that bare the ark were come unto Yarden (Jordan), and the feet of the priests that bare the ark were dipped in the brim of the water, (for Yarden (Jordan) overfloweth all his banks all the time of harvest,) that the waters which came down from above stood and rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam, that is beside Tsaretan (Zaretan): and those that came down toward the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, failed, and were cut off: and the people passed over right against Yericho (Jericho).* (Joshua 3:14-16)

Yahusha — bearing the same name as Yahoshua, *Yah saves* — crosses the same water as the symbolic entry into his own ministry of leading the prophetic-ingathering into the kingdom. The first Yahoshua led the entry into the land at the close of the wilderness wandering; the second Yahusha leads the entry into the kingdom-restoration at the close of the long exile. The water is the same. The action is parallel. The framework reads the choice of Yarden immersion as deliberate prophetic-action. The same Yarden also carries the Eliyahu / Elisha mantle-transfer.

*And Eliyahu (Elijah) took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, so that they two went over on dry ground. ... He took up also the mantle of Eliyahu (Elijah) that fell from him, and went back, and stood by the bank of Yarden (Jordan); And he took the mantle of Eliyahu (Elijah) that fell from him, and smote the waters, and said, Where is Yahuah (the LORD) Elohim (God) of Eliyahu (Elijah)? and when he also had smitten the waters, they parted hither and thither: and Elisha went over.* (2 Kings 2:8, 13-14)

The river of the prophetic mantle is the river of the Messianic investiture. The choice is not accidental.

*And Yahusha (Jesus), when he was immersed, went up straightway out of the water: and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Ruach (Spirit) of Elohim (God) descending like a dove, and lighting upon him: And lo a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.* (Matthew 3:16-17)

Three things happen at the immersion: the heavens open; the Ruach (Spirit) of Elohim descends like a dove and lights on Yahusha; the voice from heaven names Yahusha as *my beloved Son.*

The Trinitarian reading takes this scene as the proof-text of the three persons of the Trinity acting in distinct-but-coordinated ways — the Father speaking, the Son in the water, the Spirit descending. The framework reading does not require that grid. The framework reads it against the Formless-Formed structure the canon has been carrying all along.

**The Father.** The voice from heaven is the Father — the formless infinite, never seen by mortal eyes, naming his Son. The Father is heard, not seen.

*And he said, Thou canst not see my face: for there shall no man see me, and live.* (Exodus 33:20)

*No man hath seen Elohim (God) at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.* (John 1:18)

The voice is the Father's signature mode of self-revelation throughout the Tanakh — Sinai is the Father speaking from the cloud, the prophets are the Father speaking through human voices, the Bat-Qol (the daughter-voice of later Yahudi (Jewish) tradition) is the Father's voice from heaven addressing the earth. At the immersion, the Father's voice names what is the case: the man in the Yarden is his beloved Son.

**The Son.** Yahusha is the Formed — the Word drawn from the Formless, the God of every theophany, the One who walked with Adam, who appeared to Avraham, who wrestled with Ya'aqov (Jacob), who spoke from the burning bush, who stood on Sinai. The Formed has now taken on the seed of Adam through Miryam (Mary)'s line and the legal Davidic adoption through Yoseph (Joseph). The Formed in flesh stands in the water. The Formed has walked the recapitulation of Yashar'el — Egypt-sojourn (Matthew 2), Yarden-crossing-by-immersion (Matthew 3), and now the wilderness-temptation that begins in Matthew 4. The Formed is the Son the Father is naming.

**The Ruach (Spirit).** The Ruach (Spirit) of Elohim descends like a dove. The Ruach is the breath / wind / spirit of Yahuah — the divine animating presence that hovered over the waters at creation, that fell on the seventy elders, that empowered the prophets, that Yahuah promises to put within his people in Ezekiel.

*A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* (Ezekiel 36:26-27)

The framework reading does not require the Ruach to be a third co-equal person of a Trinity. The Ruach is Yahuah's own presence in the action-mode — the same divine breath that moved over the waters at creation now moving over the water at Yahusha's immersion to signal the new-creation Yahusha is initiating. The dove-form echoes both Genesis 1:2 (the Ruach *hovering* — the Hebrew *merachefet* carrying bird-imagery in the verb) and the dove of Genesis 8.

Both creation-scene precedents inform the dove-descent here.

The framework reads the scene as the unified divine action of Yahuah the Father (the formless infinite, speaking) and the Formed (the Son, in flesh, in the water) with the Ruach (the divine action-presence) visibly marking the moment. It is the same Yahuah throughout, in the Formless-Formed structure, with the Ruach as the divine animating presence. The Trinity controversy reads the scene through its three-co-equal-persons grid; the framework reads it through the Formless-Formed-with-Ruach grid. The text supports the framework reading without requiring the Trinity grid to be imported.

The voice from heaven — *This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased* — combines two Tanakh sources in one sentence.

*I will declare the decree: Yahuah (the LORD) hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee. Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.* (Psalm 2:7-8)

*Behold my servant, whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; I have put my Ruach (Spirit) upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles. He shall not cry, nor lift up, nor cause his voice to be heard in the street. A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.* (Isaiah 42:1-3)

Psalm 2 is the Davidic-king-installation psalm — the Father announcing the King. Yeshayahu 42 is the first Servant-Song — the Father announcing the Servant. The voice is naming Yahusha as both King and Servant, citing both Psalm and Prophet in one sentence. The combined-citation is the Father's deliberate naming of the Messianic identity in the dual aspect the prophets had carried. The Servant-Song's *I have put my Ruach (Spirit) upon him* is itself enacted in the Ruach-descent at the immersion. The voice names what the dove has just done.

The immersion-scene is the public investiture of the Messiah for his ministry. The Father's voice, the Ruach's descent, the Formed in the water — the unified divine action authorizing the ministry that is about to begin. Matthew 4 will open with the same Ruach driving Yahusha into the wilderness for the temptation that recapitulates Yashar'el's forty-year wilderness wandering in forty days.
