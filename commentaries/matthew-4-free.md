# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 4

Matthew 4 completes the recapitulation-Christology Matthew has been building since chapter 2 — Yahusha (Jesus) walking the path Yashar'el (Israel) walked, in his own body, succeeding where the people failed. (1) The wilderness temptation runs forty days against Yashar'el's (Israel's) forty years, on the day-for-year prophetic equivalence of Numbers 14:34. The three temptations map directly onto the three classic wilderness failures (bread-grumbling, Massah-tempting, golden-calf shortcut-kingdom), and all three of Yahusha's (Jesus's) counter-citations come from Deuteronomy 6-8 — the section of Mosheh's (Moses's) farewell discourse that instructs the next generation NOT to repeat those failures. (2) The temple-pinnacle exchange is the master-discipline for every scripture-twisting case the gospel will encounter: the devil quotes Psalm 91:11-12 selectively, omitting the qualifier *to keep thee in all thy ways* that closes the door on presumption-jumping; Yahusha (Jesus) does not debate line-by-line but brings Deuteronomy 6:16 into the conversation and lets the canon-harmonized standard close the door. Strict-literal-in-isolation produces open conclusions; harmonization-with-the-whole-canon, led by the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit), is the discipline. (3) The kingdoms-of-the-world offer is not an empty bluff — the present age IS temporarily administered by principalities-and-powers (Daniel 10:13, 20; Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19). Yahusha (Jesus) refuses the shortcut-kingdom-without-the-cross and waits for the Daniel 7:13-14 transfer, where the kaph-comparative *like the Son of Adam coming with the clouds* honors the incarnation while the harmonized identification is Yahusha (Jesus) himself, the Formed cloud-rider. (4) The move to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) and the Isaiah 9 quotation of *Galilee of the goyim* is NOT the early-gospel Gentile-inclusion proof-text. The prophetic identification in the verse is tribal — *the land of Zevulun (Zebulun), and the land of Naphtali* — the specific tribal territories of the scattered northern tribes whose land had become Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples after the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE and the foreign resettlement of 2 Kings 17:24. The light dawns on the SCATTERED NORTHERN HOUSE in the territory of their tribal inheritance. The lost-sheep-mission scope (Matthew 1:21, 10:5-6, 15:24) is lived out geographically. (5) Yahusha's (Jesus's) opening preaching-formula at v.17 — *Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand* — is word-for-word identical to Yochanan's (John's) at Matthew 3:2. Same message, intensified and incarnated, never abolished or replaced. (6) *Fishers of men* (v.19) reads against Jeremiah 16:14-16, where Yahuah (the LORD) sends *fishers* to gather the children of Yashar'el (Israel) *from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them.* The disciples are commissioned as gatherers of the scattered seed; the five-region geographic-scope summary at v.25 (Galilee, Decapolis, Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), Yahudah (Judaea), beyond Yarden (Jordan)) is the gathering-territory of the citizens of the kingdom.
