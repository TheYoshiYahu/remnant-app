# Short-form commentary on Matthew 4

The chapter overview walks the main beats — the forty-day wilderness fast as Tanakh-recapitulation of Yashar'el's (Israel's) forty years, the three temptations as inversion of the three classic wilderness failures with Yahusha (Jesus) citing Deuteronomy 6-8 (the Torah-instruction the people had failed to internalize), the scripture-twisting at the temple pinnacle answered by canon-harmonization, the move to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) and the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 9 fulfillment, the calling of the four fishermen as Jeremiah-16 gatherers, and the early-ministry geographic-scope summary. Two textual moves tighten the framework reading at the level the overview does not surface — the principalities-and-powers verses that lock the kingdoms-of-the-world offer as a real administered offer, and the 2 Kings 17:24 deportation-resettlement that locks *Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles)* as tribal-territory rather than Christian-inclusion proof-text.

**The kingdoms-of-the-world offer was a real administered offer, not an empty bluff.** The devil at Matt 4:8-9 shows Yahusha (Jesus) all the kingdoms of the world and offers them in exchange for worship. The framework reading hears the offer as real because the kingdoms of this age ARE temporarily under the dominion of the principalities and powers that fell with the Watcher-rebellion.

*But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Mikha'el (Michael), one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia. ... Then said he, Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee? and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia: and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come.* (Daniel 10:13, 20)

*For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.* (Ephesians 6:12)

*And we know that we are of Elohim (God), and the whole world lieth in wickedness.* (1 John 5:19)

The devil IS offering what he currently administers. The offer is not an empty bluff — it is the shortcut-kingdom-without-the-cross. Yahusha (Jesus) refuses on the Torah-standard, *him only shalt thou serve,* and waits for the Father's-timing kingdom-transfer the Tanakh promises.

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:13-14)

The kaph-comparative *like the Son of Adam* preserves what the text does — the figure resembles mortal-man, taking on flesh, while remaining the Formed cloud-rider; the harmonized identification across the canon is Yahusha (Jesus) himself. The everlasting kingdom Daniel saw is the kingdom Yahusha refused to short-circuit at the wilderness. The devil's offer was the kingdoms now without the cross; Yahusha chose the cross and waited for the Father's timing on the Daniel-7 transfer.

**Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles) was tribal territory of the scattered northern house, not Christian-inclusion proof-text.** Matthew quotes Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 9 at the move to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), and the Christian commentary tradition has read *Galilee of the goyim* as the early-gospel proof that the Messiah was sent to the Gentiles from the start. The framework recovers the geographic-prophetic substance. The territory became Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples after a documented historical event.

*And the king of Assyria brought men from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed them in the cities of Samaria instead of the children of Yashar'el (Israel): and they possessed Samaria, and dwelt in the cities thereof.* (2 Kings 17:24)

The northern tribes were taken east in the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE; foreign peoples were imported west to fill the depopulated territory. By Yeshayahu's day the territory had become Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples — and the prophet's identification in the verse is the TRIBAL identification, not the demographic-mixed-population identification.

*The land of Zevulun (Zebulun), and the land of Naphtali, by the way of the sea, beyond Yarden (Jordan), Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles); The people which sat in darkness saw great light; and to them which sat in the region and shadow of death light is sprung up.* (Matthew 4:15-16, quoting Isaiah 9:1-2)

*The land of Zevulun, and the land of Naphtali* — the specific tribal territories of the scattered northern tribes — *the people which sat in darkness saw great light.* The light dawns on the scattered northern house in the territory of their tribal inheritance. Matthew names the tribal-territories explicitly at the move to Kfar-Nachum: *in the borders of Zevulun and Naphtali.* The lost-sheep-mission scope set at Matthew 1:21 and locked at Matthew 10:5-6 and 15:24 is lived out geographically — the Messiah goes to the territory of the lost sheep and preaches the gathering where the gathering is to begin.
