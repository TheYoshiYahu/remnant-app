# Short-form commentary on Matthew 4

Matthew 4 carries the wilderness temptation, the move to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) where Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 9's prophetic light dawns on the tribal territory of Zevulun (Zebulun) and Naphtali, the continuation of Yochanan's (John's) kingdom-at-hand proclamation in the King's own voice, and the calling of the first four disciples as Jeremiah-16 fishers of the scattered seed. The chapter completes the recapitulation-Christology Matthew has been building since chapter 2 — Yahusha (Jesus) walking the path Yashar'el (Israel) walked, in his own body, succeeding where the people failed.

*Then was Yahusha (Jesus) led up of the Ruach (Spirit) into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil. And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.* (Matthew 4:1-2)

Yashar'el's wilderness was forty years; Yahusha's is forty days. The day-for-year prophetic equivalence is the canon's own formula.

*After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years, and ye shall know my breach of promise.* (Numbers 14:34)

The Ruach (Spirit) leads Yahusha into the wilderness for the same kind of testing Yashar'el underwent — to walk the testing-arc in his own body as the seed-of-Yashar'el-in-flesh. The Tanakh names what the wilderness-testing was for.

*And thou shalt remember all the way which Yahuah Elohayka (the LORD thy God) led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no.* (Deuteronomy 8:2)

Where Yashar'el failed — grumbling for bread, testing Yahuah at Massah for water, worshipping the golden calf for an immediate-kingdom — Yahusha succeeds. The three temptations Matthew records map directly onto these three wilderness failures, and Yahusha's three Deuteronomy-citations all come from chapters 6 through 8 — the section of Deuteronomy where Mosheh (Moses) recounts the wilderness experience as instruction to the next generation about NOT repeating those failures. The Deuteronomy-citations are not random Torah-prooftexts; they are the specific Torah-instructions about the specific failures Yahusha is now reversing.

*And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of Elohim (God), command that these stones be made bread. But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of Elohim (God).* (Matthew 4:3-4)

Yahusha quotes Deuteronomy 8:3, Mosheh's commentary on the manna-experience where Yashar'el grumbled for bread before Yahuah's provision.

*And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of Yahuah (the LORD) doth man live.* (Deuteronomy 8:3)

Where Yashar'el demanded bread on their timing, Yahusha fasts and waits for the Father's provision. The Son passes the test the people failed by holding to the Torah-instruction the people did not internalize.

*Then the devil taketh him up into the holy city, and setteth him on a pinnacle of the temple, and saith unto him, If thou be the Son of Elohim (God), cast thyself down: for it is written, He shall give his angels charge concerning thee: and in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone. Yahusha (Jesus) said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt Yahuah (the Lord) thy Elohim (God).* (Matthew 4:5-7)

The devil's move is the load-bearing methodological lesson of the chapter. He quotes scripture — a real verse, in the canon, attributed to Yahuah's own promise of angelic protection — but selectively, with the qualifier omitted that would close the door on the misuse.

*For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways. They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.* (Psalm 91:11-12)

The qualifier the devil omitted is *to keep thee in all thy ways.* Psalm 91 promises angelic protection to the one *that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High* and *abideth under the shadow of El Shaddai (the Almighty)* — the one whose life is in covenant-faithfulness with Yahuah. The angelic protection is FOR the covenant-walking life; it is not a blank check for presumption-jumping. The devil cuts the qualifier and reads the verse as a blank check.

Yahusha does not debate the cut-quote line-by-line. He says *It is written again* and brings Deuteronomy 6:16 into the conversation.

*Ye shall not tempt Yahuah Elohaychem (the LORD your God), as ye tempted him in Massah.* (Deuteronomy 6:16)

Massah was the place where Yashar'el demanded water and tested whether Yahuah was among them — *Is Yahuah (the LORD) among us, or not?* The same failure-shape as the temple-pinnacle: demanding a sign of the Father's presence rather than trusting in his promised presence. Yahusha brings the OVERRIDING Torah-word and lets the canon-harmonized standard close the door.

This is the master-discipline for every scripture-twisting case the gospel will encounter. The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system quotes Avraham (Abraham) as their credential while voiding the heart-Torah; the answer is to bring the fruit-demand from the prophets into the conversation. The post-apostolic Christian tradition quotes Paul selectively to construct the antinomian gospel; the answer is to bring the Torah, the prophets, and the whole of the canon into the conversation. The modern Hebrew Roots false-inclusion gospel quotes Romans 11's grafting language selectively; the answer is to bring the full olive-tree architecture into the conversation. The Islamic-Quran reading quotes the prior scriptures selectively to affirm them and then contradict them; the answer is the same — let the canon-harmonized standard speak. Strict-literal in isolation produces open conclusions that do not survive when the whole of scripture is brought to bear. The discipline is harmonization-with-the-whole-canon, led by the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit). Yahusha walks the discipline at Matthew 4:7. He is the model.

*Again, the devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them; and saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me. Then saith Yahusha (Jesus) unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship Yahuah (the Lord) thy Elohim (God), and him only shalt thou serve.* (Matthew 4:8-10)

The devil's offer assumes the kingdoms are his to give. The framework reads this carefully: at the present age, the kingdoms of this world ARE temporarily under the dominion of the principalities and powers that fell with the watcher-rebellion.

*But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Mikha'el (Michael), one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia. ... Then said he, Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee? and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia: and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come.* (Daniel 10:13, 20)

*For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.* (Ephesians 6:12)

*And we know that we are of Elohim (God), and the whole world lieth in wickedness.* (1 John 5:19)

The devil IS offering what he currently administers. The offer is not an empty bluff. The price is worship. Yahusha refuses on the Torah-standard — *him only shalt thou serve* — and waits for the kingdom-transfer the Father will accomplish in his own timing through the path the Father has set: the cross, the resurrection, the ascension to the Father's right hand, and the Daniel-7 transfer.

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:13-14)

The kaph-comparative — *like the Son of Adam* — is preserved as incarnation-honoring; when the canon is harmonized, the figure is Yahusha himself, the Formed who took on flesh while remaining the cloud-rider. The everlasting kingdom Daniel saw is the kingdom Yahusha refused to short-circuit. The devil offered the kingdoms now without the cross; Yahusha chose the cross and waited.

The chapter then moves to Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum).

*Now when Yahusha (Jesus) had heard that Yochanan (John) was cast into prison, he departed into Galilee; and leaving Natsareth (Nazareth), he came and dwelt in Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), which is upon the sea coast, in the borders of Zevulun (Zebulun) and Naphtali: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Yeshayahu (Isaiah) the prophet, saying, The land of Zevulun (Zebulun), and the land of Naphtali, by the way of the sea, beyond Yarden (Jordan), Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles); The people which sat in darkness saw great light; and to them which sat in the region and shadow of death light is sprung up.* (Matthew 4:12-16)

The Christian commentary tradition has read *Galilee of the goyim* as the early-gospel proof that the Messiah was sent to the Gentiles from the start — universal-inclusion confirmed by the Galilean ministry's *Gentile* designation. That reading misses the geographic-prophetic substance of Yeshayahu 9.

*Galilee of the goyim* — *gelil ha-goyim* in the Hebrew — names the territory of the northern tribes that had been most heavily depopulated and resettled with foreign peoples after the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE.

*And the king of Assyria brought men from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed them in the cities of Samaria instead of the children of Yashar'el (Israel): and they possessed Samaria, and dwelt in the cities thereof.* (2 Kings 17:24)

The northern tribes were taken east and foreigners were imported west to fill the depopulated territory. By the time of Yeshayahu 9, the territory had become Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples. But the prophetic identification in the verse is the TRIBAL identification, not the demographic-mixed-population identification. *The land of Zevulun, and the land of Naphtali* — the specific tribal territories — *the people which sat in darkness saw great light.* The people in darkness are the people of Zevulun and Naphtali, the scattered northern tribes whose territory had become Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples. The light is dawning on the SCATTERED NORTHERN HOUSE in the territory of their tribal inheritance.

Matthew names the tribal-territories explicitly: *in the borders of Zevulun and Naphtali.* The Messiah goes to the northern-tribal-territory of the scattered seed. The lost-sheep-mission scope set at Matthew 1:21 and locked at Matthew 10:5-6 and 15:24 is lived out geographically. He goes to the territory of the lost sheep. He preaches the gathering where the gathering is to begin.

*From that time Yahusha (Jesus) began to preach, and to say, Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.* (Matthew 4:17)

Yahusha's opening preaching-formula is identical to Yochanan's at Matthew 3:2 — *repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.* Word-for-word the same. Yochanan has been silenced; the Eliyahu-pattern prophet has been removed from the public stage; and the Messiah-King steps in and picks up the proclamation exactly where Yochanan left off. The kingdom-message does not pause when Yochanan is silenced. Some Christian commentary has read Yochanan and Yahusha as preaching DIFFERENT messages — Yochanan preaching law-and-repentance, Yahusha preaching grace-and-faith. The framework rejects this. Yahusha preaches the same message Yochanan preached, intensified and incarnated, never abolished or replaced.

The chapter closes with the calling of the first four disciples.

*And Yahusha (Jesus), walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brethren, Shim'on (Simon) called Kefa (Peter), and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers. And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men. And they straightway left their nets, and followed him.* (Matthew 4:18-20)

The framework reads *fishers of men* against Jeremiah.

*Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that it shall no more be said, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, that brought up the children of Yashar'el (Israel) out of the land of Egypt; But, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, that brought up the children of Yashar'el (Israel) from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them: and I will bring them again into their land that I gave unto their fathers. Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith Yahuah (the LORD), and they shall fish them.* (Jeremiah 16:14-16)

The fishers in Jeremiah are the agents Yahuah sends to gather the scattered children of Yashar'el from the north and from all the lands of their dispersion. *Fishers of men* at Matthew 4:19 is the explicit identification of the disciples with the Jeremiah-16 fishers. The disciples are being commissioned to the lost-sheep-mission scope — they are being made gatherers of the scattered seed. The fishing metaphor is not a generic missionary-call; it is the prophetic-vocational identification that connects the disciples to the second-Exodus gathering Jeremiah names.

The chapter closes with a geographic-scope summary of the early-ministry crowds.

*And there followed him great multitudes of people from Galilee, and from Decapolis, and from Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and from Yahudah (Judaea), and from beyond Yarden (Jordan).* (Matthew 4:25)

Five regions: Galilee (the northern tribal territory); Decapolis (the ten-cities region east of the Yarden); Yerushalayim and Yahudah (the southern-house centers); and beyond Yarden (the eastern-tribes' inheritance — Reuven, Gad, the half-tribe of Manasheh). The geographic-scope is the gathering-territory of the scattered seed. Yahusha's early ministry attracts crowds from precisely the regions where the citizens of the kingdom were living. The gathering is beginning, and the early-ministry geographic-scope summary is the evidence of it.
