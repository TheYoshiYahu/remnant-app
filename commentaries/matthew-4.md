# Commentary on Matthew 4

---

## §1. The wilderness temptation as recapitulation — Yahusha (Jesus) walking the path Yashar'el (Israel) walked

The framework reading carries forward from the Matthew 2 commentary — Yahusha (Jesus) IS Yashar'el (Israel). He carries the people's identity in his own person and recapitulates their history. Matthew 2 walked the Egypt-sojourn-and-return. Matthew 3 walked the Yarden (Jordan) immersion that parallels the Yashar'el-under-Yahoshua (Joshua) crossing into the land. Matthew 4 walks the wilderness-testing — forty days corresponding to Yashar'el's forty years in the Sinai wilderness through the canon's own day-for-year prophetic-equivalence formula.

The Ruach (Spirit) led Yahusha into the wilderness deliberately. The framework reads this as the Father authorizing the recapitulation. Yashar'el was led into the wilderness by Yahuah via Mosheh (Moses) at the Exodus, and the wilderness experience was Yahuah's testing of the people.

Yahusha is led into the wilderness by the Ruach for the same kind of testing — to walk the same testing-arc Yashar'el walked, in his own body, as the seed-of-Yashar'el-in-flesh.

Where Yashar'el failed in the wilderness — grumbling for bread at Exodus 16, testing Yahuah at Massah for water at Exodus 17, worshipping the golden calf for an immediate-kingdom at Exodus 32 — Yahusha succeeds. The three temptations Matthew records map directly onto these three wilderness failures, and Yahusha's three Deuteronomy-citations all come from chapters 6 through 8 — the section of Deuteronomy where Mosheh recounts the wilderness experience as instruction to the next generation about NOT repeating those failures. The Deuteronomy-citations are not random Torah-prooftexts; they are the specific Torah-instructions about the specific failures Yahusha is now reversing.

The framework reading hears this as the credential. The Messiah saves *his people from their sins* — Matthew 1:21 — as one who has walked their path in his own body and succeeded where they failed. The recapitulation is the Tanakh-grammar of his sufficiency: he can lead the gathering because he is the embodied Yashar'el who proved that the wilderness-testing can be passed when the heart-Torah is intact. He is the answer to the wilderness-failure pattern because he has personally inverted it.

## §2. The three temptations as the inversion of the three wilderness failures

**First temptation — stones to bread.**

Yahusha quotes Deuteronomy.

The Deuteronomy verse is Mosheh's commentary on the manna-experience of Exodus, where Yashar'el grumbled against Mosheh and Yahuah for bread before Yahuah had provided it.

*And the whole congregation of the children of Yashar'el (Israel) murmured against Mosheh (Moses) and Aharon (Aaron) in the wilderness: And the children of Yashar'el (Israel) said unto them, Would to Elohim (God) we had died by the hand of Yahuah (the LORD) in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the flesh pots, and when we did eat bread to the full; for ye have brought us forth into this wilderness, to kill this whole assembly with hunger.* (Exodus 16:2-3)

The failure of Yashar'el was demanding bread on their timing rather than receiving it on Yah's timing. The devil's temptation to Yahusha is the same shape: provide bread for yourself by your own action, on your own timing, outside the Father's provision. Yahusha's refusal — *Man shall not live by bread alone* — is the Torah-instruction from the wilderness-experience that Yashar'el failed to internalize. The framework reads this exchange as the deliberate inversion: where Yashar'el grumbled and demanded bread, Yahusha fasts and waits for the Father's provision. The Torah-word he cites is the very word Mosheh named as the lesson of the manna-experience. The Son passes the test the people failed by holding to the Torah-instruction the people did not internalize.

**Second temptation — temple pinnacle, Psalm 91 misquoted.**

Yahusha quotes Deuteronomy.

Massah was the place in the wilderness where Yashar'el demanded water and tested whether Yahuah was among them.

*And he called the name of the place Massah, and Meribah, because of the chiding of the children of Yashar'el (Israel), and because they tempted Yahuah (the LORD), saying, Is Yahuah (the LORD) among us, or not?* (Exodus 17:7)

The failure of Yashar'el was demanding a sign of Yah's presence rather than trusting in his promised presence.

The devil's temptation to Yahusha is the same shape: cast yourself down from the pinnacle to force the Father to display his protection visibly — a sign-test of the Father's presence. The devil quotes the Psalm.

The qualifier the devil omits is *to keep thee in all thy ways.* The Psalm-promise of angelic protection is for the one walking in Yahuah's ways; the devil cuts the qualifier to make the promise read as a blank-check angelic protection valid even for presumption-jumping from temple pinnacles. Yahusha does not debate the misquote line-by-line. He cites the OVERRIDING Torah-word that closes the door: *Thou shalt not tempt Yahuah (the Lord) thy Elohim (God).*

The framework reading hears this as the master-pattern for handling scripture-twisting. The devil quotes scripture, but selectively and out of context. The answer is not to debate the misquote on its own terms; the answer is to harmonize with the whole of scripture by citing the Torah-word that rules out the misuse. This is the same canon-harmonization discipline that the framework reads in the *like the Son of Adam* kaph-comparative passages — strict-literal in isolation can produce open conclusions that do not survive when the whole of scripture is brought to bear; the harmonization is the discipline; being led by the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) while reading is what keeps the reader from being captured by isolated technicalities. Yahusha walks the discipline at Matthew 4:7. He does not debate Psalm 91 as if Psalm 91 were the only word; he brings Deuteronomy 6:16 into the conversation and lets the canon-harmonized standard speak.

This is also the master-template for handling the inherited Christian readings of Paul. Romans, Galatians, and Hebrews can be quoted selectively to produce the antinomian gospel; the answer is not to debate the antinomian quote line-by-line within its own framing; the answer is to bring the Torah-word and the prophets and the whole of the canon into the conversation, and let the harmonized standard speak. The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system at Matthew 3 was identified as the *vipers* — the front-end of the antichrist system. The actual antichrist — the devil himself — quotes scripture in Matthew 4 in the same selective-misuse pattern the leadership-system uses. Yahusha's response is the model.

**Third temptation — kingdoms for worship.**

Yahusha quotes Deuteronomy.

*Thou shalt fear Yahuah Elohayka (the LORD thy God), and serve him, and shalt swear by his name.* (Deuteronomy 6:13)

The third wilderness-failure was the golden calf — Yashar'el worshipping a fashioned image for the immediate-kingdom-deliverance from Mount Sinai's terror, when Mosheh was delayed on the mountain.

*And when the people saw that Mosheh (Moses) delayed to come down out of the mount, the people gathered themselves together unto Aharon (Aaron), and said unto him, Up, make us gods, which shall go before us; for as for this Mosheh (Moses), the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.* (Exodus 32:1)

The pattern: trading worship of Yahuah for the offer of a kingdom-promise from a counterfeit source.

The devil's temptation to Yahusha is the same shape on its largest scale: trade worship of the Father for ALL the kingdoms of the world delivered immediately. The devil's offer assumes the kingdoms are his to give — and at the present age, in the framework reading, the kingdoms of this world ARE under the dominion of the principalities and powers that fell with the watcher-rebellion.

The devil's offer is not an empty bluff in the framework reading. The kingdoms ARE temporarily under the dominion of the rebel powers. He IS offering what he currently administers. The offer is real — and the price is worship.

Yahusha refuses on the Torah-standard: *him only shalt thou serve.* The kingdom-promise that matters is the kingdom the Father gives in his timing through the path the Father has set — the cross, the resurrection, the ascension to the Father's right hand, and the Daniel-7 transfer of the everlasting kingdom.

The kaph-comparative — *like the Son of Adam* — is preserved as incarnation-honoring; when the canon is harmonized, the figure is Yahusha himself, the Formed who took on flesh and remains who he was, is, and is to come. The everlasting kingdom Daniel saw is the kingdom Yahusha refused to short-circuit at the wilderness temptation. The devil offers the kingdoms now without the cross; Yahusha chooses the cross and waits for the Father's timing on the kingdoms. The golden-calf failure of Yashar'el was the same shape — choosing an immediate-kingdom-deliverance through a counterfeit-worship rather than waiting on Mosheh's return with the Torah from the mountain. Yahusha inverts the failure: he waits.

*Then the devil leaveth him, and, behold, angels came and ministered unto him.* (Matthew 4:11)

The angelic ministry that the devil had wanted to manipulate as a sign-test arrives unforced after the temptations are passed. The Father provides what Yahusha refused to demand. The framework reads the closing verse as the affirmation: the path of trust-in-the-Father's-timing receives what the path of presumption-and-demand cannot. The wilderness-testing is passed; the credential of the Messianic ministry is the Tanakh-recapitulation he has just lived through.

## §3. The devil quoting Psalm 91 — the framework discipline for handling scripture-twisting

The Matthew 4:5-7 exchange is the load-bearing template for the framework's reading-discipline. It deserves separate treatment because the Christian commentary tradition has mostly missed what is happening here at the methodological level.

The devil quotes scripture. He quotes Psalm 91:11-12 — a real verse, in the canon, attributed to Yahuah's own promise of angelic protection. The devil is not making up the verse. He is quoting it. And what he is doing is precisely the move every false-system in the framework's diagnostic uses: quoting scripture selectively, out of context, with the qualifier omitted that would close the door on the misuse.

The omitted qualifier is *to keep thee in all thy ways.* Psalm 91 opens by naming the conditions of the protection-promise.

*He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of El Shaddai (the Almighty). I will say of Yahuah (the LORD), He is my refuge and my fortress: my Elohim (God); in him will I trust. ... Because thou hast made Yahuah (the LORD), which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation; There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling. For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.* (Psalm 91:1-2, 9-11)

The angelic protection is FOR the covenant-walking life; it is not a blank-check for presumption-jumping. The devil cuts the qualifier and reads the verse as a blank check.

Yahusha does not debate the cut-quote line-by-line. He does not say *the verse you quoted is real but you omitted the qualifier* and walk through Psalm 91 verse-by-verse. He says *It is written again* and quotes Deuteronomy 6:16. He brings a different scripture into the conversation — a scripture that names exactly the kind of presumption the devil is proposing as forbidden — and lets the canon-harmonized standard close the door.

The framework reads this as the master-discipline for every scripture-twisting case the gospel will encounter:

The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system at Matthew 3:9 quoted Avraham (Abraham) as their credential while voiding the heart-Torah; Yochanan (John) responded by bringing the fruit-demand from the prophets into the conversation. The Pharisees throughout Matthew will quote Torah-passages selectively to defend their additions and subtractions; Yahusha will repeatedly answer not by debating their quote on its own terms but by bringing the OVERRIDING Torah-word and the prophets' clarification into the conversation. The post-apostolic Christian tradition will quote Paul selectively to construct the antinomian gospel; the framework's answer is the same — bring the Torah, the prophets, and the whole of the canon into the conversation and let the harmonized standard speak. The modern Hebrew Roots false-inclusion gospel will quote Romans 11's grafting language selectively; the framework's answer is the same — bring the full olive-tree architecture (both branches are Yashar'el; no non-olive branch grafted in; the wild olive descendants come home through the prophetic journey, not by faith-confession alone) into the conversation and let the harmonized standard speak. The Islamic-Quran reading will quote the prior scriptures selectively to affirm them and then contradict them; the framework's answer is the same — let the canon-harmonized standard speak.

The 1234-of-Truth filter from John's first letter is the master-test that disqualifies every scripture-twisting reading.

*And hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him.* (1 John 2:3-4)

Any reading of any scripture that requires the commandments to be voided is false. Yahusha will name the same diagnostic when the Sadducees come with their selective-scripture trick about the resurrection.

*Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of Elohim (God).* (Matthew 22:29)

Peter will name the apostolic warning about the pattern.

*As also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; in which are some things hard to be understood, which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.* (2 Peter 3:16)

Yahusha's move at Matthew 4:7 is the model. The discipline is not debate-on-the-devil's-terms; the discipline is harmonization-with-the-whole-canon, led by the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit). The framework reading lifts this master-pattern out of the temptation-narrative and makes it the standing methodology for every scripture-twisting encounter the gospel will face.

## §4. *Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles)* — Isaiah 9 read in the framework, not the Christian inclusion read

Matthew quotes Yeshayahu (Isaiah).

*Nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation, when at the first he lightly afflicted the land of Zevulun (Zebulun) and the land of Naphtali, and afterward did more grievously afflict her by the way of the sea, beyond Yarden (Jordan), in Galilee of the nations. The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined.* (Isaiah 9:1-2)

The Christian commentary tradition has read *Galilee of the goyim* as the early-gospel proof that the Messiah was sent to the Gentiles from the start — universal-inclusion confirmed by the Galilean ministry's *Gentile* designation. That read collapses the scattered seed of Yashar'el into a spiritual category open to self-selection and splices unrelated peoples into Yashar'el by faith-confession, and it misreads the geographic-prophetic substance of Yeshayahu 9.

The framework reading recovers the substance. *Galilee of the goyim* — *gelil ha-goyim* in the Hebrew — names the territory of the northern tribes (Zevulun, Naphtali, Asher, Yissakhar) that had been most heavily depopulated and resettled with foreign peoples after the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE. The Assyrian policy is recorded in 2 Kings.

The northern tribes were taken east and foreigners were imported west to fill the depopulated territory. By the time of Yeshayahu 9 — written shortly before or during the Assyrian deportation — the prophet is naming what the territory has become: *Galilee of the goyim,* Galilee of the mixed peoples, the territory where the descendants of the resettled foreigners now lived alongside whatever northern-tribe remnant remained.

The prophetic identification in Yeshayahu 9 is the TRIBAL identification, not the demographic-mixed-population identification. *The land of Zevulun, and the land of Naphtali* — the specific tribal territories — *the people which sat in darkness saw great light.* The people in darkness are the people of Zevulun and Naphtali — the scattered northern tribes whose territory had become Galilee-of-the-mixed-peoples. The light is dawning on the SCATTERED NORTHERN HOUSE in the territory of their tribal inheritance. The Yeshayahu 9 prophecy is a gathering-prophecy: the light comes to the people of the scattered tribes in the very territory they were torn from.

The framework reads Matthew's quotation as the locking-in of this reading. Yahusha leaves Natsareth (Nazareth) and dwells in Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) *in the borders of Zevulun and Naphtali* — Matthew names the tribal-territories explicitly. The Messiah goes to the northern-tribal-territory of the scattered seed. This is the lost-sheep-mission scope set at Matthew 1:21 and locked at the explicit instructions to the twelve and the Canaanite-woman pericope, lived out geographically.

*These twelve Yahusha (Jesus) sent forth, and commanded them, saying, Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not: but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 10:5-6)

*But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 15:24)

He goes to the territory of the lost sheep. He preaches the gathering where the gathering is to begin.

The *goyim* in *Galilee of the goyim* names the demographic state of the territory after the Assyrian deportation — the foreign peoples resettled there — but the prophetic substance is the scattered-northern-tribe-in-darkness who shall see the great light. The Christian inclusion-gospel collapses the demographic descriptor into a doctrinal universal-Gentile-inclusion declaration; the framework recovers the geographic-tribal substance the text is actually carrying.

## §5. *Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand* — Yahusha (Jesus) picks up Yochanan's (John's) words exactly

Yahusha's opening preaching-formula is identical to Yochanan's at Matthew 3.

*In those days came Yochanan (John) the Immerser, preaching in the wilderness of Yahudah (Judaea), And saying, Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.* (Matthew 3:1-2)

Word-for-word the same. The framework reads this as deliberate continuity. Yochanan has been silenced — imprisoned, per v.12 — the Eliyahu-pattern prophet has been removed from the public stage; and the Messiah-King steps in and picks up the proclamation exactly where Yochanan left off. The kingdom-message does not pause when Yochanan is silenced. The proclamation continues in the King's own voice.

This continuity matters for the framework. Some Christian commentary has read Yochanan and Yahusha as preaching DIFFERENT messages — Yochanan preaching law-and-repentance, Yahusha preaching grace-and-faith. The framework rejects this reading. Yahusha is preaching the same kingdom-at-hand message Yochanan preached. The repentance Yahusha calls for is the same Tanakh-*teshuvah* Yochanan called for. The kingdom-at-hand is the same prophetic-gathering kingdom Yochanan announced. There is no message-shift between Yochanan and Yahusha; the King-himself amplifies and incarnates the Eliyahu-pattern proclamation rather than supplanting it.

The Reformation-shape reading of *grace replaced law at the cross* and *Yochanan was the last of the law-preachers, Yahusha was the first of the grace-preachers* is the antichrist-system reading. The Yahusha of the gospels preached repentance, called for fruit, demanded obedience, and warned of the post-harvest sifting — the same message Yochanan preached, intensified and incarnated, never abolished or replaced.

## §6. *Fishers of men* — Jeremiah 16 as the framework reading

*And Yahusha (Jesus), walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brethren, Shim'on (Simon) called Kefa (Peter), and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers. And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men. And they straightway left their nets, and followed him. And going on from thence, he saw other two brethren, Ya'aqov (James) the son of Zavdai (Zebedee), and Yochanan (John) his brother, in a ship with Zavdai (Zebedee) their father, mending their nets; and he called them. And they immediately left the ship and their father, and followed him.* (Matthew 4:18-22)

The call of the first four disciples — two pairs of brothers, all fishermen, all at the Sea of Galilee. The framework reading reads *fishers of men* against Jeremiah.

*Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that it shall no more be said, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, that brought up the children of Yashar'el (Israel) out of the land of Egypt; But, Yahuah (the LORD) liveth, that brought up the children of Yashar'el (Israel) from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them: and I will bring them again into their land that I gave unto their fathers. Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith Yahuah (the LORD), and they shall fish them; and after will I send for many hunters, and they shall hunt them from every mountain, and from every hill, and out of the holes of the rocks.* (Jeremiah 16:14-16)

The fishers in Jeremiah are the agents Yahuah sends to gather the scattered children of Yashar'el from the north and from all the lands of their dispersion. The framework reads Matthew 4:19's *fishers of men* as the explicit identification of the disciples with the Jeremiah-16 fishers. The disciples are being commissioned to the lost-sheep-mission scope — they are being made gatherers of the scattered seed. The fishing metaphor is not a generic missionary-call; it is the prophetic-vocational identification that connects the disciples to the Jeremiah-16 second-Exodus gathering.

Christian commentary has flattened *fishers of men* into *missionaries who win souls for Christ.* The framework recovers the Tanakh-substance: the disciples are commissioned to fish the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el from the lands of their scattering. The audience of their fishing is the citizens of the kingdom — scattered through the nations, indistinguishable from the nations by sight, hearing only when the Shepherd's voice reaches them.

*But ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep, as I said unto you. My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.* (John 10:26-27)

The fishers' job is to cast the net broadly through the territories where the scattered sheep are, and the sheep who hear come.

The fact that the first four called are all fishermen is itself deliberate. Yahusha picks men whose vocational frame already understands net-casting, current-reading, patience-with-the-wait, and judgment-about-when-to-haul. The metaphor is not arbitrary; the men's lived experience of the work prepares them for the prophetic-fishing they will now do at the gospel-scale.

The framework also reads the *immediately left their nets* response as the Tanakh-pattern of the prophetic call. Eliyahu (Elijah) called Elisha by casting his mantle on him as Elisha was plowing; Elisha left the plow, killed the oxen, and followed.

*So he departed thence, and found Elisha the son of Shafat (Shaphat), who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he with the twelfth: and Eliyahu (Elijah) passed by him, and cast his mantle upon him. And he left the oxen, and ran after Eliyahu (Elijah), and said, Let me, I pray thee, kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow thee. And he said unto him, Go back again: for what have I done to thee? And he returned back from him, and took a yoke of oxen, and slew them, and boiled their flesh with the instruments of the oxen, and gave unto the people, and they did eat. Then he arose, and went after Eliyahu (Elijah), and ministered unto him.* (1 Kings 19:19-21)

The pattern: the prophet calls, the called one leaves the vocational frame for the prophetic frame. Yahusha's calling of Kefa (Peter), Andrew, Ya'aqov (James), and Yochanan (John) walks the Eliyahu-Elisha pattern. The Eliyahu-pattern continued in Yochanan the Immerser; the Messianic continuation extends the prophetic-vocational-call to the disciples.

## §7. The geographic scope of the early ministry — gathering-territory of the scattered seed

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people. And his fame went throughout all Syria: and they brought unto him all sick people that were taken with divers diseases and torments, and those which were possessed with devils, and those which were lunatick, and those that had the palsy; and he healed them. And there followed him great multitudes of people from Galilee, and from Decapolis, and from Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and from Yahudah (Judaea), and from beyond Yarden (Jordan).* (Matthew 4:23-25)

The geographic-scope summary at the end of Matthew 4 is read carefully in the framework. Five regions are named: Galilee — the northern tribal territory; Decapolis — the ten-cities region east of the Yarden, a Hellenistic-Greek territory with significant Yahudi (Jewish) population, in territory that had been the inheritance of Manasheh (Manasseh)'s half-tribe and of Gad in the eastern tribal allotments; Yerushalayim — the cultic-and-political center of the southern house; Yahudah — the broader southern-house territory; beyond Yarden — the territory of Reuven (Reuben), Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasheh on the east side.

The framework reading: the geographic scope is the gathering-territory of the scattered seed. Yahusha's early ministry attracts crowds from the territories where the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el are concentrated — both houses, north (Galilee) and south (Yerushalayim, Yahudah), and both sides of the Yarden (the eastern tribes' inheritance, beyond Yarden). Decapolis adds the Hellenistic-Greek territory that had become the post-Assyrian-resettlement mixed-population zone, also containing northern-tribe remnant and Yahudi (Jewish) communities. The crowds following Yahusha are drawn from precisely the regions where the citizens of the kingdom — the scattered seed — were living.

The Christian commentary tradition has often read the geographic-scope summary as evidence of Yahusha's cross-cultural appeal — the gospel attracting Yahudim and Gentiles alike from the start. The framework recovers the gathering-substance: the crowds are not random multi-ethnic followers; they are the citizens of the kingdom being drawn to the King from the territories where the scattering had distributed them. The Decapolis-region pull is the eastern-tribe-territory pull. The beyond-Yarden pull is the same. The Galilee pull is the northern-house pull. The Yerushalayim and Yahudah pull is the southern-house pull. The gathering is beginning, and the geographic-scope summary is the early-ministry evidence of it.

The framework also reads the healing-emphasis in v.23-24 as the Messianic-credential of Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 35.

*Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your Elohim (God) will come with vengeance, even Elohim (God) with a recompence; he will come and save you. Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing.* (Isaiah 35:4-6)

Yahusha healing every manner of sickness fulfills the Messianic-credential prophecy. The kingdom-at-hand proclamation is being authenticated by the kingdom-at-hand signs. This will be the criterion Yahusha himself names when Yochanan from prison sends to ask if he is the one.

*Now when Yochanan (John) had heard in the prison the works of the Messiah (Christ), he sent two of his disciples, And said unto him, Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another? Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Go and shew Yochanan (John) again those things which ye do hear and see: The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.* (Matthew 11:2-5)

The Yeshayahu 35 credential is the answer.
