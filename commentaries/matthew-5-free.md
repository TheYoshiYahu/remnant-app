# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 5

Matthew 5 opens the Sermon on the Mount — the King ascending the mountain to teach with authority, the heart-Torah laid down at the throne-place. Yahuah (Lord) who descended on Sinai in fire is the Formed who has now taken flesh and stands on the mountain; same Voice, same Torah, heart-Torah unveiled.

The Beatitudes are NOT a how-to ladder of dispositions to attain the kingdom — they are a roster of the citizens *already* in it.

The *poor in spirit* are the Tanakh-*anawim* (Isaiah 57:15); the *mourn* are those marked for preservation in Ezekiel 9:4; the *meek* inheriting the earth is Psalm 37:11 directly, the prophetic land-inheritance of the gathered remnant at the consummation; the *peacemakers* are *called the children of Elohim (God)* because they are children who make peace — the Beatitude reveals, not manufactures.

Verses 17-19 are the load-bearing centerpiece. *Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets* closes the misreading before it can open. *One jot or one tittle* binds him to the consonantal Hebrew text at the smallest-possible-mark level until heaven and earth pass.

The break-and-teach diagnostic of v.19 identifies the horn's pulpit that teaches the congregation the commandments are abolished — *called the least in the kingdom* — and names the alternative — *whosoever shall do and teach them, the same shall be called great.* Matthew 5:19 is the New Testament companion of 1 John 2:3-4.

*To fulfil* (Greek *plēroō,* Strong's G4137) does not mean *to exhaust-and-thereby-end* — the antichrist reading the pulpit installed to retire the Torah. It means *to bring to pass.* To make happen what the prophets foretold. To complete what was written.

Yahusha (Jesus) names at v.17 what his mission accomplishes: the bringing-to-pass of what the Torah and the Prophets prophesied. And what they prophesied is *the gathering of the scattered seed of Yashar'el (Israel) home through redemption.*

The Torah itself wrote the curse for covenant-breaking — the scattering that would land both houses outside the land:

*And I will scatter you among the heathen, and will draw out a sword after you: and your land shall be desolate, and your cities waste.* (Leviticus 26:33)

The prophets confirmed it in execution. The northern house was divorced and renamed *Lo-Ammi (not my people)*:

*Then said Elohim (God), Call his name Lo-ammi: for ye are not my people, and I will not be your Elohim (God).* (Hosea 1:9)

The southern house was cast out, with favour withdrawn in the scattering:

*Therefore will I cast you out of this land into a land that ye know not, neither ye nor your fathers; and there shall ye serve other gods day and night; where I will not shew you favour.* (Jeremiah 16:13)

But the same Torah that wrote the curse wrote the return path:

*If they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, with their trespass which they trespassed against me, and that also they have walked contrary unto me; and that I also have walked contrary unto them, and have brought them into the land of their enemies; if then their uncircumcised hearts be humbled, and they then accept of the punishment of their iniquity: then will I remember my covenant with Ya'aqov (Jacob), and also my covenant with Yitschaq (Isaac), and also my covenant with Avraham (Abraham) will I remember; and I will remember the land.* (Leviticus 26:40-42)

Confession of sins — own and fathers' — plus humbled heart plus *accept of the punishment of their iniquity* (saying *he was just* in the scattering) is the Torah-named door home.

*Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand* (Matthew 3:2; 4:17) is the gospel-grammar of that same Torah-named door. Yochanan (John) the Witness and Yahusha (Jesus) preach the door the Torah opened.

The return is then completed in the prophetic ingathering:

*And it shall come to pass, when all these things are come upon thee, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before thee, and thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations, whither Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) hath driven thee, and shalt return unto Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God), and shalt obey his voice according to all that I command thee this day, thou and thy children, with all thine heart, and with all thy soul; that then Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) will turn thy captivity, and have compassion upon thee, and will return and gather thee from all the nations, whither Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) hath scattered thee. If any of thine be driven out unto the outmost parts of heaven, from thence will Yahuah thy Elohim (the LORD thy God) gather thee, and from thence will he fetch thee.* (Deuteronomy 30:1-4)

But we could not walk the return path on our own. We lay divorced, scattered, no longer his people, no longer under his favour in the lands of our scattering.

To open the door home, the Messiah had to redeem us — to come where we were, to bear what the Torah's curse-system named, to break the divorce by going through death and rising the third day.

That is the death and resurrection that makes the gathering possible. *To fulfil* the Torah and the Prophets, at Matthew 5:17, is to bring-to-pass the redemption that opens the gathering.

He is raised in flesh as the firstfruits of the same gathering — for everyone who comes through the door he opened.

The Sermon on the Mount is not the abolition of the Torah for a new dispensation. It is the heart-Torah teaching for the redeemed scattered seed who are being gathered home, given new hearts, and instructed to walk in the statutes their fathers broke. *To fulfil* is the gospel of the gathering said in one Greek word.

The *righteousness exceeding the scribes and Pharisees* of v.20 is the new-heart Torah of Ezekiel 36:26-27 against the Pharisaic counterfeit, and the six *ye have heard ... but I say unto you* antitheses of vv.21-48 walk it out.

The antitheses are NOT Yahusha (Jesus) vs Mosheh (Moses): *ye have heard* references what was HEARD from the synagogue teachers' oral-tradition *takkanot,* not what was WRITTEN in the scroll.

The pattern: heart-substance restored against rabbinic truncations (murder, adultery, oaths), regulatory-concession-misread corrected (divorce — explicitly at Matthew 19:8), sphere-mistake corrected (*eye for an eye* restored to its judicial-Torah sphere; Romans 13:3-4 confirms the magistrate's continuing sword), and outright addition exposed (*hate thine enemy* is NOT in Torah; Leviticus 19:34 commands love of the stranger; Exodus 23:4-5 commands help to the enemy's ass).

*Be ye therefore perfect* (v.48) is the Hebrew *tamim* — whole, complete — the same *tamim* Yahuah (the LORD) commanded Avraham (Abraham) at Genesis 17:1 and Mosheh (Moses) at Deuteronomy 18:13. The antitheses restore the Torah; they do not abolish it.
