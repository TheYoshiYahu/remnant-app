# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 5

Matthew 5 opens the Sermon on the Mount — the King ascending the mountain to teach with authority, the heart-Torah laid down at the throne-place. The Yahuah (Lord) who descended on Sinai in fire is the Formed who has now taken flesh and stands on the mountain; same Voice, same Torah, heart-Torah unveiled. (1) The Beatitudes are NOT a how-to ladder of dispositions to attain the kingdom — they are a roster of the citizens *already* in it. The *poor in spirit* are the Tanakh-*anawim* (Isaiah 57:15); the *mourn* are those marked for preservation in Ezekiel 9:4; the *meek* inheriting the earth is Psalm 37:11 directly, the prophetic land-inheritance of the gathered remnant at the consummation; the *peacemakers* are *called the children of Elohim (God)* because they are children who make peace — the Beatitude reveals, not manufactures. (2) Verses 17-19 are the load-bearing centerpiece. *Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets* closes the misreading before it can open. *To fulfil* (Greek *plēroō*) means to fill up, NOT to exhaust-and-thereby-end (the antichrist reading). *One jot or one tittle* binds him to the consonantal Hebrew text at the smallest-possible-mark level until heaven and earth pass. The break-and-teach diagnostic of v.19 identifies the horn's pulpit that teaches the congregation the commandments are abolished — *called the least in the kingdom* — and names the alternative — *whosoever shall do and teach them, the same shall be called great.* Matthew 5:19 is the New Testament companion of 1 John 2:3-4. (3) The *righteousness exceeding the scribes and Pharisees* of v.20 is the new-heart Torah of Ezekiel 36:26-27 against the Pharisaic counterfeit, and the six *ye have heard ... but I say unto you* antitheses of vv.21-48 walk it out. The antitheses are NOT Yahusha (Jesus) vs Mosheh (Moses): *ye have heard* references what was HEARD from the synagogue teachers' oral-tradition *takkanot,* not what was WRITTEN in the scroll. The pattern: heart-substance restored against rabbinic truncations (murder, adultery, oaths), regulatory-concession-misread corrected (divorce — explicitly at Matthew 19:8), sphere-mistake corrected (*eye for an eye* restored to its judicial-Torah sphere; Romans 13:3-4 confirms the magistrate's continuing sword), and outright addition exposed (*hate thine enemy* is NOT in Torah; Leviticus 19:34 commands love of the stranger; Exodus 23:4-5 commands help to the enemy's ass). (4) *Be ye therefore perfect* (v.48) is the Hebrew *tamim* — whole, complete — the same *tamim* Yahuah (the LORD) commanded Avraham (Abraham) at Genesis 17:1 and Mosheh (Moses) at Deuteronomy 18:13. The antitheses restore the Torah; they do not abolish it.
