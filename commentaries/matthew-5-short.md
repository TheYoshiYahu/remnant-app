# Short-form commentary on Matthew 5

The chapter overview walks the main beats — the King ascending the mountain to teach the heart-Torah in his own voice (Sinai's same Voice now in flesh), the Beatitudes as the roster of the citizens already in the kingdom (not the ladder to enter it), the load-bearing v.17-19 declaration with *plēroō* read as *bring-to-pass* (not exhaust-and-end), the new-heart righteousness of v.20 against the Pharisaic counterfeit, the six antitheses as oral-tradition correction (not Yahusha vs Mosheh), and the *tamim*-completion at v.48. Two textual moves tighten the framework reading at the level the overview does not surface — the salt-and-light naming as covenant-preservation and Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49 gathering-light (not generic Christian-witness), and the Mark 7 / Deuteronomy 4 scripture-level anchors that lock the six antitheses as oral-tradition add-or-diminish correction.

**Salt and light name the covenant-preservation and gathering-light function of the gathered remnant, not a generic Christian-witness motif.** The Greek *este* at Matt 5:13-14 is indicative, not imperative — *you ARE the salt of the earth ... you ARE the light of the world.* The disciples are not commanded to become salt and light; they are named as the salt and the light they already are because the Father has drawn them. Both terms carry a specific Tanakh-substance the Christian commentary tradition has flattened into generic personal-witness motifs.

*Neither shalt thou suffer the salt of the covenant of thy Elohim (God) to be lacking from thy meat offering: with all thine offerings thou shalt offer salt.* (Leviticus 2:13)

*Ought ye not to know that Yahuah Elohim (the LORD God) of Yashar'el (Israel) gave the kingdom over Yashar'el (Israel) to David for ever, even to him and to his sons by a covenant of salt?* (2 Chronicles 13:5)

Salt in the Tanakh is the preserving-of-covenant substance — every offering carries it, the Davidic kingdom is sealed by it. The disciples are salt of the earth because they preserve the covenant the Father gave: the heart-Torah, the Sabbath, the feasts, the names. The system that voids the heart-Torah is the salt that has lost its savour and is cast out.

*And he said, It is a light thing that thou shouldest be my servant to raise up the tribes of Ya'aqov (Jacob), and to restore the preserved of Yashar'el (Israel): I will also give thee for a light to the goyim (Gentiles), that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth.* (Isaiah 49:6)

The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49 sequence is precise. The raising-up of the tribes of Ya'aqov and the restoring of the preserved of Yashar'el is the FIRST mission; the light to the goyim is the SECOND — reaching the lost sheep in the lands of their scattering, where the seed of Yashar'el has been dispersed. The disciples on the second-Sinai mountain are the present-age witness of the gathered remnant carrying that gathering-light onward — not a generic Christian-evangelism motif, but the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed mission the Messiah came to walk.

**Mark 7 and Deuteronomy 4 are the scripture-level anchors that lock the six antitheses as oral-tradition correction, not Torah supersession.** Yahusha (Jesus) names the oral-tradition-over-Torah pattern explicitly elsewhere; the Torah itself names the add-or-diminish prohibition the oral tradition violates. The *ye have heard ... but I say unto you* formula at Matt 5:21-48 is not Yahusha-vs-Mosheh; it is the same harmonization-discipline Yahusha walked against the devil's selective Psalm-91 quote at Matt 4:5-7 — bringing the overriding Torah-word into the conversation against the partial-citation distortion. *Ye have heard* — *ēkousate* — references what the people HEARD from the synagogue teachers' oral-tradition *takkanot,* not what was WRITTEN in the scroll.

*Howbeit in vain do they worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men. For laying aside the commandment of Elohim (God), ye hold the tradition of men, as the washing of pots and cups ... Making the word of Elohim (God) of none effect through your tradition, which ye have delivered.* (Mark 7:7-9, 13)

That is the diagnostic Yahusha (Jesus) names in plain language; the six antitheses of Matt 5 walk it case by case. The Torah's own prohibition names the move the tradition makes.

*Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it, that ye may keep the commandments of Yahuah Elohaychem (the LORD your God) which I command you.* (Deuteronomy 4:2)

The six antitheses are six cases of the add-or-diminish violation. Antitheses 1, 2, and 4 are *diminish* cases — murder stopped at the act, adultery stopped at the act, oaths reduced to a gradation-evasion apparatus — when the Torah always carried the heart-substance (*Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart,* Leviticus 19:17; *Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife,* Exodus 20:17). Antithesis 3 *diminishes* the Genesis-2 marriage-design by inverting Deuteronomy 24:1's regulatory-concession-to-hardness into a permission-mandate, which Yahusha (Jesus) explicitly corrects at Matt 19:8. Antithesis 5 *diminishes* the Torah's judicial-sphere by slipping *lex talionis* into the personal-revenge frame (Romans 13:3-4 confirms the magistrate's continuing sword). Antithesis 6's *hate thine enemy* is the clearest *add* — there is no such commandment in the Tanakh, and the Torah commands the opposite at Leviticus 19:34 (*the stranger that dwelleth with you ... thou shalt love him as thyself*) and Exodus 23:4-5 (*if thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again*). Yahusha is not contradicting Mosheh (Moses); he is stripping the add-or-diminish off and restoring what the Torah always carried. The contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4 stands at full force: any reading of Matthew 5 that voids the commandments is wrong — including the reading that turns the antitheses into Yahusha-vs-Mosheh.
