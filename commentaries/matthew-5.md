## §1. The mountain — the second-Sinai motif, the heart-Torah in the King's own voice

*And seeing the multitudes, he went up into a mountain: and when he was set, his disciples came unto him: And he opened his mouth, and taught them, saying* (vv.1-2).

The framework reads the setting deliberately. The mountain is not incidental scenery. Mosheh (Moses) ascended a mountain to receive Torah from Yahuah (the LORD) and brought it DOWN to the people at the base. Matthew 5 inverts the spatial frame: the Messiah goes UP, and the people come up *to him.* The teacher does not descend with a tablet — the teacher SITS, the rabbinic posture of teaching with authority, and the disciples draw near. The Torah is now spoken in the King's own voice, from the mountain, to those gathering to hear.

The framework reading hears this as the second-Sinai motif Matthew has been preparing since chapter 2. Yahusha (Jesus) recapitulates Yashar'el (Israel) in his own body — the Egypt-sojourn (Matt 2), the Yarden (Jordan) immersion (Matt 3), the wilderness testing (Matt 4) — and now arrives at the mountain. But this is not a repetition of Sinai. This is its continuation by the same Voice. Per the nature-of-God reading established at Matt 1:23 and 3:17, the Yahuah (the LORD) who spoke at the burning bush, who descended on Sinai in fire, who spoke the ten words to Mosheh (Moses), is the Formed — the Son drawn from the Formless — who has now taken flesh and stands on the mountain teaching. The same Voice. The same Torah. The intensification is in the unveiling of the heart-Torah the Sinai-Torah always carried in its substance.

The audience-structure matters. The disciples *came unto him* — those who have left their nets, those who have stepped out of the vocational frame into the prophetic frame at Matt 4:18-22. The multitudes follow and hear (Matt 7:28-29 closes the sermon with *the people were astonished at his doctrine*). The teaching is FOR the disciples in primary scope and FOR the multitudes in overhearing scope. The Sermon's address is the gathered remnant — the lost sheep being drawn — with the multitudes pulled in toward the same gathering. This is the kingdom-citizens roster being shaped before the citizens are publicly named.

The framework reading lifts the mountain-and-multitude composition as deliberate. The King is on the mountain because the kingdom is at hand and he speaks from the throne-place. The disciples are close because they have answered the call. The multitudes are present because the Isaiah-9 light has dawned in Galilee-of-the-goyim and they are being drawn. The Sermon on the Mount is the second-Sinai sermon of the kingdom-at-hand King.

## §2. The Beatitudes — the citizens-of-the-kingdom roster, not entry-conditions

*Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled. Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see Elohim (God). Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of Elohim (God). Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are ye, when men shall revile you, and persecute you, and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceeding glad: for great is your reward in heaven: for so persecuted they the prophets which were before you* (vv.3-12).

The Christian commentary tradition has often read the Beatitudes as a how-to roster — a graduated ladder of dispositions one must achieve to attain the kingdom. The framework rejects that reading. The Beatitudes are a description of the citizens of the kingdom *already* — a roster of the postures by which the gathered remnant can be identified. Each Beatitude names a prophetic-remnant marker the Tanakh has already established. Yahusha (Jesus) is not telling the disciples *become these things to enter the kingdom;* he is naming them as those who *are* these things because the Father has drawn them.

This matters for the framework's audience-identification posture. The Beatitudes are how the lost sheep recognize themselves. They read the list and find their own posture named. They are gathered because they ARE these things, not made these things by gathering. The cause-and-effect of Red Line #11 — *if you hear, you are sons of Avraham (Abraham); hearing reveals, hearing does not make* — applies to the Beatitudes too. The poor in spirit hear *theirs is the kingdom of heaven* and know themselves named.

**Poor in spirit** (v.3). The *anawim* of the Tanakh — the broken, the contrite, the destitute-in-self. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 57:15 — *I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones.* Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 66:2 — *To this man will I look, even to him that is poor and of a contrite spirit, and trembleth at my word.* The poor in spirit is the one whose self-trust has emptied; the kingdom belongs to such because such are the only ones who can receive the kingdom on the kingdom's terms. The Pharisaic-Sadducean righteousness-credential at Matt 3:9 (*we have Avraham (Abraham) to our father*) is the OPPOSITE posture — full of credential, empty of contrition. The kingdom is for the empty.

**Mourn** (v.4). The framework reads this through Ezekiel 9 directly. Ezekiel 9:4 — *Set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof.* The mourners are those whose hearts have been broken by the abominations in the holy city — those who weep over what the system has become, who carry the prophetic sorrow that the leadership-pulpit has voided the heart-Torah. They are MARKED for preservation through the day of judgment. The comfort they shall receive is the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 61 comfort *to all that mourn in Tsion (Zion) ... to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness.* The mourners are the gathered remnant of the prophetic-cry posture.

This is also the Beatitude that closes the door on Christian sentimentalism that reads *blessed are they that mourn* as a generic-grief-comfort. The mourning Yahusha (Jesus) names is the prophetic mourning over abominations — the same mourning that brings the Ezekiel-9 mark.

**Meek** (v.5). Yahusha (Jesus) quotes Psalm 37:11 — *But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.* The Hebrew is *anavim* — close cousin of *anawim* — the humble, the bowed, the unviolent-in-strength. Psalm 37 is the framework Tanakh-context: the meek inherit the LAND (*ha-arets* in the Hebrew, *gēn* in the LXX), and the wicked are cut off from the land in the same psalm. The Beatitude is a gathering-prophecy: the meek inherit the land at the end of the age. The framework reading hears this as direct continuation of the Tanakh's gathering-remnant promises — the gathered seed of promise enters the land at the consummation. The earth-inheritance is not a vague heavenly-realm aspiration; it is the prophetic land-inheritance of the gathered remnant in the millennial kingdom and beyond.

**Hunger and thirst after righteousness** (v.6). *Tsedaqah* — covenant righteousness. The Pharisaic righteousness Yahusha (Jesus) will contrast at v.20 is the counterfeit *tsedaqah* — outside-only performance for credential. The hunger Yahusha (Jesus) names is for the real thing: the heart-righteousness of the Tanakh prophetic stream (Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 51:1's *ye that follow after righteousness*; Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 23:6's *Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS)*; Psalm 42's *as the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee*). The hungering and thirsting one shall be FILLED — the *plēroō* root that will return at v.17 in *fulfil.* What fills is the heart-Torah, the new-heart promise of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:33 and Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27.

**Merciful** (v.7). Covenant *chesed* — steadfast loyalty in love. Not Christian-sentimental softness. The merciful of the Tanakh are those who walk the prophetic *chesed* posture of Hoshea (Hosea) 6:6 (*I desired mercy, and not sacrifice*) and Mikah (Micah) 6:8 (*do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)*). The merciful shall obtain mercy — the law of measure-for-measure the Tanakh repeatedly affirms. The Pharisaic-Sadducean system Yahusha (Jesus) will indict at Matt 23:23 (*ye have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith*) is the antithesis: outside-only Torah-keeping without the *chesed*-substance.

**Pure in heart** (v.8). This is the heart-Torah Beatitude — the direct echo of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:33's *I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts* and Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26's *A new heart also will I give you.* The pure-in-heart shall SEE Elohim (God) — the unique covenantal vision-promise. *No man hath seen Yahuah (the LORD) at any time* (Yochanan (John) 1:18 of the Father, the Formless); the pure-in-heart see the Formed — the Son who is the visible image of the Formless — and through him know the Father. This is the gathered-remnant promise lived out: the new-heart citizens of the kingdom see the King.

**Peacemakers** (v.9). *Shalom*-makers — those who carry the kingdom's wholeness-and-restoration ministry. *They shall be called the children of Elohim (God)* — the lineage-identification of Red Line #11 surfacing in plain text. *Children of Elohim (God)* is *sons of Yahuah,* the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise identification, the called-out for whom names were written from the foundation of the world. The peacemakers are not made children by peacemaking; they are children who make peace because that is what the children do. The Beatitude reveals; it does not manufacture.

**Persecuted for righteousness' sake** (vv.10-12). The closing Beatitude lands on the cost of the gathered-remnant posture. The system that voided the heart-Torah persecutes those who walk it. *So persecuted they the prophets which were before you* — the prophetic-witness lineage from Heb'el (Abel) through the Tanakh prophets through Yochanan (John) the Immerser stands as the testimony of the cost. The framework reads this in the seed-war terms of Genesis 3:15 and Matt 23:34-35: the persecution-pattern runs through the seed-of-the-serpent against the seed-of-the-woman across the ages. The disciples are joining a lineage of suffering witnesses. The reward is the same as the prophets'.

Eight Beatitudes — eight markers of the gathered citizens of the kingdom. The Christian commentary tradition has often listed them as virtues to cultivate. The framework reads them as identification-markers of those the Father has drawn — the roster by which the lost sheep find themselves named. The Beatitudes are not the ladder INTO the kingdom; they are the family-resemblance of the citizens already in it.

## §3. Salt of the earth, light of the world — the prophetic-witness function of the gathered

*Ye are the salt of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? it is thenceforth good for nothing, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven* (vv.13-16).

The framework reads the salt-and-light naming in two registers. First, the immediate naming of the disciples as those who already ARE the salt and the light — not those who are commanded to become so. The Greek *este* is indicative not imperative: *you are.* The Beatitudes have just named who they are; the salt-and-light verses continue the identification. Second, the prophetic Tanakh-background that supplies the substance of what salt and light mean covenantally.

Salt in the Tanakh carries the covenant-preservation register. Vayikra (Leviticus) 2:13 — *every oblation of thy meat offering shalt thou season with salt; neither shalt thou suffer the salt of the covenant of thy Elohim (God) to be lacking from thy meat offering.* Bemidbar (Numbers) 18:19 — *a covenant of salt for ever before Yahuah (the LORD).* 2 Chronicles 13:5 — *Yahuah (the LORD) Elohim (God) of Yashar'el (Israel) gave the kingdom over Yashar'el (Israel) to David for ever, even to him and to his sons by a covenant of salt.* Salt is the preserving-of-covenant substance. The disciples are salt of the earth because they preserve the covenant in the earth — the heart-Torah, the prophetic witness, the Sabbath, the feasts, the names. The system that voids the heart-Torah is the salt that has lost its savour and is cast out. The framework reads the loss-of-savour warning as direct — the leadership-pulpit that voids the commandments has lost the preserving function.

Light in the Tanakh carries the revealing-and-instructing register. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49:6 — *I will also give thee for a light to the goyim (Gentiles), that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth.* The framework reads this carefully. The servant is the light to the goyim — and the goyim, in Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49's frame, are the lands of the scattering where the seed of Yashar'el (Israel) has been dispersed (v.6 follows v.5's *bring Ya'aqov (Jacob) again to him* and *gather Yashar'el (Israel) to him* — the gathering of Yashar'el is the FIRST mission; the light to the goyim is the SECOND mission). The servant illuminates; the light reaches the lost sheep in the lands of their scattering; the gathered remnant carries the light onward. The Christian commentary that flattens Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49:6 into a generic Gentile-inclusion proof-text misses the gathering-substance the prophet is naming. The framework recovers it.

*A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid* — the visible witness of the gathered community. The framework reads this against Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 2:2-3 and Mikah (Micah) 4:1-2 — *the mountain of the house of Yahuah (the LORD) shall be established in the top of the mountains ... and many people shall go and say, Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of Yahuah (the LORD).* The visible city is the eschatological gathering-place; the disciples on the second-Sinai mountain are the present-age foreshadowing of that city.

*Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven* — the framework reads *good works* in the Tanakh-substance of *ma'asim tovim,* covenant-loyal action, heart-Torah lived out. The light is the lived life of the gathered citizens. Not preaching alone, not credential-claim, but the visible covenant-walk. The Christian commentary that has read *good works* against a *faith-not-works* framing has the grammar inverted; the framework reads it as the Beatitudes' postures lived out in visible covenant-faithful action, with the glory going to the Father whose Torah the action carries.

## §4. *Think not that I am come to destroy the law* — the load-bearing anti-antinomian declaration

*Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil. For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled. Whosoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven: but whosoever shall do and teach them, the same shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven* (vv.17-19).

These three verses are the load-bearing centerpiece of Matthew 5 for the framework. The institutional-Christian system that void the commandments has either ignored these verses entirely or developed an elaborate exegesis to neutralize them. The framework reads the verses plainly.

**Verse 17 — *Think not that I am come to destroy.*** Yahusha (Jesus) opens with a PREEMPTIVE denial. *Think not* — *me nomisēte* — *do not even suppose.* He knows the misreading is coming. He is teaching the heart-Torah at a depth no one before him has unfolded, and he knows that some will read his teaching as an attack on the written Torah. So he closes the door before the door can be opened.

*To destroy* — *kataluō* — to dismantle, to take down stone by stone, to abrogate. Yahusha (Jesus) is NOT here to dismantle the Torah. He is NOT here to dismantle the prophets. The Torah and the prophets stand. His ministry is not the demolition of the Tanakh; it is the FULFILLMENT of it.

*To fulfil* — *plēroō* — to fill up, to bring to fullness, to complete in substance. The Christian commentary tradition has frequently read *plēroō* here as *to bring to a close, to satisfy and thereby end* — as if Yahusha (Jesus) saying *I have come to fulfill* means *I am closing the Torah's relevance by exhausting it.* This is the antichrist reading. The Greek *plēroō* throughout the New Testament regularly means *to fill up* (Matt 13:48 — *which, when it was full*; John 16:6 — *sorrow hath filled your heart*; Acts 2:2 — *it filled all the house*). When applied to the law and the prophets, *plēroō* means to bring the substance of the Torah and the prophets to its intended fullness — to fill the form with its substance. Yahusha (Jesus) fills the Torah with the heart-Torah; he does not empty it.

The framework reading turns this verse on its plain meaning: the very Torah that the institutional-Christian leadership-pulpit has taught is *abolished, replaced, fulfilled-and-thereby-ended* is the Torah Yahusha (Jesus) said he came NOT to destroy. The horn's pulpit has spoken precisely the opposite of the King's opening declaration. The contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4 applies the moment the antinomian reading enters: the system that voids the commandments cannot know him.

**Verse 18 — *Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law.*** The eschatological terminus is the dissolution of the present heavens and earth (2 Kefa (Peter) 3:10-13; Revelation 21:1) — the consummation. Until then — *until heaven and earth pass* — every letter of the Torah stands.

*One jot or one tittle.* The *jot* is the *yod* — the smallest letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The *tittle* is the *kotz* — the small ornamental stroke on certain Hebrew letters, the smallest mark on the page. Yahusha (Jesus) is binding himself to the consonantal Hebrew text at the SMALLEST-POSSIBLE-MARK level. Not one letter passes. Not one stroke of one letter passes. The Torah stands in its entirety through this age until the consummation.

*Till all be fulfilled* — *heōs an panta genētai* — *until all things come to pass.* Some Christian commentary has read this as *until I, on the cross, fulfill it.* That reading violates the verse on two counts. First, the parallel clause is *till heaven and earth pass* — the parallel terminus is the eschatological consummation, not the crucifixion. The two clauses bracket the same timeframe. Second, the immediate context (v.19's *whosoever shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven*) presupposes the commandments are STILL operative in the kingdom of heaven Yahusha (Jesus) is establishing. If v.18 meant *until the cross,* v.19's binding force evaporates the moment v.18's terminus is reached. The framework reads the two clauses as parallel: *until the consummation of all things,* the Torah stands.

**Verse 19 — the diagnostic for the institutional-Christian leadership-pulpit.** *Whosoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven.*

The framework reads this verse as the direct diagnostic for the modern Judaizer movement — institutional Christianity's leadership-pulpit. The diagnostic has TWO components: *break* + *teach men so.* The personal breaking is one thing; the breaking-coupled-with-teaching-others-to-break is the diagnostic Yahusha (Jesus) names. The horn's pulpit teaches the congregation that the commandments are abolished — that the Sabbath is moved, that the dietary laws are voided, that the feasts are replaced, that the heart-Torah of obedience is contrary to grace. This is *break and teach men so.* Yahusha (Jesus) names them: *called the least in the kingdom of heaven.*

Two framework observations on the *least* designation:

First — Yahusha (Jesus) does NOT say *not in the kingdom at all.* He says *least* (*elachistos*). Mercy is held open. The institutional-Christian congregation that has been *taught* the antinomian gospel by the pulpit is not categorically out; some hear the Shepherd's voice and turn. The system's teachers are the ones diagnosed first; the followers are victims of inherited interpretation. The system-not-people posture (`_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VII; voice skill *Victims-Not-Enemies*) holds. The pulpit is *least;* the pew may be the gathered hearing through the noise.

Second — the *least* designation still indicts. The framework does not soften the verse into *all paths lead home.* The institutional-Christian leadership-pulpit that teaches the breaking of the commandments is identified by the King as *least in the kingdom of heaven.* This is not honorific. This is diagnostic. The dropping of the commandments from public teaching is named by Yahusha (Jesus) himself as the kingdom-status-diminishing act. The horn's seven centuries of voiding the Torah from the pulpit fall under this verse.

The contrast: *whosoever shall DO and TEACH them, the same shall be called GREAT in the kingdom of heaven.* The reverse direction. Walk the commandments, teach the commandments, and the kingdom-status is *great.* The framework reads this as the standard of the kingdom-citizens-teaching-roster: those who carry the heart-Torah in life and in proclamation are the kingdom's great ones. Mosheh (Moses), the prophets, Yochanan (John) the Immerser, the apostolic witnesses who did and taught, the gathered remnant doing and teaching today.

Matthew 5:19 is the New Testament companion of 1 John 2:3-4. Both verses say the same thing in different grammar: keeping the commandments — and teaching others to keep them — is the proof of knowing him. The master filter of `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §III applies at full force here.

## §5. *Except your righteousness exceed* — the Pharisaic-righteousness contrast

*For I say unto you, That except your righteousness shall exceed the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no wise enter into the kingdom of heaven* (v.20).

V.20 is the bridge-verse from the load-bearing anti-antinomian declaration of vv.17-19 to the six antitheses of vv.21-48. The framework reading hears the verse precisely.

Yahusha (Jesus) is NOT saying *the Pharisaic righteousness is righteousness, but you need MORE of it.* That would be the easy misread. The Pharisaic righteousness is named in this verse as a comparative baseline that must be EXCEEDED to enter the kingdom. The framework reading is sharper: the Pharisaic righteousness is a counterfeit-form of righteousness — outside-only performance, oral-tradition expansion, void-of-heart compliance with display-value. To exceed it is not to do more of it; to exceed it is to walk the heart-Torah it counterfeits.

The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system has already been named at Matt 3:7 as the *generation of vipers* — the seed-war identification of the antichrist-front in its first-century beat (per `pharisaic-sadducean-system-as-judaizer-predecessor` thread proposed at Matt 3). The system Yahusha (Jesus) is now naming at 5:20 is the same system. He is not contrasting himself with a slightly-better-than-average righteousness; he is contrasting the kingdom-righteousness with the Judaizer-predecessor's counterfeit. The pattern Paul will identify in his letters as the *flesh-credential* gospel of the *circumcision party* — the legalistic-performance Judaizers — is the descendant pattern of the Pharisaic righteousness Yahusha (Jesus) names here. Both surface the same theology: do the outside, claim the credential, void the heart-Torah.

The heart-Torah Yahusha (Jesus) will now teach in vv.21-48 is the *exceeding* righteousness. It is the new-heart Torah of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:33, the Spirit-empowered Torah of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27. It walks the commandments not as performance-for-credential but as the natural expression of a new heart. The Beatitudes' *pure in heart* is the substance of the *exceeding.*

The contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4 still applies. The Pharisaic righteousness violates the heart-side of the commandments while preserving the outside-form. The kingdom righteousness walks both. The voice-skill's diagnostic of institutional Christianity as the modern Judaizer movement runs through this verse with full force: the modern Judaizer system follows the same flesh-credential gospel (be baptized, say the prayer, join the church, and you are saved) on a different costume. Both versions are the Pharisaic righteousness — and both will fail to enter the kingdom on Yahusha's (Jesus's) explicit standard at v.20.

## §6. The six antitheses — oral-tradition correction, not Torah supersession

The six *ye have heard ... but I say unto you* exchanges (vv.21-48) are the bulk of Matthew 5 and the most-frequently-misread block in the Sermon on the Mount. The Christian commentary tradition has often read them as Yahusha (Jesus) ABROGATING the Torah by setting his own authority above Mosheh's (Moses's). That reading collapses Yahusha (Jesus) into the *destroy the law* posture he just rejected at v.17. The framework reads the antitheses on a different axis.

**The methodological key.** Yahusha (Jesus) is not contrasting himself with the written Torah of Mosheh (Moses). He is contrasting himself with the ORAL TRADITION of the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system — the *takkanot* and *gezerot,* the fence-around-the-Torah teachings, the truncations and additions that had been laid over the Torah by the rabbinic schools across the centuries. The *ye have heard* formula — *ēkousate* — references what the people HEARD from the synagogue teachers, not what was WRITTEN in the scroll. Yahusha's (Jesus's) *but I say unto you* is the corrective restoration of the original Torah's substance, set against the rabbinic-tradition distortion of it.

The framework reading recovers the pattern at each of the six instances. Yahusha (Jesus) is doing the SAME WORK he did at Matt 4:5-7 with the devil's selective Psalm-91 quote — bringing the OVERRIDING Torah-word into the conversation against the partial-citation distortion. The discipline is the same: harmonization-with-the-whole-canon, restoration of the heart-substance the surface-citation has voided. The antitheses are the Pharisaic-counter applied to six specific cases.

**Antithesis 1 — murder and anger** (vv.21-26). *Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not kill; and whosoever shall kill shall be in danger of the judgment: But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment.*

The oral tradition stopped the murder-commandment at the bodily act. Yahusha (Jesus) restores the heart-substance. The commandment against murder always carried the heart-substance — Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:17, *Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart,* explicitly forbids the seed-of-murder at the heart level. The Torah has always taught what Yahusha (Jesus) is now teaching. He is not innovating; he is restoring what the oral-tradition's narrow surface-focus had left behind.

The escalating-curse pattern (anger / *Raca* / *thou fool*) names three intensities of the heart-violation, all subject to judgment. The altar-rule (vv.23-24 — *leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way; first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift*) names the practical priority — heart-reconciliation precedes worship. The framework reads this against the prophetic Hoshea (Hosea) 6:6 and Mikah (Micah) 6:6-8 stream — the Tanakh-prophets have always taught that sacrifice without heart is rejected. Yahusha (Jesus) speaks the same prophetic stream.

The vv.25-26 imprisonment-warning extends the heart-violation diagnostic into the practical consequence — unresolved conflict leads to judicial consequence in the present age and eschatological consequence at the day.

**Antithesis 2 — adultery and lust** (vv.27-30). *Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery: But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.*

The oral tradition stopped at the act. Yahusha (Jesus) restores the heart-substance. The Torah already named the heart-violation — *Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife* (Exodus 20:17) is the tenth commandment, naming the heart-coveting that precedes the act. Yahusha (Jesus) is unfolding the tenth-commandment-substance into the seventh-commandment-frame. The Torah taught both; the oral tradition had separated them.

The vv.29-30 radical-renunciation rhetoric (*if thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out*) is hyperbolic. The framework rules out the literal-misuse reading (no one has ever taken Matt 5:29-30 as a literal command to mutilate the body; Yahusha (Jesus) does not contradict the Torah's prohibition on bodily mutilation at Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:28). The rhetorical force is the gravity-naming: the heart-stake of the heart-violation is so high that radical-renunciation imagery is the proper register. Cut off the practice; cut off the access-pattern; the heart-Torah requires it.

**Antithesis 3 — divorce** (vv.31-32). *It hath been said, Whosoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a writing of divorcement: But I say unto you, That whosoever shall put away his wife, saving for the cause of fornication, causeth her to commit adultery.*

The Pharisaic schools had developed a body of teaching on Devarim (Deuteronomy) 24:1's *because he hath found some uncleanness in her* — the Hillel school read *some uncleanness* expansively to permit divorce for *any cause* (the wife burns the dinner, for instance); the Shammai school read *some uncleanness* narrowly to permit divorce only for sexual immorality. Yahusha (Jesus) sides with the Shammai-restrictive reading and grounds it in the original Genesis-2 marriage-design (which he will state explicitly at Matt 19:4-9 when the Pharisees raise this exact question).

The framework reads this antithesis as oral-tradition correction by restoration of the original Torah. Devarim (Deuteronomy) 24:1's *writing of divorcement* was a REGULATORY-CONCESSION to the hardness of human hearts (Matt 19:8 explicitly — *Mosheh (Moses) because of the hardness of your hearts suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so*), not a TORAH-MANDATE for permissive divorce. The oral tradition had inverted the concession into a license. Yahusha (Jesus) restores the design — divorce is permitted only for *porneia* (sexual unfaithfulness), and otherwise the original *one flesh* of Bereshit (Genesis) 2:24 stands.

The framework reading also rules out the inverse misuse — the prohibition is not a categorical no-divorce-ever stance (that misreading collapses the *porneia*-exception). The framework reads the verse as restoration of the Torah's covenantal-marriage design with the exception-clause Yahusha (Jesus) himself articulates.

**Antithesis 4 — oaths** (vv.33-37). *Ye have heard that it hath been said by them of old time, Thou shalt not forswear thyself, but shalt perform unto Yahuah (the LORD) thine oaths: But I say unto you, Swear not at all.*

The oral tradition had developed elaborate oath-formulas with gradations of binding — swearing by heaven, by the earth, by Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), by the temple, by the gold of the temple, by one's own head. Yahusha (Jesus) will indict the same gradation-system explicitly at Matt 23:16-22 (*whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor*). The whole apparatus had become a tool for evading the binding-force of oaths through technical loopholes — say the oath in the lower-binding formula and you can break it without penalty.

Yahusha (Jesus) cuts through the apparatus. *Swear not at all* — eliminate the gradation-system entirely. *Let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.* The heart-Torah substance is the integrity of speech: a yes that means yes, a no that means no. The Torah commanded against false oaths (Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:12 — *ye shall not swear by my name falsely*; Bemidbar (Numbers) 30 — vow-binding-and-release). Yahusha (Jesus) does not abolish oath-taking categorically (Paul will swear oaths in his epistles — 2 Cor 1:23; Gal 1:20; he is calling God to witness, which is the legitimate-oath form). He cuts the gradation-evasion-system and restores the heart-substance: speak true, and your speech needs no fortification.

**Antithesis 5 — eye for an eye** (vv.38-42). *Ye have heard that it hath been said, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth: But I say unto you, That ye resist not evil: but whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also.*

The framework reading is critical here because the Christian-pacifist tradition has often misused this antithesis to claim Yahusha (Jesus) abolished the judicial-standard of the Torah. The framework rejects this misuse.

The *eye for an eye* of Exodus 21:24, Vayikra (Leviticus) 24:20, and Devarim (Deuteronomy) 19:21 was a JUDICIAL standard — the *lex talionis* — that LIMITED retaliation to proportional justice administered by the courts. It was a restraining-of-vengeance principle, not a license for personal-vengeance. The judicial system of Yashar'el (Israel) was to administer proportional consequences for harm; the principle prevented escalating-cycle vengeance and prevented under-punishment as well.

The oral tradition had often slipped *lex talionis* out of the judicial sphere and into the personal-revenge sphere — applying it as a green-light for personal retaliation. Yahusha (Jesus) restores the principle to its sphere by cutting off the personal-revenge misuse: *resist not evil* on the personal level; *turn the other cheek* on the personal level; *if any man will sue thee at the law, and take away thy coat, let him have thy cloke also* on the personal level. The personal-disciple ethic is the higher path of non-retaliation, not because the judicial principle is wrong, but because the disciple does not personally administer *lex talionis* — the courts do.

The framework reads this antithesis as preserving the judicial-Torah while restoring the personal-disciple ethic. The *lex talionis* remains operative as the courts' standard (Romans 13:3-4 — the magistrate *beareth not the sword in vain*; he is *the minister of Elohim (God), a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil*). The disciple does not retaliate personally. Both stand together. The Christian-pacifist absolutization of the antithesis collapses one for the other.

The vv.41-42 extension (*whosoever shall compel thee to go a mile, go with him twain; give to him that asketh thee*) names the practical-living register of the heart-Torah generosity. The disciple's posture in the present age — under occupation by Rome, under oppression by the principalities — is to go beyond the demanded compliance and live generously toward even the enemy. This is the *chesed* of the merciful Beatitude lived out.

**Antithesis 6 — love your neighbor and hate your enemy** (vv.43-48). *Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy: But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you.*

The framework reads this antithesis with special care because it surfaces the most-explicit oral-tradition-ADDITION case in the six. *Love thy neighbour* is Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:18 — a real Torah-commandment. *And hate thine enemy* is NOT IN THE TORAH. There is no such commandment in the Tanakh. The oral tradition (and the Qumran community's *War Scroll* explicitly taught a *hate-the-sons-of-darkness* doctrine; some rabbinic strands had similar exclusionary additions) had ADDED *hate thine enemy* to the Torah's *love thy neighbor.* Yahusha (Jesus) is not contradicting Mosheh (Moses); he is correcting an oral-tradition INTERPOLATION that had been spliced onto the Torah.

The framework reads this as the master-case for the oral-tradition-correction pattern. The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system had layered the Torah with additions and subtractions; one of the additions is the *hate thine enemy* clause. Yahusha (Jesus) strips the addition off and restores the Torah's actual command: *love thy neighbor as thyself* (Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:18) is the Torah's word, and Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:34 extends the love-command explicitly to the STRANGER (*the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself*). The Tanakh's command was love-of-neighbor AND love-of-stranger; the oral tradition had inserted a hate-of-enemy clause; Yahusha (Jesus) extends the love-command to the enemy as well — restoring the full sweep of the Torah's heart-substance.

The framework reading also rules out the Christian-pacifist misuse of this antithesis (paralleling the same misuse on antithesis 5). The personal-disciple ethic is love-of-enemy; the judicial-Torah is preserved; the eschatological-judgment of Yahuah (the LORD) against the abominations of the counterfeit-system is preserved (per `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VII — *He brings desolation on her for her abominations*; *He stands on the Mount of Olives to war with all the nations*). The disciple loves the personal enemy and does not personally administer judgment; Yahuah (the LORD) administers eschatological judgment on the systems and the principalities in his timing.

*Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect* (v.48) closes the six antitheses with the heart-Torah climax. *Perfect* — *teleios* — is the Greek translation of the Hebrew *tamim,* which in the Tanakh means *whole, complete, blameless* (Bereshit (Genesis) 17:1 — *I am El Shaddai (the Almighty God); walk before me, and be thou perfect (tamim);* Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:13 — *Thou shalt be perfect (tamim) with Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God)*). The Beatitudes' *pure in heart* finds its completion at v.48. The heart-Torah Yahusha (Jesus) has just unfolded across six antitheses calls the disciple to whole-hearted *tamim*-walk before the Father — the same standard the Torah has always named.

The antitheses, taken together, do not abolish the Torah. They restore it. They strip the oral-tradition distortions off — the truncations of antitheses 1, 2, and 4 (act-only readings), the inversion-of-concession of antithesis 3 (divorce permission), the sphere-mistake of antithesis 5 (personal vs judicial), and the outright addition of antithesis 6 (*hate thine enemy*) — and they reveal the heart-Torah underneath. The Torah is intensified, not abolished. The contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4 stands: the kingdom-righteousness keeps the commandments at heart-depth.

## §7. The Sermon's interior posture — kingdom-citizens already, kingdom-walking now

The framework reading lifts the Sermon's interior architecture for the reader. The Beatitudes name who the citizens of the kingdom already ARE; the salt-and-light verses name what they ARE in the world's frame; the vv.17-19 declaration locks the Torah as the standard the citizens walk; v.20 names the counterfeit-righteousness the citizens exceed; the antitheses unfold what walking the heart-Torah looks like in six specific cases; v.48 names the *tamim*-completion the citizens grow toward.

This is the kingdom-citizens-living-as-kingdom-citizens architecture. The Sermon is not a how-to manual for salvation-earning. It is a description of the gathered remnant's life and the standards by which it walks. The thesis-over-prescription rule of the voice skill applies: the Sermon dismantles the counterfeit-righteousness, names the heart-Torah, invites the gathered remnant to walk what they are. It does not prescribe practice as the program of salvation; it describes the practice the gathered already carry because the Father has drawn them.

The audience-identification posture of the framework's evangelism applies. The Sermon is heard by the disciples and the multitudes. The disciples — the gathered, the called, the lost-sheep-returning — hear themselves named and described. The multitudes — those still on the edge, still being drawn — hear the description and either find themselves recognized in it or do not. *His sheep hear his voice* (Yochanan (John) 10:27); the Sermon is the Shepherd's voice articulated at length. The hearers who hear are the gathered; the hearers who do not hear are revealed by the same hearing-test. This is not a manipulation; this is the prophetic discrimination the kingdom-proclamation accomplishes by its own substance.

The contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4 stands as the master filter (per `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §III and the voice skill's *1234 of Truth*): if any reading of Matthew 5 voids the commandments, the reading is wrong. The framework reads the chapter as the Torah-intensifying, Pharisaic-counterfeit-dismantling, heart-Torah-naming sermon of the second-Sinai King. The reading honors what the text says.

## §8. The eschatological filter — the kingdom of heaven inaugurated, not yet consummated

The Sermon's references to *the kingdom of heaven* (vv.3, 10, 19, 20) sit on the same eschatological filter `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §IV established and matthew-3.md §2 carried forward. The kingdom-at-hand is INAUGURATED with Yahusha's (Jesus's) ministry — it is at hand, available, being entered — but it is not YET CONSUMMATED. The consummation awaits the day of Yahuah (the LORD), the cosmic signs, the Olivet-judgment, the gathering of the wheat, the burning of the tares, the millennial reign of the Messiah on the earth.

The framework reading of the Beatitudes' future-tense promises (*they shall be comforted; they shall inherit the earth; they shall be filled; they shall obtain mercy; they shall see Elohim (God); they shall be called the children of Elohim (God)*) lands in this eschatological frame. The future tenses are the consummation-tenses. The citizens of the kingdom ARE now (present indicative); they shall RECEIVE the consummation-blessings then (future passive). The earth-inheritance of the meek (v.5) is the prophetic-land-inheritance at the consummation. The seeing of Elohim (God) by the pure in heart (v.8) is the eschatological-vision promise the prophets have named.

This eschatological filter rules out the Christian-amillennial collapse of the future-promises into present-spiritual-fulfillments. The meek do not currently *inherit the earth* in a spiritualized internal sense; they shall inherit the earth at the day of Yahuah (the LORD) — the same earth, restored, after the dreadful day has cleansed the abominations. The kingdom of heaven is not entirely realized in the present; the kingdom is at hand, the King is sitting on the mountain, and the consummation is ahead.
