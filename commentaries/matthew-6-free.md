# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 6

Matthew 6 is the middle of the Sermon on the Mount and the practical specification of the kingdom-citizens-righteousness against the Pharisaic-counterfeit form Yahusha (Jesus) named at 5:20. (1) The structure across alms, prayer, and fasting (vv.1-18) is identical: *to be seen of men* identifies the counterfeit, *thy Father which seeth in secret* names the kingdom-citizens form. Yahusha (Jesus) does NOT abolish the Torah practices — Deuteronomy 15:7-8 commands alms; the Tanakh teaches public prayer; Isaiah 58:6-7 names the heart-fast. The same act flows from two different hearts; the motive separates them. (2) The Lord's Prayer (vv.9-13) is given as a *manner,* not a magic-formula. *Hallowed be thy name* reads against Ezekiel 36:23-24, where Yahuah (the LORD) sanctifies his great Name *which was profaned among the heathen* by taking the scattered house *from among the heathen* and gathering them out of all countries into their own land. The sanctifying of the Name happens THROUGH the gathering. *Hallowed be thy name* is a gathering-prayer. *Thy kingdom come* extends the same arc to the Daniel 7:13-14 transfer to *like the Son of Adam* with the clouds (kaph honoring the incarnation; harmonized identification is Yahusha (Jesus) himself). (3) The forgiveness-economy of vv.14-15 collapses the institutional-Christian *once saved, always saved* doctrine. *If ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.* The receiving of forgiveness from the Father is conditioned on the giving of forgiveness to the brother. The Tanakh has been teaching this architecture for centuries: names of the wheat can be blotted out for lawlessness (Exodus 32:33; Ezekiel 18:24; Hosea 7:13). Grace is real, freely offered — and grace can be forfeited by the citizens who refuse to walk its economy. (4) The *single eye / evil eye* of vv.22-23 is NOT a generic spiritual-vision metaphor about looking at the right thing. It is the Hebrew idiom the Tanakh has used consistently — *tov-ayin* (good-of-eye) is GENEROSITY; *ra-ayin* (evil-of-eye) is STINGINESS (Proverbs 22:9, 23:6-7; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 28:22). The literary context locks the reading: vv.22-23 sit between the treasures-in-heaven teaching (vv.19-21) and the Mammon-or-Elohim (God) binary (v.24). The single eye whose body is full of light is the open-handed person laying up treasure in heaven; the evil eye whose body is full of darkness is the grasping person laying up treasure on earth. (5) *Ye cannot serve Elohim (God) and mammon* (v.24) — Matthew leaves *mammon* untranslated. The Aramaic *mamona* names not just wealth but the PERSONIFIED POWER of wealth, a competing principality (Ephesians 6:12). Matthew 6:24 is Eliyahu's (Elijah's) Carmel-confrontation (1 Kings 18:21) in the disciple's own economic life. (6) The closing seek-first priority and trust-economy (vv.25-34) is the manna-pattern of the wilderness lived by kingdom-citizens. The chapter does not abolish a single Torah practice; it names what the practice looks like when the heart that walks it has been brought home.
