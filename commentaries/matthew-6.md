The display-vs-secret diagnostic across three core practices of covenant-life — alms (vv.1-4), prayer (vv.5-8), and fasting (vv.16-18); the Lord's Prayer (vv.9-13) as the kingdom-citizens prayer pattern in seven phrases; the forgiveness-economy and the grace-that-can-be-lost (vv.14-15); treasures-in-heaven (vv.19-21); the good-eye / evil-eye Hebrew idiom of generosity (vv.22-23); the Mammon-or-Elohim binary (v.24); the take-no-thought / seek-first-the-kingdom teaching (vv.25-34). ## §1. The display-vs-secret structure — the unified diagnostic of the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness in practice

*Take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them: otherwise ye have no reward of your Father which is in heaven* (v.1).

Matthew 6 opens with the *do not your alms before men* directive that frames the whole next section. The framework reads vv.1-18 as a unified three-part diagnostic. Yahusha (Jesus) names three core practices of covenant-life — alms, prayer, fasting — and walks the same display-vs-secret contrast through each. The pattern is identical in all three units: the Pharisaic-counterfeit form practices the act *to be seen of men* and receives its reward in the seeing; the kingdom-citizens form practices the act *in secret* and the Father *which seeth in secret* rewards openly.

This is the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness of Matt 5:20 specified in practice. The vv.17-19 declaration locked the Torah as the kingdom's standard; v.20 named the Pharisaic righteousness as the standard the kingdom-citizens exceed; vv.21-48 walked the antitheses to show oral-tradition correction at six cruxes; and now vv.1-18 walk three practical cases where the Pharisaic-counterfeit-form of the practice — alms, prayer, fasting — is contrasted with the kingdom-citizens form. The same diagnostic structure.

The framework reading hears the *to be seen of men* refrain as the seed-marker of the Judaizer-predecessor system. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness is performance-for-credential, public-display for honor-from-men, religious-act for visible-reputation. The kingdom-citizens righteousness is heart-Torah for the Father's eyes alone, with the visible witness of *good works* (Matt 5:16) flowing as the natural outcome — not as the engineered purpose. Matt 5:16's *let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven* is not contradicted by Matt 6:1's *take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them.* Matt 5:16's *seen* is the visible-life-of-covenant-faithfulness that glorifies the Father; Matt 6:1's *seen* is the engineered-public-display that seeks self-glory. The motive separates them. The same act can flow from either motive; the kingdom-citizens form flows from one only.

**Alms in secret** (vv.2-4). *Therefore when thou doest thine alms, do not sound a trumpet before thee, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, that they may have glory of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But when thou doest alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth: That thine alms may be in secret: and thy Father which seeth in secret himself shall reward thee openly.*

The framework reads *the hypocrites* (*hupokritai* — the Greek for stage-actors playing roles for an audience) as the Pharisaic-leadership-system Yahusha (Jesus) has already identified. The *trumpet* image is hyperbolic — there is no historical evidence of literal trumpet-sounding for alms-giving; the rhetorical force is the visibility-engineering pattern. The Pharisaic-counterfeit form announces alms; the kingdom-citizens form practices alms so quietly that *the left hand* does not know what the right does — an idiom for the practice's freedom from any self-witnessing apparatus.

The Tanakh-substance is consistent. Tehillim (Psalm) 41:1 — *Blessed is he that considereth the poor: Yahuah (the LORD) will deliver him in time of trouble*; Mishlei (Proverbs) 19:17 — *He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto Yahuah (the LORD); and that which he hath given will he pay him again.* The Torah commands alms-giving extensively (Devarim (Deuteronomy) 15:7-11; Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:9-10 on the poor's gleaning-rights; Devarim (Deuteronomy) 24:19-22 on the standing-grain remembrances). The framework rejects any reading that turns Matt 6:1-4 into a downgrade of alms-giving. Alms-giving stands. The motive-correction is what the verse specifies.

The Father's reward-economy is named openly: *thy Father which seeth in secret himself shall reward thee openly.* The Father sees the secret act. The reward — in present-age covenant-blessing and in eschatological-consummation — is the Father's response. The Pharisaic-counterfeit form receives the reward-of-being-seen now and forfeits the Father's reward. This is a real exchange, not a metaphor.

## §2. Prayer in secret — the closet posture against the synagogue-and-streets display

*And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly. But when ye pray, use not vain repetitions, as the heathen do: for they think that they shall be heard for their much speaking. Be not ye therefore like unto them: for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him* (vv.5-8).

The framework reads the prayer-unit as the same diagnostic specified at the prayer-practice. The Pharisaic-counterfeit form prays publicly for visibility. The kingdom-citizens form prays in the closet (*tameion* — the inner room, the storeroom; the private space).

Two distortions are named — first, the *to be seen of men* engineered-display the Pharisees walked; second, the *vain repetitions* (*battalogeō* — to babble, to use empty repetition) the heathen practiced as if the volume or repetition manipulated the Deity into response. The framework reads both as the same underlying error: prayer as performance, prayer as transaction, prayer as a thing one DOES TO get an outcome FROM Elohim (God). The kingdom-citizens prayer is the opposite — relational address to the Father who already knows the need.

The framework rules out the misuse of v.6 against public prayer categorically. The Tanakh teaches public prayer extensively — Shlomo's (Solomon's) temple-dedication prayer was public (1 Kings 8); the Tanakh psalms include public-prayer forms; the apostolic communities prayed publicly (Acts 1:14, 2:42, 4:24-31). Yahusha (Jesus) himself prayed publicly at the raising of El'azar (Lazarus) — *Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me. And I knew that thou hearest me always: but because of the people which stand by I said it, that they may believe that thou hast sent me* (Yochanan (John) 11:41-42). The Matt 6:5-6 prohibition is against PERFORMATIVE prayer — prayer engineered for self-display — not against gathered-community prayer. The motive separates the two forms; both are practiced in the kingdom-citizens community.

The *for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him* lock at v.8 frames prayer in the relational register the Lord's Prayer will articulate. Prayer is not the conveying of information the Father lacks; prayer is the kingdom-citizens orientation of the heart toward the Father, the alignment of the citizens' wills with the Father's kingdom, the practice of trust in the Father's foreknowledge and provision. The Tanakh-substance: Tehillim (Psalm) 139 — *thou understandest my thought afar off ... there is not a word in my tongue, but, lo, O Yahuah (the LORD), thou knowest it altogether.* The Father knows before the citizens ask. Prayer continues anyway, because prayer is not transactional.

## §3. The Lord's Prayer — the kingdom-citizens prayer pattern in seven phrases

*After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen* (vv.9-13).

The framework reads the Lord's Prayer as the kingdom-citizens prayer-PATTERN, not a rote-recitation magic-formula. *After this manner* — *houtōs* — *in this way, on this pattern.* Yahusha (Jesus) is teaching the SHAPE of kingdom-citizens prayer; he is not commanding the words be recited verbatim as a religious-practice. The Christian commentary tradition's reduction of the Lord's Prayer to a recitation-formula has often emptied the framework substance; the framework recovers it.

Each phrase is Tanakh-rooted, and each phrase walks a framework axis.

**Phrase 1 — *Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.*** The Father-address is the *Avinu* address of the Tanakh. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 63:16 — *Doubtless thou art our father, though Avraham (Abraham) be ignorant of us, and Yashar'el (Israel) acknowledge us not: thou, O Yahuah (the LORD), art our father, our redeemer.* Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 64:8 — *But now, O Yahuah (the LORD), thou art our Father.* Devarim (Deuteronomy) 32:6 — *Is not he thy Father that hath bought thee?* The kingdom-citizens address the Father in the family-frame the Tanakh has already established.

*Hallowed be thy name* — *hagiazō* — the verb form of *qadosh*, set apart, sanctified. The framework reads this as the direct Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36 substance. Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:23 — *I will sanctify my great name, which was profaned among the heathen, which ye have profaned in the midst of them; and the heathen shall know that I am Yahuah (the LORD), saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD), when I shall be sanctified in you before their eyes.* The sanctifying-of-the-Name happens through the GATHERING of the scattered house. The kingdom-citizens prayer for the sanctification of the Name is the prayer for the gathering. The Name has been profaned in the lands of the scattering; the gathering is the means of its sanctification. The kingdom-citizens are participants in this sanctification through the same gathering. *Hallowed be thy name* is a gathering-prayer.

**Phrase 2 — *Thy kingdom come.*** The eschatological-kingdom prayer. The Daniel 2:44 *the Elohim (God) of heaven shall set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed* substance; the Daniel 7:13-14 transfer of the everlasting kingdom *with the clouds of heaven* to *like the Son of Adam* (kaph-comparative preserved per Red Line #12 as incarnation-honoring; the harmonized identification is Yahusha (Jesus) himself). The kingdom-citizens at Matt 6:10 pray for the consummation of the kingdom Yahusha (Jesus) refused to short-circuit at Matt 4:8-10. The kingdom is at hand (Matt 3:2, 4:17); the citizens pray for its consummation; the timing is the Father's.

**Phrase 3 — *Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.*** The Torah-restoration-on-earth prayer. The framework reading: the Father's will is the heart-Torah, the *good and acceptable and perfect will* (Romans 12:2) Mosheh's (Moses's) Torah and the prophets articulate. In heaven this will is fully obeyed; on earth, the will is contested by the rebellion-of-the-watchers (per `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration` thread from Matt 4). The kingdom-citizens pray for the consummation when the earth carries the heaven-pattern. This is the eschatological-millennial-kingdom prayer in another register.

**Phrase 4 — *Give us this day our daily bread.*** *Ton arton hēmōn ton epiousion dos hēmin sēmeron.* *Epiousios* is an unusual Greek word — possibly *for the coming day,* possibly *for the existing day,* possibly *necessary-for-being.* The framework reads the bread-petition against the manna-pattern of Exodus 16. The wilderness manna was DAILY provision — *that I may prove them, whether they will walk in my law, or no* (Exodus 16:4). The double-portion was for Shabbat. The daily-trust-on-the-daily-provision is the framework's *daily bread.* Yahusha (Jesus) at Matt 4:4 cited Devarim (Deuteronomy) 8:3 against the first wilderness-failure: *man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of Elohim (God).* The kingdom-citizens prayer for daily-bread carries both registers — the physical provision and the every-word-from-the-mouth-of-Elohim Torah-bread the Father feeds the citizens with.

**Phrase 5 — *And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.*** The forgiveness-petition. *Opheilēmata* — debts in the moral-account sense; the Aramaic *chov* and Hebrew *chov* carry the same range. The framework reads this against the Yom Kippur architecture — the sin-debt at the heart of the covenant-relationship requires atonement, the kingdom-citizens carry the atonement-substance Yahusha (Jesus) embodies, and the citizens themselves carry the forgiveness-economy in their own dealings with debtors. The *as we forgive* parallel structure is conditional, and Yahusha (Jesus) will lock the condition explicitly in vv.14-15 (treated at §4 below). The kingdom-citizens carry a forgiveness-economy or they forfeit their own forgiveness.

**Phrase 6 — *And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.*** *Mē eisenenkēs hēmas eis peirasmon, alla rhusai hēmas apo tou ponērou.* The wilderness-testing motif from Matt 4 surfaces. *Peirasmos* — testing, trial. Ya'aqov (James) 1:13 will clarify that Elohim (God) does not tempt anyone; the petition is not about *do not cause us to be tempted* but *do not lead us into the testing-place beyond what we can bear* — the same protective-care of 1 Corinthians 10:13's *Elohim (God) is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able.* *Deliver us from evil* — *apo tou ponērou* — possibly *from the evil one* (Satan, the devil who tested Yahusha (Jesus) at Matt 4). The framework reads the petition as kingdom-citizens prayer for protection from the principalities-under-watcher-rebellion-administration that contest the kingdom in the present age (per `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration` thread). The disciples Yahusha (Jesus) called and is now teaching face the same kind of testing he just faced in the wilderness; the petition is for the Father's protective leading.

**Phrase 7 — *For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.*** The doxology. Manuscript-evidence-wise this clause is absent from some early manuscripts and present in others; the framework does not adjudicate the textual question but reads the doxology's substance as Tanakh-anchored in 1 Chronicles 29:11 — *Thine, O Yahuah (the LORD), is the greatness, and the power, and the glory, and the victory, and the majesty: for all that is in heaven and in the earth is thine; thine is the kingdom, O Yahuah (the LORD), and thou art exalted as head above all.* The kingdom-citizens close the prayer-pattern with the kingdom-power-glory-eternity attribution to the Father; the same attribution David made at the dedication of the temple-materials. The kingdom-citizens prayer ends in the kingdom-acknowledgment.

The seven phrases together form the kingdom-citizens prayer pattern: address-the-Father, sanctify-the-Name (which is the gathering), pray-for-the-kingdom-coming (the consummation), pray-for-the-will-done-on-earth (the Torah-restoration), petition-for-daily-provision (the manna-trust), petition-for-forgiveness-in-the-forgiveness-economy, petition-for-protection-from-the-testing, close-in-the-doxology. This is the framework-grammar of kingdom-citizens prayer.

## §4. The forgiveness-economy and the grace-that-can-be-lost

*For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses* (vv.14-15).

Yahusha (Jesus) appends a direct elaboration of the *as we forgive our debtors* petition of v.12. The framework reads vv.14-15 as the conditional-forgiveness teaching the Christian commentary tradition has frequently softened by various exegetical maneuvers. The framework reads the verses plainly.

The structure is conditional. *If ye forgive ... your heavenly Father will also forgive.* *If ye forgive NOT ... neither will your Father forgive.* The receiving of forgiveness from the Father is coupled to the giving of forgiveness to the brother. This is not a transactional-quid-pro-quo earning-of-forgiveness; this is the relational-economy of the kingdom. The kingdom-citizens carry the forgiveness-substance in both directions — receiving from the Father, extending to the brother — or the substance is broken at the kingdom-citizens end and the receiving-side closes.

The framework reads this verse as a member of the `grace-from-name's-sake` thread (LANDED S74) and a member of the larger pattern `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §III names: the *grace, and the opportunity that can be lost.* The Father's forgiveness is real grace — freely offered, freely received. But the kingdom-citizens' continuing-reception of the Father's forgiveness is contingent on the kingdom-citizens carrying the forgiveness-economy in their own walk. The grace is offered; the forfeit is real. Hoshea (Hosea) 7:13 anchors the broader pattern — *though I have redeemed them, yet they have spoken lies against me* — the redeemed who depart from the forgiveness-economy lose what they had. Yahusha (Jesus) will return to this pattern explicitly at the parable of the unforgiving servant (Matt 18:23-35), where the king reverses the forgiveness when the forgiven servant fails to extend forgiveness.

The institutional-Christian *once saved, always saved* doctrine (the eternal-security teaching) collapses on Matt 6:14-15 immediately. The verse names a continuing-conditional structure to the receiving of forgiveness. The framework reads this as one of the load-bearing NT witnesses to the `grace-and-the-opportunity-that-can-be-lost` thread that DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md §9 identifies as a free-tier curated thread for the chapter-end card — the framework-distinctive teaching the app surfaces back to the Tanakh roots (Hoshea (Hosea) 7:13, Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 18, Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 33:12-13, Exodus 32:33, Tehillim (Psalm) 69:28, Devarim (Deuteronomy) 28, Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 18:7-10, Bemidbar (Numbers) 14, 1 Samuel 15, Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 20:33-38) on every chapter that touches it.

## §5. Fasting in secret — the third practice in the three-part diagnostic

*Moreover when ye fast, be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly* (vv.16-18).

The third unit in the display-vs-secret diagnostic. Fasting is a Tanakh-substantial practice — the Day-of-Atonement fast is commanded (Vayikra (Leviticus) 16:29-31, 23:27-32), the Yom Kippur affliction-of-the-soul; the prophets called fasts at moments of national repentance (Yo'el (Joel) 1:14, 2:15; Yonah (Jonah) 3:5-9); David fasted (2 Samuel 12); Daniel fasted (Daniel 9, 10); Yahusha (Jesus) himself just fasted forty days (Matt 4:2). Fasting stands. The motive-correction is what the verse specifies.

The Pharisaic-counterfeit form practiced fasting with *disfigured faces* — ostentatious-pallor for visible-recognition. The framework reads the *disfigure* (*aphanizō* — to make unsightly, to render unrecognizable) as the visibility-engineering pattern in another register. The kingdom-citizens form practices fasting with *anoint thine head, and wash thy face* — the normal-grooming pattern that conceals the fast from public view. The act is between the citizen and the Father.

The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58 fast-substance teaching hovers over this passage. *Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke? Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house?* (Isaiah 58:6-7). The framework reads Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58 as the Tanakh-charter of the kingdom-citizens fast — the fast joined to *chesed*-action toward the oppressed, the heart-Torah expressed in care for the poor. The Matt 6:16-18 form-correction sits inside the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58 substance-correction. The kingdom-citizens fast is not the Pharisaic-display-pallor; it is the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 58 heart-fast.

The three-part display-vs-secret diagnostic closes at v.18. Alms, prayer, fasting — three Tanakh-substantial practices the kingdom-citizens walk; three motive-corrections against the Pharisaic-counterfeit form. The Father's reward-economy is named in all three: *thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly.* The Pharisaic-counterfeit form has its reward in the seeing-of-men; the kingdom-citizens form has its reward in the Father's seeing.

## §6. Treasures in heaven, the eye as lamp — generosity in the Hebrew register

*Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal: But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal: For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also* (vv.19-21).

The framework reads the treasures-in-heaven teaching as the continuation of the display-vs-secret diagnostic at the broader economic-orientation level. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness accumulates visible-credential — religious-display, public-recognition, social-honor — which is *treasure on earth.* The kingdom-citizens-righteousness accumulates *treasure in heaven* — the eschatological-reward the Father stores for the citizens who walk the kingdom in secret. Earth-treasure is vulnerable to moth (*sēs*), rust (*brōsis* — corrosion, eating-away), and thieves; heaven-treasure is permanent.

The framework reads the Tanakh-stream behind the teaching. Mishlei (Proverbs) 11:4 — *Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death.* Mishlei (Proverbs) 23:4-5 — *Labour not to be rich ... riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.* Tehillim (Psalm) 49 — the futility-of-riches psalm: *for he seeth that wise men die, likewise the fool and the brutish person perish, and leave their wealth to others.* The Tanakh's wisdom-tradition has always named the earth-treasure economy as futile in the day of wrath. Yahusha (Jesus) walks the same stream.

*For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also* — the diagnostic-verse. The framework reads this as the heart-orientation-test. The disciple discovers what the heart actually serves by tracing where the treasure has been laid up. This is the heart-Torah diagnostic in another register.

*The light of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light. But if thine eye be evil, thy whole body shall be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great is that darkness!* (vv.22-23).

The framework reads vv.22-23 with particular care because the Christian commentary tradition has often spiritualized the *single eye / evil eye* contrast into a generic spiritual-vision metaphor (*single eye* as singleness-of-spiritual-focus). The framework recovers the Hebrew-idiom.

The *good eye* and *evil eye* in Hebrew idiom describe GENEROSITY and STINGINESS. Mishlei (Proverbs) 22:9 — *He that hath a bountiful eye* — the Hebrew is *tov-ayin,* literally *good of eye* — *shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.* The good-eye person is generous; the *bountiful eye* is the generous heart. Mishlei (Proverbs) 23:6 — *Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye* — Hebrew *ra-ayin,* literally *evil of eye* — *neither desire thou his dainty meats: For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.* The evil-eye person is stingy; the heart is grasping; the hospitality is performative-but-not-substantive. Devarim (Deuteronomy) 15:9 — *thine eye be evil against thy poor brother* — the same idiom in Torah-context, naming the stinginess-toward-the-poor that the Torah forbids. Mishlei (Proverbs) 28:22 — *He that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him.*

Greek *haplous* (*single,* Matt 6:22) and *ponēros* (*evil,* Matt 6:23) carry the same idiomatic range when translating Hebrew thought. *Haplous* in the LXX renders *tam* — sound, whole, generous-without-double-mind. *Ponēros* renders *ra* — evil, including the grasping-stinginess sense in the Mishlei (Proverbs) passages.

The framework reading: Matt 6:22-23 sits BETWEEN the treasures-in-heaven teaching (vv.19-21) and the mammon-or-Elohim binary (v.24). The literary context locks the reading. The *single eye* whose body is *full of light* is the GENEROUS person who lays up treasure in heaven through generous-giving; the *evil eye* whose body is *full of darkness* is the STINGY person who lays up treasure on earth through grasping. The Tanakh-idiom precisely fits the surrounding teaching on the disciple's economic orientation. The Christian-spiritualization of *single eye* into a generic spiritual-vision-purity loses the framework substance entirely.

The *how great is that darkness!* lamentation at v.23 closes the unit. The framework reads this as direct: the stingy-heart of the disciple who professes to serve the Father but accumulates earth-treasure is a darkness that has invaded the disciple's whole life. The light that is supposed to be in the body has become darkness through the disciple's mammon-orientation. The intensity-marker (*how great is that darkness!*) names the gravity.

## §7. Mammon as principality; take no thought; seek first the kingdom

*No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve Elohim (God) and mammon* (v.24).

The Mammon-or-Elohim binary closes the economic-orientation block. The framework reads *mammon* — Aramaic *mamona,* untranslated by Matthew because the term names the personified-power-of-wealth, not just the wealth itself. *Mammon* is treated as a master, a competing lord. The disciple either serves Elohim (God) or serves Mammon; the two are mutually exclusive.

The framework reads Mammon against the `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration` thread from Matt 4. The principalities and powers that fell with the watcher-rebellion administer the kingdoms-of-this-age in temporary delegation. Mammon is one of the systemic-powers in that administration — wealth treated as the organizing-power of life. The devil at Matt 4:8-10 offered the kingdoms-and-glory in exchange for worship; Mammon at Matt 6:24 offers a similar trade at the individual-economy level. The disciple cannot serve both.

The Tanakh-stream is consistent. The first commandment — *thou shalt have no other gods before me* (Exodus 20:3) — rules out competing masters categorically. Devarim (Deuteronomy) 6:5 — *thou shalt love Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might* — the whole-heart standard the dual-allegiance breaks. Eliyahu (Elijah) at Mount Carmel — *how long halt ye between two opinions? if Yahuah (the LORD) be Elohim (God), follow him: but if Baal, then follow him* (1 Kings 18:21) — the same forced-choice the disciple now faces between Elohim (God) and Mammon.

*Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment? Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?* (vv.25-26).

The take-no-thought teaching extends from the mammon-binary. The kingdom-citizens who serve Elohim (God) and not Mammon walk a trust-economy — confidence in the Father's covenant-provision, freedom from the anxious-grasping that characterizes the Mammon-service. *Take no thought* — *mē merimnaō* — *do not be anxious, do not be torn-apart-with-care.* The verb does not abolish prudent planning; it abolishes anxious-grasping.

The fowls-of-the-air and lilies-of-the-field illustrations name the Father's covenant-provision economy. The fowls do not plant-and-store; yet the Father feeds them. The lilies do not toil-or-spin; yet the Father clothes them in glory exceeding Shlomo's (Solomon's). The Tanakh-substance is Tehillim (Psalm) 147:9 (*He giveth to the beast his food, and to the young ravens which cry*); Iyov (Job) 38-41 (the creation-care testimony from the whirlwind); Tehillim (Psalm) 104 (the comprehensive creation-care psalm). The Father's covenant-care extends to the fowls and the lilies; how much more to the kingdom-citizens.

The framework reads the *O ye of little faith* (v.30) as kindness, not rebuke. Yahusha (Jesus) names the disciples' faith-as-small without dismissing them; the teaching is FOR the small-faith to grow. The same Yahusha (Jesus) who at Matt 4:18-22 called fishermen out of their nets is now teaching them to trust the Father for the daily-provision they used to secure by their own labor.

*But seek ye first the kingdom of Elohim (God), and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you. Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof* (vv.33-34).

The closing verse of the chapter locks the framework's central kingdom-citizens orientation. *Seek ye FIRST the kingdom of Elohim (God), and his righteousness.* The priority-clarification. The *first* (*prōton*) is not chronological-first-and-then-the-other; it is priority-first-as-organizing-principle. The kingdom-citizens orient first toward the kingdom — the gathering, the consummation, the Father's will done in earth — and toward *his righteousness* — the heart-Torah Yahusha (Jesus) has been unfolding throughout the Sermon. The daily-provision is *added unto you* in the framework of that priority.

The framework reads *his righteousness* as the kingdom-citizens *tsedaqah*-orientation specified. The hungering-and-thirsting Beatitude (5:6) finds its present-age practice here: seek his righteousness, walk the heart-Torah, and the Father provides what the day requires. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness (5:20) is not what the citizens seek; the Father's righteousness — the heart-Torah, the kingdom-walking — is.

The *take no thought for the morrow* and *sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof* close the Sermon-middle on the day-by-day frame. The kingdom-citizens walk one day at a time in the Father's provision. The manna-pattern. The wilderness-trust the Yashar'el (Israel) failed at and Yahusha (Jesus) walked perfectly at Matt 4. The disciples now learn the same daily-trust the Father has been teaching since the wilderness.
