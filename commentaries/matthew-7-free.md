# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 7

Matthew 7 closes the Sermon on the Mount, and it contains one of the most load-bearing passages in the entire New Testament for the framework's contrary-to-Torah test. (1) *Judge not* (v.1) is constantly weaponized as a categorical prohibition on all moral discernment, but the reading collapses on the next sentence — *with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged* presupposes that the disciple WILL judge; the variable is the standard. The mote-and-beam parable restores brother's-mote-removal (v.5). The Torah commanded the rebuke at Leviticus 19:17. And v.6 demands active discrimination outright: *Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine.* The discernment is intensified, not abolished. (2) Ask-seek-knock (vv.7-11); Luke's parallel at 11:13 names what *the good things* most centrally are — the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) who writes the heart-Torah on the citizens, fulfilling Ezekiel 36:26-27. (3) The Golden Rule (v.12) is named as the summary of *the law and the prophets* — the heart-substance of the Tanakh-revelation, NOT a substitute for it. The King who just bound himself and the kingdom-citizens to every jot and tittle at 5:17-19 is now naming the heart of what the Torah and prophets express. (4) The narrow-gate (vv.13-14) is remnant-language. The framework refuses both universalist-softening and institutional-Christian-mass-salvation. *Many* on the wide way are those who break-and-teach (Matthew 5:19) and the *Lord, Lord* community of vv.21-23. (5) *Beware of false prophets* (vv.15-16) draws Deuteronomy 13:1-3 forward — signs and wonders are real; the prophet is still false if he teaches against the commandments. Torah-fidelity is the test, not supernatural credential. (6) Verses 21-23 dismantle the institutional-Christian gospel at the kingdom-entry level. *Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father.* The community Yahusha (Jesus) is describing confesses him *Lord, Lord* in emphatic acclamation, prophesies in his name, casts out devils in his name, does *many wonderful works* in his name — and hears *I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.* The Greek for *iniquity* is *anomia* — *a-* (without) plus *nomos* (law) — TORAHLESSNESS. The same word stands behind 1 John 3:4's *sin is the transgression of the law* and 2 Thessalonians 2:7's *mystery of iniquity* (*mystērion tēs anomias*). This IS the diagnostic for the institutional-Christian antinomian-gospel community in advance: verbal confession, supernatural ministry under the name, and TORAH-VOIDING walking. Grace can be forfeited at full New Testament voltage. 1 John 2:3-4 and Matthew 7:21-23 are the same diagnostic in two registers. (7) The two builders (vv.24-27) close the Sermon: both HEAR; only the hearer-AND-doer stands. The doing IS the foundation-establishing act, not a downstream-fruit-that-may-or-may-not-follow.
