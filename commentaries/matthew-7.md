## §1. *Judge not* — the framework reading against the Christian-tolerance-absolutization

*Judge not, that ye be not judged. For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again. And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother's eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye? Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye? Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother's eye. Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest they trample them under their feet, and turn again and rend you* (vv.1-6).

The framework reads *Judge not* with particular care because the Christian commentary tradition has often weaponized v.1 as a categorical prohibition on moral-discernment of any kind — *Jesus said do not judge, therefore the believer must extend non-judgmental acceptance to every behavior, every doctrine, every teacher, every claim.* That reading collapses the verse against its own context and against the rest of Yahusha's (Jesus's) ministry.

The verse is followed IMMEDIATELY by *for with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.* The next sentence presupposes that the disciple WILL judge — the question is the STANDARD of the judgment. *With what judgment* (*en hō krimati*) — *with what KIND of judgment* — names the variable. The disciple is being instructed on HOW to judge, not commanded to abolish all judgment.

The mote-and-beam parable then specifies the form. The hypocritical-judgment pattern: the disciple sees the *mote* (*karphos* — a small piece of dry matter, a splinter) in the brother's eye while harboring a *beam* (*dokos* — a large load-bearing wooden beam) in his own. The corrective is NOT *therefore stop seeing the brother's mote.* The corrective is *cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother's eye.* The judgment of the brother's fault is RESTORED, after the disciple has dealt with his own. The standard is consistent self-examination FIRST, then helpful correction of the brother. This is the same Tanakh-pattern of Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:17 — *thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him* — which commands the rebuke, not the abolition of the rebuke. The mote-removal is part of the brotherhood-love-pattern.

The pearls-before-swine verse (v.6) then specifies the OUTWARD-direction discrimination. *Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine.* The disciple is commanded to KNOW the difference between *the holy* and *the dogs,* between *pearls* and *swine.* This requires active moral-discernment — exactly what the modern Christian-tolerance-absolutization rules out. The disciple who cannot tell the holy from the dogs cannot obey v.6. The framework rules out any reading of vv.1-5 that contradicts v.6 in the same paragraph.

The framework reading of vv.1-6 as a unified teaching: judge the brother with the same standard you apply to yourself; deal with your own faults first; then help the brother with his; and recognize the difference between the brother-receiving-correction and the dogs-and-swine who reject the holy. The discernment is INTENSIFIED, not abolished. The framework reads this against the same `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VII posture — *speak truth in love* (Ephesians 4:15); the prophetic-cry against abominations is preserved; the system-not-people distinction holds.

The voice-skill's *Victims-Not-Enemies* posture aligns with this reading. The verse does not abolish the dismantling of false systems; it constrains the disciple to walk the dismantling with self-examined integrity. The horn's pulpit, the modern Judaizer movement, the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness Yahusha (Jesus) has been indicting since Matt 3 — all stand named. The kingdom-citizens name them with the beam first removed. This is the framework reading.

## §2. Ask, seek, knock — the Father's covenant-care for the gathered

*Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you: For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened. Or what man is there of you, whom if his son ask bread, will he give him a stone? Or if he ask a fish, will he give him a serpent? If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children, how much more shall your Father which is in heaven give good things to them that ask him?* (vv.7-11).

The framework reads vv.7-11 as continuation of the Lord's Prayer's relational-trust frame at Matt 6:9-13. The kingdom-citizens approach the Father in confidence because the Father's covenant-care is sure. The threefold *ask / seek / knock* is the progressively-deeper kingdom-citizens prayer-posture, and the threefold *given / find / opened* names the corresponding Father's-response.

The earthly-father / heavenly-Father comparison at vv.9-11 walks the *kal va'chomer* rabbinic argument-form (the *light and weighty* — if a lesser case holds, how much more the greater). If earthly-fathers — who are *evil* (the framework reads this not as total-depravity-Calvinism but as the post-Genesis-3 condition of fallen humanity living under the watcher-rebellion administration; even fallen-and-flawed earthly fathers give *good gifts* to their children) — know how to give good gifts, *how much more* shall the heavenly Father who is unfallen and unflawed give *good things* to the citizens who ask. The argument-shape locks the kingdom-citizens prayer-confidence.

Luke 11:13 carries the parallel teaching and specifies the *good things*: *how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?* The framework reading: THE good gift is the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit). The kingdom-citizens prayer-petition is most centrally for the indwelling Spirit who writes the heart-Torah on the citizens (per Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:33; Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27). The Father's covenant-care for the gathered is most fundamentally the gift of the Spirit who enables the kingdom-walking. The Matt 7:11 *good things* — *agatha* — names the kingdom-substance the Father gives, with the Spirit at the center.

The framework rules out the prosperity-gospel misuse of vv.7-11. The *ask / seek / knock* is not a wealth-and-blessing-claim formula. The Father gives *good things,* and the framework definition of *good* is the kingdom-substance — the Spirit, the heart-Torah, the gathering-promise, the daily-bread of Matt 6:11, the deliverance from the evil one of Matt 6:13. The vv.7-11 verses sit inside the Sermon's broader teaching on the kingdom-citizens orientation; they do not turn into a wealth-promise template apart from that teaching.

## §3. The Golden Rule named by Yahusha (Jesus) as *the law and the prophets*

*Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets* (v.12).

The framework reads v.12 as a load-bearing Torah-affirmation. Yahusha (Jesus) himself names the principle *do unto others as ye would that they should do to you* as the summary of *the law and the prophets* — *houtos gar estin ho nomos kai hoi prophētai.* The Torah and the prophets. The whole Tanakh-revelation.

Two framework observations.

First, the principle is NOT a replacement of the Torah's specific commandments. Yahusha (Jesus) has just spent Matt 5:17-19 binding himself and the kingdom-citizens to every jot and tittle of the Torah; he has spent Matt 5:21-48 restoring the heart-Torah at six cruxes; he has spent Matt 6:1-18 specifying the kingdom-citizens form of three core practices. The Golden Rule at Matt 7:12 cannot abolish what the King has just affirmed. The Golden Rule is the HEART-SUBSTANCE of the Torah and the prophets in summary-form, not a substitute for them. The Christian commentary tradition that flattens the Golden Rule into a generic ethical principle replacing the Torah's specifics — *the Torah was complicated but Jesus simplified it to just love your neighbor* — collapses the verse against the Sermon's whole architecture. Yahusha (Jesus) is naming the heart of what the Torah and prophets EXPRESS; he is not voiding what they express.

Second, the parallel rabbinic-formulation. The school of Hillel (a generation before Yahusha (Jesus)) had a famous version of the Rule: *what is hateful to you, do not do to your fellow; this is the whole Torah, the rest is commentary — go and learn.* The framework reads Hillel's formulation as a Tanakh-substantial summary the Pharisaic-tradition itself had carried; Yahusha (Jesus) walks the same teaching, articulated in the positive-direction (*do unto others* rather than *do not do*). This is significant because it shows Yahusha (Jesus) is not contradicting the Tanakh-substance of the rabbinic-teaching at its best; he is contradicting the oral-tradition DISTORTIONS layered over the Torah (the antitheses of Matt 5:21-48). Where the rabbis articulated the heart of the Torah faithfully, Yahusha (Jesus) walks the same teaching. The framework distinguishes between the Tanakh-substance the Pharisaic-tradition occasionally preserved and the oral-tradition distortions the Pharisaic-system overlaid; Matt 7:12 is the kind of substance-affirmation the framework can name without inheriting the system's distortions.

The framework reads Matt 7:12 as the heart-Torah's social-application summary. The Tanakh's commandments toward the neighbor — Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:18's love-of-neighbor, Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:34's love-of-stranger, the Tanakh's prohibitions on theft and false-witness and covetousness, the prophets' demands for justice-and-mercy-and-humble-walk — these are summed in the Golden Rule's substance. The kingdom-citizens who walk the Torah's commandments are walking what the Golden Rule names; the kingdom-citizens who walk the Golden Rule are walking the Torah's commandments. The two are mutually-coextensive at the heart-substance level.

## §4. The narrow gate and the few — remnant-language

*Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it* (vv.13-14).

The framework reads the narrow-gate teaching as remnant-language and rules out the Christian-universalist softening that has often diluted it.

The structure is binary and quantitatively-asymmetric: TWO gates, TWO ways, TWO destinations. The wide gate / broad way leads to destruction; *many there be which go in thereat.* The strait gate / narrow way leads to life; *few there be that find it.* The numerical-asymmetry is explicit. The framework will not soften it.

The Tanakh-stream behind the teaching is the remnant-of-Yahuah (the LORD) pattern. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 10:21-22 — *The remnant shall return, even the remnant of Ya'aqov (Jacob), unto the mighty El (God). For though thy people Yashar'el (Israel) be as the sand of the sea, yet a remnant of them shall return: the consumption decreed shall overflow with righteousness.* The remnant is by definition a subset; the *consumption decreed* names the larger consequence. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 4:2-3 — the surviving remnant of Tsion (Zion) and Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) called holy. Tzefanyah (Zephaniah) 3:12-13 — *I will also leave in the midst of thee an afflicted and poor people, and they shall trust in the name of Yahuah (the LORD). The remnant of Yashar'el (Israel) shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies.*

The framework reads the narrow-gate against the `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §V pattern — Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 9 (the mark on those who sigh and cry), Zecharyah (Zechariah) 13:8-9 (the third part left alive), Zecharyah (Zechariah) 12:10 (the surviving third looks on the pierced one). The remnant is consistently named as a minority — the third-part, the marked, the few who find. Yahusha (Jesus) at Matt 7:13-14 walks the same prophetic stream.

This rules out two readings the framework refuses:

First — the Christian-universalist softening that reads *many enter the wide way* as *many believers go astray on side-issues* while preserving an implicit-universal-salvation in the background. The framework rejects this. *Destruction* (*apōleia*) is the framework's destruction — exclusion from the kingdom, the second death, the post-harvest disposal of the tares and the unfruitful wheat. *Many* are there. The verse does not soften.

Second — the institutional-Christian-mass-salvation reading that assumes the church-pew is automatically on the narrow way. The framework rejects this too. The Sermon on the Mount has just specified at Matt 5:19 the diagnostic of breaking-and-teaching-others-to-break (the modern Judaizer movement's leadership-pulpit identified) and will specify at Matt 7:21-23 the *Lord-Lord* community on the wide way. The mere claim-of-Yahusha (Jesus) is not the proof-of-the-narrow-way; the heart-Torah-walking is.

The *few there be that find it* lock at v.14 names the actual finding-pattern. *Find* (*heuriskō*) — they are FOUND in the narrow way; the way does not just open for any who claim it. The framework reads this against Red Line #11's hearing-reveals-not-makes pattern: those who find are those whose names were written in the Book of Life from the foundation of the world AND who walk the heart-Torah through the post-harvest sifting. The narrow gate corresponds to the remnant-of-the-gathered.

## §5. False prophets and the fruit-test — the seed-war diagnostic

Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit. A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit. Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them* (vv.15-20).

The framework reads vv.15-20 as the seed-war diagnostic of Bereshit (Genesis) 3:15 specified at the false-prophet-identification level. The false prophets *come to you in sheep's clothing* — they LOOK like the gathered remnant, they wear the sheep-costume — *but inwardly they are ravening wolves.* The framework's seed-war architecture is direct here: the seed-of-the-serpent walks among the seed-of-the-woman in disguise, and the disciple's task is to discern.

The wolf-in-sheep's-clothing is a wolf; the costume does not make the wolf into a sheep. The fruit-test exposes the underlying nature.

## §6. *Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord* — the load-bearing anti-antinomian declaration of the Sermon

*Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity* (vv.21-23).

This is the most load-bearing passage in Matthew 7 for the framework — and one of the most load-bearing passages in the whole New Testament for the contrary-to-Torah test of 1 John 2:3-4. The institutional-Christian system has either ignored this passage or developed elaborate exegesis to neutralize it. The framework reads it directly.

**Verse 21.** *Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.*

Yahusha (Jesus) opens with a direct denial of the verbal-confession-as-entry-criterion gospel. *Saying Lord, Lord* — the verbal-confession of Yahusha (Jesus) as Lord — is NOT THE ENTRY CRITERION to the kingdom. The Pauline citation often used to claim it IS (Romans 10:9-10's *if thou shalt confess with thy mouth*) cannot stand alone as the entry-criterion against Matt 7:21's explicit statement; Romans 10 is teaching one piece of a broader pattern, and the framework reads Paul as harmonizing with the King's own teaching (`scripture-twisting-and-the-harmonization-discipline` thread; Red Line #2's *no Christian doctrine can stand as load-bearing in our work*; the antinomian flattening of Romans 10 is the antichrist reading).

The actual entry-criterion is *doing the will of the Father.* The framework reads the *will of the Father* as the heart-Torah-walking. The Father's will is articulated in the Torah and the prophets — the same Torah and prophets Yahusha (Jesus) bound himself to at Matt 5:17-19; the same Father's-will the Lord's Prayer petitions for at Matt 6:10 (*thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven*). The doer-of-the-Father's-will enters the kingdom; the verbal-confessor without the doing does not.

This is the contrary-to-Torah test articulated at the kingdom-entry-criterion level. The voice-skill's *1234 of Truth* (1 John 2:3-4) and the present verse say the same thing: *He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar* (1 John 2:4); *Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord ... but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven* (Matt 7:21). Both verses indict the verbal-confession-without-the-walking pattern. The kingdom-citizens are doers of the Father's will; the *Lord, Lord* sayers who do not do are not in.

**Verse 22.** *Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works?*

The framework reads this verse with particular care because of who Yahusha (Jesus) is describing. These are not the obvious-pagan unbelievers. These are people who:

1. Confess Yahusha (Jesus) as Lord — twice, in emphatic acclamation.
2. Prophesied IN HIS NAME. They were active in supernatural-prophetic ministry under his name.
3. Cast out devils IN HIS NAME. They were active in deliverance-ministry under his name.
4. Did many wonderful works (*dunameis pollas* — many works of power) IN HIS NAME.

This is the IMPRESSIVE religious-ministry-performance community. They are not just church-attenders; they are active in supernatural-gifted-ministry under Yahusha's (Jesus's) name. By every visible-credential the modern Christian-charismatic and Christian-evangelical communities track, these people pass the test. Yahusha (Jesus) describes them in the future tense — *many will say* — meaning this is a prophetic-warning about a future community that WILL show up at the judgment-day claiming the credentials.

The framework reads this as a direct prophecy about the institutional-Christian community in its modern forms. The charismatic-movement that prophesies in Yahusha's (Jesus's) name; the deliverance-ministries that cast out demons in Yahusha's (Jesus's) name; the miracle-and-healing ministries that do wonderful works in Yahusha's (Jesus's) name — ALL of these are the population Yahusha (Jesus) is describing at v.22. The credentials are impressive. The verbal-confession is explicit. The supernatural-ministry is active. And Yahusha (Jesus) will say to *many* of them — not some, not a few, MANY — what he says at v.23.

**Verse 23 — the load-bearing diagnostic.** *And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.*

The framework reads every word of this verse.

*I never knew you* — *oudepote egnōn humas.* Yahusha (Jesus) names them as never-known. This is not *I knew you once and now reject you;* this is *I never knew you at all.* The relational-knowing — the *yada* of the Tanakh covenant-relationship — never existed between Yahusha (Jesus) and these supernatural-ministry-performers. Their entire ministry under his name was performed without his actual knowing of them. The credentials were real; the supernatural-acts apparently produced their effects; but the underlying knowing-relationship was absent.

How is this possible? The framework reading: the principalities-under-watcher-rebellion-administration can produce supernatural effects through humans who invoke Yahusha's (Jesus's) name without belonging to him. The Tanakh teaches this — Devarim (Deuteronomy) 13:1-3 names the false prophet whose sign-or-wonder COMES TO PASS while teaching the people *let us go after other gods.* The signs are real; the prophet is false. The supernatural-effect is not the proof of relationship; the heart-Torah-walking is.

*Depart from me* — *apochōreite ap' emou.* The framework reads this against Red Line #11's wheat-vs-unfruitful-wheat distinction. The *depart from me* language matches the post-harvest sifting of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 20:38 — *I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me.* This is the discrimination-of-the-wheat AFTER the gathering. The people at Matt 7:22 had the appearance of being wheat — they were gathered, they were active in supernatural-ministry under Yahusha's (Jesus's) name — but the post-harvest sifting reveals them as having been on the wide way. They are dismissed in the discrimination.

*Ye that work iniquity* — *hoi ergazomenoi tēn anomian* — *the WORKERS OF LAWLESSNESS.* This is the diagnostic phrase. *Anomia* — *without-law* — literally means *TORAHLESSNESS* in the framework reading. The Greek *a-* (without) + *nomos* (law) names the without-Torah condition. The people Yahusha (Jesus) is dismissing are the workers of TORAHLESSNESS. They prophesied, cast out demons, and did wonderful works WHILE LIVING WITHOUT THE TORAH. Their ministry was active; their Torah-walking was absent. They confessed Yahusha (Jesus) verbally; they violated the commandments practically.

The framework reads v.23 as the DIRECT diagnostic for the institutional-Christian antinomian-gospel community. The community formed under that teaching confesses Yahusha (Jesus) as Lord, often manifests supernatural-gifts in his name, and lives WITHOUT the Torah. Yahusha (Jesus) calls them *workers of torahlessness.* He says *I never knew you.* He says *depart from me.*

This is the most-frequently-evaded verse in the institutional-Christian commentary tradition. The Christian commentaries have variously: read *iniquity* as some-other-sins-not-Torah-violation (the very translation flattening of *anomia* into *iniquity* obscures the without-Torah substance); read *I never knew you* as applying to some-fringe-community-of-extreme-cases (preserving the assumption that the institutional-mainstream is safe); read the verse as a warning to apply to ourselves while extending mass-comfort to the church-pew (collapsing the warning's force). ## §7. The two builders — *na'aseh v'nishma* hearing-AND-doing as the kingdom-citizens foundation

*Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock. And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it* (vv.24-27).

The framework reads the closing parable as the Sermon-on-the-Mount summary at the foundation-level. The DIFFERENTIATOR between the wise and the foolish is *heareth ... AND doeth them.* Both the wise and the foolish HEAR the sayings of Yahusha (Jesus); the wise hears AND does; the foolish hears AND does not. The hearing alone is not the foundation; the hearing-AND-doing is.

The framework reads this against the Tanakh-pattern of *na'aseh v'nishma* — Exodus 24:7's *we will do, and we will hear* (KJV: *All that Yahuah (the LORD) hath said will we do, and be obedient*). The Yashar'el (Israel) at Sinai responded to the Torah-presentation with the *we-will-do-and-hear* commitment — putting the DOING first. The framework reads Matt 7:24-27 as the same pattern in the Sermon-on-the-Mount frame. The kingdom-citizens hear-AND-do; the *Lord-Lord* community of v.22 hears-without-doing; the difference is the kingdom-entry-criterion.

The rock-foundation imagery walks the Tanakh-substance of Yahuah (the LORD) as the Rock. Devarim (Deuteronomy) 32:4 — *He is the Rock, his work is perfect: for all his ways are judgment.* Tehillim (Psalm) 18:2 — *Yahuah (the LORD) is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my Elohim (God), my strength, in whom I will trust.* The foundation-rock the wise man builds upon is Yahuah (the LORD) himself — and the access to that foundation is through hearing-and-doing the heart-Torah Yahusha (Jesus) has unfolded in the Sermon. The doing-of-the-sayings is what locks the citizen into the rock-foundation.

The sand-foundation is the hearing-without-doing pattern. The framework reads this as the institutional-Christian-pulpit-and-pew arrangement specifically. The pew HEARS the sayings every Sunday — *love your enemies, judge not, lay up treasure in heaven, seek first the kingdom, do unto others* — and the pulpit (the modern Judaizer movement) teaches the congregation that the heart-Torah's specific commandments are abolished by grace. The hearing happens; the doing does not. The foundation is sand. *And great was the fall of it* (v.27) — the framework reads the fall against the post-harvest sifting, the day of Yahuah (the LORD), the eschatological-test that exposes what each builder built upon.

The Tanakh-pattern of *na'aseh v'nishma* — DO-and-HEAR — appears in the Sermon-on-the-Mount summary as a deliberate inversion-of-the-Reformation-pattern-that-collapses-them. The Reformation hierarchy (faith > works, hearing > doing, grace > obedience) Yahusha (Jesus) explicitly rejects in his closing parable. The hearer-AND-doer is the wise; the hearer-without-doer is the foolish; the doing is not a downstream-fruit-that-may-or-may-not-follow; the doing IS the foundation-establishing act.

## §8. *Authority, not as the scribes* — the second-Sinai voice locked in the multitudes' astonishment

*And it came to pass, when Yahusha (Jesus) had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine: For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes* (vv.28-29).

The Sermon closes on the multitudes' response. *Astonished* — *ekplēssomai* — struck out of themselves, knocked-loose-from-the-default. The teaching landed at a register the listeners had not heard before.

The framework reads the *authority, and not as the scribes* contrast as the second-Sinai motif locking back at chapter-end. The scribes taught via tradition-chain authority — *Rabbi-X said in the name of Rabbi-Y who said in the name of...* The chain-of-tradition was the authority-source. Yahusha (Jesus) taught DIRECTLY — *but I say unto you* — in the King's own voice. The authority IS the Torah's authority (the King has bound himself to every jot and tittle at 5:18), but spoken in the voice of the One whose Voice gave the Torah at Sinai.

The framework's nature-of-God reading at Matt 1:23 and 3:17 surfaces again here. The Voice that spoke the Torah at Sinai was the Formed — the Son drawn from the Formless — who has now taken flesh and speaks the heart-Torah directly. The *authority* the multitudes recognize at v.29 is the same authority that thundered at Sinai. The scribes lacked it because the scribes lacked the relationship-with-the-Voice the Sinai-Speaker is now demonstrating in person.

The chapter closes on the recognition-of-authority by the multitudes — not on their committed-discipleship. The multitudes are astonished; some will follow; many will not. The Sermon has been delivered. The wide gate / narrow gate distinction has been named. The post-harvest sifting has been previewed. The two builders have been contrasted. The Father's-will-doing as the kingdom-entry-criterion has been locked. The multitudes hear the authority; what they do with it now reveals what they are.
