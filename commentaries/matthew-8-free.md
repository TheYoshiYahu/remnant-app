# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 8

Matthew 8 is the post-Sermon healing ministry — the credentialing-miracles that authenticate the kingdom-at-hand proclamation. (1) The FIRST miracle Matthew records after the Sermon is the leper-cleansing, and it ends with an explicit Torah-affirming instruction. The King's touch does NOT defile him — the leper is CLEANSED instead; the Formed-in-flesh is the cleansing-source whose touch reverses the defiling-flow. He then commands the cleansed man to *shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Mosheh (Moses) commanded* — the Leviticus 14:2-3 cleansing-procedure stands. The institutional-Christian narrative of Yahusha (Jesus) as the abolisher-of-the-Torah collapses on Matthew 8:4 at the very inauguration of the healing ministry. (2) The centurion is NOT the Gentile-inclusion proof-text. Luke's parallel (Luke 7:4-5) supplies the background: he is a God-fearer (likely a proselyte) embedded in the Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) gathering — exactly the kind of figure the framework would expect in the post-Assyrian-resettlement mixed-territory Galilee. He has ATTACHED himself to the covenant-community. (3) *Many shall come from the east and west* (v.11) is NOT the Christian-replacement proof-text. *East and west* is the Tanakh-prophetic-gathering directional-formula: Isaiah 43:5-6 (*I will bring thy seed from the east, and gather thee from the west*), Isaiah 49:12, Psalm 107:2-3, Zechariah 8:7-8. The many who come are the GATHERED SCATTERED SEED of Yashar'el (Israel) coming home; they sit down with the patriarchs in the kingdom because they ARE the seed of the patriarchs gathered home. The *children of the kingdom cast out into outer darkness* (v.12) names the post-harvest sifting of Ezekiel 20:34, 37-38 — those who held position-by-lineage-claim without the heart-Torah walk are purged from among the gathered wheat. The verse does not categorically dismiss the Yahudi (Jewish) community; the framework's posture toward Yahudah (Judah) is unwavering. (4) The evening-healings (vv.16-17) are anchored to Isaiah 53:4 — the Suffering-Servant's bearing of our infirmities is operative across the Messianic ministry, not at the cross alone; it joins Isaiah 35:5-6's Messianic-credential-signature (*the blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up*) that Yahusha (Jesus) will cite explicitly back to Yochanan (John) the Immerser at 11:4-5. (5) The storm-stilling (vv.23-27) is the Formed-cloud-rider's authority over creation in flesh — the same Voice that *stillest the waves* of Psalm 89:9, *maketh the storm a calm* of Psalm 107:29, *shut up the sea with doors* of Job 38:8. The disciples' question — *what manner of man is this?* — is the recognition-of-the-authority-pattern beginning to land. (6) The Gergesene exorcism reaches the eastern-tribal-inheritance of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasheh (Manasseh) — territory scattered in the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE. The herd of swine confirms the territory's first-century administration was not Torah-walking; the townspeople's economic-rejection ends the visit, but two delivered demoniacs remain as witnesses for any in the eastern tribes who later turn.
