## §1. The leper healed — the Torah-affirming first-miracle of the post-Sermon ministry

*When he was come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him. And, behold, there came a leper and worshipped him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean. And Yahusha (Jesus) put forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will; be thou clean. And immediately his leprosy was cleansed. And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, See thou tell no man; but go thy way, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Mosheh (Moses) commanded, for a testimony unto them* (vv.1-4).

The framework reads the leper-healing as a deliberate first-miracle after the Sermon on the Mount. The Sermon delivered the heart-Torah teaching across Matt 5-7; the ministry now turns to the credentialing-miracles that authenticate the kingdom-at-hand proclamation. And the FIRST miracle Matthew records after the Sermon is a healing that ends with an explicit Torah-affirming instruction.

The leper's approach itself is framework-significant. He addresses Yahusha (Jesus) as *Lord* (*kurios*) and confesses both Yahusha's (Jesus's) WILL (*if thou wilt*) and CAPACITY (*thou canst make me clean*) as the operative variables. The faith-confession is in framework form — recognition of authority, recognition of capacity, submission to the King's will. The leper does not demand; he requests.

Yahusha (Jesus) responds with a TOUCH. The framework notes this carefully. The Torah's purity-architecture (Vayikra (Leviticus) 13-14) treated leprosy as a defiling condition; touching a leper would render the toucher ritually impure under the Torah's ordinary purity-rules. Yahusha (Jesus) touches the leper and is NOT defiled — instead, the leper is CLEANSED. The framework reads this as the King-of-the-kingdom demonstrating that his presence is the ANTITHESIS of the defiling-flow; he is the cleansing-source whose touch reverses the impurity rather than absorbing it. This is consistent with the Formless-Formed framework reading — Yahusha (Jesus) is the Formed who carries the cleansing-substance the temple-architecture only signified.

But — and this is the framework-significant move — Yahusha (Jesus) immediately commands the cleansed man: *go thy way, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Mosheh (Moses) commanded, for a testimony unto them.* The cleansed leper is sent to the priest. The Vayikra (Leviticus) 14 cleansing-procedure is to be fulfilled. The offerings (two birds, cedar wood, scarlet, hyssop; later two male lambs and a ewe lamb; the cleansing rituals; the seven-day waiting; the priest's pronouncement) are to be carried out. The Torah-procedure stands. Yahusha (Jesus) does not say *now you're cleansed by my touch and the Mosheh (Moses) procedure is no longer needed.* He says *fulfill what Mosheh (Moses) commanded.*

The framework reads this as the first-miracle LOCK on the Sermon-on-the-Mount's heart-Torah teaching at the practice level. Yahusha (Jesus) just bound himself and the kingdom-citizens to every jot and tittle (Matt 5:18); his first-miracle directs the recipient of his cleansing to the Torah's procedure. The Christian commentary tradition that has framed Yahusha's (Jesus's) ministry as a transition-away-from-the-Torah collapses on Matt 8:4. The Torah-affirming first-miracle disproves the antinomian-construction at the very inauguration of the post-Sermon ministry.

The *for a testimony unto them* (*eis marturion autois*) phrase carries additional weight. The priest receives a sign: a Messianic-Torah-healer is at work. The framework reads this as deliberate witness-to-the-priesthood. The Yahudah (Judah)-priesthood operating under the Mosheh-procedure receives a leper-claim that fulfills the protocol — and the priest who certifies the cleansing-pronouncement is also being shown that the cleansing came from outside the temple-system. The witness is positioned for the priesthood's response. Some priests will hear; some will not. The Acts 6:7 *a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith* records the eventual response of some.

The framework reads Matt 8:1-4 as the deliberate Torah-affirming inauguration of the post-Sermon ministry. The pattern is locked at the first miracle: healing-and-Torah-fulfillment, not healing-and-Torah-abolition.

## §2. The centurion's servant — *great faith* in framework reading and *many from east and west* as the gathering-prophecy

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) was entered into Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), there came unto him a centurion, beseeching him, And saying, Lord, my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, grievously tormented* (vv.5-6).

The framework reads the centurion-pericope with particular care because the Christian commentary tradition has often used this passage as a Gentile-inclusion proof-text — *Jesus heals a Roman, therefore the gospel was always for Gentiles regardless of covenant lineage.* The framework recovers the gathering-substance the passage actually carries.

**Who the centurion is.** Luke 7:1-10 supplies background data Matthew condenses. The centurion *loveth our nation, and he hath built us a synagogue* (Luke 7:5). The Jewish-elders intercede on his behalf, naming his standing in the local Yashar'el (Israel) community. He is a God-fearer (perhaps a proselyte) embedded in the Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) gathering — exactly the kind of figure the framework would expect in the post-Assyrian-resettlement-and-proselytization mixed-territory Galilee. The framework reads him as a man who has ATTACHED himself to the covenant-community as far as a non-Israelite could under the second-temple architecture, not as a random pagan-Roman with no covenant-relationship.

This matters for the framework's reading of v.10. He is not the test-case for *Gentile-faith-replaces-covenant-people-faith.* He is the test-case for what KIND of faith stands out in the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community — and the contrast with what *Yashar'el (Israel)* lacks in his comparison is the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness Yahusha (Jesus) has been indicting throughout Matt 3-7, not the covenant-lineage of the scattered seed.

**The centurion's authority-recognition** (vv.8-9). *Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed. For I am a man under authority, having soldiers under me: and I say to this man, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.*

The framework reads the centurion's faith-confession in framework terms. The centurion recognizes Yahusha's (Jesus's) AUTHORITY (*exousia*) as the mechanism of the cosmic-rule structure. *I am a man under authority* — the centurion sees himself in the chain-of-command of Roman military-administration. *Having soldiers under me* — he gives orders that operate at distance. He maps this onto Yahusha (Jesus): the King has authority over disease, over the principalities-and-powers, over the cosmic-administration; the King speaks and the effect carries at distance. This is precisely the *authority* the multitudes recognized at Matt 7:28-29 — and the centurion has internalized it more deeply than any of the Yahudim (Jewish) leaders Yahusha (Jesus) has encountered.

**The framework crux — *I have not found so great faith, no, not in Yashar'el (Israel)* (v.10).** The Christian commentary tradition has often read this as a generic put-down of the Yahudim (Jewish) community — *Jesus said even the Gentiles have more faith than the Jews.* The framework reads this against the specific contemporary context.

Yahusha (Jesus) is not contrasting the centurion's faith with the covenant-substance of Yashar'el (Israel) as a people. He is contrasting it with the FAITH-LEVEL among the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) — and the dominant religious-leadership-system in contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) is the Pharisaic-Sadducean system Matt 3:7 identified as the *generation of vipers,* Matt 5:20 contrasted at the righteousness-frame, and Matt 23 will indict fully. The centurion's authority-recognition exceeds what the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness produced. This is the framework reading: the centurion's faith stands out against a leadership-system that had been credentialing-without-trusting for centuries. The verse indicts the system, not the covenant-substance of the people.

The framework also notes the centurion's confession-from-OUTSIDE the system. He has not been credentialed by the Pharisaic-establishment; he has not gone through the rabbinic-education-pathway; he is operating from God-fearer-attached-to-the-community standing. Yet his recognition of the King's authority exceeds the credentialed-insiders'. The framework reads this as the seed-war pattern showing through: the wheat is in unexpected places; the credentials of the system are not the markers of the gathering.

**The major framework crux — *many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Avraham (Abraham), and Yitschaq (Isaac), and Ya'aqov (Jacob), in the kingdom of heaven. But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth* (vv.11-12).** This passage has been weaponized for centuries as the Christian-replacement proof-text: *the Gentiles (the many from east and west) will replace the Jews (the children of the kingdom) in the kingdom because the Jews rejected Jesus.* The framework rejects this read at every level.

**The Tanakh-stream behind *east and west.*** Yahusha (Jesus) is quoting the gathering-prophecy stream of the Tanakh — the prophets' explicit promise to GATHER THE SCATTERED SEED of Yashar'el (Israel) from the lands of the dispersion.

- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 43:5-6 — *Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east, and gather thee from the west; I will say to the north, Give up; and to the south, Keep not back: bring my sons from far, and my daughters from the ends of the earth.* The *east and west* is the gathering-of-the-seed.
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 49:12 — *Behold, these shall come from far: and, lo, these from the north and from the west; and these from the land of Sinim* (probably Aswan / southern Egypt). The directional-formula names the gathering-of-the-dispersed.
- Tehillim (Psalm) 107:2-3 — *Let the redeemed of Yahuah (the LORD) say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy; And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.* The gathering-from-all-directions psalm.
- Zekharyah (Zechariah) 8:7-8 — *I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country; And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem).*

The *east and west* phrase Yahusha (Jesus) uses at Matt 8:11 is the Tanakh-prophetic-gathering directional-formula. The many who *shall come from east and west* are the GATHERED SCATTERED SEED OF YASHAR'EL (ISRAEL) coming home from the lands of their dispersion. They sit down with Avraham (Abraham), Yitschaq (Isaac), and Ya'aqov (Jacob) in the kingdom because they are the SEED of the patriarchs gathered home. The framework reading recovers the gathering-substance the Christian inclusion-read has obscured for centuries.

**The *children of the kingdom* — who are they?** *Hoi huioi tēs basileias.* The phrase names those with the STANDING of kingdom-children — those who held the position-by-lineage-claim, those who considered themselves children of the kingdom by virtue of their relationship to the covenant. In the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) context Yahusha (Jesus) addresses, this includes the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership-system AND those within the present-Yahudim (Jewish) community who relied on lineage-claim alone without the heart-Torah walk. Matt 3:9 was explicit: *think not to say within yourselves, We have Avraham (Abraham) to our father.*

The framework reads v.12 against Red Line #11's wheat-vs-unfruitful-wheat distinction directly. The *children of the kingdom* who are cast out into outer darkness are NOT all of present-day Yahudah (Judah); they are the LINEAGE-CLAIM-ALONE members of the covenant-community whose fruit was not consistent with the covenant-substance. The post-harvest sifting per Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 20:33-38 separates these from the wheat. The same dismissal-pattern Matt 7:23 names (*I never knew you; depart from me, ye that work iniquity*) applies — but at Matt 8:12 the discrimination is among present-day covenant-claimants who relied on lineage-credential alone, while Matt 7:23 was the parallel discrimination among supernatural-ministry-performing antinomian Lord-Lord confessors.

The framework's posture on Yahudah (Judah) per `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §VI is preserved: pro-Yahudah without qualification; Jew-hate is never justifiable; Yahudah (Judah) is the surviving covenant-people-with-identity. The Matt 8:12 verse does NOT contradict this posture; it discriminates among the COVENANT-CLAIMANTS at the post-harvest sifting per the King's own standard (heart-Torah-walking), which is the same standard Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 20 applies in the gathering. Some among the present-Yahudim (Jewish) community walk the heart-Torah and stand among the wheat at the sifting; some rely on lineage-claim alone and are sifted out. The same is true for the scattered seed coming home — some hear and respond and join the wheat; some do not. The post-harvest sifting cuts across both groups.

**What this verse rules out:**

- Any reading that flattens *many from east and west* into Gentile-inclusion-replacing-Israel. The Tanakh-prophetic-gathering substance is the framework reading.
- Any reading that treats *children of the kingdom cast out* as the collective dismissal of present-day Yahudah (Judah). The post-harvest sifting per Red Line #11 discriminates within the covenant-community, not against it categorically.
- Any reading that softens v.12's *outer darkness* and *weeping and gnashing of teeth* into a general-judgment language without specific application. The discrimination is real and the dismissal is real.

The framework's *Adam, the Tares, and the Sons of Belial* posture from the voice skill's *Three Categories* / Red Line #9 applies: the framework dismantles systems, not peoples; the diagnosis of the Pharisaic-leadership-system is system-not-people; the Matt 8:11-12 verse is the post-harvest sifting at the framework level, not the categorical-rejection-of-Yahudah (Judah) at the people level.

## §3. Kefa's (Peter's) mother-in-law and the evening healings — Isaiah 53:4 Messianic-credential

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) was come into Kefa's (Peter's) house, he saw his wife's mother laid, and sick of a fever. And he touched her hand, and the fever left her: and she arose, and ministered unto them. When the even was come, they brought unto him many that were possessed with devils: and he cast out the spirits with his word, and healed all that were sick: That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Yeshayahu (Isaiah) the prophet, saying, Himself took our infirmities, and bare our sicknesses* (vv.14-17).

Matthew quotes Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 53:4 as the Messianic-credential of the healing ministry. The framework reads this carefully.

Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 53 is the Suffering-Servant prophecy. The whole chapter names the Servant who bears the griefs and sorrows, who is wounded for transgressions, who carries the iniquity of the people. The Christian commentary tradition reads this in atoning-substitution frame, often centered on the cross. The framework reads the chapter in the framework's nature-of-God grid: the Servant is the Formed (Yahusha (Jesus) in flesh, per Matt 1:23 and 3:17) who bears the redemptive substance the Father has appointed; the bearing-of-infirmities is operative across the Messianic ministry, not just at the cross — the healings of Matt 8:14-17 are part of the same bearing-of-our-griefs-and-sorrows the prophet names.

Matthew's application of Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 53:4 to the HEALING ministry (rather than to the cross alone) opens a framework-aligned reading the institutional-Christian tradition has not always carried. The Servant bears infirmities in flesh-presence with the suffering, in healing-ministry to the multitudes, in the credentialing-miracles that authenticate the kingdom-at-hand. The framework reads this as the prophet's prophetic-arc fulfilled across the ministry, not collapsed into a single moment.

The vv.14-16 healings of Kefa's (Peter's) mother-in-law and the evening crowds illustrate the credentialing. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 35:5-6 (*the eyes of the blind shall be opened, the lame leap as an hart*) is the Messianic-sign Yahusha (Jesus) authenticates by his healing — the same credential he will cite at Matt 11:2-5 when Yochanan (John) sends from prison to ask. The healings are not random-acts-of-compassion; they are the Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 35 / Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 53 prophetic-credential being publicly displayed.

The framework also reads the *cast out the spirits with his word* phrase at v.16. The principalities-and-powers under the watcher-rebellion-administration (per `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration` thread from Matt 4) are recognizing and submitting to the King's word. The same authority-pattern Matt 7:28-29 named at the teaching level operates at the deliverance level. The kingdom-at-hand proclamation is being authenticated by the kingdom-at-hand power-demonstration.

## §4. Would-be followers — *the Son of Adam hath not where to lay his head* + *let the dead bury their dead*

*Now when Yahusha (Jesus) saw great multitudes about him, he gave commandment to depart unto the other side. And a certain scribe came, and said unto him, Master, I will follow thee whithersoever thou goest. And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of Adam hath not where to lay his head. And another of his disciples said unto him, Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father. But Yahusha (Jesus) said unto him, Follow me; and let the dead bury their dead* (vv.18-22).

Per Red Line #12, *Son of Man* at v.20 is restored to *Son of Adam* — the lineage-weight preserved. Yahusha (Jesus) identifies himself as the Son of Adam, the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise, the gospel's framework-identification carried since Matt 1's genealogy and the Lukan parallel's *Adam, which was the son of Elohim (God)* (Luke 3:38).

**First would-be follower** (vv.19-20). A scribe approaches with a confession of all-in commitment. Yahusha (Jesus) does not embrace; he warns. *The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of Adam hath not where to lay his head.* The framework reads this as a deliberate-disclosure of the present-age cost. The King has no home in the present age. The kingdoms-of-this-world are under the watcher-rebellion-administration (per the Matt 4 thread); the King is operating in the territory-of-his-enemy through the visible-creation that he himself called into being but that has been temporarily delegated to the rebel-administrators. The scribe's *whithersoever thou goest* needs to grasp this: there is no resting-place in the present age. The would-be follower is being filtered.

**Second would-be follower** (vv.21-22). A disciple requests permission to go bury his father before following. Yahusha (Jesus) replies: *Follow me; and let the dead bury their dead.*

The framework reads this verse with particular care. The Torah's fifth commandment (Exodus 20:12 — *Honour thy father and thy mother*) and the Tanakh's burial-piety obligations (Bereshit (Genesis) 50:5 — *let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father*; Tovit (Tobit) and other Apocrypha burial-piety contexts) treat the burial of a parent as a serious covenant-obligation. The framework rules out the Christian-commentary-misuse of this verse as a categorical anti-family-honor teaching — Yahusha (Jesus) elsewhere explicitly indicts the *Corban* (Mark 7:9-13) practice that voids the fifth commandment. He is not abolishing parent-honor.

The framework reads the verse as the kingdom-priority-clarification at the urgent moment. Two readings of *let the dead bury their dead* hold within the framework:

First — the more probable reading: *let the SPIRITUALLY DEAD (those who have not heard the kingdom-call) bury their physically-dead. You who have heard come and follow now, while the kingdom is being proclaimed.* The kingdom-at-hand moment is finite; the would-be disciple's pull to handle the family-burial first risks losing the moment. The framework reads this as urgency-of-the-kingdom-proclamation, not abolition of burial-piety.

Second — the alternative reading: the disciple's father may not yet be dead; the disciple is asking permission to wait until his father dies (possibly years away) to free himself for ministry. In which case Yahusha (Jesus) is declining the indefinite-postponement-of-following, not commenting on burial-of-the-already-dead.

Either reading preserves the framework's posture: parent-honor remains a Torah-commandment; the kingdom-at-hand call carries an urgency that cannot be indefinitely-deferred. The framework rejects the Christian-commentary that has flattened this verse into anti-family rhetoric.

## §5. Stilling the storm — the Formed-cloud-rider authority over creation

*And when he was entered into a ship, his disciples followed him. And, behold, there arose a great tempest in the sea, insomuch that the ship was covered with the waves: but he was asleep. And his disciples came to him, and awoke him, saying, Lord, save us: we perish. And he saith unto them, Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith? Then he arose, and rebuked the winds and the sea; and there was a great calm. But the men marvelled, saying, What manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him!* (vv.23-27).

The framework reads the stilling-of-the-storm against the Tanakh-pattern of the Formed-cloud-rider's authority over creation. Tehillim (Psalm) 89:8-9 — *O Yahuah Elohim (LORD God) of hosts, who is a strong Yah like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee? Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.* Tehillim (Psalm) 107:23-30 — *They that go down to the sea in ships ... He maketh the storm a calm.* Iyov (Job) 38:8-11 — *Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, as if it had issued out of the womb? ... And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed.*

The framework's nature-of-God reading at Matt 1:23 and 3:17 surfaces here. The Voice that spoke creation into being (Bereshit (Genesis) 1) and that rules the sea in the Tanakh-pattern is the Formed — the Son drawn from the Formless — who now sleeps in flesh in a fishing-boat during a storm and then wakes to rebuke the winds and the sea. The disciples' question *what manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him?* is the recognition-of-the-authority-pattern that links Yahusha (Jesus) directly to the Tanakh's creation-ruling Voice. They are beginning to see.

The framework reads *O ye of little faith* (v.26) consistent with the Matt 6:30 same phrase — kindness-with-correction, not dismissal. The disciples' faith is small but real; the King is teaching them to grow in the trust the Lord's Prayer's *deliver us from evil* and *give us this day our daily bread* petitions presuppose. The storm is a teaching-moment.

The Tanakh-stream of Yonah (Jonah) 1 should also be noted. Yonah (Jonah) slept through the storm; the sailors woke him; the storm calmed when he was thrown into the sea. The Matt 8:23-27 episode echoes Yonah (Jonah) deliberately and inverts the resolution-mechanism — Yonah (Jonah) was the cause-of-the-storm whose removal calmed it; Yahusha (Jesus) is the AUTHORITY-OVER-THE-STORM whose word calms it. The framework reads this as the *greater than Yonah (Jonah)* foreshadowing that Yahusha (Jesus) will name explicitly at Matt 12:41.

## §6. The Gadarene demoniacs and the swine — the eastern-tribal-inheritance and the unclean-economy

*And when he was come to the other side into the country of the Gergesenes, there met him two possessed with devils, coming out of the tombs, exceeding fierce, so that no man might pass by that way. And, behold, they cried out, saying, What have we to do with thee, Yahusha (Jesus), thou Son of Elohim (God)? art thou come hither to torment us before the time? And there was a good way off from them an herd of many swine feeding. So the devils besought him, saying, If thou cast us out, suffer us to go away into the herd of swine. And he said unto them, Go. And when they were come out, they went into the herd of swine: and, behold, the whole herd of swine ran violently down a steep place into the sea, and perished in the waters. And they that kept them fled, and went their ways into the city, and told every thing, and what was befallen to the possessed of the devils. And, behold, the whole city came out to meet Yahusha (Jesus): and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart out of their coasts* (vv.28-34).

The framework reads the Gergesene/Gadarene exorcism episode in the eastern-tribal-territory geographic register.

**The territory.** *The country of the Gergesenes* (or *Gadarenes* in some manuscripts; the textual variant doesn't change the framework reading) is the area east of the Sea of Galilee, in the Decapolis region. This territory — beyond the Yarden (Jordan) — was the tribal-inheritance of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasheh (Manasseh) and parts of Reuven (Reuven) (Bemidbar (Numbers) 32; Yahoshua (Joshua) 13:24-31). After the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE, the eastern tribes were among the most heavily impacted; the Decapolis-region was resettled and Hellenized; by the first century the territory had a mixed Yahudim (Jewish) and Hellenistic-Gentile population with the Hellenistic-Gentile demographic dominant.

The framework reads Yahusha's (Jesus's) crossing-to-the-other-side as the same gathering-territory pull noted at Matt 4:25 — the eastern-tribal-territories' representatives among the multitudes are surfaced again here. Yahusha (Jesus) goes to the territory of the scattered eastern tribes; he ministers in the lands of their inheritance even though the territory's contemporary population is mixed.

**The swine.** *An herd of many swine feeding.* The swine are an unclean-animal under Torah-law (Vayikra (Leviticus) 11:7-8; Devarim (Deuteronomy) 14:8). The presence of a large swine-herd in the territory confirms the contemporary administration is Hellenistic-Gentile, not Torah-walking. The framework reads the swine-herd as the visible-marker of the unclean-economy operating in the eastern-tribal-inheritance under the post-deportation-and-resettlement administration.

The framework reads the swine-drowning as deliberately-significant. The demons enter the swine; the swine drown. The unclean-economy in the territory takes a major blow at the King's arrival. The framework does not over-read this into a specific eschatological-symbol but notes the pattern: the King's presence is the antithesis of the unclean-economy; where he is, the unclean-arrangements collapse. The cleansing of the leper at vv.1-4 walked the same pattern from the purity-side; the swine-drowning walks it from the eschatological-judgment-side.

**The demons' recognition.** *What have we to do with thee, Yahusha (Jesus), thou Son of Elohim (God)? art thou come hither to torment us before the time?* The principalities-and-powers under the watcher-rebellion-administration recognize the King. They recognize him by his Sonship (*Son of Elohim*) and they recognize the eschatological-timeline (*torment us before the time*) — they know their dominion is temporary and that a day of judgment is appointed. The framework reading is consistent with `kingdoms-now-under-watcher-rebellion-administration`: the rebel-administrators know the structure they operate in is finite; they know the King has authority over them; they know their final-disposition is fixed in the prophetic-architecture (Revelation 20:10's lake-of-fire; the binding-of-Satan at the Daniel-7 transfer).

The vocative *Son of Elohim (God)* deserves a framework note. The demons' confession is precise where many human-confessors stumble. They identify Yahusha (Jesus) by his Sonship to the Father — recognizing the Formed-from-the-Formless framework directly. James 2:19 — *the devils also believe, and tremble* — names this recognition as cognitive-but-not-saving. The demons KNOW; they do not submit. The Matt 8:29 confession is the demons' fearful acknowledgment of the structure they had hoped to escape recognition by.

**The townspeople's response.** *The whole city came out to meet Yahusha (Jesus): and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart out of their coasts* (v.34). The framework's `_COMMENTARY_SUMMATION.md` §III posture on the *grace and the opportunity that can be lost* applies — the community had a chance to receive the King; they sent him away because of the swine-loss; the opportunity passes.

The framework reads Matt 8:34 as the early-modeling of the *shake the dust off your feet* response Yahusha (Jesus) will codify at Matt 10:14. The pursuit ends where the rejection lands. The King leaves the Gadarene territory and returns to Galilee. The two delivered demoniacs become witnesses in their territory (Mark 5:19-20 records this explicitly: *Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things Yahuah (the LORD) hath done for thee*).
