# Free-tier commentary on Matthew 9

Matthew 9 deepens the post-Sermon ministry through forgiveness, calling, fasting-reframing, raising-of-the-dead, and the first appearance of the Beelzebul-slander Matthew 12 will dismantle. (1) The paralytic-forgiveness pronouncement (vv.1-7) stages the verification of the Father's own prerogative now exercised by the Formed-in-flesh. The scribes' theological-premise is correct — Tanakh names forgiveness as Yahuah's (the LORD's) alone (Isaiah 43:25; Psalm 103:3) — but they cannot perceive WHO is making the claim. Yahusha (Jesus) performs the visibly-verifiable healing to legitimate the un-verifiable forgiveness-claim, and he attaches it to a title: *the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins.* The Hebrew *ben adam* carries the seed-of-Adam lineage-weight the inherited English translation has flattened out. (2) The calling of Mattityahu (Matthew) the tax-collector and the meal at his table publicly dismantle the Pharisaic-credentialed-segregation. Yahusha (Jesus) sends them to *go and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice* (Hosea 6:6) — standing in the whole prophetic stream of the same indictment against external-credential-religiosity: 1 Samuel 15:22 (*to obey is better than sacrifice*), Isaiah 1:11-17, Jeremiah 7:22-23, Amos 5:21-24, Micah 6:8 (*do justly, love mercy, walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)*). The Pharisaic-counterfeit is the inheritor of every system the prophets indicted. (3) The bridegroom-language (v.15) is direct Messianic-self-claim against the covenant-bridegroom prophecies (Isaiah 62:5; 54:5; Jeremiah 3:14; Hosea 2:19-20's betrothal of the scattered-and-divorced house). *The bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast* is the first cross-foreshadowing in the gospel-arc and the explicit endorsement of fasting for the present-age. (4) The new-wineskins parable (vv.16-17) is NOT the antinomian proof-text the Christian-commentary tradition has weaponized for centuries. The OLD wineskin is the Pharisaic-rabbinic-oral-tradition system (*takkanot* and rabbinic-fences); the NEW wine is the heart-Torah-walking life Ezekiel 36:26-27 and Jeremiah 31:33 had named — Torah-internalized, *put in their inward parts and written in their hearts.* The Torah itself is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin — it is the SUBSTANCE the new wine carries. Both are preserved: the Torah-substance stands intact; the new-heart container holds it without rupturing. (5) The bleeding-woman and Yair's daughter (vv.18-26) extend the cleansing-source pattern — the King's contact reverses the defiling-flow (Leviticus 15:25-27; Numbers 19:11). The twelve-year symmetry signals the covenant-completeness number; the raising-of-the-dead joins Isaiah 35:5-6's Messianic-credential-signature, with death-as-sleep reading against Daniel 12:2 and Isaiah 26:19. The blind men's *Son of David* confession (v.27) is direct Messianic-recognition from the Davidic-covenant prophetic stream. (6) The Beelzebul-attribution (v.34) is the first seed of the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach trajectory Matthew 12:31-32 will name; the same diagnostic remains operative — institutional Christianity today labels heart-Torah-walking as *legalism, Hebrew-Roots-deception, Judaizing.* (7) The chapter closes on Yahusha (Jesus) seeing the multitudes as *sheep having no shepherd* (v.36) — the deliberate citation of Ezekiel 34's prophetic thunder against the false-shepherds and the restoration-promise of *one shepherd, even my servant David* (34:23). The harvest reads against Isaiah 27:12 (*ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Yashar'el (Israel)*) and Joel 3:13. The Shepherd has come; the harvest is ready; the labourers are about to be sent — the Matthew 10 commission.
