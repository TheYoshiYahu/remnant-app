# Short-form commentary on Matthew 9

Matthew 9 is the post-Sermon ministry deepening from the credentialing-healings of chapter 8 into the territory of forgiveness, calling, fasting-reframing, raising-of-the-dead, *Son of David* Messianic recognition, and the first appearance of the Beelzebul slander that Matthew 12 will dismantle. The chapter closes on the *sheep having no shepherd* prophetic-naming and the *harvest is plenteous* setup that immediately commissions the twelve at Matthew 10. The framework anchors are dense: forgiveness as the Father's own prerogative now exercised by the Formed-in-flesh; the calling of a categorically-excluded tax-collector as the public dismantling of the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness; the new-wineskins parable read against the antinomian Christian-collapse; the cleansing-source King whose touch reverses the defiling-flow on both the woman with the chronic discharge and the dead child; and the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd-vision walking the multitudes the King has just seen scattered.

The chapter opens on the paralytic carried by friends.

*And he entered into a ship, and passed over, and came into his own city. And, behold, they brought to him a man sick of the palsy, lying on a bed: and Yahusha (Jesus) seeing their faith said unto the sick of the palsy; Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee.* (Matthew 9:1-2)

Two framework moves land in v.2 before the healing happens. First, the vocative — *Son.* The King addresses the man before he is healed, before any credentialed-status is offered, before any verbal-confession is required from him. The Shepherd recognizes the sheep on sight; the address itself is the recognition. Second, the forgiveness-pronouncement comes BEFORE the healing. *Thy sins be forgiven thee.* Yahusha (Jesus) speaks the forgiveness first because the scribes' reaction to it is precisely the pedagogical-moment he is staging.

*And, behold, certain of the scribes said within themselves, This man blasphemeth.* (Matthew 9:3)

The scribes are NOT theologically-illiterate. Their premise is correct. The Tanakh names the forgiveness-prerogative as Yahuah's (the LORD's) alone.

*I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions for mine own sake, and will not remember thy sins.* (Isaiah 43:25)

*Bless Yahuah (the LORD), O my soul, and forget not all his benefits: Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases.* (Psalm 103:3)

The scribes hold the correct Tanakh-premise on who can forgive sins. Their failure is not at the doctrine-level; their failure is at recognizing WHO is making the claim in front of them. The Formed-Voice that spoke Isaiah 43:25 through the prophet is standing in the room speaking in flesh. They cannot perceive the Formed because they hold the credentials of the system that has stopped listening.

Yahusha's (Jesus's) response stages the verification.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) knowing their thoughts said, Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts? For whether is easier, to say, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk? But that ye may know that the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins, (then saith he to the sick of the palsy,) Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.* (Matthew 9:4-6)

Both pronouncements are equally impossible for a mere-man and equally possible for the Formed. The forgiveness-claim cannot be visibly verified at the moment of speaking; the healing-claim CAN be visibly verified. By performing the verifiable miracle, the King legitimates the un-verifiable claim that preceded it. And he attaches the legitimation to a title: *the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins.* The Hebrew *ben adam* carries the lineage-weight the English translators flattened to *son of man.* The seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise is what the title names. The Son of Adam, the Formed-in-flesh in the line of the patriarchs, exercises the Father's prerogative because he IS the Formed who spoke through the prophet. The scribes' theological-premise is preserved AND verified at the same moment: only Yahuah forgives sins, and the one in front of them is the Formed who spoke the Tanakh's whole forgiveness-architecture into being.

The chapter moves immediately to the tax-collector's table. The two scenes are juxtaposed deliberately — the scribes who hold the correct doctrine but cannot perceive the King, and the man at the tax-collection booth who hears the call and rises.

*And as Yahusha (Jesus) passed forth from thence, he saw a man, named Mattityahu (Matthew), sitting at the receipt of custom: and he saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.* (Matthew 9:9)

Tax-collectors in the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community were categorically-despised on two grounds: they collected tribute for Roman occupation, and the Pharisaic-purity-system treated them as defiling because they handled imperial coinage and interacted continually with Gentile travelers. Mattityahu (Matthew) was, by the Pharisaic standard, in the categorical-sinner class. Yahusha (Jesus) calls him into the twelve. The pattern is the framework's posture made flesh: systems are the enemy, people are the victims. The categorical-exclusion is the system's failure; the man at the booth is the lost sheep the system has written off. The King's voice reaches the ears the credentialed-Pharisees had stopped speaking to. Mattityahu rises immediately. The hearing reveals what was already true — he was a sheep before the call; the call reveals him.

The meal at his house extends the public-defiance.

*And it came to pass, as Yahusha (Jesus) sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples. And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?* (Matthew 9:10-11)

Table-fellowship in the second-temple world was a covenant-affirming act. The Pharisaic-system extended it by restricting table-fellowship to the credentialed-as-ritually-pure. Yahusha (Jesus) breaks the segregation deliberately and publicly. The Pharisees address the disciples rather than the King — the indirection-pattern that will recur — and the King does not let the indirection succeed.

*But when Yahusha (Jesus) heard that, he said unto them, They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick. But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.* (Matthew 9:12-13)

The physician-metaphor uses the Pharisees' own self-classification against them. They have classified themselves as *whole* and the publicans-and-sinners as *sick*; on their own logic, the King's ministry to the sick is precisely what those people need. The deeper irony is that the *whole* are spiritually-sick at the diagnostic-level the Sermon's *I never knew you, depart from me, ye that work iniquity* has already named (Matthew 7:23) — they are credentialed but they are working without the Torah's heart-substance. The *sick* in their classification are the ones whose ears can be opened.

The Hosea 6:6 citation is the framework crux of the chapter on the diagnostic-system. Yahusha (Jesus) tells the Pharisees to *go and learn what that meaneth* — a Tanakh-pedagogical-prescription. They have the text. They have not learned the substance. The full prophet-verse stands behind the citation.

*For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of Elohim (God) more than burnt offerings.* (Hosea 6:6)

The Tanakh-prophetic-stream of the same indictment is dense and old. The first articulation is at Mosheh's (Moses's) successor's confrontation with Sha'ul (Saul).

*And Shemu'el (Samuel) said, Hath Yahuah (the LORD) as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of Yahuah (the LORD)? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.* (1 Samuel 15:22)

The Psalms carry it.

*Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire; mine ears hast thou opened: burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required.* (Psalm 40:6)

*For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering. The sacrifices of Elohim (God) are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O Elohim (God), thou wilt not despise.* (Psalm 51:16-17)

The major prophets carry it.

*To what purpose is the multitude of your sacrifices unto me? saith Yahuah (the LORD): I am full of the burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of fed beasts; and I delight not in the blood of bullocks, or of lambs, or of he goats... Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil; Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.* (Isaiah 1:11, 16-17)

*For I spake not unto your fathers, nor commanded them in the day that I brought them out of the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt), concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices: But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voice, and I will be your Elohim (God), and ye shall be my people: and walk ye in all the ways that I have commanded you, that it may be well unto you.* (Jeremiah 7:22-23)

The minor prophets carry it.

*I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies... But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.* (Amos 5:21, 24)

*He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth Yahuah (the LORD) require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)?* (Micah 6:8)

Yahusha (Jesus) is standing in this whole prophetic-stream when he tells the Pharisees to *go and learn* Hosea 6:6. The indictment is not new; it is the same indictment the prophets had been delivering against external-credential-religiosity for centuries. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness is the inheritor of every system the prophets named. The categorical-exclusion of the tax-collector class is sacrifice-without-mercy in flesh. The King brings the Tanakh's own diagnostic forward.

The fasting question follows.

*Then came to him the disciples of Yochanan (John), saying, Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, but thy disciples fast not?* (Matthew 9:14)

The Pharisees and Yochanan's (John's) disciples both observed Pharisaic-style twice-weekly voluntary fasts. The challenge assumes that practice as the standard-of-piety. Yahusha's (Jesus's) response identifies himself as the BRIDEGROOM — direct Messianic-self-claim against the deepest covenant-substance the prophets had named.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Can the children of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.* (Matthew 9:15)

The bridegroom-language has Tanakh-anchors that name the substance directly.

*For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee: and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy Elohim (God) rejoice over thee.* (Isaiah 62:5)

*For thy Maker is thine husband; Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts) is his name; and thy Redeemer the Holy One of Yashar'el (Israel); the Elohim (God) of the whole earth shall he be called.* (Isaiah 54:5)

*Turn, O backsliding children, saith Yahuah (the LORD); for I am married unto you.* (Jeremiah 3:14)

And the prophecy that ties the bridegroom-substance directly to the framework's gathering-thesis stands at Hosea 2.

*And I will betroth thee unto me for ever; yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies. I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness: and thou shalt know Yahuah (the LORD).* (Hosea 2:19-20)

Hosea is addressing the scattered-and-divorced house — the same house the chapter has named with the *Lo-Ammi (not my people)* of 1:9 and the gathering-prophecy of 1:10-11. The bridegroom-substance Yahusha (Jesus) claims at Matthew 9:15 IS the betrothal-restoration of the divorced-scattered seed. He is the Yahuah-of-Yashar'el whose bridegroom-substance the prophets had named, in flesh at the table.

The *bridegroom shall be taken from them* is the first explicit foreshadowing of the cross in Matthew's gospel-arc. The disciples-of-Yochanan and the contemporary community would not yet hear that, but the King is also naming the present-age-between-the-comings — the time when the bridegroom is bodily-absent and the bride waits in fasting-and-watching for the consummation the Revelation 19:7-9 wedding-of-the-Lamb will name. And the verse-half often missed: *then shall they fast.* Yahusha (Jesus) explicitly endorses fasting for the present-age. The post-ascension assembly will fast; the Acts and Didache records bear it out. Fasting is not abolished. It is reframed in the wedding-architecture.

The new-wineskins parable that immediately follows is the load-bearing framework crux of the chapter on the heart-Torah-not-abolition front.

*No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse. Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.* (Matthew 9:16-17)

The Christian-commentary tradition has weaponized this parable for centuries as the antinomian proof-text: *new wine = the gospel / old wineskin = Judaism + Torah / the gospel cannot be contained in Torah, therefore the Torah is gone.* The reading collapses Matthew 5:17-19's own anti-antinomian declaration. It collapses every prophet's promise of Torah-internalization. It collapses the Father's-character as the eternally-the-same. It is the antichrist reading.

The framework reads the parable in its actual context — the fasting-controversy Yahusha (Jesus) just answered. The OLD GARMENT and the OLD WINESKIN are the Pharisaic-rabbinic-oral-tradition-system — the *takkanot* and *gezerot* and inherited rabbinic-fences that had built up around the Torah; the same system whose categorical-classification-of-sinners the King has just defied at Mattityahu's table; the same system that fences fasting into twice-weekly mourning while the bridegroom stands at the wedding. The NEW CLOTH and the NEW WINE are the heart-Torah-walking life the King is teaching — the same substance Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) and Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) had named long before.

*A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* (Ezekiel 36:26-27)

*But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Yashar'el (Israel); After those days, saith Yahuah (the LORD), I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their Elohim (God), and they shall be my people.* (Jeremiah 31:33)

The new wine is the heart-Torah-life of Ezekiel 36 and Jeremiah 31. The Torah itself is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin — the Torah is the SUBSTANCE that the new wine carries; the *law put in their inward parts and written in their hearts* IS the Torah-internalized; the *statutes and judgments* the new spirit causes them to walk in ARE the Torah's commandments. The new-heart container is the regenerated person whose spirit can hold the heart-Torah-life without rupturing. The old wineskin — the Pharisaic-system — was built to hold external-credential-religiosity and cannot hold the heart-substance because the heart-substance rejects the categorical-classification-of-sinners and the credential-without-mercy that the system was built on. The closing phrase *both are preserved* names the both-and: the Torah-substance is preserved (the Tanakh-foundation stands intact), and the kingdom-citizens are preserved (the new-heart container holds the substance without being torn). The Spirit who lives in the new-heart citizens is the Spirit who spoke at Sinai; he does not lead them away from what he himself spoke.

The chapter then turns to the Yair-and-the-bleeding-woman intercalation.

*While he spake these things unto them, behold, there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him, saying, My daughter is even now dead: but come and lay thy hand upon her, and she shall live. And Yahusha (Jesus) arose, and followed him, and so did his disciples.* (Matthew 9:18-19)

Mark and Luke identify the ruler as Yair — a synagogue-ruler, a system-credentialed figure who recognizes the King. He is the framework's reminder that the diagnostic against the Pharisaic-system is system-not-people; not all contemporary leadership is on the wrong side. Yair, Nakdimon (Nicodemus) at John 3, and Yoseph (Joseph) of Arimathea at Matthew 27 are the framework's contemporary-leadership turning-figures. The credentials do not predetermine the hearing.

The woman with the issue of blood interrupts the journey.

*And, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment: For she said within herself, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.* (Matthew 9:20-21)

Under the Torah, a chronic discharge of blood rendered a woman ritually-impure for as long as the discharge continued, with extended purity-consequences for anyone or anything she touched.

*And if a woman have an issue of her blood many days out of the time of her separation, or if it run beyond the time of her separation; all the days of the issue of her uncleanness shall be as the days of her separation: she shall be unclean. Every bed whereon she lieth all the days of her issue shall be unto her as the bed of her separation: and whatsoever she sitteth upon shall be unclean, as the uncleanness of her separation. And whosoever toucheth those things shall be unclean, and shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the even.* (Leviticus 15:25-27)

For twelve years this woman has been categorically-isolated. The Pharisaic-purity-system would have read her contact with Yahusha (Jesus) as DEFILING the rabbi. The framework reads the King's response in the same anti-defilement pattern the leper of Matthew 8:3 displayed: the King's presence is the cleansing-source, not the absorber of impurity. His touch reverses the flow.

*But Yahusha (Jesus) turned him about, and when he saw her, he said, Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole from that hour.* (Matthew 9:22)

The vocative *Daughter* mirrors the *Son* the King addressed the paralytic with at v.2. The woman who has been categorically-excluded for twelve years is named DAUGHTER by the King at the moment of healing — reincorporation into the covenant-family at the moment of the cleansing. Her *faith* is not a creative-act that MAKES her a sheep; it is the response that REVEALS her as one. She heard reports of the King's ministry; the hearing revealed what was already true; she came; she touched; she was made whole.

The twelve-year number is not incidental. The hemorrhaging woman has been bleeding for twelve years; Yair's daughter is twelve years old. One woman has been suffering as long as the other has been alive. Twelve is the covenant-completeness number — the number of the tribes. The framework reads the symmetry as the gathering's-restorative-substance signaled in the two-figure pairing: the King restores what twelve-tribe-symbolic-time has produced as suffering.

The journey reaches Yair's house.

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) came into the ruler's house, and saw the minstrels and the people making a noise, He said unto them, Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn.* (Matthew 9:23-24)

The death-as-sleep language is deliberate-eschatological-teaching anchored in the Tanakh's resurrection-prophecy.

*And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.* (Daniel 12:2)

*Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast them out.* (Isaiah 26:19)

The maid is dead-in-medical-fact; Yahusha (Jesus) names her as sleeping because the resurrection is imminent at his word. The crowd's laughter is the same seed-war-failure-to-hear that has surfaced throughout the chapter — the contemporary community has been so saturated in Greek-philosophical-mortalism (the Sadducees explicitly rejecting bodily-resurrection per Matthew 22:23) that the death-as-sleep eschatological-framing is foreign to them.

*But when the people were put forth, he went in, and took her by the hand, and the maid arose.* (Matthew 9:25)

The King takes the dead girl's hand. Under the Torah's ordinary purity-rules, contact with a corpse renders a person ritually-impure for seven days (Numbers 19:11). The pattern of Matthew 8:3 and 9:22 holds: the King's contact is the source of LIFE-FLOW rather than the absorber of death-flow. He is not defiled by the corpse — the corpse becomes alive. The maid arose. The credentialing-display of the raising-of-the-dead joins the leper's cleansing and the woman's healing as present-age-foretastes of the resurrection-at-the-end-of-the-age.

The two blind men follow.

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, Thou Son of David, have mercy on us.* (Matthew 9:27)

The *Son of David* confession is direct Messianic-recognition from the Davidic-covenant prophetic-stream. The foundational covenant stands at 2 Samuel 7.

*And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom. He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever. I will be his father, and he shall be my son.* (2 Samuel 7:12-14)

The prophets carry the Davidic-Messianic stream forward.

*And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Yishai (Jesse), and a Branch shall grow out of his roots: And the spirit of Yahuah (the LORD) shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of Yahuah (the LORD).* (Isaiah 11:1-2)

*Behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth. In his days Yahudah (Judah) shall be saved, and Yashar'el (Israel) shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS).* (Jeremiah 23:5-6)

*And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David; he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd. And I Yahuah (the LORD) will be their Elohim (God), and my servant David a prince among them; I Yahuah (the LORD) have spoken it.* (Ezekiel 34:23-24)

The blind men confess Yahusha (Jesus) as the Davidic-Messianic shepherd-and-Branch. The framework reads this as the same hearing-pattern that has surfaced throughout the chapter: those whom the Pharisaic-credential-system has marginalized perceive the King; those who hold the credentials cannot. Hearing reveals what was already true. The blind men were sons of Adam, sons of the seed-of-promise; their faith does not CREATE their belonging — it reveals it. The healing follows.

*Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you. And their eyes were opened.* (Matthew 9:29-30)

The healing extends the Isaiah 35 Messianic-credential-signature the evening-healings of Matthew 8:17 had already invoked.

*Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing.* (Isaiah 35:5-6)

Yahusha (Jesus) will cite this same signature himself a few chapters downstream when Yochanan (John) the Immerser sends from prison to ask whether he is the one. The healing of the two blind men is one of the credential-displays the King will point Yochanan (John) back to.

The mute demoniac immediately follows.

*As they went out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed with a devil. And when the devil was cast out, the dumb spake: and the multitudes marvelled, saying, It was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel). But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.* (Matthew 9:32-34)

This is the first appearance of the Beelzebul-attribution that Matthew 12 will dismantle structurally and that Matthew 12:31-32 will name as the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) trajectory. The Pharisees confronted with a miracle they cannot deny attribute the source to demonic-agency. The reading is theologically-catastrophic. A deliverance from a demonic-affliction is, by definition, demons-being-defeated; to attribute the deliverance-source to the prince-of-demons is to claim the prince-of-demons is fighting his own kingdom. It is self-contradicting. Matthew 12 will name it so. But the trajectory begins here — the seed planted at 9:34 will grow into the explicit naming of the unforgivable-sin at 12:31-32. The pattern is the diagnostic-end-point of the system-confronted-with-the-Ruach's-credentialing: when the system cannot deny the visible-fact, it reaches for any framework — even self-contradictory ones — that lets it reject what it cannot acknowledge.

The pattern is not historical-only. Institutional Christianity does the same with heart-Torah-walking today — the Sabbath-keeping, the feast-walking, the restored-names, the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed — relabels it *legalism* or *Hebrew-Roots-deception* or *Judaizing* to maintain its categorical-rejection of what the Ruach (Spirit) is doing in the present age. The blasphemy-against-the-Ruach trajectory is not behind us. The chapter plants the seed; the diagnostic remains operative.

The chapter closes on the gathering-mission framing for the Matthew 10 commission.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people. But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.* (Matthew 9:35-36)

The *sheep having no shepherd* phrase is the deliberate-citation of the Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34 prophetic-thunder against the false-shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel).

*Woe be to the shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel) that do feed themselves! should not the shepherds feed the flocks? Ye eat the fat, and ye clothe you with the wool, ye kill them that are fed: but ye feed not the flock... My sheep wandered through all the mountains, and upon every high hill: yea, my flock was scattered upon all the face of the earth, and none did search or seek after them.* (Ezekiel 34:2-3, 6)

And the Yechezk'el-34 vision is not only diagnostic — it is restoration-promise.

*For thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out. As a shepherd seeketh out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered; so will I seek out my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.* (Ezekiel 34:11-12)

*And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David; he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd.* (Ezekiel 34:23)

Yahusha (Jesus) at Matthew 9:36 IS the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd. He sees the multitudes through the prophet's lens. The contemporary Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-failure is the same failure Yechezk'el indicted; the symptoms are the same — scattered, fainting, harassed; the prophetic-promise is being fulfilled in the King's compassion-response and the gathering-mission he is about to commission.

*Then saith he unto his disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few; Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.* (Matthew 9:37-38)

The harvest-language is the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed under the prophet's harvest-figure.

*And it shall come to pass in that day, that Yahuah (the LORD) shall beat off from the channel of the river unto the stream of Mitsrayim (Egypt), and ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Isaiah 27:12)

*Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down; for the press is full, the fats overflow.* (Joel 3:13)

The scattered seed are the harvest. The labourers are about to be sent. The next verse — Matthew 10:1 — commissions the twelve. The chapter is the gathering's-front-door for the commission that the next chapter will execute. The mission-scope will be locked at 10:5-6 as the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel); the broad-proclamation-to-the-citizens-scattered-through-the-nations is the practice; the audience is the citizens; the hearing reveals them. Matthew 9 closes with the King looking at the scattered multitudes and naming the gathering-substance the next chapter will send the twelve out to. The Shepherd has come. The harvest is ready. The labourers go.
