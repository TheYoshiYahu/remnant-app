# Short-form commentary on Matthew 9

The chapter overview walks the main beats — the paralytic healed and forgiven with the *Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins* claim at vv.1-8 (the scribes' Tanakh-premise that only Yahuah (the LORD) forgives sins is correct, and the Formed-Voice that spoke Isaiah 43:25 through the prophet stands in flesh in the room); the calling of Mattityahu (Matthew) and the *I will have mercy, and not sacrifice* citation of Hosea 6:6 at vv.9-13, standing in the whole prophetic-stream (1 Samuel 15:22, Isaiah 1:11-17, Jeremiah 7:22-23, Amos 5:21-24, Micah 6:8); the bridegroom-self-identification at v.15 against the Tanakh's covenant-bridegroom prophecies (Isaiah 62:5, 54:5; Jeremiah 3:14; Hosea 2:19-20's betrothal of the divorced-and-scattered house); the new-wineskins parable at vv.16-17 read against the antinomian Christian-collapse — the Torah is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin but the SUBSTANCE the new-heart container carries per Ezekiel 36:26-27 and Jeremiah 31:33; the Yair (Jairus)-and-bleeding-woman intercalation at vv.18-26 with the cleansing-source King reversing the defiling-flow (Leviticus 15:25-27, Numbers 19:11); the *Son of David* Davidic-Messianic confession of the two blind men at v.27; the first appearance of the Beelzebul-attribution at v.34 that Matthew 12 will dismantle as the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) trajectory; and the *sheep having no shepherd* Ezekiel-34 anchor with the *harvest is plenteous* commissioning-setup for Matthew 10 at vv.35-38. Two framework moves the overview does not surface lock the chapter at the cause-and-effect register that distinguishes the truth from the inclusion-gospel and at the eschatological-architecture register that distinguishes the resurrection-reading from the Hellenistic-mortalism the contemporary community had absorbed.

**Faith at v.22 (the bleeding woman) and at v.29 (the two blind men) is the response that REVEALS the sheep, not the act that CREATES the belonging — the cause-and-effect reversal that distinguishes the framework's reading from the false-inclusion gospel.** The Christian-commentary tradition has typically read the *thy faith hath made thee whole* and *according to your faith be it unto you* verses two ways, both wrong. The prosperity-gospel collapse reads them as *if your faith is strong enough, the healing is guaranteed.* The inclusion-gospel collapse reads them as *if you believe, you become his sheep.* The framework rejects both. The bleeding woman had heard reports of the King's ministry; the hearing revealed what was already true; her *if I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole* is the sheep's-response-to-the-Shepherd's-voice.

*But Yahusha (Jesus) turned him about, and when he saw her, he said, Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole from that hour.* (Matthew 9:22)

*And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him: and Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Believe ye that I am able to do this? They said unto him, Yea, Lord. Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you. And their eyes were opened.* (Matthew 9:28-29)

The vocative *Daughter* at v.22 mirrors the *Son* the King addressed the paralytic with at v.2 — the kingdom-citizen-recognition is in the address itself, before any verbal-confession. The faith is the open-door through which the healing-flow reaches the sheep that already are sheep; it is not the agent that manufactures the belonging. The blind men's *Yea, Lord* is the same hearing-pattern — they heard, they followed, they confessed, they received. The Christian reading that inverts the cause-and-effect — *if you confess, you become covenant* — collapses against the King's own teaching elsewhere: *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep* (John 10:26). Hearing reveals what was always true. The blind men were sons of Adam, sons of the seed-of-promise, before the call; the call reveals them; the faith is the door, not the lineage.

**Death-as-sleep at v.24 walks the Tanakh's own resurrection-architecture, not a gospel-innovation of consolation-language for the bereaved.** The contemporary community had been so saturated in Hellenistic-mortalism (the Sadducees explicitly rejecting bodily-resurrection per Matthew 22:23) that the King's *the maid is not dead, but sleepeth* drew laughter. The Hebrew library had been carrying the dead-in-the-covenant-are-sleeping framework for centuries before the Sermon.

*And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.* (Daniel 12:2)

*Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast them out.* (Isaiah 26:19)

The maid is dead-in-medical-fact; Yahusha (Jesus) names her as sleeping because the resurrection is imminent at his word. The framework reads the architecture at full weight — the patriarchs are sleeping awaiting the first resurrection (Romans 11:24's natural branches-able-to-be-grafted-in-again; Hebrews 11:39-40's *they without us should not be made perfect*); the maid is sleeping awaiting the immediate-awakening at the King's hand. The principle is one — the dead-in-the-covenant are sleeping, not finally-extinguished — and the raising-of-the-dead joins the leper-cleansing (8:3) and the bleeding-woman-healing (9:22) as present-age-foretastes of the resurrection-at-the-end-of-the-age. The King who takes the dead girl's hand without becoming defiled under Numbers 19:11's seven-day corpse-uncleanness rule is the cleansing-source pattern at the deepest register: contact with the King reverses death-flow into life-flow.
