# Commentary on Matthew 9

Matthew 9 is the post-Sermon healing ministry deepening into the territory of FORGIVENESS, of CALLING, of the NEW-WINE / NEW-WINESKIN reframing of fasting, of the raising-of-the-dead, of the *Son of David* Messianic confession by the blind, and of the first appearance of the Beelzebul-slander that Matthew 12 will dismantle. The chapter closes on the *sheep having no shepherd* prophetic-naming and the *harvest is plenteous, but the labourers are few* setup that immediately commissions the twelve at Matthew 10. The paralytic healed and forgiven (vv.1-8) — the Formed-Voice-of-Sinai exercising the Father's prerogative to forgive sins, with the *Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins* claim laid down at the inauguration of the kingdom-public-discourse on sin-and-authority. The calling of Mattityahu (Matthew) the tax-collector (vv.9-13) — system-not-people made flesh, with the *I will have mercy, and not sacrifice* Hosea 6:6 citation that Yahusha (Jesus) will cite again at Matthew 12:7. The fasting question and the new-wineskins parables (vv.14-17) — the bridegroom-Messianic-identification, the present-age-of-fasting between the bridegroom's two comings, and the new-wineskin-as-new-heart NOT new-Torah reframing against the institutional-Christian collapse. Yair's daughter and the hemorrhaging woman (vv.18-26) — the intercalation of the twelve-year-old daughter and the twelve-year-bleeding-woman, both made whole through contact with the cleansing-source King. The two blind men (vv.27-31) — *Son of David* Messianic-confession from the marginalized; *according to your faith* as the hearing-reveals-not-creates pattern; the disclosure-restraint pattern. The mute demoniac and the *prince of the devils* slander (vv.32-34) — the first appearance of the Beelzebul-attribution that Matthew 12 will name as the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach trajectory. And the *harvest is plenteous, but the labourers are few* (vv.35-38) — the gathering-mission framing for the Matthew 10 commission, with the *sheep without a shepherd* Yechezk'el-34 anchor.

---

## §1. The paralytic healed and forgiven — the Formed Voice's authority to forgive sins

The framework reads the paralytic-healing as the deliberate Matthean-narrative-staging of the FORGIVENESS-CLAIM that the post-Sermon ministry now lays down explicitly. The leper-cleansing of Matthew 8:1-4 demonstrated the King's authority over the purity-architecture's defiling-flows; the centurion-pericope demonstrated his authority over disease-at-distance; the storm-stilling demonstrated his authority over creation; the Gergesene exorcism demonstrated his authority over the principalities. Matthew 9:1-8 now demonstrates the King's authority to FORGIVE SINS — the Father's-prerogative the Tanakh assigns to Yahuah (the LORD) alone — demonstrated in flesh by the Formed-in-flesh.

The Tanakh-anchor of the forgiveness-prerogative stands at Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 43 and Tehillim (Psalm) 103.

The forgiveness and the healing are paired in the Tanakh's own architecture. Matthew 9:1-8 is the in-person demonstration of the pairing in a single body — the same Voice that spoke Psalm 103 through David is in the room speaking in flesh.

**The faith Yahusha (Jesus) sees** (v.2). Matthew compresses the Markan detail of the carrier-friends breaking open the roof to lower the paralytic — *they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed wherein the sick of the palsy lay* (Mark 2:4). The carrier-friends have brought the paralytic through obstacle to the King; their faith is the operative-variable Yahusha (Jesus) sees. The framework reads this as the hearing-reveals-not-creates pattern operating at the group-level — the carriers heard the Shepherd's voice; the hearing revealed what was already true; the response to the hearing was the immediate physical-action of bringing the suffering-one to the King.

**The vocative address.** *Son* (*teknon,* child). Tender. The Shepherd recognizes the sheep before any credentialed-status is offered, before any verbal-confession is required from him. The paralytic is addressed as *son* in the same tender register the King will use with the bleeding woman (*daughter,* v.22) — the kingdom-citizen-recognition is in the address itself.

**The forgiveness-pronouncement before the healing.** *Thy sins be forgiven thee* (v.2). Yahusha (Jesus) speaks the forgiveness BEFORE the healing. The framework reads this as deliberate-ordering for theological-display: the forgiveness comes first because the scribes' internal-thought-charge of blasphemy is the response Yahusha (Jesus) is going to use as the pedagogical-moment.

**The scribes' internal charge** (v.3). *And, behold, certain of the scribes said within themselves, This man blasphemeth.* The framework notes that the Yahudim (Jewish)-leadership-system's theological-premise here is CORRECT. Isaiah 43:25 lays out Yahuah's (the LORD's) self-naming as the SOLE-FORGIVER of sins. The scribes are not theologically-illiterate on this point; their problem is not doctrinal-failure on the forgiveness-prerogative. Their problem is the hearing-failure at the level of WHO is making the claim. They have the right premise; they reach the wrong conclusion because they cannot perceive the Formed-Voice-of-Sinai standing in front of them. The Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership-system has the credentials of the system that stopped listening — the system that, by the apostolic generation, will become the Judaizer party Galatians indicts. The same architecture is operative here; the same credential-without-perception pattern is on display.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the *which is easier* question** (vv.4-5). *And Yahusha (Jesus) knowing their thoughts said, Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts? For whether is easier, to say, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk?*

The framework reads the rhetorical question with care. Both pronouncements are equally impossible for a mere-man, equally possible for the Formed. The question is not which is theoretically-easier; the question is which can be VERIFIED. The forgiveness-claim cannot be visibly verified at the moment of speaking. The healing-claim CAN be visibly verified. By performing the visibly-verifiable miracle, Yahusha (Jesus) LEGITIMATES the un-visibly-verifiable claim that preceded it.

*But that ye may know that the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins, (then saith he to the sick of the palsy,) Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.* (Matthew 9:6)

**The *Son of Adam* claim — preserving the lineage-weight of *ben adam.*** The Hebrew *ben adam* and the Greek *huios tou anthrōpou* both carry the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise weight that the English translators flattened to *son of man.* The seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise is what the title names — the line of Adam carried forward through the patriarchs into the twelve tribes; the line of the Messianic-seed-promise; the line by which the Formed-in-flesh stands in the bloodline of all who came before him. The Son of Adam, the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise in person, exercises the Father's-prerogative to forgive sins because he IS the Formed who spoke the Torah's expiation-architecture into being at Sinai. The scribes' theological-premise (only Yahuah (the LORD) forgives sins) is preserved AND verified — the one in front of them is the Formed-Voice that spoke at Sinai.

The framework notes the public-disclosure-pattern. Yahusha (Jesus) does not yet say *because I am Yahuah (the LORD).* He says *the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins.* The Son-of-Adam title carries the lineage-weight of the seed-of-Adam-promise; it also carries the kaph-comparative figure of Daniel 7.

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:13-14)

The kaph-comparative — *one like the Son of Adam* — preserves both the resemblance (he became flesh) and the named Standard (he remains the Formed) in view at the same time. Honored at the incarnation, identified by the harmonized canon as Yahusha (Jesus) himself. The disclosure at Matthew 9:6 is being staged across the gospel; the explicit Yahuah-identification will become unmistakable over time. Matthew 28:18 will name the full disclosure: *all power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.* Matthew 9:6 is one beat of the staging.

**The crowd's response** (v.8). *But when the multitudes saw it, they marvelled, and glorified Elohim (God), which had given such power unto men.* The multitudes' framing is partial — they perceive the authority-display but interpret it within the *given to men* register. They have not yet reached the Formed-from-the-Formless recognition; they are at the *prophet-with-divine-authority* stage of understanding. The framework reads this as the staged-disclosure progressing — the gospel will surface the fuller recognition at moments like Kefa's (Peter's) confession at Matthew 16:16, the transfiguration voice at Matthew 17:5, and the resurrection-worship at Matthew 28:9. For now, the multitudes glorify Elohim (God); the staged-disclosure continues.

## §2. The calling of Mattityahu (Matthew) the tax-collector — *I will have mercy, and not sacrifice*

The framework reads the Mattityahu-calling as the deliberate-display of the system-not-people posture made flesh. Tax-collectors in the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community were despised on two grounds. First, they collected tribute for Roman occupation — a collaborator-with-the-occupying-power role that placed them in the categorical-traitor class to the kingdom-aspirations of contemporary Yahudim (Jewish) nationalism. Second, the Pharisaic-purity-system treated them as defiling because they handled Hellenistic-coin-currency with imperial-cult imagery and they interacted continually with Gentile travelers and merchants. Mattityahu was a Yahudi (Jew) in the categorical-sinner class by Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness standards.

Yahusha (Jesus) calls him to be among the twelve. The framework reads this as deliberate-system-defiance. The kingdom-citizens-roster has just been named at the Beatitudes (Matthew 5:3-12) — the *anawim,* the Ezekiel-9 mourners, the *tsedaqah*-hungerers, the *chesed*-merciful, the new-heart pure. None of those markers exclude the tax-collector who hears the Shepherd's voice and rises to follow. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness's CATEGORICAL exclusion of the tax-collector class is precisely what the King is dismantling.

The framework's posture is always system-not-people. The tax-collectors-and-sinners are the lost sheep; the system's classification of them is the system's failure, not the substance. People are victims; systems are the enemy. The King calls the categorical-victim and seats him at the inner-circle table.

**The meal at Mattityahu's house** (v.10). *And it came to pass, as Yahusha (Jesus) sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.*

The framework reads the meal-fellowship as direct-defiance of the Pharisaic-purity-system's social-segregation-architecture. Table-fellowship in the second-temple Yahudim (Jewish) world was a covenant-affirming act; the Pharisaic-system extended this by restricting table-fellowship to the credentialed-as-ritually-pure. Yahusha (Jesus) breaks the social-segregation in deliberate-public-fashion at the home of a tax-collector with *many publicans and sinners* present. The framework reads the *sinners* (*hamartoloi*) as the Pharisaic-classification-category — those marginalized by the system's purity-credentials — not as a moral-judgment-confirmed-from-above. The King is among the victims of the credential-system, eating with them, and the Father is glorified in the meal.

**The Pharisees' challenge** (v.11). *And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?* The framework notes the Pharisees address the DISCIPLES rather than the King — the indirection-pattern that will recur (the disciples-of-Yochanan at 9:14 use this approach; the Pharisaic-challenge on hand-washing at Matthew 15:2 addresses Yahusha (Jesus) about the disciples). The challenge-by-proxy is itself a Pharisaic-mannerism. Yahusha (Jesus) does not let the indirection succeed; he responds directly.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the physician-and-the-sick metaphor** (v.12). *They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.* The framework reads the metaphor in framework-irony register. The *whole* are the Pharisees in their own self-classification; the *sick* are the tax-collectors-and-sinners. Yahusha (Jesus) is using the Pharisees' OWN self-categorization to indict them — *you have classified them as sick and yourselves as whole; on your own logic, my ministry to the sick is precisely what they need.* The deeper irony is that the *whole* in their own eyes are spiritually-sick at the diagnostic-level Yahusha (Jesus) just laid down at Matthew 7:21-23 — *not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven... I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity* — and the Greek *anomia* there is *without-Torah / Torahlessness,* the same diagnostic. The credentialed in their own eyes are working without the heart-Torah; the *sick* in their classification are the ones whose ears can be opened to the Shepherd's voice.

**The Hosea 6:6 citation** (v.13). *But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.*

This is the framework crux of the chapter on the diagnostic-system. Yahusha (Jesus) cites Hosea 6:6 and tells the Pharisees to *learn what that meaneth.* He will cite the same verse again at Matthew 12:7 in the Shabbat-controversy context. The Hosea 6:6 citation is the recurring Tanakh-prophetic-anchor Yahusha (Jesus) deploys against the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness.

The Tanakh-prophetic-stream behind the citation is long, and Yahusha (Jesus) is standing in all of it. The first articulation surfaces at Shemu'el's (Samuel's) confrontation with Sha'ul (Saul).

Yeshayahu (Isaiah) carries it.

Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) carries it.

Amos carries it.

Mikhah (Micah) carries it.

Yahusha (Jesus) is standing in this whole prophetic-stream when he cites Hosea 6:6. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness's failure is precisely the failure these prophets indicted — external-religiosity-without-heart-substance, sacrifice-without-mercy, credential-without-covenant. The Hosea 6:6 *go and learn what that meaneth* is the Tanakh-pedagogical-prescription — they have the text; they have not learned the substance.

The framework also reads the citation as the implicit-naming of the diagnostic the kingdom-citizens carry. The heart-Torah of the Sermon on the Mount IS the *mercy-and-knowledge-of-Elohim (God)* substance Hosea named. The new-heart of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 IS the mercy-substance the King is teaching the disciples to walk in. The Pharisees' *go and learn what that meaneth* is the prophet's same call to come back to the substance the system has lost.

**The *not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance*** (v.13b). The framework reads the irony at full weight. The *righteous* in their own eyes cannot HEAR the call to repent because they perceive themselves as not needing repentance. The *sinners* — those whose lives have not gone according to the Pharisaic-script — can hear because they know they need to. The hearing-pattern operates here: the sheep hear when the Shepherd calls; the Pharisaic-self-credentialed cannot hear because the Shepherd's call addresses what they refuse to admit they are.

The framework reads Mattityahu's own response as the contrast-pattern in person. He was at the tax-collection booth — categorically-classified as sinner; he rose immediately and followed. The KING'S voice reached the ears the system had written off. The system's righteous remained at the meal-perimeter critiquing the host-list.

## §3. The fasting question and the new-wineskins parables — the bridegroom in present age, and the new-heart container

The framework reads the fasting-question with care. The disciples-of-Yochanan join the indirection-pattern (v.11) — they ask about Yahusha's (Jesus's) practice rather than directly questioning him. The Pharisees and Yochanan (John)'s disciples were both observing Pharisaic-style twice-weekly voluntary fasts on Mondays and Thursdays (per second-temple-period practice, attested in Luke 18:12 and the Didache 8:1). Yahusha's (Jesus's) disciples were not. The challenge is operating from the assumption that this voluntary-fasting is the standard-of-piety.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the bridegroom-self-identification** (v.15). *And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Can the children of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.*

The bridegroom-language is direct Messianic-self-claim. The framework reads the Tanakh-anchors:

*Turn, O backsliding children, saith Yahuah (the LORD); for I am married unto you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Tsiyon (Zion).* (Jeremiah 3:14)

And the prophecy that ties the bridegroom-substance directly to the gathering-of-the-scattered-and-divorced-house stands at Hosea 2.

Hosea 2 is addressing the scattered-and-divorced northern house — the same house the chapter has named with the *Lo-Ammi (not my people)* of 1:9 and the gathering-prophecy of 1:10-11.

*And it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God). Then shall the children of Yahudah (Judah) and the children of Yashar'el (Israel) be gathered together, and appoint themselves one head, and they shall come up out of the land: for great shall be the day of Jezreel.* (Hosea 1:10-11)

The bridegroom-substance Yahusha (Jesus) claims at Matthew 9:15 IS the betrothal-restoration of the divorced-scattered seed. The Yahuah-of-Yashar'el (Israel) whose bridegroom-substance the prophets named is the Formed-in-flesh standing in front of the disciples-of-Yochanan. The bridegroom-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is the King-at-table-with-the-sinners. The gathering-substance of the framework's whole arc surfaces in the King's single phrase.

**The *bridegroom shall be taken from them* phrase** (v.15b) is the first explicit-foreshadowing of the cross in Matthew's gospel narrative-arc. The framework reads this carefully. The disciples-of-Yochanan and the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community would not yet hear the cross-foreshadowing; they would hear it as some kind of absence. The framework reads the verse as Yahusha (Jesus) ALSO foreshadowing the PRESENT-AGE-BETWEEN-THE-COMINGS — the time when the bridegroom is bodily absent and the bride waits in fasting-and-watching for the consummation that the wedding-of-the-Lamb of Hitgalut (Revelation) 19 will name.

*Let us be glad and rejoice, and give honour to him: for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready... Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.* (Revelation 19:7, 9)

**The fasting-discipline IN THE PRESENT AGE** (v.15c). *Then shall they fast.* The framework notes this verse-half is often missed in Christian commentary. Yahusha (Jesus) explicitly endorses fasting as a present-age discipline — the post-ascension assembly will fast (Acts 13:2-3, 14:23; the Didache 8:1 records twice-weekly fasts on Wednesdays and Fridays for the early assembly). The framework reads the verse as both Messianic-self-identification (bridegroom in their midst) AND endorsement-of-the-fasting-discipline-for-the-present-age. Fasting is not abolished; it is reframed in the wedding-architecture.

**The new-cloth parable** (v.16). *No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse.*

The framework reads the parable in the FASTING-CONTEXT Yahusha (Jesus) just gave it. The Christian commentary tradition has typically read this as *Torah is the old garment / Christianity is the new* — flatly antinomian and flatly wrong. The framework reads the parable in its actual context: the OLD GARMENT is the FASTING-PRACTICE of the Pharisaic-system; the NEW CLOTH is the bridegroom-joy of the kingdom-at-hand. Pinning the new bridegroom-joy onto the old fasting-practice would tear the old practice apart, because the joy of the bridegroom's presence requires a different mode than the mourning-fasting mode the Pharisaic-system was operating in.

**The new-wine and new-wineskins parable** (v.17). *Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.*

**This is the load-bearing framework crux of the chapter on the heart-Torah-not-abolition front.** The Christian commentary tradition has weaponized this parable as a proof-text for the abolition-of-Torah read — *new wine = the gospel / old wineskin = Judaism + Torah / the gospel cannot be contained in Torah-Judaism, therefore Torah is gone.* The framework rejects this read entirely. The reading collapses the King's own anti-antinomian declaration in the Sermon — *Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil* (Matthew 5:17). It collapses every prophet's promise of Torah-internalization. It collapses the Father's-character as the eternally-the-same. It is the antichrist reading the framework exists to dismantle.

**What the new wine IS in the framework reading.** The new wine is the heart-Torah-walking life Yahusha (Jesus) is teaching. It is the new-heart of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36.

It is the new-covenant Torah-internalization of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31.

**What the old wineskin IS in the framework reading.** The old wineskin is the Pharisaic-rabbinic-oral-tradition-system — the *takkanot* and *gezerot* and inherited-rabbinic-fences that built up around the Torah. It is the SYSTEM that cannot contain the heart-Torah-walking the King is teaching. The system's external-credential-architecture, its purity-fencing, its sacrifice-without-mercy, its categorical-classification-of-sinners — these cannot hold the heart-Torah-living kingdom-citizens. Old wineskin = the Pharisaic-system; the same architecture the King has been dismantling at the table with Mattityahu (Matthew); the same architecture that will become the Judaizer party in the apostolic age.

**What neither the wine nor the wineskin is.** The Torah itself is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin. The Torah is the SUBSTANCE the new wine carries. The new wine is the heart-Torah-walking; the substance OF the heart-Torah-walking is the Torah itself internalized. The *law put in their inward parts and written in their hearts* of Jeremiah 31 IS the Torah-internalized; the *statutes and judgments* of Ezekiel 36 the new spirit causes them to walk in ARE the Torah's commandments. The Christian-collapse that reads *new wine = grace / old wineskin = Torah* is a categorical-misreading at every level. Matthew 5:17-19 has already locked the Torah-affirmation; Matthew 9:17 cannot now be reread to undo it.

**Why the new wine cannot go into the old wineskin.** The Pharisaic-rabbinic-system was BUILT to contain external-credential-religiosity. It cannot contain heart-Torah-walking because heart-Torah-walking REJECTS the categorical-classification-of-sinners that the system depends on, REJECTS the credential-without-substance pattern, REJECTS the mercy-without-substance pattern Hosea 6:6 indicts. The bridegroom-joy bursts the system; the heart-substance tears the credential-fabric. A new container is needed — the new-heart kingdom-citizens.

**Both are preserved** (v.17b). The framework notes the closing phrase. New wine in new wineskins preserves BOTH. The Torah-substance is preserved (the Tanakh-foundation stands intact; the heart-Torah carries the Torah's full weight); the kingdom-citizens are preserved (the new-heart container holds the substance without being torn). The Spirit who lives in the new-heart kingdom-citizens is the Spirit who spoke at Sinai; the new-heart container does not lead its inhabitant away from what the Spirit spoke; both the substance and the citizen are preserved together. The new-wine and new-wineskins parable carries the Same teaching as Matthew 5:17-19 from a different metaphor-angle. The substance is one.

## §4. Yair's daughter and the hemorrhaging woman — the King's authority over death and the defiling flows

The framework reads the Yair-and-the-bleeding-woman intercalation as a deliberate-paired pericope. Mark 5:22 and Luke 8:41 identify the ruler as Ya'ir (*he will enlighten / awaken* — Hebrew *Ya'ir* from *or* light + *yiph'il*-causative). The framework notes the synagogue-ruler is a system-credentialed figure who recognizes the King — confirming the system-not-people posture; not all of the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) leadership is on the wrong side. Nakdimon (Nicodemus) at John 3 and Yoseph (Joseph) of Arimathea at Matthew 27:57-60 are the other framework-noted contemporary-leadership figures who turned. The credentials do not predetermine the hearing.

**The pattern of intercalation** (vv.18-26). Matthew's account compresses Mark 5:21-43 and Luke 8:40-56. Yair approaches; Yahusha (Jesus) follows; the hemorrhaging woman touches his garment en route; Yahusha (Jesus) responds to the woman; the journey continues to Yair's house; the maid is raised. Mark and Luke both record the time-pressure (the messenger arriving with *thy daughter is dead, why troublest thou the Master any further?* in Mark 5:35); Matthew's compression assumes the reader's familiarity with the synoptic pattern.

**The hemorrhaging woman's twelve-year condition** (vv.20-21). *And, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment: For she said within herself, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.*

Under the Torah, a chronic-discharge of blood from a woman renders her ritually-impure for as long as the discharge continues.

For twelve years this woman has been categorically-isolated under the purity-architecture — unable to participate in the temple-worship, unable to enter the assembly for normal religious-life, treated as defiling-by-contact in every social interaction.

The framework reads her approach in the same anti-defilement-pattern as the leper of Matthew 8:1-4. The Pharisaic-purity-system would have read her contact with Yahusha (Jesus) as DEFILING the rabbi — and would have read his response as therefore requiring withdrawal-and-ritual-cleansing. The framework reads the King's response as the SAME pattern Matthew 8:3 displayed: he is the cleansing-source, not the absorber-of-impurity. His presence reverses the defiling-flow rather than being defiled by it.

**The faith-naming** (v.22). *But Yahusha (Jesus) turned him about, and when he saw her, he said, Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole from that hour.*

The framework notes the vocative *Daughter* (*thugatēr*). Tender. The same kingdom-citizen-recognition pattern as the *son* address to the paralytic at v.2. The woman who had been categorically-excluded for twelve years is named DAUGHTER by the King. The reincorporation into the covenant-family is named at the moment of healing.

The *thy faith hath made thee whole* phrase carries the framework's hearing-reveals-not-creates pattern. The woman heard reports of the King's ministry; the hearing revealed what was already true; her *if I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole* is the response-of-the-sheep-to-the-Shepherd's-voice. The framework reads the faith here as not a creative-act-that-MAKES-her-a-sheep but the response-that-REVEALS her as one. The cause-and-effect distinguishes the truth from the false-inclusion-gospel: she heard because she was a sheep; her faith did not create her belonging; the response revealed what was already true before the foundation of the world.

**The twelve-year number-symmetry.** The hemorrhaging woman has been bleeding for twelve years (v.20); Yair's daughter is twelve years old (Mark 5:42 / Luke 8:42). The framework reads the number-symmetry as deliberate. Twelve is the covenant-completeness number — the number of the tribes. Both figures suffer in the duration of one daughter's complete-life-cycle; both are made whole through contact with the King; one woman has been suffering as long as the other has been ALIVE. The framework reads this as a marker of the gathering's-restorative-substance — the King restores what twelve-tribe-symbolic-time has produced as suffering.

**The flute-players and the noise** (v.23). *And when Yahusha (Jesus) came into the ruler's house, and saw the minstrels and the people making a noise.* The professional-mourners and the family-grief have set in. Matthew compresses; Mark 5:38 records the *people that wept and wailed greatly.* The first-century Yahudim (Jewish) funeral-practice involved hired flute-players (cf. Mishnah Ketubot 4:4) and the noise-of-mourning. The household has already moved into the funeral-rite stage.

**The *not dead, but sleepeth* teaching** (v.24). *He said unto them, Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn.*

The framework reads the death-as-sleep language as deliberate-eschatological-teaching anchored in the Tanakh's resurrection-stream.

*But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days.* (Daniel 12:13)

*Consider and hear me, O Yahuah (the LORD) my Elohim (God): lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death.* (Psalm 13:3)

The New Testament continuation of the sleep-language is consistent.

*These things said he: and after that he saith unto them, Our friend Lazaros (Lazarus) sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep.* (John 11:11)

*Behold, I shew you a mystery; We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed.* (1 Corinthians 15:51)

*But I would not have you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning them which are asleep, that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope. For if we believe that Yahusha (Jesus) died and rose again, even so them also which sleep in Yahusha (Jesus) will Elohim (God) bring with him.* (1 Thessalonians 4:13-14)

The framework reads the *not dead, but sleepeth* phrase as Yahusha's (Jesus's) framing of physical-death in the resurrection-architecture of the Tanakh-prophets. The maid is dead-in-medical-fact; Yahusha (Jesus) names her as sleeping because the resurrection is imminent at his word. The death-as-sleep framework holds at every register the Tanakh-and-apostolic-witness deploys: the patriarchs are asleep awaiting the first resurrection; the maid is asleep awaiting the immediate-awakening at the King's hand; the principle is one — the dead-in-the-covenant are sleeping, not finally-extinguished.

**The crowd's laughter** (v.24b). *They laughed him to scorn.* The framework notes the response. The contemporary-Yashar'el (Israel) community had been so saturated in the Greek-philosophical-encroachment of the period (the Sadducees explicitly rejecting bodily-resurrection per Matthew 22:23; the Hellenistic-mortalism reaching into broader culture) that the death-as-sleep eschatological-framing was already foreign to the popular-religious-imagination. The King's framing draws laughter; the framework reads this as another instance of the hearing-failure at the level of those who could not perceive the resurrection-architecture of the Tanakh-prophets.

**The raising** (vv.25-26). *But when the people were put forth, he went in, and took her by the hand, and the maid arose. And the fame hereof went abroad into all that land.*

The framework reads the touch-and-arise pattern. Yahusha (Jesus) takes her hand — physical-contact with a corpse, which under the Torah's ordinary purity-rules would have rendered him ritually-impure for seven days.

*He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days.* (Numbers 19:11)

The framework reads this in the same anti-defilement pattern as the leper (Matthew 8:3) and the hemorrhaging woman (v.22): the King's contact is the source of LIFE-FLOW rather than the absorber of death-flow; he is not defiled by the corpse — the corpse becomes alive.

The framework notes the credentialing-display of the raising-of-the-dead. The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 26 prophecy anchors the resurrection-substance.

The raising of Yair's daughter is the inaugural-display in the gospel's narrative-arc (the widow of Nain at Luke 7 and Lazaros (Lazarus) at John 11 will continue the credentialing); each is a present-age-foretaste of the resurrection-at-the-end-of-the-age.

## §5. The two blind men — *Son of David, have mercy on us*

The framework reads the two-blind-men pericope as the Messianic-credential extending into the *Son of David* recognition by the marginalized. The blind, the lame, the leprous, the demoniacs — those whom the system credentials cannot help — perceive what the credentialed-with-sight do not.

**The *Son of David* Tanakh-anchor.** The title is the direct Messianic-name from the Davidic-covenant stream. The foundational covenant stands at 2 Shemu'el (Samuel) 7.

*And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom. He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever. I will be his father, and he shall be my son: if he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men.* (2 Samuel 7:12-14)

Yeshayahu (Isaiah) carries the Davidic-Messianic stream forward.

*Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts) will perform this.* (Isaiah 9:7)

Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) carries it.

Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) carries it.

*And David my servant shall be king over them; and they all shall have one shepherd: they shall also walk in my judgments, and observe my statutes, and do them... and my servant David shall be their prince for ever.* (Ezekiel 37:24-25)

The blind men confess Yahusha (Jesus) as the Davidic-Messianic shepherd-and-Branch — direct prophetic-recognition. The framework reads this as the same hearing-pattern that has surfaced at every miraculous-recognition through Matthew 8-9: those whom the system has marginalized hear the Shepherd's voice; those who hold the credentials cannot.

**The *have mercy on us* substance.** The *eleos* (mercy / *chesed*) the blind men ask for is the SAME mercy-substance Yahusha (Jesus) just cited Hosea 6:6 about (v.13). The framework reads this as the kingdom-citizens' substance in action — the blind men ask for the substance the Pharisees were just told to learn.

**The faith-confession in dialogue** (vv.28-29). *And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him: and Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Believe ye that I am able to do this? They said unto him, Yea, Lord. Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you.*

The framework reads the dialogue. Yahusha (Jesus) does not heal them immediately on the road; he leads them to the house, brings them in, asks the explicit-confession question. *Believe ye that I am able to do this?* The framework reads this as deliberate-extraction of the verbal-confession to seal the hearing-pattern. The blind men's *yea, Lord* (*nai, kurie*) is the credentialing-confession that closes the pattern: they heard, they followed, they confessed, they received.

*According to your faith be it unto you.* The framework reads the verse against the cause-and-effect of the framework's reading on hearing. The blind men's faith is not the agent that CREATES the healing; the faith is the RESPONSE that reveals them as sheep and that aligns their reception with the King's will. The healing-flow comes from the King; the faith is the open-door for the flow to reach them. The framework reads the verse to rule out both the Christian-prosperity-gospel ABUSE of the verse (*you can have anything if your faith is strong enough*) AND the cessationist-Christian-suppression of the verse (*Jesus doesn't heal anymore so the principle no longer operates*).

**The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 35:5 credential continued.** The Messianic-credential-signature of the prophet stands at Isaiah 35.

*Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.* (Isaiah 35:5-6)

Yahusha (Jesus) will cite this same signature himself a few chapters downstream when Yochanan (John) the Immerser sends from prison to ask whether he is the one.

*Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Go and shew Yochanan (John) again those things which ye do hear and see: The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.* (Matthew 11:4-5)

The healing of the two blind men is one of the inaugurating-credential-displays of the kingdom-at-hand that the King will point Yochanan (John) back to.

**The disclosure-restraint pattern** (v.30b). *And Yahusha (Jesus) straitly charged them, saying, See that no man know it.* The framework reads the restraint-of-proclamation pattern. Yahusha (Jesus) is intentionally restraining the broad-circulation of the credentialing-miracles in the early ministry. The framework reads the pattern as timing-discipline — the kingdom-at-hand is being inaugurated; the full-disclosure-of-Messianic-identity is being staged across the gospel; the moment for the open-proclamation comes in stages. The pattern (William Wrede's scholarship of 1901 called it the *messianic secret*) is the framework's preferred name for what the gospel itself displays — Yahusha (Jesus) controls the disclosure-timing.

**The blind men's disobedience** (v.31). *But they, when they were departed, spread abroad his fame in all that country.* The healed-blind-men do not keep the silence Yahusha (Jesus) charged. The framework reads this not as a moral-failure but as the practical-pressure-of-credentialing-display — the change is so visible that holding it secret becomes impossible. The fame spreads despite the King's instruction; the staged-disclosure-timing absorbs the spread.

## §6. The mute demoniac and the *prince of the devils* slander — the first appearance of the blasphemy trajectory

The framework reads the mute-demoniac pericope as the first-appearance of the Beelzebul-attribution that Matthew 12:22-30 will dismantle with the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach warning. The same slander; the same Pharisaic-response-pattern; the trajectory begins here.

**The crowd's recognition.** *It was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel).* The multitudes name the credentialing-display at the Yashar'el-historical level — there has been no comparable demonstration in the people's collective memory. The framework reads this as legitimate-recognition that nonetheless does not yet reach the Formed-from-the-Formless identification. The crowd has the data but not yet the framework.

**The Pharisaic response.** *He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.* The framework reads the Pharisees' move as the credential-misattribution pattern. Confronted with a miracle they cannot deny, they attribute the source to the wrong agency. They know the demoniac is delivered; they cannot deny the visible-fact; they reattribute the source to Beelzebul.

The Beelzebul name itself is preserved in the Tanakh as a Philistine title — *Ba'al Zevuv,* the lord of the flies — from 2 Kings 1.

*And Achazyahu (Ahaziah) fell down through a lattice in his upper chamber that was in Shomron (Samaria), and was sick: and he sent messengers, and said unto them, Go, enquire of Ba'al Zevuv (Baal-zebub) the god of Ekron whether I shall recover of this disease... But the angel of Yahuah (the LORD) said to Eliyahu (Elijah) the Tishbi, Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Shomron (Samaria), and say unto them, Is it not because there is not an Elohim (God) in Yashar'el (Israel), that ye go to enquire of Ba'al Zevuv (Baal-zebub) the god of Ekron?* (2 Kings 1:2-3)

The Pharisaic-rhetorical move recycles the Tanakh's pejorative name for the Philistine demon-deity and applies it to the prince of demons in general — *Beelzebul* (lord of the high place) or *Beelzebub* (lord of the flies); both pejorative; both demonic.

The framework reads the attribution-failure as theologically-catastrophic. The DELIVERANCE from a demonic-affliction is, by definition, demons-being-defeated. To attribute the deliverance-source to the prince-of-demons is to claim the prince-of-demons is fighting his own kingdom — a self-contradicting claim Matthew 12 will dismantle directly.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand: And if Satan cast out Satan, he is divided against himself; how shall then his kingdom stand?* (Matthew 12:25-26)

The Pharisees are not making sense; they are reaching for any framework that lets them reject what they cannot deny.

**The first beat of the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach trajectory.** Matthew 9:34 plants the seed. Matthew 10:25 will name the Beelzebul-attribution against the King; Matthew 12:22-30 will dismantle the slander structurally; Matthew 12:31-32 will name the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach as the unforgivable-sin — the deliberate-attribution of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit)'s work to demonic agency, sustained against evidence, hardened against correction.

The framework reads the trajectory as the credential-system's-failure-pattern at full-display. The institutional-religious-system, when confronted with the Ruach (Spirit)'s public-credentialing of the King, will reach for ANY framework — even self-contradictory ones — that lets them reject the credentialing. The blasphemy-against-the-Ruach is the diagnostic-end-point of that pattern: the hardening into the position that what the Ruach is doing is demonic.

The framework notes this trajectory is operative not just historically but in the modern systems the framework is positioned to dismantle. Institutional Christianity does the same with heart-Torah-walking today — the Sabbath-keeping, the feast-walking, the restored sacred names, the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed — relabels it *legalism* or *Hebrew-Roots-deception* or *Judaizing* to maintain its categorical-rejection of what the Ruach (Spirit) is doing in the present age. The same attribution-failure operates against the Ruach's-work-in-the-present-age. The Pharisaic-pattern is the architecture; the modern systems are different costumes on the same architecture.

## §7. The harvest is plenteous, the labourers are few — the gathering-mission framing for Matthew 10

The framework reads the closing pericope as the deliberate-setup for the Matthew 10 commission of the twelve. Three framework-thick phrases lock the framing.

**First: *the gospel of the kingdom*** (v.35). The framework reads this in continuity with Matthew 4:23's same phrase — *good news of the gathering* per the framework's gathering-thesis. The gospel Yahusha (Jesus) is proclaiming is the news of the gathering of the scattered seed; the multitudes coming to him are the citizens of the gathering being drawn to the King.

**Second: *as sheep having no shepherd*** (v.36). Direct Tanakh-citation-stream of the prophetic-naming of leadership-failure. The original framing of the phrase stands at Mosheh's (Moses's) succession-prayer.

*Let Yahuah (the LORD), the Elohim (God) of the spirits of all flesh, set a man over the congregation, Which may go out before them, and which may go in before them, and which may lead them out, and which may bring them in; that the congregation of Yahuah (the LORD) be not as sheep which have no shepherd.* (Numbers 27:16-17)

The phrase recurs in the Ahab-leadership-failure visualized by Mikhayahu (Micaiah) the prophet.

*And he said, I saw all Yashar'el (Israel) scattered upon the hills, as sheep that have not a shepherd: and Yahuah (the LORD) said, These have no master: let them return every man to his house in peace.* (1 Kings 22:17)

Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) carries it.

*My people hath been lost sheep: their shepherds have caused them to go astray, they have turned them away on the mountains: they have gone from mountain to hill, they have forgotten their restingplace.* (Jeremiah 50:6)

And Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34 carries the prophetic-thunder at full-weight — the indictment and the restoration-promise as a single architecture.

*Son of Adam, prophesy against the shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel), prophesy, and say unto them, Thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) unto the shepherds; Woe be to the shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel) that do feed themselves! should not the shepherds feed the flocks? Ye eat the fat, and ye clothe you with the wool, ye kill them that are fed: but ye feed not the flock... My sheep wandered through all the mountains, and upon every high hill: yea, my flock was scattered upon all the face of the earth, and none did search or seek after them.* (Ezekiel 34:2-3, 6)

The Yechezk'el-34 vision is not only diagnostic — it is restoration-promise.

And the prophet Zekharyah (Zechariah) carries the same thread.

*For the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.* (Zechariah 10:2)

*Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow, saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts): smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered: and I will turn mine hand upon the little ones.* (Zechariah 13:7)

Yahusha (Jesus) sees the multitudes through the Yechezk'el-34 lens. The contemporary-leadership-failure is the same failure Yechezk'el indicted. The King himself is the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd; the multitudes have not yet been gathered to him; the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership has failed at the same diagnostic-point Yechezk'el named.

The framework reads *fainted, and were scattered abroad* (*eskulmenoi kai errimmenoi* — harassed and helpless, exhausted and thrown-down) as the present-condition of the multitudes Yahusha (Jesus) sees. The leadership-failure has produced the same symptom-cluster Yechezk'el described — the sheep are scattered, the sheep are wounded, the sheep are without protection. The King's compassion (*esplangchnisthē,* moved-in-the-gut) is the framework's portrait of the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd's response to the flock's condition.

**Third: *the harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few*** (v.37).

Direct harvest-language. The framework reads this in continuity with the Tanakh's harvest-stream.

*Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down; for the press is full, the fats overflow; for their wickedness is great.* (Joel 3:13)

The Sermon's own framing at Matthew 3:12 carries the wheat-and-chaff harvest pattern.

*Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.* (Matthew 3:12)

And John 4 carries the same harvest-language at the Samaritan-woman pericope.

*Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest.* (John 4:35)

The framework reads the harvest at Matthew 9:37 as the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed — the multitudes Yahusha (Jesus) just saw as sheep-without-shepherd are the harvest he is now naming. The laborers Yahusha (Jesus) tells his disciples to pray for are about to be commissioned at Matthew 10. The framework reads *the harvest is plenteous* as the gathering-substance-is-vast statement: the scattered seed are present in number; the workers to gather them are few. Pray for laborers — and then BE the laborers, as Matthew 10's commissioning will immediately enact.

**The *Lord of the harvest*** (v.38). The framework reads the phrase in framework register. The harvest-Lord is Yahuah (the LORD) — the gathering-Lord per the prophets' naming. Yahusha (Jesus) instructs the disciples to pray to the Lord of the harvest; the framework's nature-of-God reading holds — the Father (formless infinite) is the Lord of the harvest at the framework level, with the Formed (Yahusha (Jesus) in flesh) operating as the present-age shepherd executing the harvest under the Father's appointment.

The framework reads Matthew 9:35-38 as the deliberate-narrative-hinge that the Matthew 10 commission immediately answers. The disciples are about to be sent. The harvest-naming and the labourer-prayer establish the substance of what they are about to do. Pray for laborers. The next sentence (Matthew 10:1) sends the twelve. The chapter is the gathering's-front-door for the commission that the next chapter will execute. The Shepherd has come. The harvest is ready. The labourers go.
