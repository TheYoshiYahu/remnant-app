## §1. The paralytic healed and forgiven — the Formed Voice's authority to forgive sins

*And he entered into a ship, and passed over, and came into his own city. And, behold, they brought to him a man sick of the palsy, lying on a bed: and Yahusha (Jesus) seeing their faith said unto the sick of the palsy; Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee* (vv.1-2).

The framework reads the paralytic-healing as the deliberate Matthean-narrative-staging of the FORGIVENESS-CLAIM that the post-Sermon ministry now lays down explicitly. The leper-cleansing of Matt 8:1-4 demonstrated the King's authority over the purity-architecture's defiling-flows; the centurion-pericope demonstrated his authority over disease-at-distance; the storm-stilling demonstrated his authority over creation; the Gergesene exorcism demonstrated his authority over the principalities. Matt 9:1-8 now demonstrates the King's authority to FORGIVE SINS — the Yahuah's-prerogative (Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 43:25 — *I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions for mine own sake, and will not remember thy sins*; Tehillim (Psalm) 103:3 — *Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases*) demonstrated in flesh.

**The faith Yahusha (Jesus) sees** (v.2). Matthew compresses the Markan detail (Mark 2:4 — *they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed wherein the sick of the palsy lay*). The carrier-friends have brought the paralytic through obstacle to the King; their faith is the operative-variable Yahusha (Jesus) sees. The framework reads this as the seed-war-hearing-pattern operating at the group level — the carriers heard the Shepherd's voice (John 10:27); the hearing revealed what was already true; the response to the hearing was the immediate physical-action of bringing the suffering-one to the King.

**The vocative address** *Son* (*teknon*, child). Tender. The framework reads this against the *Lord-Lord* / *I never knew you* pattern of Matt 7:21-23 — there is a kindness Yahusha (Jesus) extends to the sheep that the institutional-system never extended. The paralytic is addressed as *son* before he is healed, before any credentialed-status is offered, before any verbal-confession is required from him. The Shepherd recognizes the sheep.

**The forgiveness-pronouncement before the healing.** *Thy sins be forgiven thee* (v.2). Yahusha (Jesus) speaks the forgiveness BEFORE the healing. The framework reads this carefully. The Christian commentary tradition has often spiritualized this into the *real* healing being the spiritual one, with the physical-healing as accompaniment. The framework reads it as deliberate-ordering for theological-display: the forgiveness comes first because the scribes' internal-thought-charge of blasphemy is the response Yahusha (Jesus) is going to use as the pedagogical-moment.

**The scribes' internal charge** (v.3). *Some of the scribes said within themselves, This man blasphemeth.* The framework notes that the Yahudim (Jewish)-leadership-system's theological-premise here is CORRECT. Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 43:25 lays out Yahuah's (the LORD's) self-naming as the SOLE-FORGIVER of sins. The framework's `pharisaic-sadducean-system-as-judaizer-predecessor` thread does not deny the scribes' competence with the Tanakh's framework on this point; their problem is not theological-illiteracy on the forgiveness-prerogative. Their problem is the seed-war-failure-to-hear at the level of WHO is making the claim. They have the right premise; they reach the wrong conclusion because they cannot perceive the Formed-Voice-of-Sinai standing in front of them.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the *which is easier* question** (vv.4-5). *Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts? For whether is easier, to say, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk?*

The framework reads the rhetorical question with care. Both pronouncements are equally impossible for a mere-man, equally possible for the Formed. The question is not which is theoretically-easier; the question is which can be VERIFIED. The forgiveness-claim cannot be visibly verified at the moment of speaking. The healing-claim CAN be visibly verified. By performing the visibly-verifiable miracle, Yahusha (Jesus) LEGITIMATES the un-visibly-verifiable claim that preceded it. *That ye may know that the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins, (then saith he to the sick of the palsy,) Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house* (v.6).

**The *Son of Adam* claim per Red Line #12.** *Son of man* is restored to *Son of Adam* — the lineage-weight preserved. The Son of Adam, the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise, exercises the Father's-prerogative to forgive sins. The framework's Formed-from-the-Formless reading is direct: the Son of Adam has Elohim-level forgiveness-authority because he IS the Formed who spoke the Torah's expiation-architecture into being at Sinai. The scribes' theological-premise (only Yahuah (the LORD) forgives sins) is preserved AND verified — the one in front of them is the Formed-Voice that spoke at Sinai.

The framework notes the public-disclosure-pattern. Yahusha (Jesus) does not yet say *because I am Yahuah (the LORD).* He says *the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins.* The Son-of-Adam title carries the lineage-weight of the seed-of-Adam-promise; it also carries the figure-named-in-the-prophets (Daniel 7:13's kbar enash per Red Line #12's kaph-as-incarnation-honoring) without yet collapsing the disclosure into the explicit Yahuah-identification. The disclosure is being staged across the gospel; Matt 9 is one beat of the staging.

**The crowd's response** (v.8). *But when the multitudes saw it, they marvelled, and glorified Elohim (God), which had given such power unto men.* The multitudes' framing is partial — they perceive the authority-display but interpret it within the *given to men* register. They have not yet reached the Formed-from-the-Formless recognition; they are at the *prophet-with-divine-authority* stage of understanding. The framework reads this as the staged-disclosure progressing — the gospel will surface the fuller recognition at moments like Matt 16:16 (Kefa's (Peter's) confession), Matt 17:5 (the transfiguration voice), and Matt 28:9 (the resurrection-worship). For now, the multitudes glorify Elohim (God); the staged-disclosure continues.

## §2. The calling of Mattityahu (Matthew) the tax-collector — *I will have mercy, and not sacrifice*

*And as Yahusha (Jesus) passed forth from thence, he saw a man, named Mattityahu (Matthew), sitting at the receipt of custom: and he saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him* (v.9).

The framework reads the Mattityahu-calling as the deliberate-display of the system-not-people posture made flesh. Tax-collectors in the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community were despised on two grounds. First, they collected tribute for Roman occupation — a collaborator-with-the-occupying-power role that placed them in the categorical-traitor class to the kingdom-aspirations of contemporary Yahudim (Jewish) nationalism. Second, the Pharisaic-purity-system treated them as defiling because they handled Hellenistic-coin-currency with imperial-cult imagery and they interacted continually with Gentile travelers and merchants. Mattityahu was a Yahudi (Jew) in the categorical-sinner class by Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness standards.

Yahusha (Jesus) calls him to be among the twelve. The framework reads this as deliberate-system-defiance. The kingdom-citizens-roster has just been named at the Beatitudes (Matt 5:3-12) — the *anawim,* the Ezekiel-9 mourners, the *tsedaqah*-hungerers, the *chesed*-merciful, the new-heart pure. None of those markers exclude the tax-collector who hears the Shepherd's voice and rises to follow. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness's CATEGORICAL exclusion of the tax-collector class is precisely what the King is dismantling.

**The meal at Mattityahu's house** (v.10). *And it came to pass, as Yahusha (Jesus) sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.*

The framework reads the meal-fellowship as direct-defiance of the Pharisaic-purity-system's social-segregation-architecture. Table-fellowship in the second-temple Yahudim (Jewish) world was a covenant-affirming act; the Pharisaic-system extended this by restricting table-fellowship to the credentialed-as-ritually-pure. Yahusha (Jesus) breaks the social-segregation in deliberate-public-fashion at the home of a tax-collector with *many publicans and sinners* present. The framework reads the *sinners* (*hamartoloi*) as the Pharisaic-classification-category — those marginalized by the system's purity-credentials — not as a moral-judgment-confirmed-from-above. The framework's `victims-not-enemies` posture from the voice skill applies directly: the tax-collectors-and-sinners are the lost sheep; the system's classification of them is the system's failure, not the substance.

**The Pharisees' challenge** (v.11). *And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?* The framework notes the Pharisees address the DISCIPLES rather than the King — the indirection-pattern that will recur (Matt 9:14 the disciples-of-Yochanan also use this approach; Matt 15:2 the Pharisaic-challenge on hand-washing addresses Yahusha (Jesus) about the disciples). The challenge-by-proxy is itself a Pharisaic-mannerism. Yahusha (Jesus) does not let the indirection succeed; he responds directly.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the physician-and-the-sick metaphor** (v.12). *They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.* The framework reads the metaphor in framework-irony register. The *whole* are the Pharisees in their own self-classification; the *sick* are the tax-collectors-and-sinners. Yahusha (Jesus) is using the Pharisees' OWN self-categorization to indict them — *you have classified them as sick and yourselves as whole; on your own logic, my ministry to the sick is precisely what they need.* The deeper irony is that the *whole* in their own eyes are spiritually-sick at the diagnostic-level Yahusha (Jesus) just laid down at Matt 7:21-23 (*workers of torahlessness* in *anomia*-disposition); the *sick* in their classification are the ones whose ears can be opened to the Shepherd's voice.

**The Hosea 6:6 citation** (v.13). *But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.*

This is the framework crux of the chapter on the diagnostic-system. Yahusha (Jesus) cites Hosea 6:6 — *I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of Elohim (God) more than burnt offerings* — and tells the Pharisees to *learn what that meaneth.* He will cite the same verse again at Matt 12:7 in the Shabbat-controversy context. The Hosea 6:6 citation is the recurring Tanakh-prophetic-anchor Yahusha (Jesus) deploys against the Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness.

**The Tanakh-stream of the citation.** Hosea 6:6 sits within the Tanakh's prophetic-critique of hollow-religiosity. The framework reads the stream:

- 1 Shemu'el (Samuel) 15:22 — *Hath Yahuah (the LORD) as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of Yahuah (the LORD)? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.* The first articulation of the obedience-over-sacrifice principle.
- Tehillim (Psalm) 40:6 — *Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire; mine ears hast thou opened: burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required.* The Tanakh's heart-substance over ritual-substance theme.
- Tehillim (Psalm) 51:16-17 — *Thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering. The sacrifices of Elohim (God) are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O Elohim (God), thou wilt not despise.* David at the heart-substance.
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 1:11-17 — *To what purpose is the multitude of your sacrifices unto me? saith Yahuah (the LORD): I am full of the burnt offerings of rams ... Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil; learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.* The full prophetic-thunder on the hollow-ritual-without-heart-substance.
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 7:21-23 — *For I spake not unto your fathers, nor commanded them in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices: But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voice, and I will be your Elohim (God), and ye shall be my people.* The covenant-substance prior to the sacrifice-architecture.
- Amos 5:21-24 — *I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies ... But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.*
- Mikhah (Micah) 6:6-8 — *Wherewith shall I come before Yahuah (the LORD), and bow myself before the high Elohim (God)? ... He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth Yahuah (the LORD) require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)?*

Yahusha (Jesus) is standing in this prophetic-stream when he cites Hosea 6:6. The Pharisaic-counterfeit-righteousness's failure is precisely the failure these prophets indicted — external-religiosity-without-heart-substance, sacrifice-without-mercy, credential-without-covenant.

The framework reads the citation as ALSO the implicit-naming of the diagnostic the kingdom-citizens carry. The heart-Torah of the Sermon on the Mount IS the *mercy-and-knowledge-of-Elohim (God)* substance Hosea named. The new-heart of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 IS the mercy-substance the King is teaching the disciples to walk in. The Pharisees' *go and learn what that meaneth* is the Tanakh-pedagogical-prescription — they have the text; they have not learned the substance.

**The *not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance*** (v.13b). The framework reads the irony at full weight. The *righteous* in their own eyes cannot HEAR the call to repent because they perceive themselves as not needing repentance. The *sinners* — those whose lives have not gone according to the Pharisaic-script — can hear because they know they need to. The seed-war-hearing-pattern operates here: the sheep hear when the Shepherd calls; the Pharisaic-self-credentialed cannot hear because the Shepherd's call addresses what they refuse to admit they are.

The framework reads Mattityahu's own response as the contrast-pattern in person. He was at the tax-collection booth — categorically-classified as sinner; he rose immediately and followed. The KING'S voice reached the ears the system had written off. The system's righteous remained at the meal-perimeter critiquing the host-list.

## §3. The fasting question and the new-wineskins parables — the bridegroom in present age, and the new-heart container

*Then came to him the disciples of Yochanan (John), saying, Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, but thy disciples fast not?* (v.14).

The framework reads the fasting-question with care. The disciples-of-Yochanan join the indirection-pattern (Matt 9:11) — they ask about Yahusha's (Jesus's) practice rather than directly questioning him. The Pharisees and Yochanan (John)'s disciples were both observing Pharisaic-style twice-weekly voluntary fasts on Mondays and Thursdays (per second-temple-period practice, attested in Luke 18:12 and the Didache 8:1). Yahusha's (Jesus's) disciples were not. The challenge is operating from the assumption that this voluntary-fasting is the standard-of-piety.

**Yahusha's (Jesus's) response — the bridegroom-self-identification** (v.15). *Can the children of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.*

The bridegroom-language is direct Messianic-self-claim. The framework reads the Tanakh-anchors:

- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 62:5 — *As the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy Elohim (God) rejoice over thee.* Yahuah (the LORD) is the bridegroom of Yashar'el (Israel) in the Tanakh's covenant-substance.
- Hosea 2:19-20 — *And I will betroth thee unto me for ever; yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies. I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness: and thou shalt know Yahuah (the LORD).* The betrothal-restoration-of-divorced-Yashar'el (Israel) prophecy — directly relevant to the framework's `scattered-seed-gathering` thread.
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 54:5 — *For thy Maker is thine husband; Yahuah (the LORD) of hosts is his name.*
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 3:14 — *Turn, O backsliding children, saith Yahuah (the LORD); for I am married unto you.*

Yahusha (Jesus) identifies himself as the BRIDEGROOM. The framework's nature-of-God reading lands directly: the Yahuah-of-the-Tanakh whose bridegroom-substance the prophets named is the Formed-in-flesh standing in front of them. The bridegroom-of-Yashar'el (Israel) is the King-at-table-with-the-sinners.

**The *bridegroom shall be taken from them* phrase** (v.15b) is the first explicit-foreshadowing of the cross in Matthew's gospel narrative-arc. The framework reads this carefully. The disciples-of-Yochanan and the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) community would not yet hear the cross-foreshadowing; they would hear it as some kind of absence. The framework reads the verse as Yahusha (Jesus) ALSO foreshadowing the PRESENT-AGE-BETWEEN-THE-COMINGS — the time when the bridegroom is bodily absent and the bride waits in fasting-and-watching for the consummation. The wedding-of-the-Lamb of Hitgalut (Revelation) 19:7-9 is the consummation; the present age is the between-time.

**The fasting-discipline IN THE PRESENT AGE** (v.15c). *Then shall they fast.* The framework notes this verse-half is often missed in Christian commentary. Yahusha (Jesus) explicitly endorses fasting as a present-age discipline — the post-ascension assembly will fast (Acts 13:2-3, 14:23; the Didache 8:1 records twice-weekly fasts on Wednesdays and Fridays for the early assembly). The framework reads the verse as both Messianic-self-identification (bridegroom in their midst) AND endorsement-of-the-fasting-discipline-for-the-present-age. Fasting is not abolished; it is reframed in the wedding-architecture.

**The new-cloth parable** (v.16). *No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse.*

The framework reads the parable in the FASTING-CONTEXT Yahusha (Jesus) just gave it. The Christian commentary tradition has typically read this as *Torah is the old garment / Christianity is the new* — flatly antinomian and flatly wrong. The framework reads the parable in its actual context: the OLD GARMENT is the FASTING-PRACTICE of the Pharisaic-system; the NEW CLOTH is the bridegroom-joy of the kingdom-at-hand. Pinning the new bridegroom-joy onto the old fasting-practice would tear the old practice apart, because the joy of the bridegroom's presence requires a different mode than the mourning-fasting mode the Pharisaic-system was operating in.

**The new-wine and new-wineskins parable** (v.17). *Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.*

**This is the load-bearing framework crux of the chapter on the heart-Torah-not-abolition front.** The Christian commentary tradition has weaponized this parable as a proof-text for the abolition-of-Torah read — *new wine = the gospel / old wineskin = Judaism + Torah / the gospel cannot be contained in Torah-Judaism, therefore Torah is gone.* The framework rejects this read entirely.

**What the new wine IS in the framework reading.** The new wine is the heart-Torah-walking life Yahusha (Jesus) is teaching. It is the new-heart of Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 36:26-27 — *A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you ... And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* It is the new-covenant Torah-internalization of Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 31:33 — *I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts.*

**What the old wineskin IS in the framework reading.** The old wineskin is the Pharisaic-rabbinic-oral-tradition-system — the *takkanot* and *gezerot* and inherited-rabbinic-fences that built up around the Torah. It is the SYSTEM that cannot contain the heart-Torah-walking the King is teaching. The system's external-credential-architecture, its purity-fencing, its sacrifice-without-mercy, its categorical-classification-of-sinners — these cannot hold the heart-Torah-living kingdom-citizens. Old wineskin = the Pharisaic-system per the `pharisaic-sadducean-system-as-judaizer-predecessor` thread.

**What neither the wine nor the wineskin is.** The Torah itself is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin. The Torah is the SUBSTANCE the new wine carries. The new wine is the heart-Torah-walking; the substance OF the heart-Torah-walking is the Torah itself internalized. The Christian-collapse that reads *new wine = grace / old wineskin = Torah* is a categorical-misreading at every level. Matt 5:17-19 (the load-bearing anti-antinomian declaration of the Sermon) has already locked the Torah-affirmation; Matt 9:17 cannot now be reread to undo it.

**Why the new wine cannot go into the old wineskin.** The Pharisaic-rabbinic-system was BUILT to contain external-credential-religiosity. It cannot contain heart-Torah-walking because heart-Torah-walking REJECTS the categorical-classification-of-sinners that the system depends on, REJECTS the credential-without-substance pattern, REJECTS the mercy-without-substance pattern Hosea 6:6 indicts. The bridegroom-joy bursts the system; the heart-substance tears the credential-fabric. A new container is needed — the new-heart kingdom-citizens.

**Both are preserved** (v.17b). The framework notes the closing phrase. New wine in new wineskins preserves BOTH. The Torah-substance is preserved (the Tanakh-foundation stands intact; the heart-Torah carries the Torah's full weight); the kingdom-citizens are preserved (the new-heart container holds the substance without being torn). The framework reads this as the both-and architecture the Torah-and-Spirit harmony Red Line #6 names — the Spirit does not lead away from Torah; the new-heart container is for the heart-Torah-walking life; both the substance and the citizen are preserved together.

The parable joins the `heart-torah-not-abolition` thread from Matt 5 directly. The new-wine and new-wineskins teaching is the SAME teaching as Matt 5:17-19 from a different metaphor-angle. The substance is one.

## §4. Yair's daughter and the hemorrhaging woman — the King's authority over death and the defiling flows

*While he spake these things unto them, behold, there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him, saying, My daughter is even now dead: but come and lay thy hand upon her, and she shall live. And Yahusha (Jesus) arose, and followed him, and so did his disciples* (vv.18-19).

The framework reads the Yair-and-the-bleeding-woman intercalation as a deliberate-paired pericope. Mark 5:22 and Luke 8:41 identify the ruler as Ya'ir (*he will enlighten / awaken* — Hebrew *Ya'ir* from *or* light + *yiph'il*-causative). The framework notes the synagogue-ruler is a system-credentialed figure who recognizes the King — confirming the system-not-people posture; not all of the contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) leadership is on the wrong side. Nicodemus (John 3) and Yoseph (Joseph) of Arimathea (Matt 27:57-60) are the other framework-noted contemporary-leadership figures who turned.

**The pattern of intercalation** (vv.18-26). Matthew's account compresses Mark 5:21-43 and Luke 8:40-56. Yair approaches; Yahusha (Jesus) follows; the hemorrhaging woman touches his garment en route; Yahusha (Jesus) responds to the woman; the journey continues to Yair's house; the maid is raised. Mark and Luke both record the time-pressure (the messenger arriving with *thy daughter is dead, why troublest thou the Master any further?* in Mark 5:35); Matthew's compression assumes the reader's familiarity with the synoptic pattern.

**The hemorrhaging woman's twelve-year condition** (vv.20-21). *And, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment: For she said within herself, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.*

Under Vayikra (Leviticus) 15:25-27, a chronic-discharge of blood from a woman renders her ritually-impure for as long as the discharge continues, and any seat or bed she touches, and any person who touches what she has touched, also becomes impure until the evening. For twelve years this woman has been categorically-isolated under the purity-architecture — unable to participate in the temple-worship, unable to enter the assembly for normal religious-life, treated as defiling-by-contact in every social interaction.

The framework reads her approach in the same anti-defilement-pattern as the leper of Matt 8:1-4. The Pharisaic-purity-system would have read her contact with Yahusha (Jesus) as DEFILING the rabbi — and would have read his response as therefore requiring withdrawal-and-ritual-cleansing. The framework reads the King's response as the SAME pattern Matt 8:3 displayed: he is the cleansing-source, not the absorber-of-impurity. His presence reverses the defiling-flow rather than being defiled by it.

**The faith-naming** (v.22). *Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole from that hour.*

The framework notes the vocative *Daughter* (*thugatēr*). Tender. The same kingdom-citizen-recognition pattern as the *son* address to the paralytic at v.2. The woman who had been categorically-excluded for twelve years is named DAUGHTER by the King. The reincorporation into the covenant-family is named at the moment of healing.

The *thy faith hath made thee whole* is the seed-war-hearing-pattern at v.2's level: she heard the Shepherd's voice (in the reports of his ministry — *if I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole*); the hearing revealed what was already true. The framework reads the faith here as not a creative-act-that-MAKES-her-a-sheep but the response-that-REVEALS her as one. Hearing reveals; hearing does not create (per Red Line #11).

**The twelve-year number-symmetry.** The hemorrhaging woman has been bleeding for twelve years (vv.20); Yair's daughter is twelve years old (Mark 5:42 / Luke 8:42). The framework reads the number-symmetry as deliberate. The framework notes the twelve-tribes resonance — twelve as the covenant-completeness number. Both figures suffer in the duration of one daughter's complete-life-cycle; both are made whole through contact with the King; one woman has been suffering as long as the other has been ALIVE. Mishnah Ketubot 4:4) and the noise-of-mourning. The household has already moved into the funeral-rite stage.

**The *not dead, but sleepeth* teaching** (v.24). *He said unto them, Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn.*

The framework reads the death-as-sleep language as deliberate-eschatological-teaching. The Tanakh-stream of death-as-sleep:

- Daniel 12:2 — *And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.* The resurrection-prophecy's sleep-language.
- Daniel 12:13 — *But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days.*
- Tehillim (Psalm) 13:3 — *Consider and hear me, O Yahuah (the LORD) my Elohim (God): lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death.*

The New Testament continuation of the sleep-language:

- 1 Thessalonians 4:13-14 — *I would not have you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning them which are asleep ... them also which sleep in Yahusha (Jesus) will Elohim (God) bring with him.*

The maid is dead-in-medical-fact; Yahusha (Jesus) names her as sleeping because the resurrection is imminent at his word. The framework's posture on death-and-resurrection from Red Line #11 (the natural branches asleep awaiting the first resurrection) lands here at the personal-level for Yair's daughter — death is sleep before the awakening, whether the awakening is in this present-age beat (as it is for Yair's daughter) or at the eschatological-resurrection (as it is for the patriarchs).

## §5. The two blind men — *Son of David, have mercy on us*

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, Thou Son of David, have mercy on us* (v.27).

The framework reads the two-blind-men pericope as the Messianic-credential extending into the *Son of David* recognition by the marginalized. The blind, the lame, the leprous, the demoniacs — those whom the system credentials cannot help — perceive what the credentialed-with-sight do not.

**The *Son of David* Tanakh-anchor.** The title is the direct Messianic-name from the Davidic-covenant stream:

- 2 Shemu'el (Samuel) 7:12-16 — *I will set up thy seed after thee ... I will be his father, and he shall be my son ... And thine house and thy kingdom shall be established for ever before thee: thy throne shall be established for ever.* The foundational Davidic covenant.
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 9:7 — *Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom.*
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 11:1-10 — *And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Yishai (Jesse), and a Branch shall grow out of his roots* — the Davidic-shoot-Messianic prophecy.
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 23:5-6 — *Behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth* — the Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS) Messianic-prophecy.
- Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34:23-24 — *And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David; he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd.*
- Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 37:24-25 — *And David my servant shall be king over them ... and David my servant shall be their prince for ever.*

The blind men confess Yahusha (Jesus) as the Davidic-Messiah — direct prophetic-recognition. The framework reads this as the same seed-war-hearing pattern that has surfaced at every miraculous-recognition through Matt 8-9: those whom the system has marginalized hear the Shepherd's voice; those who hold the credentials cannot.

**The *have mercy on us* substance.** The *eleos* (mercy / *chesed*) the blind men ask for is the SAME mercy-substance Yahusha (Jesus) just cited Hosea 6:6 about (v.13). The framework reads this as the kingdom-citizens' substance in action — the blind men ask for the substance the Pharisees were just told to learn.

**The faith-confession in dialogue** (vv.28-29). *And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him: and Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Believe ye that I am able to do this? They said unto him, Yea, Lord. Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you.*

The framework reads the dialogue. Yahusha (Jesus) does not heal them immediately on the road; he leads them to the house, brings them in, asks the explicit-confession question. *Believe ye that I am able to do this?* The framework reads this as deliberate-extraction of the verbal-confession to seal the seed-war-hearing-pattern. The blind men's *yea, Lord* (*nai, kurie*) is the credentialing-confession that closes the pattern: they heard, they followed, they confessed, they received.

*According to your faith be it unto you.* The framework reads the verse against Red Line #11's hearing-reveals-not-creates pattern. The blind men's faith is not the agent that CREATES the healing; the faith is the RESPONSE that reveals them as sheep and that aligns their reception with the King's will. The healing-flow comes from the King; the faith is the open-door for the flow to reach them. The framework reads the verse to rule out both the Christian-prosperity-gospel ABUSE of the verse (*you can have anything if your faith is strong enough*) AND the cessationist-Christian-suppression of the verse (*Jesus doesn't heal anymore so the principle no longer operates*).

**The Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 35:5 credential continued.** *Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened.* The Messianic-credential the Suffering-Servant Isaiah-chapter named at Matt 8:17 (Isaiah 53:4) and that Yahusha (Jesus) will cite himself at Matt 11:5 as the answer to Yochanan (John)'s prison-question. The healing of the two blind men is one of the inaugurating-credential-displays of the kingdom-at-hand.

**The messianic-secret pattern** (v.30b). *And Yahusha (Jesus) straitly charged them, saying, See that no man know it.* The framework reads the restraint-of-proclamation pattern. Yahusha (Jesus) is intentionally restraining the broad-circulation of the credentialing-miracles in the early ministry. The framework reads the pattern as timing-discipline — the kingdom-at-hand is being inaugurated; the full-disclosure-of-Messianic-identity is being staged across the gospel; the moment for the open-proclamation comes in stages. The *messianic secret* pattern (as scholarship since William Wrede has called it) is the framework's preferred name for what the gospel itself displays — Yahusha (Jesus) controls the disclosure-timing.

**The blind men's disobedience** (v.31). *But they, when they were departed, spread abroad his fame in all that country.* The healed-blind-men do not keep the silence Yahusha (Jesus) charged. The framework reads this not as a moral-failure but as the practical-pressure-of-credentialing-display — the change is so visible that holding it secret becomes impossible. The fame spreads despite the King's instruction; the staged-disclosure-timing absorbs the spread.

## §6. The mute demoniac and the *prince of the devils* slander — the first appearance of the blasphemy trajectory

*As they went out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed with a devil. And when the devil was cast out, the dumb spake: and the multitudes marvelled, saying, It was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel). But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils* (vv.32-34).

The framework reads the mute-demoniac pericope as the first-appearance of the Beelzebul-attribution that Matt 12:22-30 will dismantle with the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach warning. The same slander; the same Pharisaic-response-pattern; the trajectory begins here.

**The crowd's recognition.** *It was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel).* The multitudes name the credentialing-display at the Yashar'el-historical level — there has been no comparable demonstration in the people's collective memory. The framework reads this as legitimate-recognition that nonetheless does not yet reach the Formed-from-the-Formless identification. The crowd has the data but not yet the framework.

**The Pharisaic response.** *He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.* The framework reads the Pharisees' move as the credential-misattribution pattern. Confronted with a miracle they cannot deny, they attribute the source to the wrong agency. The framework reads this against the same `scripture-twisting-and-the-harmonization-discipline` thread from Matt 4 — the Pharisees know the demoniac is delivered; they cannot deny the visible-fact; they reattribute the source to Beelzebul (*Ba'al Zevuv,* the Philistine name preserved at 2 Kings 1:2-3, 16 — *lord of the flies*; alternatively *Ba'al Zevul,* lord of the high place; both names are pejorative Pharisaic-rhetoric against demonic-authority in general).

The framework reads the attribution-failure as theologically-catastrophic. The DELIVERANCE from a demonic-affliction is, by definition, demons-being-defeated. To attribute the deliverance-source to the prince-of-demons is to claim the prince-of-demons is fighting his own kingdom — a self-contradicting claim Matt 12:25-26 will dismantle directly. The Pharisees are not making sense; they are reaching for any framework that lets them reject what they cannot deny.

**The first beat of the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach trajectory.** Matt 9:34 plants the seed. Matt 10:25 will name the Beelzebul-attribution against the King; Matt 12:22-30 will dismantle the slander structurally; Matt 12:31-32 will name the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach as the unforgivable-sin — the deliberate-attribution of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit)'s work to demonic agency, sustained against evidence, hardened against correction.

The framework reads the trajectory as the seed-war-pattern at full-display. The institutional-religious-system, when confronted with the Ruach (Spirit)'s public-credentialing of the King, will reach for ANY framework — even self-contradictory ones — that lets them reject the credentialing. The blasphemy-against-the-Ruach is the diagnostic-end-point of that pattern: the hardening into the position that what the Ruach is doing is demonic. The framework notes this trajectory is operative not just historically but in the modern Judaizer-movement diagnostic — the institutional-Christian system's continual-rebranding of heart-Torah-walking as legalism, of restored-names as Hebrew-Roots-deception, of the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed as antisemitism — the same attribution-failure operating against the Ruach's-work-in-the-present-age.

## §7. The harvest is plenteous, the labourers are few — the gathering-mission framing for Matt 10

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people. But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd* (vv.35-36).

The framework reads the closing pericope as the deliberate-setup for the Matt 10 commission of the twelve. Three framework-thick phrases lock the framing.

**First: *the gospel of the kingdom*** (v.35). The framework reads this in continuity with Matt 4:23's same phrase — *good news of the gathering* per the framework's gathering-thesis. The gospel Yahusha (Jesus) is proclaiming is the news of the gathering of the scattered seed; the multitudes coming to him are the citizens of the gathering being drawn to the King. The framework's `scattered-seed-gathering` thread carries this across the gospel.

**Second: *as sheep having no shepherd*** (v.36). Direct Tanakh-citation-stream of the prophetic-naming of leadership-failure:

- Bemidbar (Numbers) 27:17 — Mosheh (Moses) at the threshold of his death asks Yahuah (the LORD) to provide a successor *that the congregation of Yahuah (the LORD) be not as sheep which have no shepherd.* The original framing of the phrase.
- 1 Melekhim (Kings) 22:17 — Mikhayahu (Micaiah) the prophet's vision: *I saw all Yashar'el (Israel) scattered upon the hills, as sheep that have not a shepherd.* The Ahab-leadership-failure visualized.
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 50:6 — *My people hath been lost sheep: their shepherds have caused them to go astray.* The exile-leadership-failure named.
- Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34:1-10 — the full prophetic-thunder against the false-shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel) who *fed themselves, and fed not my flock* (v.8). Yahuah (the LORD) declares he will *deliver my flock from their mouth, that they may not be meat for them* (v.10).
- Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) 34:23 — *And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David; he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd.* The Davidic-Messianic shepherd-restoration prophecy.
- Zekharyah (Zechariah) 10:2 — *Therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.*
- Zekharyah (Zechariah) 13:7 — *Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow, saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts): smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered.* The smitten-shepherd-scattered-sheep prophecy Yahusha (Jesus) will cite at Matt 26:31.

Yahusha (Jesus) sees the multitudes through the Yechezk'el-34 lens. The contemporary-leadership-failure is the same failure Yechezk'el indicted. The King himself is the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd; the multitudes have not yet been gathered to him; the Pharisaic-Sadducean-leadership has failed at the same diagnostic-point Yechezk'el named.

The framework reads *fainted, and were scattered abroad* (*eskulmenoi kai errimmenoi* — harassed and helpless, exhausted and thrown-down) as the present-condition of the multitudes Yahusha (Jesus) sees. The leadership-failure has produced the same symptom-cluster Yechezk'el described — the sheep are scattered, the sheep are wounded, the sheep are without protection. The King's compassion (*esplangchnisthē,* moved-in-the-gut) is the framework's portrait of the Yechezk'el-34 shepherd's response to the flock's condition.

**Third: *the harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few*** (v.37). Direct harvest-language. The framework reads this in continuity with the Tanakh's harvest-stream:

- Yoel (Joel) 3:13 — *Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe.* Eschatological-harvest-language.
- Yeshayahu (Isaiah) 27:12 — *And ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Yashar'el (Israel).* The gathering-of-the-scattered-seed under the harvest-figure.
- The Sermon's own framing at Matt 3:12 — *whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.* The wheat-and-chaff-harvest pattern.
- John 4:35 — *Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest.* The same harvest-language in the Samaritan-woman pericope.

The framework reads the harvest at Matt 9:37 as the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed at the framework-level — the multitudes Yahusha (Jesus) just saw as sheep-without-shepherd are the harvest he is now naming. The laborers Yahusha (Jesus) tells his disciples to pray for are about to be commissioned at Matt 10. The framework reads *the harvest is plenteous* as the gathering-substance-is-vast statement: the scattered seed are present in number; the workers to gather them are few. Pray for laborers — and then BE the laborers, as Matt 10's commissioning will immediately enact.

**The *Lord of the harvest*** (v.38). The framework reads the phrase in framework register. The harvest-Lord is Yahuah (the LORD) — the gathering-Lord per the prophets' naming. Yahusha (Jesus) instructs the disciples to pray to the Lord of the harvest; the framework's nature-of-God reading holds — the Father (formless infinite) is the Lord of the harvest at the framework level, with the Formed (Yahusha (Jesus) in flesh) operating as the present-age shepherd executing the harvest under the Father's appointment.

The framework reads Matt 9:35-38 as the deliberate-narrative-hinge that the Matt 10 commission immediately answers. The disciples are about to be sent. The harvest-naming and the labourer-prayer establish the substance of what they are about to do. Pray for laborers. The next sentence (Matt 10:1) sends the twelve.
