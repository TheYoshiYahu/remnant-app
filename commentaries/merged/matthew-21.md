## The King comes to his city — meek, on the foal

Matthew 21 closes the long approach to Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) and opens the final week before the cross, and the lens it turns on is the kingdom that does not come in the natural kingdoms' show of force. The King enters his city not on a war-horse but on the foal of an ass, meek and lowly, exactly as the prophet foretold; he cleanses his Father's house; and across the rest of the chapter the stewardship of the vineyard is named for what it has done with the prophets and quietly transferred to the fruit-bearing remnant the prophets always pointed to. The week the chapter opens will end at the cross, and the King walks into it knowingly.

*And when they drew nigh unto Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), and were come to Beit-Pagi (Bethphage), unto the mount of Olives, then sent Yahusha (Jesus) two disciples, Saying unto them, Go into the village over against you, and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me. And if any man say ought unto you, ye shall say, The Lord hath need of them; and straightway he will send them.* (Matthew 21:1-3)

The entry lands at Beit-Pagi (Bethphage) — the *house of unripe figs,* a small priestly village on the eastern slope of the Mount of Olives. The village-name itself is a substance the chapter turns on: the fig-substance it carries will be the substance the King walks against the unfruitful leadership at verses 18-22 hours later. And the Mount of Olives is the place the prophet Zecharyah (Zechariah) named for the consummation — *his feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives, which is before Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) on the east, and the mount of Olives shall cleave in the midst thereof* (Zechariah 14:4). The Mount of Olives is the substance the second coming will walk out; the King ascends the same mount now at the first coming, toward the city and the cross. The Olivet discourse the King will deliver at Matthew 24 is laid on this same mountain.

The Matthean account preserves both animals — *an ass tied, and a colt with her* — where Mark, Luke, and John mention only the colt. The preservation is the Hebrew parallelism of Zechariah 9:9 read at the parallelism level — the prophet names the same mount in two clauses, *an ass* and *a colt the foal of an ass,* and Matthew preserves both. The ass is not the donkey-as-humble of modern reading; it is the royal Tanakh mount — *speak, ye that ride on white asses, ye that sit in judgment* (Judges 5:10) — the mount David's house used at Shlomoh's (Solomon's) anointing: *cause Shlomoh (Solomon) my son to ride upon mine own mule, and bring him down to Gihon* (1 Kings 1:33). And the colt no man had yet sat upon (Luke 19:30) reads with the dedicated-mount substance the Torah carries for sacred use, like the red heifer never yoked of Numbers 19:2. The *Lord hath need of them* lays the King's prerogative over the village's substance — the King the villagers do not yet see is the One they yield to without challenge, the Father's providence having prepared the mount.

*All this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, Tell ye the daughter of Tsion (Zion), Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass.* (Matthew 21:4-5)

The Matthean signature-quotation formula — *that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet* — lands the entry within the Tanakh-prophesied substance the gospel has used since Matthew 1:22. Here it attaches the entry to Zechariah 9:9:

*Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Tsion (Zion); shout, O daughter of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem): behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.* (Zechariah 9:9)

The Matthean citation preserves the daughter of Tsion (Zion), the King coming, the meekness, and the foal — but omits the *just, and having salvation* clause. The omission is not careless. That clause carries the King's vindication and deliverance the chapter is walking into but not yet walking out. The first coming carries the meekness and the foal; the *just, and having salvation* is the substance the cross-and-resurrection will unfold and the second coming will walk out fully. It is the same pattern as Luke 4:18-19, where the King cites Isaiah 61 in the wilderness synagogue and stops short at *the acceptable year of Yahuah (the LORD),* omitting *the day of vengeance of our Elohim (God)* the verse continued into. The substance is named in full on the prophet's page; the King's first coming preserves what the first coming walks into and holds back what the second coming will walk out.

*Meek, and sitting upon an ass.* The meekness is the Greek *praus,* the Tanakh *ani* — the substance of non-domination the King laid at the head of the kingdom-citizens' substance: *blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth* (Matthew 5:5), and claimed for himself: *for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls* (Matthew 11:29). The King had just named the natural kingdoms' domination at Matthew 20:25 — *the princes of the goyim (Gentiles) exercise dominion over them.* The King of Yashar'el (Israel) coming to his city does not come in that substance; he comes meek, lowly, on the foal of an ass, his kingdom-substance laid against the watcher-rebellion administration the natural kingdoms operate under — *we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities... against spiritual wickedness in high places* (Ephesians 6:12).

*And a very great multitude spread their garments in the way; others cut down branches from the trees, and strawed them in the way. And the multitudes that went before, and that followed, cried, saying, Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD); Hoshia-na (Hosanna) in the highest. And when he was come into Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), all the city was moved, saying, Who is this? And the multitude said, This is Yahusha (Jesus) the prophet of Natsareth (Nazareth) of Galil (Galilee).* (Matthew 21:8-11)

The garments spread in the way is the Tanakh substance of royal acclamation — *then they hasted, and took every man his garment, and put it under him on the top of the stairs, and blew with trumpets, saying, Yehu (Jehu) is king* (2 Kings 9:13). The branches tie into the Sukkot (Tabernacles) pattern of branches waved before Yahuah (the LORD) — *ye shall take you on the first day the boughs of goodly trees, branches of palm trees... and ye shall rejoice before Yahuah Eloheichem (the LORD your God)* (Leviticus 23:40).

*Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David.* The cry is the *hoshi'a-na* — *save now* — of the Hallel-Psalm: *save now, I beseech thee, O Yahuah (LORD)... Blessed be he that cometh in the name of Yahuah (the LORD)* (Psalm 118:25-26). It is not a generic praise-shout but the Tanakh cry of pilgrimage-deliverance the pilgrim cried at the approach to the temple. And it carries the *Son of David* the marginalized had been crying since the two blind men of Matthew 9:27, the Canaanite woman of Matthew 15:22, the Yericho (Jericho) blind men of Matthew 20:30 — the Davidic-Messianic confession the system refused to name, now cried publicly by the multitude. Psalm 118 runs through the whole chapter — the multitudes here, the children at verse 16, and the King himself at verse 42 of the husbandmen-parable all quote from it, the same Hallel the pilgrim-multitude carried into the city at every Pesach (Passover).

*All the city was moved.* The Greek *eseisthē* is the same root that names the earthquake at the crucifixion (Matthew 27:51) and the resurrection (Matthew 28:2). The city is shaken at the King's arriving, and will be shaken again at the cross and the resurrection. *Who is this?* The city's question reveals what it has not recognized. The answer — *the prophet of Natsareth (Nazareth) of Galil (Galilee)* — preserves the Galilean-light substance the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) named, *the people that walked in darkness have seen a great light* (Isaiah 9:2), which the gospel laid at Matthew 4:13-16. But the answer is true and incomplete: the King is the prophet, but he is also the Son of David the multitudes have just been crying to, and the Son of Adam about to walk the cross. The city has not yet recognized what the marginalized recognized and the multitudes now cry — the cause-and-effect of hearing the King will name, *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep* (John 10:26), operating at the level the city as a whole has not walked into.

## The house of prayer made a den of thieves

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went into the temple of Elohim (God), and cast out all them that sold and bought in the temple, and overthrew the tables of the moneychangers, and the seats of them that sold doves, And said unto them, It is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer; but ye have made it a den of thieves.* (Matthew 21:12-13)

The King walks into the temple and performs the substantial prophetic action the Tanakh prophet-pattern walked at prior moments of temple corruption. The action lands in the Court of the goyim (Gentiles) — the outermost court, the one space the goyim (Gentiles) and the ritually-impure could enter for the prayer the prophet named the temple was for. The moneychangers exchanged secular currency for the Tyrian-shekel the priestly authority required for the temple-tax the King himself had honored at Matthew 17:24-27, and the dove-sellers provided the poor person's offering the Torah named for the woman after childbirth (Leviticus 12:8) and the leper's cleansing (Leviticus 14:22). The King does not walk against the existence of the exchange or the dove-selling; he walks against their operating within the Court of the goyim (Gentiles) and displacing the prayer it was meant for. The leadership had monetized the temple and displaced prayer with the market.

*My house shall be called the house of prayer.* The King cites the prophet Yeshayahu (Isaiah) at Isaiah 56:7, and the context is the gathering:

*Also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves to Yahuah (the LORD)... every one that keepeth the Shabbat (Sabbath) from polluting it, and taketh hold of my covenant; Even them will I bring to my holy mountain... for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people. Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) which gathereth the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel) saith, Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him.* (Isaiah 56:6-8)

The Markan parallel preserves the *for all people* (*pasin tois ethnesin*) clause the Matthean abbreviates. The substance the prophet named is the Father gathering — the outcasts of Yashar'el (Israel) and the sons of the stranger as the same gathering. The Isaiah 56 stranger is the scattered seed coming home, made a stranger to its own covenant identity by the scattering — not a categorical inclusion of the unrelated nations into the covenant body. It is the same substance the King names at *other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring* (John 10:16), and the prophet Hoshea (Hosea) named at *Lo-Ammi (not my people)* becoming *the sons of the living Elohim (God)* (Hosea 1:10) — the gathering of the scattered seed of the twelve tribes the prophets laid throughout the corpus.

*But ye have made it a den of thieves.* The King cites the prophet Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) at Jeremiah 7:11 — one of the Tanakh's most substantial temple-judgment passages, the prophet standing in the gate of the First Temple:

*Trust ye not in lying words, saying, The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), The temple of Yahuah (the LORD), are these... Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Ba'al (Baal)... and say, We are delivered to do all these abominations? Is this house, which is called by my name, become a den of robbers in your eyes?* (Jeremiah 7:4, 9-11)

The Jeremiah 7 substance is the leadership trusting in the temple while walking against the covenant the temple was for. The First Temple was destroyed within a generation of Yirmeyahu's (Jeremiah's) prophecy; the King citing the same substance at the Second Temple cleansing names the same substance against the leadership and prefigures the destruction he will name explicitly at the Olivet discourse (Matthew 24:1-2). The *den of thieves* is not merely the monetization; it is the leadership using the temple as cover for its walking against the Father the temple was meant to house.

*And the blind and the lame came to him in the temple; and he healed them.* (Matthew 21:14)

The healing in the temple is itself a prophetic action against the rabbinic reading of 2 Samuel 5:8 as a categorical exclusion of the blind and the lame from the temple. The King reverses it: the blind and the lame the system excluded come to the King in the temple, and he heals them in the very court the system excluded them from. The Messianic credential the prophet named — *then the eyes of the blind shall be opened... then shall the lame man leap as an hart* (Isaiah 35:5-6) — the King had laid in the wilderness credentialing at Matthew 11:4-5, and now walks it out at the very court the system had categorically closed against it.

*And when the chief priests and scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children crying in the temple, and saying, Hoshia-na (Hosanna) to the Son of David; they were sore displeased, And said unto him, Hearest thou what these say? And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Yea; have ye never read, Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise? And he left them, and went out of the city into Beit-Anyah (Bethany); and he lodged there.* (Matthew 21:15-17)

The chief priests' *sore displeased* is the Greek *ēganaktēsan* — the same indignation the King named in the ten apostles at Matthew 20:24. *Hearest thou what these say?* is the system asking the King to silence the children. He will not. *Have ye never read, Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise?* The King cites Psalm 8:2, and the whole Psalm comes with it: *out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger... What is mortal man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of Adam, that thou visitest him?* (Psalm 8:2, 4). The children's mouth-praise is the substance that stills the enemy; the system is the enemy being stilled by the children it cannot silence; and the Psalm names the Son of Adam — the Adamic seed of promise the gospel has carried as the King's self-designation since Matthew 8:20. The substance the system cannot see is what the children walk, the multitudes walk, the marginalized walk. *And he left them, and went out of the city into Beit-Anyah (Bethany).* The King does not lodge in the city the system controls; he lodges at Beit-Anyah (Bethany) — *the house of poverty* — with the poor and marginalized his ministry has walked with from the beginning.

## The fig tree with leaves and no fruit

*Now in the morning as he returned into the city, he hungered. And when he saw a fig tree in the way, he came to it, and found nothing thereon, but leaves only, and said unto it, Let no fruit grow on thee henceforward for ever. And presently the fig tree withered away.* (Matthew 21:18-19)

The King hungers; the fig tree has leaves but no fruit; he speaks *let no fruit grow on thee henceforward for ever,* and it withers. The Markan parallel adds *for the time of figs was not yet* (Mark 11:13). The King is not expecting figs out of season from a literal tree; this is a substantial prophetic action against the leaves-without-fruit substance of the leadership of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), the Tanakh covenant-fruitfulness substance the prophets carried. The prophet Hoshea (Hosea) named it — *I saw your fathers as the firstripe in the fig tree at her first time: but they went to Ba'al-pe'or (Baal-peor)* (Hosea 9:10). Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) — *there shall be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the fig tree, and the leaf shall fade* (Jeremiah 8:13). Yo'el (Joel) — *he hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig tree* (Joel 1:7). Mikhah (Micah) — *there is no cluster to eat: my soul desired the firstripe fruit* (Micah 7:1).

The substance the King walks against is the leadership whose appearance carries no fruit — the same fruit-test the gospel has named from the wilderness immersion, *bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance... every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down* (Matthew 3:8, 10), through the Sermon's *by their fruits ye shall know them* (Matthew 7:16), to the woes against the scribes and Pharisees the King will deliver this very week at Matthew 23. He walks against the system, not the people of Yashar'el (Israel) categorically — the people are the citizens the gospel is being proclaimed to; the leadership is the unfruitful tree.

*Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Verily I say unto you, If ye have faith, and doubt not, ye shall not only do this which is done to the fig tree, but also if ye shall say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea; it shall be done. And all things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive.* (Matthew 21:21-22)

The disciples marvel, and the King extends the mustard-seed faith he laid at Matthew 17:20 — faith as a qualitative disposition, not a quantitative measurement. The mountain-moving is the Tanakh substance of the psalms and prophets: *though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea* (Psalm 46:2); *every mountain and hill shall be made low* (Isaiah 40:4); *the mountains shall depart, and the hills be removed; but my kindness shall not depart from thee* (Isaiah 54:10). *And all things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive.* This is not the name-it-and-claim-it of the prosperity-pulpit. The King adds the qualifier in the upper room — *whatsoever ye shall ask in my name, that will I do* (John 14:13) — and the apostle Yochanan (John) locks it: *if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us* (1 John 5:14). The substance is the Father's will operating through the disciple's asking, the kingdom-substance flowing through the disciple in conformity to the Father — the same kingdom-substance the King walked against the unfruitful fig tree.

## By what authority

*And when he was come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came unto him as he was teaching, and said, By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?* (Matthew 21:23)

The third day of the week opens with the King teaching in the temple and the chief priests and elders coming with the formal authority-challenge — the rabbinic credentialing: *who was your rabbi, who conferred your authority?* The King's authority was conferred not by the system but directly by the Father at the Yarden (Jordan) immersion — *this is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased* (Matthew 3:17) — and at the transfiguration mount — *this is my beloved Son... hear ye him* (Matthew 17:5). The system asks for the credentialing it recognizes because the Father's authorization is not the substance it recognizes; it asks for a substance it has already walked against.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, I also will ask you one thing... The baptism of Yochanan (John), whence was it? from heaven, or of men? And they reasoned with themselves, saying, If we shall say, From heaven; he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him? But if we shall say, Of men; we fear the people; for all hold Yochanan (John) as a prophet. And they answered Yahusha (Jesus), and said, We cannot tell. And he said unto them, Neither tell I you by what authority I do these things.* (Matthew 21:24-27)

The King does not evade; he redirects through the substance the system has already walked against. Yochanan (John) is the Eliyahu (Elijah)-pattern prophet the King named at Matthew 11:13-14 and Matthew 17:11-13, the prophet of Malachi 4:5-6 — *behold, I will send you Eliyahu (Elijah) the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Yahuah (the LORD).* The system reasons with itself and reveals political calculus rather than truth: naming Yochanan (John) as from heaven names its own rejection; naming him of men fears the multitude that holds him as a prophet. *We cannot tell* is substantially false — the system knows and refuses to name what it knows. The King's withholding is not evasion but consequence: the authority the system asks for is the substance it has already disqualified itself from receiving by rejecting the Father's credentialed prophet.

## The two sons — doing, not saying

*A certain man had two sons; and he came to the first, and said, Son, go work to day in my vineyard. He answered and said, I will not: but afterward he repented, and went. And he came to the second, and said likewise. And he answered and said, I go, sir: and went not. Whether of them twain did the will of his father? They say unto him, The first. Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Verily I say unto you, That the publicans and the harlots go into the kingdom of Elohim (God) before you. For Yochanan (John) came unto you in the way of righteousness, and ye believed him not: but the publicans and the harlots believed him: and ye, when ye had seen it, did not repent afterward, that ye might believe him.* (Matthew 21:28-32)

The first son says *I will not* and afterward repents and goes and does. The second says *I go, sir* — the *Lord, Lord* substance — and walks not. The doing of the Father's will is the substance walked, not the substance spoken. This is the parable of Matthew 7:21-23 — *not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven... And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.* The Greek for *iniquity* is *anomia* — Torah-lessness, walking against the substance the Father laid at Sinai and the Spirit empowers the citizen to walk in at the new heart of Ezekiel 36:26-27 and the new covenant of Jeremiah 31:31-34.

*The publicans and the harlots go into the kingdom of Elohim (God) before you.* Those the Pharisaic system categorically excluded walk the first son's substance — repenting and doing — and enter before the chief priests and elders who walk the second son's substance. The publican Mattityahu (Matthew) had himself walked outside the categorical-Pharisaic substance before the King's call (Matthew 9:9). This is the cause-and-effect of hearing the King names at John 10:26-27: the publicans and harlots who heard Yochanan (John) and repented heard because they were of the Father's substance; the chief priests and elders who refused to name him refused because their walking was not the substance the Father had known from the foundation. The apostolic witness carries it — *be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only* (James 1:22); *faith, if it hath not works, is dead* (James 2:17); *not the hearers of the law are just before Elohim (God), but the doers of the law shall be justified* (Romans 2:13); *hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments* (1 John 2:3-4). *Yochanan (John) came unto you in the way of righteousness* — the Tanakh *tsedaqah* substance, *in the way of righteousness is life* (Proverbs 12:28) — and the system rejected the prophet walking the very substance the system was meant to walk.

## The wicked husbandmen — the vineyard's stewardship transferred

*Hear another parable: There was a certain householder, which planted a vineyard, and hedged it round about, and digged a winepress in it, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a far country: And when the time of the fruit drew near, he sent his servants to the husbandmen, that they might receive the fruits of it. And the husbandmen took his servants, and beat one, and killed another, and stoned another. Again, he sent other servants more than the first: and they did unto them likewise. But last of all he sent unto them his son, saying, They will reverence my son. But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance. And they caught him, and cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him.* (Matthew 21:33-39)

The chapter lands its framework-major moment. The vineyard is the prophet Yeshayahu's (Isaiah's) vineyard explicit:

*My wellbeloved hath a vineyard in a very fruitful hill: and he fenced it, and gathered out the stones thereof, and planted it with the choicest vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and also made a winepress therein... For the vineyard of Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts) is the house of Yashar'el (Israel), and the men of Yahudah (Judah) his pleasant plant.* (Isaiah 5:2, 7)

The King's parable carries every element the prophet's song laid — planting, hedging, winepress, tower, looking for fruit — and adds the husbandmen and the servants. The vineyard is the covenant body of Yashar'el (Israel) the prophet named. The husbandmen are the leadership — the chief priests and elders the King has just walked against — entrusted with the stewardship but walking against what the Father gave them charge over. This is not the people of Yashar'el (Israel) as a whole; it is the leadership entrusted with the vineyard, the same distinction the tares parable, the blind-leaders-of-the-blind, and the seven woes hold. The King walks against the system, not the people. *And went into a far country* — the Father's long-suffering, the room he gives the stewards to walk the substance entrusted to them: *Yahuah (the LORD) is not slack concerning his promise... but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish* (2 Peter 3:9).

The servants are the prophets — *I have even sent unto you all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them: Yet they hearkened not unto me* (Jeremiah 7:25). The killing and stoning is the Tanakh pattern of prophet-killing: Zecharyah (Zechariah) son of Yehoyada (Jehoiada) stoned in the court of the temple (2 Chronicles 24:20-22, the same Zecharyah the King will name at the seven woes of Matthew 23:35); Uriyahu (Urijah) killed by Yehoyaqim (Jehoiakim) (Jeremiah 26:20-23); Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) cast into the pit (Jeremiah 38:6); the substance Nechemyah (Nehemiah) confessed — *they... slew thy prophets which testified against them to turn them to thee* (Nehemiah 9:26).

*Last of all he sent unto them his son.* This is the substance the apostle names — *Elohim (God)... hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things* (Hebrews 1:1-2). The husbandmen recognize the heir — *this is the heir* — and still walk the killing to seize the inheritance. The system recognizes the substance and still walks against it, the substance the gospel has named since Matthew 3:7's *generation of vipers.* *And they caught him, and cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him* — pre-figuring *Yahusha (Jesus) also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate* (Hebrews 13:12). The King will be cast out of the city and slain without the gate. The parable walks at the parabolic level what will walk out at the historical level within the week.

*When the lord therefore of the vineyard cometh, what will he do unto those husbandmen? They say unto him, He will miserably destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, which shall render him the fruits in their seasons.* (Matthew 21:40-41)

The King's question lets the system walk out the judgment against itself, as the two-sons parable had. The chief priests and elders answer correctly at the parable-level — and name the substance they themselves walk against.

*Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Did ye never read in the scriptures, The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is Yahuah's (the LORD's) doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes? Therefore say I unto you, The kingdom of Elohim (God) shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof. And whosoever shall fall on this stone shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.* (Matthew 21:42-44)

The King cites Psalm 118:22-23 — the same Hallel the multitudes cried at the entry and the children cried in the temple. The stone runs through the Tanakh — Ya'aqov's (Jacob's) pillar at Beit-El (Bethel) (Genesis 28:18-22), the Even-ezer (Eben-ezer) (1 Samuel 7:12), the *stone of stumbling and rock of offence to both the houses of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Isaiah 8:14), the *tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation* (Isaiah 28:16), the stone cut without hands that fills the whole earth (Daniel 2:34-35, 44-45), the stone with seven eyes and the headstone brought forth with shoutings (Zechariah 3:9; 4:7). The builders are the leadership entrusted with building the Yashar'el (Israel) substance; they have rejected the cornerstone the Father named and set. The apostle Kefa (Peter) restates it — *this is the stone which was set at nought of you builders, which is become the head of the corner* (Acts 4:11), and *a stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence, even to them which stumble at the word, being disobedient* (1 Peter 2:8); the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) at Romans 9:33. *This is Yahuah's (the LORD's) doing.* The Father is not thwarted by the builders' rejection; he walks the cornerstone through the rejection itself.

*Therefore say I unto you, The kingdom of Elohim (God) shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof.* The chapter-pivot, and three distinctions are load-bearing.

First, the *taken from you* addresses the leadership. The *you* is the chief priests and elders the King stands in front of, the stewards entrusted with the vineyard. It is not categorical Yashar'el (Israel) — not the people of the covenant the prophets promised to gather. It is the leadership-stewardship of the vineyard the husbandmen walked against.

Second, the *nation* is the Greek *ethnos* — singular. The Matthean Greek reads *ethnei,* one nation, not the plural *ethnesi* a categorical-nations substance would have carried. The King names a particular nation bringing forth fruit — the fruit-bearing remnant of the gathering of the scattered seed of both houses, the apostolic foundation of the twelve-tribes restoration the prophets and apostles lay across the canon.

Third, that architecture lies across the whole canon. The King himself laid it at Matthew 19:28 — *ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel).* The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) before Agrippa — *the promise made of Elohim (God) unto our fathers: Unto which promise our twelve tribes, instantly serving Elohim (God) day and night, hope to come* (Acts 26:6-7). The apostle Ya'aqov (James) addresses his epistle *to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad* (James 1:1). The apostle Yochanan (John) sees the twelve tribes sealed (Revelation 7:4-8) and the twelve tribes named on the twelve gates of the new Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), with the twelve apostles on its foundations (Revelation 21:12-14). The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names the foundation — *built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) himself being the chief corner stone* (Ephesians 2:20). And the apostle Kefa (Peter) lays the most direct restatement of the King's *nation* — written to the scattered strangers (1 Peter 1:1):

*But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people... Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of Elohim (God): which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.* (1 Peter 2:9-10)

The *holy nation* — Greek *ethnos hagion,* singular, the same grammatical form as the King's *nation* at Matthew 21:43 — restates the prophet Hoshea (Hosea) at *Lo-Ammi* becoming *Ammi*: *in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God)* (Hosea 1:10). The nation bringing forth fruit is the gathered scattered seed of the twelve tribes, walking the new heart of Ezekiel 36:26-27 and the new covenant of Jeremiah 31:31-34.

Three inherited readings of verse 43 fall to the substance the canon itself walks. The Hebrew-Roots false-inclusion that any Torah-keeping community replaces the current leadership collapses the seed-of-promise the prophets named tribe by tribe; the *nation* is not a self-selected Torah-keeping community but the substantial seed of the twelve-tribes restoration. And the Reformation sola-fide-without-fruit that treats the bringing-forth-fruit as incidental collapses the load-bearing clause itself — the nation is named *by* the bringing forth of fruit, and the fruit is the Spirit-empowered heart-Torah walking the prophets and apostles name throughout, the substance the cross opens the door home to rather than cancels.

*And whosoever shall fall on this stone shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.* The stumbling that breaks is Isaiah 8:14-15 — *a stone of stumbling... and many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken.* The stone falling and grinding to powder is the stone cut without hands of Daniel 2:34-35. Both are the Day of Yahuah (the LORD) the prophets named at the close of the Tanakh — *the day of Yahuah (the LORD) is great and very terrible; and who can abide it?* (Joel 2:11); *who may abide the day of his coming?... for he is like a refiner's fire* (Malachi 3:2); *the day that cometh shall burn them up... that it shall leave them neither root nor branch* (Malachi 4:1).

*And when the chief priests and Pharisees had heard his parables, they perceived that he spake of them. But when they sought to lay hands on him, they feared the multitude, because they took him for a prophet.* (Matthew 21:45-46)
