## The questioning-day, and the One David called Lord

Matthew 22 is the system's questioning-day at the temple — the Tuesday of the entry week the prior chapter opened. The leadership comes at the King with three trap-questions in turn, and each one collapses against the Tanakh the questioners claimed to walk. Then the King turns the questioning back on them with a single question of his own, and the chapter's strongest lens comes into view: who is the Messiah, and how can David call his own descendant *Lord*? The answer is the nature-of-God reading the whole gospel has carried since the *Immanu-El (God with us)* of the opening chapter — one Yahuah (God), Formless in source, Formed in expression — and it is the question the inherited Pharisaic reading, like the inherited Christian formula that came after it, cannot resolve. The chapter ends the public engagement: from that day no man durst ask him any more questions, and the system walks past questioning into the plot to kill him.

## The marriage feast — and the wedding garment

*And Yahusha (Jesus) answered and spake unto them again by parables, and said, The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain king, which made a marriage for his son, And sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden to the wedding: and they would not come. Again, he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage. But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise: And the remnant took his servants, and entreated them spitefully, and slew them. But when the king heard thereof, he was wroth: and he sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned up their city.* (Matthew 22:1-7)

The marriage-feast is the third parable in a sequence the King has walked against the chief priests and elders since the authority-challenge of Matthew 21:23 — the two sons, the wicked husbandmen, and now the marriage feast — one continuing substance against the leadership the prior chapter named. The wedding-banquet is the Messianic feast the prophets laid:

*And in this mountain shall Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts) make unto all people a feast of fat things... He will swallow up death in victory; and Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD) will wipe away tears from off all faces.* (Isaiah 25:6, 8)

The same prophet laid the invitation — *ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters... come, buy wine and milk without money and without price* (Isaiah 55:1) — and the wedding garment: *he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness* (Isaiah 61:10). The apostle Yochanan (John) will name the consummation in the same substance — *the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready. And to her was granted that she should be arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints* (Revelation 19:7-8).

The *bidden* who refuse are the leadership-stewards the prior parable named as the husbandmen — the leadership of the house of Yahudah (Judah) and the house of Yashar'el (Israel) the Father tended the vineyard for, now bidden to the feast. Not the people as a whole; the leadership entrusted with the substance. Their refusal walks three ways: indifference (*they made light of it* — the Greek *amelēsantes,* the same neglect the letter to the Hebrews warns the citizen against at *how shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation,* Hebrews 2:3); the natural economy walked against the kingdom (*one to his farm, another to his merchandise* — *ye cannot serve Elohim (God) and mammon,* Matthew 6:24); and the prophet-killing the prior parable named through Zecharyah (Zechariah) son of Yehoyada (Jehoiada), Uriyahu (Urijah), and Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) — *they slew thy prophets which testified against them to turn them to thee* (Nehemiah 9:26).

*He sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned up their city.* The judgment pre-figures the destruction of the Second Temple the King names explicitly at the Olivet discourse — *there shall not be left here one stone upon another* (Matthew 24:2) — and the lament *your house is left unto you desolate* (Matthew 23:38). The historical substance is the Roman armies of Titus at the 70 CE destruction of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem), the same Tanakh pattern of the goyim (Gentiles)-administration as instrument of judgment — *O Ashshur (Assyria), the rod of mine anger* (Isaiah 10:5). This is real judgment against the leadership, not the consummation of the Day of Yahuah (the LORD) the prophets named for the end; the King will distinguish carefully at the Olivet discourse between *the end is not yet* (Matthew 24:6) and *immediately after the tribulation of those days* (Matthew 24:29).

*Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they which were bidden were not worthy. Go ye therefore into the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage. So those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was furnished with guests.* (Matthew 22:8-10)

The highways — the Greek *diexodous tōn hodōn,* the partings of the highways — is the broader gathering. This is not the categorically-unrelated nations gathered into the covenant. It is the scattered seed of the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel), scattered among the nations, being gathered home — the substance the King restricted his mission to (*go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel),* Matthew 10:6) now extended at the parabolic level to the broader gathering the prophets named. The prophet Hoshea (Hosea) named it — *in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God)* (Hosea 1:10, which the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) cites at Romans 9:25-26). Yeshayahu (Isaiah) named it — *Adonai (the Lord) shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people... and gather together the dispersed of Yahudah (Judah) from the four corners of the earth* (Isaiah 11:11-12). Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) — *I will take the children of Yashar'el (Israel) from among the goyim (heathen), whither they be gone* (Ezekiel 37:21). And Zecharyah (Zechariah) — *I will hiss for them, and gather them; for I have redeemed them* (Zechariah 10:8).

The *both bad and good* gathered is the pre-sifting substance — the wheat and tares of Matthew 13:24-30, the dragnet of Matthew 13:47-50: broad proclamation first, post-harvest sifting after. The gathering is broad; the sifting comes in the very next verses.

*And when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment: And he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment? And he was speechless. Then said the king to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.* (Matthew 22:11-13)

The wedding garment the king categorically requires is the *robe of righteousness* the prophet named (Isaiah 61:10), contrasted with the citizen's own righteousness — *all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags* (Isaiah 64:6). It is what the post-exilic prophet Zecharyah (Zechariah) walked at Yehoshua (Joshua) the high priest:

*Now Yehoshua (Joshua) was clothed with filthy garments... Take away the filthy garments from him... Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment.* (Zechariah 3:3-4)

The garment is the righteousness given and walked — the Father provides it, the citizen puts it on and walks in it. The apostolic substance is the same — *the fine linen is the righteousness of saints* (Revelation 19:8); *he that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life* (Revelation 3:5); *blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life* (Revelation 22:14). It is the heart-Torah walking the prophets named at Jeremiah 31:33 and Ezekiel 36:26-27 — the new heart, the new spirit, the Spirit causing the citizen to walk in the Father's statutes.

Hold what the garment rules out. It is not the imputed-righteousness-without-substance-walked the Reformation collapsed the gospel into. The Reformation truncated the gospel at *welcomed in by faith,* *saved by grace through faith,* *not under law but under grace* — and the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) himself categorically rejected that standalone reading the very next breath: *what shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? Elohim (God) forbid* (Romans 6:1-2). The post-Reformation grammar walks the gospel as if the door is the destination; the apostolic substance walks it as the door into the walking of the Way, with the tree of life right at the end of *keeping his commandments* (Revelation 22:14). The wedding garment is the substance walked in after the door is passed through — put on by the new heart, walked out in the Father's statutes.

The man without the garment is who the King's own mouth named at the close of the Sermon — *not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven... And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity* (Matthew 7:21-23). The Greek for *iniquity* is *anomia* — Torah-lessness. *I never knew you* is spoken to one who entered the feast without walking the substance the feast was meant to be walked in. This is the post-harvest sifting: the wheat can be blotted out by walking without fruit, and the warning to be blotted out is spoken to wheat (Revelation 3:5); the tares cannot be added, because they were never wheat.

*Friend* — the Greek *hetaire,* the polite address that precedes correction, the same the King used at the laborers' parable (Matthew 20:13) and at Yahudah's (Judas's) betrayal (Matthew 26:50). The man is *speechless* (Greek *ephimōthē*), pre-figuring the chapter's close at verse 46 — the whole system silenced at the King's substance, the non-answer the chief priests walked at the authority-challenge (*we cannot tell*), the Pharisees and Herodians at verse 22, the Sadducees at verse 33, and the Pharisees at verse 46. *Outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth* is the Day of Yahuah (the LORD) substance — *behold, the day of Yahuah (the LORD) cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger* (Isaiah 13:9); *a day of darkness and gloominess, a day of clouds and thick darkness* (Zephaniah 1:15).

*For many are called, but few are chosen.* (Matthew 22:14)

The closing line restates the laborers' parable at Matthew 20:16. The *called* (Greek *klētoi*) is the broad proclamation the highways gathering walked — the kingdom broadcast broadly to the scattered. The *chosen* (Greek *eklektoi*) is the substance walked in the wedding garment — revealed by the substance walked. The relationship is the cause-and-effect of hearing the King names at John 10:26-27 — *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep... my sheep hear my voice.* It is not *if you respond, you become covenant*; it is *if you are covenant, you respond when called.* The hearing reveals what the names written in the Book of Life from the foundation of the world already are; the tares cannot hear because they were never wheat. And yet the wheat can fail to bear fruit and be blotted out (Revelation 3:5); names are not added, only blotted out. This is the substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names at *chosen us in him before the foundation of the world* (Ephesians 1:4) — hearing reveals, it does not create.

## Render unto Caesar

*Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might entangle him in his talk. And they sent out unto him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Master, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of Elohim (God) in truth, neither carest thou for any man: for thou regardest not the person of men. Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?* (Matthew 22:15-17)

The Pharisees and Herodians are an unlikely alliance — the Pharisees the Torah-strict tradition walking against the Roman administration the Herodians derived their authority from, the Herodians the party of Herod's house in collaboration with that administration. They had no natural alliance, and yet against the King they unite, the same way the Pharisees and Sadducees had united at Matthew 16:1. The flattery — *we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of Elohim (God) in truth* — is true substance the speakers themselves walk against, the *say, and do not* the King will name at Matthew 23:3.

*Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?* The *tribute* (Greek *kēnson*) is the Roman poll-tax imposed since Yehudah (Judea) became a direct Roman province in 6 CE — the tax that provoked the Zealot revolt of Yehudah (Judas) the Galilean, who, as the historian Yosef ben Mattityahu (Josephus) records, called his countrymen cowards for paying a tax to Rome and submitting to mortal men as lords after Elohim (God). The trap is binary: answer *yes,* and the Zealot substance walks against the King as a collaborator; answer *no,* and the Roman administration walks against him as seditious. No answer is safe.

*But Yahusha (Jesus) perceived their wickedness, and said, Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites? Shew me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a penny. And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription? They say unto him, Caesar's. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto Elohim (God) the things that are Elohim's (God's).* (Matthew 22:18-21)

The penny is the *dēnarion* — the silver coin the poll-tax was paid in. The denarius bears the bust of Tiberius Caesar with the inscription *TI CAESAR DIVI AVG F AVGVSTVS* — *Tiberius Caesar, son of the divine Augustus, Augustus* — and Tiberius's mother Livia on the reverse as *Pontifex Maximus,* supreme high-priest. The coin carries the substance of Caesar's divinity-claim the Torah's second commandment categorically walks against — *thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image... thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them* (Exodus 20:4-5). The coin in the speakers' purse in the temple court itself is its own admission.

*Whose is this image?* The Greek *eikōn* is the same word the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) uses of the Son — *the image of the invisible Elohim (God)* (Colossians 1:15) — and of the citizen — *conformed to the image of his Son* (Romans 8:29) — the same Septuagint Greek of the creation: *let us make man in our image* (Genesis 1:26). The coin bears Caesar's image; the citizen bears the Father's. *Render* (Greek *apodote*) is to give back what was already the substance of what it came from. The coin belongs to Caesar, stamped by Caesar; the citizen belongs to Elohim (God), stamped by Elohim (God).

This is not the modern collapse into a separation-of-church-and-state with two co-equal spheres. The natural administration is what the Father permits for an age — the substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names as operating under *principalities... the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places* (Ephesians 6:12), not a co-equal substance to the Father's. The Constantinian conflation built the Christendom the Reformation inherited; the substance the King walks at the coin is what the Constantinian collapse walked away from. The apostles restate it — *let every soul be subject unto the higher powers... render therefore to all their dues: tribute to whom tribute is due* (Romans 13:1, 7); *submit yourselves to every ordinance of man for the Lord's sake... fear Elohim (God). Honour the king* (1 Peter 2:13, 17). And yet *unto Elohim (God) the things that are Elohim's (God's)* lays the categorical precedence the apostles walked at *we ought to obey Elohim (God) rather than men* (Acts 5:29). When the natural administration walks against the Father, the citizen walks the Father's substance — Shadrakh (Shadrach), Meishakh (Meshach), and Aved-Nego (Abed-nego) refusing the image of Nevukhadnetstar (Nebuchadnezzar) (*we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image,* Daniel 3:18); Daniel praying against the decree of Daryavesh (Darius) (Daniel 6:10); the prophet Eliyahu (Elijah) against the Ba'al (Baal) of Ach'av (Ahab) and Yezevel (Jezebel) (1 Kings 18); the apostle Yochanan's (John's) vision of the beast and the mark the citizen cannot receive even into suffering — *here is the patience and the faith of the saints* (Revelation 13:10). The posture: sit out the armed revolt the Zealots walked; sit out the collaboration the Herodians walked; speak up the image of Elohim (God) the citizen carries. *They marvelled, and left him, and went their way.* The trap collapsed on itself.

## The Sadducees and the resurrection

*The same day came to him the Sadducees, which say that there is no resurrection, and asked him, Saying, Master, Mosheh (Moses) said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.* (Matthew 22:23-24)

The Sadducees are the priestly aristocracy of the temple administration — the *Tsadoqim,* descended from Tsadoq (Zadok) the priest (2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Kings 1:32-39), the line the prophet Yechezk'el (Ezekiel) named at the restored-temple vision (Ezekiel 44:15). They walk the Torah-only substance, the prophets and the writings walked against, the resurrection and the angels and the spirit denied — *for the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection, neither angel, nor spirit* (Acts 23:8). But the substance is a self-disqualification at the very Torah they claim to walk: the angels are at Genesis 18 and 19 and 22 and 28 and 32, Exodus 3 and 14 and 23 throughout; the Spirit is at Genesis 1:2, Exodus 31:3, Numbers 11:25 throughout. They walk against the Torah they ostensibly walk.

The levirate-marriage substance they build the trap on is the Torah statute at Deuteronomy 25:5-10 — the brother of a man dying childless raises up seed in the dead brother's name, that his name be not put out of Yashar'el (Israel), the substance Genesis 38 and Ruth 4 walk out, the same substance the Davidic line was built on. They construct a hypothetical of seven brothers and one wife, not as a substantial question but as a mockery meant to name the resurrection absurd.

*Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of Elohim (God). For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of Elohim (God) in heaven.* (Matthew 22:29-30)

The rebuke is double — not knowing the scriptures the Sadducees claim to walk, and not knowing the power of the Father to do what they cannot imagine. *As the angels of Elohim (God) in heaven* must be read carefully. The angels of Elohim (God) in heaven are the non-fallen angels still in heaven, the *sons of Elohim (God)* who *shouted for joy* (Job 38:7). This is not a categorical non-marrying of all angels. The Torah itself at Genesis 6:1-4 named the watchers who walked out of it — *the sons of Elohim (God) saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives.* The prophet Chanok (Enoch) names them at 1 Enoch 6-7 — Shemyaza and the chiefs of tens taking wives and defiling themselves — and the apostle Yahudah (Jude) at Jude 6: *the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation.* The King names the non-fallen, still-in-heaven angels walking the non-marrying substance, and the resurrected walk the same — beyond the natural marriage substance the *from the beginning it was not so* of Matthew 19:8 had laid against the hardness-of-heart allowance.

*But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by Elohim (God), saying, I am the Elohim (God) of Avraham (Abraham), and the Elohim (God) of Yitschaq (Isaac), and the Elohim (God) of Ya'aqov (Jacob)? Elohim (God) is not the Elohim (God) of the dead, but of the living.* (Matthew 22:31-32)

The King cites the very Torah the Sadducees claim to walk — Exodus 3:6, the Father's self-disclosure at the burning bush. The Father says *I am,* not *I was,* the Elohim (God) of the patriarchs. The relationship continues in the present tense; the patriarchs must be the living. If they were permanently extinct, the Father would be the Elohim of the dead. He is the Elohim of the living — the patriarchs are asleep, awaiting the first resurrection. The Tanakh carries the resurrection through and through — *many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake* (Daniel 12:2); *thy dead men shall live* (Isaiah 26:19); *I will ransom them from the power of sheol* (Hosea 13:14); *I know that my redeemer liveth... in my flesh shall I see Elohim (God)* (Job 19:25-26); the valley of dry bones (Ezekiel 37:1-14); *thou wilt not leave my soul in sheol* (Psalm 16:10). The apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names it — *them also which sleep in Yahusha (Jesus) will Elohim (God) bring with him* (1 Thessalonians 4:14) — and the apostle Yochanan (John): *this is the first resurrection* (Revelation 20:5).

Hold what this rules out. It is not the inherited-Christian immediate-disembodied-soul going to heaven or hell at death. The patriarchs are not presently walking in a conscious disembodied state; they are asleep, and the Father relates to them as the living he will raise. The grief of the citizen who loses a loved one is not *grandmother is in heaven looking down*; it is *she sleeps in Yahusha (Jesus) until the King's coming to raise her,* the substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names at 1 Thessalonians 4. *And when the multitude heard this, they were astonished at his doctrine.* The Sadducean substance walked away silenced at the Torah it ostensibly walked.

## The great commandment

*Then one of them, which was a lawyer, asked him a question, tempting him, and saying, Master, which is the great commandment in the law? Yahusha (Jesus) said unto him, Thou shalt love Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind. This is the first and great commandment. And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets.* (Matthew 22:35-40)

The Pharisees regroup after the Sadducean rival is silenced and send the lawyer (Greek *nomikos,* the Torah-specialist) forward as the system's most competent question-maker, *tempting* the King (Greek *peirazōn,* the same word the satanic substance walked at the wilderness temptation). The rabbinic tradition counted the six hundred and thirteen commandments — three hundred sixty-five negative, two hundred forty-eight positive — and debated the ranking; naming one against the others is the trap.

The King's answer cites the Shema at Deuteronomy 6:4-5 — *Shema (Hear), O Yashar'el (Israel): Yahuah (the LORD) our Elohim (God) is one Yahuah (LORD): And thou shalt love Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might.* The Shema is the uniqueness of the Father the prophets carried throughout — *I am Yahuah (the LORD), and there is none else* (Isaiah 45:5); *I am the first, and I am the last; and beside me there is no Elohim (God)* (Isaiah 44:6). It is not the post-Nicene three-co-equal-persons-of-one-substance, and not the modalist collapse of Father and Son into one undifferentiated being. The Father is the Formless Source — the substance the seraphim covered their faces before at Isaiah 6, of whom *no man hath seen Elohim (God) at any time* (John 1:18). The Son is the Formed Expression from the Source — the One Mosheh (Moses) met in the bush (Exodus 3:2), who spoke face to face as a man with his friend (Exodus 33:11), whom Daniel saw *like the Son of Adam* (Daniel 7:13), the One the Father's voice named at the transfiguration (Matthew 17:5). The Father and the Son are substantially distinct in Source and Expression, substantively one in that the Son is the Word the Father speaks — *the Word was Elohim (God)... all things were made by him* (John 1:1, 3). The Shema names the uniqueness of that one substance, not three co-equal persons.

*Heart + soul + mind* (the Markan and Lukan parallels add *strength*) is the whole substance of the citizen given to the Father, not fragmented or allocated. *Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself* cites Leviticus 19:18 — the neighbor is the covenant fellow-citizen and the stranger who joins the covenant: *the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you... for ye were strangers in the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt)* (Leviticus 19:34), the same sons-of-the-stranger substance of Isaiah 56:6 the prior chapter walked at the temple cleansing. The Lukan Good Samaritan parable (Luke 10:25-37) extends it toward where mercy meets need — not the modern-liberal categorical-universal-neighbor collapse, but mercy walked where need is encountered, and the Shomroni (Samaritan) who walks it is himself the remnant the Pharisaic system categorically excluded.

*On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets.* The Greek *krematai* is suspension from a load-bearing point. This is not the post-Reformation collapse where the two commandments replace the Torah and the citizen need walk only the two. The Torah and the prophets are suspended from the love of the Father and the neighbor — they are the detailed walking-out of that love in the substantive life of the citizen, not replaced by it. This is the heart-Torah the prophets named — *I will put my Torah in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts* (Jeremiah 31:33); *a new heart... and cause you to walk in my statutes* (Ezekiel 36:26-27); *to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)* (Micah 6:8); *I desired mercy, and not sacrifice* (Hosea 6:6). The Mosheh (Moses) substance binds the two together — *Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) will circumcise thine heart... to love Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God) with all thine heart* (Deuteronomy 30:6); *love Yahuah (the LORD) thy Elohim (God), and keep his charge, and his statutes* (Deuteronomy 11:1). And the apostles restate it — *love is the fulfilling of the law* (Romans 13:10); *all the law is fulfilled in one word* (Galatians 5:14); *the royal law* (James 2:8); *this is the love of Elohim (God), that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous* (1 John 5:3). Love of the Father is the keeping of the commandments; the two commandments and the Torah and the prophets are one substance.

## Whose son is the Messiah — the One David called Lord

*While the Pharisees were gathered together, Yahusha (Jesus) asked them, Saying, What think ye of Messiah (Christ)? whose son is he? They say unto him, The son of David. He saith unto them, How then doth David in spirit call him Lord, saying, Yahuah (the LORD) said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool? If David then call him Lord, how is he his son?* (Matthew 22:41-45)

The King now turns the questioning back. The system has walked three trap-questions and the King has walked out of each; now he asks the question the chapter has been building toward. *What think ye of Messiah (Christ)? whose son is he?* The Pharisaic answer — *the son of David* — is correct as far as it goes. The Davidic covenant the prophet Natan (Nathan) named — *I will set up thy seed after thee... I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever* (2 Samuel 7:12-13) — runs through the prophets: *unto us a child is born... upon the throne of David* (Isaiah 9:6-7); *a rod out of the stem of Yishai (Jesse)* (Isaiah 11:1); *a righteous Branch... and this is his name whereby he shall be called, Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS)* (Jeremiah 23:5-6); *David my servant shall be king over them* (Ezekiel 37:24). The Messiah is the son of David.

But the King cites Psalm 110:1:

*Yahuah (the LORD) said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.* (Psalm 110:1)

*David in spirit* — David the king-prophet, *being a prophet* (Acts 2:30), speaking in the Spirit. And the Tanakh Hebrew carries the substance with precision. The first *Lord* is *Yahuah,* the tetragrammaton, the Father — the Formless Source of whom *there shall no man see me, and live* (Exodus 33:20), whom *no man hath seen... at any time* (John 1:18). The second *Lord* is *Adoni* — *my Lord,* the second-person-singular possessive, the One David calls his own Lord. David, the king the Messiah is to descend from, calls the Messianic figure *my Lord.* The natural substance has the descendant subordinate to the ancestor; the substance David walks at Psalm 110 has the Messianic descendant as greater than David. The Messiah is the son of David and greater than David — the Davidic lineage the Pharisees acknowledge, and the substantively-divine substance they cannot resolve.

This is the nature-of-God reading at the highest level the gospel reaches. The Formless Source (Father, *Yahuah*) speaks to the Formed Expression (Son, *Adoni*); the two are substantially distinct (the *Yahuah said unto my Adoni* preserves the distinction at the most-substantial level) and substantively one (the One David calls *my Lord* is of the divine substance, *the Word was Elohim (God)... all things were made by him,* John 1:1, 3). The point is not to argue the Pharisees down, nor the Christian formula that came after them; it is to show what each inherited reading, held in good faith, quietly misses — the simpler, older thing the Psalm shows. And read against the canon, the Psalm rules out three inherited readings of the nature of God in good order.

The first is the Trinitarian three-co-equal-persons-of-one-substance the post-Nicene formula reached for — fully co-equal, without subordination in the eternal substance. Psalm 110 has the Father telling the Son to sit on his right hand and making the enemies the Son's footstool — substantive subordination in the Source-Expression substance. Read it plainly and the co-equal reading has nowhere to put it; the King himself walks the same submission at *my Father is greater than I* (John 14:28), *the Son also himself be subject unto him that put all things under him* (1 Corinthians 15:28), and *of that day and hour knoweth no man... but my Father only* (Matthew 24:36). The divinity the formula wanted to guard is already here in the Psalm, and older than the formula.

The second is the modalist collapse — the Father and Son as one undifferentiated being. The Psalm preserves the distinction at the most-substantial level: the Father speaks *to* the Son. It is not the Father speaking to himself; it is two substantively distinct substances, the Source and the Expression.

The third is the Arian substance of the Son as a created being made at a point in time. The One David calls *my Lord* is of the divine substance *Yahuah* speaks to — the Word the Father expresses eternally, not made from nothing: *in the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with Elohim (God), and the Word was Elohim (God)* (John 1:1). It is the substance the King walks at *I and my Father are one* (John 10:30) — substantially one in the Source-Expression, substantively distinct in the Source and the Expression.

*Sit thou on my right hand* is the substance the whole apostolic witness walks out. Psalm 110 is the most-quoted Tanakh Psalm in the apostolic substance — the King himself before the Sanhedrin (*ye shall see the Son of Adam sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven,* Matthew 26:64); the apostle Kefa (Peter) at Pentecost (*Yahuah (the LORD) said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand... Elohim (God) hath made that same Yahusha (Jesus), whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Messiah (Christ),* Acts 2:34-36); Stephan (Stephen) (*Yahusha (Jesus) standing on the right hand of Elohim (God),* Acts 7:55-56); the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) at Romans 8:34, 1 Corinthians 15:25, Ephesians 1:20, Colossians 3:1; the Hebrews substance at 1:3, 1:13, 5:6, 7:17, 8:1, 10:12, 12:2, carrying the priestly order of Melek Tsadiq (Melchizedek) of Psalm 110:4; the apostle Kefa (Peter) at 1 Peter 3:22; and the apostle Yochanan (John) at Revelation 3:21. *Till I make thine enemies thy footstool* is the eschatological substance the apostle Sha'ul (Paul) names — *he must reign, till he hath put all enemies under his feet. The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death* (1 Corinthians 15:25-26) — the Day of Yahuah (the LORD) the prophets named at Isaiah 13, Joel 2-3, Ezekiel 38-39, Zechariah 14, the King's own Olivet discourse, and the apostle Yochanan's (John's) Revelation 19-20.

This is the same Formless-Formed grid the transfiguration walked at the Father's voice naming the Son. There the Father's voice carried a quadruple citation — the prophet-like-unto-Mosheh of Deuteronomy 18:18, the Servant of Isaiah 42:1, the royal Son of Psalm 2:7, and the substance the present chapter walks, Psalm 110:1. The King now brings Psalm 110 from the Davidic prophetic substance into his own mouth at the temple, at the highest level the Synoptic gospels reach.

*And no man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.* (Matthew 22:46)

The closing line lays the substance the whole chapter walked. The non-answer the man without the wedding garment walked at the king's substance (*he was speechless*) is what the whole system walks at the close — the chief priests and elders at the authority-challenge (*we cannot tell,* Matthew 21:27), the Pharisees and Herodians at verse 22, the Sadducees at verse 33, and now the Pharisees at verse 46. The system has been progressively silenced across two chapters, and the public engagement is categorically closed.

The substance prepares what comes next. The chapter following walks the King's own mouth categorically against the Pharisaic system — the seven woes the system has no substance to answer. Matthew 24-25 walks the Olivet discourse, laying out the final judgment and the coming of the King the Psalm 110 *till I make thine enemies thy footstool* just named. And Matthew 26 forward walks what the chief priests and elders already determined — *consulted that they might take Yahusha (Jesus) by subtilty, and kill him* (Matthew 26:4) — the substance the wicked-husbandmen parable already named at *come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance* (Matthew 21:38). The system walks past public questioning into the plot to kill. And the Son at the Father's right hand the Psalm 110 named is the substance the resurrection and the ascension will walk out — the substance the apostles proclaim throughout the book of Acts and the letters. The chapter closes the public questioning; the way to the cross, the resurrection, and the Son at the Father's right hand is open.
