## The third day — the firstfruits raised

Matthew 28 closes the gospel at the empty tomb and the Great Commission, and the lens that opens it is the feast calendar the Lamb has been walking through. The crucifixion fell on the Pesach (Passover); the resurrection falls on the morrow after the Shabbat (Sabbath) within the feast — the day of the Wave Sheaf. The King of Yashar'el (Israel) rises as the firstfruits the Torah pictured, and the chapter holds five inherited readings in check: the Sunday-substituting-the-Shabbat reading at verse 1, the three-co-equal-persons formula at the baptism, the Torah-abrogation reading at the closing command, the categorical nations-inclusion reading of *all nations,* and the anti-Yahudi (anti-Jewish) misuse of verse 15. And the system-not-people posture holds to the last page: the indictment is of the corrupt Sanhedrin leadership, never of the Yahudi (Jewish) people, for the King is himself a Yahudi (Jew) of the tribe of Yahudah (Judah), and the twelve he sends are Yahudim (Jews).

## The Wave Sheaf and the faithful women

*In the end of the sabbath, as it began to dawn toward the first day of the week, came Miryam (Mary) Magdalene and the other Miryam (Mary) to see the sepulchre.* (Matthew 28:1)

The women kept the Shabbat (Sabbath) before they came — *they returned, and prepared spices and ointments; and rested the sabbath day according to the commandment* (Luke 23:56). The faithful keeping is in the text, and it tells against the reading that the resurrection day replaced the Shabbat (Sabbath). The first day of the week here is the day of the Wave Sheaf Offering the Torah commanded within the Feast of Unleavened Bread: *on the morrow after the sabbath the priest shall wave it... and ye shall count unto you from the morrow after the sabbath... seven sabbaths shall be complete* (Leviticus 23:11, 15). The King rises on the Wave Sheaf day as the firstfruits: *now is Messiah (Christ) risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept... Messiah (Christ) the firstfruits; afterward they that are Messiah's (Christ's) at his coming* (1 Corinthians 15:20, 23). And the fifty-day count from this day runs to Shavu'ot (Pentecost), where the Spirit is poured out (Acts 2:1-4) — *I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh* (Joel 2:28). The Pesach (Passover) at the cross, the Wave Sheaf at the resurrection, Shavu'ot (Pentecost) at the upper room: the King walks the Torah's own feast architecture. The Wave Sheaf morning does not abolish the Shabbat (Sabbath) — the Shabbat (Sabbath) runs forward into the reign itself: *from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me, saith Yahuah (the LORD)* (Isaiah 66:23). The Constantine Sunday Law of 321 CE and the Council of Laodicea did the substituting; the Father did not.

And it is the women who come — Miryam (Mary) Magdalene and the other Miryam (Mary), who kept the faithful witness from the cross (Matthew 27:55-56) through the burial (Matthew 27:61) to this dawn, while the male disciples had fled (Matthew 26:56). They walk the Tanakh pattern of the faithful women — Shifrah (Shiphrah) and Pu'ah (Puah) (Exodus 1:15-22), Miryam (Miriam) the prophetess, Devorah (Deborah), Channah (Hannah) — and they will be the first witnesses of the resurrection.

## The angel, the earthquake, and the irony of the guard

*And, behold, there was a great earthquake: for the angel of Yahuah (the LORD) descended from heaven, and came and rolled back the stone from the door, and sat upon it. His countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow: and for fear of him the keepers did shake, and became as dead men.* (Matthew 28:2-4)

The earthquake bookends the crucifixion's quake (Matthew 27:51) — the Father's act at the cross and the Father's act at the tomb — the Tanakh's sign: *then the earth shook and trembled* (Psalm 18:7); *I will shake the heavens, and the earth* (Haggai 2:6). The angel of Yahuah (the LORD) descends, the Tanakh figure who appeared to Hagar (Genesis 16:7), to Avraham (Abraham) at the binding (Genesis 22:11), at the burning bush (Exodus 3:2), to Gid'on (Gideon) (Judges 6:11) — *his countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow,* the glorified description of Daniel 10:6 and Revelation 1:14. The stone is rolled away not so the King can leave — the risen King passes through shut doors (John 20:19) — but so the empty tomb can be witnessed.

And the irony closes the Father's reply to the council. The guard they set to prevent a resurrection report (Matthew 27:62-66) becomes the Roman imperial witness *of* the resurrection — the keepers shaking and falling as dead men. It is the pattern of the Father turning the enemy's device against itself: Pharaoh in the sea (Exodus 14:17-18), Haman on his own gallows (Esther 7), Nevukhadnetsar (Nebuchadnezzar) humbled (Daniel 4). The watch set to bury the witness guarantees it.

## The announcement — He is risen, as he said

*And the angel answered and said unto the women, Fear not ye... He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay. And go quickly, and tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead; and, behold, he goeth before you into Galilee; there shall ye see him.* (Matthew 28:5-7)

*Fear not ye* is the Father's comfort across the Tanakh — *Fear not, Avram (Abram): I am thy shield* (Genesis 15:1); *Fear thou not; for I am with thee* (Isaiah 41:10). *He is risen, as he said* fulfills every resurrection prediction the King gave — the sign of Yonah (Jonah), *so shall the Son of Adam be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth* (Matthew 12:40); *the third day* (Matthew 16:21); *after I am risen again, I will go before you into Galilee* (Matthew 26:32). And it walks the Tanakh's resurrection cluster: *thou wilt not leave my soul in sheol; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption* (Psalm 16:10); *after two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up* (Hosea 6:2); *when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin, he shall see his seed, he shall prolong his days* (Isaiah 53:10); *many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake* (Daniel 12:2).

*Come, see the place where the Lord lay* is the come-and-see posture itself — the same invitation Philip gave Natan'el (Nathanael): *Come and see* (John 1:46). The work is laid open; the women look. And *he goeth before you into Galilee* is the shepherd going before the sheep — *smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered* (Zechariah 13:7), the scattered sheep now gathered back to the shepherd. Galilee matters: it is *Galilee of the goyim (Gentiles)... the people which sat in darkness saw great light* (Isaiah 9:1-2; Matthew 4:15-16) — the territory of the scattered northern house of Yashar'el (Israel). The King's ministry ran from the Galilee of the scattered seed, and from there the commission to the scattered seed among the nations will go out.

## The first appearance — go tell my brethren

*And as they went to tell his disciples, behold, Yahusha (Jesus) met them, saying, All hail. And they came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him. Then said Yahusha (Jesus) unto them, Be not afraid: go tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me.* (Matthew 28:8-10)

The King's first resurrection appearance is to the faithful women — they held him by the feet and worshipped, a physical resurrection, not a Gnostic spiritualized one: *a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have* (Luke 24:39). And they *worshipped him* — the Formless source worshipped in the Formed expression, the King receiving the worship.

*Go tell my brethren* carries the term the King defined of himself — *whosoever shall do the will of my Father which is in heaven, the same is my brother* (Matthew 12:50); *the least of these my brethren* (Matthew 25:40). And Sha'ul (Paul) names exactly whose brethren they are, citing the vindication psalm of the cross: *he is not ashamed to call them brethren, saying, I will declare thy name unto my brethren* (Hebrews 2:11-12, quoting Psalm 22:22); *he took on him the seed of Avraham (Abraham). Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto his brethren* (Hebrews 2:16-17); *that he might be the firstborn among many brethren* (Romans 8:29). The brethren are the seed of promise — the gathered remnant of the twelve tribes, not categorical humanity. The Father's gathering of those tribes is the thing the prophets named: *recover the remnant of his people* (Isaiah 11:11); the two sticks made one (Ezekiel 37:15-28); the new covenant *with the house of Yashar'el (Israel), and with the house of Yahudah (Judah)* (Jeremiah 31:31); *ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God)* (Hosea 1:10).

And the cause-and-effect of hearing holds even here, at the resurrection, against the inclusion gospel's inversion. *Ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep... My sheep hear my voice* (John 10:26-27) — the names written from the foundation of the world (Revelation 13:8; 17:8). It is not *respond and become* a son; it is *if you hear, you are* a son of Avraham (Abraham), a son of Adam, the seed of promise — heard because you were these things, the hearing revealing what was always true.

## The bribery and the system, not the people

*And when they were assembled with the elders, and had taken counsel, they gave large money unto the soldiers, saying, Say ye, His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept... So they took the money, and did as they were taught: and this saying is commonly reported among the Yahudim (Jews) until this day.* (Matthew 28:11-15)

The watch reports what they saw, and the leadership buys a lie. It is the corrupt leadership the seven woes exposed — *woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!* (Matthew 23) — walking the bribery the prophets named: *a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise* (Deuteronomy 16:19); *every one loveth gifts, and followeth after rewards* (Isaiah 1:23); *the heads thereof judge for reward... and the prophets thereof divine for money* (Micah 3:11). And the cover story collapses on its own terms: sleeping soldiers cannot witness a theft, and the disciples who fled (Matthew 26:56) and denied (Matthew 26:69-75) are no night-raiders against a Roman watch.

*This saying is commonly reported among the Yahudim (Jews) until this day* is the verse the inherited church misused to brand a whole people — and it names a leadership cover-up walked out into the broader community, not a collective guilt. The people in the system are victims of the deception the leadership handed them in good faith. And the Pharisaic-Sadducean leadership the King stood against is the first costume of one architecture the chapter quietly names by exposing it: a flesh-credential gospel — *be circumcised, follow the traditions, submit to the leadership, you are in.* The same architecture wears four costumes after it. Institutional Christianity from Constantine forward: *be baptized, say the prayer, join the membership, submit to the hierarchy, you are saved.* Hebrew Roots' false inclusion: *believe and Torah-keep and you are Yashar'el (Israel), regardless of paternal blood.* Islam: *say the shahada, keep the pillars, you are in the covenant transferred to Ishmael.* Four costumes, one flesh-credential gospel, one denial of the twelve-tribe gathering. And the one test sorts them all: *hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar* (1 John 2:3-4). The system, never the people.

## The mountain — they worshipped, but some doubted

*Then the eleven disciples went away into Galilee, into a mountain where Yahusha (Jesus) had appointed them. And when they saw him, they worshipped him: but some doubted.* (Matthew 28:16-17)

The eleven are the twelve minus Yahudah (Judas), before Mattityahu (Matthias) is added (Acts 1:15-26) — the twelve to be restored, for the twelve-tribe architecture holds: *ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Matthew 19:28); *the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb* (Revelation 21:14). The mountain is the Tanakh's place of the Father's word — Sinai, where the Torah was given; Tsion (Zion); the Mount of Beatitudes; the Mount of Transfiguration; the mountain of Yahuah's (the LORD's) house the prophets named (Isaiah 2:2; Micah 4:1).

And Matthew does not airbrush the moment: *they worshipped him: but some doubted.* The Greek is hesitation, not rejection — the same honest doubt that the risen King met in T'oma (Thomas), *except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails... I will not believe* (John 20:25), and in the disciples who *yet believed not for joy* (Luke 24:41). The King gives the Great Commission to a worshipping and doubting eleven. The Father works through imperfect disciples.

## All power is given unto me

*And Yahusha (Jesus) came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.* (Matthew 28:18)

*Pasa exousia* — all authority — over heaven and earth, is Daniel's vision come to its hour: *there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away* (Daniel 7:14). Daniel preserves the kaph — *one like the Son of Adam* — honoring the incarnation: the Formed cloud-rider who took on flesh while remaining who he was. The cloud-riding is the Father's own signature — *who maketh the clouds his chariot* (Psalm 104:3); *Yahuah (the LORD) rideth upon a swift cloud* (Isaiah 19:1) — and the dominion *was given,* the Formless source giving into the Formed expression: *Elohim (God) also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name* (Philippians 2:9). This is the enthronement the King named at his trial — *the Son of Adam sitting on the right hand of power* (Matthew 26:64) — fulfilled at the resurrection, Psalm 110:1's *Sit thou at my right hand* made good. And it is the last Adam taking up the dominion the first Adam lost: *thou hast put all things under his feet* (Psalm 8:6); *we see Yahusha (Jesus)... crowned with glory and honour* (Hebrews 2:9). The counterfeit's dominion is never everlasting — the little horn that thinks *to change times and laws* (Daniel 7:25) has its dominion taken away (Daniel 7:26). Only the Formed expression holds the kingdom that shall not pass.

## Go ye therefore, and teach all nations

*Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit): Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.* (Matthew 28:19-20)

*Go ye therefore* — the *therefore* draws straight out of the dominion just given: the commission rests on the all-power. And here the inherited reading takes *teach all nations* — *panta ta ethnē* — as the unlocking of the mission to the nations categorically. But the King had locked the scope twice: *Go not into the way of the goyim (Gentiles)... but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Matthew 10:5-6); *I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Yashar'el (Israel)* (Matthew 15:24). The commission does not unlock that scope — it extends it *territorially,* because the lost sheep of both houses are now scattered *among* the nations. *Panta ta ethnē* is the territories — go into the lands of the nations and make disciples of the scattered seed dwelling there. It is the second recovery the prophets named: *Yahuah (the LORD) shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people... and gather together the dispersed of Yahudah (Judah) from the four corners of the earth* (Isaiah 11:11-12); the two sticks made one nation (Ezekiel 37:22); the new covenant with the two houses (Jeremiah 31:31). The mission runs out from Galilee of the scattered seed into the broader territories — *to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad* (James 1:1); *to the strangers scattered throughout* (1 Peter 1:1). The three categories hold: the house of Yahudah (Judah), the scattered seed of the house of Yashar'el (Israel), and the nations. The individual of the nations may hear and be granted recognition — as the centurion was, as Rachav (Rahab) and Ruth and Naaman were — but the categorical grafting of the nations into the covenant is not what this commission does. The nations' place is the reign, receiving priestly instruction from the gathered remnant: *ye shall be named the Priests of Yahuah (the LORD)* (Isaiah 61:6); the nations flowing to the mountain (Micah 4:1-3). His sheep hear his voice because they were sheep; those who reject, the dust is shaken off (Matthew 10:14).

*Baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit)* names the relational architecture the whole gospel has carried — and it is neither the three-co-equal-persons formula, nor the modalist collapse, nor the Arian created Son. The Father is the Formless source, the one no eye sees — *whom no man hath seen, nor can see* (1 Timothy 6:16). The Son is the Formed expression drawn from him — *the Word was made flesh* (John 1:14) — the same Formed One the Tanakh shows: *before Avraham (Abraham) was, I am* (John 8:58); *that Rock was Messiah (Christ)* (1 Corinthians 10:4). The Spirit is the divine presence and action, the Father and Son yielded out (Joel 2:28; John 14:26). The three were all present at the Yarden (Jordan) baptism — the Father's voice from heaven, the Son in the water, the Spirit as a dove (Matthew 3:16-17) — and at the transfiguration. And the apostles baptized *in the name of Yahusha (Jesus)* (Acts 2:38; 8:16; 10:48; 19:5), because the name Yahusha (Jesus) *is* "Yahuah (the LORD) saves," the name above every name (Philippians 2:9) — the one baptism into the one relational reality.

*Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you* closes the gospel exactly where it opened. At the Sermon on the Mount the King had said: *Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil. For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled* (Matthew 5:17-18). The Matthean bookend lands here: the Torah is not abolished, it is internalized — *I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts* (Jeremiah 31:33); *cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them* (Ezekiel 36:27). The King's commandments are the heart-Torah of the Sermon on the Mount, the two on which *hang all the law and the prophets* (Matthew 22:40). This is the place the Reformation's truncated grammar fails: *saved by grace through faith, not of works* (Ephesians 2:8-9) was never meant to stop there — the very next breath is *created in Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) unto good works* (Ephesians 2:10). Grace is the means of return *to* the commandments, not the replacement of them. *Do we then make void the law through faith? Elohim (God) forbid: yea, we establish the law* (Romans 3:31); *they that keep the commandments of Elohim (God), and the faith of Yahusha (Jesus)* (Revelation 14:12). And the verdict the King already gave stands over any profession without obedience: *I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity (anomia, torahlessness)* (Matthew 7:23).

*Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world* closes the largest bookend of all. The gospel opened on the second name of the child: *they shall call his name Immanuel, which being interpreted is, Elohim (God) with us* (Matthew 1:23). It ends on the promise made good — Elohim (God) with us, from the incarnation to the end of the age. *The end of the world* is *heōs tēs synteleias tou aiōnos,* the end of the age — the Day of Yahuah (the LORD) and the reign the Olivet discourse named. The presence runs through the Spirit (John 14:16-18) all the way to the closing panel: *the tabernacle of Elohim (God) is with men, and he will dwell with them* (Revelation 21:3). And *Amen* closes the gospel — the Hebrew word of truth and faithfulness, the name the King himself carries: *these things saith the Amen, the faithful and true witness* (Revelation 3:14).

So the gospel that opened with the seed running through the men, the genealogy of the King, and the Formed One coming in the flesh closes with that same King raised as the firstfruits, given all dominion, sending his Yahudi (Jewish) apostles to gather the scattered seed of the twelve tribes from among the nations — baptizing them into the one Yahuah (God), Formless in source and Formed in expression; teaching them the Torah written now on the heart; and present with them, Immanuel, to the end of the age. The Matthew sweep closes. Amen.
