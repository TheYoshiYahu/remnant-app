## The healing ministry opens by sending a man to the priest

Matthew 8 opens the post-Sermon ministry. The Sermon delivered the heart-Torah across chapters 5-7; now the ministry turns to the credentialing-miracles that authenticate the kingdom-at-hand proclamation. And the very first thing the King does after binding himself and the kingdom-citizens to every jot and tittle of the Torah (Matthew 5:18) is heal a leper and send him to the priest to fulfill what Mosheh (Moses) commanded. The institutional-Christian narrative of Yahusha (Jesus) as the transition-away-from-Torah collapses on Matthew 8:4 at the very inauguration of the healing ministry. The chapter's miracles are not a new dispensation displacing the old; they are the Formed-in-flesh demonstrating that the cleansing the temple-architecture only signified is now standing in the territory in person — and pointing the cleansed back into the Torah's own procedure.

## The leper cleansed — the Torah-affirming first miracle

*When he was come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him. And, behold, there came a leper and worshipped him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean. And Yahusha (Jesus) put forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will; be thou clean. And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.* (Matthew 8:1-3)

The leper's approach is itself framework-significant. He addresses Yahusha (Jesus) as *Lord* — the Greek *kurios* — and confesses both the King's WILL (*if thou wilt*) and CAPACITY (*thou canst make me clean*) as the operative variables. The faith is in the King-recognition form: recognition of authority, recognition of capacity, submission to the King's will. He does not demand; he requests.

The King responds with a TOUCH. The Torah's purity-architecture treated leprosy as a defiling condition; touching a leper would ordinarily render the toucher ritually impure:

*And the leper in whom the plague is, his clothes shall be rent, and his head bare, and he shall put a covering upon his upper lip, and shall cry, Unclean, unclean. All the days wherein the plague shall be in him he shall be defiled; he is unclean: he shall dwell alone; without the camp shall his habitation be.* (Leviticus 13:45-46)

Yahusha (Jesus) touches the leper and is NOT defiled — instead the leper is CLEANSED. The King's presence is the antithesis of the defiling-flow; he is the cleansing-source whose touch reverses the impurity rather than absorbing it. This is the nature-of-God reading at work: he is the Formed who carries the cleansing-substance the temple only signified. And then comes the move that locks the Sermon's Torah-teaching at the practice level:

*And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, See thou tell no man; but go thy way, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift that Mosheh (Moses) commanded, for a testimony unto them.* (Matthew 8:4)

The cleansed leper is sent to the priest. The Torah's cleansing-procedure is to be fulfilled:

*This shall be the law of the leper in the day of his cleansing: He shall be brought unto the priest: And the priest shall go forth out of the camp; and the priest shall look, and, behold, if the plague of leprosy be healed in the leper; Then shall the priest command to take for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop ... And he shall sprinkle upon him that is to be cleansed from the leprosy seven times, and shall pronounce him clean.* (Leviticus 14:2-4, 7)

The offerings, the rituals, the seven-day waiting, the priest's pronouncement — they are to be carried out. The King does not say *now you're cleansed by my touch and the Mosheh procedure is no longer needed.* He says *fulfill what Mosheh commanded.* The Christian commentary that frames the ministry as a transition-away-from-the-Torah disproves itself on this verse: the first miracle is healing-AND-Torah-fulfillment, not healing-AND-Torah-abolition.

The *for a testimony unto them* carries weight. The Greek *eis marturion autois* — *for a witness to them.* The Yahudah (Judah)-priesthood operating under the Mosheh-procedure receives a leper-claim that fulfills the protocol, and the priest who certifies the pronouncement is shown that the cleansing came from outside the temple-system. The witness is positioned for the priesthood's response — and it bore fruit in some quarters within the apostolic generation:

*And the word of Elohim (God) increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Yerushalayim (Jerusalem) greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.* (Acts 6:7)

## The centurion's servant — great faith, and an individual mercy that is not inclusion

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) was entered into Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum), there came unto him a centurion, beseeching him, And saying, Lord, my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, grievously tormented. And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, I will come and heal him. The centurion answered and said, Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed. For I am a man under authority, having soldiers under me: and I say to this man, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.* (Matthew 8:5-9)

The Christian commentary tradition has often used this passage as a foreign-inclusion proof-text — *Jesus heals a Roman, therefore the gospel was always for the nations regardless of covenant lineage.* The passage does not carry that. Luke's parallel supplies the background Matthew condenses: the elders of the Yahudim (Jews) intercede on the centurion's behalf, naming his standing in the local community — *he loveth our nation, and he hath built us a synagogue* (Luke 7:5). He is a God-fearer, perhaps a proselyte, embedded in the Kfar-Nachum (Capernaum) gathering — exactly the kind of figure to expect in the post-Assyrian-resettlement mixed-territory of Galilee, a man who has ATTACHED himself to the covenant-community as far as a foreign-born man could, not a random pagan with no covenant-relationship at all.

And the distinction matters, because what happens here is an individual-mercy, not an absorption into the seed-of-promise. The one entry-mechanism for a foreign-origin person into the seed is the marriage covenant — a daughter-of-the-nations absorbed into a son-of-Yashar'el's seed-identity through the two-become-one of Genesis 2:24. No foreign MAN ever converts into the seed; a man cannot change his father's blood, and the foreign men who attach to the covenant always were and always will be strangers and sojourners. The centurion receives mercy — his servant healed — without becoming seed-of-promise; he stands alongside the Canaanite woman of Matthew 15, Naaman the Syrian, and the widow of Tsarephath as the cases that prove the line precisely by receiving mercy without crossing it. His faith is honored; his standing as a *ger* attached to the community is honored; the covenant-distinction is not dissolved by either.

The centurion recognizes the King's authority — the Greek *exousia* — as the mechanism of the cosmic-rule structure. *I am a man under authority* — he sees himself in the chain-of-command of Roman military administration; *having soldiers under me* — he gives orders that operate at distance. He maps this onto the King: the King has authority over disease, over the principalities-and-powers, over the cosmic administration; the King speaks and the effect carries at distance. This is exactly the *authority* the multitudes recognized at Matthew 7:28-29 — and the centurion, from outside the credentialed Pharisaic system, has internalized it more deeply than the credentialed leaders the King has met.

*When Yahusha (Jesus) heard it, he marvelled, and said to them that followed, Verily I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Yashar'el (Israel).* (Matthew 8:10)

The Christian reading takes this as a put-down of the Yahudim (Jewish) community — *even the Gentiles have more faith than the Jews.* That is not what it says. The King is not contrasting the centurion's faith with the covenant-substance of Yashar'el (Israel) as a people; he is contrasting it with the FAITH-LEVEL the dominant religious-leadership-system in contemporary Yashar'el (Israel) produced — the Pharisaic-Sadducean system Matthew 3:7 called the *generation of vipers,* the system Matthew 23 will indict in full. The centurion's authority-recognition exceeds what the credentialing-without-trusting establishment produced. The verse indicts the system, not the people. The posture toward Yahudah (Judah) is unwavering — Yahudah (Judah) is the surviving covenant-people-with-identity, and Jew-hatred is never justified by any verse. The man's faith stands out against a leadership-system that had been credentialing-without-trusting for centuries; the seed-war pattern shows through — the wheat surfaces in unexpected places, and the system's credentials are not the markers of the gathering.

**Many from east and west.** Then the King speaks one of the most weaponized verses in the chapter:

*And I say unto you, That many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Avraham (Abraham), and Yitschaq (Isaac), and Ya'aqov (Jacob), in the kingdom of heaven. But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.* (Matthew 8:11-12)

The Christian-replacement reading runs: *the Gentiles (the many from east and west) will replace the Jews (the children of the kingdom) in the kingdom because the Jews rejected Jesus.* The framework rejects this at every level by recovering the Tanakh-stream the King is quoting. *East and west* is not a metaphor for the nations; it is the prophetic-gathering directional-formula the prophets had used for centuries to name the ingathering of the scattered seed:

*Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east, and gather thee from the west.* (Isaiah 43:5)

*Behold, these shall come from far: and, lo, these from the north and from the west; and these from the land of Sinim.* (Isaiah 49:12)

*Let the redeemed of Yahuah (the LORD) say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy; And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.* (Psalm 107:2-3)

*Thus saith Yahuah Tseva'ot (the LORD of hosts); Behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country; And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Yerushalayim (Jerusalem): and they shall be my people, and I will be their Elohim (God), in truth and in righteousness.* (Zechariah 8:7-8)

The many who *shall come from east and west* are the GATHERED SCATTERED SEED of Yashar'el (Israel) coming home from the lands of their dispersion. They sit down with Avraham (Abraham), Yitschaq (Isaac), and Ya'aqov (Jacob) in the kingdom because they ARE the seed of the patriarchs gathered home — the wild olive of the broken-off line, returning to the root of the fathers. The verse is the gathering-prophecy, not the replacement-prophecy.

And the *children of the kingdom* cast out are not all of present-day Yahudah (Judah). The Greek is *hoi huioi tēs basileias* — those with the STANDING of kingdom-children, those who held the position by lineage-claim alone without the heart-Torah walk. Yochanan (John) the Immerser had already named the diagnosis:

*And think not to say within yourselves, We have Avraham (Abraham) to our father: for I say unto you, that Elohim (God) is able of these stones to raise up children unto Avraham (Abraham).* (Matthew 3:9)

Lineage-claim alone is not the qualifying credential. The casting-out is the post-harvest sifting of the wheat — the same sifting Ezekiel named:

*And I will bring you out from the people, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out. And I will bring you into the wilderness of the people, and there will I plead with you face to face... And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant: And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me: I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Yashar'el (Israel): and ye shall know that I am Yahuah (the LORD).* (Ezekiel 20:34-38)

The *children of the kingdom* cast into outer darkness are the lineage-claim-alone members whose fruit was not consistent with the covenant-substance — the same dismissal as Matthew 7:23's *I never knew you; depart from me,* but here the discrimination is among present-day covenant-claimants who relied on lineage-credential alone, where 7:23 was the parallel discrimination among the supernatural-ministry-performing antinomian *Lord, Lord* confessors. The framework dismantles systems, not peoples: some among present-day Yahudah (Judah) walk the heart-Torah and stand among the wheat; some rely on lineage-claim alone and are sifted out; some of the scattered seed coming home hear and respond, and some do not. The sifting cuts across both groups. The verse names the sifting, not the categorical rejection of either. It does not soften the *outer darkness* and *weeping and gnashing of teeth* into vague general-judgment language, and it does not collapse into Gentile-inclusion-replacing-Israel. Both errors are ruled out by the Tanakh-stream the King is quoting.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto the centurion, Go thy way; and as thou hast believed, so be it done unto thee. And his servant was healed in the selfsame hour.* (Matthew 8:13)

The King heals from outside the credentialed system at the request of a man who recognized the King from outside the credentialed system. The mercy is real; the faith is honored; the gathering-pattern of the broader mission is foreshadowed.

## Kefa's mother-in-law and the evening healings — the Servant bearing our infirmities

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) was come into Kefa's (Peter's) house, he saw his wife's mother laid, and sick of a fever. And he touched her hand, and the fever left her: and she arose, and ministered unto them. When the even was come, they brought unto him many that were possessed with devils: and he cast out the spirits with his word, and healed all that were sick: That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Yeshayahu (Isaiah) the prophet, saying, Himself took our infirmities, and bare our sicknesses.* (Matthew 8:14-17)

Matthew anchors the evening-healings to Isaiah 53 — and the anchoring opens a reading the institutional-Christian tradition has not always carried. Isaiah 53 is the Suffering-Servant prophecy, and the Servant is the Formed — Yahusha (Jesus) in flesh, the *Immanuel — Elohim (God) with us* of Matthew 1:23 — bearing the redemptive substance the Father appointed. Matthew applies the bearing-of-infirmities not to the cross alone but to the HEALING ministry: the Servant's bearing of our griefs is operative across the whole Messianic ministry, not collapsed into a single moment.

*Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did esteem him stricken, smitten of Elohim (God), and afflicted.* (Isaiah 53:4)

The healings are not random acts of compassion; they are the Messianic-credential prophecy publicly displayed. The signature the prophet had named lives in Isaiah 35:

*Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.* (Isaiah 35:5-6)

This is the credential the King will cite himself a few chapters downstream, when Yochanan (John) the Immerser sends from prison to ask whether he is the one who should come:

*Yahusha (Jesus) answered and said unto them, Go and shew Yochanan (John) again those things which ye do hear and see: The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.* (Matthew 11:4-5)

The list at Matthew 11:5 is the Isaiah 35:5-6 signature, point by point. And the *cast out the spirits with his word* names the deliverance-register of the same authority. The principalities-and-powers under the watcher-rebellion administration — the powers that fell with the Watchers, the gods of the nations assigned to the seventy peoples — recognize and submit to the King's word. The same authority-pattern Matthew 7:28-29 named at the teaching level operates here at the deliverance level: the kingdom-at-hand proclamation authenticated by the kingdom-at-hand power-demonstration.

## Would-be followers — the Son of Adam with nowhere to lay his head

*Now when Yahusha (Jesus) saw great multitudes about him, he gave commandment to depart unto the other side. And a certain scribe came, and said unto him, Master, I will follow thee whithersoever thou goest. And Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of Adam hath not where to lay his head. And another of his disciples said unto him, Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father. But Yahusha (Jesus) said unto him, Follow me; and let the dead bury their dead.* (Matthew 8:18-22)

The English *son of man* hides what *Son of Adam* names. The Hebrew *ben adam* and the Greek *huios tou anthrōpou* carry the seed-of-Adam lineage-of-promise on which the whole framework rests — the specific Adamic paternal line traced through Matthew's chapter-1 genealogy back to the seed Yahuah (God) breathed into Adam. The capitalization at v.20 is significant: *the Son of Adam* — *the* (Greek *ho*) plus the lineage-title — is the King naming himself as THE seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise. This is title-naming, distinct from the three places the canon preserves the kaph-comparative — Daniel 7:13's *kbar enash* and Revelation 1:13 / 14:14's *homoion huion anthrōpou,* which render as *one like the Son of Adam,* the kaph honoring the incarnation truth that he resembled mortal-man because he took on flesh while remaining the Formed. Here at Matthew 8:20 the kaph is absent; the King names the title directly.

The Hebrew library carried this named-title centuries before, in the throne-room vision of Enoch the righteous:

*And there I saw One who had a head of days, and His head was white like wool, and with Him was another being whose countenance had the appearance of a man, and his face was full of graciousness, like one of the holy angels. And I asked the angel who went with me, who showed me all the hidden things, concerning that Son of Adam, who he was, and whence he was, (and) why he went with the Head of Days? And he answered and said unto me: This is the Son of Adam who hath righteousness, with whom dwelleth righteousness, and who revealeth all the treasures of that which is hidden, because Yahuah (God) of Spirits hath chosen him, and whose lot hath the pre-eminence before Yahuah (God) of Spirits in uprightness for ever.* (1 Enoch 46:1-3)

The vision names the figure twice — *that Son of Adam* and *the Son of Adam who hath righteousness* — named, identified by role, located beside the Head of Days, chosen, given the pre-eminence for ever. The title is the Christ's; the figure is the Christ in heavenly vision before the incarnation — the Formed, who proceeds from the Head of Days and came in the flesh as Yahusha (Jesus). At Matthew 8:20 the King walks the same architecture, naming himself the same titled figure 1 Enoch had named. The Christian commentary that flattens *son of man* to a generic *mortal human* loses both the lineage-weight *Son of Adam* carries and the named-title parallel 1 Enoch secures.

The two would-be followers are filtered. The scribe confesses all-in commitment — *whithersoever thou goest* — and the King warns rather than embraces: *the foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of Adam hath not where to lay his head.* The King has no home in the present age. The kingdoms of this world are under the temporary delegation that came down with the watcher-rebellion administration — the same principalities Paul names at Ephesians 6:12, the same kingdoms the devil offered the King from a mountain at Matthew 4:8-10. The King operates in the territory of his enemy, through a visible creation he himself called into being but that has been temporarily delegated to the rebel-administrators. The scribe's *whithersoever* needs to grasp that there is no resting-place in the present age.

The second would-be follower asks to bury his father first, and the King replies *let the dead bury their dead.* This is not a categorical anti-family-honor teaching — the Torah's fifth commandment treats parent-honor as a serious covenant-obligation:

*Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which Yahuah Elohayka (the LORD thy God) giveth thee.* (Exodus 20:12)

And the patriarchal-burial obligations carried the same weight in the Tanakh narrative:

*And Yoseph (Joseph) said unto Pharaoh, My father made me swear, saying, Lo, I die: in my grave which I have digged for me in the land of Kena'an (Canaan), there shalt thou bury me. Now therefore let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father, and I will come again.* (Genesis 50:5)

The King is the one who indicts the *Corban* practice that voids the fifth commandment by oral-tradition workaround:

*For Mosheh (Moses) said, Honour thy father and thy mother; and, Whoso curseth father or mother, let him die the death: But ye say, If a man shall say to his father or mother, It is Corban, that is to say, A gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; he shall be free. And ye suffer him no more to do ought for his father or his mother; Making the word of Elohim (God) of none effect through your tradition.* (Mark 7:10-13)

He is not abolishing parent-honor; he is its defender. Matthew 8:22 is a kingdom-priority clarification at an urgent moment. Two readings hold within the framework. The more probable: *let the spiritually-dead — those who have not heard the kingdom-call — bury their physically-dead; you who have heard, come and follow now while the kingdom is being proclaimed.* The kingdom-at-hand moment is finite. The alternative: the father may not yet be dead, and the disciple is asking to wait until he dies, possibly years away — in which case the King declines the indefinite postponement, not the burial itself. Either way, parent-honor remains a Torah-commandment, and the kingdom-call carries an urgency that cannot be indefinitely deferred. The Christian flattening of this verse into anti-family rhetoric is ruled out.

## Stilling the storm — the Formed cloud-rider over creation, and the Jonah inversion

*And when he was entered into a ship, his disciples followed him. And, behold, there arose a great tempest in the sea, insomuch that the ship was covered with the waves: but he was asleep. And his disciples came to him, and awoke him, saying, Lord, save us: we perish. And he saith unto them, Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith? Then he arose, and rebuked the winds and the sea; and there was a great calm. But the men marvelled, saying, What manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him!* (Matthew 8:23-27)

The stilling walks the Tanakh-pattern of Yahuah's (God's) authority over creation. The Psalms named the Voice that rules the sea:

*They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters; These see the works of Yahuah (the LORD), and his wonders in the deep. For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof... Then they cry unto Yahuah (the LORD) in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses. He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof are still.* (Psalm 107:23-29)

And the wisdom-tradition named the Voice that set the sea's boundaries at creation:

*Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, as if it had issued out of the womb?... And brake up for it my decreed place, and set bars and doors, And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed?* (Job 38:8-11)

The Voice that spoke creation into being and rules the sea in the Tanakh-pattern is the Formed — the Son drawn from the Formless — who now sleeps in flesh in a fishing boat and then wakes to rebuke the winds and the sea. The same Voice that thundered the ten words at Sinai and that the multitudes recognized at Matthew 7:29 now exercises the same authority over wind and sea. The disciples' *what manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him* is the recognition-pattern beginning to land — linking the King directly to the creation-ruling Voice. The *O ye of little faith* reads as it did at Matthew 6:30: kindness-with-correction, not dismissal; the disciples' faith is small but real, and the storm is a teaching-moment in the trust the Lord's Prayer presupposes.

The narrative deliberately echoes Yonah (Jonah) 1 and then inverts the resolution. Yonah (Jonah) went down into the ship and slept through the storm; the sailors woke him in distress; and the storm calmed only when Yonah (Jonah) was REMOVED — he was the CAUSE of the storm whose removal restored the calm:

*But Yonah (Jonah) was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep. So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? arise, call upon thy Elohim (God), if so be that Elohim (God) will think upon us, that we perish not.* (Jonah 1:5-6)

Matthew walks the same setup beat-for-beat — King in the ship, storm rising, disciples waking the sleeper — and then inverts. The King is NOT the cause of the storm; he is the AUTHORITY OVER it. He does not need to be removed; he speaks, and the wind and the sea obey. The disciples' question registers the recognition that this is a figure of a wholly different order than the rebellious prophet of the Yonah (Jonah) story — and the King's own naming downstream locks the foreshadowing:

*The men of Ninveh (Nineveh) shall rise in judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: because they repented at the preaching of Yonah (Jonah); and, behold, a greater than Yonah (Jonah) is here.* (Matthew 12:41)

The Hebrew library's narrative-type is restored AND inverted. The King does not introduce a new storm-and-prophet pattern; he walks the library's existing pattern and reveals himself as its fulfillment at greater voltage.

## The Gadarene demoniacs and the swine — the King reaches the eastern tribes

*And when he was come to the other side into the country of the Gergesenes, there met him two possessed with devils, coming out of the tombs, exceeding fierce, so that no man might pass by that way. And, behold, they cried out, saying, What have we to do with thee, Yahusha (Jesus), thou Son of Elohim (God)? art thou come hither to torment us before the time? And there was a good way off from them an herd of many swine feeding. So the devils besought him, saying, If thou cast us out, suffer us to go away into the herd of swine. And he said unto them, Go. And when they were come out, they went into the herd of swine: and, behold, the whole herd of swine ran violently down a steep place into the sea, and perished in the waters. And they that kept them fled, and went their ways into the city, and told every thing, and what was befallen to the possessed of the devils. And, behold, the whole city came out to meet Yahusha (Jesus): and when they saw him, they besought him that he would depart out of their coasts.* (Matthew 8:28-34)

The geography is the point. *The country of the Gergesenes* (the Gadarenes in some manuscripts; the variant does not change the reading) is the area east of the Sea of Galilee, in the Decapolis, beyond the Yarden (Jordan) — the tribal-inheritance of Gad and the half-tribe of Manasheh (Manasseh) and parts of Reuven (Reuben). The Torah's allotment is recorded in detail:

*And Mosheh (Moses) gave inheritance unto the tribe of Gad, even unto the children of Gad according to their families. And their coast was Yazer, and all the cities of Gil'ad (Gilead)... And he gave inheritance unto the half tribe of Manasheh (Manasseh): and this was the possession of the half tribe of the children of Manasheh (Manasseh) by their families.* (Joshua 13:24-25, 29)

After the Assyrian deportation of 722 BCE, the eastern tribes were among the most heavily impacted; the Decapolis was resettled and Hellenized; by the first century the territory carried a mixed Yahudim (Jewish) and Hellenistic population with the Hellenistic demographic dominant. The King's crossing-to-the-other-side is the same gathering-territory pull noted at Matthew 4:25's multitudes from beyond the Yarden (Jordan): he goes to the territory of the scattered eastern tribes, ministering in the lands of their inheritance even though the contemporary population is mixed.

The swine confirm it. *An herd of many swine feeding* — and swine are unclean under Torah-law:

*And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.* (Leviticus 11:7)

The presence of a large swine-herd marks the contemporary administration as Hellenistic, not Torah-walking — the visible marker of the unclean-economy operating in the eastern-tribal-inheritance under the post-deportation administration. And the swine-drowning is deliberately significant: the demons enter the swine, the swine drown, and the unclean-economy takes a major blow at the King's arrival. Where he is, the unclean arrangements collapse. The leper-cleansing walked the same pattern from the purity-side; the swine-drowning walks it from the judgment-side.

The demons recognize him — *what have we to do with thee, Yahusha (Jesus), thou Son of Elohim (God)? art thou come hither to torment us before the time?* They recognize his Sonship and they recognize the eschatological-timeline: they know their dominion is temporary and a day of judgment is appointed. Ya'aqov (James) named the cognitive-recognition-without-submission of the rebellious spirit-beings:

*Thou believest that there is one Elohim (God); thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble.* (James 2:19)

The demons KNOW; they do not submit. The *time* they reference is the appointed judgment for the rebellious spirit-beings:

*And the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet are, and shall be tormented day and night for ever and ever.* (Revelation 20:10)

The judgment-architecture is fixed; the demons know it; they know they have a temporary dominion now and a fixed end at the appointed time.

The townspeople's response is the rejection-of-the-King-by-the-community-economically-dependent-on-the-unclean-economy. The swine-herd represents wealth; the swine-herd is dead; the King is asked to leave. The economic loss eclipses the deliverance of two men, and the opportunity to receive the King passes. This is the early-modeling of the response the King will codify shortly downstream:

*And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.* (Matthew 10:14)

The pursuit ends where the rejection lands. The good news is proclaimed broadly because the citizens are scattered and only the citizens hear; those who reject, we shake the dust off our feet and do not cast our pearls before swine; the nations as nations are not chased into a worship they will not give. The King leaves the territory and returns to Galilee — but the two delivered men remain as witnesses, as Mark's parallel records:

*Howbeit Yahusha (Jesus) suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things Adonai (the Lord) hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee. And he departed, and began to publish in Decapolis how great things Yahusha (Jesus) had done for him: and all men did marvel.* (Mark 5:19-20)

The pursuit ended; the witness remained — a testimony to any in the eastern tribes who later turn.
