## The Voice that alone forgives sins, in the room in flesh

Matthew 9 deepens the post-Sermon ministry from the credentialing-healings of chapter 8 into forgiveness, calling, fasting-reframing, raising-of-the-dead, the *Son of David* recognition, and the first appearance of the Beelzebul-slander that Matthew 12 will dismantle. The chapter's strongest turn is at its opening: the King forgives a paralytic's sins, and the scribes' theological premise is entirely correct — the Tanakh assigns the forgiveness of sins to Yahuah (the LORD) alone. Their failure is not in the doctrine but in the hearing. They cannot perceive WHO is making the claim in front of them. The same Voice that spoke Isaiah 43 and Psalm 103 through the prophets is standing in the room in flesh — the Formed drawn from the Formless, the God of every Tanakh theophany, now exercising the Father's own prerogative in a body. The whole chapter turns on that recognition, and on the cause-and-effect the inclusion-gospel inverts: the sheep hear the Shepherd because they are sheep; the hearing reveals what was always true; it does not manufacture it.

## The paralytic healed and forgiven — the Son of Adam's authority on earth

*And he entered into a ship, and passed over, and came into his own city. And, behold, they brought to him a man sick of the palsy, lying on a bed: and Yahusha (Jesus) seeing their faith said unto the sick of the palsy; Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee.* (Matthew 9:1-2)

The leper-cleansing demonstrated the King's authority over the purity-architecture; the centurion's servant, his authority over disease at distance; the storm-stilling, his authority over creation; the Gergesene exorcism, his authority over the principalities. Now Matthew stages the demonstration of the King's authority to FORGIVE SINS — the prerogative the Tanakh assigns to Yahuah (the LORD) alone:

*I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions for mine own sake, and will not remember thy sins.* (Isaiah 43:25)

*Bless Yahuah (the LORD), O my soul, and forget not all his benefits: Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases.* (Psalm 103:3)

Forgiveness and healing are paired in the Tanakh's own architecture, and Matthew 9 is the in-person demonstration of that pairing in a single body — the same Voice that spoke Psalm 103 through David in the room speaking in flesh.

The faith the King SEES is the carrier-friends'. Mark records the detail Matthew compresses — *they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed wherein the sick of the palsy lay* (Mark 2:4). The carriers have brought the paralytic through obstacle to the King; their faith is the operative variable the King sees — the hearing-reveals-not-creates pattern at the group level. They heard the Shepherd's voice; the hearing revealed what was already true; the response was the immediate physical action of carrying the suffering one to the King. And the King's address is tender: *Son* (the Greek *teknon,* child). The Shepherd recognizes the sheep before any credentialed status is offered, before any verbal confession is required — the same tender register he will use with the bleeding woman a few verses later. The kingdom-citizen-recognition is in the address itself.

*And, behold, certain of the scribes said within themselves, This man blasphemeth. And Yahusha (Jesus) knowing their thoughts said, Wherefore think ye evil in your hearts? For whether is easier, to say, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and walk? But that ye may know that the Son of Adam hath power on earth to forgive sins, (then saith he to the sick of the palsy,) Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house.* (Matthew 9:3-6)

The scribes' theological premise is CORRECT. Isaiah 43:25 names Yahuah (the LORD) as the sole-forgiver of sins; they are not theologically illiterate on the point. Their problem is the hearing-failure at the level of WHO is making the claim. They have the right premise and reach the wrong conclusion because they cannot perceive the Formed-Voice-of-Sinai standing in front of them. Both pronouncements — *thy sins be forgiven thee* and *arise, and walk* — are equally impossible for a mere man and equally possible for the Formed. The forgiveness-claim cannot be visibly verified at the moment of speaking; the healing-claim CAN. By performing the visibly-verifiable miracle, the King LEGITIMATES the unverifiable claim that preceded it. The scribes' premise is preserved and verified at once: the one in front of them is the Formed-Voice that spoke at Sinai.

And he attaches the claim to a title. The English *son of man* hides what *Son of Adam* names. The Hebrew *ben adam* and the Greek *huios tou anthrōpou* carry the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise — the line of Adam carried forward through the patriarchs into the twelve tribes, the line of the Messianic-seed-promise, the line by which the Formed-in-flesh stands in the bloodline of all who came before him. The Son of Adam, the seed-of-Adam-line-of-promise in person, exercises the Father's prerogative to forgive sins because he IS the Formed who spoke the Torah's expiation-architecture into being at Sinai.

This title-naming is distinct from the kaph-comparative the canon preserves at Daniel 7:13:

*I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of Adam came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him. And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.* (Daniel 7:13-14)

At Daniel 7:13 the text preserves the kaph — *one LIKE the Son of Adam* — honoring the incarnation truth that the figure resembled mortal-man because he took on flesh while remaining the Formed cloud-rider; the harmonized canon identifies that figure as Yahusha (Jesus) himself, to whom v.14's everlasting kingdom belongs. At Matthew 9:6 the kaph is absent; the King names the title directly — *the Son of Adam HATH POWER.* The cloud-rider Daniel saw is the Son of Adam who here exercises the Father's prerogative to forgive sins on earth, with Matthew 28:18's *all power is given unto me in heaven and in earth* as the full-disclosure beat this is one step toward.

*But when the multitudes saw it, they marvelled, and glorified Elohim (God), which had given such power unto men.* (Matthew 9:8)

The multitudes' framing is partial — they perceive the authority but interpret it within the *given to men* register, at the *prophet-with-divine-authority* stage, not yet the Formed-from-the-Formless recognition. The staged-disclosure continues; the fuller recognition will surface at Kefa's (Peter's) confession at Matthew 16:16, the transfiguration voice at Matthew 17:5, and the resurrection-worship at Matthew 28:9.

## The calling of Mattityahu — I will have mercy, and not sacrifice

*And as Yahusha (Jesus) passed forth from thence, he saw a man, named Mattityahu (Matthew), sitting at the receipt of custom: and he saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.* (Matthew 9:9)

This is the system-not-people posture made flesh. Tax-collectors were despised on two grounds: they collected tribute for Roman occupation — collaborators with the occupying power, in the categorical-traitor class to contemporary kingdom-aspirations; and the Pharisaic-purity-system treated them as defiling, because they handled Hellenistic coin-currency bearing imperial-cult imagery and interacted continually with foreign travelers and merchants. Mattityahu was a Yahudi (Jew) in the categorical-sinner class by the standards of the Pharisaic counterfeit-righteousness. And the King calls him to be among the twelve. The kingdom-citizens roster has just been named at the Beatitudes — the *anawim,* the mourners, the hungerers for *tsedaqah,* the merciful, the pure in heart — and none of those markers exclude the tax-collector who hears the Shepherd's voice and rises. The system's categorical exclusion is precisely what the King dismantles. People are victims; systems are the enemy; the King seats the categorical-victim at the inner-circle table.

*And it came to pass, as Yahusha (Jesus) sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.* (Matthew 9:10)

Table-fellowship in the second-temple world was a covenant-affirming act, and the Pharisaic system restricted it to the credentialed-as-pure. The King breaks the social-segregation in deliberate public fashion at a tax-collector's home with *many publicans and sinners* present. The *sinners* (the Greek *hamartoloi*) is the Pharisaic classification-category — those marginalized by the system's purity-credentials — not a moral judgment confirmed from above. The King is among the victims of the credential-system, eating with them.

*And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?* (Matthew 9:11)

The Pharisees address the DISCIPLES, not the King — the indirection-pattern that recurs (the disciples of Yochanan (John) use it at v.14; the hand-washing challenge at Matthew 15:2 addresses the King about the disciples). The King does not let the indirection succeed:

*But when Yahusha (Jesus) heard that, he said unto them, They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.* (Matthew 9:12)

He uses the Pharisees' OWN self-categorization against them — *you have classified them as sick and yourselves as whole; on your own logic, my ministry to the sick is precisely what they need.* And the deeper irony is that the *whole* in their own eyes are sick at the diagnostic level the Sermon just laid down — *I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity,* where the Greek *anomia* is *without-Torah.* The credentialed in their own eyes are working without the heart-Torah; the *sick* in their classification are the ones whose ears can be opened to the Shepherd's voice.

*But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.* (Matthew 9:13)

This is the chapter's crux on the diagnostic-system. The King cites Hosea 6:6 and tells the Pharisees to *learn what that meaneth* — and he will cite the same verse again in the Shabbat-controversy at Matthew 12:7. It is the recurring Tanakh-anchor he deploys against the counterfeit-righteousness:

*For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of Elohim (God) more than burnt offerings.* (Hosea 6:6)

He is standing in a long prophetic stream. Shemu'el (Samuel) opened it at Sha'ul's (Saul's) confrontation:

*And Shemu'el (Samuel) said, Hath Yahuah (the LORD) as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of Yahuah (the LORD)? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.* (1 Samuel 15:22)

Isaiah carried it:

*To what purpose is the multitude of your sacrifices unto me? saith Yahuah (the LORD)... Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil; Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.* (Isaiah 1:11, 16-17)

Jeremiah carried it:

*For I spake not unto your fathers, nor commanded them in the day that I brought them out of the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt), concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices: But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voice, and I will be your Elohim (God), and ye shall be my people: and walk ye in all the ways that I have commanded you, that it may be well unto you.* (Jeremiah 7:22-23)

Amos carried it:

*I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies... But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.* (Amos 5:21, 24)

And Mikhah (Micah) carried it:

*He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth Yahuah (the LORD) require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Elohim (God)?* (Micah 6:8)

The King stands in this whole stream. The counterfeit-righteousness's failure is exactly the failure these prophets indicted — external religiosity without heart-substance, sacrifice without mercy, credential without covenant. And the failure is never the Torah's. The Torah is not the thing the prophets indicted; the prophets indicted the hollowing-out of the Torah's own heart-substance — the *mercy and knowledge of Elohim (God)* the Torah always carried. The heart-Torah of the Sermon IS that mercy-substance; the new-heart of Ezekiel 36:26-27 IS what the King is teaching the disciples to walk in. *Go and learn what that meaneth* is the prophet's same call to come back to the substance the system lost.

And *I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance* carries the irony at full weight. The *righteous* in their own eyes cannot HEAR the call to repent, because they perceive no need of it. The *sinners* — those whose lives have not gone according to the Pharisaic script — can hear, because they know they need to. The sheep hear when the Shepherd calls; the self-credentialed cannot, because the call addresses what they refuse to admit they are. Mattityahu is the contrast in person: categorically classified as sinner at the tax-booth, he rose immediately and followed. The King's voice reached the ears the system had written off, while the system's righteous stood at the meal-perimeter critiquing the host-list.

## The bridegroom and the new wineskins — fasting reframed, the heart-Torah preserved

*Then came to him the disciples of Yochanan (John), saying, Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, but thy disciples fast not?* (Matthew 9:14)

The Pharisees and Yochanan's disciples both observed voluntary twice-weekly fasts; the King's disciples did not. The challenge assumes the voluntary-fasting is the standard of piety. The King answers with a direct Messianic self-claim:

*And Yahusha (Jesus) said unto them, Can the children of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.* (Matthew 9:15)

The bridegroom-language reaches into the Tanakh's own covenant-bridegroom prophecies — the betrothal of Yahuah (the LORD) to his people:

*Turn, O backsliding children, saith Yahuah (the LORD); for I am married unto you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Tsiyon (Zion).* (Jeremiah 3:14)

And Hosea ties the bridegroom-substance directly to the gathering of the scattered-and-divorced northern house — the house named *Lo-Ammi (not my people)* at Hosea 1:9:

*And I will betroth thee unto me for ever; yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies. I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness: and thou shalt know Yahuah (the LORD).* (Hosea 2:19-20)

*And it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living Elohim (God). Then shall the children of Yahudah (Judah) and the children of Yashar'el (Israel) be gathered together, and appoint themselves one head, and they shall come up out of the land: for great shall be the day of Jezreel.* (Hosea 1:10-11)

The bridegroom-substance the King claims IS the betrothal-restoration of the divorced-scattered seed. The Yahuah-of-Yashar'el (Israel) whose bridegroom-substance the prophets named is the Formed-in-flesh standing before the disciples of Yochanan. The gathering-substance of the whole arc surfaces in a single phrase.

*The bridegroom shall be taken from them* is the first explicit cross-foreshadowing in Matthew's narrative-arc — though the disciples would hear it only as some kind of absence. It also foreshadows the present-age-between-the-comings, when the bridegroom is bodily absent and the bride waits in fasting-and-watching for the consummation the wedding of the Lamb will name:

*Let us be glad and rejoice, and give honour to him: for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready... Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb.* (Revelation 19:7, 9)

And *then shall they fast* explicitly endorses fasting as a present-age discipline — the half of the verse Christian commentary often misses. The post-ascension assembly will fast (Acts 13:2-3; 14:23). Fasting is not abolished; it is reframed in the wedding-architecture.

*No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse.* (Matthew 9:16)

Read in the fasting-context the King just gave it, the OLD GARMENT is the fasting-practice of the Pharisaic system; the NEW CLOTH is the bridegroom-joy of the kingdom-at-hand. Pinning the new bridegroom-joy onto the old fasting-practice tears the old apart, because the joy of the bridegroom's presence requires a different mode than the mourning-fasting mode the system was operating in.

*Neither do men put new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved.* (Matthew 9:17)

This is the load-bearing crux of the chapter on the heart-Torah-not-abolition front. The Christian commentary tradition has weaponized this parable as a proof-text for the abolition-of-Torah — *new wine = the gospel; old wineskin = Judaism and Torah; the gospel cannot be contained in Torah-Judaism, therefore Torah is gone.* The reading is the antichrist reading, and it collapses at every level. It collapses the King's own anti-antinomian declaration — *Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil* (Matthew 5:17). It collapses every prophet's promise of Torah-internalization. It collapses the Father's character as the eternally-the-same.

The new wine is the heart-Torah-walking life the King is teaching — the new heart of Ezekiel 36 and the new-covenant Torah-internalization of Jeremiah 31:

*A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.* (Ezekiel 36:26-27)

*But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Yashar'el (Israel); After those days, saith Yahuah (the LORD), I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their Elohim (God), and they shall be my people.* (Jeremiah 31:33)

The old wineskin is the Pharisaic-rabbinic-oral-tradition system — the *takkanot* and *gezerot* and inherited fences built up around the Torah. It is the SYSTEM that cannot contain the heart-Torah-walking the King is teaching, because that walking REJECTS the categorical-classification-of-sinners the system depends on, REJECTS credential-without-substance, REJECTS the sacrifice-without-mercy that Hosea 6:6 indicts. The bridegroom-joy bursts the system; the heart-substance tears the credential-fabric; a new container is needed — the new-heart kingdom-citizens.

And the Torah itself is NEITHER the wine NOR the wineskin. The Torah is the SUBSTANCE the new wine carries. The *law put in their inward parts and written in their hearts* of Jeremiah 31 IS the Torah internalized; the *statutes and judgments* of Ezekiel 36 the new spirit causes them to walk in ARE the Torah's commandments. The collapse that reads *new wine = grace / old wineskin = Torah* is a categorical misreading at every level. *Both are preserved* is the closing key: the Torah-substance is preserved (the foundation stands intact, the heart-Torah carries its full weight) and the kingdom-citizens are preserved (the new-heart container holds the substance without rupturing). The Spirit who lives in the new-heart citizens is the Spirit who spoke at Sinai; he does not lead his inhabitant away from what he spoke. The parable carries the same teaching as Matthew 5:17-19 from a different metaphor-angle. The substance is one.

## Yair's daughter and the bleeding woman — the cleansing-source over the defiling flows and death

*While he spake these things unto them, behold, there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him, saying, My daughter is even now dead: but come and lay thy hand upon her, and she shall live. And Yahusha (Jesus) arose, and followed him, and so did his disciples.* (Matthew 9:18-19)

Mark and Luke name the ruler Ya'ir — Hebrew *Ya'ir,* *he will enlighten / awaken.* He is a synagogue-ruler, a system-credentialed figure who recognizes the King — confirming again that the framework dismantles systems, not peoples: not all of the contemporary leadership is on the wrong side. Nakdimon (Nicodemus) at John 3 and Yoseph (Joseph) of Arimathea at Matthew 27:57-60 are the other leadership-figures who turned. The credentials do not predetermine the hearing.

*And, behold, a woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment: For she said within herself, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole.* (Matthew 9:20-21)

Under the Torah, a chronic discharge of blood renders a woman ritually impure as long as it continues:

*And if a woman have an issue of her blood many days out of the time of her separation... all the days of the issue of her uncleanness shall be as the days of her separation: she shall be unclean... And whosoever toucheth those things shall be unclean, and shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the even.* (Leviticus 15:25-27)

For twelve years she has been categorically isolated — unable to participate in temple-worship, treated as defiling-by-contact in every social interaction. Her approach is the same anti-defilement pattern the leper walked. The Pharisaic-purity-system would read her contact as DEFILING the rabbi and requiring his withdrawal-and-cleansing. The King's response is the same pattern Matthew 8:3 displayed: he is the cleansing-source, not the absorber-of-impurity; his presence reverses the defiling-flow rather than being defiled by it.

*But Yahusha (Jesus) turned him about, and when he saw her, he said, Daughter, be of good comfort; thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole from that hour.* (Matthew 9:22)

The vocative *Daughter* (the Greek *thugatēr*) mirrors the *Son* he addressed the paralytic with — the same kingdom-citizen-recognition, before any verbal confession. The woman categorically excluded for twelve years is named DAUGHTER at the moment of healing: reincorporation into the covenant-family at the cleansing. And *thy faith hath made thee whole* is the cause-and-effect the inclusion-gospel inverts. It is not the prosperity-gospel's *if your faith is strong enough, the healing is guaranteed,* nor the inclusion-gospel's *if you believe, you become his sheep.* She had heard the reports of the King's ministry; the hearing revealed what was already true; her *if I may but touch his garment* is the sheep's response to the Shepherd's voice. The faith is the open door through which the healing-flow reaches the sheep that already is a sheep; it is not the agent that manufactures the belonging. The King's own teaching elsewhere locks the order: *ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep* (John 10:26). Hearing reveals what was always true.

The number-symmetry is deliberate. The woman has bled twelve years; Yair's daughter is twelve years old. Twelve is the covenant-completeness number — the number of the tribes. One woman has been suffering as long as the other has been alive; both are made whole through contact with the King — a marker of the gathering's restorative substance: the King restores what twelve-tribe-symbolic-time has produced as suffering.

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) came into the ruler's house, and saw the minstrels and the people making a noise, He said unto them, Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn.* (Matthew 9:23-24)

The professional mourners — first-century funerals hired flute-players — and the family-grief have set in. The household is already in the funeral-rite. And the crowd laughs, because the contemporary community had been so saturated in Hellenistic-mortalism (the Sadducees explicitly rejecting bodily-resurrection at Matthew 22:23) that the King's *the maid is not dead, but sleepeth* sounded foreign. But death-as-sleep is not a gospel-innovation of consolation-language; the Hebrew library had carried the dead-in-the-covenant-are-sleeping framework for centuries:

*And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.* (Daniel 12:2)

*Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast them out.* (Isaiah 26:19)

The maid is dead in medical fact; the King names her as sleeping because the resurrection is imminent at his word. The architecture holds at full weight — the patriarchs are sleeping awaiting the first resurrection, the natural branches of the fathers whom Yahuah (God) is able to graft in again (Hebrews 11:39-40's *they without us should not be made perfect*); the maid is sleeping awaiting the immediate awakening at the King's hand. The principle is one: the dead-in-the-covenant are sleeping, not finally extinguished.

*But when the people were put forth, he went in, and took her by the hand, and the maid arose. And the fame hereof went abroad into all that land.* (Matthew 9:25-26)

The King takes the corpse's hand — contact that under Numbers 19:11's purity-rules would render him ritually impure for seven days. It is the same anti-defilement pattern as the leper and the bleeding woman, at its deepest register: the King's contact is the source of LIFE-FLOW, not the absorber of death-flow. He is not defiled by the corpse; the corpse becomes alive. The raising of Yair's daughter is the inaugural display in the gospel's narrative-arc — the widow of Nain at Luke 7 and Lazaros (Lazarus) at John 11 will continue it — each a present-age foretaste of the resurrection at the end of the age.

## The two blind men — Son of David, have mercy on us

*And when Yahusha (Jesus) departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, Thou Son of David, have mercy on us.* (Matthew 9:27)

The Messianic-credential extends into the *Son of David* recognition by the marginalized. The blind, the lame, the leprous, the demoniacs — those whom the system credentials cannot help — perceive what the credentialed-with-sight do not. *Son of David* is the direct Messianic name from the Davidic-covenant stream that founds at 2 Samuel 7:

*And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom... I will be his father, and he shall be my son.* (2 Samuel 7:12-14)

Isaiah carried it forward:

*And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Yishai (Jesse), and a Branch shall grow out of his roots: And the spirit of Yahuah (the LORD) shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of Yahuah (the LORD).* (Isaiah 11:1-2)

*Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of Yahuah Tseva'ot (LORD of hosts) will perform this.* (Isaiah 9:7)

Jeremiah carried it:

*Behold, the days come, saith Yahuah (the LORD), that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper... and this is his name whereby he shall be called, Yahuah Tsidkenu (THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS).* (Jeremiah 23:5-6)

And Ezekiel tied the Davidic-Messianic shepherd directly to the gathering and to Torah-walking:

*And David my servant shall be king over them; and they all shall have one shepherd: they shall also walk in my judgments, and observe my statutes, and do them... and my servant David shall be their prince for ever.* (Ezekiel 37:24-25)

The blind men confess the King as the Davidic-Messianic shepherd-and-Branch — direct prophetic recognition, the same hearing-pattern that has surfaced at every miraculous recognition through chapters 8-9: those the system marginalized hear the Shepherd's voice; those who hold the credentials cannot. And the *mercy* (the Greek *eleos,* the Hebrew *chesed*) they ask for is the very mercy-substance the King just cited Hosea 6:6 about — the blind men ask for the substance the Pharisees were told to learn.

*And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him: and Yahusha (Jesus) saith unto them, Believe ye that I am able to do this? They said unto him, Yea, Lord. Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you. And their eyes were opened.* (Matthew 9:28-29)

*According to your faith be it unto you* is the cause-and-effect again. The blind men heard, followed, confessed *Yea, Lord,* and received. They were sons of Adam, sons of the seed-of-promise, before the call; the call reveals them; the faith is the door, not the lineage. And the opening of their eyes is the Messianic-credential the prophet had named:

*Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped. Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.* (Isaiah 35:5-6)

The King will cite this very signature when Yochanan (John) the Immerser sends from prison to ask whether he is the one — *The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them* (Matthew 11:5). The healing of these two is one of the inaugurating credential-displays the King will point Yochanan back to.

*And Yahusha (Jesus) straitly charged them, saying, See that no man know it. But they, when they were departed, spread abroad his fame in all that country.* (Matthew 9:30-31)

The King restrains the broad circulation of the credentialing-miracles in the early ministry — the full disclosure of Messianic identity is being staged in stages, and the King controls the timing. The healed men do not keep the silence; the change is so visible that holding it secret is impossible. The staged-disclosure absorbs the spread.

## The mute demoniac and the prince-of-devils slander — the first beat of the blasphemy trajectory

*As they went out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed with a devil. And when the devil was cast out, the dumb spake: and the multitudes marvelled, saying, It was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel). But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils.* (Matthew 9:32-34)

The multitudes name the display at the historical level — *it was never so seen in Yashar'el (Israel)* — legitimate recognition that does not yet reach the Formed-from-the-Formless identification; they have the data but not the framework. The Pharisaic response is the credential-misattribution pattern. Confronted with a miracle they cannot deny, they attribute the source to the wrong agency. They know the demoniac is delivered; they cannot deny the visible fact; they reattribute the source to Beelzebul.

The name is preserved in the Tanakh as a Philistine title — *Ba'al Zevuv,* the lord of the flies — at 2 Kings 1:

*And Achazyahu (Ahaziah) fell down through a lattice... and he sent messengers, and said unto them, Go, enquire of Ba'al Zevuv (Baal-zebub) the god of Ekron whether I shall recover of this disease... But the angel of Yahuah (the LORD) said to Eliyahu (Elijah) the Tishbi... Is it not because there is not an Elohim (God) in Yashar'el (Israel), that ye go to enquire of Ba'al Zevuv (Baal-zebub) the god of Ekron?* (2 Kings 1:2-3)

The Pharisaic move recycles the Tanakh's pejorative name for the Philistine demon-deity and applies it to the prince of demons. And the attribution is theologically catastrophic: deliverance from demonic affliction is, by definition, demons being defeated. To attribute the deliverance-source to the prince-of-demons is to claim the prince-of-demons is fighting his own kingdom — the self-contradiction Matthew 12 will dismantle directly:

*And Yahusha (Jesus) knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand: And if Satan cast out Satan, he is divided against himself; how shall then his kingdom stand?* (Matthew 12:25-26)

The Pharisees are not making sense; they are reaching for any framework that lets them reject what they cannot deny. This is the first beat of a trajectory: Matthew 10:25 will name the Beelzebul-attribution against the King himself; Matthew 12:22-30 will dismantle the slander structurally; Matthew 12:31-32 will name the blasphemy-against-the-Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit) as the deliberate attribution of the Spirit's work to demonic agency, sustained against evidence, hardened against correction. The credential-system, confronted with the Spirit's public credentialing of the King, reaches for any framework — even self-contradictory ones — that lets it reject the credentialing.

And the pattern is not only historical. Institutional Christianity does the same with the heart-Torah-walking the Spirit is restoring today — the Sabbath-keeping, the feast-walking, the restored sacred names, the gathering of the scattered seed — relabeling it *legalism* or *Hebrew-Roots-deception* or *Judaizing* to maintain its categorical rejection of what the Spirit is doing in the present age. The Pharisaic-pattern is the architecture; the modern systems are different costumes on the same architecture. And the people inside those systems are victims of what they were handed; it is the architecture that stands under the diagnostic, not the deceived.

## The harvest is plenteous — the gathering-mission framing for Matthew 10

*And Yahusha (Jesus) went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.* (Matthew 9:35)

*The gospel of the kingdom* stands in continuity with Matthew 4:23 — the good news of the gathering of the scattered seed. The multitudes coming to him are the citizens of the gathering being drawn to the King.

*But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.* (Matthew 9:36)

*As sheep having no shepherd* is a direct Tanakh citation-stream — the prophetic naming of leadership-failure. It opens at Mosheh's (Moses's) succession-prayer:

*Let Yahuah (the LORD), the Elohim (God) of the spirits of all flesh, set a man over the congregation... that the congregation of Yahuah (the LORD) be not as sheep which have no shepherd.* (Numbers 27:16-17)

It recurs in the Ahab-leadership-failure Mikhayahu (Micaiah) saw:

*And he said, I saw all Yashar'el (Israel) scattered upon the hills, as sheep that have not a shepherd: and Yahuah (the LORD) said, These have no master.* (1 Kings 22:17)

Jeremiah carried it:

*My people hath been lost sheep: their shepherds have caused them to go astray, they have turned them away on the mountains: they have gone from mountain to hill, they have forgotten their restingplace.* (Jeremiah 50:6)

And Ezekiel 34 carries the prophetic-thunder at full weight — the indictment and the restoration-promise as one architecture:

*Son of Adam, prophesy against the shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel)... Woe be to the shepherds of Yashar'el (Israel) that do feed themselves! should not the shepherds feed the flocks?... My sheep wandered through all the mountains, and upon every high hill: yea, my flock was scattered upon all the face of the earth, and none did search or seek after them.* (Ezekiel 34:2-3, 6)

*For thus saith Adonai Yahuah (the Lord GOD); Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out... so will I seek out my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.* (Ezekiel 34:11-12)

*And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David; he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd.* (Ezekiel 34:23)

The King sees the multitudes through the Ezekiel-34 lens. The contemporary leadership-failure is the same failure Ezekiel indicted; the King himself is the Ezekiel-34 shepherd, *one shepherd, even my servant David,* come to seek the scattered. The *fainted, and were scattered abroad* — the Greek *eskulmenoi kai errimmenoi,* harassed and thrown-down — is the present condition the leadership-failure produced: sheep scattered, wounded, unprotected. The King's compassion (*esplangchnisthē,* moved in the gut) is the Ezekiel-34 shepherd's response to the flock's condition.

*Then saith he unto his disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few; Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.* (Matthew 9:37-38)

The harvest is the gathering-of-the-scattered-seed. The Tanakh-harvest stream stands behind it:

*Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down; for the press is full, the fats overflow; for their wickedness is great.* (Joel 3:13)

*And it shall come to pass in that day, that Yahuah (the LORD) shall beat off from the channel of the river unto the stream of Mitsrayim (Egypt), and ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Yashar'el (Israel).* (Isaiah 27:12)

The multitudes the King just saw as sheep-without-shepherd are the harvest he now names. The scattered seed are present in number; the workers to gather them are few. The *Lord of the harvest* the disciples are told to pray to is the Father — the formless infinite, the gathering-Lord per the prophets — with the Formed in flesh operating as the present-age shepherd executing the harvest under the Father's appointment. Pray for laborers — and then BE the laborers, as the very next chapter will enact: the harvest-naming and the labourer-prayer establish the substance of what the twelve are about to do. The Shepherd has come. The harvest is ready. The labourers go.
