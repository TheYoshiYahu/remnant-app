## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul writes to a divided, fleshly assembly of **called-out ones** — Israel and the grafted-in seed
gathered at Corinth (he calls the wilderness generation *our fathers,* 10:1, to a largely
nations-background assembly: they are grafted into the commonwealth of Israel, the scattered seed
gathered home, **not** a separate "church" that replaced Israel). The whole letter assumes:

- **Torah is not abolished — it is the air the letter breathes.** Paul keeps the feast and tells them
  to (5:7–8); he settles a discipline case by *the law* (Deuteronomy's *put away the evil,* 5:13);
  he reasons sexual ethics from Genesis (6:16); he grounds apostolic support in *the law of Moses*
  (9:8–9); he reads the wilderness narratives as binding *types... written for our admonition*
  (10:11); he appeals to *the law* on order (14:34); and he states the thesis flat: **circumcision is
  nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandments of Elohim** (7:19).
- **Grace and Torah are not opposed.** The cross is the power and wisdom of Elohim (1:24); the
  keeping of the commandments is the shape of the redeemed life (7:19). Never read a "law vs grace"
  antithesis into this letter. Where Paul speaks of being *under the law / without law* (9:20–21) it
  is **missionary accommodation** — he became as a Jew to win Jews, as without-law to win the
  nations — yet he is explicit he is **not without Torah toward Elohim, but under the Torah toward
  Messiah** (*ennomos Christou,* 9:21): Torah-bound to the Messiah, never Torah-free.
- **The "works of the law" / Galatians-Lie reading applies where justification language surfaces**
  (chiefly 1:29–31, 6:11, the glorying-in-flesh passages): the boast that is excluded is the
  **flesh-credential boast** — wisdom, status, party-loyalty (*I am of Paul... of Apollos*) — not
  Torah-keeping. *He that glorieth, let him glory in Yahuah (LORD)* (1:31, citing Jeremiah 9:24).
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No platonic/disembodied resurrection.** Chapter
  15 is a **bodily** resurrection — the last Adam undoing the first Adam's death (Genesis 2–3),
  death *swallowed up in victory* (Isaiah 25:8), at *the last trump* (15:52, the same trumpet of
  Isaiah 27:13 and Revelation 11:15).
- **Christology (the Formed):** Yahusha (Jesus) is the Formed drawn from the Formless — the God of
  the Tanakh interactions come in flesh. He is the *spiritual Rock that followed them* in the
  wilderness (10:4 / Exodus 17, Numbers 20) — the One who was already with Israel. *To us there is
  but one Elohim (God), the Father... and one Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ)* (8:6): the
  Father is the One; Yahusha is the Formed Son through whom are all things, bearing the divine name,
  **not** a co-equal-persons second person and **not** a mere man. Preserve the pull's distinction.
