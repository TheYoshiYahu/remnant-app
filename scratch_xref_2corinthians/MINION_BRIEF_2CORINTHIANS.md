## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul writes to the same divided, afflicted assembly of **called-out ones** — Israel and the grafted-in
seed gathered at Corinth — defending his apostleship and pleading for the collection for the saints at
Jerusalem. The whole letter assumes:

- **Torah is not abolished — it is the air the letter breathes.** The new covenant Paul ministers
  (3:6) is the covenant of Jeremiah 31 and Ezekiel 36 — the **same Torah** written now *not in tables
  of stone, but in fleshy tables of the heart* (3:3). He calls the assembly to *cleanse ourselves from
  all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness* (7:1); he commands separation in the
  words of the Torah and the prophets — *be ye separate... touch not the unclean thing* (6:17).
- **★ 2 Corinthians 3 is the most abuse-prone chapter in the letter — guard it.** *The letter killeth,
  but the spirit giveth life* (3:6) is **NOT** "the Torah is abolished / the written law is bad." The
  *letter that kills* is the externally-engraved code **without the new heart** — the covenant
  administration of Deuteronomy that brings the curse (Deuteronomy 27–28) upon a people who have the
  commandment graven on stone but a heart of stone (Ezekiel 36:26). The *spirit that gives life* is
  the **same Torah** written on the heart by the Spirit per Jeremiah 31:33 (*I will put my law in
  their inward parts, and write it in their hearts*) and Ezekiel 36:26–27 (*a new heart... and cause
  you to walk in my statutes*). The glory of Moses' face (Exodus 34:29–35) was real and from Yahuah;
  the *veil* is over the heart of the reader, not a defect in the Torah; *that which is done away*
  (3:11) is the **fading-glory ministration of condemnation under the old administration**, not the
  commandment of Yahuah. **Never render this chapter as Torah-against-Spirit or letter-against-grace.**
- **Grace and Torah are not opposed.** Where Paul boasts, he boasts in Yahuah (10:17, citing Jeremiah
  9:24) — never a flesh-credential. The new creature (5:17) walks in the renewed obedience the
  prophets promised.
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No platonic/disembodied hope.** The *house not
  made with hands, eternal in the heavens* (5:1) is the resurrection body, not a disembodied escape;
  *we groan... not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed
  up of life* (5:4) — the same bodily hope as Isaiah 25:8.
- **★ 2 Corinthians 6:14–18 is the gathering/separation.** *Come out from among them, and be ye
  separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you* (6:17) weaves
  Isaiah 52:11, Ezekiel 20:34, and the indwelling promise *I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and
  I will be their Elohim (God), and they shall be my people* (6:16, citing Leviticus 26:11–12 and
  Ezekiel 37:27), with the Davidic adoption *I will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and
  daughters* (6:18, citing 2 Samuel 7:14 / Isaiah 43:6). This is the regathered, separated covenant
  people — the temple of the living Elohim — NOT a new institution replacing Israel.
- **Christology (the Formed):** Yahusha (Jesus) is the Formed drawn from the Formless. *All the
  promises of Elohim (God) in him are yea, and in him Amen* (1:20). He *who knew no sin* was *made...
  sin for us, that we might be made the righteousness of Elohim (God) in him* (5:21 / Isaiah 53). The
  closing benediction names *the grace of the Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ), and the love of
  Elohim (God), and the communion of the Holy Spirit (Ruach HaKodesh)* (13:14) — the Father is the
  One; Yahusha is the Formed Son who bears the divine name; **not** a co-equal-persons grammar and
  **not** a mere man.
