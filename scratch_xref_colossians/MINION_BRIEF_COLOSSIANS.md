## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul writes from prison to the **called-out ones** at Colosse — Israel and the grafted-in seed of the
nations — against a syncretistic "philosophy" (2:8) of ascetic rule-keeping, angel-worship, and
calendar-policing imposed by *men*. The whole letter assumes:

- **★★ Colossians 1:15–20 is THE load-bearing passage — the hymn of the Formed Son. GUARD THE
  CHRISTOLOGY (this is the twin of Philippians 2:6).** *Who is the image of the invisible Elohim
  (God), the firstborn of every creature: For by him were all things created... all things were
  created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist. And he is
  the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead... For it
  pleased the Father that in him should all fulness dwell* (1:15–19). Read this through the framework:
  **Yahusha is THE FORMED — the Son drawn out of the invisible, Formless Father, the visible image of
  the One who cannot be seen, the firstborn (the pre-eminent heir, the *beginning*), through whom the
  Father made and upholds all things, in whom the Father was pleased to make *all fulness* dwell.**
  Note the grammar of *order*: he is *the image OF* the invisible Elohim (he images Another); *it
  pleased the FATHER* that the fulness should dwell in him (the Father is the source who gives); he is
  *firstborn* (brought forth, pre-eminent — Psalm 89:27 *I will make him my firstborn, higher than the
  kings of the earth*). This is **NOT a co-equal-persons proof-text** and **NOT** "Jesus is a created
  thing." *Firstborn of every creature* does not mean *first creature made* — it means the pre-eminent
  One **through whom** the creating was done (1:16, the very next verse), the Heir over all. Build the
  hymn on **Genesis 1:1 / 1:26–27** (the image; *let us make man in our image*), **Proverbs 8:22–30**
  (Wisdom brought forth before the works, the craftsman beside Him), **Psalm 89:27** (the firstborn),
  with **Wisdom of Solomon 7:25–26** (*the brightness of the everlasting light... the image of his
  goodness* — a Formed-witness, verify and frame as the Formed, not a co-equal person) and the NT
  twins (John 1:1–3, Hebrews 1:2–3 *the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person...
  by whom also he made the worlds*, Philippians 2:6, Revelation 3:14 *the beginning of the creation of
  Elohim*). **Never render this as trinitarian co-equality, modalist collapse, OR a created/Arian
  Christology.** The Formed Son: visible image of the invisible Father, firstborn-Heir, in whom the
  Father set all fulness to dwell.
- **★★ Colossians 2:14–17 is THE supersessionist-trap block — GUARD IT EXACTLY.** *Blotting out the
  handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way,
  nailing it to his cross* (2:14); *Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect
  of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: Which are a shadow of things to come; but
  the body is of Messiah (Christ)* (2:16–17). The trained reading says "the law / the Sabbath / the
  feasts were nailed to the cross and abolished; let no one make you keep them." **That is the
  antichrist reading. Render the framework reading:**
  - The *handwriting of ordinances* (the *cheirographon*, a hand-written **certificate of DEBT**) that
    *was against us* / *contrary to us* = **the record of debt, the written verdict of guilt the broken
    commandments held over us** — the indictment, NOT the Instruction itself. What is *blotted out* and
    *nailed to the cross* is the **debt-bond cancelled** (the same act as Ephesians 2:15's *enmity*,
    the dividing decree of separation, abolished — the wall that kept the nations out). Weave Isaiah
    43:25 / 44:22 (*I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions... I have blotted out, as a
    thick cloud, thy transgressions*) and Ezekiel 18:4 (the soul that sins — the debt). The Torah is
    not the enemy nailed up; **the certificate of our debt under it is.**
  - 2:16–17 *let no MAN judge you* in meat/drink/**holyday/new moon/sabbath** = let no ascetic-gnostic
    critic condemn your **KEEPING** of them. These are *a shadow of things to COME* (present/future
    tense — they still cast their forward shadow, the substance is Messiah's). The feasts, new moons,
    and Sabbaths are the appointed rehearsals **still pointing forward** to what is fulfilled in
    Messiah's body — **kept, not abolished.** Weave Leviticus 23 (the feasts of Yahuah), Isaiah 66:23
    (*from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship
    before me*, the age-to-come keeping them), Ezekiel 46:1–3 (new moon + sabbath in the restored
    temple), Hebrews 10:1 / 8:5 (the law *a shadow of good things to come*). **NEVER render Colossians
    2 as "the Sabbath / feasts / Torah abolished."** The thing removed is the debt-certificate; the
    feasts remain the shadow whose body is Messiah.
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** The *mystery... hid
  from ages... now made manifest* — *Messiah in you, the hope of glory* — *among the Gentiles* (1:26–27)
  = the grafted-in nations brought into the one commonwealth of Israel (the two-house regathering, the
  Ephesians-2 *one new man*), NOT a new institution replacing Israel.
- **Christology (the Formed):** see 1:15–20 + 2:9 (*in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead
  bodily*) above — the Father is the invisible One; Yahusha the Formed Son who is His visible image,
  in whom the Father was pleased to set all fulness.
