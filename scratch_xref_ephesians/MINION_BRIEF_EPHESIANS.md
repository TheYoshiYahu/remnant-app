## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul writes to the assembly of **called-out ones** at Ephesus — Israel and the grafted-in seed of the
nations gathered into ONE covenant people — unveiling *the mystery of his will... that... he might
gather together in one all things in Messiah* (1:9–10). The whole letter assumes:

- **The mystery is the RE-GATHERING of the one olive tree, not a new institution.** *He might gather
  together in one all things in Messiah (Christ)* (1:10); the nations once *aliens from the
  commonwealth of Yashar'el (Israel), and strangers from the covenants of promise* (2:12) are *made
  nigh by the blood of Messiah* (2:13) and brought INTO the commonwealth — grafted in, not a
  replacement of it. This is the two-house regathering (Ezekiel 37:15–28, the two sticks made one;
  Hosea 1:10–11, the children of the living Elohim gathered under one head).
- **★ Ephesians 2:11–22 is the load-bearing chapter — the middle wall, the two made one. GUARD IT.**
  *For he is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition
  between us; Having abolished in his flesh the enmity, even the law of commandments contained in
  ordinances; for to make in himself of twain one new man* (2:14–15). The *both* / *twain* made *one
  new man* is the two houses — **Judah and scattered Israel/the nations among whom Israel was
  sown** — reconciled into one body, **NOT** the Church replacing Israel and **NOT** Jew-and-Gentile
  as two religions merged. The *middle wall of partition* and *the law of commandments contained in
  ordinances* that is *abolished* is the **dividing barrier** — the enmity, the hedge of separation
  that fenced the nations OUT (the soreg, the ordinances that kept the ger at a distance) — **NOT** the
  moral Torah, **NOT** the Sabbath/feasts/the commandments written on the heart. The *enmity* slain is
  the hostility, not the Instruction. The result is the *commonwealth of Yashar'el (Israel)* opened, a
  *holy temple in Yahuah (Lord)* (2:21, the Leviticus 26 / Ezekiel 37:27 indwelling) — the gathered,
  not a substitute people. **Never render 2:15 as "the Torah is abolished."**
- **★ Ephesians 2:8–9 grace through faith — keep the COMPLETED SENTENCE.** *For by grace are ye saved
  through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of Elohim (God): Not of works, lest any man
  should boast* (2:8–9) does **NOT** stop there and does **NOT** mean "the Torah is abolished / works
  don't matter." The very next verse completes it: *For we are his workmanship, created in HaMashiach
  Yahusha (Christ Jesus) unto good works, which Elohim (God) hath before ordained that we should walk
  in them* (2:10). Grace is the **means of the dead being quickened and brought home** (2:1,5) — the
  return — and the *works* excluded from boasting are the **flesh-credential** (the *Circumcision in
  the flesh made by hands,* 2:11; the self-made claim that boasts), not the obedience the prophets
  promised would be written on the heart. Build the thread so 2:8–10 is ONE movement: saved by grace
  *unto* the good works ordained to walk in. Never sever grace from the renewed obedience.
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** The covenants of
  promise (2:12) are opened to the nations, not annulled; the household of Elohim is built *upon the
  foundation of the apostles and prophets* (2:20) — the SAME house.
- **★ Ephesians 5:22–33 marriage = Messiah and the assembly — the betrothed Yashar'el restored.** *For
  this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two
  shall be one flesh. This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Messiah (Christ) and the church*
  (5:31–32, citing Genesis 2:24). The bride is the gathered covenant people — the betrothed bride
  Yahuah married at Sinai and divorced/regathers (Hosea 2:19–20, Ezekiel 16:8–14, Isaiah 54:5, Isaiah
  62:5) — *sanctif[ied] and cleanse[d]... with the washing of water by the word* (5:26, the Ezekiel
  16:9 washing). **NOT** a new bride replacing Israel; the marriage figure is the covenant restored.
- **Torah affirmed, not abolished, in the household code.** *Honour thy father and mother; (which is
  the first commandment with promise;)* (6:2–3) quotes the fifth word of the Ten (Exodus 20:12 /
  Deuteronomy 5:16) as **standing law with its promise intact** — Paul grounds household obedience IN
  the commandment.
- **Christology (the Formed):** Yahusha (Jesus) is the Formed drawn from the Formless — *one Yahuah
  (Lord), one faith, one baptism, One Elohim (God) and Father of all* (4:5–6, the Shema, Deuteronomy
  6:4 / Zechariah 14:9); *the Elohim (God)... of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach* (1:17) — the Father is
  the One, Yahusha the Formed Son who bears the divine name; **not** a co-equal-persons grammar and
  **not** a mere man.
