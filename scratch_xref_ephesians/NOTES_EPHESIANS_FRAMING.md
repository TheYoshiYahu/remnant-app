## ★ CHAPTER 2 — the two made one / the middle wall / grace through faith (the load-bearing chapter)

- **`ephesians-2-the-middle-wall-broken-down-the-two-made-one-ezekiel-37`** (Tanakh + Extras + NT;
  Ezekiel 37:17,22 + Isaiah 57:19 + Hosea 1:10 + Baruch 4:37 + Romans 11:17 + Colossians 2:14).
  The *both* / *twain* made *one new man* (2:14–15) = the TWO HOUSES — Judah + scattered Ephraim sown
  among the nations — reconciled into one body per Ezekiel 37's two sticks made one nation, NOT the
  Church replacing Israel. The thing *abolished* (the *middle wall of partition,* *the law of
  commandments contained in ordinances,* the *enmity*) = the DIVIDING BARRIER / the ordinances of
  separation that fenced the houses apart and fenced the nations out (Colossians 2:14 the handwriting
  of ordinances / the certificate of debt nailed to the cross) — NOT the moral Torah, NOT the Sabbath,
  NOT the feasts, NOT the commandments written on the heart. Peace spoken to *far* and *near* (Isaiah
  57:19) = the scattered house and the house of Judah healed and joined; Hosea 1:10–11 the gathering
  under one head; Romans 11:17 the nations grafted INTO the same olive tree, never a replacement.

- **`ephesians-2-by-grace-through-faith-unto-the-good-works-ordained-ezekiel-37-36`** (Tanakh + Extras
  + NT; Ezekiel 37:5,14 + Ezekiel 36:26,27 + Wisdom of Solomon 11:23 + Colossians 2:13). 2:8–9 grace
  through faith read as the COMPLETED SENTENCE with 2:10: saved by grace UNTO the *good works which
  Elohim hath before ordained that we should walk in them.* Grace = the means of the dead being
  quickened (Ezekiel 37 dry bones raised/regathered) and brought home; the *works* excluded from
  boasting = the flesh-credential (the *Circumcision in the flesh made by hands,* 2:11), NOT the
  obedience the prophets promised. The ordained *good works* ARE the statutes the Spirit was promised
  to cause us to walk in (Ezekiel 36:27 *cause you to walk in my statutes* / 36:26 the heart of flesh)
  — the Torah written on the heart, not abolished and not replaced.

- ch2 third thread: `ephesians-2-the-household-of-elohim-the-holy-temple-and-the-corner-stone-leviticus-26`
  (Isaiah 28:16 + Psalm 118:22 + Leviticus 26:11 + Ezekiel 37:27) — the gathered as the holy temple /
  habitation (the Leviticus 26 / Ezekiel 37:27 indwelling), the commonwealth opened, not a substitute.

## ★ CHAPTER 5 — the bridegroom and the bride

- **`ephesians-5-the-bridegroom-and-the-bride-two-shall-be-one-flesh-genesis-2-hosea-2`** (Tanakh +
  Extras + NT; Genesis 2:24 + Ezekiel 16:8–9 + Hosea 2:19 + Isaiah 54:5 + Isaiah 62:5 + Tobit 8:6 +
  Matthew 19:5). 5:31–32 (Genesis 2:24, *they two shall be one flesh... I speak concerning Messiah and
  the church*): the bride = the gathered covenant people, the **covenant wife RESTORED** — washed
  (Ezekiel 16:9 the bridal washing = *the washing of water by the word,* 5:26), put away for harlotry
  and re-betrothed forever (Hosea 2:19), *thy Maker is thine husband* (Isaiah 54:5), rejoiced over as
  a bridegroom over the bride (Isaiah 62:5). NOT a new bride replacing Israel.

## ★ CHAPTER 6 — the Torah affirmed + the whole armour of Elohim

- **`ephesians-6-honour-thy-father-and-mother-the-first-commandment-with-promise-exodus-20`** (Tanakh +
  Extras; Exodus 20:12 + Deuteronomy 5:16 + Sirach 3:6–8). 6:2–3 quotes the fifth word of the Ten as
  *the first commandment with promise,* its promise of long days intact — the Torah AFFIRMED, the
  household code resting ON the commandment.
- **`ephesians-6-the-whole-armour-of-elohim-isaiah-59`** (Tanakh + Extras; Isaiah 59:17 + 11:5 + 11:4
  + 52:7 + 49:2 + Wisdom of Solomon 5:17,18,20). The whole armour is the armour of Yahuah HIMSELF
  (Isaiah 59:17 *he put on righteousness as a breastplate, and an helmet of salvation*) handed to the
  saint piece by piece; the one offensive weapon is the word (the Servant's sword-mouth Isaiah 49:2 /
  the Branch's rod Isaiah 11:4); Wisdom of Solomon 5:17–20 re-speaks the same Isaiah-59 armour of the
  LORD. The battle is His; the saint fights in His strength and His armour, not his own.
- ch6 third thread: `ephesians-6-we-wrestle-not-against-flesh-and-blood-the-war-with-the-spirit-powers-1-enoch-15`
  (Extras; 1 Enoch 15:8,10 — the giants become the evil spirits, the origin of the spirit-foes of 6:12).
