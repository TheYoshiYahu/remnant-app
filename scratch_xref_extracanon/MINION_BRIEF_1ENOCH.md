## THE FRAMEWORK (apply to every weave)
1. **Son of Adam, not son of man.** 1 Enoch 46–71 *names* the **Son of Adam / the Elect One** —
   this is the Messiah: the Formed who proceeds from the Head of Days (the Father), chosen and
   named before creation, who came in flesh as Yahusha (Jesus). The source text already says
   "Son of Adam." Bind it to Daniel 7, the Gospels, Revelation.
   - **KAPH DISCIPLINE (critical):** Daniel 7:13 / Revelation 1:13 / 14:14 use the *kaph-
     comparative* — *one **like** the Son of Adam.* 1 Enoch 46–48–62 *names* him (title, not
     comparative). When you cross-link Enoch's named Son of Adam to Daniel 7:13, say so plainly:
     Daniel sees *one like the Son of Adam* (the figure resembles mortal-man because he took on
     flesh, while remaining the Formed cloud-rider); Enoch names that same One. Do **not** flatten
     the two constructions into one, and do **not** drop the "like" when quoting Daniel 7:13.
   - The Head of Days = the Father (formless). The Son of Adam / Elect One = the Formed Son,
     ordered under the Father, worshipped with Him yet distinct from Him. Not co-equal-persons,
     not modalism, not a mere man, not a created angel.
2. **Election precedes confession.** 1 Enoch's books of the living, names named before creation
   (48:3), names blotted but never added (108:3) → the framework's election-before-hearing.
   The elect/righteous are a people, not a self-selected class.
3. **Two-house gathering / scattered seed.** Where Enoch sees the righteous gathered, the
   plant of righteousness, Israel as the sheep regathered (the Animal Apocalypse) — read it as
   the gathering of the twelve tribes (Ezekiel 37, Hosea 1, Isaiah 11), NEVER a church replacing
   Israel and NEVER nations-grafted-by-confession.
4. **Torah stands.** The judgement is for breaking the covenant; the righteous *keep* the way.
   Never frame law as curse. The Watchers' sin is rebellion against the Creator's order.
5. **Watchers / Genesis 6.** The fallen Watchers, their descent, the giants/nephilim, the
   teaching of forbidden arts, their binding in the abyss until judgement — this is the canon's
   own Genesis 6:1–4, carried forward by Jude and 2 Peter. The seed-war (tares sown among the
   wheat) runs from here.
6. **Victims, not enemies; dismantle systems, not people.** (Rarely in view for Enoch, but hold it.)
