# Minion brief — Adam & Eve (Life of Adam and Eve) cross-references ("It Ain't New" / Come-and-See)

You author the cross-reference apparatus for **Adam & Eve** (edition `pseudepigrapha`,
book `adam-eve`, **freshly repaired** text — 94 chapters / 312 verses, R.H. Charles 1913).
The book is a DUAL recension under one book_id:
- **Apocalypse of Moses = adam-eve chapters 1–43** (the Greek life; Eve's narrative of the Fall,
  Adam's sickness and death, the soul's ascent, the resurrection promise, the burial).
- **Vita Adae et Evae = adam-eve chapters 44–94** (the Latin life, local ch 1–51; the penance in
  the rivers, the devil's confession of his fall, Adam's rapture/vision, the messianic prophecy,
  the tables of stone and clay, the seventh-day rest).

The two recensions RETELL the same events. Thread BOTH, but anchor on each recension's distinctive
verses and DO NOT mechanically duplicate a weave you already built in the other half — vary the
canon you reach for, or thread only the richer of two parallel passages.

The apparatus weaves this book's verses (as the SOURCE/anchor) out to the Tanakh, the NT (canon),
and the already-live extra-canon books (`enoch`/`1-enoch`, `jubilees`, `jasher`) where there is
GENUINE textual/thematic kinship. Thesis: *it ain't new* — the Fall, the seed that bruises the
serpent's head, the resurrection of Adam's body, the oil of mercy, the devil cast down for refusing
to bow, the judgement by water then fire — are already in Moses and the Prophets, and the apostles
echo them. Adam & Eve is in the **bidirectional scripture-level pool** (a restored pseudepigraphon):
threads anchor on adam-eve, members run out to canon + the live extra-canon books.

### ★★ Christology (Red Line) — the Formed-Son Messiah, NOT a co-equal trinitarian person
**Chapter 85 (Vita 42) is the great messianic prophecy** and the Christological keystone of the
whole book: *"When five thousand five hundred years have been fulfilled, then will come upon earth
the most beloved king Messiah, the son of Elohim, to revive the body of Adam and with him to revive
the bodies of the dead. He Himself, the Son of Elohim, when He comes will be baptized in the river
of Jordan... then He will anoint from the oil of mercy all that believe in Him. And the oil of mercy
shall be for generation to generation for those who are ready to be born again of water and the
Ruach HaKodesh to life eternal."* Handle this as the GENUINE messianic hope — the Formed Son, sent
by the Father, the anointed deliverer who raises Adam's body. Weave to **Isa 61:1-3** (anointed to
preach... the oil of joy), **Dan 9:24-26** (Messiah the Prince, to anoint the most Holy), **Isa
53:5,11** (the suffering-then-vindicated servant), **Matt 3:16-17 / Mark 1:9-11** (baptized in
Jordan, the Father's voice — the oil of mercy = the Spirit descending), **John 3:5** (born again of
water and the Spirit — the SAME phrase), **John 5:28-29 / 1 Cor 15:21-22** (in Adam all die, in the
Messiah all made alive). The Son is the FATHER'S beloved king, distinct from and sent by the Father
— do NOT frame him as a co-equal/co-eternal trinitarian person. ⚠ The Apocrypha "Jesus"=Joshua trap
does NOT apply here: this book names Adam/Eve/Seth/Michael and the Messiah/Son of Elohim explicitly,
and the figure in ch85 IS genuinely the Messiah.

### ★★ THE PROTOEVANGELIUM (ch26) — the seed that bruises the serpent's head
ch26:1-4 is the curse on the serpent, climaxing in **26:4** *"and I will put enmity between thee and
his seed: he shall bruise thy head and thou shalt bruise his heel until the day of Judgement."*
Weave this to **Gen 3:14-15** (the original), **Rom 16:20** (the Elohim of peace shall bruise Satan
under your feet shortly), **Rev 12:9,17** (the dragon... and the remnant of her seed), **Luke 10:18-19**
(I beheld Satan as lightning fall... power to tread on serpents), **Heb 2:14** (destroy him that had
the power of death). This is THE keystone of the Apoc. Moses half — the first gospel, already in
Moses. (Self-link `jubilees` ch3 where Eden/the Fall is retold.)

### ★★ THE RESURRECTION OF ADAM'S BODY (ch41 / restoration ch39, and Vita ch85,91,94)
ch41:2 *"Again I promise to thee the Resurrection; I will raise thee up in the Resurrection with
every man who is of thy seed."* This is the book's beating heart. Weave to **Dan 12:2** (many that
sleep in the dust shall awake), **Ezek 37:12-14** (I will open your graves... and bring you into the
land — the two-house resurrection), **1 Cor 15:21-22,42-44** (in Adam all die... it is raised in
incorruption), **John 5:28-29**, **Job 19:25-27** (in my flesh shall I see Elohim), **Hos 13:14**.
ch39 (Adam restored to his former glory and set on the throne of his deceiver) → **Rev 3:21** (sit
with me in my throne), **Luke 10:18**, **Rom 5:17** (reign in life). ch94 the seventh-day rest = the
sign of the resurrection and the rest of the age to come → **Heb 4:9-10**, **Gen 2:2-3**.

### ★ THE DEVIL'S FALL & REFUSAL TO WORSHIP THE IMAGE (Vita ch55–60, esp. 57–59)
The devil confesses he was cast out for refusing to worship Adam, the image of Elohim:
ch58:3 *"If He be wrath with me, I will set my seat above the stars of heaven and will be like the
Highest."* — a near-verbatim parallel to **Isa 14:13-14** (I will exalt my throne above the stars of
Elohim... I will be like the most High). Weave ch55–59 to **Isa 14:12-15** (how art thou fallen,
O Lucifer), **Ezek 28:12-17** (thou wast perfect... till iniquity was found in thee), **Luke 10:18**
(I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven), **Rev 12:7-9** (Michael... the dragon cast out),
and self-link **1 Enoch 6** (the Watchers' rebellion) where genuine. ch56-57 (Adam made in the image
and likeness of Elohim, the breath of life) → **Gen 1:26-27**, **Gen 2:7**, **Col 1:15**. Keep the
envy/pride of the adversary as the root — do not invent doctrine the text doesn't carry.

### ★ THE OIL OF MERCY (ch9–13, 36, 40, 79–85)
Adam, dying, sends Seth and Eve to paradise to beg the oil of mercy from the tree; the angel answers
it cannot be had now but only in the last days (ch13), fulfilled in the Messiah (ch85). Weave the
oil-of-mercy quest to **Ps 23:5 / 45:7** (anointed with the oil of gladness), **Isa 61:1-3** (the oil
of joy for mourning), **John 3:5** and the Messiah's anointing — the mercy deferred to Adam is poured
out in the Formed Son. The "tree of mercy/tree of life" in paradise → **Gen 2:9 / 3:22-24**,
**Rev 2:7 / 22:2,14** (the tree of life in the midst of the paradise of Elohim).

### ★ CAIN & ABEL (ch1–4, Vita ch66–67) and SETH (ch4, ch67)
ch1 (Cain and Abel born), ch2-3 (Eve's dream of Abel's blood, Abel found murdered), Vita ch66 (Cain
slays Abel) → **Gen 4:1-10** (the voice of thy brother's blood crieth), **Heb 11:4**, **1 Jn 3:12**
(Cain... slew his brother), **Matt 23:35** (the blood of righteous Abel); self-link **jubilees ch4**
(Cain/Abel retold) and **jasher** where genuine. ch4 / ch67 Seth born "in place of Abel" →
**Gen 4:25**, **Gen 5:3** (Adam begat a son in his own likeness, Seth), **Luke 3:38** (Seth, Adam).
