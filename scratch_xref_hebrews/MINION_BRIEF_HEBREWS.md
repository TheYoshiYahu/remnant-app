### HEBREWS-SPECIFIC WATCHPOINTS (per chapter — these get scrutinized at the voice gate)

**The governing frame for the WHOLE book:** Hebrews argues the *superiority and finality of
Messiah's priesthood and once-for-all offering* — NOT the abolition of the Torah. Every "better,"
"new," "first/second," "old," "changed," "vanisheth away," "taketh away the first" refers to the
**Levitical priestly and sacrificial administration**, which is fulfilled and superseded in the
once-for-all offering of Yahusha after the order of Melek Tsadiq. The **covenant is renewed, not
replaced**: its defining clause (quoted twice, 8:10 and 10:16) is *I will put my laws into their
mind, and write them in their hearts* (Jeremiah 31:33) — **the SAME Torah, now internalized**, the
heart-circumcision the prophets foretold. Never let Hebrews read as Sabbath/feasts/clean-food/
moral-law abolished. The Sabbath in particular is *affirmed* (4:9, a rest *remaineth*). The whole
sanctuary system is read as the *copy and shadow of the heavenly pattern* (8:5; Exodus 25:40) —
real, God-given, and pointing to its fulfilment, not a mistake corrected.

- **Hebrews 1:** the Son = the Formed — *the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his
  person* (1:3). A catena of seven Tanakh quotations proving the Son above the angels: *Thou art
  my Son, this day have I begotten thee* (1:5 / Psalm 2:7 + 2 Sam 7:14); *let all the angels of
  Elohim worship him* (1:6 / Deut 32:43); *who maketh his angels spirits* (1:7 / Psalm 104:4);
  ***Thy throne, O Elohim, is for ever* (1:8–9 / Psalm 45:6–7)** — the enthroned anointed Son;
  *Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth* (1:10–12 / Psalm 102:25–27);
  *Sit on my right hand* (1:13 / Psalm 110:1). Build threads on the actual Psalms. Christology:
  the Formed enthroned, who has a Father — not a co-equal second person, not the Father.
- **Hebrews 2:** lower than the angels for a little while — *What is man, that thou art mindful of
  him? or the son of Adam, that thou visitest him?* (2:6–8 / Psalm 8:4–6); *I will declare thy
  name unto my brethren* (2:12 / Psalm 22:22); *I will put my trust in him… Behold I and the
  children* (2:13 / Isaiah 8:17–18). He took *flesh and blood* and *the seed of Abraham* (2:14–16)
  — the Formed truly incarnate; a *merciful and faithful high priest* (2:17). Quote the Psalms and
  Isaiah in full.
- **Hebrews 3:** Yahusha and Moses BOTH faithful — *Moses verily was faithful in all his house, as
  a servant* (3:5 / Numbers 12:7), Messiah *as a son over his own house* (3:6). **Do NOT read
  Moses as denigrated or the Torah as inferior** — Moses is praised as faithful; the contrast is
  servant-in-the-house vs son-over-the-house, not true-vs-false. The wilderness warning: *To day
  if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts* (3:7–11 / Psalm 95:7–11). Unbelief =
  faithlessness that forfeited the rest.
- **Hebrews 4:** **the Sabbath-rest watchpoint — Hebrews AFFIRMS the Sabbath, it does not abolish
  it.** *There remaineth therefore a rest (a keeping-of-sabbath) to the people of Elohim* (4:9);
  *For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as Elohim did
  from his* (4:10 / Genesis 2:2, *Elohim did rest the seventh day*). The weekly Sabbath is the
  sign and foretaste of the eternal rest, not a discarded shadow. *The word of Elohim is quick,
  and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword* (4:12). Our great high priest *touched with
  the feeling of our infirmities* (4:15).
- **Hebrews 5:** Messiah a high priest *called of Elohim, as was Aaron* (5:4–5 / Psalm 2:7), *after
  the order of Melchisedec* (5:6,10 / Psalm 110:4). *Though he were a Son, yet learned he obedience
