## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

John writes so the scattered elect may *know that ye have eternal life* (5:13) — and the whole letter is
a set of plain tests of that knowing. Light versus darkness, love versus hate, truth versus the lie, the
Spirit of truth versus the spirit of antichrist — and through every test runs the SAME proof: **do you
keep His commandments?** The Torah is not abolished; it is the very measuring-line of fellowship.

- **★★★ 1 John 2:3–4 — hereby we do know that we know him, IF we keep his commandments. THE MASTER FILTER —
  GUARD IT WITH FULL WEIGHT.** *And hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He
  that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him* (2:3–4).
  Read through the framework: this is **the framework's own test verbalised by the apostle** — Torah-keeping
  is the evidentiary proof of knowing Yahuah. The root is the Torah's own self-test: **Deuteronomy 11:1**
  (*thou shalt love Yahuah thy Elohim, and keep his charge, and his statutes, and his judgments, and his
  commandments, alway*); **Deuteronomy 11:22** / **Deuteronomy 30:16,20** (*to love Yahuah... to keep his
  commandments* = life); **John 14:15** (*If ye love me, keep my commandments*); **John 15:10** (*If ye
  keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love*). The man who claims to know Him and *keepeth not his
  commandments* is named *a liar* — there is no knowing-Him severed from obedience. **Do NOT spiritualise
  "his commandments" into a vague new-covenant feeling — they are the commandments John names "from the
  beginning" (2:7), the Torah. Build the master-filter thread on Deuteronomy 11 / 30, and surface its
  summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing, with the Torah-keeping-as-the-test guard explicit.**
- **★★ 1 John 2:7 — no new commandment, but an old commandment which ye had from the beginning. GUARD IT.**
  *Brethren, I write no new commandment unto you, but an old commandment which ye had from the beginning.
  The old commandment is the word which ye have heard from the beginning* (2:7; cf. 2:24, 3:11, 2 John 5–6).
  Read through the framework: John explicitly DENIES that he preaches a new law — the commandment is **old,
  from the beginning** (*Deuteronomy 6:5–6* the Shema *these words... shall be in thine heart*; *Leviticus
  19:18* love thy neighbour, the commandment from of old). The "newness" of 2:8 is only its fresh shining
  *because the darkness is past* — not a replacement of the Torah. **Do NOT read 2:7–8 as old-law-abolished /
  new-law-given; John says the opposite. Build the old-commandment-from-the-beginning thread.**
- **★★★ 1 John 3:4 — sin is the transgression of the law. THE ANTI-ANTINOMIAN ANCHOR — GUARD IT WITH FULL
  WEIGHT.** *Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law*
  (3:4 — *hē hamartia estin hē anomia*, sin IS lawlessness). Read through the framework: **this is the
  apostle's own definition of sin, and it is the Torah.** Where there is no law there is no transgression
  (Romans 4:15); sin is *anomia* — the absence/violation of the Torah. The root: **Psalm 119:142** (*thy
  law is the truth*); **Daniel 9:11** (*all Yashar'el have transgressed thy law*); **Romans 7:7** (*I had
  not known sin, but by the law*). If the law were abolished, sin itself would be undefinable — but John
  defines sin AS law-breaking. **This is the verse that makes the whole framework's "the Torah still
  defines righteousness" explicit in the New Testament. Build the sin-is-the-transgression-of-the-law
  thread on Psalm 119 / Daniel 9, and surface its summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing.**
- **★★ 1 John 5:2–3 — this is the love of Elohim, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are
  not grievous. GUARD IT.** *By this we know that we love the children of Elohim, when we love Elohim, and
  keep his commandments. For this is the love of Elohim (God), that we keep his commandments: and his
  commandments are not grievous* (5:2–3). Read through the framework: **love and commandment-keeping are
  the SAME thing** — John defines the love of Elohim AS keeping His commandments, exactly as the Torah does:
  **Deuteronomy 30:11** (*this commandment... is not hidden from thee, neither is it far off*), **Deuteronomy
  30:14** (*the word is very nigh unto thee... that thou mayest do it*) — *his commandments are not
  grievous* is Moses' own *it is not too hard for thee*. Also **Deuteronomy 10:12–13**, **Joshua 22:5**,
  **John 14:15**. **Do NOT split love from obedience — John welds them. Build the love-is-keeping-his-
  commandments thread on Deuteronomy 30, and surface its summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing.**
- **★ 1 John 3:11–15 — Cain who slew his brother = Genesis 4 (and the seed of the wicked one).** *For this

- **★ 1 John 1:1 + 1:5 — that which was from the beginning / the Word of life / Elohim is light. The Formed
  and the Genesis-1 light.** *That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen

- **★ 1 John 4:1–3 — try the spirits / every spirit that confesseth NOT that Yahusha is come in the flesh
