## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul charges his true son Timothy to keep the assembly at Ephesus in **sound doctrine** against those who
*teach otherwise* (1:3). The whole letter assumes:

- **★ 1 Timothy 1:8–11 — the law is GOOD if used lawfully. GUARD IT.** *we know that the law is good, if
  a man use it lawfully; Knowing this, that the law is not made for a righteous man, but for the lawless
  and disobedient... for murderers... for whoremongers... for menstealers, for liars, for perjured
  persons* (1:8–10). Read through the framework: Paul is **affirming the Torah, not abolishing it** — the
  law is *good* (cf. Psalm 19:7 *the law of Yahuah is perfect*, Romans 7:12 *the law is holy, and the
  commandment holy, and just, and good*). The vice-list in 1:9–10 is **the Ten Words walked through** —
  murderers (sixth), whoremongers/abusers (seventh), menstealers (eighth — Exodus 21:16), liars/perjurers
  (ninth) — the law convicts the lawless. To "use the law lawfully" is to use it as the Torah uses
  itself: as the standard that exposes sin, not as a ladder of self-justification. **Do NOT render this
  as "the law is only for the unsaved / the believer is free from the law."**
- **★★ 1 Timothy 2:5 — ONE Elohim, ONE Mediator. GUARD IT (Christology — the Formed).** *For there is
  one Elohim (God), and one mediator between Elohim (God) and men, the man HaMashiach Yahusha (Christ
  Jesus)* (2:5). Read through the framework: this is the Shema's *one Elohim* (Deuteronomy 6:4) — the
  Father is the ONE Elohim — and the **one Mediator is the MAN, the Formed**, who stands between (the
  mediator role of Moses, Deuteronomy 5:5; the daysman longed for in Job 9:33; Galatians 3:19–20). The
  verse itself names him *the MAN HaMashiach Yahusha* — keep the Father the one Elohim and the Son the
  Formed Mediator. **Do NOT collapse the one Elohim and the one mediator into co-equal persons; the verse
  distinguishes them.**
- **★★ 1 Timothy 3:16 — Elohim manifest in the flesh = the Formed. GUARD IT.** *great is the mystery of
  godliness: Elohim (God) was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached
  unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory* (3:16). Read through the
  framework: this is **the Formed Son made visible** — the same confession as Colossians 1:15 (*the image
  of the invisible Elohim*) and Philippians 2 (emptied and exalted). The invisible Father is *manifest*
  in the Son who bears his Name and glory (cf. the Elect One revealed, the Son of Adam given dominion,
  Daniel 7:13–14). *Received up into glory* = the exaltation by the Father. Keep the Father the source
  and the Son the Formed in whom the Father is made manifest. **Do NOT render this as proof of a
  co-equal-persons trinity; it is the mystery of the invisible Elohim made manifest in his Formed Son.**
  (Twin of the Col 1 / Phil 2 hymns — guard like the firstborn / kenosis passages.)
- **★★ 1 Timothy 4:1–5 — every creature good / nothing to be refused. GUARD IT — this is NOT the
  abolition of clean and unclean.** *in the latter times some shall depart from the faith... Forbidding
  to marry, and commanding to abstain from meats, which Elohim (God) hath created to be received with
  thanksgiving... For every creature of Elohim (God) is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be
  received with thanksgiving: For it is sanctified by the word of Elohim (God) and prayer* (4:1–5). Read
  through the framework with care: what Paul rebukes is **the doctrine of devils — the GNOSTIC/ascetic
  ban that FORBIDS marriage and COMMANDS abstinence from foods** as if matter were evil. His answer is
  that *every creature of Elohim is good* (Genesis 1:31 *Elohim saw every thing that he had made, and,
  behold, it was very good*) and food *received with thanksgiving* is *sanctified by the word of Elohim
  and prayer*. The phrase *the word of Elohim* is itself the key: the food that is *sanctified by the
  word of Elohim* is the food the **word of Elohim already names as food** (the creatures Leviticus 11 /
  Deuteronomy 14 give for meat) — received now with thanksgiving rather than with the ascetic's
  superstitious ban. This is **NOT** a repeal of the clean/unclean distinction; it is a rebuke of the
  heresy that calls Elohim's good provision evil. **Do NOT render 4:4 as "all foods including the unclean
  are now permitted / Leviticus 11 is abolished."** Frame the thread on Genesis 1:31 (creation good) and
  the thanksgiving over food, with the guard that *them which believe and know the truth* (4:3) are those
  who receive Elohim's appointed provision with thanks.
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** The sound doctrine, the
  law used lawfully, the one Mediator, and the good fight belong to the one olive tree, the household of
  Elohim (3:15).
- **Christology (the Formed):** *the man HaMashiach Yahusha* (2:5), *Elohim manifest in the flesh* (3:16),
  *the King eternal, immortal, invisible, the only wise Elohim* (1:17), *the blessed and only Potentate...
  who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto* (6:15–16). Keep the
  Father the one invisible Elohim and source; the Son the Formed Mediator who is manifest and received up.
  No co-equal-persons grammar, no modalist collapse.
