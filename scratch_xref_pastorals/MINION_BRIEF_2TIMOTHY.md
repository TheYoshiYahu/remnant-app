## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul, ready to be offered (4:6), charges his beloved son Timothy to guard the deposit and endure. The
whole letter assumes:

- **★★ 2 Timothy 3:14–17 — the HOLY SCRIPTURES known from a child = the TANAKH, given by inspiration.
  GUARD IT.** *from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto
  salvation* (3:15); *All scripture is given by inspiration of Elohim (God), and is profitable for
  doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness* (3:16). Read through the
  framework: the *holy scriptures* Timothy knew *from a child* are **the Tanakh — the Law, the Prophets,
  and the Writings — the only scripture that then existed.** Paul declares the Tanakh *given by
  inspiration of Elohim* and *profitable... for instruction in righteousness* — it is **affirmed as the
  living, profitable word, NOT superseded.** The Tanakh's own claim: *the law of Yahuah is perfect*
  (Psalm 19:7); *every word of Elohim is pure* (Proverbs 30:5); *for ever, O Yahuah, thy word is settled
  in heaven* (Psalm 119:89). **Do NOT render 3:16 as "this proves the NT replaced/completes the OT" — the
  scripture in view IS the Tanakh, and Paul calls it inspired and profitable, the standard for the man of
  Elohim.**
- **★ 2 Timothy 1:9–10 — saved and called according to grace given before the world began. GUARD IT.**
  *Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to
  his own purpose and grace, which was given us in HaMashiach Yahusha (Christ Jesus) before the world
  began* (1:9); *who hath abolished death, and hath brought life and immortality to light* (1:10). Read
  through the framework: *not according to our works* excludes **boasting in flesh/merit, NOT the
  Torah** — election and calling rest on Elohim's own purpose and covenant love (Deuteronomy 7:7–8 *not
  because ye were more in number... but because Yahuah loved you*). *Abolished death* = the conquest of
  the grave (Isaiah 25:8 *he will swallow up death in victory*, Hosea 13:14), the resurrection hope —
  **NOT the abolition of the law.** Do NOT read this as a grace-vs-Torah antithesis.
- **★★ 2 Timothy 2:8 / 2:19 — the risen Formed Son and the seal of the foundation.** *Remember that
  Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) of the seed of David was raised from the dead* (2:8 — the Father
  raised the Son; the Formed, the seed of David per 2 Samuel 7:12, Isaiah 11:1); *the foundation of
  Elohim (God) standeth sure, having this seal, Yahuah (Lord) knoweth them that are his. And, Let every
  one that nameth the name of HaMashiach (Christ) depart from iniquity* (2:19 — Numbers 16:5 *Yahuah will
  shew who are his*, Isaiah 52:11 *depart... be ye clean*). Keep the Father the One who raises; the Son
  the Formed, raised, of the seed of David.
- **2 Timothy 3:8 — Jannes and Jambres. EXTRAS-relevant.** *Now as Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses,
  so do these also resist the truth* (3:8). These are the magicians of Pharaoh named in the Hebrew
  library; **Jasher names them** (verify with dump_canon and weave if the witness is clean — the
  resisters of Moses as the type of the last-days deceivers; cf. Exodus 7:11 the magicians who withstood
  Moses with their enchantments).

  words, the holy scriptures, and the crown of righteousness belong to the one olive tree.
- **Christology (the Formed):** *raised from the dead* (2:8), *the Lord, the righteous judge, shall give
  me at that day* (4:8): the Father raises and judges through the Formed Son. No co-equal-persons grammar.
