## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

James writes *to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad* (1:1) — **the dispersed two-house remnant
of Yashar'el**, not a generic "church." The whole letter assumes the Torah is in force and being kept:

- **★ James 1:1 — the twelve tribes scattered abroad = the two-house dispersion.** *James, a servant of
  Elohim (God) and of the Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Lord Jesus Christ), to the twelve tribes which are
  scattered abroad* (1:1). Read through the framework: this is the **dispersed of Yashar'el** — the
  scattered seed Yahuah promised to gather (Deuteronomy 30:1–4 *if any of thine be driven out... Yahuah
  will gather thee from all the nations*; Ezekiel 37:21–22 the two sticks made one nation; Isaiah 11:11–12
  *gather the dispersed of Yahudah... the outcasts of Yashar'el*; the twelve tribes blessed, Genesis 49;
  cf. 1 Peter 1:1 *to the strangers scattered* the same address). The "twelve tribes" are literal — the
  covenant people in dispersion, being re-gathered to one olive tree. Build the scattered-twelve-tribes /
  two-house thread.
- **★★ James 1:22–25 — be DOERS of the word, not hearers only / the perfect law of liberty. GUARD IT —
  this is the Torah AFFIRMED, not abolished.** *But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only,
  deceiving your own selves... But whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty, and continueth therein,
  he being not a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the work, this man shall be blessed in his deed*
  (1:22,25). Read through the framework: *the perfect law of liberty* is **the Torah of Yahuah** —
  *the law of Yahuah is perfect, converting the soul* (Psalm 19:7); the liberty is the freedom of the one
  who keeps it — *I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts* (Psalm 119:45). Doing, not merely
  hearing, is the Sinai demand itself — *all that Yahuah hath said will we do, and be obedient* (Exodus
  24:7); *be ye doers... not hearers only* re-speaks Deuteronomy 5:1, *learn them, and keep, and do them*,
  and rebukes Ezekiel 33:31–32 (*they hear thy words, but they do them not*); cf. Matthew 7:24–27 the doer
  on the rock, Romans 2:13 *the doers of the law shall be justified*. **Do NOT render "the perfect law of
  liberty" as a NEW law that replaced the Torah, nor as "freedom FROM the law."** It is the Torah kept
  from a willing heart. Build the doers-not-hearers / perfect-law-of-liberty thread and surface its
  summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing.
- **★★ James 2:8–12 — the royal law / the whole law / guilty of all. GUARD IT — the Torah is one and
  binding.** *If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as
  thyself, ye do well* (2:8); *For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is
  guilty of all. For he that said, Do not commit adultery, said also, Do not kill* (2:10–11); *So speak
  ye, and so do, as they that shall be judged by the law of liberty* (2:12). Read through the framework:
  *the royal law* is **Leviticus 19:18** (*thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself*) — quoted *according
  to the scripture*; the *whole law* is the Torah as an indivisible whole (*cursed be he that confirmeth
  not all the words of this law to do them*, Deuteronomy 27:26); the two commands James cites are the
  **Decalogue verbatim** — *Thou shalt not kill... Thou shalt not commit adultery* (Exodus 20:13–14 /
  Deuteronomy 5:17–18). James holds believers to the WHOLE Torah and says they *shall be judged* by it.
  **This is the opposite of "the law is abolished."** Build the royal-law / whole-law thread on
  Leviticus 19:18 + the Decalogue and surface its summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing.
- **★★★ James 2:14–26 — faith without works is dead / justified by works (Abraham offering Yitzhaq,
  Rahab). THE KEYSTONE — GUARD IT WITH FULL WEIGHT.** *What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say
  he hath faith, and have not works? can faith save him?... Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead,
  being alone... Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he had offered Isaac his son upon the
  altar? Seest thou how faith wrought with his works, and by works was faith made perfect? And the
  scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed Elohim (God), and it was imputed unto him for
  righteousness... Ye see then how that by works a man is justified, and not by faith only. Likewise also
  was not Rahab the harlot justified by works... For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith
  without works is dead also* (2:14–26). **DO NOT read this in Reformation grammar — it is NOT "the faith
  that justifies is never alone," NOR a works-vs-grace antithesis to be defused.** Read it the way James
  means it, through the framework: **living faith is Torah-obedient faith; works are the evidence and the
  COMPLETION of faith, not its rival.** James welds the two Abraham texts that the framework holds
  together as ONE pattern — **Genesis 15:6** (*Abraham believed Yahuah; and he counted it to him for
  righteousness* — the belief) and **Genesis 22:9–12,16–18** (*offered Isaac his son upon the altar* —
  the obedience that *made faith perfect*) — and reads them through the keystone **Genesis 26:5**
  (*Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws*). The
  Second-Temple library already read it exactly this way: **1 Maccabees 2:52** (*Was not Abraham found
  faithful in temptation, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness?* — Genesis 15:6 and the Aqedah
  fused, the very fusion James makes) and **Sirach 44:20** (*Abraham... kept the law of the most High...
  when he was proved, he was found faithful*). **Rahab** (*justified by works*, 2:25) acted her faith —
  *Yahuah your Elohim, he is Elohim in heaven above* (Joshua 2:11) made good by hiding the spies (Joshua
  6:25). The conclusion *faith without works is dead* is the framework's own: an obedience-less "faith"
  is no living faith at all. **Build the faith-perfected-by-works / Abraham-and-Rahab thread on Genesis
  15:6 + Genesis 22 + Genesis 26:5 (+ 1 Maccabees 2:52 + Sirach 44:20) and surface its summary_md
  VERBATIM in your return for Yoshi's blessing, with the living-Torah-faith framing explicit — NOT a
  grace-vs-works debate.**
- **★ James 4:5–6 — Elohim resisteth the proud, giveth grace unto the humble (Proverbs 3:34 quoted).**
  *But he giveth more grace. Wherefore he saith, Elohim (God) resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto
  the humble* (4:6). This is **Proverbs 3:34** quoted *according to the scripture* (*he scorneth the
  scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly*; the same verse 1 Peter 5:5 quotes). Grace flows to the
  humble who submit and draw nigh (4:7–10 / Zechariah 1:3, Isaiah 57:15). Build it.
- **★ James 5:12 — swear not / let your yea be yea = the Name honored, the third word kept.** *swear not,
  neither by heaven, neither by the earth... but let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay* (5:12). Read
  through the framework: this guards **Leviticus 19:12** (*ye shall not swear by my name falsely*) /
  Exodus 20:7 / Deuteronomy 23:21–23 / Ecclesiastes 5:4–6 — reverence for the Name and for the vow, NOT
  an abolition of the lawful oath. (cf. Sirach 23:9–11 *accustom not thy mouth to swearing*.)
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** The doing of the word,
  the royal law, the whole law, faith perfected by obedient works — all belong to the one olive tree, the
  twelve tribes being gathered.
- **Christology (the Formed):** *the faith of our Lord Yahusha HaMashiach (Lord Jesus Christ), Yahuah
  (Lord) of glory* (2:1) and *the coming of Yahuah (Lord)* (5:7–8) — read with the framework as the glory
  and the coming of the Formed Son who bears the Father's Name and glory. *Every good gift... cometh down
  from the Father of lights* (1:17) keeps the Father the source. *There is one lawgiver* (4:12) and *one
  Elohim* (2:19) — Deuteronomy 6:4. Keep the Father the source; the Son the one through whom he saves and
  who comes. No co-equal-persons grammar, no modalist collapse.
