## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul left Titus in Crete to *set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city*
(1:5). The whole letter assumes:

- **★ Titus 1:2 — eternal life promised before the world began (the Father's purpose).** *In hope of
  eternal life, which Elohim (God), that cannot lie, promised before the world began* (1:2). Read through
  the framework: this is the **Father's eternal purpose** — the twin of 2 Timothy 1:9 (*grace... given us
  in HaMashiach Yahusha before the world began*) — the covenant of life set in the Father's counsel from
  of old (cf. the everlasting covenant, the sure mercies). *Elohim that cannot lie* echoes Numbers 23:19
  (*Elohim is not a man, that he should lie*). Keep the Father the source of the promise.
- **⚠ Titus 1:10–16 — the unruly talkers of the circumcision / "unto the pure all things are pure" /
  "nothing pure." GUARD IT — this is NOT anti-Torah.** *there are many unruly and vain talkers and
  deceivers, specially they of the circumcision... teaching things which they ought not, for filthy
  lucre's sake* (1:10–11); *Not giving heed to Jewish fables, and commandments of men, that turn from
  the truth* (1:14); *Unto the pure all things are pure: but unto them that are defiled and unbelieving
  is nothing pure; but even their mind and conscience is defiled* (1:15). Read through the framework with
  care: what Paul rebukes is **corrupt MEN — the deceivers who teach for money, the Jewish FABLES and
  the COMMANDMENTS OF MEN** (1:14) — **NOT the Torah of Yahuah**. *Jewish fables and commandments of men*
  are the traditions of men that make void the word (the very thing Yahusha rebuked, Mark 7:7 *teaching
  for doctrines the commandments of men*, Isaiah 29:13). And *unto the pure all things are pure* is about
  **the defiled conscience**, not the clean/unclean law: to the defiled and unbelieving *nothing* is pure
  *because their mind and conscience is defiled* — purity is a matter of the heart standing right, not a
  declaration that the distinctions of Leviticus 11 are void. **Do NOT render 1:15 as "all foods are now
  clean / the dietary law is abolished."** Frame the rebuke on the deceivers and the commandments of men,
  and 1:15 on the defiled conscience.
- **★★ Titus 2:11–14 — the grace of Elohim that TEACHES US TO DENY UNGODLINESS / a peculiar people,
  zealous of good works. GUARD IT — the great anti-antinomian keystone.** *For the grace of Elohim (God)
  that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly
  lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world... who gave himself for
  us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of
  good works* (2:11–14). Read through the framework: **grace does not abolish obedience — grace TEACHES
  it.** The very grace that appeared is the *schoolmaster* that trains us *to deny ungodliness* and live
  *righteously* (i.e. according to the righteousness the Torah defines). The redeemed are *a peculiar
  people* — the exact phrase of **Exodus 19:5** (*ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all
  people*) and **Deuteronomy 14:2** (*Yahuah hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself*) —
  the covenant people *zealous of good works*, *redeemed from all iniquity* (cf. Ezekiel 37:23 *neither
  shall they defile themselves... but I will... cleanse them*; Psalm 130:8 *he shall redeem Yashar'el
  from all his iniquities*). This is **grace UNTO Torah-keeping, NOT grace INSTEAD OF the Torah** — the
  great answer to antinomianism. **Build the grace-that-teaches / peculiar-people thread on Exodus 19:5 +
  Deuteronomy 14:2 and surface its summary_md VERBATIM for Yoshi's blessing, with the grace-unto-
  obedience guard explicit.**
- **★ Titus 3:5 — not by works of righteousness = flesh-merit excluded, NOT the Torah abolished. GUARD
  IT.** *Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the
  washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Ruach HaKodesh (Holy Spirit)* (3:5). Read through the
  framework: *not by works of righteousness which WE have done* excludes **flesh-merit, self-earned
  standing, boasting** — the same guard as 2 Timothy 1:9 (*not according to our works*) and Ephesians
  2:8–10 — **NOT** the Torah of Yahuah. Salvation is by his mercy (Deuteronomy 7:7–8 election by love,
  not by their righteousness; Deuteronomy 9:5 *not for thy righteousness*); the *washing of regeneration*
  and the *renewing of the Ruach* is the new heart and clean water of Ezekiel 36:25–27 (*I will sprinkle
  clean water upon you... a new heart also will I give you... and cause you to walk in my statutes*) —
  which leads STRAIGHT into walking in his statutes, not away from them. **Do NOT render 3:5 as "works of
  the law cannot save, therefore the law is abolished."** The new birth produces the obedient life
  (3:8 *that they... might be careful to maintain good works*).
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** The sound doctrine,
  the grace that teaches obedience, the peculiar people, and the washing of regeneration belong to the
  one olive tree.
- **Christology (the Formed):** *the great Elohim and our Saviour Yahusha HaMashiach* (2:13) — read with
  the rest of the framework as the glory of the appearing of the Formed Son who bears the Father's Name
  and glory; *Elohim our Saviour* (1:3; 3:4) is the Father, and the salvation comes *through Yahusha
  HaMashiach our Saviour* (3:6). Keep the Father the source and the Son the one through whom he saves.
  No co-equal-persons grammar, no modalist collapse.
