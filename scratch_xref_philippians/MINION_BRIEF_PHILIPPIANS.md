## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul writes from prison to the **called-out ones** at Philippi — Israel and the grafted-in seed of the
nations — partners with him *in the gospel from the first day* (1:5). The whole letter assumes:

- **★ Philippians 2:5–11 is THE load-bearing passage — the kenosis hymn. GUARD THE CHRISTOLOGY.**
  *Let this mind be in you, which was also in HaMashiach Yahusha (Christ Jesus): Who, being in the form
  of Elohim (God), thought it not robbery to be equal with Elohim (God): But made himself of no
  reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant... he humbled himself, and became obedient unto
  death... Wherefore Elohim (God) also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above
  every name: That at the name of Yahusha (Jesus) every knee should bow... and that every tongue should
  confess that Yahusha HaMashiach (Jesus Christ) is Lord, to the glory of Elohim (God) the Father*
  (2:5–11). Read this through the framework: **Yahusha is THE FORMED — the Son drawn out of the
  Formless Father, who bears the Father's own name.** The hymn's whole movement is **SUBMISSION**: the
  Formed *emptied himself* (made himself of no reputation), *humbled himself*, became *obedient*, and
  then the **Father** *highly exalted him* and *gave* him the name — and the bowing of every knee is
  *to the glory of Elohim (God) the FATHER* (2:11). This is **NOT a co-equal-persons proof-text** and
  **NOT** "Jesus claiming to be God the Father." The *form of Elohim* he was in is the divine glory the
  Formed shares as the One brought forth (cf. John 17:5); *equal with Elohim* is the thing he *did not
  grasp/clutch at* — he laid it down. The name *above every name* is the Father's own Name placed upon
  the Son (the Name YHWH he carries). **Build the hymn-thread on Isaiah 45:23** (*unto me every knee
  shall bow, every tongue shall swear* — YHWH speaking; the knee bows to the Name the Formed bears),
  with Isaiah 53 (the obedient suffering Servant) and the Father-exalts-the-Son frame. Never render
  this as trinitarian co-equality, modalist collapse, or a created/mere-man Christology. The Formed
  Son, ordered under the Father, exalted BY the Father.
- **★ Philippians 3:2–9 the flesh-credential / "the righteousness which is of the law" — GUARD IT.**
  *Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers, beware of the concision* (3:2 — the mutilation party who
  preach circumcision-as-a-credential, the same opponents as Galatians); *we... worship Elohim (God)
  in the spirit... and have no confidence in the flesh* (3:3). Then Paul's own flesh-catalogue:
  *Circumcised the eighth day, of the stock of Yashar'el (Israel)... a Hebrew of the Hebrews; as
  touching the law, a Pharisee... touching the righteousness which is in the law, blameless* (3:5–6).
  *But what things were gain to me, those I counted loss for HaMashiach (Christ)* (3:7); *not having
  mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of HaMashiach
  (Christ), the righteousness which is of Elohim (God) by faith* (3:9). **What Paul counts loss is the
  FLESH-CREDENTIAL — pedigree and Torah-keeping wielded as a self-made claim that boasts before
  Elohim — NOT Torah-obedience itself, and NOT the Torah abolished.** He says he WAS *blameless*
  touching the law (3:6); he does not call the law refuse, he calls his *own righteousness* — legal
  standing achieved as a credential — refuse, set against *the righteousness which is of Elohim by
  faith* (the gift-righteousness of Genesis 15:6, Habakkuk 2:4, Jeremiah 23:6, *Yahuah our
  righteousness*). This is the SIBLING of Ephesians 2:8–9 (the boast excluded) and Galatians (works of
  the flesh as credential). **Never render 3:9 as "the Torah is dung / abolished."** The excluded thing
  is the self-righteous boast; the embraced thing is the faith-righteousness the prophets promised.
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** *Our conversation
  (citizenship) is in heaven* (3:20) = the Jerusalem above, the gathered commonwealth (cf. the two-house
  regathering), not a new institution replacing Israel.
- **Christology (the Formed):** see 2:5–11 above — the Father is the One; Yahusha the Formed Son who
  bears the divine name, exalted BY the Father, to the Father's glory.
