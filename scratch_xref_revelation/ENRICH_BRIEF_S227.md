# S227 — Revelation Roots Enrichment Brief (Yoshi's interpretive KEY)

## The mission
Revelation invents almost no new vocabulary. It RECYCLES the Hebrew prophets' imagery,
and the Second-Temple apocalyptic corpus supplies the rest. **The Prophets are the decoder
ring; the extra-canonical books are the rest of the dictionary.** Your job: for the
Revelation threads in your assigned chapters, ADD the missing **prophetic** and
**extra-canonical** cross-reference *members* so every symbol's roots are present and
navigable — not just named in prose, but linked as real cross-references.

**You ADD only. Never rewrite or delete existing threads, summaries, or members.** You are
appending new `cross_references` + new `cross_reference_thread_members` rows to existing
live threads (by slug).

## The standard for each thread
canon/NT weave (already present) **+ its prophetic source(s) + its extra-canonical
parallel(s)** — exhaustively, but ONLY where a *real* parallel exists. Do NOT force a
parallel that isn't genuinely there. A thread whose symbol simply has no extra-canonical
echo (e.g. a bare Sinai/Exodus covenant promise) may stay without one — but most of
Revelation's symbols DO have prophetic and apocalyptic roots, so look hard.

Two failure modes to avoid equally: (1) leaving a symbol's prophetic/apocalyptic root
uncited when it plainly exists; (2) bolting on a weak/forced parallel to hit a quota.

## The framework lens (do not drift mainstream)
- Revelation is **cyclical, not linear**; one judgment of the souls of the dead.
- **Two-house**: 144,000 / tribes / two witnesses = Judah + Ephraim/Joseph regathered
  (Ezekiel 37 two sticks), Israel made whole, NOT a gentile church replacing Israel.
- **Formed Christology**: the Son / Lamb / cloud-rider is Yahusha, the Formed drawn from the
  Formless Father, who has a Father; the "like the Son of Adam" kaph (1:13, 14:14) is
  preserved and incarnation-honoring.
- **Election before confession**: names written in the book of life from the foundation
  (13:8, 17:8); names are blotted out, never added.
- **Torah-affirming**: the saints are the commandment-keepers (12:17, 14:12, 22:14); the
  beast "thinks to change times and laws"; the mark is the anti-Torah counterfeit of the seal.
- **Son of man → Son of Adam** everywhere (lineage of Adam). Keep the kaph "like" at 1:13/14:14.
- Babylon/harlot = the unfaithful covenant-city / spiritual harlotry through the prophets —
  NO modern sectarian (Rome/Vatican) import.

## VERBATIM QUOTE REQUIREMENT (the fidelity gate is unforgiving)
Every `note` you write quotes the TARGET verse and MUST be verbatim from the parsed edition

```
PY
```
NOTE the file name for pseudepigrapha is `pseudepigrapha-charles-vol2.json`; for apocrypha it
is `apocrypha.json`. The EDITION SLUG used in SQL is different from the file name for
pseudepigrapha: in SQL use edition slug **`pseudepigrapha`** but the file is
`pseudepigrapha-charles-vol2.json`. For apocrypha the SQL edition slug AND file are `apocrypha`.
Book slugs in SQL = the book_id you see in the JSON (e.g. `1-enoch`, `2-esdras`, `tobit`,
`the-wisdom-of-solomon`, `ecclesiasticus`, `2-baruch`, `testaments-xii`,
`baruch-with-the-letter-of-jeremiah`).

Quote a clean, self-contained clause from the verse (you may use `...` to elide), but every
word you keep must appear exactly as in the source. Wrap the quote in `*...*` and end with the
citation, e.g. `*...* (1 Enoch 46:1)`. Book names in citations are conventional English
(1 Enoch, 2 Esdras, Daniel, Isaiah), never Hebrew.

```sql
WITH input(src_edition, src_slug, src_ch, src_v,
           tgt_edition, tgt_slug, tgt_ch, tgt_v, tier, note) AS (VALUES

  FROM input i
```

### Rules that keep the gates green
- `src` is ALWAYS a `canon`/`revelation` verse that the target thread actually covers (pick a
  verse inside the thread's anchor span; read the thread's INSERT to see its anchor verses).
- `tier`: use `'extras'` for any member whose target is an extra-canonical edition or whose
  thread is already `extras`; use `'free'` for canon-only prophetic adds. (When unsure, `extras`.)
- Every (edition,book,chapter,verse) must RESOLVE — verify the verse exists in the parsed JSON
  before you write it. A non-resolving tuple silently drops and fails the resolve gate.
- Do not duplicate an existing member (read the chapter's existing members first).
- member sort_order: start at 21 and increment per new member within a thread, so you never
  collide with the existing 1..n members.
