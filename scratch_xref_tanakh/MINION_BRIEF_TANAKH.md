## STEP 2 — AUTHOR THE SQL FRAGMENT (follow this template EXACTLY)

**A. Temp view** — uniquely named with YOUR tag, scoped to the full edition set:
```sql
CREATE TEMP VIEW _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup AS
SELECT e.slug AS edition_slug, b.slug AS book_slug, c.chapter_number, v.verse_number, v.id AS verse_id
  FROM verses v JOIN chapters c ON v.chapter_id=c.id JOIN books b ON c.book_id=b.id
  JOIN editions e ON b.edition_id=e.id
 WHERE e.slug IN ('canon','enoch','jubilees','jasher','apocrypha','apocrypha-charles-vol1','pseudepigrapha','adam-eve-conflict','apocalypse-of-abraham','ascension-isaiah','sonnini-acts-29');
```
(`<TAG>` = e.g. `ge01` for Genesis 1. The session prefix `s3NN` is given to you at spawn.)

**B. cross_references INSERT** from `WITH input(src_edition,src_slug,src_ch,src_v,
tgt_edition,tgt_slug,tgt_ch,tgt_v,tier,note) AS (VALUES ...)`:
- **SOURCE is ALWAYS your canon Tanakh verse:** `'canon','<book_slug>',ch,v`.
- **TARGET** is any library verse (canon Tanakh/NT, or an extras edition).
- **Tier per-row** (cast `::content_tier`): canon target (Tanakh + NT) = **`'free'`**;
  extra-canonical target = **`'extras'`**.
- Join the view twice (`sv` on src, `tv` on tgt), `WHERE sv.verse_id <> tv.verse_id`,
  `ON CONFLICT (source_verse_id, target_verse_id, source) DO NOTHING`. `source='manual'`.
- The `note` quotes the TARGET verse IN FULL in italics with its citation in parentheses, then
  1–3 sentences naming the framework connection back to your source verse (quote the source
  phrase inline in italics too).

```sql
INSERT INTO cross_references (source_verse_id, target_verse_id, source, note, tier_required)
SELECT sv.verse_id, tv.verse_id, 'manual', i.note, i.tier::content_tier
  FROM input i
  JOIN _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup sv ON sv.edition_slug=i.src_edition AND sv.book_slug=i.src_slug AND sv.chapter_number=i.src_ch AND sv.verse_number=i.src_v
  JOIN _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup tv ON tv.edition_slug=i.tgt_edition AND tv.book_slug=i.tgt_slug AND tv.chapter_number=i.tgt_ch AND tv.verse_number=i.tgt_v
 WHERE sv.verse_id <> tv.verse_id
ON CONFLICT (source_verse_id, target_verse_id, source) DO NOTHING;
```

**C. threads INSERT** — one per theme. `tier_required` = `'free'` if EVERY member is canon,
`'extras'` if ANY member is extras. `sort_order` from YOUR assigned band. Anchor start/end = the
canon source verses bounding the thread. `summary_md` walks the whole thread in Come-and-See
prose, quoting the load-bearing verses in full in italics. `ON CONFLICT (slug) DO NOTHING`.
```sql
INSERT INTO cross_reference_threads (slug, title, summary_md, anchor_verse_id_start, anchor_verse_id_end, tier_required, sort_order)
SELECT '<slug>', E'<title>', E'<summary_md>',
       sv.verse_id, ev.verse_id, '<tier>', <sort_order>
  FROM _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup sv, _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup ev
 WHERE sv.edition_slug='canon' AND sv.book_slug='<book_slug>' AND sv.chapter_number=<ch> AND sv.verse_number=<startv>
   AND ev.edition_slug='canon' AND ev.book_slug='<book_slug>' AND ev.chapter_number=<ch> AND ev.verse_number=<endv>
ON CONFLICT (slug) DO NOTHING;
```

**D. thread_members INSERT** — link each cross_reference to its thread with `sort_order` +
`member_note` (a short come-and-see line naming the target + the connection). Match the
cross_reference by source+target verse via the view:
```sql
INSERT INTO cross_reference_thread_members (thread_id, cross_reference_id, sort_order, member_note)
SELECT t.id, cr.id, <n>, E'<member_note>'
  FROM cross_reference_threads t
  JOIN _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup sv ON sv.edition_slug='canon' AND sv.book_slug='<book_slug>' AND sv.chapter_number=<ch> AND sv.verse_number=<srcv>
  JOIN _s3NN_<TAG>_lookup tv ON tv.edition_slug='<tgt_ed>' AND tv.book_slug='<tgt_slug>' AND tv.chapter_number=<tgt_ch> AND tv.verse_number=<tgt_v>
  JOIN cross_references cr ON cr.source_verse_id=sv.verse_id AND cr.target_verse_id=tv.verse_id AND cr.source='manual'
 WHERE t.slug='<slug>'
ON CONFLICT (thread_id, cross_reference_id) DO NOTHING;
```

Use `E'...'` literals; escape every apostrophe as `''`. **SLUGS use English book-name
fragments**, prefixed `<book_slug>-<chapter>-`, naming the weave — e.g.
`genesis-1-the-formed-spoke-and-the-light-was-good`,
`genesis-3-the-seed-of-the-woman-shall-bruise-the-serpents-head`. Check every slug against
`EXISTING_SLUGS.txt` — none may already appear there.
