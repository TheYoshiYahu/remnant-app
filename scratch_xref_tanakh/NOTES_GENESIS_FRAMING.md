## Pack 1 (Gen 1–3) — LIVE, 17 threads/70 members
- **Gen 1 "And Elohim said" / "Let us make"** — framed as the Formed Son drawn from the Formless,
  the agent of creation who HAS a Father (John 1:1-3, Col 1:16, Heb 1:2); explicitly NOT
  trinitarian co-equal-persons grammar, NOT modalism. The Gen-1-broad-mankind vs Gen-2:7-Adam
  distinction noted in coverage, woven without collapsing the chapter's broad-creation register.
- **Gen 1:14 luminaries** — surfaced as the appointed-times (moedim) ROOT that Leviticus 23
  builds on (Ps 104:19). Load-bearing for the whole calendar architecture.
- **Gen 2:2-3 Sabbath** — creation-rooted, hallowed BEFORE Sinai, the foundation Exod 20:11
  grounds the command on, the perpetual sign (Exod 31:16-17), the rest that *remaineth* (Heb 4:9),
  made for Adam (Mark 2:27), kept by all flesh in the new heavens/earth (Isa 66:23). Thread does
  NOT prescribe practice — the verses dismantle the abolished-Sabbath lie. (Christianized
  "superseded/just-for-the-Jews" default overridden.)
- **Gen 2:7** — the SEPARATE forming of the seed-of-promise Adam → last Adam type (1 Cor 15:45).
- **Gen 2:24 one-flesh** — the marriage-COVENANT / seed-absorption mechanism (wife absorbed into
  husband's seed-identity; figure of Messiah-and-assembly Eph 5:31), not generic romance.
- **★ Gen 3:15 protoevangelium** — the institution of the literal seed-war (serpent's seed vs the
  woman's Seed running the whole canon); *made of a woman* (Gal 4:4) the Formed Son crushing the
  head; victory shared with the body (Rom 16:20) and the war still on *the remnant which keep the
  commandments* (Rev 12:17, holding the 1 John 2:3-4 filter). Wheat/tares fullness (Matt 13)
  deliberately NOT pulled here — this is the enmity instituted, not the harvest.
- Excluded as members (bad parse): Wisdom 2:24 (trailing apparatus glyph), 1 Enoch ch25,
  Adam-Eve Conflict (no book slugs in this parse).
