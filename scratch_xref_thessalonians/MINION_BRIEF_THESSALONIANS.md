## THE GOVERNING FRAME (read before you author — your default trained reading is the error to override)

Paul, Silvanus, and Timotheus write to the **called-out ones** at Thessalonica — Israel and the
grafted-in seed of the nations who *turned to Elohim (God) from idols to serve the living and true
Elohim (God)* (1 Thess 1:9). The whole correspondence assumes:

- **★★ 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18 — THE resurrection-and-gathering. GUARD IT.** *the dead in Messiah
  (Christ) shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them
  in the clouds, to meet Yahuah (Lord) in the air* (4:16–17), at *the trump of Elohim (God)* (4:16).
  Read this through the framework: this is **the one great resurrection-ingathering at the last trump**
  — the same event as Isaiah 27:13 (*the great trumpet shall be blown, and they shall come which were
  ready to perish... and shall worship Yahuah*, the regathering of the scattered outcasts), 1 Corinthians
  15:51–52 (*at the last trump... the dead shall be raised*), Matthew 24:31 (*a great sound of a
  trumpet... gather together his elect from the four winds*), and the resurrection of Daniel 12:2 and
  Ezekiel 37 (the graves opened, the two-house people raised). The *caught up together... to meet the
  Lord* is the gathered commonwealth of Israel meeting the returning King — **NOT a secret, pre-tribulation
  rapture detached from Israel, and NOT a separate program for a "church" that replaces Israel.** Build
  the thread on the trump-of-gathering and the resurrection of the Tanakh; frame the *meeting in the
  air* as the ingathering of the one regathered people. Do not import the dispensational secret-rapture
  reading.
- **★★ 2 Thessalonians 2:1–12 — the falling away and the man of sin. GUARD IT.** *that man of sin be
  revealed, the son of perdition; Who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called Elohim
  (God)... so that he as Elohim (God) sitteth in the temple of Elohim (God), shewing himself that he is
  Elohim (God)* (2:3–4); *whom Yahuah (Lord) shall consume with the spirit of his mouth* (2:8). This is
  **the antichrist figure already drawn in the Tanakh**: Daniel's little horn / the king who *shall
  exalt himself, and magnify himself above every god, and shall speak marvellous things against the
  Elohim of elohim* (Daniel 11:36; cf. Daniel 7:25, 8:25), the self-deifying tyrant of Isaiah 14:13–14
  (*I will exalt my throne... I will be like the most High*) and Ezekiel 28:2 (*thou hast said, I am a
  God... yet thou art a man*). His consuming *with the spirit of his mouth* (2:8) is Isaiah 11:4 (*with
  the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked*). Note 2:1: *our gathering together unto him* — the
  SAME ingathering as 1 Thess 4 (the day cannot be severed from Israel's regathering). Build the man-of-
  sin thread on Daniel + Isaiah 14 + Ezekiel 28 + Isaiah 11:4. **Do NOT render the falling away / the
  temple as Israel cast off; the deceiver is the counterfeit, not the covenant people.**
- **⚠ 1 Thessalonians 2:14–16 — the persecutors who killed the Lord and the prophets. HANDLE WITH
  CARE.** *the Yahudim (Jews): Who both killed the Lord Yahusha (Lord Jesus), and their own prophets,
  and have persecuted us... for the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost* (2:14–16). This is **NOT a
  blanket curse on the Jewish people and NOT replacement theology.** Paul is himself a Yahudi (Jew)
  writing of the *specific* persecutors — the same pattern the Tanakh and the Messiah name: the killing
  of the prophets (Nehemiah 9:26 *slew thy prophets which testified against them*, 2 Chronicles 36:16,
  Matthew 23:31–37, Acts 7:52 *which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted?*). The wrath
  falls on the persecuting opposition, not on Israel-as-such — *Elohim hath not cast away his people*
  (Romans 11:1–2). Build the thread on the prophets-killed pattern; **do NOT render it as "the Jews are
  rejected / the church replaces Israel."**
- **No replacement theology. No two-covenant split. No law-vs-grace antithesis.** *the day of Yahuah
  (Lord)* (1 Thess 5:2), the resurrection, and the gathering belong to the one olive tree, the
  regathered house of Israel and the grafted-in nations.
- **Christology (the Formed):** *his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead* (1 Thess 1:10) — the
  Father raised the Son; the Son is the Formed, sent and exalted, returning to gather. Keep the Father
  the source and the Son the Formed who comes; no co-equal-persons grammar.
