51.  go then, and eat the fat, and drink the sweet, and send portions to them that have
52.  nothing; for the day is holy unto the Lord: and be not sorrowful; for the Lord will bring you to honour.
53.  So the Levites published all things to the people, saying, This day is holy;
54.  be not sorrowful. Then went they their way, every one to eat and drink, and make merry, and to give portions to them that had nothing,
55.  and to make great cheer; because they under- stood the words wherein they were instructed, and for the which they had been assembled.
