not be unpunished’); also Qo. v. 10 (Hebr. 9) (‘he that loveth silver shall not be satisfied with silver’, &c.). For
the same theme (the dangers of love of money) cp. also 1 Tim. vi. 9.

that pursueth hire. Or rather ‘gain, profit’ ; cp. xlii. 9, vii. 18.

appended to this verse in #} and S (see critical note) cp. Prov. xi. 4 (‘Riches profit not in the day of wrath, but
righteousness delivereth from death’).
