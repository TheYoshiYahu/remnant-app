(6) XLIZ. 9-14. The subject of this subsection is the care of daughters. The same subject is touched upon in
Vil, 24-25, Xxvi. 10-12.

9. A daughter ... treasure of sleeplessness... Edersheim cites some of the Talmudic dicta on the subject
of daughters and women generally, among others the following : ‘ Happy he that has male children; woe to him that
has female children _(Qidd. 82 b; Baba bathra 16 6, &c.); ‘Women are of light mind’ (Shadé. 33 6; Qidd. 806) ; and
the well-known one in ge Aboth, ‘prolong not converse with a woman’ (i.5). The Blessing in the Jewish Morning
Prayer may also be cited: ‘ Blessed art Thou, O Lord... who hast not made me a gentile ...a slave... a woman.’
But the harshness of tone manifest in Ben-Sira towards women generally was much mitigated in later (Rabbinical)
Judaism under the influence of the Pharisees.
