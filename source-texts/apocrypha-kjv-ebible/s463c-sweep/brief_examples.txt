CARD NOTE EXAMPLES (from the approved 1 Enoch close-out):

*And at that hour that Son of Adam was named In the presence of Yahuah (Lord) of Spirits, And his name before the Head of Days.* (1 Enoch 48:2). *And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name Yahusha (JESUS): for he shall save his people from their sins* (Matthew 1:21). The name given at the manger had already been named before the Head of Days. Enoch saw the Son of Adam named in the presence of Yahuah (Lord) of Spirits before there was a Bethlehem to be born in; the angel's instruction to Yoseph (Joseph) is that name arriving on earth. The one who saves his people is the one who was named before his people existed.

*And in those days in one place the fathers together with their sons shall be smitten And brothers one with another shall fall in death Till the streams flow with their blood. For a man shall not withhold his hand from slaying his sons and his sons’ sons, And the sinner shall not withhold his hand from his honoured brother: From dawn till sunset they shall slay one another.* (1 Enoch 100:1–2). *And a man's foes shall be they of his own household* (Matthew 10:36). The King is quoting Micah: *a man's enemies are the men of his own house* (Micah 7:6). Enoch saw it in blood — fathers and sons smitten in one place, brother falling on brother from dawn till sunset. The seed war of Genesis 3:15 runs inside families, not only between nations; two brunettes can bear a son of Belial, and Eli's own sons were sons of Belial. Hearing the Shepherd's voice divides a house because not everyone in the house is a sheep.

*And again Yahuah (Lord) said to Raphael: ‘Bind Azâzêl hand and foot, and cast him into the darkness: and make an opening in the desert, which is in Dûdâêl, and cast him therein. And place upon him rough and jagged rocks, and cover him with darkness, and let him abide there for ever, and cover his face that he may not see light.* (1 Enoch 10:4–5). *How can one enter into a strong man's house, and spoil his goods, except he first bind the strong man?* (Matthew 12:29). Binding is how Yahuah (God) deals with the strong man. Raphael was sent to bind Azazel hand and foot, cast him into darkness, cover him with rocks, and leave him there until the judgment. When Yahusha (Jesus) cast out devils and said the strong man had to be bound first, he was describing something that had been done before and would be done again — the binding of the serpent for the length of the reign (Revelation 20:2). The exorcisms were the spoiling; the binding is older than Galilee.
